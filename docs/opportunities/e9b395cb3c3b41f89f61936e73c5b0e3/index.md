# Notice of Intent to Sole Source – Cross-Domain Enterprise All-Source User Repository (CENTAUR) III System IDIQ Ceiling Increase

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 15:04:37.095-04
- Solicitation Number: CENTAUR-26

## Summary

In accordance with RFO FAR 5.101, the Army Contracting Command - Aberdeen Proving Ground (ACC-APG) intends to issue a sole-source modification to increase the contract ceiling of the existing Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract W56KGY-23-D-0005 awarded to Raytheon Company. The proposed modification will increase the total overall ceiling amount by $27,345,375.95 to a new total of $72,893,824.77.
