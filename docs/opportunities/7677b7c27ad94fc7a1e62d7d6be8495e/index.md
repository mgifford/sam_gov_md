# Notice of Intent to Sole Source - Mobile Crash Barrier

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-29 13:32:21.093-04
- Solicitation Number: A063786

## Summary

Procurement of one (1) Delta Scientific Corp MP 5000 mobile crash barrier. Notice of Intent - 341 SFS Mobile Crash Barrier Notice of Intent to Sole-Source: The 341st Contracting Squadron intends to award on a sole-source basis a contract to Delta Scientific Corporation (DSC) to support the Malmstrom Air Force Base (MAFB). MAFB currently uses two other MP5000 barricades and uses other DSC systems as their permanent barriers installed at the installation gates. Multiple base personnel are trained in DSC’s system including the MP5000 and parts between DSC are interchangeable. DSC is the proprietary owner of the other barricade systems currently installed on base and the sole manufacturer of their system(s). DSC does not sell any products/services to third parties/distributors and has an established supply line. Therefore, only Delta Scientific Corporation is capable of fulfilling this requirement. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE OFFERS, and a solicitation will not be issued. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which shall be considered. Any response to this notice must show clear and convincing evidence that the competition of this requirement would be advantageous to the Government. All responsible sources may submit a capability statement, proposal, or quotation, which shall be considered by the agency. If no affirmative written response is received, the award will be made without further notice. Oral communications are not acceptable in response to this notice. RESPONSES ARE DUE NO LATER THAN 15 June 2026 by 3:30PM MDT at 341 CONS, Malmstrom AFB to Jean-Denis Ducimetiere, Contract Specialist at jean-denis.ducimetiere@us.af.mil. Please see the attached specifications for further details.
