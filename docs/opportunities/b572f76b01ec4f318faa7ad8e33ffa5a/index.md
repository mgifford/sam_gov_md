## Summary

Description of purpose work nature and volume: The chosen contractor shall provide complete construction services including labor, materials, equipment, and supervision required to complete the Correct Imaging Tower Deficiencies project for the Cincinnati VAMC. This project requires a full roof replacement above A2013 & A2016, a lead-lined door replacement in A2016 (in favor of something like a traditional double-door where both sides open with operator) and improved cooling capacity within CT equipment room A2016A. The [Correct Imaging Tower Deficiencies] project is intended to replace the full roof above A2013 & A2016, along with lead-lined door replacement in A2016 (in favor of something like a traditional double-door where both sides open with operator). Additionally, this project will add cooling capacity to CT control room A2016A through the placement of a dedicated ductless split system with capacity to cool at a rate of up to 30k BTU/HR. The installation of this should be all inclusive of all carpentry, power and plumbing work. The above construction Statement Of Work (SOW) constitutes a basic outline of work and in no way outlines all of the details for the construction of this project. It is recommended that a detailed inspection of the premises, drawings and specification be initiated to determine the needs and conditions for the construction of this project. Period of Performance: All phases of work, including project closeout documentation, to be completed & provided within 180 calendar days after issuance of the Notice To Proceed (NTP). The magnitude of construction project is estimated between $500,000 and $1,000,000 Location of the work: All work to be performed, according to the drawings and specifications at the: Cincinnati VA Medical Center 3200 Vine Street Cincinnati, Ohio 45220-2637 Tentative dates for issuing invitations, opening bids, and completing contract performance; The Invitation for Bids (IFB) should be posted on/about 05-29-2026 and the Bid Opening should be within 30-45 days thereafter. Plans will be available for inspection without charge: The specification and drawings will be available electronically with the IFB when released. Date by which requests for the invitation for bids should be submitted: There will be no physical copies provided. Set-Aside: This solicitation is set-aside for service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) concerns. Amount to be charged for solicitation documents: Not Applicable. ***END OF SYNOPSIS***
