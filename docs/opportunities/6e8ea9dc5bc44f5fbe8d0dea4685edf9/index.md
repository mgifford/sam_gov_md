# Industry Day - Human Resource Solutions, GEN VI Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity Multiple Award Contracts

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-22 09:20:38.256-04

## Summary

The Army Contracting Command – New Jersey (ACC-NJ), on behalf of the Human Resource (HR) Solutions Program Office, is hosting an Industry Day for the Sixth Generation (GEN VI) Personnel Lifecycle Support (PLS) and Recruiting Management Administrative Support (RMAS) Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) Multiple Award Contract Suites. Interested contractors may attend in-person or virtually. The Government anticipates release of a DRAFT Request for Proposal via SAM.gov prior to the industry day. Please continue to monitor SAM.gov for additional updates.
