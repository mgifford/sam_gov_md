# UTV - Utility Task Vehicle

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 11:15:56.021-04
- Solicitation Number: IHS1524247

## Summary

The Lawton Indian Hospital (LIH), Indian Health Service (IHS) has a need for UTV (Utility Task Vehicle). The Current UTV is considered outdated and inefficient due to escalating maintenance costs and frequent repairs. The cost of maintaining this unit now exceeds the operational efficiency and cost-effectiveness of the current UTV models available on the market. Operational Needs include Delivery of Packages, Medical Supplies, Property Equipment across the Hospital Campus while driving across a variety of terrain. The contractor shall supply one (1) Utility Task Vehicle (UTV) like or equal to the Bobcat model UV34.
