## Summary

PRESOLICITATION NOTICE LAB Courier Service for Durham VA Medical Center and CBOCs The Department of Veterans Affairs, Durham VAMC in Durham, North Carolina, has a requirement for a contractor to provide lab courier services in accordance with the Performance Work Statement. The NAICS code for this action is 492110, Building Inspection Services with an SBA Size Standard of 1500 Employees. The Product Service Code is S209, Couriers and Express Delivery Services. The solicitation being issued will be set-aside 100% for Service Disabled Veteran Owned Small Businesses. Only one award will be made. It is anticipated that the solicitation will be made available on or around Wednesday, May 27, 2026. The solicitation and any amendments will be posted on this website, www.sam.gov Contract Opportunities. This notice is for informational purposes only. The Government is not obligated to issue a solicitation as a result of this notice. No other information is available until issuance of the solicitation. All interested parties should register on this website. It is the offerors responsibility to check the web site periodically for any amendments/notices to the solicitation.
