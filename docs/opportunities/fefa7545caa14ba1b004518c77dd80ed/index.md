# 16WS Cloud Platform BPA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-27 09:55:31.315-04
- Solicitation Number: FA460026A16WSBPA

## Summary

55 CONS is conducting a Sources Sought to determine the interest and availability of vendors for a Firm Fixed Price (FFP) Blanket Purchase agreement for Cloud Service Provider (CSP) credits set forth within the attached Statement of Need (SON). We are intending to award this as a competative Brand Name requirement with a certified Amazon Web Services reseller. Offerors may submit alternate "equal to" proposals for consideration by the governement. Salient Characteristics must include: -The company must be able to provide Cloud Service Provider (CSP) capabilities in regions designated for US Government only use and commercial. -Those CSPs must have the capability to host Impact Level 6 (IL6) based on the DoD Cloud Computing Security Requirements Guide (SRG). -The company must be authorized to offer capabilities in those US Government only regions up to IL6. - The company must be capable of providing payment services (credits) on behalf of wholly governement operated accounts. Please review attached Statement of Need and submit capabilities packages/letters of interest to Mr. Casey Hupton at casey.hupton.1@us.af.mil and TSgt Jennifer Prestridge at jennifer.prestridge.1@us.af.mil.
