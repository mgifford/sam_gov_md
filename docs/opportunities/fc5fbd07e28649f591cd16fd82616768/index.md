# Angle Block Sets

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 09:07:54.847-04

## Summary

The U.S. Army Contracting Command-Redstone, Operations, Services, & Training Division, plans to obtain information to serve as market research to help identify potential sources that can provide a brand name purchase of purchase of ten (10) Angle Block Sets, this equipment will drastically increase angle measurement capability through the entire USATA organization. All 8 reference labs, the Training lab, and the ESD TV lab should receive a set. These items shall be delivered to the Enterprise Support Division (ESD), Building 5435 Fowler Road, Redstone Arsenal, AL. The Angle Block Sets shall include the following specifications or equivalent (if not brand name): Specifications: Measuring Ranges: 0-99° Accuracy: Must have accuracy of ±1 second (arcsecond) Blocks Per Set: 16 Blocks Blocks Included: 6 Blocks: 1°, 3°, 5°, 15°, 30°, 45° | 5 Blocks: 1?, 3?, 5?, 20?, 30? | 5 Blocks: 1?, 3?, 5?, 20?, 30?
