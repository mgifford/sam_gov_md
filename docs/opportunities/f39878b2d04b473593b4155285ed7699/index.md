## Summary

The Marine Corps Logistics Command, Contracts Department (CONT) intends to solicit and contract with Comet Technologies USA Inc DBA YXLON International (CAGE 6XWP3) of Shelton, Connecticut on a sole-source basis for Preventative Manintenance and Service for YXLON International X-Ray-System Modle MG452. No solicitation will be posted on this website for this requirement. A Justification and Approval document is attached to this notice. Any potential source may contact the persons indicated above to discuss their ability to offer the required services for future acquisitions.?
