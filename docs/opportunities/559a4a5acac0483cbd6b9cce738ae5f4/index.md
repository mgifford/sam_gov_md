# This SSS is to notify companies that the Defense Logistics Agency, Redstone Arsenal, Alabama, is seeking qualified sources for the acquisition of the following items in support of the CH-47 CHINOOK weapon system.

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 11:32:50.133-04
- Solicitation Number: (SPRRA1-26-R-0045)

## Summary

No telephone requests will be accepted. Direct your requests and communication to: ATTN: DLA-AHD1, Justin Taylor, Contract Specialist, via e-mail: justin.2.taylor@dla.mil. For request for solicitation, provide your name, the company name and Cage Code and a telephone number. The Government proposes to acquire on a Single Purchase contract the following parts that are applicable to the CH-47 CHINOOK weapon system. See attached idenitifcation of requirements (IOR). Production Lead Time (PLT) with and without First Article Testing is listed above in Days After Contract Award (DACA) with deliveries to TINKER AFB, OK, 73145-8000. These items are restricted to: THE BOEING COMPANY, CAGE CODE: 77272 For PN 145D3500-17, The rights to use the data needed to purchase this part from additional source(s) are not owned by the Government and cannot be purchased, developed, or otherwise obtained. It is uneconomical to reverse engineer this part. For PN 145G0031-1, The rights to use the data needed to purchase this part from additional source(s) are not owned by the Government and cannot be purchased, developed, or otherwise obtained. It is uneconomical to reverse engineer this part. All offerors must meet prequalification requirements in order to be eligible for award. Firms that recognize and can produce the required item described above are encouraged to identify themselves. Firms are encouraged to seek source approval in order to compete for future solicitations by visiting AvMC's public website: https://www.avmc.army.mil/Directorates/SRD/TechDataMgmt All responsible sources may submit an offer, which shall be considered by the Agency. The closing date annotated is an estimated date and may be adjusted dependent upon the date of release for solicitation; however, the solicitation will not close prior to the date stated above. Foreign Firms are reminded that all requests for solicitation must be processed through their respective embassies.
