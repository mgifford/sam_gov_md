# IDIQ - Laboratory Gloves

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 09:22:50.675-04
- Solicitation Number: PR-000784

## Summary

The FBI intends to award a single award, Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract for latex and nitrile laboratory gloves, for the Laboratory Division (LD). This requirement is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. Please see the attached RFP for complete details and instructions. Questions pertaining to the solicitation are due by 12:00 PM/EST on Friday, May 15th, 2026. All questions must be submitted using the enclosed Offeror Question Submission Attachment. Proposals must be received by 5:00 PM/EST on Wednesday, May 20th, 2026. Amendment 1 - 5/5/26 - Questions and Answers Excel Sheet attached, Updated Product Pricing Matrix attached, Updated Glove Specifications attached. Amendment 2 - 5/13/2026 - Solicitation reopened and response period extended. Buy American compliance requirements have been removed. TAA compliance requirements have been removed for Latex gloves. Pricing column added for Optional 6-month extension. Please see updated attachments. Amendment 3 - 5/14/2026 - Clarification on TAA compliance: Nitrile gloves must still meet TAA requirements, while TAA compliance is no longer required for Latex gloves.
