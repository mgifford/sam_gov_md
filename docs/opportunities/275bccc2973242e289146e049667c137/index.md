## Summary

The purpose of this amendment is to publish additional questions with answers. The updated document is "B 08 - Bulk Ice Questions and Answers 2". Additionally, the Statement of Work has been updated to read an estimated quantitiy of 112,000 pounds versus the originally posted estimated 80,000 pounds. The purpose of this amendment is to answer a question. The question is on attached document "B 08 - Bulk Ice Questions and Answers" This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number W912LM-26-Q-A010 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ), to provide bulk cubed bagged ice for Camp Ripley in Little Falls, MN.
