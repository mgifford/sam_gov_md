# Flatbed purchase and installation for the Sawtooth National Forest

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 13:00:07.148-04
- Solicitation Number: 1240LP26Q0073

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1240LP26Q0073 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for purchase and installation of flatbed on a 2026 Ford F-550 (agency owned). This request is for the Sawtooth National Forest in Jerome, Idaho 83338. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 336211. The small business size standard is 1,000 employees. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency. See Attachment 1 – Solicitation Terms and Conditions for applicable clauses, instructions, and evaluation criteria. See Attachment 2 for the Statement of Work with Specifications. If submitting a quote package, please include a signed SF1449 (fill out blocks 17 and 30a-c), include a completed Schedule of Items from page 1 of Attachment 1, and include a Technical Capability statement in your technical proposal.
