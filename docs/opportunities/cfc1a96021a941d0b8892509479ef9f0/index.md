# Comprehensive Cost and Requirement (CCaR) DoW Enterprise Maintenance

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 13:15:27.89-04
- Solicitation Number: FA877026RB100

## Summary

Provision of an enterprise-level usage and maintenance license for the Department of War (DoW) acquisition community (including Air Force, Army, and Navy) to utilize the CCaR software. The Acquisition Domain consists of all organizations that currently use CCaR under previously purchased CCaR Named License Agreements, all organizations required to use CCaR in accordance with DAFI63-101/20-101, Army Reg 70-1, ASA(ALT) SAAL-ZR CFO Operating Policy Number 13.3 (dtd 1 Jul 24) and any additional organizations whose primary responsibility is to execute RDT&E, Procurement investment dollars and program data. The enterprise maintenance license shall place no limits on the number of Acquisition Domain CCaR users. The license must allow for an unlimited number of users across all DoW components, currently expected to be approximately 24,950 users. Maintenance shall include installing all CCaR software upgrades and patches that are made generally available by the Contractor to its customers.
