# Flagpole Installation Services and Materials

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 11:24:29.523-04
- Solicitation Number: W912PX26QA005

## Summary

The 410th Contracting Support Brigade (CSB), Regional Contracting Office (RCO) – Miami, in support of the Joint Interagency Task Force South (JIATF South) located at Truman Annex, Bldg 4180, Key West, FL 33040, is issuing on a competitive basis 100% Total Small Business set-aside, Firm Fixed Price Purchase Order. This requirement is for the furnishing of all labor, materials, tools, and equipment necessary for the installation of two (2) new flagpoles, each approximately 25-30 feet in height. The flagpoles must match the existing flagpoles in material, finish, and style to ensure uniformity. Installation must meet or exceed hurricane wind load requirements for Monroe County, Florida, and comply with the Unified Facilities Criteria (UFC) and all applicable local, state, and federal codes. All work planning and coordination must begin within 5 days of contract award, and all work must be completed no later than 180 days after award. This announcement constitutes the Solicitation. NOTE: To be considered for award, the quoter must be the direct provider of the installation services. Quotes or proposals submitted by third-party brokers, agents, or entities acting solely as pass-through organizations will be deemed non-responsive and excluded from evaluation. The quoter must affirmatively demonstrate in their technical proposal that they possess the necessary in-house personnel, equipment, and expertise to execute the primary flag pole installation directly.
