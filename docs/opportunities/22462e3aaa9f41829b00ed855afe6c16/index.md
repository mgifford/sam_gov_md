# RFI_Next-Generation Nett Warrior EUD

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-22 10:38:34.871-04
- Solicitation Number: RFI-W91CRB26-NW-SS

## Summary

ACC-APG, on behalf of Product Manager Soldier Maneuver Sensors (PdM SMS), is issuing this Request for Information to conduct market research on commercially available or near-production-ready handheld devices that can serve as the next-generation End User Device (EUD). Disclaimer: This is an RFI for market research purposes only and does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP) or a promise to issue an RFP in the future. The Government will not award a contract based on this RFI, nor will it pay for any information or administrative costs incurred in response to it.
