# Census 2030 Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment (FF&E) April 28, 2026

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-28 16:33:53.444-04

## Summary

The United States Census Bureau (USCB) is issuing this Request for Information (RFI) to gather market research on industry capabilities to furnish, deliver, install, and support furniture, fixtures, and equipment (FF&E) as described in Section 2.0. This RFI is for planning purposes only and does not constitute a solicitation, Request for Proposal (RFP), or commitment by the USCB. The USCB seeks information from qualified vendors capable of supporting variable quantities and phased delivery timelines for a physical footprint that includes one office in every state, with up to 162 locations nationwide, including Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico.
