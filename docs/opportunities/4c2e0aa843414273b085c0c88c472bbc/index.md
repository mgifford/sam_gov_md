# Correctional Fire Brigade Training

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 14:24:55.549-04
- Solicitation Number: 15BNAS26Q00000052

## Summary

This Statement of Work (SOW) outlines the requirements for providing a Correctional Fire Brigade Training Program that will enable BOP personnel to achieve certification as Interior Structural Fire Brigade Members under NFPA 1081. The training program must meet or exceed the standards set forth by NFPA 1081 and align with BOP operational requirements.
