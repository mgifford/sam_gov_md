(5) Background: The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), VA Southern Healthcare System (VASNHS) Las Vegas, NV VA Medical Center, which located at 6900 N. Pecos Rd., N. Las Vegas, NV 89086 intends to procure design-build services to install parking lot shades & reconfigure parking lot. Project Scope: The scope of this project shall provide complete design services to deliver a comprehensive solution by using parking lot improvement documents (see Section

10.0 Reference Material), engineering calculations, and preparation of construction documents compliant with OSHA, OHS, and VA Master Specifications as basis of design to reduce vehicle-pedestrian conflicts, enhance accessibility, and protect Veterans and staff from extreme heat, supporting VA s mission to provide a safe, accessible, and comfortable healthcare environment. The Design Build (DB) Contractor shall provide all Architect Engineer (A/E) design services, labor, materials, equipment, supplies, investigations, and professional supervision necessary to develop and deliver the complete design. The Contractor shall also furnish all required construction period services in support of this contract. Design must meet Veterans Affairs Design Guides and Design Criteria and Veterans Affairs Master Specifications at the technical information library TIL (www.cfm.va.gov/TIL). Design must follow all current applicable codes, including: International Building Code (I.B.C) National Fire Protection Association (N.F.P.A) Occupational Safety and Health Administration (O.S.H.A) Contractor shall provide all the equipment, labor, materials, tools and supplies required for construction of the project in accordance with the drawings and specifications including Davis Bacon Wage Rate. The Design Build contractor will be responsible for providing a complete set of construction specifications along with design drawings and engineer calculations for this project. Estimated Schedule for Design Build & Construction Time (reference SOW Section 3): Overall PoP (Parallel Design & Construction) Design & Preconstruction (to 65%): Target ~120 calendar days from Design NTP, including BOD/SUE/Geotech, QCP, and 35%/65% workshops. Construction (separate NTP; parallel start): Target ~320 340 calendar days from Construction NTP (issued after VA acceptance of 65%). Final Design (95%/100% IFC): Complete by ~Design NTP + 210 days, supporting early fabrication/field work. Closeout: ~45 calendar days for as builts, O&M, warranties, etc. Total PoP (Design NTP Final Acceptance): ~515 555 calendar days (~17 18.5 months), to be confirmed at 35% based on AE findings, utility conditions, and hospital operations. Please refer to the attached SOW for details.
