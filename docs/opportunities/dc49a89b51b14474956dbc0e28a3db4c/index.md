# Technical Support Services NS 2027-2032

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-19 14:04:36.215-04
- Solicitation Number: PANRSA-26-P-0000036468

## Summary

This is a non-personal service(s) contract to provide technical support services in support of the U.S. Army Garrison – Redstone Arsenal (USAG-Redstone) Directorate of Public Works (DPW), to include specialized database managers and analysts, facility and real property survey specialists and inspectors, geographic information systems technicians, quality assurance specialists, drafters, and engineers to maintain the reimbursable programs and activities that sustain the various tenant missions on Redstone Arsenal. These services will perform a wide variety of tasks related to public works and support any division within the USAG-Redstone DPW. These services are required for direct and indirect support of reimbursable customer requirements and may vary in scope and magnitude to reflect the current condition of customer funded projects, and any requirements for managing tenant requests for recurring support in various tenant support agreements. The contractor shall provide personnel, management, and any other items and services not Government furnished to perform the services defined in this PWS. Services shall be performed in support of USAG-Redstone DPW and shall be performed primarily at Redstone Arsenal, Alabama.
