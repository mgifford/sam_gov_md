## Summary

Amendment A0001 The purpose of this amendment is to provide answers to industry submitted questions during the RFQ period and add Attachment 5 - Specifications. No additional questions will be answered unless deemed in the best interest of the Government by the Contracting Officer. ANSWERS TO INDUSTRY SUBMITTED QUESTIONS: QUESTION 1:“Do you have any specs on the hose?” ANSWER: See Attachment 5 - Specifications This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; offers are being requested, and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1202SC25Q2706 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Collapsible Hose, 3”x25’, with female quick coupler and male quick adapter (NFES 0621) This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 332912. The small business size standard is 1,000 employees. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which shall be considered by the agency. *** SEE FOUR (4) ORIGINAL ATTACHMENTS *** *** AMENDMENT 0001: Added ATTACHMENT 5 SPECIFICATIONS ***
