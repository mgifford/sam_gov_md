REQUEST FOR INFORMATION (RFI) NOTICE Country Indonesia Project Name Millenium Challenge Account – Indonesia II Title One MSME Data Platform Market Sounding Procurement Ref. Number IND2-2026-50003 Publication Date 11 May 2026 Submission Deadline 5.00 pm Jakarta, Indonesia time (GMT+7) on 15 June 2026 1. The Millennium Challenge Account – Indonesia II (MCA-Indonesia II), acting as the implementing entity for the Compact Program, hereby invites interested vendors to respond to this Request for Information (RFI) for the One MSME Data Platform initiative under the MSME Finance Project. The purpose of this RFI is to gather market feedback, technical insights, and commercial perspectives on the potential development, operation, governance, and commercialization of a national MSME data platform intended to support MSME financing, financial inclusion, data interoperability, and private sector participation in Indonesia’s MSME ecosystem. This RFI is issued solely for information and planning purposes and does not constitute a solicitation.
