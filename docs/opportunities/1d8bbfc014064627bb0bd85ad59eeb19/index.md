## Summary

New service contract to provide PM, repair, and replacement parts to service a Government owned asset. The services requested are to provide PM services with options on one (1) Colenta X-ray Film Processor, model no. M43IC This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; proposals are being requested and a written solicitation will not be issued.
