## Summary

The FBI intends to award a single award, Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract for latex and nitrile laboratory gloves, for the Laboratory Division (LD). This requirement is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. Please see the attached RFP for complete details and instructions. Questions pertaining to the solicitation are due by 5:00 PM/EST on Friday, May 1st, 2026. All questions shall be submitted using the enclosed Offeror Question Submission Attachment. Proposals must be received by 5:00 PM/EST on Monday, May 11th, 2026. Amendment 1 - 5/5/26 - Questions and Answers Excel Sheet attached, Updated Product Pricing Matrix attached, Updated Glove Specifications attached. Proposal dealine updated to Monday, May 11th, 2026 5:00 pm EST.
