# Operations and Maintenance for Axiell Software Products

- Agency: LIBRARY OF CONGRESS
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-11 12:11:13.495-04
- Solicitation Number: LCLGR26P0019

## Summary

The Library of Congress has a requirement to renew its maintenance support plan for its Axiell software products. The contractor shall provide support and maintenance of the Audio Visual Collection Mangement Systems software products licensed from Axiell. The attached Justification for Sole Source is in support of this acquisition.
