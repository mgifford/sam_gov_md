## Summary

Enclosed is a Request for Quote (RFQ) for project # WHAY202001, Construct Main Gate Canopy. If you would like to submit a quotation, please follow the instructions in the attached SF 1442 document for proper submission of all required documents. Quotations are due 05 June, 2026 at 11:00AM EST. All quotes and associated documents must be in English. Incomplete quotes will not be accepted. A pre-proposal conference and site visit has been scheduled for 20 May 2026 at 10:00AM EST. The conference will convene at the Civil Engineering Squadron Conference Room, Building # 207 located at the 105th Airlift Wing, Stewart ANGB, One Maguire Way, Newburgh, NY 12550-5075. All attendees must pre-register for the conference. Instructions for pre-registering can be found in the attached SF 1442 solicitation document. All offerors shall be registered in the SAM (System for Award Management) database at https://www.sam.gov prior to submittal of their quote as prescribed under RFO FAR 4.203-1(b). Failure to be registered at time of quote submission may deem the offeror’s quote to be considered non-responsive and eliminated from any further award consideration. Therefore, offerors are highly encouraged to register immediately if they are interested in submitting a response to this requirement.
