## Summary

LAB COURIER SERVICES: The contractor shall provide regularly scheduled pickups of laboratory specimens, x-rays, medical equipment, etc. from DURHAM VA Health Care System as listed below and deliver to locations throughout eastern North Carolina, in accordance with the terms and conditions of this requirement. In addition to the scheduled weekday pickup/delivery, there are other non-scheduled deliveries both within Durham and to other CBOC locations within North Carolina. See SF1449 solicitation 36C24626Q0536 and its attachments.
