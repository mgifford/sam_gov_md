# Peak Scientific Genius XE35 120V Nitrogen Generator Service and Maintenance at PSNS&IMF

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 15:27:16.006-04
- Solicitation Number: N4523A26Q1314

## Summary

The Regional Maintenance Center at Puget Sound Naval Shipyard & Intermediate Maintenance Facility (PSNS&IMF) intends to award a standalone, a 5-year (base + 4 option year) service contract for the Peak Scientific Genius XE35 120V Nitrogen Generator that includes annual preventative maintenance visits and on demand service visits when needed. Noun Name: Nitrogen Generator Service and Maintenance Description: The Peak Scientific Genius XE35 120V Nitrogen Generator is a piece of equipment necessary for the operation of the Thermo Fisher Dionex Ion Chromatograph System 6000 with ISQ-EC Mass Spectrometer. The nitrogen generator utilizes the surrounding air to generate high-purity nitrogen for high performance instrumentation including mass spectrometry. The Peak Scientific Genius XE35 120V Nitrogen Generator will require annual preventative maintenance visits and on demand service visits when needed Scope: The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to perform the acts listed in the service contract as defined in this Performance Work Statement except for those items specified as government furnished property and services Period of Performance - 1 Jun 2026 – 31 May 2027 Please complete pricing on attached combined synopsis solicitation document and submit required documents by 17 Apr 2026, 9:00 am PST. Questions are due by 22 May 2026 at 9:00 am PST.
