# Justification for Exception to Fair Opportunity (JEFO) – ANSYS Multi-Physics Simulation Software Suite

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-13 10:35:23.593-04
- Solicitation Number: OMAS-JEFO-2026-75F40124F80112

## Summary

In accordance with FAR RFO 16.507-6(3), the Government is posting this Justification for an Exception to Fair Opportunity for a logical follow-on order under a multiple-award IDIQ contract. This action supports the continued provision of the ANSYS Multi-Physics Simulation Software Suite for the Food and Drug Administration (FDA). The exception is based on economy and efficiency as a logical follow-on to a previously competed order.
