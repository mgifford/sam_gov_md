# Travis AFB, CA - 60 AES C-130 Paint and repair

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 18:36:19.995-04

## Summary

60 AES needs a painting and repair service for a C-130 Trainer. The objective of this requirement is to repaint the exterior of the C-130 FUT, replacing the markings of the Delaware National Guard with the 60th Aeromedical Evacuation Squadrons markings. This project ensures proper environmental requirements are met and accurately denotes the unit responsible for the Fuselage Trainer (FuT). In accordance with the Statement of Work (SOW). Any questions or clarification shall be routed to both point of contacts (POC) listed no later than 07 May 2026 04:30PM PST. any inquiry submitted past the deadline will be not accepted. Quotes shall be provided to both POC no later than 19 May 2026 at 12:00PM PST, hunter.losen@us.af.mil and juliet.diggs.1@us.af.mil.
