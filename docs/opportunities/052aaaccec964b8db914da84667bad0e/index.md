## Summary

THIS IS A NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD A SOLE SOURCE PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT AND IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE QUOTES. The Defense Health Agency Contracting Activity (DHACA), Healthcare Contracting Division - West (HCD-W), Echo Branch intends to negotiate and award a firm-fixed price purchase order (PO) contract for the provision of all services, materials, and equipment necessary to provide required Reagents and Maintenance Service for Flow Cytometry Instruments for Madigan Armey Medical Center, Pathology Department in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 6.302-1 Sole Source acquisitions and FAR RFO 12.102 (a) only one source being available to meet the Government’s needs, to: Beckman Coulter, Inc 250 Kraemer Blvd Brea, CA 92821-6232 UEI: Z4DRFM3LMJB8 CAGE: 80740 The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this requirement is 325413 – In-Vitro Diagnostic Substance Manufacturing; the business size standard is 1,250 (# of employees). The Product Service Code (PSC) is 6550 - In Vitro Diagnostic Substances, Reagents, Test Kits, and Sets. The requirement is for Reagents and Maintenance Service contract for Madigan Armey Medical Center. The items being purchased are reagents and testing materials necessary to perform flow cytometry testing using Government-owned Navios 10 analyzer and TQ Prep workstation, as well as maintenance, service, and training on the analyzers for the Department of Pathology, Core Laboratory Services at Madigan Army Medical Center, Joint Base Lewis-McChord, WA. Flow cytometry is a critical tool used to specifically characterize and identify types of white blood cells, and any alteration in that cell type that may be responsible for the onset of infectious diseases, autoimmune disorders or immunodeficiencies.
