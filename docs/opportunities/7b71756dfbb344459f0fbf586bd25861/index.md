# On-Site System Assessment of the Clear Water Rinse System

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 17:23:42.386-04
- Solicitation Number: N6817126QN048

## Summary

This SOW defines the requirements for the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) to conduct a comprehensive onsite diagnostic assessment of the non-operational Clear Water Rinse System (CWRS) located at Keflavik Air Base, Iceland. The primary objective is to perform a root cause analysis of the system's failures, determine the full extent of necessary repairs, and provide actionable recommendations and a cost estimate to restore the system to full factory specifications.
