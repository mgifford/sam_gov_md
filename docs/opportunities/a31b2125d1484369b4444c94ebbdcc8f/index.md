# ROUNDTOP RFI

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-06 13:57:27.713-04
- Solicitation Number: DARPA-SN-26-43

## Summary

The Biological Technologies Office (BTO) of the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) seeks to solicit input from industry, academia, government laboratories, and non-traditional performers regarding the most advanced methods to rapidly mitigate and re-purpose large volumes of spilled Petroleum, Oils, and Lubricants (POLs).
