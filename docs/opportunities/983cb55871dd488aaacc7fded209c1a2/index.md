## Summary

The USDA Forest Service (USFS) intends on awarding a competitive BPA Call Order against the BPA for 12444726Q0031 – NC Vegetation Management BPA for Nantahala National Forest/Cheoah-Tusquitee Ranger District in NC. Location: Cheoah Ranger District, Robbinsville, NC 28771 This is not a solicitation. This synopsis notification of intent to award a BPA Call order does not constitute a request for proposals or submission of any information in response to this notification. This procurement is restricted to those approved companies eligible for call orders under the NC Vegetation Management BPA for Nantahala National Forest/Cheoah-Tusquitee Ranger District in NC.
