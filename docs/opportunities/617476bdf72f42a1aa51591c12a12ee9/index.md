# Notice of Intent to Award - Whiteriver Service Unit Steam Sterilizer, Reverse Osmosis, Washer Equipment Preventive Maintenance

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-29 11:48:34.928-04
- Solicitation Number: IHS1524421

## Summary

The Government hereby submits a notice of intent to award a sole source, firm fixed price purchase order to Getinge USA Sales, LLC., under NAICS code 811210 Medical and surgical equipment repair and maintenance services, by the Phoenix Area Office (PAO) Indian Health Services (IHS) under the authority of FAR 13.106-1(b)(2) (Soliciting from a Single Source). The requirement is for a contractor to provide Steam Sterilizer, Reverse Osmosis, Washer Equipment Preventive Maintenance on Getinge equipment at the Whiteriver Service Unit. This is NOT a request for quotes or proposals.
