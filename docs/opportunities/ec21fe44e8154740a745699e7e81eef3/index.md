## Summary

The purpose of this notice is to post the Justification for Limited Source Justification (LSJ) under Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Subpart 8.4; GSAR Subpart 538.7104-3(b)(iii). Washington Headquarters Services, Acquisition Directorate (WHS/AD) awarded a Firm Fixed Price (FFP)/Time and Materials (T&M) Hybrid Bridge Contract to PM2 Strategies, LLC (formerly named D.K. Jones Consulting, LLC) to provide Support Services for the Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD), Manufacturing Technology (ManTech) Program. This contract was awarded as sole source under the authority of FAR 8.4; GSAR Subpart 538.7104-3(b)(iii), "the order should be issued on a sole source basis in the interest of economy and efficiency because it is a logical follow-on to an FSS order already issued on a competitive basis". This notice provides a copy of the approved justification in accordance with FAR 6.305(a) and (d)(1).
