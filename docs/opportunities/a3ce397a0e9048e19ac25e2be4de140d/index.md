## Summary

This requirement is solicited as a Combined Synopsis/Solicitation using streamline procedures consistent with FAR Part 12 as modernized under the Revolutionary FAR Organization (RFO) framework. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotations are being requested, and a written solicitation document will not be issued. This solicitation is a Request for Quotations (RFQ). The solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular (FAC) 2023-06 (5 October 2023).The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 337211 Wood Office Furniture Manufacturing, with a small business size 1,000 employees. The Jacksonville District USACE Contracting Division ? Procurement Section is seeking to transform existing open office space into two fully enclosed offices, by way of installing floor to ceiling walls, doors, and glass, to transfer natural light from existing windows within RM. USACE Resource Management (RM) requires the services of a commercial contractor to provide the supplies and services needed to complete this transformation. The site of this project is the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) RM, 5TH Floor West, located at 701 San Marco Blvd, Jacksonville FL 32207.All interested companies must provide a quotation utilizing the attached pricing sheet: Pricing Sheet. A site visit to access the facility is not mandatory for interested quoters, but it is highly recommended. The site visit is scheduled Wednesday, June 3, 2026, at 10:00 Eastern Standard Time.
