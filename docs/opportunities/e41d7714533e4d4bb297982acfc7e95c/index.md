# Repair of B-2 Submerged Pump, Fuel Pumping Element & Fuel Pump Unit

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 12:36:56.755-04
- Solicitation Number: FD20302600529

## Summary

The United States Air Force through Air Force Sustainment Center (AFSC), Tinker AFB 73145 is contemplating the Repair of B-2 Submerged Pump, Fuel Pumping Element & Fuel Pump Unit. The contractor shall provide all labor, facilities, equipment and all material to accomplish repair. The work encompasses the disassembly cleaning, inspection, maintenance re-assembly, testing, and finishing actions required to return the item to a serviceable condition in accordance with the solicitation requirements and all attachments. A firm-fixed price Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity type contract is contemplated with a 3-year basic period of performance.
