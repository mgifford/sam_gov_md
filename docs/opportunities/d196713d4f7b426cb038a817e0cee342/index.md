# CONSOLIDATION OF CONTRACT REQUIREMENTS DETERMINATION FOR Contractor Logistics Support (CLS) for the JPM CBRN Integrated Early Warning (IEW) Defense CBRN Response Force (DCRF) and Command & Control CBRN Response Element (C2CRE-B) Missions

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Consolidate/(Substantially) Bundle
- Posted: 2026-05-20 15:07:38.73-04
- Solicitation Number: W911SR-27-R-CREB

## Summary

Notice of Intent to Consolidate Requirements for Contractor Logistics Support (CLS): Defense Chemical Biological Radiological Nuclear (CBRN) Response Force (DCRF) and Command & Control Chemical Biological Radiological Nuclear (CBRN) Response Element (C2CRE-B) Missions This notice is published in accordance with Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) FAR 7.107-3(b)(1). The Senior Contracting Official has determined that the consolidation of the DCRF and C2CRE-B logistics requirements is necessary and justified. The redacted determination is attached. The formal solicitation will be issued to Joint Enterprise Contracted Logistics and Services Support II (JE-CLaSS II) Multiagency Contract (MAC) Indefinite-Delivery Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) holders no earlier than 7 days from the date of this notice.
