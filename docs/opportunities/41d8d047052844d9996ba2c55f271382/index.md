## Summary

THERE IS NO SOLICITATION AVAILABLE AT THIS TIME. REQUESTS FOR A SOLICITATION WILL NOT RECEIVE A RESPONSE. THIS SOURCES SOUGHT SERVES AS THE OFFICIAL PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE IN ACCORDANCE WITH RFO 12.201-1(c)(2) AND RFO 5.101(a) The 673d Contracting Squadron (673 CONS), Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson (JBER), Alaska, is conducting market research to identify potential sources that can supply public address systems. 1.
