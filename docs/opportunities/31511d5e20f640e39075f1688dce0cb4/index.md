# Notice of Intent to Lease Space Using Other Than Full and Open Competition, On-Airport, Mobile, Alabama

- Agency: GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 13:46:25.064-04
- Solicitation Number: 5AL0237

## Summary

The U.S. Government intends to award a superseding lease using other than full and open competition for an existing lease for the Transportation Security Administration in Mobile, Alabama at Brookley Field Mobile (BFM). The Government requires office and related space yielding a minimum of 2,739.00 ANSI/BOMA office area and seven (7) adjacent parking spaces. The space must be located on airport property, be contiguous, and all services, supplies, utilities, partitioning, and tenant alterations must be included as part of the rental consideration. Minimum Sq. Ft. (ABOA): 2,739 Maximum Sq. Ft. (ABOA): 3,027 To submit a location for inspection, contact this office by 6/17/2026 at 11:59 PM CST. (Submissions by other than the owner or manager of a property must be accompanied by a letter from the owner granting permission to make a general offering of the space). An Expression of Interest (EOI) shall include an estimated Shell Rent and Operating Rent rate per ABOA. Interested parties should send expressions of interest to: Sean Munger Lease Contract Officer 857-276-9603 sean.munger@gsa.gov
