# 19SG2026Q0010-Supply of Playground Equipment for U.S Embassy Senegal

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 04:19:27.89-04
- Solicitation Number: 19SG2026Q0010

## Summary

Enclosed is a Request for Quotations (RFQ) issued by the U.S. Embassy in Senegal for the procurement and delivery of a commercial-grade playground equipment. Eligibility is restricted to specialized firms possessing valid operating permits in this sector. If your company wishes to compete for this contract, please execute the required portions of the attached documentation in accordance with Section 3 instructions, and deliver your submission to the address designated on the attached Standard Form 1449. The U.S. Government intends to award a contract/purchase order to the responsible company submitting an acceptable quotation at the lowest price. We intend to award a contract/purchase order based on initial quotations, without holding discussions, although we may hold discussions with companies in the competitive range if there is a need to do so. Quotations are due by June 29, 2026. not later than 12:00 noon (local time). No quotations will be accepted after this time. Your quotation (one technical and one financial) shall be submitted electronically to dakarsolicitation@state.gov In order for a quotation to be considered, you must also complete and submit the following: 1. SF-1449 2. Section I, Pricing 3. Section 5 Representations and Certifications 4. Additional information as required in Section 3 5. Proof of SAM Registration Offerors shall be registered in the SAM (System for Award Management) database at https://www.sam.gov prior to submittal of their offer/proposal as prescribed under FAR 4.1102. Failure to be registered at time of proposal submission may deem the offeror’s proposal to be considered non-responsible and no further consideration will be given. Therefore, offerors are highly encouraged to register immediately if they are interested in submitting a response to this requirement. Direct any questions regarding this solicitation in writing to DakarSolicitation@state.gov. Questions must be written in English and sent not later than June 18, 2026
