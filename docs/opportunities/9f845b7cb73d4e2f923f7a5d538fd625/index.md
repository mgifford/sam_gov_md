# NOTICE OF CANCELLATION: DHS S&T OCIO IT SUPPORT SERVICES JOINT INDUSTRY DAY

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-19 11:04:32.848-04
- Solicitation Number: CANCELLATION_DHS_OCIO_JOINT_INDUSTRY_DAY_2026

## Summary

NOTICE OF CANCELLATION: DHS S&T OCIO IT SUPPORT SERVICES JOINT INDUSTRY DAY This notice is issued to inform interested parties that the DHS S&T OCIO IT Support Services Joint Industry Day, previously scheduled under this announcement, has been cancelled. The Government appreciates the interest and participation from industry partners regarding these requirements. At this time, the Government is actively reviewing Request for Information (RFI) responses. An acquisition strategy has not yet been developed. As a result, there are no further updates available regarding acquisition strategy or procurement timeline. This cancellation notice does not constitute a cancellation of any current or future requirements associated with DHS S&T OCIO IT Support Services Joint Industry Day. The Government reserves the right to issue future notices, requests for information (RFIs), solicitations, or other acquisition-related communications as deemed appropriate. All interested parties are encouraged to continue monitoring the DHS Acquisition Planning Forecast System (APFS ) https://apfs-cloud.dhs.gov/ for any future updates related to these requirements. No reimbursement will be made for any costs associated with participating in the planned DHS S&T OCIO IT Support Services Joint Industry Day.
