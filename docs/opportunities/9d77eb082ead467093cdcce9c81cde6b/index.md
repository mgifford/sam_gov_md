## Summary

The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) Palm Springs Field Office has issued a sole source award to Tablet Command, INC, for a mobile application that can display data from the cooperator's dispatch centers. On March 31, 2026, a Justification for Full and Open (JOFOC) was posted to SAM.gov under DOILFBO26001 as a "Special Notice" with a response date of April 14, 2026. As of April 14, 2026, no responses were received, therefore determining there were no other sources that could meet the needs of this requirement. The awarded value is $48,372.50 which includes a base period plus one option period. The base period is from May 1, 2026-April 30, 2027.
