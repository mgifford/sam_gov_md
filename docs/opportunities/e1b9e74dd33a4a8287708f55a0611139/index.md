## Summary

This is a notice to request information from industry to help the U.S. Department of Commerce, National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) determine the availability and capability of small businesses and other than small businesses to provide labor, supervision, tools, material, and equipment for preventative maintenance and emergency services for the Continuum and I/NET building automation systems specified at the NIST Gaithersburg, MD campus. See attached Sources Sought Notice and Statement of Work (SOW) for details.
