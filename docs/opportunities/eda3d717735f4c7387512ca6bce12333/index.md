## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1202SC26Q2704 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Fly-Tent Sunscreens. Fly sunscreen 20’ x 20’; 80% black sunscreen shade cloth mesh fabric with #5.5 grommets spaced every 2 feet on all 4 sides, with 10 guy ropes per sunscreen and 1 dowel with 2 holes per rope. Neocord 25’ long, ¼” thick cord. Dowel 1” round, 4” long with two holes 7/6” wide and 2” apart. Packaged in 23”x19”x10” 51ect, regular slotted RSC carton. Provide a brief description of the requirement. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 314910 The small business size standard is 500. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
