# BDOC Upgrade for Yokota Air Base, Japan

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 01:19:09.32-04
- Solicitation Number: FA520926Q0041

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, tools, vehicle, materials, supervision, and other items and services necessary except that specifically identified as Government Furnished Property or Government Furnished Equipment for the BDOC Upgrade at Yokota Air Force Base, Japan. The scope of the requirement is as folllows. Proccurement of the items listed in the attached item list Removal of existing equipment Installation of the new equipment User training for the equipment. Note 1: This project will be performed in its entirety in the country of Japan. The successful offeror must able to perform work in Japan. Note 2: Funds are not presently available for this effort. No award will be made under this solicitation until funds are available. Note 3: Any interested vendor shall send an email to the POC's listed below under Contact information and to clifford.cruz.1@us.af.mil to express their interest in the requirement.
