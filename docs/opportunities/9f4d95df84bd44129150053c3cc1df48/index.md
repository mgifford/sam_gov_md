# Request for Industry Feedback - Construction Manager as Advisor (CMa) services, NAVFAC Washington

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-08 13:32:19.336-04
- Solicitation Number: RFI_NAVFACWASHCMASERVICES

## Summary

Amendment 01: Please utilize the attached "CMa Submission Form" to submit responses. Supporting documentation to questions, comments, and/or areas of concerns is allowed. NAVFAC Washington is seeking constructive feedback from Industry on the attached draft Statement of Work, draft evaluation criteria, and suggested pricing structure for a potential future opportunity related to Construction Manager as Advisor (CMa) services for the NAVFAC Washington. Feedback can be questions, comments, areas of concern, or similar. This Request for Feedback is for informational purposes only and does not constitute a solicitation or commitment by NAVFAC Washington. NAVFAC Washington does not intend to award a contract on the basis of this request or otherwise pay for the information solicited. Participation in this effort is strictly voluntary. Please identify any proprietary information submitted. Any of the information stated above is subject to change. If a formal RFP is generated at a later date, NAVFAC Washington will publish a solicitation notice. Responses and/or questions shall be submitted via email to Lindsay Brown at Lindsay.m.brown14.civ@us.navy.mil.
