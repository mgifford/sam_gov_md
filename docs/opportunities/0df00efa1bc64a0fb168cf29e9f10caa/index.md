# Child & Youth Summer Camps

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 11:15:30.218-04
- Solicitation Number: W912K626QA005

## Summary

The Maryland National Guard Child and Youth Program provides team building, resiliency, and leadership opportunity for Military Dependents. The summer camps will facilitate youth leadership training that focuses on leadership training and life-skill building activities that prepare youth to cope with the challenges of being a military youth and reduce risk-taking behaviors. All-inclusive nature/wilderness camp setting within 100 miles radius of 219 29th Division St, Baltimore, MD 21201. The venue must provide meals for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, as well as lodging. Please see attached PWS for more information. Period of Performance: 22-26 Jun 2026 and 03-07 Aug 2026.
