# Sources Sought Notice PLA Survey - O&M with Incidental Repair and Minor Construction, IDIQ MATOC in Support of DHA, East of the Mississippi Region

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-12 11:30:26.727-04

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Mobile District, is soliciting comments from the construction community addressing the potential use of a Project Labor Agreement (PLA) for a large scale O&M with Repair and Minor Construction incidental to the O&M mission project. The aforementioned is the seed project for the proposed IDIQ MATOC for the Maryland Region (W9127825RA077).
