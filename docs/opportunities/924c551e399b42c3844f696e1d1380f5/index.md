# Sole Source Justification - Highly Specialized Strategic Budgeting and Congressional Liaison Support Services

- Agency: ENERGY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-08 17:21:06.662-04
- Solicitation Number: 2026-DOE-AMP-0018-SSJ

## Summary

This posting includes a Sole Source Justification for the award of a non-competitive, time and materials contract to Mercia Advisors, LLC. The services required encompass expert-level support for budget formulation, financial planning, strategic planning, policy analysis, and critical Congressional liaison functions for HFO. This sole source acquisition is made under the authority of FAR 6.302-1 ("Only one responsible source") due to Mercia Advisors, LLC's unique qualifications, including established relationships with Congressional committees, interagency coordination expertise, and unparalleled institutional knowledge of HFO's mission and funding. A notice of intent was posted, and market research confirmed no other sources could meet these specific requirements without unacceptable delays or mission risk. The complete Sole Source Justification outlining this rationale is attached.
