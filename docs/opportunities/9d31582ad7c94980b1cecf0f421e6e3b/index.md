# Misawa AB Playground Equipment Removal and Installation

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-28 02:15:00.934-04
- Solicitation Number: FA520526QB062

## Summary

The United States Air Force, 35th Contracting Squadron, Misawa AFB, Japan is seeking potential sources for the removal of existing playground equipment and the purchase and installation of new playground equipment in an indoor space. This is a commodity purchase. The North American Standard Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code anticipated for this acquisition is 423910 and the Product Service Code (PSC) is 7830. Interested vendors please reach out to the listed POCs below with your capability statement to be added to the intersted parties list. 1. Location: Multi-purpose facility on Misawa AB, Japan. 2. Playground must meet NPPS or equivalent and japanes safety standards. 3. This requirement will include the design of the layout of the playground bases on drawings to be proivded. Responses to this notice are not considered offers and cannot be accepted by the Government to form a binding contract. No solicitation exists; therefore, do not request a copy of the solicitation. The decision to solicit for a contract shall be solely within the Government’s discretion. Any information submitted by offerors to this request for information is voluntary. This request for sources sought is not to be construed as a commitment by the Government, nor will the Government reimburse any costs associated with the submission of information in response to this notice. Direct all questions concerning this posting to the below POCs: TSgt Loraine Ermel, Contracting Specialist, loraine.ermel@us.af.mil 1st Lt Kade Forrester, Contracting Officer, kade.forrester.1@us.af.mil Please reach out tot eh listed POCs to be added to teh interested vendor list.
