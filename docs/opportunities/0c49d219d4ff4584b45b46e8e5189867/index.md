# Collapsible Containers

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-05-01 11:47:37.555-04
- Solicitation Number: H9225726RE013

## Summary

This is a COMBINED SYNOPSIS/SOLICITATION for Collapsible Containers for Marine Forces Special Operations Command (MARFORSOC). This announcement is prepared in accordance with the format in Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Part 12 and 15 as supplemented with additional information in this notice. The solicitation is being issued as a Request for Proposal (RFP); solicitation number is H92257-26-R-E013. All interested parties may submit a proposal which shall be considered by the agency. This is a supply contract. The solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular (FAC) 2026-01, (effective March 13, 2026) and the DFARS Publication Notice (DPN) 20251110. The Product Service Code (PSC) for this requirement is 8145. The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this requirement is 326199. The proposed acquisition is set aside for Small Business. TYPE OF CONTRACT The Government intends to award a single Firm Fixed Price (FFP) type contract as a result of this solicitation. The Government reserves the right and may award based solely on information contained in the proposal. It is the Government’s intention to award without discussions. Offerors are encouraged to present their best technical and price proposal in their initial submission. Award will be made on the basis of the lowest evaluated price of proposals meeting or exceeding the acceptability standards for non-cost factors. The contract shall be awarded on an all or none basis, do not submit partial proposals. All proposals submitted shall be the offeror’s Best Proposal as it is the Government's intent to entertain one (1) final proposal only; this includes any discounts on volume. Offerors must scrutinize this solicitation and ensure their proposal and submission complies with all requirements. It is not sufficient to merely state your proposal complies with the solicitation requirements. You must clearly identify how your proposal meets ALL of the solicitation requirements. See attached RFP H9225726RE013 and Attch1 - Collapsible Containers Salient Characteristics for more information.
