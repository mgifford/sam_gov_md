# CYBER NATIONAL MISSION FORCE (CNMF) CONSTRUCTION

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 16:27:36.633-04
- Solicitation Number: W912DR26RA010

## Summary

The Cyber National Mission Force - Mission Operations Facility (CNMF-MOF) project requirements focus on the design and construction of a State-of-the-Art Mission building within the Central Campus site at Ft. Meade, Maryland. The primary facility is approximately 750,000 gross square feet (GSF) in size and will accommodate approximately 2,400 people. The state-of-the-art facility shall allow for a variety of tenant occupancy functions and flexible size and configurations to conduct the mission, administrative, and support services. The project also includes the expansion of the multi-level East Campus Parking Structure 4 (ECPS 4) for 1,758 spaces of structured parking. The overall CNMF-MOF parking requirement is 85% of the MOF projected seat count and may be accommodated through the expansion of ECPS 4 and surface parking. All required utilities, connections, and secure telecommunication distribution systems shall be fully integrated into the facilities. The project also includes but is not limited to a pedestrian bridge connecting the CNMF-MOF to the East Campus Building 5 (ECB 5); relocation of the Construction Surveillance Technician (CST) trailers; on-site/off-site roadway improvements; storm water management facilities; site restorations, reforestation; existing on-site utilities improvements; installation of new utilities; demolition of an existing building; and life safety generation.
