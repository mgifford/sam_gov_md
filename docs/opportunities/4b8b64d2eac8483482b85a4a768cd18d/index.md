# Tanium Software Subscription Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-21 07:58:33.342-04
- Solicitation Number: H98210-26-Q-E-001

## Summary

This action is for the award of a new, firm-fixed-price Department of War (DoW) Enterprise Software Initiative (ESI) order to Carahsoft Technology, Corp. for Tanium endpoint security management software subscriptions. This procurement is necessary to ensure the continuity of critical cybersecurity capabilities for the Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) and to comply with a new, mandatory-for-use DoW agreement.
