# Yakutat Bldg 101 Removal Action

- Agency: TRANSPORTATION, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 16:00:49.5-04
- Solicitation Number: 697DCK-26-R-00272

## Summary

The FAA has a requirement for a Removal Action (RA) at Bldg 101 in Yakutat, Alaska to remove petroleum contaminated soils with contaminant concentrations that exceed applicable cleanup criteria. - This work is to excavate and dispose of up to 800 tons of petroleum impacted soil near the Yakutat Airport. - Petroleum impacted soils are predominantly present 4-8 feet below ground surface (bgs) but extend to the surface and down to 10’ bgs in some locations. Multiple utilities including telephone and power run along the excavation area margins, fiber optic runs through the norther portion of the excavation area, and multiple additional utilizes are located in the general site vicinity. - Groundwater is present at ~5-6’ bgs the site so the contractor must manage soil moisture to prevent pooling water in waste disposal vessels. - The site is partially covered by a ~6500 square foot laydown/pad area that must be excavated through to remove the petroleum soils and it must be put back during site restoration. Multiple semi-mobile trailers with temporary utility hookups are located on the onsite pad and must be removed during work and replaced after completion. This work must be conducted in July/August of 2026 (during seasonal low water levels).
