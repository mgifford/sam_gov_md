# Dover AFB - Aircraft Prep and Paint (KC-10, C-133, C-124, C-45)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-28 17:46:58.369-04
- Solicitation Number: FA449726Q0032

## Summary

The Air Mobility Command (AMC) Museum at Dover Air Force Base (AFB), DE requires corrosion control, surface preparation, and historically accurate exterior painting services for four static display artifact aircraft, including the KC-10A, C-133B, C-124A, and C-45G. Site Visit: Offerors are highly encouraged to attend a site visit to inspect the current condition of the four (4) aircraft and the worksite on the AMC Museum ramp. This will be the only opportunity provided for a site visit. a. Date and Time: 5 May 2026 at 9:00 AM EDT. b. Location: Attendees shall meet at the main entrance of the Air Mobility Command (AMC) Museum, Dover AFB, DE. c. RSVP Requirement: All interested attendees must RSVP via email to 2Lt Jonathan Goodwin (jonathan.goodwin@us.af.mil) & 2Lt Travis Ambrose (travis.ambrose.1@us.af.mil) no later than 4 May 2026 at 1:00 PM EDT. The email must include the full name of each attendee to coordinate base access. Attendees who do not RSVP may be denied entry. d. Disclaimer: Information provided during the site visit is for clarification purposes only and shall not be considered binding. Any changes to the solicitation requirements will be made only by a formal written amendment to the RFQ.
