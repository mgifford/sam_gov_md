# Hazardous Rated LED Fixtures

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-22 14:39:02.026-04
- Solicitation Number: 20260003810

## Summary

SOURCES SOUGHT SYNOPSIS ANNOUNCEMENT Navy Supply Command Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound (NAVSUP FLCPS) is posting a sources sought synopsis as a means of conducting market research to identify parties having an interest and resources to support the ships ammunition cargo holds for Hazardous Rated LED Fixtures for installation in the Ammunition Cargo Holds for the USNS WASHINGTON CHAMBERS – MILITARY SEALIFT COMMAND. The applicable North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code assigned to this procurement is 335132 with a small business size standard of 600 employees. The results of the market research will contribute to future procurement process decisions. No feedback or evaluations will be provided regarding any submissions on the sources sought. Please provide responses NLT 29 May 2026 at 12:00 PM EDT. The results of the market research will contribute to the determination that only one or more multiple responsible sources exist that have the required capabilities and interest. DESCRIPTION OF SUPPLIES: The Sources Sought for the AMMUNITION CARGO HOLD HAZARDOUS RATED LED FIXTURES are for the following items which MUST meet the following ratings and requirements: White LED 120-277 VAC, 50/60 Hz input voltage and 66-watt max Certified for IP65, ABS, UL 844, and UL 1598A Size no bigger than 30.5” x 7.7” x 4.9” White LED 347-480 VAC, 50/60 Hz input voltage and 66-watt max Certified for IP65, ABS, UL 844, and UL 1598A Size no bigger than 30.5” x 7.7” x 4.9” White LED with ability to switch to Red and Amber 120-277 VAC, 50/60 Hz input voltage and 66-watt max Certified for IP65, ABS, UL 844, and UL 1598A Size no bigger than 30.5” x 7.7” x 4.9” White LED with ability to switch to Red and Amber 347-480 VAC, 50/60 Hz input voltage and 66-watt max Certified for IP65, ABS, UL 844, and UL 1598A Size no bigger than 30.5” x 7.7” x 4.9” All responses to this notice should be emailed to: Heidi Brennan, heidi.r.brennan.civ@us.navy.mil. The following should be included in the response: Organization name, address, Cage Code, email address, website, telephone number, large or small business size status under NAICS Code 335132, and Tailored capability statement addressing the particulars of this effort, with appropriate documentation supporting claims of capability, including place of manufacture. Provide the details of any sales for similar supplies your company has made to the Government of any civilian agencies in the past. ***THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR QUOTE***. This request for sources sought does not constitute a request for quote; submission of any information in response to this market survey is purely voluntary and the Government assumes no financial responsibility for any costs incurred.
