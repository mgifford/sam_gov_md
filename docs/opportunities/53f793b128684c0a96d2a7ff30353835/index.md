Instructions to Offerers: • All offerers shall submit a revised quote that explicitly acknowledges and reflects the updated period of performance. • Revised quotes must be submitted electronically no later than the new closing date and lime specified in block 8 of the SF 1449. • The complete quote must be sent via email to all Points of Contact listed below. The 18th Space Defense Squadron (18 SDS) and U.S. Space Forces – Space (S4S) require an unfiltered network access point to access resources provided by commercial partners that are otherwise inaccessible through standard Department of War (DoW) networks. This CIS capability allows administrators to download patches for locally managed network switches and gives space operators access to commercial resources in support of Space Domain Awareness. Locations of Performance The contractor shall provide the required services to the demarcation points at the following two locations: • Building 7000: S4S, 816 13th Street, Vandenberg SFB, CA 93437 • Building 8401: 18 SDS, 1521 Utah Ave, Vandenberg SFB, CA 93437 Posted Date: April 09, 2026 Response Date: April 20, 2026, 3:00 PM PDT Classification Code: DG11 – IT AND TELECOM INTERNET NAICS Code: 517111 – Wired Telecommunications Carriers Points of Contact: Contracting Specialist: Jeff Weeks, jeffrey.weeks.5@spaceforce.mil Contracting Officer: Scott Yeaple, Scott.yeaple@spaceforce.mil
