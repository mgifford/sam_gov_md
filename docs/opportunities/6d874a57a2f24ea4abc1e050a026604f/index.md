# USMC Live Fire Evaluation Tool (LFET) Commercial Solutions Opening

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-12 17:23:53.883-04
- Solicitation Number: M67854-26-I-8050

## Summary

Program Management Training Systems (PM TRASYS) intends to issue a Commercial Solutions Opening (CSO) to solicit innovative commercial products and technologies in support of the Live-Fire Evaluation Tool (LFET) training solution. The goal of the CSO is to identify a commercial solution that enhances Marine lethality via improving weapons proficiency and skill development by providing real time live fire shot detection and scoring capability. Any contract resulting from the CSO will be a hybrid contract consisting of fixed-price Contract Line Numbers (CLINs) for systems and contractor logistics support and cost CLINs for Travel and Other Direct Costs to provide LFET to the Marine Corps. At this juncture, the Government estimates that the resultant contract will have a total potential period of performance of five years. The Government anticipates awarding a single contract resulting from the CSO. REQUEST FOR INFORMATION: The Government is interested in receiving your insight on the draft CSO (attached) hereby. Interested offerors should submit a concise response (maximum 2-page white paper or 5 slides) within 10 calendar days of this draft posting addressing the following 3 questions ONLY: Question 1: If this is posted as an actual CSO, will you plan to respond to the CSO and compete for the requirement? If so, please provide your business name, size, and point of contact. Question 2: What updates do you recommend to the draft CSO to assist you with the decision to respond and compete for the contract award? Question 3: Will this requirement enable you to provide an innovative solution? If not, what changes would you recommend ensuring an innovative solution is provided? Submit RFI responses via e-mail no later than the closing date (See Response Date) of this RFI to the following POCs: Anthony Genao, Contracting and Agreements Officer, PM TRASYS (COM: 571-564-1093, anthony.genao@usmc.mil) JoAnn Wesley, Contract Specialist, PM TRASYS (COM: 407-380-8491, joann.o.wesley@usmc.mil) DISCLAIMER: THIS REQUEST FOR INFORMATION (RFI) IS ISSUED SOLELY FOR INFORMATION AND PLANNING PURPOSES ONLY. THE GOVERNMENT WILL USE THE INFORMATION RECEIVED TO DETERMINE ITS ACQUISITION STRATEGY. THE INFORMATION PROVIDED IN THIS NOTICE IS SUBJECT TO CHANGE AND IS NOT BINDING ON THE GOVERNMENT. THE U.S. GOVERNMENT WILL NOT PAY FOR INFORMATION REQUESTED NOR WILL IT COMPENSATE ANY RESPONDENT FOR ANY COST INCURRED IN DEVELOPING INFORMATION PROVIDED TO THE UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT.
