Potential Offerors must have current online Representations and Certification application on file with SAM.gov. *Potential Offerors must also be verified in the SBA Veteran Small Business Certification Program as an SDVOSB to be eligible for award. The Contracting Officer (CO) will use the SBA s Small Business Search (SBA DSBS) (https://search.certifications.sba.gov/) to verify SDVOSB status until further guidance is provided by the Department of Veterans Affairs. The applicable North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code is 236220 Commercial and Institutional Building Construction, and the small business size standard is $45.0 Million. PSC Code is Z2DB Repair and Alteration of Laboratories and Clinics. In accordance with VAAR 836.204, the disclosure of the magnitude of this construction project is anticipated to be between $5,000,000.00 and $10,000,000.00. Questions regarding this pre-solicitation notice must be sent in writing to Mr. Rosario Chaidez, Contracting Officer, NCO 21 at email: Rosario.Chaidez1@va.gov The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Network Contracting Office (NCO) 21 will be issuing a Request for Proposal (RFP) for a general construction contractor for the project titled: Renovate Imaging and Catheterization Lab, 570-21-220, Fresno VA Medical Center identified in the Statement of Work (SOW) located at: 2615 East Clinton Avenue, Fresno, CA, 93703-2223 to be attached at a future date. Work includes, but is not limited to the following: Vendors must be able to provide all materials, labor, tools, equipment, transportation, supervision, project management, administrative support, and specialized expertise to perform the work stipulated in the Statement of Work and Construction Drawings what will be posted with the solicitation at a future date. The project is to focus on the renovation of the 10,200 square feet Catheterization Lab located on the second floor of the East Wing for Veterans Hospital. This will involve the relocation of the existing ultrasound and fluoroscopy equipment to the renovated imaging suite. The major remodeling will include moving the radiology rooms (3), ultrasound rooms (2), patient restroom, central staff work area, communications closet, and electrical room, as well as building a new ultrasound room. The scope of work includes complete Architectural, Structural, Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing provided for the new and renovated areas. This includes, but is not limited to, new finishes, removal and installation of walls, floors, and ceilings, lighting, power, HVAC, and plumbing etc. Vendors must demonstrate the ability to meet all requirements. The complete SOW, Specifications, Drawings, and any attachments, including all details, will be published when the Solicitation is posted online to SAM.gov. The estimated period of performance (PoP) for this requirement is 730 calendar days from the Notice to Proceed (NTP). This is a pre-solicitation announcement only. Full Solicitation documents will be available on or about May 20, 2026. The full solicitation will contain all necessary proposal instructions.
