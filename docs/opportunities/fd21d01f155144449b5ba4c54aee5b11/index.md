## Summary

SPE7LX26RX015 NAICS: 333618 - Other Engine Equipment Manufacturing Mfg., 1,500 Employees FSC: 2815-DIESEL ENGINES AND COMPONENTS Response Date: 6/17/2026 Title: FLYWHEEL NSN(s) 2815-01-148-3771 is being solicited. It is procured in accordance with approved manufacturer CAGE(s) and part number(s). The solicitation is for an Indefinite Delivery Contract (IDC) and the FOB point is Origin subject to DLA’s First Destination Transportation initiative. The proposed procurement contains an option to extend the contract period one or more times at the discretion of the government. The total shall not exceed 5 years. A written offer is required to be returned as instructed in the solicitation by the date and time specified. DIBBS quotes are not acceptable; but a PDF copy of your proposal/quote may be uploaded to DIBBS. FEDERAL, MILITARY AND COMMERCIAL SPECIFICATIONS CANNOT BE PROVIDED BY DLA WEAPONS SUPPORT (COLUMBUS). While price may be a significant factor in the evaluation of offers, the final contract award decision will be based upon a combination of factors as described in the solicitation.
