## Summary

May 15, 2026: The registration form has been updated to include the ability to sign up for a one-on-one session with ACC-RI and JMTC leadership and indicate interest for a future site tour. If you are interested in a one-on-one or a tour, please resubmit the form. The form is located at https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/ZB1e7Pj0wj Draft Agenda (subject to change) 0900-0930: Registration 0930-1000: Opening Comments 1000-1030: Public Private Partnerships (P3) Overview 1030-1045: Break 1045-1200: JMTC Capabilities and Requirements Briefing RIA-JMTC Background Steel Forecast Aluminum Forecast Equipment and Automation Public Private Partnerships External Support Informational Resources 1200-1300: Lunch Break 1300-1630: Networking in Heritage Hall 1300-1630: 30 minute one-on-one discussions ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- May 6, 2026: Vendors can register for this Industry Day at https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/ZB1e7Pj0wj. Optional questions are included to help shape the topics of the industry day. Registrations are due Tuesday, May 26, 2026. All visitors without a Common Access Card (CAC) will be required to register at the Rock Island Arsenal Visitor Center and have an appropriate form of identification. Please see https://home.army.mil/ria/about/visitor-information for details on acceptable identification. The access form has been attached to this posting. It is HIGHLY recommended that individuals attending complete the form and bring it with them to expedite access for themselves and all attendees. Further details and an agenda will be forthcoming. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Army Contracting Command - Rock Island (ACC-RI), on behalf of Rock Island Arsenal Joint Manufacturing and Technology Technology Center plans to hold an Industry Day on June, 4th 2026. It is tentatively scheduled to be held at Rock Island Arsenal. This posting is to notify Industry of the Government's intent to hold the Industry Day to assist with planning and travel arrangements. Registration information and agenda will be forthcoming in a subsequent posting.
