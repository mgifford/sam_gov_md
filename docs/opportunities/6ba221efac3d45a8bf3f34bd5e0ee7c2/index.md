# Bear Creek Service & Emergency Gates Replacement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 14:11:58.06-04
- Solicitation Number: W9128F26RA079

## Summary

19 May 2026 - Updated with Site Visit Sheet ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The work includes fabricating and installing new service gates and emergency gates to fit current gate slots at Bear Creek Dam, Lakewood, Colorado. The gate slots must be measured for accuracy and the existing drawings shall be used for information only. The inlet tunnel liner, gate slots, and marine door will be repainted. The work related to the main hydraulic gate hoist system will be limited to the replacement of bonnet gaskets and O-ring seals between the gate stem and the new gate. The hydraulic oil piping for the low-flow gate, between the new cylinder and the flexible hoses on the hoist deck, will also be replaced. The low-flow gate cylinders will be redesigned, fabricated, shop-tested, and installed to operate the low-flow gates on the new service gates. All incidental related work is also included in this scope. Mechanical scope of work is illustrated on drawing sheets M-001 thru M-105 and detailed requirements are specified in specification sections 05 56 09.00 26 GATE SEALS and 35 05 40.14 10 HYDRAULIC HOISTING SYSTEMS FOR CIVIL WORKS STRUCTURES.
