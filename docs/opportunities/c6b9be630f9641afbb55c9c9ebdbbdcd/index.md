## Summary

The Great Lakes Acquisition Center (GLAC) Milwaukee, WI is soliciting businesses for services to provide DirecTV Satellite TV Services at Edward Hines, Jr VA Hospital in Hines, IL. The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to provide DirecTV Satellite TV Services.
