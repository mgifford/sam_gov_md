# OEC Mailroom Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 16:57:18.399-04
- Solicitation Number: W9115126QA034

## Summary

This requirement is for non-personal, commercial services to provide continuous and uninterrupted mailroom and production support for the Operational Evaluation Command (OEC) at Fort Hood, TX. The core need is for two (2) full-time, security-cleared personnel to manage all incoming/outgoing mail, operate reproduction equipment for both classified and unclassified documents, and handle secure message traffic. The period of performance is a 12-month base year with four (4) one-year option periods. These services are essential for the daily administrative and operational functions of the OEC. The work will be performed on-site at Government facilities.
