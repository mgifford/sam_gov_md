# Shuttle Bus Rentals

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 13:44:51.58-04
- Solicitation Number: FA302226Q0006

## Summary

Columbus AFB has a requirement to provide bus transportation and drivers for the America 250th Freedom Fest for the period of 4 July 2026. The contractor will provide all materials, equipment, management, coordination, labor, and transportation required to shuttle visitors from the pick up point to the drop off point. All questions will be due 11 May 2026, by 12:00P.M. CST. Note: An updated version of the Statement of Work has been placed in the Attachments.
