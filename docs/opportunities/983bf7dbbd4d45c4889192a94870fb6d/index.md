# Uninterrupted Power Supply Replacements at Morristown (MRX), Nashville (OHX), and Atlanta, GA (FFC)

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 18:51:36.118-04
- Solicitation Number: 1305M326Q0029

## Summary

This requirement is for the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), National Weather Service (NWS) Southern Region (SR), Weather Forecast Offices (WFO) at the Morristown (MRX), Nashville (OHX) and Atlanta (FFC) locations. The purpose of this requirement is to supply and install the Eaton 93PM UPS system, with lithium battery backup for 15 minutes, at each of the three WFO locations as well as provide the removal and disposal of the existing UPS’s, battery cabinet with batteries and any associated debris.
