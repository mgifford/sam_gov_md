# Short Term Office Rental for Southern MS

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 09:32:23.3-04
- Solicitation Number: 12RADAQ0004

## Summary

Description: Short-term Office Rental in the D’Iberville or Biloxi, Mississippi. ? Boundaries for location: within D’Iberville city limits or Biloxi city limits, excluding Biloxi peninsula of the gulf coast. ? Office space: Minimum of 1,600 square feet to accommodate 8 federal employees. The space must be move-in ready with no tenant improvements required prior to occupancy. ? Rental shall be full service to include all utilities, janitorial services, triple net costs, etc. included in the monthly rental rate. ? The office space shall meet the minimum requirements identified in the International Building Code for office space occupancies (HVAC, Flooring, wall, ceiling finishes, restrooms). ? The office space shall be ADA compliant and shall provide easy access to the public. ? The office space shall provide for sole access and control by the USDA Rural Development. ? Adequate security - exterior doors must lock. ? Parking for 5 visitors and 3 government vehicles. ? Available for a base year of 12 months with 2 possible option year periods of 12 months each. ? Desired occupancy to begin as soon as possible upon award. ? The office space shall have access to telephone and internet service. ? The office shall include at least 3 private offices. ? The office shall include a Conference Room that can host minimum of 15 people.
