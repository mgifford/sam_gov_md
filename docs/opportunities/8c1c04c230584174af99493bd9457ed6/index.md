## Summary

The Recreation One Stop Program (R1S) is issuing this RFI as a means of gathering industry’s feedback on draft documents of a potential procurement for the next Recreation One Stop Support Services procurement. The documents provided are key components, but not a complete package of all documents that will be provided should a Request for Proposals (RFP) be issued. The current contract ends September 30, 2028. The Forest Service previously conducted market research in 2024. This RFI is intended to provide the Government with feedback on requirements that are in draft and may change based on industry’s feedback along with continued Government requirements development. 05/20/2026 - Vendor Question & Answer Document added
