## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Subpart 12.6 as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested and a written solicitation will not be issued. The solicitation number is W50S77-26-Q-0004. This solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular (FAC) 2026-01 dated March 13, 2026, and DFARS Defense Publication Notice Current to DPN20251011. The 147 Attack Wing Base Contracting Office intends to award a Firm-Fixed-Price Contract for Snap-On toolkits. The North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 332216 and the small business size standard is 750EMP. This requirement does not have a Small Business set-aside and is full and open competition. See attached documents for full details.
