# Streambank Stabilization Project – Little Wheeling Creek Site – EWP 2025 Ohio County, West Virginia

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 11:45:48.218-04
- Solicitation Number: 12FPC326B0005

## Summary

NRCS is in need of construction services (not considered a commercial service) for erosion control in Ohio County, West Virginia. The erosion control measures will consist of laying wire mesh gabions, preparing foundation on shallow bedrock; rebar anchoring and riprap foundation protection. Details provided in Solicitation attachments. Site Showing Details: Site Showing will be held in Ohio County, West Virginia on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, at 10:00am. Interested bidders will meet at; Eagles Den 9647 National Road, Valley Grove, WV 26060 We will also drive to the work site to give the prospective bidders a chance to see it and ask questions.
