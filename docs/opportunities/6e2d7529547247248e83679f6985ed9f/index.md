# LES Health Insurance, U.S. Embassy Ashgabat, Turkmenistan

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF

- Posted: 2026-05-21 06:15:51.231-04

## Summary

The Regional Procurement Support Office (RPSO) Frankfurt hereby issues this Combined Synopsis/Solicitation for the establishment of a single-award firm-fixed-price Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) for the provision of health insurance coverage for Locally Employed Staff (LES) and their eligible dependents at U.S. Embassy Ashgabat, Turkmenistan. All interested and responsible offerors are invited to submit offers in strict accordance with the instructions, terms, and conditions contained in the attached solicitation documents. IMPORTANT: Offerors must ensure full compliance with all stated requirements, coverage specifications, and submission guidelines. Questions and offers must be received by the specified due date and time indicated in the solicitation. Late submissions will not be considered. Failure to comply with solicitation requirements may result in offer rejection. For questions or clarifications, please follow the procedures outlined in the solicitation documents.
