# Hardware and software maintenance Image Trac 6400 Scanner 85880

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-01 14:31:04.451-04
- Solicitation Number: May-1-MW-008

## Summary

This requirement is to provide full-service hardware and software support and maintenance for the ImageTrac 6400 scanner 85880 located in room 1R115 of the Census Bureau Headquarters in Suitland, MD. This renewal provides required hardware maintenance and software support on scanning equipment used to reduce wasteful paper production processes and automate survey processing methods saving money by decreasing manpower and resources necessary to complete scheduled milestones and without impeding the Agency's mission.
