## Summary

DISCLAIMER This RFI is issued solely for information and planning purposes only and does not constitute a solicitation. All information received in response to this RFI that is marked as proprietary will be handled accordingly. In accordance with FAR 15.201(e), responses to this notice are not offers and cannot be accepted by the Government to form a binding contract. Responders are solely responsible for all expenses associated with responding to this RFI. INDUSTRY DAY VETERAN INTEGRATED SERVICE NETWORK 23 and NETWORK CONTRATCING OFFICE 23 are hosting an Industry Day for authorized distributors and manufacturers of GASTROSCOPES and COLONOSCOPE Systems. Please join the Department of Veterans Affairs, Veteran Integrated Service Network 23 (VISN 23) for an onsite Industry Day as it relates to the Iowa City VA Medical Center s need for gastroscope and colonoscope systems. This invitation is open to authorized distributors and scope manufacturers who believe they might have the capability to provide the necessary instrumentation to the Iowa City VA Medical Center. The purpose of this industry day is to conduct market research as well as to survey the market to ascertain whether-or-not there are other sources willing and capable of providing the needed supplies and services. This Industry Day will also allow potential sources to submit a non-binding statement of interest and possible documentation demonstrating their capabilities. The interested parties will visit the medical center during their scheduled time to demonstrate the capabilities of their products.
