## Summary

This is one of ten (10) Design-Build Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contracts as part of a strategic sourcing vehicle for construction services with a $10 billion ceiling, awarded to ten qualified vendors. This multiple award IDIQ contract structure enables efficient procurement of design-build services for multiple projects. Each individual project will be competed among the ten IDIQ holders, ensuring a fair and transparent award process, with each project ultimately awarded to one of the ten vendors to design and construct CBP facilities, including checkpoints, sector headquarters, border patrol stations, and air facilities throughout the Unites States.
