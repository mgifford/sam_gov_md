## Summary

C-5 Hydraulic Valve Repair The Air Force has a requirement for depot-level repair for various C-5 mechanical components. The three items listed below are to be included in this effort: 60 GPM Hydraulic Relief Cartridge Valve - NSN: 1650001059655 PN: 1636 Temperature Compensated Pressure Regulator - NSN: 1650002418862 PN: 854550-5-1 20 GPM Hydraulic Dual Shuttle Valve - NSN: 4820001614098 PN: 5046-1 This acquisition will be a 100% Small Business Set Aside. This repair effort will utilize a Requirements Type Contract. The Contract period of performance will be five (5) years. It will consist of a one (1) year basic period and four (4) one (1) year ordering periods. The Best Estimated Quantities for each ordering period are listed below.
