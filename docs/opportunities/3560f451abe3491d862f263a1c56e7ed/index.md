# Motorola Radio Infrastructure and hand- held/mobile radio maintenance and support services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-26 15:36:33.511-04
- Solicitation Number: SP4701-26-Q-MOTR

## Summary

This RFI is issued seeking small business resellers capable of providing Motorolsa radio Infrastructure and hand-held/mobile radio maintenance and support services. DLA operates a variety of radio communication systems and equipment at several DLA locations throughout the world, such as ASTRO25 Trunked Systems. Given the criticality of these communications systems to daily operational functions, DLA must ensure that all trunked radio equipment is functioning at all times. The primary means of communications for DLA Emergency Services is the Trunked Radio System. This Statement of Work identifies the Government’s requirements to ensure continuous operational efficiency related to the DLA Enterprise Radio communications infrastructure. The objective is to maintain an Enterprise Trunked Radio System Maintenance contract for DLA radio infrastructure, services, and subscriber units. There will be Infrastructure Repair with Life Cycle Support Services to include Technical Support and other services as indicated in the below chart.
