## Summary

This Sources Sought Notice is for market research purposes only and shall not be considered an Invitation for Bids, Request for Quotations, or a Request for Proposal. Do not submit a proposal or quote in response to this notice. The Department of Veterans Affairs is seeking Diagnostic Medical Physicist Equipment Inspection Services in support of Medical Imaging Services for the Tennessee Valley Healthcare System to include the following campus locations: VA Medical Center, Nashville Campus 1310 24th Avenue South Nashville, Tennessee 37212-2637 Clarksville VA Clinic 782 Weatherly Drive Clarksville, TN 37043 Chattanooga VA Clinic 6401 Shallowford Road Chattanooga, TN 37421 Alvin C York VA Medical Center 3400 Lebanon Pike Murfreesboro, TN 37129 The period of performance will tentatively be a five-year Indefinite Delivery Contract with one-year ordering periods. . Interested parties are responsible for adequately marking proprietary, restricted, or otherwise sensitive information contained in their response. Interested business concerns with the capability to provide services can submit a capability statement addressing ability to meet the requirements outlined in the attached Performance Work Statement.
