## Summary

Amendment 1 of Solicitation 12314426Q0118 clarifies that this is a total small business set aside and that NAICS Code is 541611 through updated Standard Form 1449 block 10. Amendment also provides answers to questions posed by potential vendors. See attached Q&A Spreadsheet (RFQ 12314426Q0118 Amd 1 Att 1 Q&A Responses). Closing date and time remains the same. No other changes to soliciation. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12314426Q0118 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Technical Advisory Support Services supporting the transition of Food for Peace to the US Department of Agriculture (USDA). This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is NAICS 541611 The small business size standard is $24.5M This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
