# Cole Ranch Farmhouse Demolition Services

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1240LP26Q0074 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Demolition Services. This is a non-personal services requirement. The U.S. Forest Service requires demolition and removal of three (3) structures located on Forest Service property at Cole Ranch outside Durango, CO. These structures are no longer mission-essential and present safety, security, and environmental concerns. Work includes complete removal of structures and associated appurtenances, disposal of debris, site restoration, and documentation. If interested in doing a site visit please schedule a time with Ryan Tolle for Tuesday, Wednesday or Friday of the week of May 18-22 at ryan.tolle@usda.gov. Offerors or quoters are urged and expected to inspect the site where services are to be performed and to satisfy themselves regarding all general and local conditions that may affect the cost of contract performance, to the extent that the information is reasonably obtainable. In no event shall failure to inspect the site constitute grounds for a claim after contract award. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 238910. The small business size standard is $19 Million. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency. Please refer to the attachments page for RFQ details.
