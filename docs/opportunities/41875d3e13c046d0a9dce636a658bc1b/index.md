The purpose of this contract is to have a vendor to provide Construction Specialties Acrovyn Handrails and End Caps at Greater Los Angeles VA Healthcare System, Los Angeles, CA. The vendor shall provide following requirements: Provide 10,000 linear feet of Acrovyn 4000 Woodgrain/Brushed Metal patterned Handrail Model HRB 4CPN, blunt shaped profile, with 1 1/2" grip surface and 5 1/2" face. Handrail units shall include the Quick Lock mounting system and all components required for complete installation. Color: 1312 Burmese Teak. All handrail covers, recycled bumpers, and aluminum retainers shall be supplied in lengths up to 10 feet maximum. Provide 600 of Acrovyn 4000 Woodgrain/Brushed Metal patterned End Caps for Model HRB 4CPN. Color: 1312 Burmese Teak. If your company is interested and capable of providing the required supplies/services, you MUST provide the information indicated below OR your intent, interest to participation, and information provided will not be considered as valid for research purposes.
