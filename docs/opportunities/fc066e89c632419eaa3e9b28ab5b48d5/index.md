# Turnout Firefighting equipment

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 16:14:07.276-04
- Solicitation Number: W912MM26QA016

## Summary

The South Dakota National Guard has a requirement for Turnout Firefighter protective equipment for both structure and wildland firefighting. This will require the contractor to measure and fit the individual firefighters at the training location in Rapid City, SD. This will be a 100% small business set aside resulting in a firm fixed price contract. The solicitation will be posed on or around May 26th, 2026. More information will be provided with the full solicitation. An active Sam.gov registration is required for this contract.
