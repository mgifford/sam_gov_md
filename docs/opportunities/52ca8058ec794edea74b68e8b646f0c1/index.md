# Mechanic Labor

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-26 12:07:48.83-04
- Solicitation Number: 1301344316

## Summary

SOURCES SOUGHT SYNOPSIS ANNOUNCEMENT Naval Supply Command Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound (NAVSUP FLCPS) is posting a sources sought synopsis as a means of conducting market research to identify parties having an interest and recourse to support NAVSUP FLCPS for MHE/SMSE and pool assets that are maintained on either a scheduled or unscheduled basis. The applicable North American Industry Classification System {NAICS) code assigned to this procurement is 811310 with a small business size standard of 12,500,000.00. The results of the market research will contribute to future procurement process decisions. No feedback or evaluations will be provided regarding any submissions on the sources sought. Please provide responses NLT 29 May 2026 at 3:00 p.m. Pacific Daylight Time. The results of the market research will contribute to the determination that only one or more multiple responsible sources exists that have the required capabilities and interest.
