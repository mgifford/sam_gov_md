## Summary

SPECIAL NOTICE NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD SOLE SOURCE I. The Network Contracting Office (NCO) 16, located at Galleria Financial Center, 5075 Westheimer Rd, Ste 750, Houston, TX 77056 in support of Central Arkansas Veterans Health Care System (CAVHCS), intends to solicit and award Sole Source, Firm-Fixed-Price, Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) with one Large Business source, Quidelortho Sales Company, LLC. Quidelortho Sales Company, LLC 9975 Summers Ridge Rd San Diego, CA 92121-2997 CAVHCS has a requirement for brand name ortho vision analyzer to include reagents, service support and consumables that are already compatible with the government owned ortho workstations immunohematology analyzer. To include current inventory inclusive of cartridges, reagents and controls. Ortho has a six-well gel card that only works with government owned ortho workstations currently in use at the CAVHCS. Quidelortho Sales Company, LLC., is currently the only authorized provider for the analyzers, reagents, controls, consumables and service support for the ortho vision analyzer, ortho workstations and IDS-MATS Gel Card systems. The instrument is designed to automate in vitro immunohematology testing of human blood utilizing ID-Micro Typing System (ID-MTS ) gel card technology. The ortho vision analyzer automates test processing functions including liquid pipetting, reagent handling, incubation, centrifugation, reaction grading and interpretation, and data management requirements using cards and digital image processing. Consumable and reagents include but not limited to: MTS A/B/D MONO & REV GRP 5X20, 100 Cards/PG MTS A/B/D/A/B/D MONO GRP 5X20, 100 Cards/PG MTS AB MONO GROUP 5X20, 100 Cards/PG MTS ANTI-A,B MONO GEL CD 1X20, 20 Cards/PG MTS ANTI-IGG CARD 5X20, 100 Cards/PG 0.8% AFFIRMAGEN 2X10ML 0.8% RESOLVE A 11X3ML 0.8% RESOLVE C 22X3ML 0.8% SURG 3X10ML MTS ROUND DIL2 100ML/5BTLS MTS ROUND DIL2+ 100ML/5BTLS ORTHO 7% BSA OV SINGLE USE DILUTION TRAY OV SINGLE USE EVAPORATION CAPS 250 Ortho Daily QC Simulated Whole Blood Control MTS-ID Pipette Tips-Racked 12X96 Ortho Daily QC Kit Buffered Gel Cards II. The North American Industry Classification (NAICS) code for this acquisition is 325413, In-Vitro Diagnostic Substance Manufacturing. Size Standard 1250. III. This procurement is being conducted under General Services Acquisition Regulation (GSAR) subpart 538.7104-3(b)(ii)-Soliciting from a single source. IV. The anticipated award date is October 1, 2026.
