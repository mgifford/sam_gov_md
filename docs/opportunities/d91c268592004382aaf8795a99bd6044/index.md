# 20-Ton Air Hoists

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 14:19:23.895-04
- Solicitation Number: N0040626Q0242

## Summary

Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound (FLCPS) intends to solicit for 5 each 20-ton air hoists. All responsible sources may submit a quotation, bid, or proposal, as appropriate, which will be considered by the agency. Quotes will be evaluated in accordance with RFO 12.203, the Government will select the most advantageous offer based on the following evaluation factors: technical including delivery date, price, and past performance in no order of relative importance. Anticipated Award Date: June 2026 Contracting Officer: Homer Shoup, homer.o.shoup.civ@us.navy.mil
