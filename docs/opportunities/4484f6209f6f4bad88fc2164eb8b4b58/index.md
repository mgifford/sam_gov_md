# Metal Aircraft Sunshades Maintenance

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-12 16:28:37.784-04
- Solicitation Number: FA480026SSNMSS

## Summary

The 633d Contracting Squadron has a need to perform inspections and maintenance including all repair services to three aircraft metal roof sunshades on Joint Base Langley- Eustis (JBLE), Langley Air Force Base (AFB), Virginia (VA), to include but not limited to: structure LED lights, bird nesting mitigation, lightning protection inspection, obstruction lighting, safety reflective devices, fall protection and any other attached features. The project's goal is to maintain serviceable, safe aircraft shelters. Performance period will consist of one (1) base year 30 September 2026 - 29 September 2027, with four (4) subsequent option years. The Government anticipates making award on or about 30 June May 2026, with the Request for Quote (RFQ) release date on or about 25 May 2026.
