## Summary

NOTE: This will be a Sealed Bidding Solicitation, all responses to this Invitation For Bid (IFB) shall be submitted via FedConnect ONLY. Email proposals WILL NOT be accepted. The Western Area Power Administration (WAPA, Rocky Mountain Region (RMR) has a requirement for a Contractor to provide labor, materials, tools, equipment, transportation, and other resources necessary to complete this project. The Weld Substation Stage 05 Construction Project enhances WAPA’s ability to provide reliable/cost-efficient electrical power by replacing the end of life KV1A transformer and station service. WAPA ordered the new KV1A transformer in 2023 and expects delivery in 2027. WAPA benefits from the station service replacement by resolving deteriorating and potentially unsafe conditions within the existing station service switchgear. The principal components of work include the following: Removal and installation of a 230/115-13.8-KV, 150MVA, transformer KV1A, removal and installation of associated 230/115KV take-off structure, KV1A tertiary station service feed, installation of a new station service, removal of the existing station service, and installation of new KV2A tertiary station service feed, associated foundations, structures, associated electrical equipment, removal of control building single-phase AC panels, installation of control building 3-phase AC panels, and site development for Weld Substation, Stage 05.
