# TRAINING INFRASTRUCTURE SUPPORT

- Agency: TRANSPORTATION, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought

- Solicitation Number: 6973GH-26-R-TRAINING_INFRASTRUCTURE_SUPPORT

## Summary

The Government requires training infrastructure support for the National Airspace System (NAS) Academy at the Mike Monroney Aeronautical Center (MMAC). This Performance Work Statement (PWS) details the requirements for maintaining, repairing, and supporting training systems and equipment used by the FAA Academy (Academy) and its partners. These systems, including Terminal and EnRoute Automation, Radar, Communication, Navigation, and Environmental, are in training labs, classrooms, and other buildings across the MMAC. In specific instances, support may be needed for off-site training within the Oklahoma City area. The scope of this contract covers both existing and newly acquired systems. A DRAFT Performance Work Statement (PWS) is provided as an attachment to this market survey to inform and assist interested vendors of the technical work required. Please see attached Market Survey Notice for details.
