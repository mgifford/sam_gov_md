# Solicitation for HF1 Steel IDIQ

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 18:50:01.193-04
- Solicitation Number: SP800026R0016

## Summary

The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) has a worldwide mission to esnure a continued supply of products to the National Defense Stockpile (NDS). This requirement is for DLA to acquire High Fragmentation 1 (HF-1) Steel for Strategic Materials. This acquisition will be conducted in accordance with FAR Part 12 procedures. DLA intends to solicit and award an Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract for HF-1 Steel with one (1) five (5)-year delivery period. Under the authority of 10 U.S.C. §3204(a)(3)(A) as implemented by the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 6.103-3(b)(1), the requirement for HF-1 Steel is being solicited sole source. See the attached combined Synopsis/Solicitation for more information.
