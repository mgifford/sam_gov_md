# CCURE 9000 Upgrade

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 09:04:38.744-04
- Solicitation Number: W9136426S0025-1

## Summary

THIS REQUEST IS FOR SOURCES SOUGHT AND IS ISSUED SOLELY FOR INFORMATION AND PLANNING PURPOSES ONLY AND DOES NOT CONSTITUTE A SOLICITATION. THE SUBMISSION OF PRICING, CAPABILITIES FOR PLANNING PURPOSES, AND OTHER MARKET INFORMATION IS HIGHLY ENCOURAGED AND ALLOWED UNDER THIS REQUEST IN ACCORDANCE WITH (IAW) FAR 15.201(e). The Ohio Army national Guard located at the Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) requires a comprehensive renovation of the existing access control system located in Building 2 at the DLA facility in Columbus, OH. The purpose of this project is to replace the outdated infrastructure with a modern, secure CCURE 9000 access control system to enhance security, improve operational efficiency, and ensure compliance with current standards. Your response to this Sources Sought, including any capabilities statement, shall be electronically submitted, on or prior to the designated due date, to the Contract Specialist, Brice Royer, in either Microsoft Word or Portable Document Format (PDF), via email brice.a.royer.mil@army.mil & noah.m.beasley.mil@army.mil
