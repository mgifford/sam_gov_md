## Summary

This is a DRAFT RFP for the United States Special Operations Command (USSOCOM)s Preservation of the Force and Families (POTFF) Program Support, third iteration (POTFF III). Please be advised that this posting does not represent the complete final solicitation package; however, it includes all information and documentation available at this time. This notice is for informational and planning purposes only to provide Industry with the most current information and to receive Industry feedback. This announcement does not constitute a formal solicitation, and proposals are not being requested at this time. The Government may issue a final RFP (H9240026RE002) based on the feedback received, which will be conducted using full and open competition.
