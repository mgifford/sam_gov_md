# DPW Airfield Maintenance Tractor

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 08:45:18.582-04
- Solicitation Number: W912CM26QA017

## Summary

The Regional Contracting Office - Wiesbaden (RCO-W) intends to solicit and competitively award a contract for an Airfield Maintenance Tractor. This requirement will be a Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) contract. Any Questions are due no later than 1 June 2026 by 10 am Central European Time (CET). The Solicitation will close on 30 June 2026 at 10 am Central European Time (CET). The Solicitation, questions received, and any amendments will be posted to SAM.gov. IMPORTANT: Offerors must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) database at the time of submission of quotes. For registration instructions, visit the SAM website: http://www.sam.gov NOTE: All offerors shall be in the English language. All dates and times refer to Central European Time (CET).
