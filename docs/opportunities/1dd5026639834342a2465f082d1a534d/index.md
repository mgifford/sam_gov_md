- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-05-21 11:52:45.767-04

## Summary

This solicitation is for the provision of regional refuse collection, disposal, and recycling services for Naval District Washington (NDW). Services shall be performed at multiple installations located within a 100-mile radius of the Washington Navy Yard, D.C.
