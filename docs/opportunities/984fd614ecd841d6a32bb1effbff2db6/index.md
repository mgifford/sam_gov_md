## Summary

The 502d Contracting Squadron (502 CONS/PKA?B) at JBSA?Lackland is planning a procurement for multiple dormitory furnishings in support of the Naval Technical Training Dormitory, Building 10055. Vendors interested in providing these commodity items are requested to submit a PDF capability statement demonstrating core competencies, relevant past performance, and the information listed below. Responses should be sent via email. The Government is currently utilizing NAICS: 337126 Household Furniture (except Wood and Upholstered) Manufacturing; PSC: 7105 Household Furniture; Size Standard: 950 Employees. The anticipated award is Firm Fixed Price (FFP). This new requirement is for the procurement, delivery, removal, installation, assembly, relocation, and disposal of designated furnishings to support 96 student rooms.
