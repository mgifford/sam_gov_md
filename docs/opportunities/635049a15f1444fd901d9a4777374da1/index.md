# Faraday/EMI Tent

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 14:44:17.431-04
- Solicitation Number: M6700126Q0071

## Summary

MCI-EAST RCO has a requirement for one (1) Faraday/EMI tent aboard Camp Lejeune, North Carolina in accordance with the specifications of the solicitation. The proposed action is set-aside for Total 100% Small Business concerns. The best suited NAICS and PSC identified for this requirement are NAICS 314910 Textile Bag and Canvas Mills and PSC 8340 Textiles, Clothing & Equipage. Contractors interested in doing business with the Government must be registered in SAM. The complete Request for Quotation, including a detailed specification and component list is attached. All responsible small business sources may submit a quotation which may be considered.
