# NFSR 373 Aggregate Hauling

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 16:34:34.789-04
- Solicitation Number: 12444126Q0038

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12444126Q0038 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for loading and hauling MAG ABC aggregate from Prescott National Forest stockpile location at Willow Work Center to National Forest System Road (NFSR) 373, Thumb Butte Road. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 237310 The small business size standard is $45 This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
