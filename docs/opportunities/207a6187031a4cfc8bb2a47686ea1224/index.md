# CHIPS R&D Compact Mobile Closed-Cycle Cryostat - Combined Sources Sought/Notice of Intent to Sole Source

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF

- Posted: 2026-05-28 10:00:33.162-04
- Solicitation Number: NIST-SS26-CHIPS-136

## Summary

***THIS IS A COMBINED SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE AND NOTICE OF INTENT TO SOLE SOURCE*** The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) is seeking information from sources that may be capable of providing a large sample stage cryostat. If no alternate sources are identified, the Government intends to issue a Sole Source Award to Attocube Systems GMBH, Eglfinger Weg 2, Haar, 85540, Germany (UEI: GUM6Q15FDTG4) under the authority of FAR 12.102(b) The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this acquisition is 334516. The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Quantum Measurement Division (QMD) in the Physical Measurement Laboratory (PML) develops novel spectroscopic measurement techniques to probe key physicochemical properties of low dimensional, advanced quantum materials that will enable future technologies to move past conventional digital computing. Determining the fundamental processes of quasiparticles and related charge migration and phase change phenomena are key aspects of work performed in QMD. As part of the CHIPS Act, researchers from NIST are actively pursuing a comprehensive program in metrology research and development aimed at bolstering the U.S. semiconductor industry. A pivotal aspect of their strategic objectives is outlined in one of the seven Grand Challenges—specifically, Grand Challenge 1. This challenge focuses on enhancing metrology tools and measurement science dedicated to assessing the Purity, Properties, and Provenance of materials, which are critical for advancing the semiconductor sector. NIST has a need for a Compact Mobile Closed-Cycle Cryostat that meets or exceeds the following draft minimum specifications: Line Item 0001: Description: Compact mobile closed-cycle cryostat for combined optical spectroscopy and electrical measurements Quantity: 1 Self-contained compact optical cryostat with integrated optical breadboard. Vacuum pump and all necessary accessories included System must be able to be rolled by a single individual into and out of spectrometer location Water cooled compressor with helium lines longer than 12 M in order to be separated from experiment Fully automated temperature control (vacuum, cooldown/warmup, T control) Cool down time: < 5 h (to reach 5 K) Sample: Environment: cryogenic vacuum Space (diameter): > 70 mm Exchange: easy access via removal of vacuum shroud Ultra-low vibrations at cold plate: < 7 nm Temperature range: < 4 K to > 300 K Temperature stability: < 20 mK > 30 electrical contacts into sample area required Sample positioners Close loop piezo control of position for X and Y positioning of sample beneath objective (detailed below) Line Item 0002: Description: Incorporated low temperature objective Quantity: 1 High magnification (50 X) apochromatic objective to operate within a low temperature environment (down to 4 K) Maximum response to optical excitation at 532 nm Clear aperture > 4.5 mm Focal length f > 2.5 mm Numerical Aperture NA >

0.8 Working distance WD > 0.6 mm Close loop piezo control of objective position in Z positioning NIST conducted market research from March 2026 to May 2026 by speaking with colleagues, performing internet searches, attending conferences, and speaking with vendors to determine what sources could meet NIST’s minimum requirements. The results of that market research revealed that only Attocube Systems GMBH (UEI: GUM6Q15FDTG4) appears to be capable of meeting NIST’s requirements.
