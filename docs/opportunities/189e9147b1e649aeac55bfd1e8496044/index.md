- Posted: 2026-05-19 13:43:40.708-04

## Summary

Under Solicitation 47QMC22R0014, 2023 Light Duty Vocational Trucks Program, The General Services Administration (GSA) is announcing the 2026 Fall Open Season period for existing contractors to provide Next Model Year (NMY) information on awarded line items, as well as for existing contractors or new interested offerors to propose new models under the program. All models must comply with the applicable Federal Standards and purchase descriptions listed in GSAFleet.gov. GSA will hold a virtual Industry Day on May 19, 2026, from 1:00 PM – 2:30 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) to go over the Open Season process. All interested parties should attend.
