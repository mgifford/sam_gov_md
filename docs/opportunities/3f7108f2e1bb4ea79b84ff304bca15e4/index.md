## Summary

SOLICITATION-DESCRIPTION 36C26126R0038 Project 654-22-003 Replace Cath Lab and Dietetics Floring in Building 1 and 1D at the VA Sierra Nevada Health Care System Solicitation Closing Date: June 12, 2026, at 04:30 PM PT RFI Closing Date: May 27, 2026, at 2:00 PM PT Pre-proposal Site Visit: May 20, 2026, at 1:00 PM PT. Meet up location will be the flag poles at main entrance off Kirman Street. Address: VASNHCS, 975 Kirman Ave., Reno, NV 89502 NAICS Code: 236220 Small Business Size Standard: $45M 100% Set Aside: SDVOSB Type of Contract: Firm-Fixed Price General Scop of Work: the purpose of this project is to replace flooring in the Cath Lab & Dietetics Services (kitchen) spaces located in Building 1 and 1D in the VA Sierra Nevada Health Care System (VASNHCS). The contractor should replace approximately 850 sq. ft of the Cath Lab floor, and 1,200 sq. ft of the kitchen space. The work shall include demolition, recycling and disposal of existing flooring, and installation (new construction) of new flooring. The work should also account for temporary removal, storage, and reinstallation of furnishing and equipment as required for the successful replacement of flooring products as well as infection control and life safety requirements. The contractor shall completely prepare site for building operations, including demolition and removal of existing flooring, and furnish all services, materials, supplies, temporary equipment and items, tools, specialty services, and project supervision. The Contractor shall furnish all labor and materials needed to perform all demolition, replacement, and installation required by this scope and specifications. There will be one pre-solicitation site visit scheduled, please reference the solicitation for date, time, and meet-up location for the site visit. Offerors are strongly encouraged to attend the pre-solicitation site visit. (End of Solicitation)
