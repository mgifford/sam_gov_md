# Radar Data Processor Refresh

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-05-06 09:27:42.551-04
- Solicitation Number: N00019-23-RFPREQ-APM299-0010
- Awardee: LOCKHEED MARTIN CORPORATION Owego NY 13827 USA
- Award Amount: 99481601.00

## Summary

Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR) awarded a Cost-Plus Fixed Fee (CPFF) Delivery Order under Basic Ordering Agreement (BOA) N0001923G0002 on a sole source basis to Lockheed Martin Rotary and Mission Systems (LM RMS) in Owego, NY. This order is for design, development, integration, and test of the radar data processor technology refresh into the M H-60R AN/APS-153 radar system for both domestic and Foreign Military Sales (FMS) variants. The period of performance is April 2026 through April 2031.
