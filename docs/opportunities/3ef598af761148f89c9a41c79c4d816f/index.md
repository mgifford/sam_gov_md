# VA Roseburg Healthcare System Fixed Dental Prosthetics

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-01 10:01:25.138-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C26026P0382
- Awardee: KEATING DENTAL ARTS, INC. Irvine CA 92606 USA
- Award Amount: 2190455.00

## Summary

This award is to fabricate high quality dental prosthetics for the VA Roseburg Healthcare System (VARHCS) Dental Service and provide transportation and delivery services directly to and from the Roseburg and Eugene clinics. The Contractor shall provide dental laboratory support for the fabrication of fixed prosthetics to the VARHCS Dental Service.
