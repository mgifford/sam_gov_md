# Industry Day - Program Depot Management (PDM)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 13:56:49.764-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8575

## Summary

The AFLCMC/WAK Program Depot Maintenance (PDM) office is hosting a virtual industry day on Tuesday, May 26, 2026, via Microsoft Teams to discuss F-15 aircraft maintenance strategies. Event Details & Focus Areas Format: Private 45-minute virtual sessions. Purpose: To engage with industry vendors on Program Depot Maintenance (PDM) and related inspections, particularly concerning F-15SA conversion jets. Capabilities Sought: Nondestructive inspections (on/off aircraft), asset installation, and Government Furnished Property (GFP) management (including calibration and serviceability). The event aims to gather input on maintenance, modification, functional testing, and delivery of F-15 aircraft, as detailed in the Draft Performance Work Statement referenced in Sources Sought FA8575_F15_PDM - Program Depot Management (PDM). Action Required: Please RSVP to chantel.cummings@us.af.mil no later than 12:00 PM on 5/21/2026. Please review the attached instructions to select your time slot.
