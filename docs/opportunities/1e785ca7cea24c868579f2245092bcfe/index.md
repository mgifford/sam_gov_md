Background: The PRO EVACUATION SYSTEM - The ISOLITE PRO manufacturer by Zyris, Inc, name brand or equal is an all-in-one illuminated dental isolation system designed to facilitate both anterior and posterior procedures efficiently and consistently. The system provides shadowless illumination with three brightness levels of white light and three levels of True amber cure-safe mode to prevent premature resin curing, especially useful during posterior procedures. Supporting both anterior and posterior treatments with included adapters and a dedicated hose with a built-in Y-adapter, the ISOLITE PRO allows for higher patient throughput per chair with simple, one-time installation, ultimately enhancing patient safety, comfort, and procedure efficiency.
