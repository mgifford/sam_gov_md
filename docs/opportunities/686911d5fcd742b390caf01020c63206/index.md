- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-04-27 16:42:19.543-04

## Summary

Amendment 04/27/2026: Government has identified that the Statement of Work and Solicitation documents state that the forklift model needed is the “Raymond 7200-R335TT”. The correction has been made and now states that the model needed is the “Raymond 7200-R35TT”. Solicitation will be extended to allow for quotes that have already been submitted to make corrections as needed. Updated solicitation and SOW to correct model number. This is a Brand Name Requirement. 60 LRS is looking for a Raymond handling equipment model 7200- R35TT. The contractor shall provide the requirement under a Firm Fixed Priced contract in accordance with (IAW) attached Statement of Work (SOW). Contractors, distributors, and or suppliers shall qoute in accordance with all attachments associated with this opportunity to be considered for award. Any questions are due to the POC no later than 21 April 2026 at 4:30PM PST.
