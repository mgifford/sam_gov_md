# Grand Cut-To-Length Stewardship IRSC

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 14:33:49.33-04
- Solicitation Number: 12363N26Q4043

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. Using this format, no separate presolicitation is required and this announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Solicitation number 12363N26Q4043 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Grand Cut-to-Length Stewardship IRSC (Integrated Resource Service Contract). Award shall be made under the R5 Pacific Southwest Stewardship BPA. Only current BPA holders will be eligible to submit quotes. An open-continuous RFQ is posted on sam.gov for the R5 Pacific Southwest Stewardship BPA. Vendors interested in being awarded a BPA should respond to RFQ Number: 12363N22Q4055. Across 82 acres, using Cut-to-Length logging systems that operate on a bed of slash, included sawtimber shall be cut, processed, forwarded to landings and hauled off-Forest. In one unit, high stumps shall be treated. Small trees shall be thinned and standing dead shall be cut and all material removed to landings. Small fuels and existing dead and down material shall be treated by mastication and/or removed to landings. Biomass and cull logs shall be hauled off-Forest. To facilitate product removal, temporary roads shall be installed and decommissioned following use and road maintenance shall be performed. This service acquisition is a total small business set-aside. The set-aside relates to the business status of the contractors submitting quotes and does not limit where the wood products may be sold. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 113310 Logging. The small business size standard is 500 employees. All responsible sources that have been awarded R5 Pacific Southwest Stewardship Blanket Purchase Agreements for work on the Lake Tahoe Basin Management Units may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
