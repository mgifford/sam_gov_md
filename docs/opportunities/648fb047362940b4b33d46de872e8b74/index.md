# Golf Course Rough Mower

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 15:06:12.198-04
- Solicitation Number: FA489726Q0017

## Summary

The 366 Force Support Squadron at Mountain Home Air Force Base requires the procurement of one (1) commercial-grade golf course Rough Mower. This equipment is a mission essential asset required to maintain the structural integrity of the turf, ensure patron safety, and preserve the morale, welfare, and recreation standards of the installation's golf facility.
