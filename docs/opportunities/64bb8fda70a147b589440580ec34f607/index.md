# Rental of an Underbridge Inspection Vehicle and Driver, US Army Corps of Engineers, New England District, Concord, MA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 14:44:21.519-04
- Solicitation Number: W912WJ26QA099

## Summary

THE WORK CONSISTS OF: The Contractor shall provide an articulated boom type underbridge inspection vehicle equipped with a communication system such that verbal contact can be maintained between the bucket/platform and main control station. A driver shall also be provided to serve as the driver of the vehicle. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) will provide personnel to operate the basket. The underbridge inspection vehicle will be used by USACE structural engineers to inspect seven (7) Corps-owned bridges at various sites in Connecticut, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, and Vermont. A list of bridges and photographs of each bridge is provided. Some of these bridges have a small turning curve at its entrance and narrow bridge decks with a maximum curb-to-curb dimension of 10’-0”. In order to accommodate access to these bridges, the Contractor shall provide an underbridge inspection vehicle to be used for all inspections. The inspection vehicle weight shall be limited to the lowest weight in table “LIST OF BRIDGES”. The Contractor is also required to provide appropriate traffic control items including cones, safety (orange) flags, and signage (2 each of, “Road Closed”, “Lane Closed”, “Men Working”, and hand held “Stop/Slow” signs) which will be required at various bridges. This procurement is set-aside for small business vendors. The applicable NAICS code is 532490 with a Small Business Size Standard of $40 Million. ALL VENDORS MUST HAVE AN ACTIVE REGISTRATION IN SAM.GOV AT THE TIME OF SUBMISSION IN ORDER TO BE CONSIDERED. ANY VENDOR THAT DOES NOT HAVE AN ACTIVE SAM.GOV REGISTRATION WILL BE REJECTED AS NON RESPONSIVE.
