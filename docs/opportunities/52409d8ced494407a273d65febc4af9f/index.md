## Summary

SOURCES SOUGHT FOR PMA-213 NAVAL AIR TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS FOR PROGRAM/PROJECT MANAGEMENT SUPPORT SERVICES INTRODUCTION PMA-213 is a program office under the Program Executive Officer, Tactical Aircraft (PEO (T) responsible for providing relevant, sustainable, decisive air traffic management capabilities to get the warfighter to, through and back from the fight. The portfolio “Core/Commodity” multi-platform capability enablers include: Ship Air Traffic Management (ATM) Systems: Joint Precision Approach and Landing Systems (JPALS), Radar Landing Systems (RLS), SPN-50 Maritime Air Radar for Tactical Aircraft, Tactical Air Navigation (TACAN); Shore Air Traffic Management Systems: Shore Air Traffic Control Systems, Expeditionary Air Traffic Control Systems, Shore Landing Systems (Instrument Landing Systems and Precision Approach Radar); Combat Identification (CID): Air Systems, Ship Systems (Mode 5, OE-120, Real-Time Digital power system Simulator (RTDS), Emerging System), Next Generation CID; International Programs; Advanced Development: Technical Integration, Technology Development, Data Management Development, Readiness Cell. The DRAFT Performance Work Statement (PWS) attached to this Sources Sought notice supports domestic and Foreign Military Sales (FMS)/international products. The Contractor shall provide project management, acquisition management and administrative support services for PMA-213?development projects/activities. These services are integral to the execution of development?projects/activities supporting combat aircraft under the cognizance of PMA-213. This Market Research tool is being used to identify potential and eligible firms, of all sizes, prior to determining the method of acquisition and issuance of a solicitation. The results of the Sources Sought will be utilized to determine if any Small Business Set-Aside opportunities exist. Additionally, this source sought is being used to gather constructive information from Industry to improve the request for proposal (RFP).
