## Summary

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention utilizes the SUDAAN statistical software from Research Triangle Institute. SUDAAN is a statistical software package used for the analysis and fine-tuning of statistical data for reporting purposes and is specifically designed for analysis of cluster-correlated data. The SUDAAN is proprietary software sold only by Research Triangle Institute.
