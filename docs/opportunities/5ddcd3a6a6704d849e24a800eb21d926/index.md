## Summary

"SEE ATTACHED" Dear Prospective Offeror: EMBASSY OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA John Adams Park 1, 2244 BZ Wassenaar The Netherlands May 11, 2026 SUBJECT: RFQ: 19NL80-26-Q-00014 Specialized Janitorial Services - Health Unit The U.S. Embassy The Hague invites qualified vendors to submit proposals for specialized janitorial services at our Health Unit. This is a firm-fixed-price contract opportunity for healthcare facility cleaning services. Mandatory Site Visit: We will conduct a mandatory site visit on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, at 10:00 AM local time at the U.S. Embassy, John Adams Park 1, 2244 BZ Wassenaar, The Netherlands. Attendance is required; failure to attend may result in disqualification. To participate in the site visit, please email the identification details of your representative to: TheHagueContracts@state.gov no later than Monday, May 12. 2026 at 17.00 hours. Minimum qualifications: 5 years KVK registration and healthcare/medical facilities cleaning experience, hospital-grade cleaning capability. Please submit your quotation via e-mail to TheHagueContracts@state.gov, referencing our Solicitation number 19NL80-26-Q-0014, to the attention of Contracting Officer Jonathan P. Wedd on May 22, 2026 at 17.00 hours local time. Quotations received after this deadline will not be accepted. To ensure your quotation is considered, please complete and submit the following: SF-18 (blocks 13, 14, 15, and 16) The Pricing Schedule (section 10 of the Scope of Work) For any questions regarding this solicitation, please direct them in writing to Jonathan P. Wedd, Contracting Officer. Questions must be written in English and can be sent via email to TheHagueContracts@state.gov. Alternatively, you may call +31 70 310 2248. Thank you for your attention to this matter. Enclosures: As noted Sincerely, Jonathan P. Wedd "SEE ATTACHED"
