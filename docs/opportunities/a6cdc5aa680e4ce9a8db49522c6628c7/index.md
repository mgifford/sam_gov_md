# Maintain Exterior Finishes

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-27 12:09:53.316-04
- Solicitation Number: A063822

## Summary

Project titled “Exterior Improvements Seed Project” includes constructing, maintaining and repairing the exterior of buildings and structures around Malmstrom Air Force Base. Work may include window replacement/installation, exterior painting, masonry repair, soffit and fascia repair, parapet wall repair, siding repair, exterior insulation and finish system (EIFS) repair, overhead door replacement, exterior man doors replacement and other work associated with maintenance and repair on the exterior of buildings and structures.
