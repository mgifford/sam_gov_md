# Redwood RunMyJobs Software and Maintenance Subscription Renewal

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-11 07:23:24.215-04
- Solicitation Number: CENS_041127-26-0037

## Summary

The United States Census Bureau intends to award a Sole Source Firm Fixed Price Purchase Order to Redwood Software International, Inc. to provide Redwood RunMyJobs On-Premises Software and Maintenance Subscription for a Base and two 12-Month Option Periods. The statutory authorities for this sole source Order are in accordance with Revolutionary Federal Acquisition Regulations Overhaul (RFO FAR) 6.103-1. All interested Contractors must demonstrate capability of meeting requirements. This Notice of Intent is not a request for competitive Proposals. Interested Individuals capable of doing this type of work must submit their written Capabilities Statements that demonstrate the identified capabilities and UEI number via electronic submission to Shannon Crandall Barrington, at Shannon.K.Crandall@census.gov and Pamela Miller at Pamela.A.Miller@census.gov no later than 10am Eastern Time on May 26, 2026. No information will be taken by telephone or mail. Any questions regarding this notice should be directed to Shannon Crandall Barrington, at Shannon.K.Crandall@census.gov and Pamela Miller at Pamela.A.Miller@census.gov as soon as possible, but not later than 10am Eastern Time on May 26, 2026. Any such Statement of Capabilities received by the deadline will be evaluated solely for the purpose of determining whether or not to conduct this procurement on a competitive basis. A determination by the Government not to compete this requirement based upon responses to this Notice is solely within the discretion of the Government.
