# FLFO, Inspect & Clean Water Tank

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 16:45:18.354-04
- Solicitation Number: 140P6026Q0033

## Summary

This is a request for quote (RFQ) for Inspection and Cleaning of a 35,000 gal water tank at Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument(FLFO). This RFQ is for a Firm-Fixed Priced contract using NAICS code 221310 - Water Supply and Irrigation Systems with a size standard of $35.0 Million. Your firm is requested to supply and install the required items as set forth in the attached Scope of Work (SOW), provided agreement can be reached as to the price and other terms of the contract. You are requested to submit a quote to the Department of Interior (Attn: Nicholas Murray, Contracting Officer, nicholas_murray@ios.doi.gov). This request does not constitute a notice to proceed with performance of the services, nor shall it be considered as a commitment on the part of the Government. Your cooperation in submitting the requested information within the time specified is appreciated. See ADDENDUM TO 52.212-1 INSTRUCTIONS TO OFFERORS - COMMERCIAL PRODUCTS AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES (Mar 2023) for information on how to submit quote. All responsible sources may submit a response which, if timely received, must be considered by the agency.
