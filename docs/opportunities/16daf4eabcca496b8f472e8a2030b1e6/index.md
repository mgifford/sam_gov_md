# Arctic Survival Snow Mobility System

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 19:02:46.619-04
- Solicitation Number: FA500426Q0014

## Summary

This requirement is for specialized snow mobility equipment to outfit a 12-person team for operations in an Arctic environment. The primary need is to ensure the team can safely and efficiently traverse long distances over varied and extreme snow-covered terrain, which includes both mountainous and flat or rolling landscapes. Please pay special attention to the PWS and Salient characteristics attachments, as some items are "Or Equal" and some are not.
