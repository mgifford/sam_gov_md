# Rainwater Drainage Inspection - Conduct optical survey of underground sewage at the US Consulate General Frankfurt

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 04:51:28.408-04

## Summary

Rainwater Drainage Inspection The U.S. Consulate General Frankfurt invites you to submit an offer for a firm fixed price to: Conduct optical survey/camera-inspection of all wastewater underground sewage and manholes at the US Consulate General Frankfurt. The Consulate will conduct a pre-quotation visit at the site on Friday, 22. May 2026 at 10:00 am (local time) at the U.S. Consulate General Frankfurt, Wetzlarer Strasse, 60435, Frankfurt am Main. Interested parties must register to attend (maximum 2 representatives per offeror) by 1300 CET on Wednesday, 20 May 2026. Attendance of the pre-quotation visit at the site on Friday, 22. May 2026 at the U.S. Consulate General Frankfurt is a mandatory prerequisite for submitting a final quote. This also applies to any subcontractor the offeror wishes to engage.
