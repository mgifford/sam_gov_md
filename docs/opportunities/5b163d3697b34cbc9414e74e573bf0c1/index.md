# Pre-Solicitation - Notice of Intent to Sole Source for XRL Integration

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 11:16:48.456-04
- Solicitation Number: N00019-25-RFPREQ-JSFCDS-0038

## Summary

The F-35 JPO has a requirement to support the Foreign Military Sales (FMS) customers by (1) establishing connectivity between a Mission Data (MD) development site at Eglin AFB, FL, and a MD Validation & Verification (V&V) site at Lockheed Martin Aeronautics in Fort Worth, TX, and (2) providing the required hardware and software for said development and test. This effort is referred to XRL Integration and supports the creation of FMS Mission Data Files (MDF). The contractor shall be responsible for procuring, delivering, and installing the necessary hardware, software, and network infrastructure that supports MDF efforts and for interconnectivity.
