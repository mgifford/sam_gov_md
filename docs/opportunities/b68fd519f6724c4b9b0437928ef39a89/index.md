# Excavator

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 10:20:35.21-04

## Summary

The Contractor shall procure and provide delivery of a Compact/Mini Excavator meeting the following requirements: • Air-Conditioned Cab • Hand Controls • Integrated Display • Digger Bucket • Integrated Dozer Blade • Soft Turf/Rubber Tracked • Swinging Boom with Full 360 Degree Rotation Carriage • Hydraulic Operation • Diesel Engine • Power Minimum: 40hp • Dig Depth Minimum: 10’ • Ground Level Reach Minimum: 15’ • Dump Clearance Minimum: 10’
