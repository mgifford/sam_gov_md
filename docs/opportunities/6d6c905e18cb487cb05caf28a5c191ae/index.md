# Wi-Fi Internet for McConnell AFB Unaccompanied Housing

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 14:16:59.507-04
- Solicitation Number: FA462126Q0012

## Summary

The contractor shall provide a commercial Wi-Fi solution across four (4) multi-story dormitories (see "Attachment 2-Bldgs. Plans 196-340-342-350"; attachments available upon request through DoD SAFE: “196 – As-Builts," “340 – As-Builts," “342 – As-Builts," “350 – As-Builts" for materials used, covering a total of 444 rooms, plus common areas. The service must be completely separate and isolated from official government networks. Please carefully review the "Solicitation - FA462126Q0012" document and "Attachment 1-SOO Unaccompanied Housing Wi-Fi". The supporting documents are Attachment 2-Bldgs Plans 196-340-342-350 and Attachment 3-WD 2015-5341 Rev No 31. These documents will be the primary resource for Offerers to generate their Technical, Quotes, and Past Performance Volumes. The contractor must submit "Attachment 4-Registration Access Request" and return to lindsay.manning.1@us.af.mil, torrin.black@us.af.mil, and wesley.neaves@us.af.mil (22 CONS) prior to 1 May 2026 1000 CDT. Any Offerer who does not submit this attachment will be denied attendance at the Site Visit scheduled for 8 May 2026, 1000 CDT. Attendance at the site visit is not required to submit, offer, or be considered for the award. All questions submitted by 11 May 2026, 1000 CDT to 22 CONS will be answered with an attachment added to this post labeled "Attachment 5-Dorm Wi-Fi QA" on 13 May. Any question submitted after 11 May 2026 1000 CDT may not be addressed or answered.
