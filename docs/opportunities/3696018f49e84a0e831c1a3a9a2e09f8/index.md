## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotations are being requested, and a written solicitation will not be issued. This combined synopsis constitutes a solicitation and incorporates provisions and clauses in effect through federal acquisition circular 2024-02. The solicitation number is 70Z036-26-Q-STAPONCE751. This acquisition is 100% set aside for small businesses OR unrestricted. The NAICS code is 561710 , with a size standard of $19.5M. The contract type will be a firm fixed-price purchase order. The evaluation process will be Lowest Price Technically Acceptable. The United States Coast Guard (USCG) has a requirement to conduct a termite treatment and fumigations in multiple buildings at USCG Station Ponce De Leon in New Smyrna Beach, FL 32169. Specific requirements are detailed in the Statement of Work (SOW). All bidders are encouraged to visit the site to verify all work details and to become familiar with the existing conditions under which the work will be performed prior to submitting their quotes. Site visits may be conducted Monday through Friday, between the hours of 8:00 AM and 3:00 PM, excluding federal holidays. To arrange a site visit, please contact MKC Hunter Watkins at : (386) 690-5831 at least 24 hours prior to desired site visit date. OR Ste visits are not required for this solicitation. All necessary information regarding the requirements is provided in the Statement of Work. Failure to conduct a site visit does not relieve bidders of responsibility for properly estimating the difficulty or cost of successfully performing this work. The Government assumes no responsibility for any assumptions, understandings, or representations made by any of its officers or agents unless such information is included in the solicitation, SOW, or other written documents.
