## Summary

This notice announces the award of a firm-fixed-price purchase order to 6 Axis, LLC. for the procurement of four (4) complete power system refresh kits for the upgrade and modernization of the custom electrical power generation, storage, and control systems supporting the VLP autonomous operations. These packages support the upgrade and modernization of an existing system. The award was made on a sole-source basis under the authority of FAR 6.103-1, as the contractor was determined to be the only source capable of manufacturing a product that meets the Governments essential technical requirements.
