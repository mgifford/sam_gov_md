# Springfield Refregerated Unit Power Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest

## Summary

Description - Springfield Refrigerated Unit Power, Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, Springerville Ranger District This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 127EAX26Q0039 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) to provide power to a 40’ refrigerated container in Springerville, AZ. Work includes trenching and backfill of approximately 300’ from a 3-phase power source to the refrigerated container’s location, wiring, electrical hookup, and all electrical components to provide functioning power to the unit. The refrigerated unit is located on the Springerville Ranger District in Springerville. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 237130 The small business size standard is 45.M This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency. All questions are due no later than Wednesday, May 20, 2026 at 12:00 MST. A organized site visit will be held on May 13, 2026 at 10:00 AM MST. Meeting location will be 445 E Navajo St, Springerville, AZ 85938. If you plan on attending the site visit, please email Elal Segev at elal.segev@usda.gov.
