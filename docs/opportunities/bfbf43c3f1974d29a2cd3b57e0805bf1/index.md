## Summary

The Department of Veterans Affairs, Veterans Health Administration, Network Contracting Office (NCO) 2, Buffalo, New York intends to solicit quotes from contractors capable of providing medical gas preventative maintenance and testing service at the VA s New York Harbor Healthcare System (Brooklyn, Manhattan and St. Albans campuses). The VA anticipates issuing an RFQ for this requirement on or about May 19, 2026, to be posted to sam.gov. The anticipated period of performance for the Base Year is 6/15/2026 through 6/14/2027 with four one-year option periods. This requirement is being solicited as a service-disabled veteran owned small business (SDVOSB) set-aside. All instructions for submitting quotes will be provided in the solicitation. Perspective offerors must be registered in System for Award Management (SAM) at the time of submission of quotes. Contractors may obtain SAM information at https://www.sam.gov. NAICS Code is 811210 (Electronic and Precision Equipment Repair and Maintenance) with a Small Business Size Standard of $34.0 M. The classification code is J065. Questions concerning this announcement should be directed to the Contract Specialist using the email link provided. Telephone requests for information or questions will NOT be accepted. Please email requests or questions to Chris Weider at the following e-mail address: Christopher.Weider@va.gov and ensure that the subject line includes: RFQ no. 36C24226Q0595. Contracting Office Address: Department of Veterans Affairs, Network Contracting Office (NCO2), 3495 Bailey Avenue, Building 3, 2nd floor, Buffalo, NY 14215.
