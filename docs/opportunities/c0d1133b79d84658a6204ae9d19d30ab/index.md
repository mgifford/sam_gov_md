## Summary

Amendment 0001 (15 MAY 2026) The purpose of this amendment is to: Incorporate the consolidated Questions and Answers (Q&A) document. Extend the quote submission deadline. The deadline for receipt of quotes is changed FROM: 25 MAY 2026 at 0900 EST TO: 01 JUN 2026 at 0900 EST All other terms and conditions remain unchanged. Quoters MUST acknowledge receipt of this amendment in their quote submission email. Please note: The deadline for submitting questions has passed. The Government is not accepting any further questions regarding this solicitation or this amendment. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The United States Army, 3rd Special Forces Group (Airborne), has a recurring requirement for a commercial 2-Day Force Protection Driver Training Course. The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, vehicles, and facilities necessary to conduct hands-on driver training focused on evasive and defensive driving techniques in potential high-threat environments. Please note: There is a strict geographic performance requirement that the contractor's training facility must be located within a sixty (60) minute one-way drive time of Fort Bragg, North Carolina. This is a Request for Quotation (RFQ) under Solicitation Number H9242126QE026 to establish a single-award Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract for a commercial service, solicited in accordance with FAR Part 12. The Government intends to award a contract to the responsible quoter whose quote, conforming to the RFQ, is determined to represent the best value to the Government based on price and other factors. This acquisition is a 100% Small Business Set-Aside.
