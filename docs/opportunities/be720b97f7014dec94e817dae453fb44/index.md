## Summary

------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Q&A Memorandum: Uploaded 11 May 2026 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12445526Q0062 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for CPF Woodtick Trail FR2107 Repairs. PROJECT SUMMARY: The USFS is pursuing a Purchase Order to provide road repair construction services on the Woodtick Trail in the Walker Ranger District, Chippewa National Forest. Activities include but are not limited to roadway reconditioning, spot graveling, culvert installation, beaver dam removal, and gravel resurfacing. Services shall be performed by the awardee in accordance with the specifications, terms, and conditions of this solicitation, including those found in the solicitation attachments. SET-ASIDE: This solicitation is set aside 100% for Small Business entities under NAICS 237310 - Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction (Size Standard: $45M). PROJECT LOCATION: Chippewa National Forest, Walker Ranger District. See solicitation and its attachments for additional location details. PROJECT MAGNITUDE: between $500,000 and $1,000,000 SOLICITATION POINT OF CONTACT: Matthew (Matt) Killian, Contracting Officer matthew.killian@usda.gov PLEASE REVIEW ALL SOLICITATION MATERIALS IN-FULL, INCLUDING ALL INSTRUCTIONS TO OFFERORS, PRIOR TO SUBMITTING A QUOTATION. LATE QUOTATIONS AND/OR QUOTATIONS THAT DO NOT COMPLY WITH SAID INSTRUCTIONS MAY BE DETERMINED NON-REPONSIVE AND ELIMINATED FROM AWARD CONSIDERATION.
