Project Description: NIST, OFPM, the purpose of this project is to remove and replace domestic water piping and valves. Remove approximately 350 linear feet of existing 6-inch cast iron domestic water piping and replace it with PVC piping. Work includes the removal and replacement of one 6-inch fire hydrant valve, one 6-inch water main valve and one 6-inch branch valve. Work location is the NIST Boulder Campus, Daycare center lawn, Building 26 to the front of Building 3 main entrance sidewalk. Sources Sought: Interested and capable sources are requested to electronically submit a Capabilities Statement with supporting supplemental data supporting the exact product that is recommended.
