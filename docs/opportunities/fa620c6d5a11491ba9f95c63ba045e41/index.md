## Summary

The United States Department of State (DOS) Bureau of Overseas Buildings Operations (OBO) requires engineering and construction services for replacement of cooling towers pipelines at CGOB. Replace all the cooling tower piping. All exterior and interior piping shall be replaced with new. The selected contractor "Tahzib Engineering Services" is presently engaged under Contact # 19PK5025C7000 for the replcement of cooling towers and AHUs, incluidng associated exterior piping work. Given their ongoing presence on-site and successful fulfillment of all technical and documentation requirements. Said contractor is the most suitable contractor to execute this job.
