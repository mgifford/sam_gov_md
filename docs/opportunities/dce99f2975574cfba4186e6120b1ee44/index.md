# Request for Offers: Stair Pressurization System Inspections and Testing at FAA fFacilities in Alaska

- Agency: TRANSPORTATION, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 19:09:08.373-04
- Solicitation Number: 697DCK-26-R-00021

## Summary

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) is issuing this Request for Offers (RFO) for Stair Pressurization System Inspections and Testing at multiple FAA facilities in Alaska, in accordance with the Statement of Work (SOW). The technicians must be certified to work on the Stair Pressurization System equipment and components and any adjustments and repairs. See Sections H and L for key Personnel Requirements for details. There is NO set-aside for this requirement. Award will be made as a firm-fixed price contract, Base Year Plus 4 Option Years. Questions must be in written form and submitted to Angela.Furukawa@faa.gov by June 4, 2026, 4:00 PM Pacific Time. DO NOT CALL FOR QUESTIONS. Responses to this RFO must be received by the bid due date and time, June 26, 2026, 4:00 PM Pacific Time, in the inbox of Angela.Furukawa@faa.gov. See submission details under Section L of this RFO. Responses to this RFO must clearly reference the RFO number, 697DCK-26-R-00021. Offerors must have an active SAM registration in SAM.gov at the time of submission and no exclusions. The contracting officer’s physical office is in Des Moines, WA. NOTE: Acquisition Management System (AMS) applies to the FAA in place of the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) and various other provisions of Federal acquisition law.
