# Pick Up & Drop Off Laundry IDIQ (5-Year)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 17:34:34.884-04
- Solicitation Number: W912K3-26-Q-A010

## Summary

Provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to perform Laundry/Dry Cleaning Services for the Washington Army National Guard as defined in the attached Performance Work Statement (PWS). The Washington National Guard intends to award a single firm fixed-price, 5-year, Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract for these services. All services will be funded on a task order basis, there is no funding associated with the initial award. The first task order will be awarded in excess of the minimum guarantee of $2,000.00.
