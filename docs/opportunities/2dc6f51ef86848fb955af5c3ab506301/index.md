## Summary

The 355 AMXS AGE is requesting a Brand Name Only (BNO) commodity purchase and installation of an AutoCrib VX1000. The AutoCrib provides immediate access to on-hand parts and consumables, eliminating delays associated with tool searches, manual checkout processes, and unavailable A components. This reduces ground support equipment downtime and helps keep HH-60W aircraft on schedule for inspections, repairs, and pre-flight requirements. This Request for Quote (RFQ) is being solicited under a Total Small Business Set-Aside.
