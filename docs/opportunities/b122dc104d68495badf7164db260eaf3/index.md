THE CONTRACT LINE ITEMS ARE AS FOLLOWS: CLIN 0001 Description: WILDERNESS LEADERSHIP EXPEDITION- One 4 night / 5 day wilderness expedition in the Olympic National Park for 10 participants. This includes transportation to/from Sea-Tac airport to the trailhead, required backpacking gear, food, and two professional wilderness guides. All elements of route planning, reconnaissance, and emergency medical procedures are handled by the guides. Wilderness skills training is included in the event to increase confidence and enjoyment of the natural environment. Immunity to Change workshop (ITC in the dirt) and adaptative leadership sessions will be part of the expedition.
