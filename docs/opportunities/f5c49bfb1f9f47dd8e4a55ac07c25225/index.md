# Trackless Moving Target (TMT) Antenna - Network Relay Tower

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-08 14:28:49.958-04
- Solicitation Number: PANMCC-26-P-0000-046312

## Summary

This Sources Sought Notice is for informational and market research purposes only. It does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP), Request for Quote (RFQ), or a promise to issue a solicitation in the future. This notice does not commit the Government to contract for any supply or service. All information submitted in response to this announcement is voluntary; the Government will not pay for information requested nor will it compensate any respondent for any cost incurred in developing information provided to the Government. The 918th Contracting Battalion is conducting market research to identify potential sources capable of providing a Trackless Moving Target (TMT) Antenna. The Government is seeking to identify qualified and responsible vendors who can meet the technical specifications and a highly accelerated delivery schedule. Technical Requirements The anticipated requirement is for two (2) TMT Antennas that must meet the following minimum salient characteristics: Compatibility: Must have programmed control for TMT Systems Antenna Mast: 31.5 feet high with a tripod base that extends for stability Two (2) inch diameter mast 50-pound load capability Collapsible for transport Capability to mount antenna array on without causing instability in normal weather conditions Adjustable to varying terrain to ensure system can be used throughout all range complexes Antenna: Capable of expanding operations exponentially of the TMT system on the range complex Omni-Directional Capable of controlling both current and future TMT systems that Fort Carson may acquire Hardware: Power cables for antenna Power box for system Cables for radio-frequency relay to antenna Attachment hardware for antennas and all other system requirements Delivery Information: Vendor must be capable of delivering the equipment to Fort Carson, Colorado no later than 16 May 2026 Information Requested Company Namer and Address Point of Contact (Name, Title, Phone Number, Email Address) CAGE Code Capability Statement with equipment description, size, and delivery date All responses to this notice must be submitted electronically via email to SFC Anahi Garcia at anahi.garcia2.mil@army.mil. Please include "Sources Sought Response: TMT Antenna - [Your Company Name]" in the subject line of the email. Response Deadline: Responses must be received no later than 1600 MDT on 08 May 2026.
