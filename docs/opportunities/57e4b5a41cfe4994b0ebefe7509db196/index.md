# Electric Arc Furnace removal ,acquisition and installation

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 08:24:19.179-04
- Solicitation Number: W519TC26R028

## Summary

U.S. Army Contracting Command - Rock Island (ACC-RI) has a requirement to provide the following: Detailed design engineering, specification development, project management, equipment installation, concrete work, construction services, equipment commissioning, training and the procurement of (1) 5-Ton Electric Arc furnace (with the option for a second) and necessary supporting equipment such as bag house dust collection system, compressed Air and Argon storage tanks, (1) 5-ton AOD system, plus the demolition and removal of the existing 5-Ton Arc furnace. Please review attachments for complete requirement. Note a site visit for this requirement is scheduled for 8 May 2026 at 8am.
