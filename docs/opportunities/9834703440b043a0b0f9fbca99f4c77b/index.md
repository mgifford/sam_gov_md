# F-35 Wheel Storage and Transportation Container

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-15 16:25:18.873-04
- Solicitation Number: F4A3306119A001

## Summary

The 325th contracting squadron is conducting market research to identify contractors capable and willing to supply Wheel storage and transportation containers for F-35 wheels IAW the attached Draft Salient Characteristics. DISCLAIMER: These request for information is for market research purposes only and IS NOT a request for quotation. The purpose of this notice is to conduct market research in accordance with Revolutionized FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 10 to determine the availability and capability of businesses to provide this commodity at Tyndall Air Force Base, Florida. Interested vendors shall provide the following: Information in sufficient detail regarding their capabilities and capacity to satisfy the requirement. Estimates lead time
