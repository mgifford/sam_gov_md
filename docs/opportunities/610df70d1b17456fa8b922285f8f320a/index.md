## Summary

This amendment is to add the questions and answers and to extend the close date to 12 May 2026. This amendment is to extend the close date to 29 April 2026. The Louisiana Army National Guard (LAANG) requires laundry services for a total of 120 personnel, generating an estimated maximum of 600lbs of laundry per week. This is to be performed in accordance with the attached PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT (PWS). The contractor shall always maintain an adequate workforce for the uninterrupted performance of all tasks defined within the attached PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT (PWS).
