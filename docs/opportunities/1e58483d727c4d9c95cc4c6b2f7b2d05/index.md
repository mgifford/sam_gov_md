# Solar Street Light Replacement

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 13:58:21.207-04
- Solicitation Number: 140A2326Q0135

## Summary

The AZ Navajo Central Agency, Chinle Office has a requirement for replacement of solar street lights around the BIE/BIA administrative campus. The current lighting system is nonfunctional and requires replacement. The contractor shall provide all labor, supplies, design, material, safety, personnel and equipment for proper installation of new solar powered street lights.
