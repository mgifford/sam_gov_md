# Saipan Lodging Services BPAs

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 00:25:39.755-04
- Solicitation Number: FA523426Q0005

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial services using a Blanket Purchase Agreement prepared in accordance with RFO part 12. This announcement constitutes only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number FA523426Q0005 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ) for Lodging BPA for the 21 Combat Air Base Squadron (CABS). Supplier shall provide commercial lodging for Government personnel in accordance with the attached Lodging Performance Work Statement - 30 April 26 and Lodging Price List when requested by an individual identified on the Authorized Caller letter. The period of performance is 6 months from date of Blanket Purchase Agreement establishment. All work shall be in accordance with commercial and industrial standards. (Refer to the Statement of Work and BPA Terms and Conditions for more information) This acquisition is Total Set Aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. Payment will be paid by Government Purchase Card (GPC), Government Travel Card (GTC), or Wide Area Workflow (WAWF). All costs associated with the use of these cards (i.e. transaction fees) shall be included in the price identified in the Price List for applicable type of room.
