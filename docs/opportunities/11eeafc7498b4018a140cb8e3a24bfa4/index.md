# Shredding Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 14:24:23.217-04
- Solicitation Number: W912DR26QA035

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) has a requirement for Document and Media Shredding services at the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) ? Washington Aqueduct Division located in three different geographic locations. One location at 6000 Mac Arthur Blvd (FRE), Bethesda, Maryland and two locations in the District of Columbia at 5900 Mac Arthur Blvd (DAL), NW and 2500 1st Street (MCM), NW. The documents and media (CDs, disks, phones, and hard drives) to be shredded are ?Controlled Unclassified Information? (CUI) and may contain personally identifiable information (PII) and other sensitive but unclassified information. Special requirement for shredding of documents and media shall be required per Contract Line Item Number (CLIN). These documents must be destroyed in a manner that will prevent disclosure. Refer to AR 380-5 or DoD 5105.21-M-1.
