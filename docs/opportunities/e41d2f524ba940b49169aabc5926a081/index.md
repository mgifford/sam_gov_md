## Summary

Synopsis: This announcement constitutes a Souces Sought Notice. THIS IS NOT a Request for Quote (RFQ) or a Request for Proposal (RFP). This announcement is for information and planning purposes only and is not to be construed as a commitment by the government, implied, or otherwise, to issue a solicitation or award a contract. The Department of Veteran Affairs (VA), Strategic Acquisition Center (SAC), is seeking qualified sources to provide Health Insurance for Service Dogs for the Veterans Health Administration (VHA) Prosthetic Sensory Aid Services (PSAS) organization. The purpose of this notice is to determine interest and capability of potentially qualified Businesses (All Sizes) relative to the North American Industry Classification code (NAICS): 521128 Other Direct Insurance (except Life, Health, and Medical) Carriers. To make an appropriate acquisition decision for this requirement, the Government will use responses to this sources sought synopsis. This type of solicitation issued, and the manner of advertisement will also depend on the response to this sources sought synopsis. Description: Scope of Work: Prosthetic Sensory Aid Services (PSAS) is requesting Health Insurance for Service Dogs owned by U.S. Military Veterans, located throughout the U.S. Period of Performance: To Be Determined. Submission requirements for responses to this sources sought synopsis: Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business, Veteran Owned Small Business, Small Business, and Large Business offerors interested in submitting a proposal for this project, if it is formally advertised, must be registered and be verified in their respective coordinating databases and must provide the correct NAICS code to include Contractor s Unique Entity ID (SAM), name of the firm, address, phone and point of contact. Where to send responses: Point of Contacts: Katelyn Wardman (Contract Specialist) & Max Andrade (Contract Officer) Email: Katelyn.Wardman@va.gov and Max.Andrade@va.gov Response Deadlines: As an interested Company/Party towards the Upcoming Solicitation Requirement: May 20, 2026, 03:30 PM, EST See attached documents: Service Dogs Health Insurance PWS End of Document
