# CFCD STK Annual License

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-28 09:18:17.132-04

## Summary

Mission and Installation Contracting Command, Fort Gordon , GA intends to negotiate/award a sole-source contract with Ansys Government Initiatives (AGI) 220 Valley Creek Blvd Exton, Pennsylvania 19341 for the following items: STK Premium (Space). The NAICS code is 513210. The STK Premium (Space) allows you to modify satellite mission requirements, design trajectories, conduct space operations, perform trade studies using advanced optimization algorithms, integrate real time data from actual constellations in orbit, simulate electro-optical and infrared sensor systems and conduct, and allows you to plan multidomain concept of operations for space, air, and terrestrial assets in a single mission environment. The authority for this non-competitive award is 10 USC 2304(c)(1), Only one responsible Source. The Government intends to procure this sole source requirement under Simplified Acquisition Procedures in FAR part 12. THIS NOTICE IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE PROPOSALS and a solicitation will not be issued. Interested concerns must show clear and convincing evidence that competition of this requirement would be advantageous to the Government. If no affirmative written responses are received by Friday May 1, 2026, the award will be made without further notice. Oral communications are not acceptable in response to this notice. A determination by the government not to compete this proposed requirement is solely within the discretion of the government
