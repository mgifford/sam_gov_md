## Summary

This project consists of all work, including labor, equipment and materials necessary to run all new electrical conductors to provide power to cutter and boat shore tie connections as well as pier lighting at Station Sandy Hook in accordance with the attached Statement Of Work (SOW). All wages and benefits shall be in accordance with the SCA and wage rate determinations for the Place Of Performance and Period Of Performance.
