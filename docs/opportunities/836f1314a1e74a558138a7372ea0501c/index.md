# Third-Party Review of the USDA's Center for Veterinary Biologics (CVB) Authorities, Regulations, and Guidance Documents

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-02 17:21:09.929-04
- Solicitation Number: 12639526Q0102

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12639526Q0102 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for the comprehensive third-party review and assessment of the USDA's Center for Veterinary Biologics’ (CVB) statutory authorities, regulations, memorandums, notices and other guidance and supporting documents. This acquisition is not set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 541690. The small business size standard is $19.0. This acquisition is an unrestricted requirement. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
