# Helicopter Hangar Door (HHD) Controller Replacement for DDG Navy Ship

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-06 07:19:43.94-04
- Solicitation Number: N6817126QT013

## Summary

The Department of the Navy, Naval Supply Systems Command (NAVSUP) FLC Rota, Spain; hereby publicizes its intention to solicit, negotiate, and award a Commercial, Firm-Fixed-Price purchase order on a noncompetitive basis with Rockwell Automation Inc., from Cleveland, Ohio, and CAGE code 4H047. The requirement is for a Helicopter Hangar Door (HHD) Controller Replacement for a DDG Navy ship, QTY (1), P/N R1USB10440-0001. This is a direct replacement of P/N R1USB10440-0001 for which Rockwell Automation Inc., is the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM). A determination by the government not to compete based upon responses to this notice is solely at the discretion of the government. However, all responsible sources may submit a capability statement which shall be considered by the agency. The applicable NAICS Code is 334419 - Other Electronic Component Manufacturing, and the size standard is 750 employees. The point of contact for this announcement is julius.j.arrascue-pastor.civ@us.navy.mil Be advised that the aforementioned information is anticipatory in nature and is not binding. A copy of the Request for Quote will be made available upon request. Any comments, questions or concerns regarding this notice may be submitted via e-mail to julius.j.arrascue-pastor.civ@us.navy.mil by 21 MAY 2026 at 11am local, Rota, Spain time. Questions by phone or fax will not be considered.
