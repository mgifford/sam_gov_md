# Chiller Replacement JBSA FSH Bldg1029

## Summary

This solicitation closing date will be extended as we are awaiting sufficient answers from site visit questions. JBSA Fort Sam Houston, TX requires a chiller replacement. This requirement is being solicited as a 100% Total Small Business set aside. The NAICS code is 238220, Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors, size standard is $19M. PSC J041. Quotes submitted by small business concerns will be accepted by the Government. Any quote that is submitted by a contractor that is not a small business concern will not be considered for the award. Refer to technical criteria listed in the attached RFQ. Refer to the attached RFQ and SOW for information regarding this requirement. Please provide quote to POCs listed in the RFQ. Questions/Concerns: All questions/concerns must be submitted in writing by email no later than Friday, 22 May 2026 at 10:00 am CDT. Responses to questions will be posted by the Government on the SAM.gov website, as received. Questions submitted after this date/time will not be considered. Site Visit: Site visit is highly recommended as Contractors are responsible for verifying site conditions. A site visit is scheduled for 20 May 2026, 10 AM CDT at JBSA Fort Sam Houston, TX. The meeting place will be the JBSA Fort Sam Houston, Walters Street Visitor Center 892 Hood St, San Antonio, TX 78234. Departure to the site will be promptly at 10:00 am CDT. Recommend arriving 30 minutes earlier to sign in, wait in line and retrieve your pass at the visitor center. Interested parties are required to have a copy of a valid REAL ID Driver’s License, vehicle registration and insurance on hand in order to receive a base pass. If the drivers license has not been updated to the REAL ID, interested parties will need a passport along with a drivers license. If this type of documentation is not provided a base pass will not be issued and attendance will not be granted. Attachments: 1. Atch 1. Statement of Work for Replacement of 150-Ton Chiller at Building 1029 JBSA FSH TX 2. Atch 2. SCA Wage Determination 2015-5253 Rev 29 3. Solicitation - FSH Chiller B1029 - Draft
