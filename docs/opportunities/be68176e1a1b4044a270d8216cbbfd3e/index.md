# Learning & Professional Education Program Subscription

- Agency: TREASURY, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 19:29:50.618-04
- Solicitation Number: 26_31_APMO

## Summary

At a summary level, the Government has interest in and is seeking information regarding the capability of firms who can provide services pertaining to training. LB&I requires unlimited access to a training subscription with a centralized digital library of accredited continuing education programs, course materials, and technical publications designed to support a minimum of 3,500 compliance employees.
