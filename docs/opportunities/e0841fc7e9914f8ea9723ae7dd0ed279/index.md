# RFI Blockchain Analytics

- Agency: TREASURY, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Special Notice

## Summary

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Criminal Investigation (CI), is conducting market research to identify vendors capable of providing blockchain analytics, intelligence, investigative support, and related capabilities in support of cyber-enabled financial crime investigations involving digital assets. Additional details are provided in the attached Request for Information (RFI) document.
