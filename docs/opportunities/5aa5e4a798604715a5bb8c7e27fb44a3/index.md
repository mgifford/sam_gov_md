# Burkart Flutes

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 12:46:24.147-04
- Solicitation Number: N0018926QW102

## Summary

U.S. Navy Fleet Bands require 2 Lillian Burkart flutes with customized headjoints. These instruments are essential to all naval ceremonial, public outreach, and diplomatic missions worldwide. Burkart flutes are the benchmark for performance quality, world-class customer support, and long operating life. They represent the best quality and value to the United States government.
