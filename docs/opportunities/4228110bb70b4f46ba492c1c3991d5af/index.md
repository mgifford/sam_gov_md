# Vulkan Couplings-American Vulkan Corporation (AVC)

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 17:49:16.573-04
- Solicitation Number: 70Z08526QIBCT0043

## Summary

The government intends to award a sole source contract to American Vulkan Corporation (AVC) with fixed price delivery orders for couplings and elements used on Seaside Buoy tender (WLB-225), Coastal Buoy tender (WLM-175), Medium endurance cutter (WMEC-270), Inland construction tender (WLIC-160) and River Buoy (WLR-75/65) tender class vessels using genuine Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts.
