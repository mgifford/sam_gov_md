## Summary

The Government requires Protective Coating (PC) services at Scott Air Force Base (AFB), Illinois. This is a follow-on requirement to the 2 current PC Blanket Purchase Agreements (BPAs), FA440723A0002 and FA440723A0001. The required services consists of furnishing all labor, materials, vehicles, equipment, tools, supervision, and administration required to perform protective coating maintenance as described in the Statement of Work (SOW) (except Government-Furnished materials) for the execution of painting and application of protective coatings; pavement markings applicable to existing roads, existing parking lots at Scott AFB, IL. In accordance with (IAW) the SOW, the tasks are general building and pavement coatings. The requirements are not military unique. The required services are commercial services and will use Simplified Acquisition Procedures (BPAs) to satisfy this requirement as defined in RFO
