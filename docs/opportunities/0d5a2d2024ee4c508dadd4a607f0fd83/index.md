# Moose Run Stewardship IRSC- R2, Arapaho and Roosevelt NF, Sulphur RD

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 11:26:13.708-04

## Summary

This procurement is restricted to the approved companies eligible for call orders under the R2 Rocky Mountain Restoration Project (RMRP) Stewardship BPA for the Arapaho and Roosevelt National Forests in the state of Colorado. This requirement may be awarded to one vendor as a BPA Call Order against BPA's previously solicited on the Government Point of Entry (GPE). The government will not provide feedback or evaluations to companies regarding their submissions in response to this notice that are not eligible for awards under the R2 RMRP Stewardship BPA. A site visit is scheduled for Monday, June 8, 2026 to view the project area and discuss the objectives of this contract. The meeting will take place at 12:00 pm Mountain Time at the Fraser Valley Sports Complex, 220 County Rd 522, Fraser, CO 80442. RSVP TO Seth Jennings by 4 pm 6/5/2026 at seth.jennings@usda.gov or 970-531-5720 if you plan to attend.
