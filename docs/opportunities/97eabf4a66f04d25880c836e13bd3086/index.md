# FUEL GASOLINE FOR MP VEHICLES

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 14:56:08.254-04
- Solicitation Number: PR16005065

## Summary

The U.S. Embassy in Bogotá has a requirement for Regular gasoline (fuel)covering the period from June 1 to August 31,2026. Fuel supply and delivery must be made to the U.S. Embassy Compound located at Carrera 45 No.24B-27 from four to six partial deliveries per month. MINIMUM AND MAXIMUM AMOUNTS: During this period, the Government shall place orders totaling a minimum of 1 gallon of regular gasoline. This reflects the purchase order minimum for this period of performance. The amount of all orders shall not exceed 65.000 gallons of regular gasoline. Partial deliveries of fuel shall be made in trucks form 2.000 to 5.000 gallons capacity using Colombian standard industry hose/coupling connections. 1. Net 30 day payment upon submsiion of invoice using the vendor portal and delivery of fuel. 2.Payments to be made via EFT. 3. Price per gallon must be provided.
