# Air University Student Management System

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-13 14:32:42.111-04
- Solicitation Number: AU-SMS

## Summary

Air University is a military education institution delivering undergraduate degrees, graduate degrees, and non-degree programs to support the Department of Air Force and Department of War requirements for Professional Military Education, Professional Continuing Education, and Professional Developmental Education. Headquarters (HQ) Air University (AU) requires a contractor to provide a commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) Student Management System to meet or exceed AU’s requirement for an information system for higher education that will manage the complete student lifecycle from applications for admission through program completion to include transcript services.
