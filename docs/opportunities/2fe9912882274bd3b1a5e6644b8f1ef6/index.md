# Sole Source - Bi2 Technologies, LLC

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-26 14:38:36.938-04
- Solicitation Number: 70CTD026C00000006

## Summary

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) issued a sole source award to BI2 Technologies, LLC for iris biometric recognition technology for offender recognition and access to a biometric information system to allow ICE agents to quickly authenticate the identity of subjects during field operations. A redacted sole source justification is provided in accordance with Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) FAR 6.301.
