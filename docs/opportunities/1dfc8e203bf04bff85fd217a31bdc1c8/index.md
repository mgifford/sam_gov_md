# EXTERIOR WALK-IN FREEZERS

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 10:42:57.224-04
- Solicitation Number: 140G0126Q0151

## Summary

The U.S. Geological Survey Columbia Environmental Research Center (CERC) requires the purchase of two walk-in freezers to be delivered to Columbia MO, within 60 days from the date the contactor receives the purchase order. There are no option periods. Competitive Request for Quotations (RFQ) 140G0126Q0151 is a small business set aside under NAICS code is 333415 Air-Conditioning and Warm Air Heating Equipment and Commercial and Industrial Refrigeration Equipment Manufacturing. The size standard is 1,250 employees. Question Deadline 5/19/2026 2:00pm MT The anticipated award date of the contract is 6/15/2026. A vendor must be registered at https://www.sam.gov to be awarded the purchase order. Vendor must be active under 333415 NAICS Code in SAM at time of award. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which shall be considered by the agency. Quotations shall be submitted to Mindie Dixon at mindie_dixon@ios.doi.gov
