# Portable Roadway

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-27 16:01:06.158-04
- Solicitation Number: FA481926CDDAR

## Summary

Heavy-Duty Portable Roadway System Description: Portable Roadway / Rapid Runway / Matting System for support of Crash, Damaged, and Disabled Aircraft Recovery (CDDAR) operations to ensure heavy recovery vehicles and equipment can safely access a disabled aircraft on unprepared terrain. The primary function is to provide a rapidly deployable and durable surface to prevent recovery assets from bogging down in soft soil, sand, or mud for off-runway recovery scenarios. System Specifications Deployment Method: May be interlocking, modular, foldable, etc.Product must be able to be manually assembled by a small team to form a secure, continuous surface. Total Coverage Area: 20-40 Sq. Meters
