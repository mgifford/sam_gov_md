# FY26 Furniture for Commanders Conference Room

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-28 11:33:41.639-04
- Solicitation Number: FA330026Q0030

## Summary

The 42d Contracting Squadron, Maxwell Air Force Base AFB, AL, is seeking to renovate the Commander’s Executive Office Suite in Building 1404 and procure new office furniture for the Commander’s executive staff. The contractor shall furnish all necessary equipment, including delivery, assembly, disassembly, and training on equipment. All feedback and information received may be used to determine the appropriate acquisition strategy. The Government is seeking vendors who are able to satisfy the requirements outlined in the attached DRAFT STATEMENT OF REQUIREMENT (SOR).
