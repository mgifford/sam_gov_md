# CEC Production and Repair Ceiling Increase

- Solicitation Number: N00024-22-C-5218

## Summary

The Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) intends to award a sole?source contract modification for continued Production and Repair Services for the Cooperative Engagement Capability (CEC) systems configuration to L3Harris Technologies, Inc. (L3Harris), formerly L3 Technologies, Inc., of Camden, New Jersey. Contract N0002422C5218 was awarded to L3Harris in FY2022 via full and open competition. This action will procure increased quantities of AN/USG-xB units, SDPs, and associated spares beyond the initial contract award.. The CEC program provides a sensor network with integrated fire control capability that significantly improves strike force air and missile defense capabilities by coordinating measurement data from strike force air search sensors on CEC-equipped units into a single, integrated, real-time, composite track air picture. The CEC sensor netting system improves Naval Carrier Strike Group (CSG) and Expeditionary Strike Group (ESG) area air defense capabilities by extracting and distributing sensor-derived information such that the superset of this data is available to participating CEC Cooperating Units (CU). CEC fuses the distributed data into a single fire control quality air track picture that significantly improves unit track precision, consistency, and continuity; expands detection range; and improves reaction time. CEC also improves strike force effectiveness by improving overall situational awareness and by enabling longer range, cooperative, multiple, or layered engagement strategies.
