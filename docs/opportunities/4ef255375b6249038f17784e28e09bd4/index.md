The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), VISN 02, Network Contracting Office 2, is seeking sources that can provide one (1) Utility Terrain Vehicle that at a minimum meets the following salient characteristics, there is no brand name requirement for this procurement, an equivalent product will be accepted as long as it meets the salient characteristics. General Requirements The scope of this procurement request is to procure a UTV for the Engineering Service to use on the Northport VAMC Campus. A UTV is required to safely and efficiently transport personnel, tools, equipment, and materials to locations that are not accessible by standard vehicles. The facility spans 268 acres, includes off road, unimproved, and restricted-access areas where routine maintenance, utility work, and infrastructure inspections must be conducted. Objective - The Vendor will provide the following: Crew cab 6 passenger UTV with All Weather Cab Enclosure with off road capability. Salient Characteristics will include but not limited to: All Trail Tires (on/off road) Front Power Windows Doors 4 LCD rider information center 4-Wheel Hydraulic Disc with Dual-Bore Front and Rear Calipers Dual LED Headlights Mud Guards if available Electronic Power Steering 4500 LB Winch w/ Rope, Auto Stop and wireless remote 4-stroke twin cylinder DOHC Engine 82 HP minimum 16.3 or

17.5 Turning Radius Gas-assist dump box with Lock & Ride accessory integration Minimum 1100 Lb. Payload Capacity Minimum 2500 Lb. Towing Capacity 14 Ground Clearance 14 Wheels All-Terrain Tires Anti-theft system Factory Installed Heat and Air conditioning with defrost function Delivery Provide equipment within 60 days of award.
