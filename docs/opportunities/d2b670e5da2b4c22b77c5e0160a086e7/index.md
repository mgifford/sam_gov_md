# W50S6X26Q0003 Electric Air Compressor

## Summary

***AMENDMENT 01*** The purpose of this amendment is to 1) extend the deadline for offer submission, 2) to announce the scheduling of a site visit, 3) to update the RFQ document to exclude GSA language that is no longer compatible with the current solicitation method, and 4) update the listed provisions and clauses document to reflect recent changes to acquisition regulations. The details for each of these items may be found below: The deadline for offer submissions has been extended from Monday May 11th, 2026, to Monday May 25th, 2026. A site visit will take place on Tuesday May 12th, 2026 at 1300 hours EST. The location of the site visit will be the same as the shipping address/place of performance. If you wish to attend the site visit you must fill out the attached base access request form v2 and submit the filled-out form to the CS and CO listed on this solicitation for processing NLT COB Monday May 11th, 2026. Please ensure only employees that are listed on your submitted form attend the site visit. Anyone that is not listed on your submitted form will not be able to access the base for the site visit. Please see section 4.3-1 in the attached updated RFQ for a full scope of all changes that have been made. Please see the attached and updated PnC document for a full list of all provisions and clauses included in this request. ------------------------------------------- The Government intends to award a single, firm-fixed-price purchase order for one (1) new electric air compressor. This is a commodity procurement where installation and removal services are considered incidental to the purchase. The awarded contractor shall provide all equipment, materials, and labor necessary to deliver, install, test, and dispose of the old unit as specified herein. CLIN 0001: Electric Air Compressor: 1 EACH The required specifications for the air compressor are as follows: - Air Tank Capacity: 240 Gallons - Pressure Rating: 200 Pounds per Square Inch (PSI) - Power Unit: Dual 25 Horsepower (HP) electric motors - Electrical System: Three-phase, compatible with 200V or 230/460V - Required Features: Automatic tank drain; low oil control; and dual controls CLIN 0002:Installation, Testing, & Removal: 1 JOB All labor and equipment required for the professional installation and testing of the new compressor (CLIN 0001) and the disconnection and off-site disposal of the existing unit. This includes all necessary personnel, offloading equipment, and adherence to environmental regulations for the disposal of the old unit. See attached RFQ for full details.
