# USCG JUNEAU ALASKA GYM MEMBERSHIP SERVICES

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 12:13:25.94-04
- Solicitation Number: 37360PR26000001

## Summary

PROPOSAL DUE DATE AMENDED TO READ: TUESDAY, 26 MAY 2026 AT 3:00 PM (ALASKA TIME ZONE). ALL PROPOSALS TO BE EMAILED TO DENO.A.STAMOS@USCG.MIL; BENJAMIN.R.REEDY@USCG.MIL AND ROBBIN.M.KESSLER@USCG.MIL SOW/SPECS ALSO ATTACHED AS ATTACHMENT Contractor shall provide 24-hour access Fitness Club Memberships to USCG personnel in the Juneau, Alaska area for up to 252 active-duty Coast Guard members, in addition to 70 monthly pool passes (to be distributed by the US Coast Guard) for the City and Borough of Juneau’s Swimming Pools. Contractor’s facility must be able to accommodate more than 50 overall users at one time during any point of the day. Contractor shall have all required facilities located within the immediate proximity of Juneau, AK, USA and have staff present at the facility/facilities no less than Monday-Friday from 6 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. During hours unmanned by a staff member, video cameras are required. USCG personnel will be provided with instructions on how to access the facility/facilities during unmanned timeframes. *Childcare is not to be included in the fitness club memberships*
