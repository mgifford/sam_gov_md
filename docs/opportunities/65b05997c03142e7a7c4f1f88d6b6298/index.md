# Sole Source Award to Fluke Electronic Corp

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-01 16:10:29.214-04
- Solicitation Number: M6700426Q0007

## Summary

The Marine Corps Logistics Command, Contracts Department (CONT) intends to solicit and contract with Fluke Electronics Corp (CAGE 4U744) of Everett, Washington on a sole-source basis for the Calibration and repair of multiple units. The North American Industry Classification Standard (NAICS) code assigned is 334515-Instrument Manufacturing for Measuring and Testing Electricity and Electrical Signals. No solicitation will be posted on this website for this requirement. A Justification and Approval document is attached to this notice. Any potential source may contact the persons indicated above to discuss their ability to offer the required services for future acquisitions.
