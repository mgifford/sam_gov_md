# TX-SAN MARCOS ARC-REHAB SHOP-GARAGE

## Summary

TX - SAN MARCOS AQUATIC RESOURCES CENTER (ARC) - REHABILITATE SHOP & 2 STALL GARAGE BUILDING, SAN MARCOS, TX DESCRIPTION: The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service has a multi-discipline construction project for work that requires demolition and installation of garage doors, replacement of lighting, ceiling grids/tiles, personnel doors and gutters, terminate, cap and remove existing gas lines from the building and remove and replace existing roof with new 24-gauge roofing panels at the San Marcos ARC per the Statement of Work (SOW). Please read the entire Request for Proposal (RFP) and all attachments prior to submitting quote. Solicitation number 140FC126R0017 is being issued as an RFP. This solicitation is for a Firm Fixed Price contract. This is a 100% Total Small Business Set-Aside solicitation with an associated NAICS code of 236220 - Commercial and Institutional Building Construction. This award will be made on a Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA) basis. Award will be made to the responsible Quoter whose quotation conforms to the solicitation requirements, is determined technically acceptable, and offers the lowest evaluated price. The Government will evaluate quotations for technical acceptability and price. System for Award Management (SAM) Registration: A prospective awardee shall be registered in the SAM database prior to award. Information on registration may be obtained via the Internet at https://sam.gov/content/entity-registration Proposal Submission: Proposals (including all required documents) must be submitted via e-mail to john_ferrall@ios.doi.gov, no later than June 22, 2026, 5:00 pm EDT. Please indicate the following in the subject line: Proposal Submission-140FC126R0017. Request for Information (RFI) Submission: To maintain a complete record of inquiries and questions and to provide more detailed and faster responses, all questions shall be in writing via email (NO PHONE INQUIRIES). Questions may be emailed to john_ferrall@ios.doi.gov. Inquiries received after June 8, 2026, 5:00 pm EDT, may not receive a response. If necessary, questions received will be answered through an Amendment to this Solicitation. Contracting Point of Contact: Drew Ferrall, Contract Specialist Department of the Interior US Fish and Wildlife Service john_ferrall@ios.doi.gov
