# Electrical

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 11:05:44.352-04
- Solicitation Number: W519TC26R2024

## Summary

This synopsis is for informational purposes only. This is not a request for quote and no solicitation exists. The Blue Grass Army Depot (BGAD), located in Richmond KY, has a requirement for Blanket Purchase Agreement(s) for electrical maintenance and repairs to explosion-proof electrical systems and components. The North American Industry Classification System or NAICS code is 238210 Electrical Contractors and other wiring installers with a small business size standard of $19M. The agency will be placing Master Blanket Purchase Agreements (BPAs) with the following vendors: Hepburn Consultants, Inc., and Intersteel Group, Inc. Both of these companies have an extensive history with BGAD, are local small businesses, have access to all areas of the depot, a deep understanding of the explosion-proof electrical needs of the depot and the location of most if not all systems. You are welcome to submit your company’s capabilities for our review.
