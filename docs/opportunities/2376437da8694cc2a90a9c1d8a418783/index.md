## Summary

This contract award is for Kenneling Services in support of the U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Canine Enforcement Program (CEP) at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) and the Los Angeles/Long Beach (LALB) Seaport. The contract was awarded as a single-award, firm fixed-price Indefinite-Delivery, Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) contract under full and open competition, using FAR Part 12 procedures for commercial services.
