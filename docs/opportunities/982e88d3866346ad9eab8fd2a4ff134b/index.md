GENERAL INFORMATION Title: Desktop OCT (Optical Coherence Tomography) for non-invasive imaging of biological tissues Background Information: OCT (Optical Coherence Tomography) imaging provides non-invasive approach for 3D optical section to exam structure and integrity of live biological tissues. While this approach is widely used in eye clinics and vision research labs for study structure and function of the eye, Thorlabs recently developed a desktop version to image other biological samples, including cell and tissue cultures, organoids, and small live animals. National Eye Institute Visual Function Core is seeking to purchase this instrument to expand research capacities for intramural research communities.
