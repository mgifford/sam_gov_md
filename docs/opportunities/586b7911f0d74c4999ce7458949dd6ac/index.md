# Flameholder Repair Services, 144th Fighter Wing

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 11:41:22.845-04
- Solicitation Number: W50S6T26QA011

## Summary

The 144th Fighter Wing at Fresno Air National Guard Base, Fresno CA, is synopsizing a requirement for repair services for flameholders used in F100 engine which powers the F-15C/D fighter jets operated by 144 FW. Work shall consist of furnishing all labor, tools, and equipment necessary to repair F100 flameholder parts to serviceable condition in support of the Air Force Enhancement Program (AFREP); and in accordance with technical orders (TO); and special qualifications identified elsewhere in the performance work statement (PWS). The service provider must have an approved Source of Approved Repair (SAR) letter certified by the MAJCOM AFREP Manager. The service provider shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary. A draft PWS is attached to this announcement for reference.
