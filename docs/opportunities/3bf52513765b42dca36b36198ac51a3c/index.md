# Concrete pad and walkway installation at the YEH

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 02:03:38.629-04
- Solicitation Number: 19KS7026Q0031

## Summary

Please note that this solicitation is not ready to be issued as of this date. The U.S. Embassy in Seoul, Republic of Korea, requires design and installation services for a pedestrian walkway and concrete pad at the field located within the Yongsan Embassy Housing (YEH) Compound in Yongsan-gu, Seoul, Korea. All responsible sources may submit a quotation, which will be considered by the Contracting Officer. The Government plans to issue solicitation in May 2026. The solicitation will be posted on https://www.sam.gov and the Embassy website. No paper copies will be made available. Please see the RFQ for the actual response date and for specific quotation requirements. Please refer to attached file for further detail.
