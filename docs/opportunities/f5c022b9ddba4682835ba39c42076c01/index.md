# Siemens maintenance services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 09:55:48.463-04
- Solicitation Number: PANDHA26P0000039731

## Summary

The Defense Health Agency Contracting Activity (DHACA), Healthcare Contracting Division-Southeast (Europe) (HCD-SE-Europe) – Landstuhl, Germany, in support of the Europe Command, will be issuing on a competitive Firm Fixed Price Purchase Order. This requirement is for a non-personal services contract to furnish all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, and other items necessary to perform Siemens maintenance services, including the comprehensive preventive maintenance and repair of diagnostic imaging equipment utilized at Landstuhl Regional Medical Center. The anticipated period of performance includes a base year followed by four (4) consecutive 12-month option periods. This announcement constitutes the formal synopsis, with the complete Solicitation and Performance Work Statement (PWS) scheduled for release on or around 15 May 2026. Interested vendors are advised to monitor SAM.gov and refer to the final solicitation for specific details regarding technical requirements and submission instructions.
