## Summary

SUBJECT: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation for the Cleveland Range model No. SGL40T1 40-Gal Tilting Skillet Braising Pan for the Nutrition Service Department at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center (WRNMMC) This requirement is in support of Walter Reed National Military Medical Center in Bethesda, Maryland for procurement of Cleveland Range model No. SGL40T1 40-Gal tilting skillet braising pan. The DHA Healthcare Division - Northeast intends to award one Firm Fixe Price contract in accordance with FAR Part 12- Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services to the responsible offeror whose offer conforming to the solicitation will be most advantageous to the Government, price and other factors considered. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; proposals are being requested and a written solicitation will not be issued. This solicitation document incorporates provisions and clauses from Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), FAC 2026-01; supplemental Regulations from the Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS), DFARS Change 11/10/2025; and supplemented by the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) In accordance with Department of War (DoW) Procurement Directive. To be accepted and eligible for evaluation, quotations must be prepared in compliance with these instructions and all the terms and conditions contained in this document. The Government intends to evaluate offers and award a contract without discussions with offerors. Therefore, the offeror's initial offer should contain the offeror's best terms from a price and technical standpoint. The Offeror agrees to hold the price in its offer firm for 90 calendar days from the date specified on the Quote. The Solicitation ID for this requirement is HT001700012411872, This Requirement is being solicited as a 100% small business set-aside under NAICS code 333241 – Food Product Machinery Manufacturing. The table below provides the itemized list of materials required for this requirement, interested parties shall fill the table with applicable information as it pertains to this requirement; i.e Unit price, extended price, estimated delivery, etc. F.O.B Destination Walter Reed National Military Medical Center BLDG 54 8901 Rockville Pike Bethesda, MD 20889 Offers are due 25 May 2026, by 5:00PM EST. The Primary POCs for this requirement is Contract Specialist, Cinthia G. Funes (cinthia.g.funes.civ@health.mil). All submitted quotations for this RFQ must be submitted to both specialists by the offer due date.
