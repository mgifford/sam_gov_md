# Request for Information - Professional and Advisory Services

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 18:08:17.071-04
- Solicitation Number: 134000-26-0009

## Summary

REQUEST FOR INFORMATION BIS requires contractor support services to enable the compliance division to meet its mission as a national security–focused regulator. The purpose of this contract is to provide on-site contractor personnel, management, and expertise in support of numerous core functions. The contractor shall deliver consistent, professional, and outcomes-based services that ensure the effective execution of OICTS’s statutory and regulatory responsibilities. This Notice does not commit the Government to contract for any supply or service. This is not a Request for Proposal (RFP) and offers will not be accepted. No pricing or cost data shall be submitted. If the information is submitted, it will not be considered. The Government will not make an award resulting from this Sources Sought Notification. Any information received in response will be used to develop a potential solicitation with the intent to make a Government contract award. This notice does not guarantee the release of a solicitation. Responses to the notice will not be disclosed outside the Government; however, Respondents shall consider information received may be used to shape a future public solicitation and therefore avoid providing proprietary information that could otherwise be disclosed through the solicitation process. Responses to this notice are voluntary and costs will not be reimbursed. No pricing or cost data shall be submitted. If the information is submitted, it will not be considered. The due date for responses to this notice is Monday May 11, 2pm Eastern Time to Jameka Anderson (JAnderson1@doc.gov).
