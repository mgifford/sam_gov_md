## Summary

This pre-solicitation notice is for informational purposes only. This is not a request for quote and no solicitation exists. The Blue Grass Army Depot (BGAD), located in Richmond KY, has a requirement for Blanket Purchase Agreement(s) for Water, Wastewater and Boiler Chemicals and Repairs as well at other misc. parts. The North American Industry Classification System or NAICS code is 221320 Sewage Treatment Facilities with a small business size standard of 35.0M. The agency will be placing a Sole Source Master Blanket Purchase Agreements (BPAs) with NAC Heavy Highway Inc. They are a local small business fully cleared for access to BGAD’s Restricted Area, Technical Expertise to repair both our Wastewater/Water Treatment Facility and all of our Boiler Systems to include a long history of repairs and locations of all equipment for these reasons, it only makes sense to Award to NAC Heavy Highway Inc. You may submit your company’s capability for consideration.
