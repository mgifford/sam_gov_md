## Summary

This is a COMBINED SYNOPSIS/SOLICITATION for commercial items prepared in accordance with format in FAR Subpart 13; simplified acquisition procedures apply. VA will conduct the procurement according to a combination of FAR Part 12, Acquisition of Commercial Items and Part 13, Simplified Acquisition Procedures as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. The solicitation number is 36C25626Q0761 and is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ). The proposed contract action is for Storage Units Services for Overton Brooks VA Medical Center located at 510 E Stoner Ave, Shreveport, LA 71101 for which the Government intends to solicit competitive quotes. Interested persons may submit quotes, and all eligible quotes must be received prior to the response date listed on Contract Opportunities to be considered by the Government. This solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular (FAC) 2024-02. Quotes are due electronically to rachel.babin@va.gov no later than the response date listed on Contract Opportunities. Only electronic quotes will be considered. Phone calls will not be accepted, all questions must be submitted via email to rachel.babin@va.gov NLT 11:00 AM Central Standard Time (CST) on May 15, 2026.
