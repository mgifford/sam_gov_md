No sub-contracting opportunity is anticipated. The North American Classification System (NAICS) code for this acquisition is 561612 ($25.5 Million dollars). Notice to potential offerors: All offerors who provide goods or services to the United States Federal Government must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) at www.sam.gov and complete Online Representations and Certifications Application (ORCA). All interested Offerors should submit information by e-mail to felicia.simpson@va.gov All information submissions to be marked Attn: Felicia Simpson, Contract Specialist and should be received no later than 10:00 am MST on May 13, 2026. Area of work: The project work area is on the exterior facade, south and east of Parking Structure 3. The banner installation will take place on government owned land at the Jennifer Moreno VA Medical Center. The Contractor will need to provide a cost proposal based on the Scope of Work. General Requirements: Provide all labor, materials, tools and equipment, and design-build services necessary for the fabrication, delivery and installation of the project described below and other specific tasks as further defined by this Statement of Work. Both design and construction services are parts of this project. Demolition scope shall include but not be limited to: There is no demolition associated with this project. Project Requirements: The scope of work for this project consists of furnishing and installing the following: The survey of the existing conditions at PG3 associated with the installation and attachment of the banners. The banner attachment strategy proposal for a smooth and seamless banner. The banners to be printed on durable open mesh, strong enough to resist wind loads and allow for wind and air passage. The banner installation needs to include components to be tied to existing structural elements on PG3, but not to include drilling into the existing parking garage concrete structure. Installation proposal and VASD approval is required, prior to commencement of the work. Production, delivery and installation of the banners per elevation drawing location and size. Installation of the banners to allow for future banner removal and replacement. Need to post permits in accordance with VASD Safety Office Notify the COR and cc the CO of any activity outside the working hours. A VA representative should always be present.
