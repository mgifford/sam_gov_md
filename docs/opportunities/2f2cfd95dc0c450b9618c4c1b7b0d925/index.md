## Summary

THIS IS THE OFFICIAL REQUEST FOR QUOTE (RFQ) FOR TECNAU 18" ROLL CARTS (BRAND NAME REQUIREMENT) The Internal Revenue Service (IRS), Correspondence Production Services (CPS) Detroit, MI requires the purchase of 75 Tecnau 18" Roll Carts. The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) prints, inserts and mails correspondence to taxpayers and corporations from three (3) IRS Correspondence Production Services (CPS) locations (Detroit, MI; Bloomington, IL and Ogden, UT). The IRS uses roll carts to provide easy transport of rolls weighing 1,000lbs from a pallet to the printing machine unwinder, eliminating manual lifting. Cart height matches the standard pallet for easy transfer. Rolls never touch the floor, keeping them clean and free from damage during movement. See Attached Request For Quote (RFQ). Please review the entire RFQ and provide info as required. Vendor must be an authorized Tecnau Roll Cart reseller.
