Responders are solely responsible for all expenses associated with responding to this RFI. Statement of Work Tree Trimming & Weed Control DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES. The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, tools, supervision, transportation and other items and services necessary to ensure that all trees at the Phoenix VA are properly trimmed and debris is cleaned up and removed. At times a tree may become diseased or a danger to property or persons; when this is identified by Contractor, COR or Contracting Officer a determination may be made to remove that tree completely. Removal of Any Tree must first be in writing and approved by Contracting Officer with the determination prior to removal. Tree trimming debris should be recycled as yard waste wherever possible. Weed control services will include quarterly spray applications and two pre-emergent treatments annually to maintain long term effectiveness. APPLICABLE REFERENCES: Trees include but not limited to: Palm (including various versions such as Fan and Date but not limited to), Mesquite, Palo Verde, Torche, Brazil and other various and unknown trees. Weed control in all areas of the compound. TASKS: Trim all Palm Tree - All trees will be inspected quarterly, trimmed back and maintained. Additionally, a minimum of one time per year palm tree services shall include: 1) Trim and skin all campus palm trees. 2) Trim all old fronds and seed PODs. 3) Complete clean-up and remove all debris. The service is for approximately 60 trees. Date palm and fan palms are included. All Other Trees on Property All trees other than palm, will be inspected quarterly, trimmed back whereas branches are not rubbing or in danger of doing damage to property which includes but is not limited to all buildings and parking structures including solar panel canopies or persons. Complete clean-up and removal of all debris. Weed Control Services will include quarterly spray applications and two pre-emergent treatments annually to maintain long term effectiveness. All areas on the hospital compound will be treated. High foot traffic areas, parking lot islands, all rock areas. SCHEDULE OF WORK: The contractor shall visit site four times per year, one time per quarter. Desired months for service shall be January, April, July and October. Task Visits to the site should not affect VA operations or interfere with patient visits.
