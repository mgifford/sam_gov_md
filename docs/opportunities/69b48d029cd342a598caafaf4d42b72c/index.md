# PAE RAS June 2026 Industry Day

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-21 12:57:17.522-04

## Summary

The Navy’s Portfolio Acquisition Executive for Robotics and Autonomous Systems (PAE RAS) is announcing a 2-day PAE RAS-focused Industry Day Event. Registration for the event is open through June 2nd, through completion of the following form: https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/pQyuCARda1 What: This event is intended to inform industry, academia, and private equity partners on the PAE RAS establishment, operating model and mission. In addition, the agenda also features an interactive session on Government contracting and focused discussions on the PAE RAS architectural framework and digital environment. Location: San Diego, CA (Venue to be provided to registered attendees) Date: June 10-11, 2026 Time: June 10, 0830 – 1200 & 1300 – 1630 | June 11, 0930 - 1430 Day 1/June 10 features identical morning and afternoon plenary sessions to allow more industry partners to participate. The sessions will involve speakers and panels to discuss the generation of RAS-focused Tailored Offsets that are scalable, deployable, adaptable, and cost effective to deliver integrated capabilities to the warfighter at the speed of relevance. Registration is on a first-come first-serve basis until the event is full. Day 2/June 11 is a smaller-group event with the agenda broken into three tracks. Track One will focus on the architectural future of PAE RAS, Track Two will focus on the Digital Environment, and Track Three will focus on Contracting with the Government. Invitations to Day 2 will also be determined on a first come-first serve basis. Additional Notes: Due to space limitations, attendance from each organization will likely be limited to a maximum of two representatives. Organizations should deconflict attendees internally before submission. Additional registrations may be declined. Food/beverage will not be provided but are available on-site.
