PRELIMINARY STATEMENT OF WORK Description The Minneapolis VA Health Care System requires a services solution to automate nursing resource management processes, to include: Staff scheduling and staffing activities; real-time decision support for staffing management, enhance the availability of data and information relevant to patient care and deployment of nursing assets, provides self-scheduling options to nursing staff at multiple levels. This system will be cloud ready. Objectives Implement an automated, cloud-based, product and software management service tailored for managing nurse staffing and nursing assets throughout the facility. Match staff in numbers, skill and competency to support the acuity and needs of patients. Reduce the time and costs associated with scheduling and staffing activities. Manage budgets and costs. Accurately track staff movement. Use real-time data to support nurse staffing and nurse staff management decisions. Retrieve and organize data through a robust reports mechanism. Eliminate redundant work and improve data reliability through product interfaces with VHA data systems. Meet requirements as outlined by statute, VHA regulations and directives and local policies. Scope An integrated system and automated data extraction tool to effectively assign staffing resources and monitor resource management and utilization of nursing assets. This system will have proven ability to interface with a variety of VHA data management systems and provide minimally for unilateral data exchange. Capability for bilateral data exchange is preferred. The intent is to purchase products/services and not individual or facility software licenses. The primary users of the system will be nursing executives, nursing management, program assistants and front-line nursing staff. Tasks or Requirements Support appropriate responses to the fluctuations in patient census and acuities by using embedded decision support tools. Ensure adequate staffing numbers and skills mix based on patient acuity and other care related variables. Demonstrated ability to interface with VHA data systems for the collection, retrieval and organization of period-defined (hourly, shift, daily, monthly, annually, etc.) including, but not limited to : management of nursing resources and associated costs nursing hours (HPPD) data patient admission, discharge, and transfer activity clinically relevant reports Demonstrated ability to support compliance with public law and other regulatory and agency mandates for staffing regulations and monitors. Demonstrated ability to consolidate data from multiple sources to support multiple administrative decision functions into a single system (licensure, certifications, emergency notifications, etc.) Possess robust reporting capability including both parameters defined standardized and ad hoc reports. Demonstrated ability to accommodate staff self-scheduling based on sets of predetermined parameters. Deliverables or Deliver Schedule (Salient Characteristics) Specifications Utilizes an HL7 interface with Vista to transfer ,(unidirectional and bidirectional) real-time data including, but not limited to: Patient admission, discharge and transfer information Snapshots of inpatient census at pre-determined intervals Select staff data and information from human resource and payroll automated systems Capable of cloud computing with VHA approved entities. Vendor offers product as a service with no requirement to purchase actual software licenses. Vendor is responsible for support, general maintenance, fixes-patches, interface and updates installations which are included in the service cost. Vendor supplied 24/7 support. Required functional capabilities: Staff Scheduling Tools: demographic, skills and competency tracking tools create and maintain prospective schedules create schedules in increments of 6 weeks with flexibility for fewer or more weeks. create prospective schedules for, at a minimum 12 months. provide real-time staffing provide for auditing of changes made in the product by users including date/time and name of individual making changes provide archival functions allow staff to sign up for open and overtime shifts with notifications via email, text or users preferred method of communication. allow shift swapping among peers provides a module or function that specifically allows supervisors to view , interact and monitor shift schedules from a central or birds-eye vantage point. accommodate multi-level permissions-based access manage position control Staff Evaluation/Performance Monitoring Syst ability to maintain repositories of anecdotal notes for individual staffs performance evaluations ability to track: licensure currency, specialty certifications, skills-based competencies and education Tools for staff to self- report information into a professional portfolio. Rule engines to facilitate scheduling: apply regulations hour limits, time off, requirements, staffing ratios, etc) as they apply to individuals being scheduled to work: apply union requirements stored in the system to create staffing schedules use staff preferences to create staffing schedules. match competencies to staffing needs. match nursing staff with patient needs. apply unit preference to competencies, e.g., ICU nurses are ACLS certified. apply permanent schedules requests to when generating schedules. apply rotating shifts rules when generating schedules. accommodate scheduling changes based on patient census apply patient acuities to staffing needs track hours per patient day generate alternate work schedules allow or restrict swapping unit assignments based on competencies. restrict overtime assignment based on defined rules/regulations predict staffing needs based on previous trends (eg. high surgery days) provide functionality to maintain disaster preparedness (notifications and callbacks). Communication Tools: creates a personalized web-based dashboard for all stakeholders web-Based for employee access web-based for manager access provide options for staff bidding on open positions (Staff to staff communications (e.g. to swap shifts) produce reports for budge, operations and strategic planning.
