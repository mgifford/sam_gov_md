# POM Strategic Prioritization Training and Tool

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-27 15:16:17.204-04
- Solicitation Number: FA6643-26-POM-INDUSTRYDAY

## Summary

THIS IS A VIRTUAL INDUSTRY DAY ONLY. This event is intended for information and planning purposes only – it does not constitute a Commercial Solutions Opening (CSO) or a promise to issue a CSO in the future. This Notice does not commit the Government to contract for any supply or service. The Air Force Reserve is not seeking proposals at this time and will not accept unsolicited proposals. Responders are advised that the Government will not pay for any information or administrative costs incurred in response to this event; all costs associated with responding to this event will be solely at the interested party’s expense. Not participating or responding to this event does not preclude participation in any future CSO, if any is issued. HQ AFRC/PKA will host a Virtual Industry Day to provide industry partners with an overview of the POM Strategic Prioritization Training and Tool requirement. This event serves the purpose of conducting market research to identify capable sources, as well as solicit feedback on the draft Statement of Objectives and other topics. Reference Sources Sought/Request for Information Notice ID FA6643-26-RFI-POM on SAM.gov for additional information regarding this effort. Event Date/Time: 4 Jun 2026 / 0900-1100 EST Format: Virtual via Microsoft Teams Tentative Agenda: 0900 – 0915 – Opening Remarks 0915 – 0930 – Statement of Objectives Overview 0930 – 1100 – Questions and Answers Registration: All participants must register NO LATER THAN 03 JUN 2026 BY 1500 EST by completing the information at the following link: https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/ckNQb1CkQt Registration is limited to 3 representatives per company. Questions: Questions may be submitted ahead of time NO LATER THAN 01 JUN 2026 BY 1500 EST by emailing them to the Primary and Alternate POCs. Transcripts of all questions and government responses, as well as the attendance roster will be published to SAM.gov following the event.
