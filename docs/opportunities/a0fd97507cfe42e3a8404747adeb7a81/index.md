# Synopsis: Cavalier Fire Protection and Emergency Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 12:34:07.104-04
- Solicitation Number: FA465926R0001

## Summary

The 319th Contracting Squadron intends to issue a request for proposal (RFP) FA4659-26-R-0001 for a commercial service of Fire Protection and Emergency Services at Cavalier Space Force Station (CSFS), North Dakota. The contractor is to furnish all labor, tools, equipment, materials (other than those specified as furnished by the Government), and supervision necessary to perform Fire Protection and Emergency Services on a 24-hour 7 days a week basis, and in accordance with the performance work statement. A formal solicitation will be issued on or around 1 June 2026. The solicitation will result in a single, firm-fixed price contract. The contract will be awarded with a base period of 1 November 2026 - 31 October 2027, with four, twelve (12) month option periods to follow. The total period of performance, given all options exercised, will be 1 November 2026 - 31 October 2031. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is 561990, All Other Support Services, and the small business size standard is $16.5M. This acquisition will be solicited as a total small business set-aside. The requirement is subject to a Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) in conjunction with the International Association of Firefighters, Local I-18. A formal RFP will be issued via sam.gov. Paper copies of the solicitation will not be mailed. Prospective offerors are responsible for checking sam.gov to obtain the solicitation and any amendments or other information pertaining to the solicitation. All offerors MUST be register in the System for Award Management (SAM) data base or their proposal MAY NOT be considered for award. Any questions can be directed to SrA Jackelyn Richardson at jackelyn.richardson.1@us.af.mil and Ms. Melissa Pearce at melissa.pearce.1@us.af.mil.
