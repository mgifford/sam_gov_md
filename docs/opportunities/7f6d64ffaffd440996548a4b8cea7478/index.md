# SOURCES SOUGHT - Storm Drain Study Cantonment Area Phase 2

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-19 12:20:06.375-04
- Solicitation Number: FA461026Q2222

## Summary

1.0 Description This is a Request for Information (RFI) for market research purposes only. 30 CONS is seeking to identify sources capable of providing cleaning and surveying of storm drains across the Vandenberg Space Force Base cantonment area. The information provided in response to this RFI will be used to assess the marketplace and inform a potential future acquisition. This is not a solicitation for proposals, a request for quotations, or an invitation for bids. The Government will not award a contract based on this RFI, nor will it reimburse any costs associated with preparing or submitting a response.

2.0 Background This requirement is for a contractor to provide all management, tools, supplies, equipment, and labor necessary to perform cleaning, surveying, and reporting for the storm drain system located in the Cantonment Area at Vandenberg Space Force Base, California. The scope includes performing CCTV inspections, updating geospatial (GIS) data, providing drawings of the surveyed culverts, and delivering a comprehensive written study report. All interested vendors should look at the attached PWS and appendices for more information.

3.0 Submission Instructions Interested parties are requested to submit a response electronically to Chase French at chase.french@spaceforce.mil and Kristofer Clark at kristofer.clark@spaceforce.mil no later than 27 May 2026, 1:30 PM PDT. Please ensure the subject line of your email reads: "Response to RFI FA461026Q2222 – Storm Drain Study Cantonment Area Phase 2" Your response should include: Company Name and Address. Point of Contact (Name, Title, Email, and Phone Number). CAGE Code and UEI Number. Business Size (e.g., Small Business). A brief statement of capability (not to exceed 5 pages) detailing your ability to meet the requirements listed in the PWS. Please ensure the subject line of your email reads: "Response to RFI FA461026Q2222 – Storm Drain Study Cantonment Area Phase 2"

5.0 Disclaimer This RFI does not constitute a commitment, implied or otherwise, that a procurement action will follow. All information received will be treated as public knowledge and will not be considered proprietary unless marked as such. The Government reserves the right to use the information provided to develop a future solicitation.
