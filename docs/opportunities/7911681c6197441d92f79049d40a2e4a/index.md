# NetApp System Health Check Services at Goodfellow AFB

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 16:18:35.7-04
- Solicitation Number: FA303026Q0017

## Summary

This notice is a Request for Quotation under a total small business set-aside for a Base + 4 option years service at Goodfellow AFB, Texas. Description of Services: The 17 TRSS requires semi-annual, on-site NetApp System Health Check Services for equipment residing on the Intelligence Training System - Operations (ITS-OPS) and Unclassified Training System (UTS) networks. Due to the nature of these systems, all performing personnel must possess a Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) security clearance. Additionally, the vendor must be an authorized reseller of the NetApp Health Check Service (Part # PS-TM-CONSULT-DAY-TE-ZA), and shall adhere to manufacturer’s recommended methods. Please review the attached Documents for more information. Instructions: Please review the documents attached for more information and submit all questions by 4 June 2026 at 10:00 AM CDT. Please submit your quotation to SrA Mouhamed Sarr at mouhamed.sarr.1@us.af.mil AND at TSgt Gregory Mangio at gregory.mangio.1@us.af.mil by 12 June 2026 at 12:00 PM CDT
