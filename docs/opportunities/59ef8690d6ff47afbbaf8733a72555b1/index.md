# Pyrotechnics

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 11:52:26.165-04
- Solicitation Number: DJA-26-AEET-PR-0290_0001

## Summary

ATF requires the purchase of pyrotechnic items in support of the Advanced Explosives Disposal Techniques (AEDT) training program. The products identified under this requirement are commercial fireworks classified as Hazard Class 1.3G for shipping and transportation purposes. The specific item dimensions identified in this requirement are critical to the safe execution of training exercises and associated counter-charge calculations. Utilization of alternative sizes or configurations would require continual reconfiguration of each training evolution to ensure compliance with established range limitations, safe separation distances, and counter-charge effectiveness requirements necessary for the safe disposal of the items. Accordingly, the specified dimensions are considered essential to maintaining operational safety, training consistency, and mission effectiveness. This procurement is unrestricted and will be a single-award Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP). Contractor(s) shall provide a firm-fixed price quote and complete technical evaluation. Contractors will be evaluated using the following NAICS 32599 and PSC 1370. The vendors shall use the table in the solicitation to form their quote. Attachment A – Pricing Table and return with vendor quote (attached).
