# Gila National Forest Motor Grader Mulcher with Boom

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 09:55:16.516-04
- Solicitation Number: 12444326Q0031

## Summary

Description This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12444326Q0031 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for the procurement of one (1) motor grader mulcher with boom for vegetation trimming operations on National Forest System roads. The mulcher will be operated on side of a motor grader to cut and trim vegetation alongside forest roads. The equipment shall provide all needed accessories to be installed on forest service motor grader to provide cutting of vegetation along roads. The equipment shall be new, unused, and of current production. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 333120 The small business size standard is 1,250 employees. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
