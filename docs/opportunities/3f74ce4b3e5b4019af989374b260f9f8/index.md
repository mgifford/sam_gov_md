# Canine Kenneling and Veterinary Service - Charleston, SC

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 14:38:02.847-04
- Solicitation Number: 70B03C26Q00000075

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis and solicitation for Kenneling and Veterinary Services on an as needed, daily basis, for a facility located within twenty (20) miles of the Charleston Area Port, 200 E. Bay Street, Charleston, SC 29401, with a responsible contractor. The current requirement is for one (1) dog; however, this number may fluctuate depending on the requirements of the work, and the quantity of dogs may increase or decrease over the duration of the service period. This requirement is a total set-aside for small business under NAICS 812910. The successful quoter will receive a single-award BPA for a five (5) year service period. See the attached Request for Quote 70B03C26Q00000075 package for the full synopsis and solicitation.
