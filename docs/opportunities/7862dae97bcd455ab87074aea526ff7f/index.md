# Family Advocacy Program (FAP) Annual Training

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 04:02:15.001-04
- Solicitation Number: W564KVQA035

## Summary

The Contractor shall provide all nonpersonal services necessary to deliver a four-day Family Advocacy Program (FAP) training for IMCOM Europe staff at the Edelweiss Lodge in Garmisch, Germany, in accordance with the Performance Work Statement (PWS). The Contractor shall furnish all required personnel, instructors, facilitators, airfare for all instructors and facilitators, travel, lodging, supplies, materials, and any automated data processing equipment (ADPE) the Contractor requires to perform the services. The Contractor shall also provide any additional resources necessary to conduct the onsite training.
