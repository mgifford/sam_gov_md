# Z--GOSP 315426, Rehabilitate Asphalt Pavement Parking Areas

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 13:51:48.425-04
- Solicitation Number: 140P2026R0044

## Summary

The work includes parking lot, drive aisle, and roadway improvements at four (4) locations within Golden Spike National Historic Park. Work specifically includes: pavement rehabilitation, new concrete sidewalk and ADA parking stalls, curbing repair, minor shed demolition, placement of asphalt millings, minor grading work, curb stop installation, and new MUTCH signage.
