# ZION- Crawford Dam Removal and Diversion

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 12:38:51.624-04
- Solicitation Number: 140P6426B0008

## Summary

SOLICITATION NOTICE: The National Park Service, Central Contracting Operations, Ohio Major Acquisition Buying Office, is issuing an Invitation for Bids (IFB) for the Crawford Dam Removal project for Zion National Park in southwest Utah. The Work includes but is not limited to partial removal and disposal of an existing concrete dam and headgate structure comprised of concrete, rock, rebar, regrading of the North Fork Virgin River for a distance of approximately 300 feet, construction of a new intake structure including a fish screen, removal and disposal of approximately 275' of existing CMP pipe and concrete manhole, installation of approximately 285' of 18"Ø ADS N-12 pipe (approximately 40' to be placed within 24"Ø CMP pipe), removal of a concrete turnout box, reseating of an existing 12" flume, approximately 150 feet of boulder embankment rip rap and installation of approximately 350' of smart ditch SOLICITATION NUMBER: 140P6426B0008 PROJECT MAGNITUDE: Between $1,000,000 and $5,000,000 TYPE OF CONTRACT: This will be a firm-fixed price contract. Construction Wage Rate Requirements for Washington County, UT will apply. North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) CODE CLASSIFICATION and SET ASIDE: The NAICS Code is 237990, and the size standard is $45 million. This project is set aside 100% for Total Small Businesses. SITE VISIT: See Section L in the solicitation for site visit information The contracting office will not provide hardcopies or email copies of the solicitation. REGISTRATIONS: In order to be awarded a contract, an offeror must have an UEI number. Also, the offeror must be registered and active in the System for Award Management (SAM), website at http://www.sam.gov, and have completed their Online Representations and Certifications at the time of the solicitation close date once the solicitation is posted. POINT OF CONTACT: Christopher Bauer, Contracting Officer, christopher_bauer@ios.doi.gov, 330-468-2500 x5.
