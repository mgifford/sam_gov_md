## Summary

The project requires converting all electrical systems from 120/208-volt, 60 Hz to 230/400-volt, 50 Hz power, masonry and carpentry repair, upgrading of plumbing systems, and painting of the residence to restore functionality to modern standard and aesthetic appeal. The contractors are advised to visit the site, verify the existing site conditions to develop their proposal.
