# Catering service for 4th of July event

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 10:07:02.451-04
- Solicitation Number: PR15995804

## Summary

The U.S. Embassy in Tirana is seeking quotes for catering service for 4th of July event. U.S. Embassy Tirana intends to award a fixed price Purchase Order with the most favorable bidder for above mentioned service. Bidder(s) will be selected based on lowest price technically acceptable criteria. Pre-event tasting will be arranged at the Embassy premises with the vendors within a reasonable price range. If you would like to participate, please send a request via email at TiranaBids@state.gov Proposals are due by May 19, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. (local time).
