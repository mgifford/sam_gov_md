# 8-Station Shower Trailer Rental

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 09:17:44.152-04
- Solicitation Number: FA301626Q0109

## Summary

AMENDMENT 1 - The purpose of this amendment is to provide an attachment that addresses and resolves questions received from contractors. Please note that inquiries requesting sensitive information or specific information about other requirements will not be addressed. This is a combined Synopsis/Solicitation for the rental of a quantity of one (1) 8-Station Shower Trailer. The Contractor shall deliver one (1) 8-Station Shower Trailer with a door or wall separation, leaving four (4) showers on each side. The trailer must allow for clear designation of separate male and female shower areas. The Contractor shall deliver the trailer to the designated area with all related showers and electrical components in good working order. Repairs will be performed as necessary to ensure the trailer is placed reliably and effectively back into service. The contractor shall clean the trailer two (2) times weekly and service/pump wastewater twice (2) weekly. The contractor shall furnish all supervision, transportation, and equipment for the rental of an 8-Station Shower Trailer. The Contractor shall deliver the trailer to the designated area with all related showers and electrical components in good working order. Repairs will be performed as necessary to ensure the trailer is placed reliably and effectively back into service. The contractor shall clean the trailer two (2) times weekly and service/pump wastewater twice (2) weekly. Please see attached STATEMENT OF WORK for full requirement details. Vendors shall respond to this RFQ by the deadline posted by reviewing and filling out the attached RFQ Document and submit via email to both the POC’s indicated below: mario.hernandez.19@us.af.mil & norena.phillips@us.af.mil QUESTIONS/CONCERNS must be submitted to the above POC’s NO LATER THAN Monday, 11 May 2026 at 4:00PM CDT. Questions/concerns submitted after the deadline noted above will not be accepted nor considered. Please refer to all attachments for additional information. RFQ FA301626Q0109 Statement of Work Shower Trailer Rental 8 May 2026
