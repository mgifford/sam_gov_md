# sUAS Components

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 14:39:50.077-04
- Solicitation Number: M6700126Q0043

## Summary

MCI-EAST RCO has a requirement for various sUAS components aboard Camp Lejeune, North Carolina in accordance with the specifications of the solicitation. The proposed action is set-aside for Total 100% Small Business concerns. The best suited PSC and NAICS identified for this requirement are PSC 1550 – Unmanned Aircraft and NAICS 336413 – Other Aircraft Parts and Auxiliary Equipment Manufacturing. Contractors interested in doing business with the Government must be registered in SAM. The complete Request for Quotation, including a detailed specification and component list is attached. All responsible small business sources may submit a quotation which may be considered.
