# Secure Facility Advisors

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-08 08:12:11.125-04
- Solicitation Number: 632674144

## Summary

This is an exception to fair opportunity issued due to urgent and compelling circumstances that created a sole source environment for this effort. Due to the unexpected decision to not renew the current Interagency Agreement (7600A ), there was insufficient time to complete a competitive acquisition by the need-by date and would not allow for a sufficient necessary transition period for a new contractor. By awarding this effort on a sole source basis, the Government will have the necessary lead-time to pursue a competitive follow-on and allow for a sufficient/successful transition period in Fiscal Year 2027 when a new contract can be awarded. The nature of this award is for the services of secure facility advisors and construction security technicians (CSTs) to advise the Government on facility matters, including routine maintenance and improvement projects, to support/manage critical information technology, audiovisual, and communications functions performed in secure facilities, which are required to be fully operational 24/7/365. The facility advisors routinely inspect the facilities and advise the Government on necessary repairs, improvements, and renovations.
