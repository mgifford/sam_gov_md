Sources Sought Bird monitoring at Duck and Chisik islands The U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) is conducting market research to determine the availability of qualified businesses capable of bird monitoring on Duck and Chisik Island as described in the attached statement of work. This sources sought announcement is not a request for quote or proposal and the Government is not committed to award of a purchase order or contract pursuant to this announcement.
