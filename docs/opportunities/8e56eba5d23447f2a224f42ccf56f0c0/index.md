# North Chancery Elevator Replacement, US Embassy Budapest

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 09:12:33.456-04
- Solicitation Number: 19GE5026R0066

## Summary

North Chancery Elevator Replacement, US Embassy Budapest, Hungary This is a notice of intent to award a sole source purchase order. The Department of State intends to award a sole source contract to KONE Felvono Kft, of Budapest, Hungary. The firm fixed-priced order will provide for the supply and installation of three new elevators to replace the three elevators installed in the North Chancery of the U.S. Embassy. In accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 6.103-1, the government has determined that KONE Felvono Kft is the only responsible source able to supply and install elevators that are fully integrable with the building’s other KONE elevators and its proprietary controls and firmware. This is not a request for competitive proposals and no solicitation is available. All responsible sources may submit a capability statement, which shall be considered by the agency. Interested sources must respond in writing with clear and convincing evidence to support their ability to provide the required services within fifteen (15) days of this publication. A determination by the Government not to compete this action based on responses received is solely within the discretion of the Government. Authority: (FAR) 6.103-1, Only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. Companies should respond by written correspondence, no later than Thursday, May 28th 2026,
