# Small Craft SME and Maintenance Tech Support Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-20 20:20:40.805-04
- Solicitation Number: N0060426_Small_Craft_SME_SS

## Summary

Sources Sought The Underwater Construction Team TWO (UCT-TWO) requires a contractor to provide a Small Craft Subject Matter Expert (SME) and Maintenance Technician to deliver comprehensive maintenance, repair, operational support, and training for the UCT-TWO’s small craft inventory in Port Hueneme, CA. The contractor shall be responsible for all aspects of equipment readiness, including preventative and corrective maintenance, parts management, and operator training to mitigate existing capability gaps for UCT-TWO, at Naval Base Ventura County in Port Hueneme, CA. NAVSUP Fleet Logistics Center Pearl Harbor (FLC PH) Regional Contracting Department has determined the required services are commercial in nature and intends to issue a Firm-Fixed Price Contract. This is a follow-on to N0024421P0463 with an estimated period of performance of 30 September 2026 to 29 September 2027 plus 4 – 12-month option periods. Wage Determination 2015-5625 is applicable.
