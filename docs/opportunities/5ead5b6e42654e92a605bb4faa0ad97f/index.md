# ET COLD SPRAY SMART SPEC

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 12:57:15.414-04

## Summary

The Air Force intends to solicit and award a firm fixed price, Sole Source purchase order to RELIACOAT TECHNOLOGIES LLC Bohemia, NY; CAGE Code (5l4D4), under the authority of RFO 5.101(a) for ET COLD SPRAY SMART SPEC ICP-8 and ICP-9 Sensors - Execute pilot program to fully leverage data from new technology to improve spray dynamics, train operators, develop new spray parameters, and further lab testing of ICP-8 and ICP-9 sensors coupons ISO 76 PMXG Cold Spray efforts for the Oklahoma City Air Logistics Complex (OC-ALC) for the PERFORMANCE PERIOD OF 15 MAY 2026 15 MAY 2029. The NAICS code for this requirement is 541380 with a size standard of $19M. Contractor registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) – which results in receiving a unique, active CAGE Code – is required prior to receiving any contracts or orders from the Government in accordance with RFO 4.203-1. Contractors can register electronically at https://sam.gov/ . Interested persons may identify their interest and capability to respond to the requirement by submitting a quote and information. Electronic procedures will be used for this solicitation through SAM at https://sam.gov/. No telephone requests.
