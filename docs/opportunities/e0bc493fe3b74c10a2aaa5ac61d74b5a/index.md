- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 00:58:27.616-04

## Summary

Misawa Air Base is seekig qualified vendors to provide a non-personal service. The contractor shall, under the guidance of the Protestant Worship Service Chaplain(s), serve as the primary music director to provide choral and congregational musical direction in support of and meet the needs of a weekly Protestant (Sunday) Worship Service at the Misawa AB Chapel, Bldg. 358 IAW attached PWS.
