## Summary

The contractor shall provide, but not be limited to, 33,280 phlebotomist hours per year with the option to increase by an additional 11,050 hours by contract modification as location needs demand for health care services listed in the contract for Veteran beneficiaries of the Department of Veterans Affairs, located at the Michael E. DeBakey VA Medical Center (MEDVAMC), 2002 Holcombe Boulevard, Houston, TX 77030.
