# Bruker Maldi-Tof Equipment Maintenance

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-28 16:57:37.161-04
- Solicitation Number: BAC260003

## Summary

The MALDI-TOF bioanalyzer provides an important capability for the identification of bacterial antigens collected from multiple Peruvian, Honduran and other study sites. Preventive maintenance is essential for the proper operation of the equipment and needs to be conducted on an annual basis per manufacturer guidance. The service has to be at Lima, Peru.
