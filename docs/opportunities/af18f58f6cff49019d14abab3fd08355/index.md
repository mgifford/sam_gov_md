# Kavo Dental Handpiece Repair Biomedical Technical Training

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-13 15:23:58.258-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C25726Q0563

## Summary

VA Texas Valley Coastal Bend Healthcare System is requesting Technical Training for VA Dental Handpiece repair in support of Dental Service. This course will benefit the VCB Biomedical department staff. The course program must provide the Biomedical staff the core fundamentals, preventative maintenance, component level troubleshooting, and repair. The technical training will be facilitated by Kavo Certified instructor for all Training & Repair technical courses and must be able to provide the complete preventative maintenance and repair training needed. Training course will be for 2 Biomedical staff to attend and must be onsite in-person training. The training provided must allow for 2 VA Biomedical staff to complete the technical requirements for onsite Biomed level service maintenance and repairs for VCB Dental Kavo Handpieces.
