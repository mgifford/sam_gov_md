# Roads/Pavements & Misc. Civil Work Indefinite- Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) Contract, Misawa Air Base, Japan

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 21:07:19.291-04
- Solicitation Number: FA520526R0001

## Summary

The scope of the contract encompasses repair, maintenance and construction of pavement structures such as roads and parking lots, and related work including but not limited to curb/gutter, drainage pipes, manhole, earthwork, sidewalks, markings, traffic signs, restoration and demolition of structure/obstacle, and other miscellaneous work in Misawa Air Base (MAB), Japan and locations within 40 km radius from MAB, including its associated sites of Hachinohe Fuel Terminal, with the exception of the MAB Airfield and Draughon Range.
