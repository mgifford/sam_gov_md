# Crane and Rigger Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 16:07:01.406-04
- Solicitation Number: W911SG26QA036

## Summary

A follow-on requirement to provide all resources and certified personnel necessary to perform crane, rigging, and 18-wheeler flat-bed transport to load and unload Japanese HAWK RADAR equipment, missiles, and sea-vans at McGregor Range, NM in accordance with the performance work statement (PWS). This action is a follow-on requirement to contracts W911SG-25-P-A023 and W911SG-19-P-0065 and is a 100% small business set-aside..
