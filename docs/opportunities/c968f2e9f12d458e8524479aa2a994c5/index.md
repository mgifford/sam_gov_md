## Summary

The United States Embassy in Kingston, Jamaica invites quotations from authorized resellers/dealers for the supply and delivery of Variable Frequency Drives that meets the below minimum specifications. EATON SVX050A1-4A1B1 x 3 each Adjustable Frequency Drive, 3-Phase Independent, Series: SVX9000, 380/500/480 VAC, 72/87 A, 50 hp, 9.3 in Width, 10.1 in Depth, 24.8 in Height, NEMA 1/IP21/Open Enclosure, LCD Display, Panel Mounting EATON SVX040A1-4A1B1 x 1 each Adjustable Frequency Drive, 3-Phase Independent, Series: SVX9000, 380/500/480 VAC, 61/75 A, 40 hp, 9.3 in Width, 10.1 in Depth, 24.8 in Height, NEMA 1/IP21/Open Enclosure, LCD Display, Panel Mounting EATON SVX030A1-4A1B1 x 2 each Adjustable Frequency Drive, 3-Phase Independent, Series: SVX9000, 380/500/480 VAC, 46/61 A, 30 hp, 10-1/2 in Width, 25 in Depth, 11.8 in Height, NEMA 1/IP21/Open Enclosure, LCD Display, Panel Mounting EATON SVX010A1-4A1B1 x 4 each Adjustable Frequency Drive, 3-Phase Independent, Series: SVX9000, 380/500/480 VAC, 16/23 A, 10 hp, 5.6 in Width, 8.4 in Depth, 16-1/2 in Height, NEMA 1/IP21/Open Enclosure, LCD Display, Panel Mounting Delivery Terms Delivery shall be FOB Destination - SUVI Global Services - Doral, FL. 33178. US. The Contractor shall be responsible for all costs associated with packaging, handling, transportation and delivery.
