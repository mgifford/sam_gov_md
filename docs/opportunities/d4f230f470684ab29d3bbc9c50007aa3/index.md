# FDA approved F-18 Fludeoxyglucose unit doses

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 15:38:04.863-04
- Solicitation Number: W81K0026QA194

## Summary

This is a supply contract to provide Dwight David Eisenhower Army Medical Center (DDEAMC) Department of Radiology (Nuclear Medicine). The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to perform preparation and delivery of an estimated four hundred and fifty (450) FDA approved F-18 FDG unit dose radiopharmaceuticals as defined in this Statement of Work except for those items specified as Government furnished property and services.
