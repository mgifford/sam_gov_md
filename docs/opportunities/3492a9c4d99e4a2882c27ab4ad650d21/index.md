# Medium-Voltage Direct Current Filter”

- Agency: ENERGY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 10:17:27.685-04
- Solicitation Number: RFP-2026-10201

## Summary

The National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR) is currently seeking proposals to add a Medium-Voltage Direct Current (MV-DC) Filter at NLR's Flatirons Campus. This setup will open a wide range of research opportunities, especially in the context of modern power systems and renewable energy integration. Key research areas will include power quality improvement, grid-forming and grid-following control, advanced control algorithms, DC microgrid research, and hardware-in-the-loop simulations. Please see RFP number RFP-2026-10201 and its eight (8) attachments for more information.
