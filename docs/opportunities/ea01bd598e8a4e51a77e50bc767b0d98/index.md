# Verizon Gap J&A

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-11 11:53:23.088-04
- Solicitation Number: 70Z07926FISD20001

## Summary

The USCG requires continued, uninterrupted service and support of the existing circuits that have not transitioned to the competitively awarded follow-on task order. Under this task order, Verizon will continue to maintain the current state of data circuits, equipment, configurations, and managed services until the follow-on task order awardee, AT&T, takes ownership of the circuits. This includes services that provide the transport, data circuits, voice circuits, wireless circuits, and satellite circuits necessary to maintain USCG operations without service interruptions.
