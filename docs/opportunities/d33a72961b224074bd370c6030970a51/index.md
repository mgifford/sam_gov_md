# Courtroom Testimony and Report Writing Training

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 20:29:53.316-04
- Solicitation Number: 70B03C26Q00000152

## Summary

This is a Combined Solitation/Synopsis Notice for commerial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with Revolutionary FAR Overhaul Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotes are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. This is Total Small Business Set-Aside announcement to request quotes for training and educational services to support the Yuma Border Patrol Station who conducts highway interdiction operations that result in currency and contraband seizures, potentially leading to criminal prosecutions and forfeiture actions. The services offered must meet at least the minimum requirements found in Attachment 1 - Statement of Work (SOW). All interested vendors shall provide a firm-fixed-price quote for professional training services to design/align, deliver, and support three (3) iterations of a Courtroom Testimony and Report Writing course to personnel from the Yuma Station Highway Interdiction Team and supervisors, and must fulfill all the requirements stated in the attached SOW. All quotes must be submitted before this contract opportunity notice closes; any quotes received after will not be considered. Attachments: Attachment 1 - Statement of Work Combined Solicitation and Synopsis_03C26Q0152_Courtroom Testimony and Report Writing Training
