# DLA Distribution Professional Analytical Services (DDPAS) ID/IQ

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 10:41:59.796-04
- Solicitation Number: SP330026D5002

## Summary

The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) is seeking to identify qualified sources capable of providing professional, technical, and analytical services to improve business operations (NAICS 541611). The primary objective is to analyze and enhance the organic storage and distribution network's ability to support sustainment strategies for the Department of War (DoW), Whole of Government (WOG), and Foreign Partners. This effort requires robust analytical capabilities, process design, network modeling, and comprehensive program management support.
