# CERFP Prepared Meal Services - JBCC, Massachusetts

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 11:13:57.806-04
- Solicitation Number: W912JD26QA014

## Summary

The New England CBRNE Enhanced Response Force Package (CERFP) has a requirement for prepared meal services to feed approximately 140 Soldiers for their three-day Collective Training event. The contractor must provide all personnel, equipment, tools, materials, supervision, and quality control necessary to provided prepared (bagged) meals as defined in section 5 of the performance work statement (PWS). This competitive solicitation is to be issued as: 100% set-aside under Small Business IAW RFO 19.104 – Small Business Set-Aside The United States Property and Fiscal Office (USPFO) Contracting Office intends to award a firm-fixed-price (FFP) purchase order, resulting from this solicitation. Period of Performance: 14 July 2026 to 16 July 2026 Location: Joint Base Cape Cod, Connery Ave, Buzzards Bay, MA 02542. Amendments: Amendments to this solicitation will be posted at https://sam.gov/content/opportunities Paper copies of the amendments will NOT be individually mailed. By registering to receive notifications on Contracting Opportunities, quoters will be notified by email of any new amendments that have been issued. Failure to acknowledge an amendment may result in your offer being considered unacceptable. Basis for Award: The Government intends to award a single purchase order to a responsible vendor whose quote represents the best value to the Government, with appropriate consideration given to three (3) evaluation factors.
