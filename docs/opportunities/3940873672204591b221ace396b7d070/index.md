2.00 EA Intelect Focus Shockwave - Cart LOCAL STOCK NUMBER: 64609 ***BRAND NAME OR EQUAL*** Statement of Work (SOW) Shockwave Treatment Machine *BRAND NAME OR EQUAL* Project Description: Acquisition and delivery of the following: Shockwave Treatment Machine (2), carts for machine (2) and probe attachments to change penetration depth (2). Background: The device emits acoustic waves whose energy can propagate through tissue and benefit patients through pain relief, vascularization, tissue regeneration and destruction of calcium deposits. It is becoming a standard of care throughout other VAs as it offers clinicians a non-invasive treatment of multiple Musculoskeletal (MSK) conditions. The service is looking to follow the lead and expand their treatment capabilities through this procurement. This procurement has been requested to be brand name or equal to the Intelect Focus Shockwave machine as it has the capabilities needed of the request, with the probe attachments that expand the care options for providers. Salient Characteristics BRAND NAME OR EQUAL : A Shockwave Treatment machine The device needs to generate penetrating acoustic waves (shockwaves) through electromagnetic (or similar) generation of extracorporeal shock waves. The generation should occur within a handpiece connected to the main machine and transmitted to the patient through a probe at the end of the handpiece. Settings to change parameters of the device and better individualize therapy included on the hardware, such as but not limited to frequency of shockwaves and energy output. The purpose and use of these shockwaves should be focused on rehabilitative purposes, with therapeutic penetration of the shockwaves intended to be a noninvasive treatment option for clinicians. Accessories should be included that allow the penetration depth and focal zone of the shockwaves be adjusted. This capability will likely be possible by way of different probe attachments and should be included if the base unit does not come supplied with these attachments. A cart should be included for easy transport of the total package. Training shall be provided at no cost or as otherwise outlined via a line item quote.
