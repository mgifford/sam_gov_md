# Acrylic Graphic Paneling and Signage

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 09:41:18.173-04
- Solicitation Number: 832674285

## Summary

Custom acrylic framing panels with drywall anchors and clips. Custom lettering, logo graphics etc. All finished products are to be shipped to the DISA Europe office located in Stuttgart, DEU. The government will be performing the installation of the acrylic paneling onsite. In order to avoid any shipping delays and reduce the likelihood of damage in transit the government will only accept quotes with shipping by air directly to the DISA Europe office in Stuttgart, DEU. The specific list of required supply items are stated on the attached EPL (attach 1). AMEND 0001- Add instructions to offerers via addendum to FAR 52.212-1. Updated FAR clause 52.212-2. AMEND 0002- Add attachment 2 DISA Logos Powerpoint document. Note: This purchase is strictly for the procurement of commodities (supplies). There are a limited number of items requiring printed logos, which will be provided in .ai format. Full color printing of these logos is required. AMEND 0003- Issued to notify offerors that submission of a copy of their sam.gov record with quotes is required. Any offeror that is not classified under reps and certs as a small business with less than 500 employees under NAICS code 339950 (Sign Manufacturing) will not be awarded this effort. The closing date is extended per multiple offeror requests. The new closing date is a hard date and no further extensions will be granted. Q&A Attachment Posted as an attachment.
