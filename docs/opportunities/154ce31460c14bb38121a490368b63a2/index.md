# News Monitoring Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 07:43:42.121-04
- Solicitation Number: W91QEX26QA011

## Summary

The 410th Contracting Support Brigade (CSB), Regional Contracting Office (RCO) – Miami, in support of the US Southern Command, Public Affairs, will be issuing on a competitive Firm Fixed Price Purchase Order. This requirement is to provide a non-personal services contract to furnish all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, and other items necessary to perform news monitoring services using a cloud-based subscription platform. In addition, the Contractor shall deliver an AI-enhanced web-based interface that aggregates and analyzes broadcast news and social media content. This enhanced capability will automatically generate insights, sentiment analysis, and recommendations by processing millions of data points using natural language processing and machine learning algorithms. The award is for a base year plus four (4), 12-month options. This announcement constitutes the synopsis. The complete Solicitation with the Performance Work Statement will be released on or around 6 May 2026. Vendors should refer to the solicitation for details regarding the requirements.
