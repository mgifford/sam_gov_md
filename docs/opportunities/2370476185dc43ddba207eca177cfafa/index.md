# Market Survey for Tactical Networks (TACNET) In-Service Engineering Agent (ISEA)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-13 10:55:47.839-04
- Solicitation Number: N6600127R0001

## Summary

Naval Information Warfare Center Pacific (NIWC Pacific) is issuing this Market Survey for determining Small Business capabilities and is soliciting information from potential sources to provide In-Service Engineering Agent (ISEA) support for Afloat and Ashore Tactical Networks for NIWC Pacific’s Tactical Networks Branch (Code 41250). The support will include, at a minimum, the following areas: Management, Engineering, Technical, Integrated Logistics Support (ILS), and Configuration Management. The following attachments are available: Market Survey - N6600127R0001 and Attachment 1 - Draft SOW. This notice is for information and planning purposes only and is not to be construed as a commitment by the Government and does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP). Reference number is: N66001-27-R-0001. The deadline for submission of questions is 26 May 2026, 1400 PDT. Responses to this Market Survey shall be submitted via the Forms link: https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/rP6F5XCiCV by 15 June 2026, 1400 PDT.
