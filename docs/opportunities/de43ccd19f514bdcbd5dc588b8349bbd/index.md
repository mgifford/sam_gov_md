# Hologram Licensing Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 15:47:14.245-04
- Solicitation Number: W9124D26QA136

## Summary

The Government requires non-personal services for a contractor to be the sole provider of Officially Licensed Hang Tags, Hologram Stickers, Package Seals, and Track and Trace Technologies. The service is needed to protect the Army's brand integrity, prevent trademark infringement, provide auditable tracking of licensed products, and enhance the Army's public image. The service supports the Office of the Chief of Public Affairs (OCPA). Performance shall occur at the contractor's facility. This service is commercial, as secure printing and online brand protection portal services are customarily available in the commercial marketplace.
