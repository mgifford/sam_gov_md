# Seymour Johnson Air Force Base Concessionaire Contract (Coin Operated Gaming)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 14:25:24.144-04
- Solicitation Number: AmusementConcessionaire

## Summary

This is a Sources Sought notice issued solely for market research; it is not a Request for Proposals (RFP) and does not commit the Government to award a contract. The Government is conducting research to identify interested and capable parties to provide Amusement Concessionaire Services at the Phantom Lanes Bowling Center, Seymour Johnson Air Force Base. The requirement consists of the installation and maintenance of eight (8) video game machines and one (1) claw game for a base period of 18 September 2026 through 17 September 2027, with options to extend for up to four additional years. Any resultant contract actions from this notice will follow the guidance provided in DAFI 34-301 - Nonappropriated Funds Personnel Management and Adminstration Please see attached draft SOW for more information. Responses will be due by May 22nd to the listed POCs.
