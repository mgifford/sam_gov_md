# Lake Alva Campground Improvements

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 16:31:22.621-04
- Solicitation Number: 1240LU26Q0055

## Summary

Lolo National Forest Alva Lake Camground improvements. The Campground is approximately 60 miles north of Missoula, MT along highway 83. This project replaces the existing water system, electrical system, and re-paves a loop of the Lake Alva Campground. The magnitude of the project is between 500,000 and 1,000,000.
