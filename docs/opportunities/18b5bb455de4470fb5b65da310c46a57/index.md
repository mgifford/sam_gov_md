# Sydor Technologies

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 09:57:49.273-04
- Solicitation Number: SHOP-PR-26-001538

## Summary

The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) intends to negotiate a one-time, sole source, fixed price contract with Sydor Instruments LLC, 78 Schuyler Baldwin Dr., Fairport, New York 14450-9100. The purpose of this acquisition is to procure a Ballistic Yaw Sensor System and Velocity Screen systems, etc. for the FBI. The FBI has prepared a Sole Source justification on a basis of other than full and open competition for the acquisition of the ballistic system needed. A copy of this sole source justification is available upon request. Sydor Instruments LLC is the sole manufacturer of these devices and does not authorize any other vendor to resell.
