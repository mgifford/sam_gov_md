## Summary

The Unaccompanied Alien Children Bureau (UACB), within the Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR), requires continued services to support the operation, maintenance, and administration of its Learning Management System (LMS), a mission-critical platform used to deliver, track, and manage required training for Federal staff and external care providers supporting the Unaccompanied Alien Children (UAC) program.
