## Summary

The U.S. Department of Agriculture Natural Resources Conservation Service has a requirement for Easement Restoration and Emergency Watershed Protection Obstruction Removal and Disturbed Land Revegetation services in the state of South Dakota. The anticipated length of this Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract is five years, subject to Government needs and requirements, each task order issued under this IDIQ contract shall have its own specific period of performance. This request is for a Firm-Fixed Price Quote for USDA-NRCS SOUTH DAKOTA STATE OFFICE.
