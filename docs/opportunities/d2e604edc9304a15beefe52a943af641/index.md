# Sources Sought for Family Connection Services at Rock Island Arsenal

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-11 12:01:10.85-04
- Solicitation Number: W519TC-26-R-FCRI

## Summary

This is a Sources Sought Notice and NOT a solicitation announcement. The purpose of this notice is to gain knowledge of potential qualified sources to perform Family Connection Services in support of US Army Garrison-Rock Island Arsenal (USAG-RI), as detailed in the attached Performance Work Statement (PWS). The requirement is for direct group-based services provided to military families in the Quad City Area. These services include dissemination of parenting material, facilitation of a weekly playgroup, and a monthly fatherhood activity. The U.S. Army Contracting Command-Rock Island (ACC-RI) is seeking sources only. ACC-RI intends to consider all capability statements when developing its finalized acquisition strategy and resulting solicitation(s). Please submit all capability statements as outlined below by 11:00 A.M. CDT on 18 May 2026 by email to sydney.e.johnson18.civ@army.mil and kathleen.m.ward20.civ@army.mil. This is for information only which may be used for planning purposes and should not be construed as a Request for Quote or as an obligation on the part of the Government. The Government does not intend to award a contract on the basis of this request or otherwise pay for the information requested. ACC-RI will only utilize the information you provide in developing its acquisition approach for future requirements. Please provide a capability statement to include information about your ability to perform this requirement, making sure to address the following areas: 1. Vendor Information: Provide your Size Standard, CAGE Code, and Unique Entity Identification (UEI). 2. Personnel Qualifications: Confirm that all employees providing direct services to families have a bachelor’s degree in social work, psychology, human services, or family services and a minimum of two years’ experience in working with parents, children, and families? 3. Personnel Certification: Confirm if employees and/or supervisors providing direct service are certified as a Parent Educator through a program such as Healthy Families or America, Parents as Teachers, or another reputable program. 4. Relevant Experience: Describe experience with the various elements listed in the Performance Work Statement.
