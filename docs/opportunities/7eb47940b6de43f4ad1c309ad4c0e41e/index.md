# Agilent PMA

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-28 13:00:37.541-04
- Solicitation Number: PR-26-001527

## Summary

PLEASE READ THIS NOTICE CAREFULLY, AS IT IS THE ONLY NOTICE THAT WILL BE PUBLISHED. The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) intends to award a firm?fixed?price preventative maintenance contract to Agilent Technologies, 2850 Centerville Rd, Wilmington, DE 19808?1610. The contract will provide OEM maintenance services and software updates for a twelve (12) month period. The FBI Laboratory Division (LD) requires OEM maintenance to preserve warranty coverage for the Agilent Bravo liquid handling robot and Agilent PlateLoc instruments. Only Agilent?certified engineers are authorized to service these systems, access proprietary software, and supply OEM parts. Third?party service is not permitted and would void the manufacturer’s warranty. Market research confirms that Agilent Technologies is the sole source capable of meeting these requirements. A Justification for Other Than Full and Open Competition is available upon request. This notice is posted exclusively on SAM.gov. The applicable NAICS code is 334516 (Analytical Laboratory Instrument Manufacturing), and the PSC is J066 (Maintenance/Repair/Rebuild of Instruments and Laboratory Equipment). The Government intends to negotiate with only one source under FAR 6.302?1, Only One Responsible Source. This notice is not a request for competitive quotations. However, in accordance with FAR 5.207(16)(ii), responsible sources may submit a capability statement, proposal, or quotation for consideration. Submissions must be emailed to: Adelle Bolton, Contracting Officer Email: albolton@fbi.gov Deadline: 5:00 PM EST, June 10, 2026 Responses must include: Clear and convincing evidence that competition would benefit the Government The company’s Unique Entity ID (UEI) Business size designation Information received will be used solely to determine whether a competitive procurement is appropriate. No reimbursement will be provided for any costs associated with responding to this notice. This notice does not obligate the Government to issue a solicitation or award a contract. Vendors must be registered in SAM.gov. The FBI’s financial system interfaces directly with SAM; therefore, vendors must ensure that all SAM registration information—including TIN, EFT, UEI, addresses, and points of contact—is accurate. EFT information in SAM will be used for payment processing.
