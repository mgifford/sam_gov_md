## Summary

The Contractor shall provide all management, tools, supplies, equipment, and labor necessary to perform all work in accordance with these specifications. Work includes the design and installation and removal of fencing and replacement of the fences at Base Supply building 158, the Thrift Store, building 530, Kaye Auditorium building 708, the Base Chapel building 712, and the Security Forces building 926, on Columbus AFB., Ms. All materials will be contractor furnished. This general outline of work does not, in any way, limit the responsibility of the Contractor to perform all work as specified herein. NO ADVANCED PAYMENTS WILL BE MADE FOR THIS CONTRACT.
