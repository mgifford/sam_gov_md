## Summary

Solicitation: 191NLE26Q0074 - INL Colombia Navy AI Equipment Background: The U.S. Embassy's International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs (INL) Section in Bogotá, Colombia, seeks to support the Colombian Navy in implementing an artificial intelligence (AI) server-based architecture system designed to enhance interdiction capabilities. Colombia produces an estimated 2,600 metric tons of cocaine annually, with the majority smuggled out of the country via riverine corridors leading to the Pacific Ocean and Caribbean Sea. The computer hardware and software outlined in this Statement of Work will provide the Colombian Navy the tools necessary to analyze large amounts of operational data.
