# Mass Comparator

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 12:43:05.937-04
- Solicitation Number: PANRSA26P0000033349

## Summary

Purchase Description Procurement of Manual Mass Comparators The U.S. Army TMDE Activity (USATA) has a requirement to purchase three (3) Mass Comparators models or equivalent. These units are needed as a life-cycle replacement for an existing model that is becoming inoperable. The unit shall be delivered to USATA’s Army Physical Standards Labs located at Building 5435, Fowler Road Redstone Arsenal. The Mass Comparators will also include the additional measurements to support USATA’s mission as follow:

1.0 Requirements: • Mass Comparator Model XPR6U (6g) Maximum measurement capability of 6.1g, with a readability of 0.1 mg • Mass Comparator Model XPR26003LC (26g) Maximum measurement capability of 26.1g, with a readability of 1 mg • Mass Comparator Model AX106 (100g) Maximum measurement capability of 111g, with a readability of 1 mg

2.0 Delivery Requirements Shipping Address: U.S. Army TMDE Activity, Attn: Bradford Suggs, Building 5435, Redstone Arsenal, AL 35898
