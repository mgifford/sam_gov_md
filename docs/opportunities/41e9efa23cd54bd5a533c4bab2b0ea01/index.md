## Summary

All sources submitting a quote or quote, as applicable, including U.S. sources, must already have, or obtain the requisite business licenses directly from the ROK Government, and conduct business in accordance with all applicable ROK laws, including but not limited to, rules and policies regarding labor, taxation, and immigration. Any contractor not already doing business in Korea as a local source, must submit adequate verification in its initial quote or quote, that it has the requisite authorization and capacity to compete for the requirement as a local source, and to perform the contract as a local source. If an initial quote does not fully demonstrate this authorization and capacity, it will not be further considered for award. Whether an offeror qualifies as a local source shall be determined by the Contracting Officer.****** VEHICLE SPECIFICATIONS A. ITEM 0001. 15Tdum truck, diesel-powered, left-hand drive, electrical tilt cab, rear view camera, auto grease system, ZF12 Gear transmission, button start, and heat/air conditioner Quantity: One (1) Note: Vehicles and furnished accessories shall comply with Korea Motor Vehicle Safety Standard in effect on the date of manufacture. The Government reserves the right to accept alternate specifications as noted below. B. DIMENSIONS Overall Length (mm) Minimum 7,000 Overall Width (mm) Minimum 2,450 Overall Height (mm) Minimum 3,300 Engine (cc): Minimum 10,000 Transmission: Auto ZF 12 Gear Seating Capacity Minimum 2 C. REQUIREMENTS. (1) Seat Belts: Shall be installed on all seats (2) Seats: Artificial leather seats (3) LED working light, LED strobe rectangular light (4) Brake: ABS with ASR (5) PTO gearbox on transmission (6) Button Start & Smart Key (7) Electrical Power Tilt Cab System (8) Rear view Camera (9) Auto Grease System (10) Reinforced cargo bed with metal plates: Floor: 9T Side wall: 6T Rear door: 9T Read door frame: 6T x 6T Rear safety bumper (11) Bracket for Snowplow attachment and installment of controller (12) LED bar on cabin top: 1250mm(L) X 300mm(W) X 129mm(H) (13) PA system: Amp, Speaker (Max. 135dB) and Mic install (14) On-the-spot training at the time of delivery for end users reference operation of equipment (15) Warranty: 3 years from the date of delivery. During the warranty period, the contractor shall provide maintenance service on the warranty guaranteed parts with 100% coverage at no cost. If repairs cannot be performed on the spot, the contractor is responsible for moving vehicles to their facility. (16) Certification and Technical Publications (In Korean): a. Certificate of manufacturer for each vehicle that specifies USFK as the vehicle’s owner. b. Operator’s manual & technical manual (parts catalog, service manual, electrical scheme) in Hangul & English in a books and digital files for end users. D. INTENDED USE. The vehicles covered by specifications are intended for general use by the U.S. Government in earthworks. The vehicles furnished hereunder shall be delivered ready for immediate use. E. SAFETY. New vehicles shall comply with Korean domestic safety and exhaust emission control requirements. F. DELIVERY POINT. Door-to-Door delivery. The place listed below may be changed within 300km without any additional cost to the government. Warrior Base (Paju), ATTN: Mr. Taylor, Joseph D. (0503-322-3262) G. DELIVERY DATE. The vehicles shall be delivered to the delivery point specified above within 180 calendar days after delivery order is issued. H. INSPECTION AND ACCEPTANCE. The receiving officer or his authorized representative at the respective delivery point shall conduct inspection for condition, quality, quantity count, and final acceptance of the supplies delivered under this delivery order.
