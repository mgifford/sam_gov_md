# F-16 SATCOM Dorsal Fairing Antenna Assembly Kit

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 10:30:08.868-04
- Solicitation Number: FA823226SATCOM

## Summary

The U.S. Government is requesting information in an effort to identify vendors with the expertise, capabilities, and experience to successfully produce Satellite Communication (SATCOM) Dorsal Fairing Antenna Kits. Please see the attached Sources Sought Synopsis for details. This SSS is issued solely for information and planning purposes only – it does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP) or a promise to issue an RFP in the future. This SSS does not commit the U.S. Government to contract for any supply or service whatsoever. This is a sources-sought only, which, as part of market research, is being issued in accordance with (IAW) Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) subpart 15.201(e). Responses to the SSS and/or questions shall be provided via e-mail to Contracting Officer, Capt Ryan Lambert at ryan.lambert.8@us.af.mil and Mr. Eric Dumpert at eric.dumpert.1@us.af.mil. Responses are due by Monday 08 June 2025, 2:00 PM (MDT).
