# Crash Investigation Sampling System (CISS) – Quality Control Center (QCC)

- Agency: TRANSPORTATION, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 14:25:25.48-04
- Solicitation Number: 693JJ926R000031

## Summary

The objective of this Contract is to provide support for the Quality Control Center (QCC) for NHTSA’s Crash Investigation Sampling System (CISS). This includes establishment and operation of a data and performance quality control center; quality control review of CISS cases for up to seventy-three (73) PSUs (sixty-five (65) currently operational and eight (8) anticipated to be added during contract performance); sampling review of PCRs entered by PSUs; participation in training, technical meetings, and workshops; and conducting quality control site visits to PSU locations.
