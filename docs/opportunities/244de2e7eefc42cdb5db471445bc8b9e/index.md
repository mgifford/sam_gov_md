# PNF Airstrip Maintenance

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 13:43:56.303-04
- Solicitation Number: 1240LR26Q0006

## Summary

The Krassel Ranger District of the Payette National Forest is requesting bids and work proposals with a cost estimate and timeline from contractors for work to complete annual maintenance to grade and resurface Payette National Forest Airstrips within the Frank Church – River of No Return Wilderness. There are 4 airstrips: Chamberlain, Cold Meadows, Cabin Creek and Soldier Bar. These strips are FAA designated and are labeled on Forest maps and Aeronautical charts
