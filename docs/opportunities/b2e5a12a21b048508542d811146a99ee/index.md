# Chicken Egg & Select Seafood Price Data

- Agency: LABOR, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-08 10:35:44.688-04
- Solicitation Number: OAA-26-00299

## Summary

The purpose of this RFI is to gather information about potential subscription services to obtain aggregated current month chicken egg and select seafood price data. The Bureau of Labor Statistics, Producer Price Index (PPI) Program Office has a need to purchase aggregated current month chicken egg and select seafood price data to assist the PPI in calculating indexes. *** Please see attached for additional information. *** SPECIAL NOTICE: SOURCES SOUGHT & REQUEST FOR INFORMATION (RFI). THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS. This notice does not represent a commitment by the Government to issue a solicitation or award a contract. The Government is not obligated to and will not pay for information received as a result of this announcement. No reimbursement will be made for any costs associated with providing information in response to this announcement and any follow-up information requests to include an invite to further explain/demonstrate aspects of their proposed approach. Requests for a solicitation will not receive a response. Information provided in response to this RFI is submitted voluntarily and is not being requested as part of a solicitation for actual pricing
