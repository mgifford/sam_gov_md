## Summary

The Tuscaloosa VA Medical Center has requirements for two Automated Urinalysis Analyzers, a primary and back-up analyzer for urinalysis testing. Analyzers are needed to conduct urinalysis tests on a Cost-Per-Test (CPT) / Cost-Per-ReportableResult (CPRR) (including all calibrators, quality controls, etc.) lease for an integrated automated urinalysis system.
