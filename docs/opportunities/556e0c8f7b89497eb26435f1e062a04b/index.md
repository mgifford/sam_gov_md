# Solicitation Notice of Intent to Award a Sole Source Contract: Aeronix, Inc - Software License Upgrade and Maintenance

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 17:36:50.612-04
- Solicitation Number: FA930226Q0043

## Summary

This notice follows the pre-solicitation notice ID F1S0AW6071A001 which was posted on 17 April 2026. The Government intends to award a sole-source contract to Aeronix, Inc under the authority of RFO 12.201-1. Solicitation Number: FA930226Q0043 Acquisition for Commercial Products and Services (RFO Part 12) procedures will be used for this requirement. The Government intends to solicit and negotiate with only one source under the authority of RFO 12.201-1. The intended source is Aeronix, Inc. The requirement is to acquire Aeronix’s Tactical Data Link Studios (TDLS) software and maintenance of WinIDM. The vendor shall provide Tactical Data Link Studios (TDLS) software and maintenance and maintenance of WinIDM. Attachments Please see the attached Request for Quotation (RFQ) document for the full schedule of supplies/services, delivery instructions, and applicable provisions and clauses. Response(s) must be received no later than 3:00 p.m. Pacific Daylight Time on 18 May 2026. Submissions must be sent via email to Contract Specialist, Jasmine Waterman (jasmine.waterman@us.af.mil) or Contracting Officer, Susana Dryer (susana.dryer@us.af.mil) While the Government intends to sole-source this requirement, all responsible sources may submit a capability statement, proposal, or quotation, which shall be considered by the agency. No late submissions will be accepted. For additional information regarding this solicitation contact: Primary Point of Contact: Jasmine Waterman Contract Specialist jasmine.waterman@us.af.mil Secondary Point of Contact: Susana Dryer Contracting Officer Susana.dryer@us.af.mil
