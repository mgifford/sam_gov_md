# DDWG Air Compressors

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 13:28:59.222-04
- Solicitation Number: SP3300-26-Q-0139

## Summary

This Request for Quotation (RFQ) is being issued to establish a firm-fixed-price (FFP) purchase order for 3 air compressors at DLA Distribution Warner Robins, GA (DDWG). The Government intends to award one (1) contract as a result of this RFQ and will be issued on a Standard Form (SF) 1449. This acquisition is not a Defense Priorities and Allocations System (DPAS) rated procurement.
