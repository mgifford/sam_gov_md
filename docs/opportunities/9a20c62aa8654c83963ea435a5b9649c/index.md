## Summary

Project Title: Renovate Imaging and Catheterization Lab 570 21 220 Fresno VA Medical Center Project Number: 570-21-220 Description: The Department of Veterans Affairs, Network Contracting Office (NCO) 21, seeks vendors to provide all labor, materials, equipment, supervision, transportation, project management, and specialized expertise required to perform the renovation of approximately 10,200 square feet of the Catheterization Lab located on the second floor of the East Wing at the Fresno VA Medical Center. Work includes relocation of existing ultrasound and fluoroscopy equipment to the renovated imaging suite; reconfiguration of radiology rooms, ultrasound rooms, patient restroom, staff work areas, communications and electrical rooms; construction of a new ultrasound room; and full Architectural, Structural, Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing work for all new and renovated spaces, including new finishes and the removal and installation of walls, floors, ceilings, lighting, power, HVAC, and plumbing systems. The period of performance (PoP) is 730 calendar days from the Notice to Proceed (NTP). Point of Contact regarding this Solicitation is Mr. Rosario Chaidez, Contracting Officer at email: Rosario.chaidez1@va.gov.
