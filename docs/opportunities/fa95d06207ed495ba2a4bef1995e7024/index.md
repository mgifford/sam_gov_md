# Disposable Cups

- Agency: GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 14:34:19.59-04
- Solicitation Number: 47QSSC-26-Q-0163

## Summary

This is a Request for Information (RFI), SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE, issued by the General Services Administration (GSA) General Supplies and Services are seeking information from prospective vendors interested in providing disposable cups for the National Stock Number (NSN) Program. At this time, the Office of Shared Services Delivery is gathering information for a procurement which may result in a Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) award according to Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR)

8.4 Federal Supply Schedules, under the GSA Multiple Award Schedule Program (MAS). The Sources Sought Questionnaire needs to be completed by the vendor and returned in SAM.gov. Please see the RFI document and the related attachments which define the requirements.
