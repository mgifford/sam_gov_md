GENERAL SCOPE The United States Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Southwestern Division, Middle East District (SWM) anticipates a potential Integrated Design and Construction (IDaC) requirement to support the pre-construction activities and construction of secure facilities that are ICD-705 compliant at Al Udeid Air Base, Qatar. These requirements stem from the Air Force Central Command (AFCENT) and the Al Udeid Air Base 2040 Strategic Master Plan (SMP 2040). Anticipated projects may include one or more of the following: A hardened, underground, secure, Combat Center Building (CATCODE 141446, Combat Center Building) Squadron operations buildings supporting a variety of airframes and missions to include, but not limited to, bombers, fighters, and unmanned aircraft systems for intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR). Secure administrative headquarters buildings (CATCODE 141446, Combat Center Building). Operation and Administrative Facilities such as command and control facilities, administrative offices, operational readiness and life support facilities, multiple unit buildings, and secure warehouse facilities. Site Security and Access Control such as access control and guard house complex, secure perimeter fencing, integrated physical and electronic security measures, Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), and Closed Circuit Television (CCTV) surveillance. Site Infrastructure and Building Systems such as parking, parking garages, antennae yard and shelter building. The North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for the potential design requirement is anticipated to be 541330 Engineering Services. The North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for the potential construction requirement is anticipated to be 236220 Commercial and Institutional Building Construction.
