# Fleet Advanced Tactics Training Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 16:28:09.412-04
- Solicitation Number: N5037326PR00018

## Summary

Doctrine, tactics, techniques and procedures (TTP), and Advanced Tactics Training. The Contractor shall provide support for development of doctrine, tactics techniques, and procedures and advanced tactics training in the Navy Information Warfare (IW) mission areas of Information Operations (IO) and related information related capabilities (IRCs), Electronic Warfare (EW), Cryptology, Command & Control (C2) systems, Cyber Operations (CO) specifically focused on Defensive Cyber Operations (DCO) and Department of Defense Information Network (DODIN) operations, Meteorology & Oceanography (METOC), Space Operations, and Intelligence (INTEL).
