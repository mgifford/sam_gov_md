## Summary

This is a solicitation for commercial supplies in accordance with the information in Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 12 - "Acquisition of Commercial Items" as supplemented with the additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. This solicitation will be available for download on or about 29 April 2026 under Request for Quotation (RFQ) number N0018926QL120 at the following website: https://beta.sam.gov/ . This solicitation documents and incorporates provisions and clauses in effect through RFO 23 April 2026 and DFARS Change Notice 20251110. It is the responsibility of the contractor to be familiar with the applicable clauses and provisions. The clauses may be accessed in full text at these addresses: https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul and https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars . This acquisition will result in a Firm-Fixed-Price purchase order under NAICS 334516. The Small Business Size Standard is 1,000 employees. Required delivery date: NLT than 30 Sept 2026. Delivery Location: United States Naval Observatory 3450 Massachusetts Ave NW Washington, DC USA Responses should be submitted via email to Keisha Gordon-Blair at keisha.o.gordon-blair.civ@us.navy.mil . Questions regarding this solicitation may be submitted to Mrs. Keisha Gordon-Blair at keisha.o.gordon-blair.civ@us.navy.mil no later than 11:00am EST, 05 May 2026. Quotes shall be submitted to Mrs. Keisha Gordon-Blair at keisha.o.gordon-blair.civ@us.navy.mil no later than 11:00am EST, 07 May 2026. Quote submissions received after the stated submission deadline may not be considered for award.
