# D--T1 Data Circuit, Telecom Services Trinity Dam

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-19 20:33:21.83-04
- Solicitation Number: 140R2026Q0080

## Summary

The Bureau of Reclamation is seeking interested vendors that can satisfy the objectives below. There is a recurring requirement for Facilities Access Service for T1 Data Circuit for the Central Valley Operations Office (CVOO). The T1 Circuit is a dedicated circuit for the Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system and is provided by TDS Happy Valley Telecom. The SCADA system is used between Trinity Dam and Keswick Dam to control operations. The circuit itself is controlled by a computer to keep the circuit on and off and the monthly service fee allows that access. An applicable Performance Work Statement is attached for reference. This notice is not to be construed as a commitment by the Government to issue a solicitation or ultimately award a contract. Responses will not be considered as proposals or quotes. No award will be made from this notice. The Government will not be responsible for any cost incurred by the respondents to this notice. This notice is for market research and information purposes only. NAICS code for this requirement is 517111 Wired Telecommunications Carriers and the applicable small business size standard is 1,500 employees. All businesses with the capability and availability to fulfil the requirement under the applicable NAICS code are invited to submit a response to this notice. If your organization is interested in providing this service under contract, please respond to this announcement and include the required following information. 1. Name of organization, address including zip code, SAM UEID, point of contact name, telephone number, and email address. 2. Confirmation of organization’s active System for Award (SAM) registration with the expiration date. 3. Business size and classifications that include any designations as Certified Small Business, HUBZone Certified Small Business Concern, Section 8(a) contractor, Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business, Small Disadvantaged Business, Women Owned Small Business, or Large Business Concern. 4. Information that would demonstrate your business has the capability and resources to meet the specifications of the Performance Work Statement. When responding to this Sources Sought notice, vendors should provide their SAM UEID number and describe their experience providing these services. Notice Number 140R2021Q080 has been assigned and should be referenced in all correspondence regarding this announcement. Please send your responses to Sherry Heibeck via email to sherry_heibeck@ios.doi.gov before 3:00 P.M. Pacific Daylight Time (PDT) on Friday, June 5, 2026.
