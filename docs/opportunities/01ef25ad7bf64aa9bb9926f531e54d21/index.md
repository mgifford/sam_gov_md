## Summary

Page 3 of 3 Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Network Contracting Office 2 (NCO 2) intends to award a sole source contract to Hologic Sales and Service, LLC for preventative maintenance and repair services for Hologic imaging equipment. Period of performance: 09/15/2026 - 09/14/2027 with a provision of four (4) option years. Basic description of the requirement: Hologic Sales and Service, LLC (Hologic) is the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) and is the sole authorized licenser of equipment/software and provider of maintenance and support. Hologic will provide a full-service agreement to include: authorized/trained service engineers, travel, labor, equipment, tools and materials, OEM parts, repair services, telephone and remote diagnostic support, and software updates to the hardware and software in place as required, and preventative maintenance.
