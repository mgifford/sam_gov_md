# BLDG 4 SERVER ROOM COOLING AND POWER UPGRADES

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 16:28:03.673-04
- Solicitation Number: N6449826Q5147

## Summary

The Naval Surface Warfare Center Philadelphia Division (NSWCPD) proposes to award a competitive single award Firm Fixed Price (FFP) Construction Contract resulting from this Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Building 4 - Server Room Cooling And Power Upgrades located at the Philadelphia Navy Yard (also known as the Philadelphia Naval Business Center (PNBC). This requirement is being solicited as a 100% Small Business Set Aside. A site visit will be held Thursday, June 4, 2026 at 9:00 AM (EDT). All requests to attend the site visit, including visitorname, email, company name and address, must be sent to rishi.m.mathur.civ@us.navy.mil and david.a.rhoads8.civ@us.navy.mil no later than 12 PM (EDT), Tuesday, June 2 2026.
