# Remote Monitoring Services Source Sought Notice

- Agency: UNITED STATES AGENCY FOR GLOBAL MEDIA, BBG
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-27 09:01:46.426-04
- Solicitation Number: T001-26-IQ-00005

## Summary

The U.S. Agency for Global Media (USAGM), TSI Broadcast Technologies Division (T/EBD), is seeking preliminary information, for market research purposes only, from capable and reliable sources who may be interested in submitting a proposal to provide Remote Monitoring Services. The requirement is for the monitoring and schedule planning of medium wave and shortwave transmission, using both remote monitoring services and other remote receivers to be completed in Finland. The purpose of this notice is to gain knowledge of potential local qualified businesses capable of delivering these services being requested.
