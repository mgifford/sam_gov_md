# ONE (1) FIVE-AXIS LASER MACHINING CENTER

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 11:55:25.629-04
- Solicitation Number: SPE4A8-26-Q-0034

## Summary

Solicitation is in accordance with FAR 13.5. RFQ SPE4A8-26-Q-0034. Procurement of ONE (1) FIVE-AXIS LASER MACHINING CENTER in accordance with Purchase Description 26-13-1202 Dated MARCH 31, 2026. The contractor shall provide ONE (1) FIVE-AXIS LASER MACHINING CENTER that includes the following, Shipping, Installation, Foundation, Rigging, Training, Technical Data, Machinery Software Agreement Package, Machinery Remote Agreement Package, Machinery Performance Agreement Package and IUID Tag. Place Of Performance Tinker Air Force Base, OK. This solicitation will be solicited for award of a Firm Fixed Priced Contract. Solicitation will be in accordance with Other Than Full and Open Competition as prescribed by FAR 6.302-1, Only One Responsible Source and No Other Supplies or Services Will Satisfy Agency Requirements. FOB at Destination with Inspection at Destination and Acceptance at Destination. QAP 175a shall apply. All contractual performance period requirements shall be complete within 355 days after receipt of award. The final contract award decision may be based upon a combination of lowest price technically acceptable (LPTA), past performance and other evaluation factors as described in the solicitation. The estimated solicitation issue date is May 13, 2026. A copy of the solicitation will be available via the DLA Intranet Bid Board System (DIBBS) at https://www.dibbs.dla.mil/RFQ to obtain a copy of the solicitation. Choose the RFQ you wish to download.
