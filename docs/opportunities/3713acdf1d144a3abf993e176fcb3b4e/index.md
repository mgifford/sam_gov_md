## Summary

See attached Justification and Approval related to the following Award Notice. The Naval Information Warfare Center, Pacific (NIWC Pacific) has awarded contract N6600126C0039 to Peraton, Inc. (CAGE: 0HD54). The contract is cost plus fixed fee (CPFF) in the amount of $90,685,458 for ExpeditionaryUnmanned Systems (ExUS) Operations and Fleet Support Representative (FSR) Support at NIWC Pacific and Fleet commands which have fielded MK 18 systems. Operations support includes the operation and maintenance of MK 18 UUV systems, mission support equipment, and 11M Rigid Hull Inflatable Boats (RHIBS). Support will include test and evaluation operations at NIWC Pacific and to forward deployed Fleet Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) Expeditionary Mine Countermeasures (ExMCM) companies. FSR support includes critical on-site operations, maintenance, and training support for fielded MK 18 systems. The period of performance consists of a 12-month base period and four 12-month option periods.
