# Drop-Off & Pick-Up Laundry Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-05 12:18:57.855-04
- Solicitation Number: FY26-Laundry

## Summary

The Washington National Guard is conducting market research to determine availability and business size/type of sources. The requirement is for pick-up & drop-off laundry services to be conducted on a bi-weekly basis. The location of service is Camp Murray (Tacoma), Washington. Interested parties should respond to this request via email to dennis.k.jutras.civ@army.mil. Responses should include a brief description of how prospective contractors would perform the service or capabilites statement. - Location of business performing the services - Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) - Cage Code (if applicable) - SBA Business Size/Status - Other than Small Business, Small Business, or specific Small Disadvantaged Business category **Interested parties must be registered in SAM with the NAICS code 812320, with pick-up and drop off laundry services being part of their existing business model.
