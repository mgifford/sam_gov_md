# 2026 PAE - Maneuver Air Industry Days, Fort Rucker, AL

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 14:53:20.246-04

## Summary

The United States Army Aviation Center of Excellence invites you to the Portfolio Acquisition Executive (PAE) Maneuver – Air Industry Days, “Delivering Capability Today” 24-26 August 2026 at Fort Rucker, AL to facilitate interaction between Aviation Industry personnel and Senior Military Leaders; communicate our modernization strategy and promote collaboration; allow Industry representatives the opportunity to display current capabilities.
