# Welding Machine

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 15:25:19.684-04
- Solicitation Number: W50S7Z26QM001

## Summary

The Louisiana Air National Guard has a requirement to procure one (1) Miller Dynasty 800 TIG Welder, 208-600 V, brand name or equal, with two (2) hand remotes and required accessories. The welder is required for precision TIG welding of aerospace ground equipment, support equipment, and off-equipment aircraft components. The system shall be compatible with existing Miller equipment and controls already in use by the unit and shall be delivered as a complete, functional welding system.
