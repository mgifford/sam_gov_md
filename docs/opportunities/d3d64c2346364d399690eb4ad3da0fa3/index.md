# US Forest Service Gila National Forest Tent Rental

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 13:55:29.732-04

## Summary

This Structural Tent will house our fire supplies brought in to support Incident Management Teams as well as supplies returning from Incidents. For the 2026 Fire Season. Due to federal budget restraints the government wasn't able to post a solicitation until now, the tent rental would start as soon as the award is made and run until 31 Augest 2026. This will be a firm fixed price contract with payment being made via IPP.gov only. Quote shall be emailed to Chris Wissen at christopher.wissen@usda.gov no later then 18th May, 2026.
