# Streambank Stabilization Project –Gabions Thunderhill Run G Street – EWP 2025 Mineral County, West Virginia

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF

- Posted: 2026-04-28 19:39:05.39-04

## Summary

NRCS is in need of construction services (is now considered a commercial service) for erosion control in Mineral County, West Virginia. The erosion control measures will consist of laying wire mesh gabions, preparing foundation on shallow bedrock; rebar anchoring and riprap foundation protection. Details provided in Solicitation attachments. Site Showing Details: Site Showing will be held in Mineral County, West Virginia on Tuesday, May 27, 2026, at 10:00am. Interested bidders will meet at 299 S E Street, Keyser, WV 26726. We will also drive to the work site to give the prospective bidders a chance to see it and ask questions.
