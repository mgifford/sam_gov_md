# 1 AS Special Air Mision Pilot Indoctrination Training

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 15:44:05.961-04
- Solicitation Number: FA286026Q4013

## Summary

Description:

1.0 PURPOSE

1.1 The 316th Contracting Squadron (316 CONS), in support of the 1st Airlift Squadron (1 AS), is seeking information from contractors to develop unclassified, virtual training for new-hire pilots transitioning from military jets to commercial-style aircraft. With approximately two pilots completing Special Air Mission Pilot Indoctrination Training (Indoc) each month, this digital training delivered as high-quality videos, interactive CBTs, or a combination will mitigate Instructor Pilot shortages and support direct-to-simulator routing. Eligible contractors must utilize full-time, dedicated personnel with an average of at least 20 years of U.S. commercial aviation experience, including backgrounds with major U.S. airlines and the FAA. The contractor must deliver the initial two modules within 60 days of award, with the remaining modules delivered within 180 days. Temporary, escorted access will be granted for necessary videography on aircraft flight decks. Once delivered, the squadron will retain these virtual courses for long-term reference during and after Indoc completion.
