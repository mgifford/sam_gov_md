# Learning & Professional Education Program Subscription

- Agency: TREASURY, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-05 17:16:59.938-04

## Summary

At a summary level, the Government has interest in and is seeking information regarding the capability of firms who can provide services pertaining to training. LB&I requires unlimited access to a training subscription with a centralized digital library of accredited continuing education programs, course materials, and technical publications designed to support a minimum of 3,500 compliance employees. 5/5/2026 - Vendor response form has been updated and the due date has been extended to 5/15/2026.
