## Summary

General Information Notice Type: Presolicitation / Sources Sought Project Title: Employee Onboarding Training Video Proposed Solicitation Number: TBD Agency / Office: Department of War / US Air Force / 30 CONS/ PKB Location: Lompoc, CA 93437 NAICS Code: 512110 (Motion Picture and Video Production) Product Service Code (PSC): T006 (Photo/Map/Print/Publication—Film/Video Tape Production) Posting Date 5/12/2026 Anticipated Solicitation Date: 5/27/2026 Description of Requirement BACKGROUND To enhance the effectiveness and accessibility of 30 FSS onboarding training, the squadron requires the development of a modern, engaging training video for new employees. Traditional training methods can be time-consuming and lack the engagement needed for effective knowledge retention. This project will convert an existing training slide deck into a streamlined, high-impact video to better embed key concepts and enhance learner engagement

2.0 MINIMUM SPECIFICATIONS The primary objective is to produce a 60-minute training video based on a Government-furnished slide deck. The final product will be a professional, easy-to-understand, and inclusive training tool. • Develop one 60-minute training video for supervisor and leadership training. • Use the Government-provided training slide deck as the primary source of information. • Create a smart, streamlined video that is engaging for the target audience. • Structure the video into logical sections that correspond with the sections in the training slide deck. • Incorporate professional voice-over and on-screen captions to enhance understanding and inclusiveness. • Ensure the final video has a logical structure and is easy to comprehend. • Ensure the final video has 1920 x 1080 (Full HD) resolution and has optimal video quality of at least 25 fps. • Ensure the final video has high-quality audio to ensure viewer engagement and retention.

3.0 DESCRIPTION OF WORK The contractor shall provide all necessary services to create and deliver a complete, high-quality training video. • Contractor shall review the Government-furnished training slide deck to understand the content and learning objectives. • Contractor shall develop a script and storyboard for the video, organizing the content into logical sections that mirror the slide deck. • Contractor shall create all necessary graphics, animations, and visual elements to produce an engaging video. • Contractor shall record a professional voice-over for the entire video. • Contractor shall produce and edit the video to a final length of approximately 60 minutes. • Contractor shall integrate synchronized captions (closed captioning) into the final video. • Contractor shall deliver a draft version of the video to the Government representative for review and feedback. • Contractor shall incorporate Government feedback and deliver the final video file in a standard, high-definition format (e.g., MP4).

4.0 COMPLIANCE The Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal, state, local, and county laws, as well as Air Force regulations governing the execution of these services.

5.0 PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE The project shall be completed between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027. The complete digitization and upload of all records should take no longer than 180 calendar days from the project start date.
