# RFI - IDMT Critical Care Courses - Amendment 0001

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-27 15:15:28.234-04
- Solicitation Number: FA301626Q7083

## Summary

Amendment 0001: The purpose of this amendment is to provide answers to industry questions, establish a final question submission deadline of April 30, 2026 at 2:00 PM CT, and provide an updated Statement of Need removing the mobile training option. Please see attached documents for full details. The RFI response deadline remains unchanged. The 502d Contracting Squadron, Joint Base San Antonio is issuing this Request for Information (RFI) to conduct market research and identify potential sources capable of providing a high-fidelity, hands-on procedural skills training course for U.S. Air Force Independent Duty Medical Technicians (IDMTs). See attached RFI, Statement of Need, and Schedule of Supplies.
