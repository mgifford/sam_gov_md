## Summary

DESCRIPTION OF REQUIREMENT:? The U.S. Coast Guard requires services for the contractor to furnish?all labor, tools, equipment,?materials,?supervision?and incidentals?for?the removal and replacement of carpet tile for the Facilities Design & Construction Center (FD&CC) Seattle offices on the 26th Floor of the Jackson Federal Building, to include the removal and replacement of the rubber wall base. The Government intends to issue a firm-fixed-price purchase order for this project.?The full scope of work and specifications are included in the solicitation documents. PROJECT ADDRESS: U.S. Coast Guard FD&CC Seattle 915 2nd Ave, Room 2664 Seattle, WA 98174 United States CLASSIFICATION: This acquisition is set-aside 100% for?SMALL BUSINESS. This tailoring includes but is not limited to the following: In accordance with FAR 13.004, a quotation is not an offer, and any clause provision to the contrary is hereby modified to be consistent with FAR 13.004.
