## Summary

Identifier: 36C25726Q0576 Purpose The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), Veterans Health Administration (VHA), Network Contracting Office (NCO) 17, is conducting market research to identify vendors capable of providing a brand name or equal Thompson Cervical Retractor System for the South Texas Veterans Health Care System in San Antonio, Texas. Requirement Overview The VA requires delivery, installation, testing, adjustments, and staff training for a Cervical Retractor System used in anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) procedures. This requirement is further defined in the attached Statement of Work. Delivery Location Audie L. Murphy Memorial Veterans Hospital Attn: Surgical Service 7400 Merton Minter San Antonio, TX 78229 Submission Requirements Interested vendors shall provide business information, capability statements, evidence of meeting salient characteristics, socioeconomic status, and delivery lead time. Response Deadline Responses are due May 20, 2026 at 10:00 AM CST. Submission Instructions Email responses to: Janice Malbon (janice.malbon@va.gov) Subject Line: 36C25726Q0576 Thompson Cervical Retractor System - Brand Name or Equal
