Christopher Baker Christopher.Baker@noaa.gov FIRM FIXED PRICE CLIN 0001 – BASE PoP Services, non-personal, to provide all labor, equipment and materials (unless otherwise provided herein) necessary for Provide the services for an emergency pump-off of approx. 16,000 Gallons of Diesel Fuel mixed with ~8% seawater from NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer. REQUIRMENT: • Pump-off of approx. 16,000 Gallons of Diesel Fuel mixed with ~8% seawater from NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer. Removal and disposal must be in accordance with all local, state and federal regulations. • Connection is through 4.0” camlock fitting. SCOPE OF WORK The Contractor shall provide all labor, supervision, equipment, materials, transportation, containment, and incidentals necessary to perform an emergency fuel pump-out operation from the NOAA vessel. Services shall include, but are not limited to: • Mobilization of qualified personnel and equipment to the vessel location within the required response timeframe. • Provision of pumps, hoses, fittings, containment systems, spill response equipment, and vacuum or tanker trucks as required. • Safe transfer of approximately 16,000 gallons of marine diesel fuel from the vessel’s fuel tanks. • Transportation of recovered fuel to an approved facility. • Compliance with all applicable federal, state, local, and environmental regulations. • Spill prevention and immediate spill response assistance during all transfer operations. • Coordination with NOAA representatives, port authorities, and applicable regulatory agencies. • Documentation of quantities removed, chain of custody, disposal manifests (if applicable), and operational reports.
