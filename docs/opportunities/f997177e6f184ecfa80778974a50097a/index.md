# Grounds Maintenance Services for USDA, AMS, Cotton & Tobacco Program, Memphis, TN

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 16:09:03.756-04

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12639526Q0122 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Grounds Maintenance Services for USDA, AMS, Cotton & Tobacco Program located at 3275 Appling Road, Memphis, TN 38133. USDA intends to award a five (5) year IDIQ contract for grounds maintenance services as a result of this solicitation. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 561730 The small business size standard is $9.5 Million. This acquisition is set-aside for SBA Certified Economically Disadvantaged Women Owned Small Businesses (EDWOSB). All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
