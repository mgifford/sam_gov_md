## Summary

Tomah VA Medical Center would like to procure 18 Mobile Shower/Toilet Chair that will be used for showering and toileting needs of long-term care residents who reside in the Community Living Center. For use on the dementia care unit of the Tomah VAMC Community Living Center, where patient showering and toileting occur. There is no installation or training required for this government contract. Next a minimum of 1 manual is required.
