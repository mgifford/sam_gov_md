# FA4626-26-FIRE SUPPRESSION SERVICES

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 16:25:38.06-04

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number FA462626Q0017 is issued as a request for quote (RFQ) for Red Horse Squadron (RHS) Fire Suppression Services. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. Line itemes are as follows: 1. Annual inspection and service for estimated 258 portable fire extinguishers. 2. 6-year maintenance including recharging for estimated 43 portable fire extinguishers. 3. 12-year hydrostatic testing of estimated 22 portable fire extinguishers. 4. Purchase of an estimated additional 10 portable fire extinguishers. More details of requirements will be on the Attached statement of Work. Questions pertaining to this requirement shall be submitted no later than 2:00 PM MST, 26 May 2026 to the Contracting Administrator SSgt Perry at william.perry.27@us.af.mil and the Contracting Officer Karen Broughton at karen.broughton@us.af.mil
