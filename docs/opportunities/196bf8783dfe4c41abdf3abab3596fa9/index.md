- Posted: 2026-05-19 11:22:50.768-04

## Summary

The 97th Contracting Squadron, Altus Air Force Base, Oklahoma, intends to issue a Request for Quote (RFQ) for acquisition of aircraft cleaning compound at Altus AFB, Oklahoma. Any questions shall be submitted via email to the below points of contacts by 25 May 2026 at 1330 CST (1:30 PM CST). This acquisition will fall under the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code 325998. This is a 100% Small Business Set Aside. Prospective contractors must be registered in the System for Award Management Database (SAM) prior to submission of quotes. Offerors are further advised that failure to register in the DoD SAM prior to submission of proposal will render your firm ineligible for award. Recommend registering immediately in order to be eligible for timely award. Any questions regarding this notice must be submitted in writing via email to SSgt Jamir Folsom, Contract Administrator, (580) 481-7303, jamir.folsom@us.af.mil. Alternate contact, Scott E. Swain, Contracting Officer, (580) 481-6210, scott.swain.2@us.af.mil. See attachments for full details.
