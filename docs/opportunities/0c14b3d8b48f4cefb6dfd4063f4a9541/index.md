## Summary

The Mission and Installation Contracting Command Contracting Office at Joint Base Lewis McChord, Washington (MICC-JBLM) is issuing this combined synopsis/solicitation for Digital Communication and Graphic Design Services Contract at JBLM, WA. The period of performance is as follows: Phase-in Period: 18 June 2026 through 30 June 2026 Base Year: 1 July 2026 through 30 June 2027 Option YR1: 1 July 2027 through 30 June 2028 Option YR2: 1 July 2028 through 30 June 2029 This requirement will be 100% set aside for Small Business concerns under NAICS code 541519 – Other Computer Related Services, size standard is $34,000,000.00 per year and PSC of R422. To be considered, please adhere to the FAR 52.212-2, Instructions to Offerors, located within the solicitation. To be eligible for award, offeror must be registered as a Small Business under the appropriate NAICS code in the System for Award Management (SAM) at sam.gov. Also please verify that your Cage Code are current. Offerors are reminded to carefully review solicitation documents issued, as the requirements specified in the solicitation shall govern. This solicitation is issued in electronic format only and will be available on the Internet at: https://sam.gov. It is the Offeror’s responsibility to check the internet address provided as necessary for any posted changes to this solicitation, adherences, and all amendments. Quotes must be submitted via email to Manuel E. Rosas, manuel.e.rosas.civ@army.mil on or before the solicitation end date, of May 27, 2026 at 10:00 AM Pacific Daylight Time.
