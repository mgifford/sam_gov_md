## Summary

Synopsis: This announcement constitutes a Special Notice. THIS IS NOT a Request for Quote (RFQ) or a Request for Proposal (RFP). This announcement is for information and planning purposes only and is not to be construed as a commitment by the government, implied or otherwise, to issue a solicitation or award a contract. The Department of Veterans Affairs Veteran Health Administration, Prosthetics Sensory Aids Service (PSAS) has a need for Desktop Video Magnification devices for visually impaired Veterans. Video Magnification Devices offer visually impaired users the ability to view objects at a variety of levels, from extremely low to extremely high magnification. The purpose of this acquisition is to establish a reliable supply source for contracted items used by Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Centers (VAMCs), ensuring consistent product availability across all facilities nationwide. This notice is to determine interest and capability of potentially qualified Businesses (All Sizes) relative to the North American Industry Classification code (NAICS): 334220 - Radio and Television Broadcasting and Wireless Communications Equipment Manufacturing. To support an informed acquisition decision, the Government will host an Industry Day. Due to the ongoing government shutdown, additional details will be provided to each vendor regarding whether the event will be held in person or virtually. All appointments will be scheduled in advance. At this time, specific requirements have not been finalized. However, each vendor is expected to bring 3 4 desktop device models for physical demonstration. While additional models may be discussed, space constraints will not allow for more than 3 4 devices per vendor. Each vendor will have 1 to 1.5 hours to present the key features of their desktop models and address the topics outlined in the attached questions as part of their oral presentation. Vendors are also asked to bring supporting literature for both the demonstration models and any additional models (not physically present) that fall within the two general categories of interest.
