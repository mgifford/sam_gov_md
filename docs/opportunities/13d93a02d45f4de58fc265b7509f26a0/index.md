# Presolicitation Notice: Department of State, U.S. Embassy Monrovia New Embassy Compound Utility Optimization Improvement

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 09:02:21.771-04

## Summary

This is a Pre-Solicitation Notice – interested companies do NOT need to submit any documentation at this time. The solicitation for qualification packages will be posted on SAM.gov at a later date. The U.S. Department of State (DOS), Bureau of Global Acquisitions, on behalf of the Bureau of Overseas Buildings Operations (OBO), has a requirement for Design-Build (D/B) Construction Services for a Utility Optimization Improvement project at the Existing New Embassy Compound in Monrovia, Liberia. The Project is described below. OBO seeks to commission our nation’s top constructors to produce facilities of outstanding quality and value. This acquisition is small business set aside subject to the Omnibus Diplomatic Security and Antiterrorism Act of 1986, as amended, 2025 NDAA § 7214, (Public Law 118-159) (“Omnibus Act”). This solicitation will consist of two phases: Phase I – Minimum Mandatory Requirements (MMRs) Phase 1 Proposal Request from Offerors for Eligibility Determination (see below); and Phase II – Request for Phase 2 Proposals (Technical and Price) from Eligible or Conditionally Eligible Offerors. This will be set aside for small businesses (SB). See attachment for more details.
