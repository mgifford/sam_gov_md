# Primary Crash Alert System Notice of Intent to Sole Source

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-19 11:56:54.758-04

## Summary

The government intends to sole source the acquisition of fiber optic phone/analog extenders (parts TCRM191R-01-01-PP, TC1901R-L1ST-2-12, TC1901S-L1ST-2-12) from TC Communications. Failing to purchase this system from the original manufacturer will result in receiving incompatible parts, prolonging the current system outage and the associated increased response times for in-flight emergencies at Cannon AFB. See specifications below: TCRM191R-01-01-PP 4U 19in Mux Card Cage for RackMount Card (-10C to 50C), Dual Power Cards, 115/240V AC - North American type and 115/240V AC - North American type with Power Back Plane TC1901R-L1ST-2-12 Fiber Optic Telephone/Analog Extender, RackMount Card (-10C to 50C), 1300nm SM (20dB) ST, FXO/FXS, 12V DC TC1901S-L1ST-2-12 Fiber Optic Telephone/Analog Extender, Stand Alone (-10C to 50C), 1300nm SM (20dB) ST, FXO/FXS, 12V DC TC3PS-TB-25-01 Power Adapter for Standalone Unit, -20C to 70C, 12VDC output, Terminal Block 5.0mm,
