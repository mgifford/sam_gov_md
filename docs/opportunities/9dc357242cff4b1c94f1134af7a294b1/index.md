# Madigan Laundry Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 14:25:52.016-04
- Solicitation Number: HT941026QE003

## Summary

This requirement is for non-personal healthcare laundry and related services for Madigan Army Medical Center (MAMC) and multiple Medical Treatment Facilities (MTF) in the health service area (HSA) to include MAMC, at Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM) WA, Puyallup Medical Home, Puyallup, Washington, and South Sound Medical Home, Lacey, Washington. As a performance based contract, the requirements are stated in terms of desired results with associated quality standards. Healthcare laundry services shall be performed in a manner that shall maintain satisfactory services. Laundry services are considered mission essential functions and an essential Contractor service.
