# Global Unified Airbase Readiness and Defense (GUARD) Capabilities Sources Sought

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-08 08:06:06.019-04
- Solicitation Number: FA2381777

## Summary

**Update 08 May 2026 0800** Questions are due on 27 May 2026 1500 EDT. Answers will be posted no later than 3 June. The Force Protection Division (AFLCMC/ESS) is seeking information regarding the acquisition and sustainment of Integrated Base Defense (IBD) physical security and force protection electronic security systems, equipment, and system integration capabilities. The purpose of this initiative is to better understand industry capability to provide electronic security intrusion detection systems capabilities to the Air Force Security Forces community as a means to deter, anticipate, deny, detect, report, delay, assess, and track hostile and potentially hostile forces and targets for the protection of resources, work environments, and personnel.
