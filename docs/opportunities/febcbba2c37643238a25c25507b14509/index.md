# P-8A Poseidon Identification Friend Foe Interrogator (IFFI)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 17:37:54.403-04
- Solicitation Number: N00019-26-RFPREQ-APM290-0726

## Summary

The Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR), Maritime Patrol and Reconnaissance Aircraft Program Office (PMA-290) is seeking information on capability of development and manufacturing of the AN/UPX-43(V)1 Identification Friend Foe Interrogator (IFFI) shipset and IFFI tray; providing the requisite knowledge and engineering support. This Sources Sought is not, however, a request for inquiries concerning subcontracting opportunities. Telephonics Corporation is the sole designer, developer, and manufacturer of the AN/UPX-43(V)1 and associated IFFI tray Shipset for use in the P-8A. NAVAIR plans to award a base year of approximately four (4) shipsets in support of Foreign Military Sales (FMS) requirements and two (2) options for emergent FMS and domestic requirements. The options are anticipated to include up-to twenty (20) shipsets per year. The AN/UPX-43(V)1s are expected to be manufactured and delivered between FY27 and FY30.
