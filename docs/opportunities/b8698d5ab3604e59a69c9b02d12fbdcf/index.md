- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF

- Posted: 2026-05-26 15:58:45.191-04

## Summary

The Indian Health Service, Navajo Area, Gallup Service Unit, Gallup Indian Medical Center, located at 516 E Nizhoni Blvd, Gallup, NM, 87301, has an upcoming requirement for sink fixtures. The North American Industry Classification System or NAICS code is 332913 – Plumbing Fixture Fitting and Trim Manufacturing with a small business size standard of 1,000 employees. The Request for Quotations No. 75H71026Q00171 in accordance with 12.201-1 will be issued on or by Wednesday, May 27, 2026. Evaluation factors may include technical capability to meet the government need, delivery, and price. Quotes will be due on or by Wednesday, June 3, 2026. The acquisition is a 100% Indian Small Business Economic Enterprise (ISBEE) set aside. In accordance with 52.204-7 the offeror shall have an active Federal Government contracts registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) when submitting an offer or quotation in response to this solicitation and at the time of award. For additional information, contact Mr. Stephen Silversmith, Contract Specialist, at Stephen.Silversmith@ihs.gov. THIS IS NOT A SOLICITATION.
