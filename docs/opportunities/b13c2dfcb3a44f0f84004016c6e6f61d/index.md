# CMSI Marine Traffic Licenses

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-19 14:36:34.689-04

## Summary

The US Naval War College (NWC) requires a subscription to Kpler MarineTraffic maritime domain awareness and vessel-tracking services, including access for approximately 1–10 user licenses that will be shared across the NWC research community and relevant analytical stakeholders. The subscription will provide access to real-time and historical AIS vessel tracking data, voyage reconstruction tools, geospatial analytics, port-call monitoring, and maritime intelligence functions required to support research, teaching, and operational analysis related to global naval and commercial maritime activity.
