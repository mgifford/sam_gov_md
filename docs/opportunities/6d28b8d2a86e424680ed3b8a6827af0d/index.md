# Ft. Bliss Washer and Dryer Repair Serives

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 22:56:38.825-04
- Solicitation Number: PANMCC26P0000043224

## Summary

The Mission and Installation Contracting Command – Fort Bliss is conducting market research through this Sources Sought notice to identify qualified vendors for the maintenance and repair of Government-owned, coin-free washers and dryers at Fort Bliss, Texas, and its outlying training areas. This requirement is essential to ensure the operational readiness and quality of life for enlisted soldiers residing in barracks, supporting daily hygiene and uniform standards across approximately 2,827 laundry appliances. Interested parties are invited to review the attached Sources Sought and Performance Work Statement (PWS) for detailed requirements, including preventive maintenance, corrective repairs, parts procurement, and compliance with all applicable regulations. All services will be performed on-site at Fort Bliss, Texas. Attachments: Sources Sought Notice Performance Work Statement (PWS) Questions Due: All questions must be submitted in writing via email to the Contract Specialist by 11 June 2026 .Response Due Date: Capabilities statements are due no later than 4:00 PM MST, 16 June 2026 . Vendors are encouraged to review all attachments and submit a comprehensive response in accordance with the instructions provided. This is not a solicitation; responses are for market research purposes only.
