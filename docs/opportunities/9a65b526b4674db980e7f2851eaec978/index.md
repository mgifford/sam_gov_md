# Leroux East Stewardship IRSC- Region 3- Southwest Stewardship BPA- Coconino NF, Flagstaff RD

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 11:06:33.726-04
- Solicitation Number: 12363N26Q4052

## Summary

**UPDATE: Amendment 002: Changes contract Period of Performance from 11/15/2026 to 11/21/2027. Changes to the Call Order, Appendix A, and Appendix B are made in red font and revised documents are attached. These changes are primarily to address additional year of operations and associated timing restrictions for operations. Due to these changes, the due date for Quotes is extended from 5/18/2026 to 5/26/2026 at 4:00 PM MST. *UPDATE: Amendment 001 is now posted and includes notes and attendance from the site visit held on 04/23/2026. This procurement is restricted to the approved companies eligible for call orders under the R3 Southwest Stewardship BPA for the Coconino National Forest in the state of Arizona. This requirement may be awarded to one vendor as a BPA Call Order against BPA's previously solicited on the Government Point of Entry (GPE). The government will not provide feedback or evaluations to companies regarding their submissions in response to this notice that are not eligible for awards under the R3 Southwest Stewardship BPA.
