- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

## Summary

The U.S. Army Contracting Command - New Jersey (ACC-NJ), on behalf of the U.S. Army Combat Capabilities Development Command - Armaments Center (DEVCOM-AC), has issued a Firm-Fixed Price (FFP) contract to DS Government Solutions Corp. under solicitation W15QKN-26-R-A026. This procurement providees for ABAQUS software licenses, comprehensive maintenance, technical updates, and specialized training to support the command's finite element analysis software requirements. Purchase Order W15QKN-26-P-A029 was awarded on May 14, 2026 from this solicitation. This is a FFP Purchase Order with a period of performance (PoP) of 12 months. The corresponding Sole Source Justification and Approval utilizing the authority FAR 13.501(a)(1)(ii), sole source (including brand name) under the authority of the simplified procedures for certain commercial items.
