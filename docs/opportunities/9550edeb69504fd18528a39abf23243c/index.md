# J&A for Supply and Delivery of an Estimated 150,000 MMBTU of Liquefied Natural Gas over 3 deliveries to Naval Station Guantanamo Bay, Cuba

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-19 11:41:56.256-04
- Solicitation Number: N69450-26-0514

## Summary

Supply and delivery of an estimated 150,000 MMBtu of liquefied natural gas (LNG) over the three deliveries to Naval Station Guantanamo Bay (NSGB), Cuba. The period of delivery is from the date of contract award through 12 June 2026. This purchase order was issued pursuant to 10 USC 3204 (a)(2) Unusual and Compelling Urgency, as imple-mented by FAR 6.103-2.
