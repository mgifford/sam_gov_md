# RNEC Absolyte VRLA Batteries Replacement BLDG 1014

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-05-21 12:42:09.035-04
- Solicitation Number: W911RZ-Q-A016

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes only solicitation. The quotes shall refer to the solicitation number W911RZ-26-Q-A016. This solicitation document incorporated provisions and clauses that are in effect through Federal Acquisition Regulation FAC # 2026-01, dated 03/13/2026 and Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) Change # DFARS Change 01/01/2026. The Mission Installation Contracting Command (MICC) - Fort Carson intends to award a Firm-Fixed-Price Contract to a total small business set-aside for the acquisition of the RNEC ABSOLYTE VRLA BATTERY CELLS BLDG. 1014 at Fort Carson, Colorado. See Performance Work Statement (PWS) for details. The North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is NAICS Code: 335910, Battery Manufacturing, size standard 1250 Employees. This will be a totally small business set aside. The Product Service Code (PCS) is for Service J059 Maintenance, Repair, and Rebuilding of Equipment-Electrical and Electronic Equipment Components and for Product 6140 Batteries Rechargeable. Description of Requirements The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to Furnish and Labor to install 24 new Absolyte Value Regulated leadacid (VRLA) battery cells, Model Number: 100A29 GNB Absolyte VRLA battery cells, modular steel tray housings, Positive +/Negative – terminate plate, Frame Retardant Transparent Module Cover (plexiglass) with standoff, and Alarm Control Unit Card Frame for CN-1 Building 1014 aboard Fort Carson. This also requires the removal of the old battery cell IAW the PWS. The new battery cells shall be the same brands and equal in amp-hours and not refurbished or renewed. The contractor shall reconnect existing Alarm/control cable hardness on each battery cell and reinstall Ni-Cad Neutra Mat as defined in this PWS. The contractor shall recycle of the old battery IAW regulation
