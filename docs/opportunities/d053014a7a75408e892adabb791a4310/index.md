# Mastercam Software Renewal

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 08:22:25.439-04
- Solicitation Number: HC102826R0035

## Summary

Synopsis solicitation HC102826R0035. The Government intends to issue a sole source order to the sole authorized reseller of Mastercam products, MLC CAD Systems, Inc. Brand-name, commercial, proprietary Mastercam software is required for Puget Sound Naval Shipyard & Intermediate Maintenance Facility (PSNS & IMF). Software support is effective on the Date of Award through December 31, 2026. However, the Government is authorized to any new releases or upgrades between January 1, 2026, and the date of award at no additional cost. The redacted Software Class J&A is posted. Responsible sources may submit a completed and signed solicitation response.
