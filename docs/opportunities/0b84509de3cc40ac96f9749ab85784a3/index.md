Rationale for Sole Source The new BD FACSLyric systems are fully compatible with the VA s existing BD instruments, software, and antibodies currently in use, ensuring operational continuity and preventing the need to replace existing reagents or revalidate established testing protocols. Transitioning to a different manufacturer would require extensive staff retraining, significantly disrupt testing throughput, and delay critical patient reporting. By maintaining alignment with current system interfaces, workflows, and staff proficiency, BD equipment avoids these operational disruptions and supports uninterrupted delivery of diagnostic services. 3.
