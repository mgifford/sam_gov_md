The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), Network Contracting Office 2, VISN 02, is seeking sources that can provide three (3) large commercial grade, ride-on lawn mowers that at a minimum meets the following salient characteristics with a brand name or equal product for the VA Northport Healthcare System: The VA has a preference for domestically manufactured end items (manufactured in the United States). Background: VAMC Northport Grounds Department needs three new large, ride-on mowers to replace end-of-life mowers that are several decades old. The new mower should be large enough to handle large areas, sturdy enough to hold up to heavy usage in rough terrain and be easy to operate and repair. PHYSICAL SPECIFICATIONS: 1) Engine: Diesel 3-cylinder, liquid cooled engine.

36.8 HP @ 3,000 rpm. Torque peak 80.7 ft-lbs. @ 1,950 rpm. Displacement 1,642 cc. High idle: 3,150 rpm (no load). Remote mounted air cleaner. Fuel filter water separator. EPA Tier 4/Stage V compliant. 2) Radiator: Rear mount, cross flow, 8.5 fins per 1 , 8 quart capacity. 3) Fuel Capacity: 12 gallons 4) Traction Drive: Parallel hydraulic system with full time 4-wheel drive. 5) Ground Speed: Up to 15 mph going forward and up to 6 mph in reverse. 6) Mow Speed: Up to 15 mph going forward. 7) Moving Rate: Mows up to 8.7 acres per hour with 72 deck. 8) Main Frame: All welded tubular and formed steel. 9) Implement Lift: Twin 2.5 bore, 1.25 rod hydraulic cylinders operate lift arms. Hydraulic counterbalance for optimum traction and stability. 10) Tires: Front: 24x12-12.4 ply. Rear 18x9.5-8.4 ply. 11) Brakes: Dynamic braking through hydrostat and foot pedal. Parking Brake: Hand-actuated drum type on front left and right traction wheels. 12) Seat: Mechanical Suspension Seat 13) Steering: Hydraulic Power Steering. Tilt steering wheel released and locked by a foot actuated lever. 14) Operator Area: Operator step, beverage holder, control arm, and retractable seat belt 15) Instrumentation: Onboard LCD display that shows gauges, alerts/faults, service reminders, electrical system diagnostics. Indicates coolant temps, oil pressure, alternator voltage, engine hours and engine rpm. 16) Controls: Foot-operated pedal for forward and reverse. Manually adjustable forward speed limiter. Control arm hand-operated throttle, PTO switch, deck/implement lift lower switch, ignition switch. 17) Electrical: 12V maintenance-free battery with 540 cold cranking amps at 0 degrees. 80-minute reserve capacity at 80 degrees F. 18) Ground Clearance: 8.3 19) Length: 107 20) Width: 53.5 21) Wheelbase: 52 22) Weight: 1,886 lbs. 72 Inch Side Discharge Deck Recycler Kit Mechanical Suspension Seat Wrench Storage Bag Kit USA Charging Kit CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATION: Must be fully assembled upon delivery. Purchased components must meet two requirements. The component is top of the line in quality and replacement parts are locally available to the customer throughout the United States. WARRANTY: Prospective vendors shall be able to provide direct manufacturer support and shall directly issue a manufacturer's limited factor warranty of the government, should cover (2) two-years parts & labor, excluding batteries & tires. DELIVERY LOCATION: The delivery area is located: Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center, 79 Middleville Road, Building 36, Northport, New York 11768.
