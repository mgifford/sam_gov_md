# F117 Engine Rotator Kits

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-13 12:03:06.233-04
- Solicitation Number: FA813226Q0035

## Summary

The Air Force intends to solicit and award a firm fixed price, sole source purchase order to Hydratight Operations, Inc. (Antigo, Wisconsin; CAGE Code 87641), under the authority of RFO FAR 12.201-1, for Four (4) Enerpac F117 Engine Rotator Kits for the Oklahoma City Air Logistics Complex (OC-ALC). The NAICS code for this requirement is 336412 with a size standard of 1,500 employees. Contractor registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) – which results in receiving a unique, active CAGE Code – is required prior to receiving any contracts or orders from the Government in accordance with FAR 4.11. Contractors can register electronically at https://sam.gov/. Interested persons may identify their interest and capability to respond to the requirement by submitting a quote and information. Electronic procedures will be used for this solicitation through SAM at https://sam.gov/. No telephone requests.
