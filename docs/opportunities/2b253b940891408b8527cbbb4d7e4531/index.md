Notice of Intent to Sole Source Award THIS IS A NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD A SOLE SOURCE CONTRACT AND IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE QUOTES. U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Communications Branch intends to award a firm fixed price sole source under the authority of FAR subpart 13.106-1(b)(1)(i) and (ii), for purchases not exceeding the Simplified Acquisition Threshold (SAT), Contracting Officers may solicit from only one source if the circumstances deem only one source is reasonably available (e.g., urgency, exclusive licensing agreements, brand name, or industrial mobilization).: Calhoun Quarry, INC. 201 W Batchtown RD Batchtown, Illinois 62006-5074, United States The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this requirement is 212312, Crushed and Broken Limestone Mining and Quarrying, and the Product Service Code (PSC) for this requirement is 5610, Mineral Construction Materials, Bulk. The object of this sole source award is to require the purchase Furnish and deliver rock to Two Rivers National Wildlife Refuge. Method of delivery will be ¿tail gate spread¿ of aggregate stone (CA-6) .
