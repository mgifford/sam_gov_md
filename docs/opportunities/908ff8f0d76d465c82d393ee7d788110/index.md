# Intent to Award Sole Source Delivery/Task Order to Anascorp Distribution LLC for Anascorp Injection, Scorpion Antivenom, IHS, Whiteriver Service Unit, Whiteriver, AZ

## Summary

This is a notification of an intent to award three Delivery/Task Orders sole source contract to Anovorx Distribution LLC. under NAICS code 325412 – Pharmaceutical Preparation and Manufacturing for the Whiteriver Service Unit for a period of performance from June 1, 2026 to April 14, 2027 under the authority of FSS Limited Source Justification, Orders Against FSS, FAR FAR 8.405-6(a)(1)(i)(B)), Only One Source. The Phoenix Area Pharmacy Consultant is requesting one Delivery/Task Order Sole Source Award to Anovorx Distribution, LLC, distributor for Rare Disease Therapeutic Inc FSS #36F79718D0404, to provide Anascorp, Centroides (Scorpion) Immune F(ab’)2(Equine), Injection on a as needed basis from June 1, 2026 to April 14, 2027 for the clinics and hospitals under the Phoenix Area. Anovorx Distribution LLC will provide a Anascorp Injection on a As Needed Basis. Anovorx Distribution, LLC is the only contractor to offer ANASCORP [Centruroides (Scorpion) Immune F(ab’)2 (Equine) Injection] is an equine-derived antivenom indicated for treatment of patients with clinical signs of scorpion envenomation. Anascorp is composed of venom-specific F(ab’)2 fragments of immunoglobulin G (IgG) that bind and neutralize venom toxins, facilitating redistribution away from target tissues and elimination from the body. Physician will initiate treatment with Anascorp Injection as soon as possible after scorpion sting in patients who develop clinically important signs of scorpion envenomation, including but not limited to loss of muscle control, roving or abnormal eye movements, slurred speech, respiratory distress, excessive salivation, frothing at the mouth and vomiting. Anascrop Injection is widely used by the Southwest Regional Indian Health Services Hospitals and Clinics including Tucson Area, Albuquerque Area and Phoenix Area.
