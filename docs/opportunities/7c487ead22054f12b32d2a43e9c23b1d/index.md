## Summary

TITLE: FTW480 Fire Protection Repairs – Bldg 3030, Fort Wainwright, Alaska (FTW480) Identify set aside type PSC Code: Y1EZ Construction of Other Industrial Building NAICS Code: 236220 Commercial and Institutional Building Construction Solicitation Number: W911KB26RA038 This is a presolicitation notice only for a competitive Small Business set aside Request for Proposal (RFP) for Fire Protection Repair at Bldg 3030. The scope of this project is to bring building 3030 life safety systems into code compliance to provide a complete and usable facility. Scope of work will consist of removal and replacement of the fire suppression system, upgrade of the emergency lighting systems, and new existing pathways from office areas located within the warehouse bays. The estimated dollar magnitude of this project is anticipated between $10, 000, 00 and $15,000, 000. The performance period will be approximately 550 calendar days. The procurement will be conducted using two phase source selection procedures and the award will be based on best value tradeoff source selection procedures, considering the price and non-price factors cited in this announcement and solicitation. All Offerors must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) (https://www.sam.gov/SAM/). Joint ventures must be registered in SAM as the joint venture. Offerors are advised to begin this process when they prepare their proposal in order to ensure this registration is in place should they be selected for the award. Lack of registration in the SAM database will make an Offeror ineligible for award.
