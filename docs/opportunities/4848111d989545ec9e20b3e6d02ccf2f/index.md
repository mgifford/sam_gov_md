## Summary

The Department of War Education Activity (DoWEA) Fort Campbell Community School District requires comprehensive Integrated Pest Management (IPM) services to maintain a safe, healthy, and sanitary environment for students, faculty, and staff. The contractor shall provide all necessary management, labor, tools, general and specialized equipment, licenses, permits, bonds, and transportation to perform pest control services.
