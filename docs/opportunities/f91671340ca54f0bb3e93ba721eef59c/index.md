# FADEC Version 5.0

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 13:16:26.346-04
- Solicitation Number: N00019-25-RFPREQ-APM261-0312

## Summary

The Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR), NAS Patuxent River, MD, intends to negotiate and award a Sole-Source Order under Basic Ordering Agreement (BOA) N00019-25-G-0004 to General Electric Aerospace, 1000 Western Ave, Lynn, MA, 01905-2655, for the procurement of Full Authority Digital Engine Control (FADEC) V5.0 Software release. The proposed contract action(s) identified above are those for which the Government intends to solicit and negotiate with only one source under the authority of 10 U.S.C.3204(a)(1) and FAR 6.302-1, “Only One Responsible Source and No Other Type of Supplies or Services will Satisfy Agency Requirements.” Companies interested in subcontracting opportunities should contact GE: Ms. Brandie Roets. Director - Government Contracts, at Brandie.Roets@geaerospace.com.
