# Handheld XRF

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 07:58:04.995-04
- Solicitation Number: SP4706-26-Q-0012

## Summary

This future requirement is for the acquisition of a Handheld Portable XRF Analyzer. This equipment is used for quantitative and qualitative chemical analysis of metals and alloys as well as plating type and thickness. It is non-destructive, unless surface preparation is required, and is a necessary component for our lab to efficiently test these properties for our customers. For technical specifications and more information, reference the attached Statement of Work (SOW).
