# SeaPort-NxG Combat Systems Services (CSS) Follow-on Advance Notice

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 11:11:26.275-04
- Solicitation Number: N0017826R3103

## Summary

This is a follow-up to the Sources Sought/Advance Notice that was previously posted in the SeaPort-NxG portal. This is not a request for proposals and does not obligate the Government to award a contract or task order. This notice has been updated to include Industry Day Information. The Surface Combat Systems Center (SCSC) Wallops Island, VA requires support for management, engineering, operation, and technical expertise for activation, operations, maintenance and engineering of equipment, and systems and computer programs in support of all the Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) and Program Executive Office, Integrated Warfare Systems (PEO IWS) missions and projects performed at SCSC. The SCSC provides live and simulated integrated warfare capabilities in a net-centric, maritime environment to develop, test, evaluate, and conduct fleet operations and training for the warfighter. The SCSC facilities include the SCSC Command Headquarters area, the Captain Eric L. Washam USN AEGIS Engineering and Training Complex, the Ship Self Defense System (SSDS) Facility, the SCSC Sensor Test Site, and the Wallops Island Engineering Test Center (WIETC). THIS REQUIREMENT WILL BE SOLICITED AMONGST SEAPORT-NXG MAC HOLDERS ONLY.
