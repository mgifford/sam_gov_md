# Dell Latitude 7650 XCTO (210-BLQR) laptops

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 17:21:10.09-04
- Solicitation Number: OMAS-IHS-2026-0010

## Summary

PRESOLICITATION NOTICE Agency: Department of Health & Human Services (HHS) Operating Division: Indian Health Service (IHS) Location: Chinle Comprehensive Health Care Facility, Chinle, AZ Requirement: The Indian Health Service (IHS), Chinle Comprehensive Health Care Facility, intends to procure eleven (11) Dell Latitude 7650 XCTO (210-BLQR) laptops. Detailed specifications are provided in the attached Bill of Materials (BOM). Acquisition Approach: This requirement is for commercial products and will be conducted in accordance with FAR Part 12. The Government intends to use streamlined acquisition procedures to the maximum extent practicable. This acquisition will also be conducted in accordance with the Buy Indian Act and implementing regulations at HHSAR Subpart 326.6. Set-Aside Determination: This requirement is being considered under the Buy Indian Act and may be set aside for eligible Indian Economic Enterprises (IEE) or Indian Small Business Economic Enterprises (ISBEE). Applicable HHSAR Clauses and Provisions: HHSAR 352.226-4 – Notice of Indian Small Business Economic Enterprise Set-Aside HHSAR 352.226-5 – Notice of Indian Economic Enterprise Set-Aside HHSAR 352.226-6 – Indian Economic Enterprise Subcontracting Limitations HHSAR 352.226-7 – Indian Economic Enterprise Representation Submission Information: Interested vendors may submit quotes in response to this notice. Quotes shall be submitted via email to jesse.weidow@hhs.gov.
