# MCIA Geospatial Support Services (GSS)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-10 21:38:35.36-04
- Solicitation Number: M0026426R0010

## Summary

To view the updated solicitation and its attachments, interested parties shall go to www.SAM.gov. or the PIEE Solicitation Module. UPDATE: May 10, 2026 - the RFP is hereby amended, via M0026426R0010 Amendment 0002, to provide answers in response to questions. The solicitation documents have been revised accordingly. UPDATE: May 1, 2026 - the RFP is hereby extended through May 20, 2026, at 2:00PM Eastern Time. ORIGINAL: The scope of this effort is to provide support the Marine Corps Intelligence Activity (MCIA) with geospatial technical services to support integration and dissemination of geospatial information and intelligence products. This requirement is to provide the enhancements and technical support needed to sustain and improve the MCIA Geospatial Support Services (GSS) technology, the command centralized geospatial portal for data discovery, downloading, and sharing intelligence information across the various military, intelligence community, and mission partner network domains. This is a follow-on procurement of contract M00264-21-C-0010.
