## Summary

This is a COMBINED SYNOPSIS/SOLICITATION for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 36C25726Q0502 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ) for Mobile PET/CT Lease. This solicitation is issued as Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB), with the associated NAICS code of 532490, which has a small business size standard of $40 Million. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. The contractor shall provide the equipment and delivery of the Mobile PET/CT at Central Texas Veterans Health Care System (CTVHCS).
