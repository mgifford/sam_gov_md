# ScriptPro Maintenance RFI

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-27 13:35:32.934-04
- Solicitation Number: IHS1524457_ScriptPro_RFI

## Summary

The purpose of this RFI is to conduct market research, obtain technical information, and to determine the availability and technical capability of Indian Economic Enterprise (IEE) and Indian Small Business Enterprise (ISBEE) to provide ScriptPro pharmacy automation maintenance and support services. Responces will assist the Government in determining the appropriate acquisition method, including whether a set-aside is possible. See attached for details
