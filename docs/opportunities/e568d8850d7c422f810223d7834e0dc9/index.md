# Dugway Proving Ground Environmental Support / Central Hazardous Waste Services - RFP Amendment 0003

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 11:14:34.303-04
- Solicitation Number: W911S626RA001

## Summary

Amendment 0004 - Solicitation W911S626RA001 (Environmental Support Services - DPG) Description: The purpose of Amendment 0004 to Solicitation W911S626RA001 is to extend the proposal due date, formally incorporate mandatory cybersecurity clauses, add required Technical Exhibits, and provide responses to a portion of the industry questions received to date. Summary of Changes in Amendment 0004: Proposal Due Date: Extended to 15 June 2026 at 10:00 AM MDT Q&A Status: Added "Questions and Answers V2" containing responses to a subset of industry questions. Note: Additional questions remain in review. Cybersecurity Clauses: Formally incorporated FAR 52.240-93, DFARS 252.204-7012, 252.204-7021 (Level 2 Self), 252.204-7025 (Level 2 Self), and 252.240-7997. Technical Exhibits Added: Added TE-6 (Containers), TE-7 (90-Day Sites), and TE-8 (Title V Sources). Volume Page Limits: Volume I increased to 25 pages; Volume II increased to 35 pages. Submission Format: Clarified that all pricing data must be submitted within the single PDF for Volume I. Separate Excel files are not required. Quality Control Plan: Clarified that a full QCP is a post-award deliverable and is not required for proposal submission. Please review all attached documents carefully. Offerors must acknowledge receipt of all amendments by signing and returning page 1 of all SF 30s with their proposal submission. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Amendment 0003 - Environmental Support Services - W911S6-26-R-A001 Description: The purpose of Amendment 0003 to Solicitation W911S6-26-R-A001 is to extend the deadline for receipt of questions, provide Government responses to industry questions, update the Performance Work Statement (PWS) and Evaluation Criteria, incorporate new regulatory clauses, and attach additional documentation. Specifically, this amendment accomplishes the following: Extension of Question Deadline: The deadline for receipt of questions is extended from Monday, May 11, 2026, to Wednesday, May 13, 2026, at 10:00 AM MDT. Key Personnel Clarification: Updates PWS Sections 1.6.10 and 1.6.10.1 to explicitly clarify the qualification requirements for the Alternate Project/Site Manager position and updates the corresponding evaluation criteria in the Addendum to FAR 52.212-2. PWS and PRS Corrections: Incorporates the previously omitted PWS Section 5.10 (DTTF Inspection) and corrects the subsequent numbering and alignment discrepancies in PWS Sections 5.18 through 5.22 and the corresponding Performance Requirements Summary (PRS) matrix. PWS Air Program Updates: Updates the Clean Air Act (CAA) Technical Management section (PWS 5.16) to include preparation/revisions to the Dugway Title V Permit and Approval Order, and adds the requirement to gather periodic reporting from data calls for all Dugway and tenant activities for emission calculations. New Regulatory Requirement: Incorporates FAR Clause 52.222-90, Addressing DEI Discrimination by Federal Contractors, in accordance with Executive Order 14398. Q&A Incorporation: Attaches the formal Government responses to industry questions submitted to date during the solicitation Q&A period. Site Visit Documentation: Attaches the official Site Visit Presentation Slides and the Site Visit Attendee Roster. Cybersecurity Documentation: Attaches a new reference sheet outlining CMMC/Cybersecurity requirements for the contract. ADMINISTRATIVE NOTE REGARDING CLAUSES: Due to a technical error in the contract writing system, FAR Clause 52.203-6 (Alternate I) and FAR Clause 52.224-3 (Alternate I) are showing as "deleted" in the Contract Clauses section of the SF 30 document. This is a system error. These clauses are still applicable to this acquisition, remain in full force and effect, and will be incorporated into the final contract award. ACTION REQUIRED: Offerors must acknowledge receipt of all amendments to this solicitation by signing and returning the associated Standard Form 30s (SF 30) with their proposal submission, prior to the hour and date specified for receipt of offers. Failure to acknowledge all amendments may result in a proposal being determined unacceptable. All other terms, conditions, and the proposal due date remain unchanged. ATTACHMENTS INCLUDED IN THIS AMENDMENT: SF 30 - Amendment 0001 Revised Performance Work Statement (PWS) Revised Technical Exhibit 1 - Facilities Map ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Mission and Installation Contracting Command (MICC) - Dugway Proving Ground (DPG) has a requirement for comprehensive Environmental Support Services. The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, and supervision necessary to operate the DPG Central Hazardous Waste Storage Facility (CHWSF) and manage hazardous, non-regulated, and decontaminated chemical agent-related waste streams. Additionally, the Contractor shall execute regulatory compliance support to ensure DPG operates in accordance with its RCRA Part B Permit, Clean Air Act (CAA) Title V Permit, and English Village Landfill Permit. Core tasks include, but are not limited to: CHWSF, SWMU, and HWMU operations and inspections. RCRA and CAA Technical Management and regulatory reporting. Groundwater Monitoring, sampling, and data validation. Underground Storage Tank (UST) and TSCA (Asbestos/Lead-Based Paint) compliance. Hazardous Waste Generator formal and informal training. ACQUISITION STRATEGY & SET-ASIDE This requirement is being solicited as a 100% Total Small Business Set-Aside utilizing commercial procedures under FAR Part 12 and negotiated procedures under FAR Part 15. NAICS Code: 562112 (Hazardous Waste Collection) Size Standard: $47.0 Million Contract Type: Single-Award, Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) Period of Performance: One (1) month Phase-In, eleven (11) month Base Period, and four (4) 12-month Option Periods (Total duration not to exceed 66 months). Source Selection: Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA). SITE VISIT A site visit will be held at Dugway Proving Ground to allow offerors to inspect the CHWSF and general conditions. Date & Time: Monday, 4 May 2026 at 10:00 AM MDT RSVP Deadline: Offerors MUST register no later than 1:00 PM MDT on Wednesday, 29 April 2026. Registration: Email the Contract Specialist at amy.m.eilerts.civ@army.mil with the attendee’s Full Name, Company, Phone, US Citizen (Y/N), DOB, Driver's License # and State, and whether they hold a current CAC. SOLICITATION INQUIRIES & QUESTIONS All questions regarding this solicitation must be submitted in writing to the Contract Specialist at amy.m.eilerts.civ@army.mil no later than 10:00 AM MDT on Monday, 11 May 2026. The Government will publish official responses via a solicitation amendment on SAM.gov. PROPOSAL SUBMISSION Proposals are due no later than 10:00 AM MDT on Tuesday, 26 May 2026. Proposals shall be submitted electronically via email to amy.m.eilerts.civ@army.mil in accordance with the Addendum to FAR 52.212-1 (Instructions to Offerors) contained in the solicitation. CYBERSECURITY REQUIREMENTS (CMMC & SPRS) This contract requires the handling of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). In accordance with DFARS 252.204-7021 and 252.240-7997, offerors must have a current NIST SP 800-171 Assessment score posted in the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS) and meet the requirements for Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) prior to award.
