# Uniform Laundry Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 18:25:52.966-04
- Solicitation Number: FY26-UniformLaundry

## Summary

The Washington National Guard is conducting market research to determine the availability and size/status of sources capable of providing these services: The prospective contractor will provide shop rags/towels, floor mats and coveralls and laundry services for each. The Government requires pick-up and drop off laundry on a weekly basis. The prospective contractor will provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to perform services at the following items and locations: Seattle Sedro Wooley Montesano Fairchild Air Force Base Joint Base Lewis-McChord Yakima Training Center, Yakima Interested parties should respond to this request via email to dennis.k.jutras.civ@army.mil. Responses should include a brief description of how prospective contractors would perform the service or capabilites statement and the following information: - Location of business performing the services - Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) - Cage Code (if applicable) - SBA Business Size/Status - Other than Small Business, Small Business, or specific Small Disadvantaged Business category **Interested parties must be registered in SAM with the NAICS code 812332, with pick-up and drop off laundry services being part of their existing business model.
