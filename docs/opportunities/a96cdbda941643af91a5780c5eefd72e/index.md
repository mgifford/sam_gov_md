# IMF FF&E Bay Items

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 13:13:56.696-04
- Solicitation Number: FA460826QC079

## Summary

Combined Synopsis – Solicitation This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products prepared in accordance with RFO Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number FA460826QC079 is issued as a Request for Quote for one (1) IMF FF&E Bay Items IAW Attachment 2 – Salient Characteristics, Attachment 3 – Statement of Work, Attachment 4 - Justification and Approval. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The associated North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) number is 337127. The small business size standard is 500 employees. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. Notice to Offerors: The government reserves the right to cancel this solicitation, either before or after the closing date for receipt of quotations. In the event the government cancels this solicitation, the government has no obligation to reimburse an offerer for any costs. If funds are not available to fund the entire contract value, the Government reserves the right to re-evaluate quotations by unit price per item and issue an award on a SOME, NONE, or ALL basis for each individual item needed. Description of Requirement: This is a purchase and delivery requirement for one (1) IMF FF&E Bay Items IAW the attached documents. This acquisition is on behalf of the 2d Munitions Squadron (2 MUNS) located at Barksdale AFB, LA, and must conform to the customer's salient characteristics. Delivery Terms: Freight on Board (FOB) Destination. Contract Line Item Number (CLIN): 0001 Product/Service Description: IMF FF&E Bay Items IAW Attachment 2 – Salient Characteristics, Attachment 3 – Statement of Work, Attachment 4 - Justification and Approval Quantity: 1 Unit: Each Best Value Determination: Contract award will be based on price/technical to determine best value. Due Date and Time: Quotes must be submitted via email to the Contract Specialist and Contracting Officer listed below by 2:00 PM CDT, Friday, 05 June 2026. The following attachments are applicable to this RFQ: Attachment 1 – Provisions and Clauses Attachment 2 – Salient Characteristics Attachment 3 – Statement of Work Attachment 4 – Brand Name Justification and Approval Points of Contact (POC): Contract Specialist: SSgt Hector S.
