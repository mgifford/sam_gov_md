## Summary

THIS SOURCE SOUGHT/REQUEST FOR INFORMATION IS RESTRICTED TO DEPARTMENT OF INTERIOR/FISH AND WILDLIFE SERVICE AE MATOC HOLDERS - ARKANSAS. Other SF-330 submittals will not be considered or opened. Description: Source Sought/Request for information from DOI-FWS ARKANSAS CIVIL AE MATOC HOLDERS ONLY. The design engineer to cost shall include shall include the preparation of plans (drawings), specifications, a schedule of items, construction cost estimate, and an estimate of construction time. A 65% submittal, 90% and 100% final submission of these documents shall be provided. This project is for the design of the Albert Pike Campground and will provide design for (summized): Provide an asphalt loop road connecting to NFSR 73. Roadway shall be placed above the 100-yr flood elevation and have a ditched cross-section with maximum grades of 8%. new bridge designs as required to cross major drainages asphalt surfaced camping loops 8 camping spurs with water and 50A electrical service. 19 campsites without water and electricity 1 camp host site with water, 50A electrical service, telephone, and sewer connections. 2 CXT Double Cascadian or equivalent vault restrooms. new RV dump station with a septic system, including septic tank, distribution boxes, and field lines minimum 5’ wide pedestrian access from campsites new well house, reservoir, and all equipment including well pump, valves, 20A electric receptacle, and piping. community water hydrants at each new vault restroom and an additional 4 community water hydrants dispersed throughout the 19 campsites without individual water connections new kiosks, signage, and lighting Grade site and install ditches, culverts, and other drainage measures to allow water to drain away from all improvements Layout of campground and construction materials shall be designed by a registered Landscape Architect. 1. AGENCY: USDA-FS CSA11 2. NAICS: 541330 - Engineering Services / PSC Code C1PA Architect and Engineering- Construction: Recreational Facilities (Non-Building) 3. PLACE OF PERFORMANCE: Engineering Office (Best Qualified Firm) 4.
