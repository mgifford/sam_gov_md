ANY PRICE OFFERS RECIEVED WILL NOT BE REVIEWED / ACCEPTED AT THIS TIME. Synopsis: NASA/NSSC has a requirement for Battelle NEON Research Support Services (NRSS). NASA/NSSC intends to issue a sole source contract to BATTELLE MEMORIAL INSTITUTE, 505 King AVE Columbus, Ohio 43201-2696, under the authority of FAR 12.102(a). The Government intends to acquire Commercial items or services using the provisions, clauses and procedures prescribed in FAR Part 12 for Commercial The NAICS Code for this procurement is 513210.
