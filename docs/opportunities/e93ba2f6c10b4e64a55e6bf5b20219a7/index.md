# 250 KS4 (RIMSS) Special Support Equipment (SSE)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 14:08:46.307-04
- Solicitation Number: SIMACQ-PD-42-0049

## Summary

The Surface Combatant/Gas Turbines Engineering Branch (Code 424) of the Naval Surface Warfare Center, Philadelphia Division (NSWCPD) is the In-Service Engineering Agent (ISEA) for the Ship Service Gas Turbine Generator (SSGTG). The Navy is establishing intermediate-level (I-level) procedures for the replacement of turbine assemblies at Regional Maintenance Center (RMC) turbine shops. This initiative will provide the Navy with a repair capability for 250-KS4s not previously possessed, significantly reducing repair turnaround times and enabling the refurbishment of previously unusable carcasses.
