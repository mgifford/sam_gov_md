# Gillig Bus Repairs for Yosemite NP

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 19:32:57.881-04
- Solicitation Number: 140P8526Q0034

## Summary

DESCRIPTION. The National Park Service has a requirement for the transportation, diagnostic, and repair of Gillig manufactured diesel-electric hybrid buses in support of Yosemite National Park. This is a COMBINED SYNOPSIS / SOLICITATION for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 140P8526Q0034 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ). This solicitation is a 100% total small business set-aside in accordance with FAR 19.104-1, and the NAICS code is 811114 with an associated small business size standard of $9.0 million in average annual receipts. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clause may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. The government intends to award a single firm fixed price contract resulting from this solicitation to the responsible Offeror (pursuant to FAR subpart 9.1) whose offer conforming to the solicitation will be most advantageous to the government, price and other factors considered. The following factors shall be used to evaluate offers: price, technical capability, and past performance. QUOTES ARE DUE AT 5:00 P.M. PACIFIC TIME (PT) ON FRIDAY, May 29, 2026. Only emailed quotes will be accepted. Questions regarding this solicitation must be submitted in writing via email to lisa_henson@ios.doi.gov and reference the solicitation number in the subject line of the email, e.g., '140P8526Q0034 QUESTION.' Questions must be received by May 19, 2026, by 1:00 p.m. (PT). Questions received after this date may or may not be addressed. The government reserves the right to cancel this solicitation.
