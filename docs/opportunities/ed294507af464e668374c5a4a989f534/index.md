## Summary

NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD A SOLE SOURCE CONTRACT. THIS NOTICE IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE QUOTATIONS. This announcement constitutes the only notice that will be published. The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) intends to award a sole-source, firm-fixed-price, Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) with four (4) option years to Promega Corporation, located at 2800 Woods Hollow RD., Fitchburg, Wisconsin 53711, for the purchase of consumables.
