# Remanufacture of C-130 Fuel Boost Pump

## Summary

PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE IAW RFO 5.101(a) The United States Air Force through Air Force Sustainment Center (AFSC), Tinker AFB 73145 is contemplating issuing a firm fixed price IDIQ Type contract to support the remanufacture of the C-130 Fuel Boost Pump(s). The Fuel Boost Pump transfers fuel from the C-130's aircraft fuel tanks to the aircraft's engines. The Fuel Boost Pump has dual impellers to allow fuel to be supplied to the engines during both normal and inverted flights. The contractor shall provide all labor, facilities, equipment and all material to accomplish remanufacture. The work encompasses the disassembly cleaning, inspection, maintenance re-assembly, testing, and finishing actions required to return the item to a like-new condition in accordance with the solicitation requirements and all attachments. A firm fixed price Indefinite Delivery Requirements type contract is contemplated with a 5-year basic. The requirements set forth in this notice are defined per Purchase Request FD20302501813 as follows: Written response is required.
