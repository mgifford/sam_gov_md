# Maintenance Service that replaces current Truck Model Bed&Crane Assembly from old Chassis with a New Chassis

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 16:13:45.813-04
- Solicitation Number: 031014

## Summary

This contract is in efforts to obtain and or increase competition sought for re-solicitation of Vehicular Maintenance Service, located at McAlester Army Ammunition Plant (McAAP). The services are for replacing the current Manitex Model SC 97 Bed & Crane Assembly from old GSA Chassis and switching it to New GSA Chassis: Old GSA Chassis - G82-0536W International 7500 HT 570 New GSA Chassis - G81-C0047 Freightliner 114SD VIN: 1FVHG3FE4THWV5288, 11/2025 —54,600 GVWR Eaton Fuller 10 Speed Manual Transmission Key Service Tasks Remove and Install Old PTO Unit on new GSA Chassis Remove Crane and Install on New GSA Chassis Remove and Install Bed Assembly w/Outriggers on new GSA Chassis Replace and Replace any Hoses deemed unserviceable/leaking Remove and Reinstall Wiring Remove and Reinstall all Hydraulic Controls Remove and Reinstall Hydraulic Tank Please ask for further information in the Statement of Work and attachments.
