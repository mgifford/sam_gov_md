## Summary

THIS SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE IS SET ASIDE FOR SMALL BUSINESSES and small businesses only. This is a source sought notice for the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Mount Weather Emergency Operations Center located at 19844 Blue Ridge Mountain Road, Bluemont, VA 20135. All responses to this source sought shall be based on the description of potential requirements, found below, and the attached Specification list This notice is a Request for Information (RFI) only and is not a solicitation for proposals. The intent of this notice is to assist the Government in conducting market research to evaluate the availability of capable firms within industry. Requests for solicitation will not be responded to and no contracts shall be awarded based on any responses received from interested parties. Any information submitted in response to this sources sought notice is strictly voluntary. The Federal Emergency Management Agency is seeking prospective offerors to provide Audio Visual Broadcast Studio and Media Center Equipment support in accordance with the attached specification list. Location: Mount Weather Emergency Operations Center 19844 Blue Ridge Mountain Road, Mt. Weather, VA 20135.
