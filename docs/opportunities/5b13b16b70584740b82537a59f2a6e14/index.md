# RFI-36C25626Q0591- Elevator Preventive Maintenance and Repair Services for base year plus (4) year ordering periods at the G.V.Sonny Montgomery VAMC, Jackson MS 39216

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 10:30:13.719-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C25626Q0591

## Summary

The G.V. Sonny Montgomery VA Medical Center, Jackson MS 39216 is requesting a contractor to provide Elevator maintenance and repair services. The service/performance is to include inspections, parts, testing, and repair services for 17 elevators, 2 cart lifts and 3 dumbwaiters located at this facility, in accordance with the terms and conditions stated within the Performance Work Statement (PWS). Base Year: 10-01- 2026 to 09-30- 2027 Ordering Period 1: 10-01-2027 to 09-30-2028 Ordering Period 2: 10-01-2028 to 09-30-2029 Ordering Period 3: 10-01-2029 to 09-30-2030 Ordering Period 4: 10-01-2030 to 09-30-2031
