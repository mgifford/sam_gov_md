# Digital Badging and Micro-credentialing Solution

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 13:02:52.878-04
- Solicitation Number: FA330026Q0731

## Summary

The 42d Contracting Squadron, Maxwell AFB, AL is seeking sources that can provide for commercial digital badging and micro-credentialing solution to support enterprise-wide faculty and student development. The system will provide a scalable automated capability to issue, manage, and verify digital credentials aligned with modernizing learning, incentivizing faculty development and enhancing recognition of competency-based achievement IAW the Statement of Requirement (SOR).
