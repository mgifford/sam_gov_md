## Summary

The contractor shall perform development activities to update the design of the existing Multi-Element Anti-Jam GNSS Technology for Navigation Operation and Monitoring (MAGNOM) Position, Navigation, and Timing (PNT) Circuit Card Assembly (CCA) to incorporate Hard Problem #1 (HP#1) Security architecture and conduct Hard Problem #2 (HP#2) trade study to determine recommended design architecture.
