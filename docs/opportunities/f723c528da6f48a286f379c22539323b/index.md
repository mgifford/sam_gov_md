SCOPE: This requirement is for the procurement and delivery of hydraulic diamond wire saw systems and associated components to support execution of the Wilson Main Lock Stoplog Liner Repair Project. The Government requires the purchase of complete hydraulic diamond wire saw systems including all components necessary for full operation in heavy duty structural concrete cutting applications. The equipment must be delivered fully assembled as applicable, new, and fully operational upon delivery. The Government’s existing inventory includes Diamond Products CP40EXL and WS25 wire saw systems which establishes a Brand Name requirement for compatibility and interoperability. The use of Diamond Products equipment ensures that components, accessories, and replacement parts remain interchangeable with existing Government owned equipment. The Government anticipates a Firm Fixed-Price (FFP) Supply Contract with a Brand Name (Diamond Products) requirement. DESCRIPTION OF EQUIPMENT: The Contractor shall furnish and deliver hydraulic diamond wire saw systems manufactured by Diamond Products including all required components for full system functionality. At a minimum, the system shall include a hydraulic power unit (approximately 40HP, 460V), wire saw assembly, control station, and feed hoses (minimum 25 feet in length). All associated accessories, including but not limited to hoses, fittings, and system components shall be compatible with Diamond Products systems. Additional equipment such as pulleys, rigging components, and related accessories may be included in this procurement depending on final operational requirements. In addition to new system procurement, Diamond Products replacement parts may be required to support rehabilitation and continued operation of existing Government owned wire saw systems. The anticipated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) Code to this acquisition is 332216, Saw Blade and Handtool Manufacturing, Size Standard – 750 employees. SHIPMENT/DELIVERY: Delivery shall be made to the Tennessee River Operations Center (TROC) at Wilson Lock Florence, AL. Address and delivery details will be provided in the solicitation.
