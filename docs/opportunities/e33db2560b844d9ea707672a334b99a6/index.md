## Summary

Construction Indefinite-Delivery Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) Multiple Award Task Order Contract (MATOC) for maintenance, repair, and construction services in support of the National Guard activities in the State of South Dakota. These awards are multi-disciplined contracts consisting of two five-calendar year ordering periods. The total of individual task orders placed against this contract shall not exceed $25,000,000.00. This action is awarded as a total small business set-aside. The MATOC will include contracts W50S9626DA010 - W50S9626DA022.
