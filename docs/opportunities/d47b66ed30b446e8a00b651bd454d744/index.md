However, interested parties may identify their interest and capability to respond to this requirement no later than?5/19/2026 4:00 pm PST. Contractors must be certified through Defense Logistics Information Services (DLIS) in order to access Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or Export Controlled Information.
