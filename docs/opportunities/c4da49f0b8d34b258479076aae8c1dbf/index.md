# Library of Congress NAVCC - Digital/Analog Audio Equipment Repair

- Agency: LIBRARY OF CONGRESS
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 14:46:26.405-04
- Solicitation Number: 030ADV26Q0145

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation in accordance with the procedures of FAR Part 13. The Library of Congress (Library) requires contractor support to provide maintenance and support for digital audio playback/recording equipment installed in the Culpeper facility for the NAVCC Audio Lab. The contractor shall repair, refurbish, or replace digital audio playback/recording equipment to maintain digital/analog audio preservation performance and quality standards. See Solicitation document for full details including a list (in Section C) of the equipment to be services under the contract which includes digital and analog audio equipment. This is a lowest priced technically acceptable competition. Please see the attached solicitation document for more details including the Statement of Work, Instructions to Offerors (Section L), and the Evaluation/Award procedures (Section M). Responses are due by 06/12/2026 at 12:00PM EST. Late offers will not be accepted.
