## Summary

AMENDMENT 0001: The purpose of the Amendment 0001 is to extend the deadline for receipt of quotes from 27 May 2026 at 11:00AM Central Time to 03 June 2026 at 11:00AM Central TIme. ***************************************************************** Title: Mill Springs Mill Operator Services Classification Code: M1JA, Operation of Museums and Exhibition buildings NAICS: 712120, Historical Sites Type of Set Aside: Total Small Business PROJECT DESCRIPTION: The Nashville District Contracting Division has a requirement that will require the Contractor to furnish all labor, equipment, fuel, transportation, tools, and supplies necessary to provide the specified milling and interpretive services at Mill Springs Mill, Lake Cumberland, KY for the duration of the contract period. Mill Springs Mill is located approximately 15 miles from Somerset and 5 miles from Monticello on Highway 1275 in Wayne County, Kentucky.
