# Cedar River Left Bank - Industry Day

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-28 09:30:34.634-04

## Summary

Agency/Office: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Location: USACE Northwestern Division – Seattle (NWS) District Title: INDUSTRY DAY – CEDAR RIVER LEFT BANK LEVEE REHABILITATION LOCATION: Onsite. The onsite visit is restricted to two personnel per company. Follow pre-registration steps provided below. DATE: 02 June 2026 TIME: 09:00 AM to 11:00 PM PST DESCRIPTION: The United States Army Corps of Engineers (USACE), Northwestern Division - Seattle District (NWS) is hosting an Onsite Industry Day on 02 June 2026 to provide additional information and gain feedback from industry on an upcoming requirement for the rehabilitation of the Cedar Authorized Left Bank Levee in Renton, Washington. The Government is seeking feedback from qualified, experienced sources capable of performing this type of project. The Seattle District, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers has a requirement to repair and rehabilitate approximately 1,000 linear feet of the Cedar Authorized Left Bank Levee, which was damaged during severe flooding in December 2025. The levee is an approximately 6,400-foot-long system of earthen embankment, steel and concrete floodwalls, and closure structures, providing critical flood protection for residential, commercial, and industrial properties, as well as the Renton Municipal Airport. The December 2025 flood event stripped topsoil and displaced riprap, reducing the levee’s level of protection from a 120-year event (0.0083 AEP) to a 3-year event (0.33 AEP). Rehabilitation activities include: 1. Removal of displaced riprap and loose material. 2. Reconstruction of the riverward slope. 3. Repair of approximately 600-800 linear feet of levee, including transitions. 4. Restoration of the levee to its pre-damage level of protection. The contract solicitation and award are planned for this summer. The anticipated NAICS code may be NAICS code is 237990, Other Heavy and Civil Engineering, Construction. The proposed project is anticipated to be a competitive, firm-fixed-price contract. You must send an email to Della Overton, Contract Specialist, at della.s.overton@usace.army.mil and Otis Hatfield, Project Manager, at Otis.w.hatfield@usace.army.mil, and reference the Cedar River Levee Rehabilitation Project Onsite Industry Day in the subject line. Please respond by 1 June 2026 at 4:00 PM PST. Required personal protective equipment for the onsite visit includes safety vest and boots/safety shoes. Late respondents, those not pre-registered, or those not providing the correct information will not be granted access to the site. Participation is limited to two representatives from each interested entity. Attendance participation will be available after the industry day via SAM.gov. ATTENDING THE SITE VISIT DAY IS HIGHLY RECOMMENDED SINCE IT IS UNLIKELY THAT AN ADDITIONAL SITE VISIT WILL BE OFFERED UNTIL THE SOLICITATION PERIOD.
