## Summary

The United States Department of the Naval Medical Research Command (NMRC) proposes to enter into a contract with Meso Scale Discovery in Rockville, MD, on a basis of other than full and open competition. This requirement is for authorized approved vendors to perform services for brand name equipment. This notice is not a request for competitive proposals however If interested, the Government shall consider capability statements. NMRC's Infectious Diseases/CWID Director is in need of an assay that will allow for the detection of biomarkers that are present at very low levels in a variety of tissue samples.
