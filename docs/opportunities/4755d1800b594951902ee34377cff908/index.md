# STAT Laboratory Courier Services

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 10:11:57.001-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C24125Q0455

## Summary

RFQ: Department of Veterans Affairs - STAT Courier Services The Department of Veterans Affairs intends to award a Firm Fixed-Price Blanket Purchase Agreement for STAT and Non-STAT Courier Services for Blood and Blood Products to support the Laboratory/Pathology Departments within VISN 1. Performance will be for a period of five (5) years. The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, tools, materials, vehicles, and supervision necessary to perform this service in accordance with the attached Performance Work Statement for all VISN 1 locations; as well as local, state, and federal regulations. Quotes must be submitted by Friday February 20th, 2026 @3:00PM EST. This amended solicitation is open only to those interested parties which have submitted initial packages by the original deadline of 10/31/2025, 3PM EST. Late offers will not be considered.
