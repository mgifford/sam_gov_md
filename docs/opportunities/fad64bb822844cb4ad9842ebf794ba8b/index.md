## Summary

Motorized Trail Equipment Rental. Rent a track loader, concrete buggy, and a mini-excavator with trailers to conduct trail maintenance for Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest. Equipment is used to maintain 130 miles or mechanized trails on the Great Divide and Medford Park Falls districts.
