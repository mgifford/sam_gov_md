# Agricultural Cleaning Services at Subic Bay, Philippines

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-16 00:04:27.15-04
- Solicitation Number: M0031826QG013

## Summary

This is a solicitation for commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. Solicitation number M0031826QG013 is issued as a Request for Quote (RFQ) for non-personnel services to provide agricultural cleaning of U.S. military equipment in support of the 3d Marine Littoral Regiment's (MLR) redeployment from the Republic of the Philippines. The contractor shall provide all necessary personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, and supervision to perform agricultural cleaning and pressure washing services for 3d MLR vehicles and equipment in accordance with the Performance Work Statement. This acquisition is not set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov.
