# Pre-Season SCBA Physical and Fit Testing - Shasta Trinity, Klamath, & Modoc NF

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 16:23:58.258-04

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 127EAY26Q0058 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for pre-season SCBA Physical and Fit testing and Spirometry testing under the mandatory SCBA Program for engine personnel on the Shasta-Trinity, Modoc, and Klamath National Forests. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 624230 – Emergency and Other Relief Services. The small business size standard is $41.5M. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
