## Summary

The United States Department of Agriculture (USDA), Forest Service (FS), Intermountain Region, has a need for snow removal at two locations UWC NF Supervisors Office and Salt Lake City Ranger District (South Jordan, UT and Salt Lake City, UT). The Contractor shall furnish equipment, supplies, labor, transportation and supervision necessary to perform the required services. Both locations are currently occupied by Forest Service Employees and designated as Forest Service property.
