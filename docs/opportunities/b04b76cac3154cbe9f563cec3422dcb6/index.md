# Beckman Coulter Consumables IDIQ

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-22 15:50:02.285-04
- Solicitation Number: N0018926QB013

## Summary

This requirement is for consumable supplies for high speed immunochemistry screening analyzer systems. The consumable supplies shall be compatible with the currently installed Beckman Coulter AU5800 series clinical chemistry analyzers. The supplies required are manufactured by Beckman Coulter. Vendor shall be an Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) authorized dealer, authorized distributor or authorized reseller for the proposed equipment/system such that OEM warranty and service are provided and maintained by the OEM. All software licensing, warranty and service associated with the equipment/system shall be in accordance with the OEM terms and conditions.
