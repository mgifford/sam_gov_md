## Summary

**THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT ONLY, ANY COORESPONDANCE IS STRICTLY FOR MARKET ANALYSIS** This is a sources sought to install a video intercom system at the the air traffic control tower at Ellsworth AFB, South Dakota. All requirements to conduct work must be met and work shall be performed in accordance with the attached Statement of Objectives (SOO) and in coordination with the 28 OG on Ellsworth AFB. Please indicate if your company is willing and capable to perform the requested services via the below contact information. SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS: Interested parties who believe they are capable of meeting this requirement are invited to submit a capability statement. The statement must include, at a minimum: Company Information: Company name, address, UEI, CAGE code, point of contact (including name, title, phone, and email), and any active GSA contract numbers in all correspondence. Business Size & Socio-Economic Status: Please identify your business size (Large, Small, etc.) and any socio-economic categories (e.g., 8(a), Woman-Owned Small Business, HUBZone, Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business) under NAICS code 238210. Please submit your capability statement via email to mitchell.bryk@us.af.mil and jayna.clements@us.af.mil no later than 18 May 2026 at 3:00 P.M. MDT. Telephone inquiries will not be accepted. All correspondence shall be made through email. Thank you for your interest in this requirement.
