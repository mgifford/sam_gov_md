# D--IT, Professional & Administrative Support

- Agency: ENERGY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-08 10:46:37.145-04
- Solicitation Number: 89503626QSW000212

## Summary

The purpose of this Sources Sought Notice is to accomplish market research pursuant to FAR Part 10, and to identify qualified small businesses in the following categories 8(a), Women-Owned, Service Disabled Veteran Owned, and HUBZone that are capable of and interested in providing the Information Technology (IT), Professional & Administrative support. Additionally, this notice seeks to determine if the services can be acquired using a GSA Schedule or Governmentwide Acquisition Contracts (GWAC). Please see Sources Sought notice and the attached Drafts Scope of Work. This notice is issued solely for information and planning purposes and does not constitute a(n) Invitation for Bid (IFB), Request for Quotation (RFQ), or Request for Proposal (RFP). Additionally, there is no obligation or commitment on the part of the Government to acquire the products or services described within this notice or to conduct a solicitation for the listed services now or in the future. The Government does not intend to award a contract(s) based solely on the submissions of this notice. Respondents are advised that the Government will not pay for information submitted in response to this Sources Sought notice, nor will it compensate respondents for any costs incurred in the development/furnishing of a response. A decision not to submit a response will not preclude a vendor from participating in any future solicitation.
