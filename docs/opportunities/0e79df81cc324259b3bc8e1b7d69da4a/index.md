## Summary

Amendment 0002: The purpose of this amendment is to extend the quote due date, provide Government responses to questions, incorporate the resulting revisions into the technical specifications, instructions, and evaluation criteria, revise CLIN description, and insert FAR Clause 52.222-90. Amendment 0001: The purpose of Amendment 0001 is to The purpose of amendment one is to: (1) Add CLIN 0003; (2) Modify CLIN 0001 and CLIN 0002; (3) Update Attachment 1 "Technical Specifications Document", Attachment 2 "Section L- Instructions, Conditions, and Notices to Quoters," and Attachment 3 "Section M -Evaluation Factors for Award; and (4) Post Questions and Answers. The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency is seeking a dedicated, managed high-performance computing (HPC) solution that includes compute hardware, network-attached storage, high-speed networking, robust security controls, and comprehensive user support. The solution must meet the performance, availability, and security standards detailed herein to support the Network of Optimal Dynamic Energy Signatures (NODES) program's scientific objectives. The NODES program seeks to enable distributed, data-driven biological discovery by integrating experimental platforms, large-scale biological datasets, and advanced computational methods. NODES performers generate and analyze substantial volumes of heterogeneous biological data and rely on computational workflows to enable rapid hypothesis generation, model development, and validation across a networked ecosystem.
