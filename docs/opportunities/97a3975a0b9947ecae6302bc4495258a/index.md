# Gavins Point Powerhouse Drain Rehab

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 17:05:49.558-04
- Solicitation Number: W9128F26QA054

## Summary

Solicitation The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Omaha District, hereby announces the solicitation for “Gavins Point Powerhouse Drain Rehab”. The solicitation will close on 19 June 2026. The solicitation is restricted to Small Businesses. Project Description: This contract includes the rehabilitation of drain lines of 3-inch, 4-inch, and 6-inch diameters located on the B-Floor (EL. 1161.0) within the powerhouse at Gavins Point Dam, Yankton, South Dakota. The scope of work includes the removal of a lodged pipe snake cutter head, mechanical cleaning and CCTV inspections of piping, and providing Cured-In-Place Pipe (CIPP) lining for the rehabilitation. The estimated lengths of each line are: • 3-inch: 130 ft • 4-inch: 170 ft • 6-inch: 130 ft The Contractor will be required to commence work upon receipt of contract award and complete the work 120 calendar days after receipt of Notice to Proceed, this is subject to change after solicitation issuance. Estimated Construction Costs: The estimated construction cost of this project is between $100,000 and $250,000. NAICS Code: The Primary North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this solicitation is 238220, Plumbing Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors, with a size standard for Small Business of $19M.
