# Levee Rehab - Materials

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-11 16:20:35.149-04
- Solicitation Number: W912DW26Q1BML

## Summary

This Sources Sought Notice is being issued for market research purposes to determine the availability, capability, and interest of small business firms for a potential Government requirement. NO SOLICITATION IS AVAILABLE; requests for a solicitation will go unanswered. No award will be made from this notice. Responses will be used to determine potential acquisition strategies, understand current market capabilities, and to gain knowledge of potential sources. The Seattle District USACE anticipates a potential opportunity for the maintenance and repair for as many as 14 levees across five Washington State river basins: Nooksack, Skagit, Snoqualmie, Cedar and Green. Repairs are necessary before the next fall flood season and the target completion date for all work is 1 October 2026. If market conditions indicate this estimate may be unrealistic, respondents are encouraged to provide specific information (such as known equipment lead times or other timeline concerns) for Government consideration when responding to this Sources Sought Notice. This announcement is for the provision of materials. The following is a list of commonly procured materials for previous levee work. Riprap Embankment Material Top Course Stones Spall Rock Topsoil Mulch Clay Quarry Spalls Quarry Stone Crushed Quarry Stone Recycled Asphalt Concrete Jersey Barrier The North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for the potential requirement is anticipated to be 212319, Other Crushed and Broken Stone Mining and Quarrying. The small business size standard for the NAICS, as established by the U.S. Small Business Administration, is 550 employees. Please include the Sources Sought Notice number in the e-mail subject line. Include your company’s UEI and CAGE code in your response. We encourage you to add your name to the Interested Vendors List at this announcement.
