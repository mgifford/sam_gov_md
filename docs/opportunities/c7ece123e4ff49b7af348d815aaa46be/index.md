# Rail Curves Package 3 for MOTCO, CA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 18:22:03.325-04
- Solicitation Number: W9123826BA011

## Summary

Rail Curve Package 3 for MOTCO, Concord, CA The Government anticipates issuing an Invitation for Bid (IFB) with the intent of awarding a Firm-Fixed Price construction contract for MOTCO Rail Curve Package 3 – DBB Construction. Project Description: Scope of this project is the repair of approximately 2,500 LF by replacement, including repairs of ties, rails, turnouts, rail crossings and other track material for multiple rail curves on the tidal side of the installation at Military Ocean Terminal, Concord (MOTCO). The base bid package includes repair by replacement of curves 9 & 10 with curvature less than 12 degrees. Tracks will be constructed per standards in UFC 4-860-01FA and American Railway Engineering and Maintenance of Way Association (AREMA). The following work will be included as contract options: UXO clearance and transportation, UXO blow-in-place, Mission stand down, Wetland mitigation credit purchase All contractor and subcontractor personnel performing work under this contract must be citizens of the United States. U.S. citizenship is a prerequisite for obtaining the required Department of War (DoW) personnel security clearances. The contractor shall be responsible for ensuring all personnel comply with this requirement and all applicable federal security regulations. The solicitation will proceed as a total small business set-aside and is estimated to be issued mid- June 2026 with bids due tentatively in late-July 2026. The IFB will be issued on the Government Point of Entry known as SAM.gov and will establish a firm bid due date/time. The magnitude of construction for the anticipated project is between $6,000,000 and $9,000,000. This solicitation is anticipated to be issued using the North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code 237990 (Other Heavy and Civil Engineering Construction). The small business size standard is $45,000,000. Product Service Code is Z2LB (Repair or Alteration of Highways/roads/streets/bridges/railways). If applicable, the site visit date, time and location will be identified in the solicitation. BE ADVISED THAT THIS PROJECT MAY BE DELAYED, CANCELLED OR REVISED AT ANY TIME DURING THE PRE-SOLICITATION, SOLICITATION, SELECTION, EVALUATION, NEGOTIATION AND/OR FINAL AWARD PROCESS.
