# Notice of Intent to Award a BPA Call Order against The Land Management Integrated Resources BPA (LMIR) for Red-cockaded Woodpecker Monitoring, National Forests in North Carolina

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 14:22:47.682-04
- Solicitation Number: 12445026Q0022

## Summary

BPA Call Order Exceeding $25,000 Synopsis Requisition 1166547. The USDA Forest Service (FS), is issuing this notice. The USDA FS intends on awarding a competitive BPA Call Order against the LMIR BPA for 12445026Q0022 – (RCW) Red-cockaded Woodpecker Monitoring, National Forests in North Carolina. Original documents pertaining to the LMIR Blanket Purchase Agreement can be found in the attachments located at https://sam.gov/opp/8f26188b0e7445b3b4f9bb71484448f4/view (section of "Attachments/Links.") The Land Management Integrated Resources BPA (LMIR), 12363N23Q4023, is a National BPA that consists of a large variety of work including Professional Services, Natural Resources Restoration, Engineering, Project Management, NEPA, Communications, and more. The BPA can be utilized on all Forests and Grasslands and is available for all federal agencies to utilize. This is not a solicitation. This synopsis notification of intent to award a BPA Call order does not constitute a request for proposals or submission of any information in response to this notification. This procurement is restricted to those approved companies eligible for call orders under the Land Management Integrated Resources BPA (LMIR) for USFS. The LMIR BPA has continuous pre-solicitation sharing so that contractors may submit a proposal to join the BPA at any point in time while the BPA is active. If your firm would like to join, please contact the LMIR BPA Team at: SM.FS.lmirbpa@usda.gov Please send any questions to heather.harkley@usda.gov and nestor.riveragonzalez@usda.gov no later than 11:59 pm, EDT, May 14, 2026.
