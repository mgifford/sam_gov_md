- Posted: 2026-04-27 17:27:16.191-04

## Summary

Please be advised that this a justification notice, not a solicitation. The pupose of this is to acknowledge the justification for other than full and open competition for comprehensive medical services. IMS, INC is the current incumbent that will provide the medical services for the Metropolitan Detention Center, MDC Los Angeles (MDC Los Angeles). This service is deemed essential to maintain the healthcare needs of the inmate population. Monthly purchase orders will be taken place until a new CMS contract fulfilled. The period of performance will be from May 1, 2026 through June 30, 2026.
