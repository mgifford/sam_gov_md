# Autonomous Robotic Training System (ARTS)

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 10:26:54.11-04
- Solicitation Number: 2026073318

## Summary

The United States Secret Service is seeking an Autonomous Robotic Training System (ARTS) to support live fire and simulation munition training for law enforcement personnel. The requested supplies include a fully operational autonomous robotic system in accordance with the attached Performance Work Statement (PWS). The ARTS must be capable of autonomous operation, scenario customization, data collection, and performance assessment, with the contractor providing system integration, customization, maintenance, and training services for personnel.
