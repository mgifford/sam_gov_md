## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Subpart 12.6, Streamlined Procedures for Evaluation and Solicitation for Commercial Items as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement and the attached Request for Quotation FA527026Q0009 constitute the only solicitation being issued; the solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Regulation. The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code (532111) and applicable size standard for this procurement is listed in Block 10 on the face page of the attached RFQ. The United States Air Force – 18th Contracting Squadron, Kadena AB Japan (Okinawa) intends to award a Single Award, Requirements type Contract for LEASING VEHICLE SERVICES for U.S. Army, 1-1 ADA in Okinawa, Japan. This requirement will be for full and open competition. The period of performance for this contract will be 13 September 2026 – 12 September 2031. Please see the attached RFQ for additional details, and other terms and conditions.
