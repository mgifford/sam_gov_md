## Summary

This is a PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE for a commercial acquisition using FAR Parts 12 and 15. THIS PRESOLICITATION NOTICE IS NOT A REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP). VENDORS SHALL NOT SUBMIT FORMAL PROPOSALS UNTIL AN RFP IS POSTED TO THE SYSTEM FOR AWARDS MANAGEMENT (SAM) WEBSITE. This procurement will be solicited as unrestricted. If award cannot be made, the solicitation will be cancelled, and the requirement will be re-solicited. The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) intends to issue a Request for Proposals (RFP) on-or, about June 5, 2026, for the acquisition of flat bronze markers in support of VA s Memorial Product Services (MPS). The National Cemetery Administration (NCA) furnishes headstones, markers, niche covers, and medallions (monuments) for deceased veterans worldwide. Memorial monuments are also furnished to memorialize veterans whose remains are non-recoverable. The purpose of this requirement is to supply and to distribute flat bronze markers worldwide. The agency is contemplating single award of an Indefinite-Quantity Indefinite-Delivery (IDIQ) contract with Firm fixed price (FFP) task orders to the contractor whose submission represents best value to the Government. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this requirement is 331529 Other Nonferrous Metal Foundries.
