# LCS LM2500 Removal Rail Container Procurement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 15:40:58.853-04
- Solicitation Number: SIMACQ-PD--42-0160

## Summary

This procurement will provide services to design and fabricate six (6) storage/shipping containers for LCS-2 variant LM2500 removal rails. This specification also requires the updating of professional drawings for the prototype container. The removal rail container shall allow for the storage and shipment of the rails that protects them from general shipping damage and inclement weather.
