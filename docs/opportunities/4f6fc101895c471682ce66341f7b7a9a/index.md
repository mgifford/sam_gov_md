# SOURCES SOUGHT - Sewer Camera Trailer Upgrade

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-05 14:40:47.214-04
- Solicitation Number: FA461326SEWCAM

## Summary

The 90th Contracting Squadron is issuing this sources sought announcement for work at Francis E. Warren Air Force Base (F.E. Warren AFB), Wyoming. The North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 334310 - Audio and Video Equipment Manufacturing, with a small business standard of 750 employees. This sources sought is to be used for market analysis only to determine the availability of potential small business categories for potential set-asides. No award will be made from this sources sought announcement. No solicitation is available at this time. All responses will be used to determine the appropriate acquisition strategy for the future requirement. Based on the responses to this sources sought/request for information notice, the requirement may be set aside for small business or procured through full and open comptetition. All small business set-aside catergories will be considered. F.E. Warren AFB is conducting market research to identify potential sources that have the ability to meet the requirements below: In accordance with the Performance Work Statement, the contractor shall provide installation of one onboard generator, two flat screen monitors, an HD enabled remote controlled tractor with video cable, a cable winch with video camera reel, and the installation of this system on an existing trailer. THIS NOTICE IS FOR MARKET RESEARCH PURPOSES ONLY. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR QUOTATION, PROPOSAL, OR INVITATION FOR BID. All communication regarding this notice shall be in writing, reference "Sewer Camera System" and shall be sent electronically to the following email addresses: megan.evans.3@us.af.mil and liezel.mcintyre@us.af.mil. NO PHONE INQUIRES WILL BE ACCEPTED. Responses MUST be received no later than TUESDAY, 19 May 2026, by 11:00 AM MDT (Mountain Daylight Time). LIST OF ATTACHMENTS: Attachment 1 - Performance Work Statement dated 21 April 2026 Attachment 2 - Sewer Camera System (Trailer) Pictures Attachment 3 - Contractor Response Form
