## Summary

Procurement Award Announcement Boiler Safety Valve Procurement at Minneapolis VA Health Care System The Department of Veterans Affairs, through its Network Contracting Office, has awarded a procurement contract to United Commercial Supply, LLC. This contract is specifically for the provision of boiler safety valves at the Minneapolis VA Health Care System. This procurement ensures that the Minneapolis VA Health Care System will have access to reliable and high-quality boiler safety valves, supporting the facility's operational needs and safety requirements.
