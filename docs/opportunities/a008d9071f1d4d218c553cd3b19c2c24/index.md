Other information provided in this notice is subject to change prior to the release of the solicitation. This is not a pre-solicitation notice. Please address all questions via email to Contracting Officer Aaron Holmes at aaron.holmes@va.gov and confirm receipt. OVERVIEW: The Department of Veteran Affairs, NCO6 is conducting market research to identify potential sources for the construction of concrete slabs. The place of performance will be at the Hampton VA Medical System in Hampton, Virginia. SCOPE OF WORK: The Hampton VA Medical System requires construction services for the installation of concrete pads for e-vehicle and diesel Kubota parking. Work includes the demolition of existing stairs to be turned into a 12 x 24 concrete ramp tied into a dock area as well as excavation, grading, and preparation of subgrade and gravel base course where necessary. The scope of work also includes a 4 15 x50 concrete slab with a minimum 4000psi that requires the burying of 1-1/2 conduit. NAICS CODE: 238990 All Other Specialty Trade Contractors PRODUCT OR SERVICE CODE: Y1LZ Construction of Parking Facilities INSTRUCTION FOR RESPONDING: Companies that believe they are likely to submit quotes can respond, via email, to Contracting Officer Aaron Holmes at aaron.holmes@va.gov with the following information: Firm Point of Contact (POC), including: Name Title phone number email address Firm details, including: Firm Name Address SAM UEI number Socioeconomic status, if any (VSOB, SDVOSB, Women-owned, etc.). All interested parties, regardless of socio-economic status, are encouraged to respond. Information that demonstrates your company has the capacity to complete this project: Does the firm have the resources to complete this project within the next three to six months? What is the firm s available single and aggregate bonding capacity? Does the firm have any other forms of alternative payment protections for construction contracts? You may also attach a copy of your company s capability statement as additional evidence. Additional information may be requested by NCO6 at a later time. POINT OF CONTACT: Please submit all responses or questions to: Contracting Officer Aaron Holmes aaron.holmes@va.gov No phone calls please.
