# Air Curtain Ash Rake

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 15:53:28.596-04
- Solicitation Number: W911S226U3336

## Summary

2026 or 2027 new air curtain ash rake compatible for the Air Burners S327 Air Curtain. CONSTRUCTION: Material - High carbon steel rake head with reinforced welds; Handle -Heavy gauge steel tubular handle; Heat Rating - Suitable for continuous exposure to 1,600?2,000?F chamber temperatures; Finish - Industrial high temp black oxide or heat resistant coating. DIMENSIONS: Overall Length: 72?96 inches (6?8ft), Rake Head Width: 12?16 inches, Rake Head Thickness: 1/4in ? 3/8in plate steel, Handle Diameter: 1?1.25in WEIGHT: Approx. 12?18lbs, depending on length and head configuration. FUNCTIONAL USE - PRIMARY FUNCTIONS: Ash leveling during active burns, pulling unburned material toward the combustion zone, clearing burn chamber floor after shutdown, managing hot coals to maintain optimal burn efficiency, assisting with chamber clean out before next operational cycle. OPERATIONAL COMPATIBILITY -DESIGNED FOR USE WITH: Fire Box S Series (S111, S220, S327, S330, S460, etc.), Fire Box skid mounted and trailer mounted units, Air Curtain Destructor (ACD) military variants. SAFETY and COMPLIANCE - SAFETY FEATURES: Extended handle length for operator standoff distance, heat resistant construction to prevent tool failure, reinforced rake head to withstand heavy debris loads. Option - The optional ash clean-out implement with a blank 1in steel faceplate, for welding on a future connection supplied by the buyer. Final price shall include shipping to Ft Hood TX 76544.
