## Summary

This solicitation is being issued to establish an Indefinite Quantity Contract (IQC) that will provide stock Replenishment coverage in support of military depots. The solicitation will be unrestricted, issued under FAR Part 13.5 procedures. This item will be procured in accordance with Government drawings/specifications available through DIBBS solicitation link. Currently Section B will contain one (1) NSN item managed by DLA Weapons Support (Columbus). See table below: CLIN 0001 NSN: 2910-00-287-1912 Nomenclature: FILTER ELEMENT, FLUID Estimated Annual Demand Quantity: 12,845 The solicitation will be available on or about May 20, 2026, at http://dibbs.dscc.dla.mil/rfp. All responsible sources may submit an offer/quote which shall be considered. Drawings/specifications will be available through BSM DIBBS.
