# PRVC Systems Disposable Privacy Curtains (BRAND NAME ONLY)

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 11:16:10.377-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C26226Q0812

## Summary

The Greater Los Angeles VA Healthcare System currently has multiple inpatient and clinical areas that do not meet current privacy curtain system requirements identified under VHA Directive 1330.01. This requirement supports replacement and standardization of existing privacy curtain systems in order to improve patient privacy, infection control, maintenance efficiency, and operational readiness. The Contractor shall provide PRVC brand disposable privacy curtain systems and related track components as specified in the schedule and salient characteristics. This acquisition is for the supply and delivery
