## Summary

27 SOCES requires one (1) Lochinvar AWH06S0N-PM Armor water boiler. This boiler is needed to replace the current Lochinvar commercial boiler. Cannon is seeking information from interested parties that can provide the requirement. All prospective contractors must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM.Gov) database to be awarded a DOD contract. The product service code for this is 4520 - Space and Water Heating Equipment and the NAICS for this action is 333414 - Heating Equipment (except Warm Air Furnaces) Manufacturing (size standard: 500 employees). This is a one time, brand name purchase for one (1) boiler. Please see the attachments, for detailed characteristics for the boiler, the combind solicitation document, provisions and clauses, offerer information and certifications, as well as the conflict of interest statement and pricing worksheet for offerers to fill out.
