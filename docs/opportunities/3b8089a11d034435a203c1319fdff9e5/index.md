## Summary

This is a combined pre-solicitation notice and Solicitation notice that AFSC/PZIMA is anticipating a sole source award to Georgia Power Company (1KY73) for the 450 Ton Chiller Rental for B20128. This requirement is being solicited as a single source requirement as a continuation of service for the current 450 ton chiller that is currently in place. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES. The Contractor shall provide all labor, materials, and equipment transportation necessary to support the rental of the existing 450-Ton Air-Cooled Chiller, listed in paragraph 1.1, located at building 20128 for the 402d Commodities Maintenance Support Group (CMXG) at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia.
