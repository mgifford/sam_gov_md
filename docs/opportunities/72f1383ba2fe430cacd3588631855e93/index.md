## Summary

This SYNOPSIS is inclusive of the following solicitations: FA8556-25-R-0015 (Proven Vendors Bid B) FA8556-25-R-0016 (Unproven Vendors Bid A) This notice serves as a SYNOPSIS for procurement of the Wing and Fin Subassemblies which are subcomponents of the AIM-120X, and CATM-120X series of Advanced Medium Range Air to Air Missile (AMRAAM).
