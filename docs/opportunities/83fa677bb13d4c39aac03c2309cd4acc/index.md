# Media Services for Independence Event

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 09:21:23.021-04
- Solicitation Number: PR16005234

## Summary

The American Embassy Nassau, The Bahamas has a requirement for media services in support of the annual American Indepedence Day event being held on June 26, 2026 in Nassau, The Bahamas. A copy of the solicitation is attached detailing the services required to include photography, videography, live streaming, social media and live event displays. U.S. companies are encouraged to visit www.bahamas.gov to see requirements to operate in The Bahamas Bids are due no later than June 4, 2026
