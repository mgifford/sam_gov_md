# (SAF/AQ) Air Force Technical and Analytic Support in Professional Services [AFTAS PRO]

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-27 15:55:44.719-04
- Solicitation Number: FA701426AFTASPRO1

## Summary

This Request for Information post is an update to SAM.gov Notice ID FA701426AFTASPRO, posted on February 26, 2026. The Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Acquisition, Technology and Logistics (SAF/AQ) and the Air Force District of Washington, Contracting Directorate (AFDW/PK) are publishing this Request for Information post to conduct market research for a follow-on effort for Air Force Technical and Analytic Support in Professional Services (AFTAS PRO). This is an advisory and assistance professional services contract to provide for analysis, development, integration, and implementation of various acquisitions program planning and assessment, and other capabilities to advance the goals and objectives of the Air Force acquisition enterprise. The purpose of this post: 1. Share the updated draft PWS. The Government is seeking input from industry. Please see attached documents for questions to industry and response guidelines. 2. Survey industry to gather business information, size, and gauge interest for a virtual industry day(s). All interested please complete the survey linked: https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/TXHkWeRuTr As a general reminder, this SAM.gov post is not a request for quote/bid/proposal. The Government is not responsible or liable for any costs incurred as a result of any work performed in connection with this post. Additionally, the requirement may be substantially revised in any future RFI or potential solicitation.
