# Walk-in Freezer Repairs

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-27 12:08:55.465-04
- Solicitation Number: W50S7G26Q0001

## Summary

The 139th Mission Support Group contracting office intends to issue an Request for Quote to award a single firm fixed-price contract for services, non-personal, to provide all plant, labor, transportation, materials, tools, equipment, appliances, and supervision necessary for the renovation of the Walk-in Freezer Repairs at Rosecrans Memorial Airport, St. Joseph MO 64503. The contract duration will be 20 calendar days after notice to proceed to include inspection and punch list. This project is set aside 100% for small businesses. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code is 811310, with a small business size standard of $12.5 million. Please contact palmour.requena@us.af.mil to express interest in being included in the request for quote for this project no later than 6 May 2026 at 12pm Central Daylight Time (CDT).
