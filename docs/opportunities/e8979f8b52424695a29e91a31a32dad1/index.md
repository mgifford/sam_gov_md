# NOI - Smiths Detection Maintenance (Sole Source)

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-12 10:10:05.829-04
- Solicitation Number: 15F06726Q0000238

## Summary

PLEASE READ THIS NOTICE CAREFULLY AS IT CONSTITUTES THE ONLY NOTICE THAT WILL BE PUBLISHED. The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) intends to negotiate a one-time, sole source, firm fixed price contract with Smith’s Detection, Address: 32202 Lakeside BLVD, Edgewood, MD 21040-1102, United States for the purpose of procuring warranty and maintenance for existing Smith’s Detection Mailguard Screening systems and machines. Smith’s Detection is the sole provider of maintenance services and warranties on their machines. This notice will be distributed solely through the General Services Administration’s SAM.gov website (www.SAM.gov). Interested parties are responsible for monitoring the SAM.gov site to ensure they have the most up-to-date information about this acquisition. The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 561621 – Security Systems Services (except Locksmiths).
