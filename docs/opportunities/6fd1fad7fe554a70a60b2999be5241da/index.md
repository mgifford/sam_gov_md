# Inspired Flight 1200A unmanned aircraft system

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-11 11:20:48.129-04
- Solicitation Number: W912EE26S0008

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers - Vicksburg District (USACE-MVK), in accordance with Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 12 - Acquisition of Commercial Products and Services, intends to award a sole source purchase order to Seiler Instrument and Manufacturing Company, Incorporated (CAGE Code: 11934) for the purchase of an Inspired Flight 1200A unmanned aircraft system. This is not a Request for Quote (RFQ), and no solicitation is available for this procurement. Nevertheless, any firm or individual that believes it can meet the Vicksburg District’s requirements described herein may submit a capabilities statement, which, if received by 4:30 PM CST on 15 May 2026, will be considered. Responses to this posting must be submitted via e-mail, no phone calls will be accepted. A determination not to compete this purchase based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Federal Government. Any questions or comments should be directed to Haleigh Erwin at haleigh.j.erwin@usace.army.mil, and Dustin Cannada at Dustin.G.Cannada@usace.army.mil
