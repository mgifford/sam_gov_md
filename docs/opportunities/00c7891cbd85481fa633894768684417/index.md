- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF

## Summary

This solicitation is to provide the following functions rooms and services for an event for period of JUNE 2026 The provider should be at least four/five-star hotel in CILEGON, BANTEN. The bidders must be the hotel chain companies with the offices operating and base in Indonesia. The bidders must have an established business with a permanent address and telephone listing in Indonesia. For a Quotation above 30,000 USD, Quoters must have an active registration status in the System for Award Management (SAM) prior to submission.
