## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. The United States Department of Agricultural (USDA) - Agricultural Research Service (ARS), Northeast Area (NEA) intends to award on a sole source basis to Qiagen, LLC. Solicitation number 1232SA26Q0528 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Qiagin Ingenuity Pathway Analysis (IPA), Limited Concurrent License (LCL) software licenses in accordance with the Statement of Work. This acquisition is not set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 513210 – Software Publishers The small business size standard is $47M This acquisition is an unrestricted requirement. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency. **See Attachment 1232SA26Q0528 for applicable clauses, instructions, and evaluation criteria, Attachment 1 – Statement of Work, and Attachment 2 – J&A Restriction of Competition.**
