# NRCS-WV Kanawha County EWP Streambank Stabilization Sharon Hollow and Ohley Hollow Road

- Posted: 2026-05-21 10:12:03.111-04

## Summary

Please see attachments and comibined synopsis solicitaiton for further deatails. Kanawha County EWP 5091-001 Streambank Stabilization Sharon Hollow and Ohley Hollow Road for USDA NRCS-WV. The project consists of clearing designated areas by the removal and disposal of trees, logs, stumps, shrubs, brush, and rubbish and the shaping of the channel. Installing measures or performing work to control erosion and minimize the production of sediment and other pollutants to water and air from construction activities. Preparing the area for treatment; furnishing and placing seed, sprigs, mulch, fertilizer, inoculant, lime, and other soil amendments; and anchoring mulch in designated areas as specified. The work consists of the mobilization and demobilization of the contractor’s forces and equipment necessary for performing the work required under the contract. Establishing traffic control and maintaining safe, convenient use of public roads and right of way. Removal of surface water and ground water as necessary to perform the construction required by the contract in accordance with the specifications. It must include: (1) constructing, installing, building, and maintaining all necessary temporary water containment facilities, channels, and diversions; (2) furnishing, installing, and operating all necessary pumps, piping, and other facilities and equipment; and (3) removing all such temporary works and equipment after their intended function is no longer required. Excavation required by the drawings and specifications, as well as the disposal of the excavated materials. Construction of earth embankments, other earthfills, and earth backfills required by the drawings and specifications. *See Specifications document attached to posting for full details of required work.
