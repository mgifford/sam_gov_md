# Replace Fuel Tanks and Associated Lines - Rec Camp

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 16:15:54.488-04

## Summary

DESCRIPTION OF REQUIREMENTS: The Contractor shall remove existing two (2) 500-gallon above-ground steel industrial fuel tanks and all piping and parts associated with existing fuel tanks at the Goodfellow AFB Recreation Camp. Following removal, the Contractor shall install one (1) new Double-Walled 500-gallon Fuel Storage Tank (UL2085 – Fireguard – Above-Ground Fuel Storage Tank or approved equal) and all associated parts. For additional details and specifications, please refer to the attached Statement of Work (SOW) and site pictures.
