# Landscaping and Grounds Services for VA Fresno

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 17:16:37.435-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C26126Q0675

## Summary

This is a RFI for Landscaping and Grounds Services for VA Fresno Health Care System. The Government is seeking information on the availability of qualified vendors capable of providing Landscaping and Grounds Services. The purpose of this RFI is to gauge market research interest and gather input from potential sources. Please review the attached RFI 36C26126Q0675 document, as well as the Sample Performance Work Statement (PWS) and Sample PWS supporting documents. The QASP referenced in the PWS will be provided at soliciation and is not included with this RFI. To be considered a complete response, provide the information requested in Items 1-5 of the RFI. The last day for questions regarding this notice is May 11, 2026 at 10:00 am HST. Questions or clarifications will be sent to Shaneldylana.Pagaragan@va.gov. The anticipated posting date for the formal solicitation is early to mid-June. Vendors that provide a complete and timely response to this RFI will be notified.
