## Summary

RFI No: A030765 Date: 20 May 2026 REQUEST FOR INFORMATION (RFI) SERVICES/SUBJECT This RFI is for non-personal, severable, acquisition support services under NAICS Code 541715 – Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences (except Nanotechnology and Biotechnology) and PSC AN42 – R&D Services; Health Care-Other; Applied Research, in support of the Naval Health Research Center (NHRC). THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS (RFP) OR A REQUEST FOR QUOTATION (RFQ). IT IS STRICTLY A REQUEST FOR INFORMATION (RFI). NEITHER UNSOLICITED PROPOSALS NOR ANY OTHER KINDS OF OFFERS WILL BE CONSIDERED IN RESPONSE TO THIS RFI. NO CONTRACT WILL BE AWARDED PURSUANT TO THIS ANNOUCNCEMENT. A. DISCLAIMER IN ACCORDANCE WITH FAR 15.201(E), RESPONSES TO THIS NOTICE ARE NOT OFFERS AND CANNOT BE ACCEPTED BY THE GOVERNMENT TO FORM A BINDING CONTRACT. THIS RFI IS ISSUED SOLELY FOR INFORMATION AND PLANNING PURPOSES AND DOES NOT CONSTITUTE A SOLICITATION. NEITHER UNSOLICITED PROPOSALS NOR ANY OTHER KIND OF OFFERS WILL BE CONSIDERED IN RESPONSE TO THIS RFI. RESPONSES TO THIS NOTICE ARE NOT OFFERS AND WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED BY THE GOVERNMENT TO FORM A BINDING CONTRACT. ALL INFORMATION RECEIVED IN RESPONSE TO THIS RFI THAT IS MARKED PROPRIETARY WILL BE HANDLED ACCORDINGLY. RESPONSES TO THE RFI WILL NOT BE RETURNED. AT THIS TIME, QUESTIONS CONCERNING THE COMPOSITION AND REQUIREMENTS FOR FUTURE RFPs WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED. B. BACKGROUND As the Department of War premier Health Research Center, Naval Health Research Center (NHRC) optimizes military operational readiness through cutting-edge research on warfighters, veterans, and family health. NHRC develops and delivers operational biomedical research solutions that enhance the health, safety, readiness, and performance of our military forces. This procurement will provide acquisition services in support of the Science Acquisition Support (SAS) Department at NHRC, with a potential sixty (60) month period of performance - consisting of a six (6) month base period, four (4) 12-month options, and one (1) 6-month option (if all options are exercised). C. SCOPE/OBJECTIVE The Naval Health Research Center (NHRC) requires non-personal, acquisition services in support of their Science Acquisition Support Department. The Contactor shall provide acquisition and compliance services related to contract administration and contract supervision. The Contractor shall provide contract modification support, invoice review and Contracting Office’s Representative (COR) support, post award administration support and contractor supervision. The Contractor must have administrative experience supporting military or medical research and development. D. LOCATION OF PERFORMANCE Naval Health Research Center 140 Sylvester Rd.
