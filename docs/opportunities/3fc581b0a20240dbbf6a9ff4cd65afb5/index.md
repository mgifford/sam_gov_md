# 3 x New Passenger Vehicles - U.S. Consulate General Frankfurt, Germany

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 08:50:03.106-04
- Solicitation Number: PR15972009

## Summary

The U.S. Consulate General Frankfurt invites you to submit an offer for a firm fixed price for: 3 x Vans possessing the specifications detailed in the attached SOW to be delivered to Frankfurt am Main, Germany. The vehicles must meet all relevant German and EU specifications. The vehicles must be delivered to the U.S. General Consulate in Frankfurt, Germany no later than 120 days after receipt of the order. The price quoted in the bid must include delivery and VAT as follows: DDP – Delivered Duty Paid: The seller is responsible for the entire shipment, including customs clearance and fees [if shipped from outside of Germany] and delivering the goods to the buyer’s premises in Frankfurt. Offers within Germany must contain a VAT of 19%. Offers from outside of Germany but within the EU must contain the VAT at the country’s fixed percentage.
