# FCI Williamsburg - Chiller Preventative Maintenance/Service

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-22 14:31:26.548-04
- Solicitation Number: 15B31626-Chiller

## Summary

Presolicitation Notice (This is not a solicitation or request for quotations) The Federal Bureau of Prisons (FBOP), Federal Correctional Center (FCI) Williamsburg intends to award a firm-fixed price commercial services contract under FAR Part 12 (Simplified Procedures 12.201-1, Deviatiation Aug 2025) for preventative maintenance, equipment adjustment, tube cleaning, refrigerant leak testing, and oil change(s) of three (3) commercial chillers for a period of twelve (12) months. All supplies, materials, and tools required to perform the preventative maintenance, service and inspections shall be provided by the awarded contractor. A copy of documentation for all preventative maintenance and services rendered shall also be provided. The place of performance is FCI Williamsburg, 8301 Hwy 521, Salters, SC 29590. This acquisition will be conducted using FAR Part 12 [Deviation Aug 2025], Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services, in conjunction with the simplified procedures at FAR 12.201?1 [Deviation Aug 2025]. In accordance with FAR 12.201?1(c)(2) [Deviation Aug 2025], quotations will be solicited directly from at least three sources. This acquisition is a total small business set aside The NAICS code for this requirement is 238220 – Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors. Small Business Size Standard: $19 million The anticipated award date for this requirement is June 2nd, 2026. Any contractor interested in obtaining a contract award with the Federal Bureau of Prisons must be registered in sam.gov as a small business. This synopsis is intended only for those who would be entering into a prime contract with the FBOP for the entirety of this project. The FBOP does not award subcontracts. Any subcontracting is solely the responsibility of the prime contractor and the FBOP maintains no privity with subcontractors.??
