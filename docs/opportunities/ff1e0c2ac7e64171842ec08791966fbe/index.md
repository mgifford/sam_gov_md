# Dual Lane AI-Based Weapons Detection Systems for Rent

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 15:32:08.835-04
- Solicitation Number: N0018926QW164

## Summary

The Government seeks a qualified Contractor to provide eight (8) Dual Lane AI-Based Weapons Detection Systems for rental, delivery set-up, and pick-up. The Contractor shall also train NDW operators on proper usage and for walk-through concealed weapon detection systems to support the NAS Patuxent River Air Show. Please see the attached solicitation and its amendment for complete details.
