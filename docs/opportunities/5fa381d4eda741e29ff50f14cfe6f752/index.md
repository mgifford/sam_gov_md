# Private Office Space Rental/Workstations for ICE Personnel - Amendment 2

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 12:16:32.138-04

## Summary

The purpose of Amendment 2 is to change the quote due date to May 26, 2026, to provide anwers to the remaining questions, and to update the pricing spreadsheet to delineate between office space and workstations. The purpose of Amendment 1 is to change the quote due date to May 22, 2026 and to provide answers to some vendor questions. The remaining response to the last question is forthcoming in a subsequent amendment. The Department of Homeland Security (DHS), Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), Office of Asset and Facilities Management (OAFM) has a requirement for office spaces across the United States and its territories in order to provide appropriate working spaces for 300+ personnel across the country.
