# Request for Information - Biometric Matching Algorithm RFI No. 3

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-29 12:51:51.435-04

## Summary

The FBI is currently conducting market research on biometric matching algorithms. The FBI is inviting industry to submit information pertaining to possible solutions in the following areas: Company Ownership/Structure, Secure Build and Compilation Facility, Biometric Algorithm, Transition Approach, Scalability, Software Maintenance, Licensing, Response Times and Accuracy.
