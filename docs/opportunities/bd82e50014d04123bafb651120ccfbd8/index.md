## Summary

Request for Information (RFI) Notice ID VA-26-00040488 was posted on 02/02/2026 to SAM.gov as market research for an Enterprise Logging Warehouse solution. This notice is to inform vendors of the acquisition decision for the below requirement in accordance with (IAW) the Department of Veterans Affairs, Technology Acquisition Center Market Research Loopback procedures. IAW Revolutionary FAR Overhaul 19.106-2, the Contracting Officer has a reasonable expectation that offers would be received from two (2) or more Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) concerns. As a result, the subject requirement will be released as an SDVOSB set-aside under the existing National Aeronautics and Space Administration, Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurements contract vehicle, under the Value-Added Reseller North American Industry Classification System code 541519. This Amendment is for informational purposes only to inform previously interested vendors of the chosen acquisition path; do NOT submit any proposals or responses to this posting. The original RFI confirmation is attached to this notice to assist vendors in identifying the previous opportunity.
