# Marine Corps Tactical Instrumentation System Support Extension

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-05-28 15:46:38.759-04
- Solicitation Number: M6785421C8051P00030
- Awardee: SAAB, INC. Orlando FL 32826 USA

## Summary

Saab Inc., of Orlando, Florida, is awarded a $19,309,659 sole source modification to previously awarded fixed-price contract M67854-21-C-8051 for United States Marine Corps Force on Force Training Systems-Next (FoFTS-Next). The contract modification provides a six-month extension to Contractor Logistics Support (CLS), Post Fielding Support, and Post-Deployment Software Support (PDSS) at all current home stations, including the stand-up of Marine Corps Base Quantico. Work will be performed at Camp Pendleton, CA, 29-Palms, CA, Camp Lejeune, NC, Orlando, FL, MCB Hawaii, HI, and MCB Quantico, VA. The period of performance will end on December 31, 2026. Fiscal year 2026 operations and maintenance funds in the amount of $19,309,659 are obligated at time of award. Contract funds in the amount of $19,309,659 will expire at the end of the current fiscal year. This contract was awarded under the authority of 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(1) using noncompetitive procedures. The Marine Corps Systems Command, Quantico, Virginia is the contracting activity.
