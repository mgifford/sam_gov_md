# 26-330 Animated Film on AOA Identification

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 18:48:23.617-04

## Summary

The Alaska Region seeks professional services to develop an engaging animated film that explains the process of identifying Aquaculture Opportunity Areas (AOAs) in Alaska's state waters, in support of American seafood competitiveness and the economic growth of aquaculture in Alaska.
