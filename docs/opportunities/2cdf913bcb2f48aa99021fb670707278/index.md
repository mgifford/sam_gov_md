# IDIQ - Laboratory Gloves

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 14:27:57.899-04
- Solicitation Number: PR-000784

## Summary

The FBI intends to award a single award, Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract for latex and nitrile laboratory gloves, for the Laboratory Division (LD). This requirement is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. Please see the attached RFP for complete details and instructions. Questions pertaining to the solicitation are due by 12:00 PM/EST on Friday, May 15th, 2026. All questions must be submitted using the enclosed Offeror Question Submission Attachment. Proposals must be received by 5:00 PM/EST on Wednesday, May 20th, 2026. Amendment 1 - 5/5/26 - Questions and Answers Excel Sheet attached, Updated Product Pricing Matrix attached, Updated Glove Specifications attached. Amendment 2 - 5/13/2026 - Solicitation reopened and response period extended. TAA and Buy American compliance requirements have been removed. Please see updated attachments.
