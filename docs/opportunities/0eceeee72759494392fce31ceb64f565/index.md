# NAVY ADVANCED TRACEABILITY AND CONTROL (ATAC V)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 10:09:03.288-04
- Solicitation Number: HTC71126RE124

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number HTC71126RE124 is issued as a request for proposal (RFP) for the Navy Advanced Traceability and Control (ATAC V) Tailored Transportation Contract (TTC) for transportation services to move Freight All Kinds (FAK) repairable parts to all forty-eight contiguous states, Hawaii, Alaska, and Canada. The ATAC program consists of tracking, tracing, packing, reporting, and movement of high dollar Depot Level Repairables (DLR) Product Lines such as Not Ready For Issue (NRFI) Carcass Express (CE) and Routine (RT) priority items from point of failure to a repair facility, and ultimately back to the end user after repair of assets that are Ready for Issue (RFI) or on a Redistribution Order (RDO). Parts will be transported from two parent sites located on the West coast (San Diego, CA) and East coast (Norfolk, VA), smaller sites called Nodes, and between other Department of War (DoW) and electronic Retrograde Management System (eRMS) activities, including Contractor facilities. The transportation services will include the consolidated movement of NRFI material, RDO material, RFI material, and CE and RT material. Other transportation services include express service for Repair and Return (R&R) shipments. Repair parts range in a wide variety of sizes, from circuit cards, cardboard boxes, wooden crates and metal cans to items larger than a complete aircraft. Services also include process improvements which will focus on distribution-related functions and their effects on the total Supply Chain, improving overall levels of support to the warfighter while significantly increasing efficiency. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this requirement is anticipated to be 484230, Specialized Freight (except Used Goods) Trucking, Long-Distance with a size standard of $34.0M. The Government intends to award a single Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract resulting from this solicitation to the responsible Offeror whose offer, conforming to the solicitation, will be most advantageous to the Government, price and other factors considered in accordance with the Request for Proposal (RFP). The period of performance for this effort includes a five-year base period and an optional six-month extension per FAR 52.217-8, Option to Extend Services. Base Period: 28 February 2027 – 27 February 2032 52.217-8 Option to Extend Services: 28 February 2032 – 27 August 2032 No hardcopies of the solicitation will be issued.
