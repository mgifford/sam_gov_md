# Request for Information - Strategic Sourcing for Multi-Platform Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 13:26:45.843-04
- Solicitation Number: STRATEGIC_SOURCING_FOR_MULTI_PLATFORM_SUPPORT

## Summary

The Army Contracting Command - Detroit Arsenal (ACC-DTA) is seeking to gain knowledge of interest, capabilities and qualifications of various members of the community, to include both small and large businesses, for a five-year indefinite delivery indefinite quantity (IDIQ) contract to support the Hardware Requirements of the Capability Program Executive (CPE) - Ground Combat Platforms and it's enterprise partners. NOTE: All Request for Information (RFI) input is voluntary, and no compensation will be made for your firm's participation. Since this is a RFI, and not a solicitation or RFP for receipt of proposals or quotations, absolutely no telephone calls will be accepted concerning this notice.The Government is not obligated to issue a solicitation or award a contract as a result of this notice. Data submitted in response to this RFI will not be returned. Lastly, you may respond in total, or in part, to this RFI. However, the Government reserves the right to later solicit for all or parts of these requirements in the best interest of the Government. To view this full opportunity, please click the PIEE Solicitation Module link located in the Attachments/Links section below.
