# Enterprise Contracted Logistics Sustainment Support (ECLSS)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-20 14:19:13.929-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8527-26-R-ECLSS

## Summary

The Government is conducting market research to identify potential sources that possess the expertise and capabilities to meet the qualification requirements to provide the services needed to support the Enterprise Contracted Logistics Sustainment Support (ECLSS) services at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia. The preponderance of work will be perfomed at this location with occasional travel requirements to support field sites in both CONUS and OCONUS locations. The Government is establishing an enterprise approach for accomplishing effective Supply Chain Management (SCM) to provide global logistics support to the war fighter. The ECLSS performance tasks include functioning as the Service Provider (SP) at the enterprise level for Materiel Management, Warehousing, and related SCM activities as identified by the weapon systems. Additionally, technical support of sustainment activities associated with product support of weapon system inventory of the kind provided by a sustainment equipment specialist will be required. *ATTENTION* See attached Request for Information (RFI) for ECLSS requirement description and capability survey questions. If interested in this requirement, please send a request to Contracting Officer, Philip Holt (philip.holt@us.af.mil) and Contract Specialist, Jennifer Keller (jennifer.keller.3@us.af.mil) for a copy of the draft PWS.
