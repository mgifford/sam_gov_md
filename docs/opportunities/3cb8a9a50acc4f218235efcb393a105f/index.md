## Summary

Download 36C24926Q0230-LabCourierService-MtHomeVAMC.pdf [solicitation] and read it before submitting a response. The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) requires professional courier services between James H. Quillen VA Medical Center (JHQVAMC), and its outpatient clinics. Services must support continuity of patient care while maintaining compliance with federal, state, local, and accreditation requirements.The Contractor shall provide all personnel, supervision, vehicles, equipment, supplies, licenses, permits, and management necessary to perform laboratory courier services as described in the Performance Work Statement (PWS) and solicitation document.
