## Summary

The U.S. Army Contracting Command - New Jersey on behalf of the U.S. Army Combat Capabilities Development Command - Armaments Center (DEVCOM-AC) issued a Firm-Fixed Price (FFP) solicitation W15QKN-26-R-A026 to DS Government Solutions Corp. to acquire ABAQUS software licenses, maintenance, updates and training for the finite element analysis software suite. Purchase Order W15QKN-26-P-A029 was awarded on May 14, 2026 from this solicitation. This is a FFP Purchase Order with a period of performance of 12 months. The corresponding Sole Source Justification and Approval utilizing the authority FAR 13.501(a)(1)(ii), sole source (including brand name) under the authority of the simplified procedures for certain commercial items.
