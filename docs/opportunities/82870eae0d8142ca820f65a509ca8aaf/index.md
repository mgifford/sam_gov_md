# 62- Distress Marker Light

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 12:48:07.758-04
- Solicitation Number: SPE8E926Q0237

## Summary

Request for Proposal will be issued as unrestricted for a Distress Marker Light, NSN 6230-01-686-4184. This procurement will be for an indefinite quantity type contract. There will be a one-year base with four, one-year options. The length of any potential contract will not exceed 5 years. Option Pricing will be utilized for the four option years. Surge requirements do not exist for this NSN. This procurement will be evaluated utilizing Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA) Source Selection Procedures. A Solicitation will be issued on or around May 21 and may not be evaluated until the solicitation is issued. All responsible sources may submit a proposal which shall be considered by the agency. Copies of the solicitation can be found on http://dibbs.bsm.dla.mil. No hard copies of the solicitation are available.
