## Summary

Amendment 001- The purpose of this amendment is to add the questions and answers, incoprate clause FAR 52.222-90 as required by EO 14398 and AGAR Clause 452.203-72 in the attached Attachment 1 _ Solicitation Terms and Conditions. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1232SA26Q0441 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for DevExpress Licenses for the National Agricultural Statistics Service. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 513210. The small business size standard is $47.0M. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
