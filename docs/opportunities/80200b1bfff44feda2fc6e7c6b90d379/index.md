## Summary

The Contractor will provide JLTV training on Andersen AFB Guam. The contract will host and instruct a 10-day course that will certify personnel as operators on the Joint Light Tactical Vehicle, and certify them as instructors who can certify additional operators. The course must also certify maintenance personnel on core maintenance tasks needed to repair JLTVs to maintain the health of the fleet. Class must be taught at Building 23022/Security Forces Sustainment and utilize JLTVs owned by the 36 SFS. 36 SFS, as noted in the quote provided by Oshkosh, must provide a classroom with appropriate instruction tools, vehicles and service bays, and basic hand tools. Salient Characteristics for the JLTV Trainer/Operator/Maintainer Course: The course must meet the following characteristics at minimum: Course must certify personnel on operating Joint Light Tactical Vehicles (JLTVs). Course must certify instructors who can, in turn, certify operators. Course must certify maintainers on core tasks to maintain the JLTV fleet. Course must occur on-site (Andersen AFB) and use JLTVs owned by the 36 SFS. The anticipated NAICS Code is 611519, Other Technical and Trade Schools.
