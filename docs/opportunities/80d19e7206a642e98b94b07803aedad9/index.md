# OR- KLAMATH FALLS FWO-FISH NET REAR SYS

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 13:19:32.817-04
- Solicitation Number: 140F0S26Q0030

## Summary

The US Fish and Wildlife is seeking to purchase Fish Net Pens for Sucker Fish for delivery to Upper Klamath Lake, OR and Gerber Reservoir, OR. Each site (UKL and Gerber) requires a system of a 120' X 25' frame + walkway that has 2 each 25' X 59' X 15' net pens separated by a walk way. See attached requirements package Vendor will submit evidence they have provided fish net pen products for environments that have 10" of freeze during winters and associated floating ice which may be blown into the fish pen with net removed. The offered product must be compatible with the specifications of the "Fish Net Package" in the SOW Please submit your qualifications for the above items along with your price and CAGE code to Robert_Sung@ios.doi.gov and list "140F0S26Q0030 fish net pens" in the subject line
