# AFNORTH Interior & Exterior Cleaning

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 22:09:23.335-04
- Solicitation Number: FA481926Q0021

## Summary

Solictation Amendment FA481926Q00210001: The purpose of this amendment is to incorporate the following language into Section 52.212-2, Evaluation-Commercial Items: "The Government will proceed to award the contract to the contractor who provides the best value." This is a Request for Quotes (RFQ) for a one-time interior and exterior cleaning service at the CONR-AFNORTH (1AF) Complex, located at Tyndall Air Force Base, Florida. The scope of work includes, but is not limited to, the cleaning of interior and exterior windows, interior walls and light fixtures, exterior gutters and downspouts, and pressure washing of exterior walls and sidewalks for buildings 1210, 1212, and 1214. All interested parties are encouraged to review the attached solicitation and PWS and respond accordingly. All questions are due no later than 11 May 2026 at 1 PM CST. Questions received after this date may not be answered. All answers will be consolidated and posted to https://sam.gov/. A site visit is scheduled for 7 May 2026 at 10 AM CST for all interested parties to view the location. Attendance is not required at the site visit to be considered for award. Please email Ms. Candace Robinson at candace.robinson.1@us.af.mil and Ms. Shawna Morris at shawna.morris.2@us.af.mil by 6 May 2026 at 12 PM CST if you are interested in attending.
