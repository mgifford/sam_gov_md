## Summary

See attached Justification and Approval related to the following Award Notice. The Naval Information Warfare Center, Pacific (NIWC Pacific) has awarded contract N6600126C0027 to Prescient Edge Corporation (CAGE: 6ADQ1). The contract is cost plus fixed fee (CPFF) in the amount of $59,309,378 for the continued provision of highly specialized services in support of the Navy s Overmatch (OM) Robotic and Automated Systems (RAS) to operationalize unmanned surface, aerial, and underwater platforms to meet urgent and critical US Navy requirements. These efforts do not include the development of unmanned systems and collaborative autonomy algorithms. The period of performance consists of a 12-month base period and four 12-month option periods.
