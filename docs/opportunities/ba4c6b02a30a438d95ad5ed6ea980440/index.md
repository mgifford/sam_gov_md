## Summary

SOLICITATION-DESCRIPTION The VA Southern Nevada Healthcare System (VASNHS) Outpatient Surgery Department provides podiatry, orthopedic, vascular, ear, nose and throat (ENT), and other outpatient surgery services for veteran patients. These services are rapidly approaching their maximum exam space capacity for patient care and require existing administrative spaces to be converted into examination rooms to meet the growing demands. Currently, these existing administrative spaces are not equipped with the proper plumbing fixtures or exam lights needed for clinicians to provide care to veteran patients. Additionally, certain spaces are large and underutilized and would be best divided into two separate exam spaces. To facilitate proper care to the growing veteran population, the VA requires construction services to reconfigure these existing spaces into standardized general-purpose exam rooms. The work will be accomplished at the VA Southern Nevada Healthcare System campus located at 6900 North Pecos Road, North Las Vegas, NV 89086. (END OF SOLICITATION DESCRIPTION)
