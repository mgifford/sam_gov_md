# Rolling Bay Door Replacement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 09:42:34.545-04
- Solicitation Number: F2MPSU6O91A005

## Summary

The Department of the Air Force, 772d Enterprise Sourcing Squadron, is seeking willing and capable small businesses to provide removal, disposal, furnishing, and installation of two (2) vertical rolling steel doors of a Large Area Maintenance Shelter (LAMS) at Fort Bliss, Texas. DISCLAIMER This announcement serves as a Sources Sought Notice issued for the purpose of market research in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) / Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) - Part 10. The purpose of this Sources Sought Notice is solely for gathering information and planning. It does not constitute a solicitation, commitment, or obligation on the part of the Government. Any information provided by industry to the Government as a result of this notice is voluntary. Therefore, the Government will not pay for any information submitted in response to this notice. If a solicitation is issued, it will be announced at a later date, and all interested parties must respond to the solicitation announcement separately from the responses to this Sources Sought Notice. DESCRIPTION OF REQUIREMENT Interested vendors are advised to reference the attached Performance Work Statement (PWS) and accompanying photographs for additional details regarding the scope of work, existing conditions, technical requirements, and installation environment associated with this requirement. The PWS and photographs are provided to assist industry in assessing capability and formulating an appropriate response to this Sources Sought Notice. NORTH AMERICAN INDUSTRY CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM (NAICS) CODE The anticipated North American Industry Classification System Code (NAICS) for this requirement is 332321, Metal Window and Door Manufacturing with the corresponding size standard of 750 employees. SUBMISSION DETAILS/INFORMATION SOUGHT BY THE GOVERNMENT: 1. Your general company information, to include: Company name Address Point of contact (POC) Phone number E-mail address Commercial and Government Entity (CAGE) Code Unique Entity ID Business Size (i.e., Small Business, Other than Small) 2. Capability statement providing sufficient information demonstrating the company’s ability to perform the required work, to include: Relevant experience performing similar door installation or facility repair projects Experience with powered rolling steel door systems Product literature and/or technical specifications for proposed doors and components Whether the company intends to self-perform the work or utilize subcontractors ALL SUBMISSIONS MUST BE RECEIVED NO LATER THAN 2:00PM CENTRAL TIME ON FRIDAY, 5 JUNE 2026.
