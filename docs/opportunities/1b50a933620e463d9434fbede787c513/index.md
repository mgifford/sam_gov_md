# Intent to Sole Source 3 acres of land at Kennedy Space Center

- Agency: NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-08 09:41:31.094-04
- Solicitation Number: NOI_NASA_KSC_2026_0002

## Summary

Announcement of intent to sole source for the use of approximately 3 acres of under-utilized land at the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) Kennedy Space Center (KSC). NASA announces its intent to execute an Enhanced Use Lease (EUL) for the under-utilized acreage, located adjacent to the Turn Basin at the Kennedy Space Center.
