# RFI 191Z1026N0001 Studio Setup and Equipment Installation

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-28 07:22:03.408-04
- Solicitation Number: 191Z1026N0001

## Summary

The U.S. Embassy Baghdad (the Government) is seeking information from industry to assist with the development, planning, design, and implementation of a comprehensive studio facility. The purpose of this Request for Information (RFI) is to gather industry expertise and capabilities to help the Embassy develop requirements, specifications, and budget estimates for a complete studio setup. This market research is intended to identify all capable sources.
