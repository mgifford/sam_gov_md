# United States Government seeks to lease office space in Mount Vernon, Illinois

- Agency: GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 09:47:53.612-04
- Solicitation Number: 3IL0527

## Summary

Advertisement GSA Public Buildings Service U.S. GOVERNMENT General Services Administration (GSA) seeks to lease the following space: State: Illinois City: Mount Vernon Delineated Area: North – County Road 1500 N/ County Road 600 E/ Alma Hatchery Road/ Martin Road South – Yellow Banks Road/ S Victor Street/ Illinois Route 14 East – Illinois Route 37 West – U.S. Route 51 Minimum Sq. Ft. (ABOA): 1,100 ABOA SF Maximum Sq. Ft. (ABOA): 1,265 ABOA SF Space Type: Office and Flex Space Parking Spaces (Total): 7 Reserved Space Full Term: 15 Years Firm Term: 10 Years Option Term: N/A Additional Requirements: Office Area must be contiguous above-ground space on one floor. The Government requires secured parking area with the following specifications: Parking Spaces Specifications Minimum 10’ width for each vehicle parking space 28’ depth to accommodate vehicle length 7 secure parking spaces to be stored in an area 30’ x 80’ (~3,000 sq ft including 20% circulation) Controlled access is required in and out of the parking area. If parking area is outdoors the following specifications will also apply: 7’ tall or higher chain link fence with a access gate with a minimum width of 12’ and a single roller dual roller gate. Parking area must be asphalt or paved. Due to the nature of the Agency’s mission and in order to avoid a conflict, the proposed space must have reasonable separation from neighboring tenants whose industry is regulated by the Agency. This includes, but is not limited to, foods, drugs, biologics, medical devices, electronic products that give off radiation, cosmetics, veterinary products and tobacco products. Interested parties shall submit a list of neighboring tenants on the floor where space is being offered for the Agency so the Government can evaluate if a potential conflict exists prior to the market survey. Offered space must meet Government requirements for fire safety, accessibility, seismic, and sustainability standards per the terms of the Lease. A fully serviced lease is required. Offered space shall not be in the 100-year flood plain. Entities are advised to familiarize themselves with the telecommunications prohibitions outlined under Section 889 of the FY19 National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA), as implemented by the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR). For more information, visit: https://acquisition.gov/FAR-Case-2019-009/889_Part_B.
