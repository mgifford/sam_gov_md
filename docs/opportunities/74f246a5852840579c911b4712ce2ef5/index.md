# WBAMC Nucleic Acid Analyzer Maintenance

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 12:07:19.418-04
- Solicitation Number: W81K0026QA147

## Summary

This is a synopsis only and not a request for quote (RFQ); RFQ W81K0026QA147 is forthcoming. This synopsis is for commercial services prepared in accordance with (IAW) the format in FAR Subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional informaiton included in this notice. This requirement for full-service maintenance of four (4) government-owned nucleic acid analyzers for William Beaumont Army Medical Center (WBAMC), Fort Bliss, TX. The resultant contract (purchase order) will be a one-year base plus two (2) one-year options, the anticipated periods of which are: Base: 1 June 2026 through 31 May 2027 Option 1: 1 June 2027 through 31 May 2028 Option 2: 1 June 2028 through 31 May 2029
