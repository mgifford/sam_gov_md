# Starrett Optical Coordinate Measuring Machine

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 15:04:04.818-04
- Solicitation Number: SP4706-26-Q-0007

## Summary

This requirement is for one (1) Starrett Optical Measuring Machine, ARV300, for the Product Test Center (PTC) located in Columbus, Ohio. Delivery, to include delivery, installation, calibration, warranty and one (1) day of training for up to five (5) associates, is anticipated to be 8-10 weeks from the date of award. Quotes are due by 11:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) on Monday, May 25, 2026. Quotes must be sent via email with the subject line SP4706-26-Q-0007 OCMM, to the Contracting Officer (KO), Elizabeth Dickstein listed in the POC section of this announcement. When quoting, please submit your company's spec sheet and/or quote along with a signed copy of the SF-1449, solicitation number SP4706-26-Q-0007. It is the Governmetn's intention to award without discussions; however the Goverment and the KO retain the right to conduct discussions if necessary. Offerors are encouraged to present their best technical and price offer in their initial submission.
