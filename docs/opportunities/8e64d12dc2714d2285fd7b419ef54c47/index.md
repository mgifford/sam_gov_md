BACKGROUND & SCOPE The Government is conducting market research to identify interested and capable sources to support the Senegal MILES-IWS2 program. The requirement entails the procurement and delivery of the Multiple Integrated Laser Engagement System - Individual Weapon System 2 (MILES-IWS2) and associated support required to build partner capacity for the Senegalese Armed Forces. This effort is in support of expiring FY26 Building Partner Capacity (BPC) Section 333 requirements. 2. APPLICABLE CLASSIFICATION CODES NAICS Code: 333310 - Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing Product Service Code (PSC): 6920 - Armament Training Devices 3. SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS Interested parties are requested to review the attached Sources Sought Notice Questionnaire and Draft Performance Work Statement (PWS) for detailed capability requirements. Response Deadline: All responses must be submitted to sherrie.r.moore.civ@army.mil and received no later than 19 May 2026 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time (EDT).
