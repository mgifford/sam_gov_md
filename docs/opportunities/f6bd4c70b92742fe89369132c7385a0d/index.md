## Summary

The work under this effort will provide resource management and mission planning optimization algorithms and software to develop and transition software built on novel applied optimization mission planning algorithms for coordinating and tasking multiple platforms and sensors. Systems and Technology Research’s (STR) approach will integrate advanced modeling, simulation, and analysis (MS&A) with novel optimization algorithms to develop a robust mission planning framework.
