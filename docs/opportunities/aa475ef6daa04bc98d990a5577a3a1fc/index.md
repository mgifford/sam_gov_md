# Sawtooth National Forest Travel Trailer EN 3437

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 14:56:29.61-04
- Solicitation Number: 1240LR26Q0021

## Summary

The United States Department of Agriculture (USDA), Forest Service (FS), Sawtooth National Forest requires the purchase and delivery of a bumper-pull travel trailer to be used as temporary living quarters for field-going personnel at remote work sites on Sawtooth National Forest. Trailer towing will vary from highway use to rough and rocky road surfaces in all weather conditions. Maximum ground clearance required.
