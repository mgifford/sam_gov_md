# USDA Forest Service Cass Job Corps Center Fire and Safety Inspection

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 12:18:14.027-04
- Solicitation Number: 12318726Q0082

## Summary

The Contractor shall provide all supervision, labor, materials, tools, equipment, transportation, and expertise necessary to perform comprehensive annual inspection, testing, and maintenance of all Government-owned fire alarm systems and associated components located at Job Corps Cass Center. Services ensure the entire system is maintained in full operational condition and in compliance with all applicable codes and regulations.
