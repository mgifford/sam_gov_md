# Maintenance & Support Services for Bauer Aircraft Component Test Equipment & Related Systems

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-12 12:49:11.702-04
- Solicitation Number: 14703

## Summary

The 309th AMXG Squadron at Hill AFB, Utah requires comprehensive software and engineering support services for aircraft component test equipment, Test Program Sets, and associated control systems manufactured or developed by Bauer, Incorporated. Bauer, Incorporated possesses the intellectual property, proprietary data and specific technical capabilities necessary to fulfill the requirement. In accordanace with RFO 6.103-1(b) the Government intends to issue a sole-source solicitation and subsequent 12-month contract award to Bauer, Incorporated of Bristol, CT (CAGE: 70882 ). Any other responsible source may submit a capability statement which shall be considered by this agency. In a reply, respondents shall detail whether their firm is a large or small business and whether they are a US-owned or foreign-owned firm. A response must specifically address a firm’s authority and technical ability to provide maintenance, engineering, and software support services for Bauer aircraft component test equipment. (end)
