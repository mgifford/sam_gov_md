## Summary

This requirement was previously posted under Notice ID 14816/Solicitation No. FA8222-26-14816 and has been reissued under full and open competition basis. ------------------------------------------------------------------- Solicitation Number: FA822-26-14816V2 is issued as a Request for Quote (RFQ) for 14816 Polyalphaolefin (PAO) Chiller Skid System. The requirement consists of installing a new PAO chiller, pump, manifolds, distribution piping, controls, and instrumentation. The system will provide cooling for aircraft representative equipment. This acquisition is not a set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. See attached combined synopsis/solicitation document for full description of the requirment, along with SOW.
