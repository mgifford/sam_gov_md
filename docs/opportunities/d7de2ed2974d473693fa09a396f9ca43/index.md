1.0 INTRODUCTION: The Minneapolis Veteran Affairs Health Care System (MVAHCS) requires repairs to a 2016 Chevrolet Express Cutaway 3500 139 WB 2-Door Van. The frame is rusted around and needs repairing along with the windshield needing to be replaced.

2.0 SCOPE OF WORK: The Contractor shall supply all supervision, labor and materials to complete the vehicle s body window frame repair and windshield replacement.

3.0 GENERAL INFORMATION

3.1 Place of Performance Repair shop doing the work in Minneapolis, Minnesota. The vehicle will be delivered to the repair shop.

3.2 Hours of Operation The vehicle will be dropped off and picked up during the hours of 8:00 am to 4:00 pm CST, Monday through Friday.

3.3 Federal Holidays Do not schedule the vehicle to be dropped off or picked up on Federal Holidays or days observed in lieu thereof. The Federal holidays listed below are observed: New Year s Day Labor Day Martin Luther King s Birthday Columbus Day Presidents Day Veterans Day Memorial Day Thanksgiving Day Juneteenth Christmas Day Independence Day

4.0 STATEMENT OF WORK:

4.1 The contractor will supply all tools, materials, equipment and labor required to repair damaged body and replace the windshield on a 2016 Chevrolet Express Cutaway 3500 139 WB 2-Door Van. The contractor shall repair damage to the windshield frame, prime, and paint Paint and any sealant used must match existing color of the vehicle The contractor will replace windshield, associated hardware and sealants The new windshield will meet Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEM) specifications, Department of transportation (DOT) and the American National Standard Institute (ANSI) requirements. Any additional cost must be approved by the Contracting Officer (CO) prior to performing additional repairs. All repairs to the vehicle will be completed at the contractor s place of business.

Any work done by the contractor outside the SOW without direction from the Contracting Officer will be at the contractor s own risk. 5.2.
