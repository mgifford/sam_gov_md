# UAVs, Thermal Cameras & Training for Bosnia

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 04:46:25.868-04
- Solicitation Number: W564KV26QA034

## Summary

The government has a requirement for 4 UAVs with 4 extra battery, 4 UAV Mountable Radiometric Thermal Cameras, both UAV and camera with 2-year warranties, as well as training and certification for 16 personnel on both practical and theoretical UAV components from an independent and accredited organization.
