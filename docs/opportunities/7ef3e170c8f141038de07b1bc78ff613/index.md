# VISN 23 RFI: Lights, Booms and Video Integration Equipment and Support Services

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 17:08:20.001-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C26326Q0713

## Summary

The purpose of this Request for Information (RFI) is to determine the commercial market’s ability to provide surgical lights, booms, video integration software, equipment, accessories and maintenance/repair services to Veterans Integrated Service Network 23 (VISN 23) hospitals and clinics and to better define the requirement. No contract awards will be made from this announcement as it is for market research purposes only. This RFI is and shall not be construed as a commitment by the Government to pay for information requested. Review the attachments to this posting and respond according to the distructions within. Make sure to always utilize the most up to date version of this posting as modifications may be made.
