## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products and commercial services prepared in accordance with the format prescribed in Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 12.603, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. This document serves as an addendum to FAR provision 52.212-1, Instructions to Offerors?Commercial Products and Commercial Services, which applies to this acquisition.Competitive quotations are being requested under Request for Quotation (RFQ) No. N66001-26-Q-6141. The applicable North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this acquisition is 339999, All Other Miscellaneous Manufacturing, with a corresponding small business size standard of 550 employees. This acquisition is being conducted as a Total Small Business Set-Aside. Only quotations submitted by eligible small business concerns will be considered for award. Quotations submitted by entities other than small business concerns will not be considered.The Government requires the manufacture and delivery of picture frames and certificate/photo mats in support of the NIWC Pacific Awards Program. To be considered eligible for award, offerors shall submit pricing for all required items and quantities identified in this solicitation. Quotations that fail to include pricing for all required items and quantities may be determined non-responsive and may be excluded from consideration for award. Anticipated Contract Line Item Numbers (CLINs) are identified in Attachment 3, Pricing Worksheet.
