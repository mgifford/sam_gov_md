## Summary

Perform paramedic, emergency medical technician, and ambulance services as defined in this Performance Work Statement (PWS) except for those items specified as government furnished property and services. The contractor shall perform to the standards in this contract. The Contractor shall provide a New Mexico State licensed paramedic, licensed emergency medical technician (EMT), and fully supplied and equipped ambulance for emergency medical response and patient transport for all personnel on Kirtland Air Force Base (KAFB) and 377 MDG Campus 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. The Contractor shall be located on KAFB to provide emergency medical response to KAFB and the 377 MDG Campus, as well as subsequent necessary transport.
