# Automated Management Support Services (AMSS)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 10:17:51.882-04
- Solicitation Number: W9124L-26-R-AMSS

## Summary

THIS IS A NOTICE OF INTENDED SOLE SOURCE AWARD. Mission and Installation Contracting Command Fort Sill, Oklahoma, intends to award a SOLE SOURCE to Janus Research Group, LLC, 600 Ponder place Dr. Evans, GA 30809-3185. The requirement includes providing the Army with IT support services for classrooms and staff across the Fires Center of Excellence (FCoE), providing the essential tools and resources required to ensure every service member is mission-ready. Automated Management Support Services focuses on providing IT support for trainings including combat developments, training developments, and institutional training; participating in the force structure process; determining requirements and influencing systems development, acquisition, and fielding processes for the Field Artillery and Air Defense Artillery Schools. This requirement shall be awarded as a firm fixed price contract. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code is 541990, size standard $20M. This proposed contract action is for services for which the Government intends to solicit or negotiate with only one source under the authority of FAR 12.201-1. Interested persons may identify their interest and capability to respond to the requirements. This notice of intent is not a request for competitive proposals; however, all capabilities received within seven (7) calendar days after date of publication of this synopsis will be considered by the Government for future procurements. A determination not to compete this requirement based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. Information received shall be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement in the future. Any questions must be made in writing and directed to: Donovan Osborne, Donovan.l.osborne.civ@army.mil – Contract Specialist and Nicole English, nicole.english.civ@army.mil–Contracting Officer at Mission and Installation Contracting Command – Fort Sill, 1803 Macomb Rd, Fort Sill, OK 73503.
