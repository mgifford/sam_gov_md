## Summary

Department of Veterans Affairs Network Contracting Office 12, 115 S. 84th Street, Milwaukee, WI 53214 intends to solicit offers for Replace Exterior Lightbulbs and Exterior Lightbulb Replace Illuminated Building Signs service at the Jesse Brown VA Medical Center, Chicago, Illinois. NAICS Code 811210, size standard $34 million. This will be a one-year firm fixed price contract with no option years starting 15 JUN 2026 14 JUN 2027. Contractors that can provide please see the RFQ. The intent of the awarded contract is to Replace Exterior Lightbulbs and Exterior Lightbulb Replace Illuminated Building Signs at the Jesse Brown VAMC. The contractor will provide all labor, materials, equipment, transportation, and supervision necessary for two men to use a bosun chair and repel over the wall to replace burnt out LEDs in the logo, part of the "M" and the "E & R" for the South elevation sign on the newer section of the building. Use the bosun chair to repel and replace LEDs in the logo on the South elevation sign on the older section of the building. Also use the bosun chair to replace LEDs in the logo for the sign facing North. Once LEDs are replaced, energize the signs and verify the new LEDs are working.
