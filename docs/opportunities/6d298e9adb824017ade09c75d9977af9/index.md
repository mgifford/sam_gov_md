## Summary

This is a Request for Quote (RFQ). The government anticipates awarding a firm-fixed price contract resulting from this solicitation. All respondents must have an active registration in the System for Award Management (SAM) at www.sam.gov. Interested SDVOSBs must be listed and certified at SBA Veteran Small Business Certification (VetCert) https://veterans.certify.sba.gov/ to receive contract awards. The Department of Veterans Affairs, Network Contracting Office 16, is soliciting offers for Noninvasive Cardiac Output Monitoring System. The Contractor shall provide all labor, materials, equipment, transportation, supervision, and project management required to deliver, install, calibrate, validate, and train staff on one Starling CMMST5 system outlined in the Statement of Work (SOW). Items will be delivered to the Central Arkansas Veterans Hospital (CAVHS), Eugene J. Towbin VA Medical Center 2200 Fort Roots Drive, Bldg. 182, North Little Rock, AR, 72114. Offeror shall submit offers directly via email to T Miller, Contract Specialist, at t.miller@va.gov. Subject line of e-mail shall be 36C25626Q0811 Noninvasive Cardiac Output Monitoring System by Wednesday 3:00 PM CST on 05/27/2026.
