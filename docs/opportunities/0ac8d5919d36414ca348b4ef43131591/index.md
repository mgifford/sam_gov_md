Background -The United States Department of Justice (DOJ), Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), Firearms Training Unit (FTU) is seeking offerors for a service contract to provide quarterly (four times per year), detailed factory maintenance of the ManCom Systems, Action Target Systems, the legacy Blackwater and the Mancom modified Blackwater firearm live fire target systems.
