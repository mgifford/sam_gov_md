## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Part 12 under the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) and has incorporated provisions and clauses that are in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular (FAC) 2026-01 dated 03/13/2026. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotations are being requested and a written solicitation will not be issued. The solicitation number 70LCHS26QPFB00021 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) to provide Upgrade Three Firing Ranges for the Department of Homeland Security (DHS), Federal Law Enforcement Training Center (FLETC), located in Charleston, SC. The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 811210. This requirement is a Total Small Business Set-Aside and only qualified offerors may submit quotes.
