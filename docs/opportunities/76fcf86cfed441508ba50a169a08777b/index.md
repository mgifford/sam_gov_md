## Summary

This notice serves as the official Solicitation Announcement for Garden Maintenance Services for the American Embassy San Salvador, El Salvador. The complete solicitation package, including all specifications and detailed instructions for the submission of quotations, is attached for your review. A mandatory site visit is scheduled for June 11, 2026 at 9.30am (local time) at the American Embassy San Salvador Compound, located on Boulevard Santa Elena, Antiguo Cuscatlan, La Libertad, El Salvador. Prospective offerors are required to contact CotoAB@state.gov no later than June 04, 2026, to arrange access to the facility for the site visit. All inquiries arising during or after the site visit must be submitted in writing no later than June 23, 2026. The Government reserves the right to amend or cancel this solicitation at any time. Thank you for your interest.
