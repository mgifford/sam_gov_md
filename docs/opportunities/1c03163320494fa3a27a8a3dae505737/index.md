# Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) Multiple Award Contract (MAC) Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-14 09:32:55.881-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8652-26-R-0006

## Summary

This DRAFT Solicitation is issued for market research and planning purposes in accordance with the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 15.101. The Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) Procurement Headquarters (PK), located at 1864 4th St, Building 15, Wright-Patterson AFB, OH 45433, is announcing a DRAFT solicitation for the AFRL Multiple Award Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (AMAC) contract vehicle. This notice IS NOT a formal solicitation, Request for Proposal (RFP), or Request for Quotation (RFQ), and it DOES NOT obligate the Government to award a contract. This notice does not require the submission of formal proposals. AFRL is actively seeking industry input, opinions and responses to the provided draft documentation. Constructive feedback from industry partners is highly encouraged to help refine and finalize the solicitation. All responses, feedback, and questions regarding this draft solicitation must be submitted in writing to the AMAC organizational mailbox at: afrl.amac.mailbox@us.af.mil.
