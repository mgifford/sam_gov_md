Location: Warm Springs Health & Wellness Center, 1270 Kot-Num Road, Warm Springs, OR 977761 Scope of Work: Procure, deliver, remove existing and disposal, install, and verify operation of a Maestro2 Optical Coherence Tomography with Angiography (OCTA) system with table at Warm Springs Health & Wellness Center. Performance Period: Date of award through one year of contract. The performance period includes delivery, removal of existing equipment, installation, testing, and final acceptance.
