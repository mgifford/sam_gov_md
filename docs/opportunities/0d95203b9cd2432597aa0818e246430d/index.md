# Request for Information - Maneuver Readiness Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 13:11:24.152-04
- Solicitation Number: MANEUVER-READINESS-SUPPORT

## Summary

The U.S. Army Contracting Command - Detroit Arsenal (ACC-DTA) is issuing this Request for Information (RFI) on behalf of the Capability Program Executive (CPE) - Ground Combat Platforms and its enterprise partners. The purpose is to conduct a market survey to inform an action to acquire non-personal maneuver readiness support for ground combat vehicle program offices. This RFI will help the Government understand market capabilities and identify potential sources with the required technical expertise, resources, and experience. The Government is not obligated to issue a solicitation or award a contract as a result of this notice. Data submitted in response to this RFI will not be returned. Lastly, you may respond in total, or in part, to this RFI. However, the Government reserves the right to later solicit for all or parts of these requirements in the best interest of the Government. The Government will not pay for any information or administrative costs incurred in responding to this RFI; all costs associated with your response are the responsibility of the interested party.
