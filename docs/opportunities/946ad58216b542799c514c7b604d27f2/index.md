# Sources Sought Notice - O&M with Incidental Repair and Minor Construction, IDIQ MATOC in Support of DHA, East of the Mississippi Region

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-12 12:22:09.353-04
- Solicitation Number: W9127825RA077

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Mobile District (SAM) intends to place a brand name specification in new Operation & Maintenance with incidental Repair and Minor Construction East of the Mississippi projects at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center (WRNMMC), Bethesda, Maryland in accordance with RFO 15. Interested persons may identify their capability to respond to the requirement. This notice is not a request for competitive proposals; however, all information received within 15 days after publication of this synopsis will be considered by the Government. A determination by the Government not to include this proposed brand name specification based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. See attached NOIs for the proposed Brand Name Specifications.
