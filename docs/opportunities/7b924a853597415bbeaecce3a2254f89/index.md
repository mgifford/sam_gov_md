# Industry Day Announcement for the Protected Tactical Waveform (PTW) Joint Hub Variant

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-27 12:14:16.121-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8807-PTWJHV-IND-001

## Summary

Industry Day Announcement Protected Tactical Waveform (PTW) Joint Hub Variant (JHV) Purpose Space Systems Command (SSC), on behalf of the Satellite Communications and Positioning, Navigation, and Timing (SCPNT) Portfolio Acquisition Executive at Lost Angeles Air Force Base, CA, is conducting market research to assess industry capability to develop, produce, and field an Enterprise Protected Tactical Waveform (PTW) Joint Hub Variant (JHV) ground hub in support of worldwide military operations. This Industry Day serves as a follow?on engagement to the Protected Tactical Waveform (PTW) Joint Hub Variant Request for Information (RFI) posted on 23 March 2026 and will inform the Government’s acquisition strategy and technical requirements. Initial fielding is anticipated to begin in the 2029 timeframe. Disclaimer This announcement is issued for information and planning purposes only. It does not constitute a solicitation, request for proposals, or commitment by the Government to award a contract. The Government will not reimburse any costs incurred by respondents. The Government is not accepting unsolicited proposals at this time, and this notice does not represent a promise of a future solicitation. Industry Day Objectives The Government intends to: Provide an overview of the PTW JHV acquisition approach Share an initial set of technical requirements via a Bidders Library Obtain industry feedback on requirements and acquisition strategy Event Information Location: KBR MSEIT Facility 300 N. PCH, Suite 2000 El Segundo, CA 90245 Schedule: Tuesday, 02 June 2026 0830–1200 PT: Government Presentation 1300–1700 PT: Industry One?on?One Sessions Wednesday, 03 June 2026 0830–1700 PT: Industry One?on?One Sessions Classification: Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) Dress Code: Business Casual Attendance Eligibility Attendance is limited to U.S. Citizens only due to the presentation of sensitive information. Foreign Nationals are not authorized to attend. The Government invites participation from: Industry partners capable of producing a PTW JHV Companies able to execute a prime contract supporting USSF PTW JHV requirements Each company is limited to three (3) attendees. This limitation applies to all sessions. All attendees must present proof of U.S. citizenship (certified birth certificate, valid U.S. passport, or naturalization certificate). Failure to provide proof will result in denial of entry.
