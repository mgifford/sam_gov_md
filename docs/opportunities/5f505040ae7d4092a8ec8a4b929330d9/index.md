## Summary

Synopsis: NASA/NSSC has a requirement for Critical Flow Venturi/SNP02-SGR14-100. NASA/NSSC intends to issue a sole source contract to STUART A COLE DBA Flowmaxx Engineering under the authority of FAR 12.102(a). It has been determined that STUART A COLE DBA Flowmaxx Engineering is the sole provider of Critical Flow Venturi/SNP02-SGR14-100. NASA/NSSC will be the procuring center for this effort. Performance will be located at NASA/ Stennis Space Center (SSC). The Government intends to acquire Commercial items or services using the provisions, clauses and procedures prescribed in FAR Part 12 for Commercial The NAICS Code for this procurement is 334513. Interested organizations may submit their capabilities and qualifications to perform the effort in writing to the identified point of contact not later than 12:00 p.m. Central Standard Time on 5/11/2026.
