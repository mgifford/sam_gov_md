The VA Loma Linda Healthcare System (VALLHCS), located at 11201 Benton Street, Loma Linda, CA 92357-1000 is seeking Landscape Services including installation and removal of decomposed granite, installation of river rock, planting new shrubs, irrigation system modifications, removing and installing new Elite Plus Sodded Turf, and installation of tree staking for 20 trees. Additionally, contractor will need to remove, adjust or install new irrigation components to support the updated layout. All interested companies shall provide quotations for the following: Supplies/Services Refer to Price/Cost Schedule (Attached) STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) TITLE: Landscape and Decomposed Granite Project (VALLHCS) BACKGROUND INFORMATION: The Facility Maintenance Services at VA Loma Linda Healthcare System (VALLHS) is dedicated to maintaining safe and fully functional buildings to support the delivery of high-quality care to veterans. Current upgrades include improving decomposed granite areas, planting new shrubs, and installing an advanced drip irrigation system. Additionally, a new sprinkler system with saturation graphs will be implemented to optimize water coverage accuracy and efficiency.
