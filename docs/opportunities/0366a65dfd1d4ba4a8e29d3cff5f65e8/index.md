Title of Project: Maintenance of In-House Paging (Beeper) Systems 2. Scope of Work: TTI shall provide all resources necessary to accomplish the deliverables described in this statement of work (SOW), except as may otherwise be specified. TTI will provide maintenance, follow-on support and warranty services to the in-house paging (beeper) systems located at all facilities within VA Beckley, WV. This work is to ensure that all systems have a 100% uptime associated with them to ensure patient safety. The system will also provide full site coverage so that employees receive pages within a timely fashion 100% of the time 3. Background: The Federal Government has mandated the use of narrow-brand technology for any site that owns and operates an in-house paging (beeper) system. The VA Beckley, WV sites currently have narrow band systems installed for the use of local and code team paging. The system must have a 100% uptime associated with it, since it is imperative that patient care is not affected. To that extent, all sites have primary and backup transmitters installed along with both alpha numeric and voice pagers. 4. Performance Period: [Firm-Fixed-Price] TTI shall complete the work required under this SOW in 30 calendar days or less from date of award, unless otherwise directed by the Contracting Officer. Task Three: A detailed outline of how long services will take to restore the system to operational status TTI Response: Support Levels Level Status Initial Response Resolution Response 1 Emergent 4 Hours 48 Hours 2 High 4 Hours 96 Hours 3 Normal 4 Hours 7 Days 4 Low/Informational 48 Hours TTI Discretion Definition: Emergent: Real or perceived data loss or corruption or an essential part of the system is unusable. High: Customers effectiveness is severely compromised although all essential parts of the system can be used. Normal: The customer s effectiveness is compromised but not severely and all of the essential parts of the system can be used. Low /Informational: The customer can circumvent the problem and continue to use the system with slight inconvenience. Customer has suggestion for next generation products and/or services. Initial Response: Time for a return call from vendor to VA to acknowledge problem. Initial response is needed quickly in order to properly identify the level of urgency or impact to operations and to identify possible work around procedures or temporary measures. Example: Contract Vendor will respond to assess the problem within not more than 4 hours of notification. Resolution Response: Time until the documented fix restores functionality. Task Four: An outline of how the components will be tested to ensure operational continuity TTI Response: The paging system infrastructure, transmitter and antenna system are tested and optimized in the following order: A Turn-Key Technologies System Engineer will commence Preventive Maintenance visit by measuring the output power of the paging transmitter and reflective level of the antenna system. All wiring and connections will be checked. The transmitter interface voltage levels will be checked as well as the wiring. The Paging System Encoder line output is measured and punch down connection checked. The computer interface and telephone interface cards are examined for proper functionality. Voice quality and system coverage throughout the facility is checked. Management software is also checked for consistency. Any questions in regards to the paging system may be addressed at the conclusion of the visit. Task Five: A calendar listing of the year s annual maintenance service call for VA Beckley. TTI Response: TTI will provide a Preventive Maintenance Visit to the facility. Dates will be arranged. TTI and VA representatives will arrange precise date of the preventive maintenance visit.
