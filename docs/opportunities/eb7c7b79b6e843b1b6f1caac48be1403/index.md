# H-1 Program Supplemental Time Microwave Tool

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 16:58:25.974-04
- Solicitation Number: N68335-26-Q-1103

## Summary

5/28/2026 Update: REQUEST FOR QUOTES EXTENDED THROUGH 6/12/2026-4PM EST. Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWC-AD) Solicitation Number: N68335-26-Q-1103 Solicitation Title: H-1 Program Supplemental Time Microwave Tool The Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWC-AD) is officially releasing a Request for Proposal (RFP) for a Firm Fixed Price (FFP) contract on a competitive basis for the procurement of Peculiar Support Equipment (PSE) as outlined in the attached Solicitation N68335-26-Q-1103 package: Eval Plan, Buy Terms and CLIN Structure. The PSE items are required in support of the H-1 Program for the United States Marine Corps. This solicitation is being issued as a 100% Small Business Set-Aside in accordance with FAR Part 19. In order for the offeror to be found technically acceptable, the offeror must be considered a women owned small business under the NAICS code of the solicitation, NAICS 334419. Technical Data Package and Export Control Requirements This solicitation includes DFARS 252.204-7025, Notice of Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Requirements. The technical data package contains Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). Offerors must obtain access to the CUI drawing package to prepare a proposal. Proof of CMMC Level 2 (self-assessment) certification is required to request access to the technical data package. The Government owns the Drawing Package for Supplemental Time Microwave Tool Set, which is considered Export Controlled. All Offerors must possess a current and certified DD Form 2345 (Militarily Critical Technical Data Agreement) to request access to the drawings. Drawings will be provided to the designated data custodian listed on the certified DD Form 2345 via the DoD SAFE website. Requesting Access to Solicitation Documents To request access to the Government-provided documents associated with this solicitation, Offerors must submit the following via email: Proof of current and valid CMMC Level 2 (self-assessment) certification. A certified DD Form 2345. Requests should be sent to the Point of Contact (POC) listed below. Solicitation Number: N68335-26-Q-1103 Point of Contact and Questions The Point of Contact (POC) for this solicitation is: Sara Baye, Sara.c.baye.civ@us.navy.mil. All questions and inquiries regarding this solicitation must be submitted via email and reference Solicitation Number: N68335-26-Q-1103, Solicitation Title: H-1 Program Supplemental Time Microwave Tool. Questions must be received no later than 16:00 EST, 05/27/2026. Offerors are encouraged to review the Solicitation N68335-26-Q-1103 package thoroughly before submitting questions or requests for access. Late submissions of questions or requests for access may not be considered. This RFP shall not be construed as obligating the Government to award a contract or authorizing work to commence and shall not serve as a basis for any future claims against the Government.
