# Manx BFR Precommercial Thinning and Buck & Pile - LMIR BPA Holders Only

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 16:36:43.166-04
- Solicitation Number: 1240BG26Q0047

## Summary

This is a Special Notice/Notice of Intent to issue a Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) call against an existing BPA for the acquisition of “Pre-Commercial Thinning, Buck & Pile at Bend/Fort Rock Ranger District”. The USDA Forest Service (USFS) is requesting quotes under the Land Management Integrated Resources (LMIR) Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) 12363N23Q4023. Location: Bend/Fort Rock Ranger District of the Deschutes National Forest. This work includes pre-commercial thinning, buck & pile. The Land Management Integrated Resources BPA (LMIR), 12363N23Q4023, is a National BPA that consists of a large variety of work including Professional Services, Natural Resources Restoration, Engineering, Project Management, NEPA, Communications, and more. The BPA can be utilized on all Forests and Grasslands and is available for all federal agencies to utilize. The LMIR BPA was awarded to multiple small businesses under a competitive process and only current Land Management Integrated Resources (LMIR) BPA holders will be considered for award.
