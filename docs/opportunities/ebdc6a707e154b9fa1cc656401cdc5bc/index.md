# Mobile Laundromat, Laundry Services- Tinian

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-01 18:41:27.324-04
- Solicitation Number: FA5025-26-Q-A005

## Summary

The US Air Force is looking for vendors capable of providing mobile laundry services for members supporting recovery on Tinian. The attached Statement of Objectives (SOO) provides details regarding what is desired. If your company is unable to meet the performance objectives, but you think you could provide an alternative solution, please submit your solution. Our basic requirement is at least 5 ?60lb/load washers and 5 similar capacity dryers. We understand that this option is a much lower capacity than what the SOO requires, and therefore this option would likely be a last resort if nothing else is available. This is not a request for quotes/proposals, but estimated pricing will be accepted and used to help formulate our decision when and if we decide to formally solicit. If you have questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
