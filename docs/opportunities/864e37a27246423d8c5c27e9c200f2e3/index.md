# Unarmed Uniformed Security Force (Protective Force Services)

- Agency: ENERGY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 15:42:02.615-04
- Solicitation Number: 6-P106-P-00104-00

## Summary

Solicitation for unarmed, uniformed security guard services (Protective Force services) for the Argonne National Laboratory site in Lemont, IL 60439 and two off-site locations within 1.5 miles from the Laboratory. All services and activities performed under the terms of this contract must meet or exceed, but not limited to, State of Illinois, United States Department of Energy (DOE), and Laboratory requirements, including, but not limited to, 225ILCS447 Private Detective, Private Alarm, Private Security, Fingerprint Vendor, and Locksmith 2004, State of Illinois Security Contractor and guard licensing requirements, and all applicable DOE Directives to include DOE O 473.3A Protection Program Operations. ***Please review the RFP Letter and Instructions first, plus see the attached Zip File containing the Draft Contract and general instructions for submitting a proposal in the required 3-part format.
