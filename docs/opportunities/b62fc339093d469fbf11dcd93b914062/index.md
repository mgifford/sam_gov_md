# Cemetery Grounds Maintenance Support Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 09:56:31.914-04
- Solicitation Number: PANAPG26P0000042954

## Summary

Services include full maintenance of the cemetery and surrounding grounds, such as landscaping, tree trimming and removal, weed control, fertilization, and cleaning of headstones. Additional duties include maintaining pathways, parking areas, fencing and site features; trash removal; excavation for interments; mowing, aeration, overseeding; headstone alignment and installation; and overall grounds beautification. This requirement includes the cemetery at Aberdeen Proving Ground and the Edgewood location of Aberdeen Proving Ground. (See attached Performance Work Statement)
