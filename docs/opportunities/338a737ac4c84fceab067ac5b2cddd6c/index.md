# EOD Response Truck Outfit

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 14:54:47.756-04
- Solicitation Number: W9124B26QA006

## Summary

This is a non-personal service(s) contract to provide a service to procure and install the aftermarket automotive accessories specified in Attachment 1 of this PWS on one (1) Government provided non-tactical vehicles(NTV). All items must be installed in a manner that does not interfere with the vehicle?s operation and maneuverability. Additionally, all electrical connections must comply with established electrical standards to ensure safety and functionality. Due to external circumstances, the vehicle will not be driven more than 200 miles from Fort Irwin, California to Contractor?s installation location. Contractor?s facility should be within a 200-mile radius from Fort Irwin, California.
