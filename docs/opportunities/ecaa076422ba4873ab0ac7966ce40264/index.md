# Z2DA--Upgrade Video Assessment Surveillance System

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 09:55:59.039-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C24826R0064

## Summary

This Amendment 0003 to solicitation 36C24826R0064 for the Upgrade Video Assessment Surveillance System project, is issued to extend the proposal due date to allow interested vendors additional time to obtain supplier pricing information and complete their proposals. Additionally, to clarify that the offerors must use the camera count as per the drawings. The revised Proposal Due Date is: May 15, 2026, at 3:00 PM (AST). The period for submission of questions related to this solicitation is closed. All offerors are required to acknowledge receipt of Amendment 0003 in accordance with the instructions in the solicitation and SF-30. Except as revised by this amendment, all other terms and conditions remain unchanged and in full force and effect.
