# Western Shelter Systems LLC, Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), Gatekeeper SW-20 Shelter Kit Items

- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 14:52:40.971-04

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1202SC26Q2702 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for FY26 acquisition of Western Shelter Systems LLC. Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) "Gatekeeper" series shelter kit components and replacement parts, to include FOB (destination) shipping to the NORTHERN ROCKIES AREA INCIDENT SUPPORT CACHE (NRK) located in Missoula, MT. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 339999 The small business size standard is 550 employees. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency. ***SEE FOUR (4) ATTACHMENTS***
