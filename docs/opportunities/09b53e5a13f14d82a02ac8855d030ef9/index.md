# Parking Garages _ Long Beach CA area

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 11:36:05.857-04
- Solicitation Number: 15BNAS26Q00000064

## Summary

The Federal Bureau of Prisons National Acquistions Section, Washington, DC, intends to make an award to a single responsible entity for the provision of Parking Garage/Parking Lot Services. The purpose of the contract is to obtain 24 unreserved parking spaces for the use of the U.S. Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Prisons, Long Beach Residential Reentry Management Office (RRM), located at The Glen M. Anderson Federal Building (501 W. Ocean Blvd.) in Long Beach, CA.
