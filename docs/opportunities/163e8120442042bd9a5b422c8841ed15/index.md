# Squirro Search Engine Subscription

- Agency: LIBRARY OF CONGRESS
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-13 15:03:35.471-04
- Solicitation Number: 2026-CRS-0021

## Summary

In accordance with Library of Congress Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (LCFARS) LC06.305(a), Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) FAR 5.203(a), and FAR 12.204(b), a copy of this Justification and Approval (J&A) is being posted on SAM.gov for a reasonable time for commercial items, before entering into negotiations. In summary, it is the Library of Congress' (Library's) intent to award a contract directly to Squirro Americas Inc | UEI: TE16TCC46SX7. The Contractor shall deliver the following products and services: Squirro Cognitive Search including the Insight Engine and AI Studio (or latest compatible upgrade thereof) supporting a maximum of 10,000 possible users or not more than 50,000 searches per month; Amazon Web Service (AWS) Hosting (continuation of existing services), and Operational Support. See the attached "JA - 2026-CRS-0021 - Search Engine Subscription_Redacted" for additional information.
