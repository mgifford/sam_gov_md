# Ballistic Armored Response Vehicles - Request for Quote

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 13:34:07.582-04
- Solicitation Number: 70B01C26Q00000136

## Summary

It is the intent of CBP/SOD to procure generic Ballistic Armored Response Vehicles (BARVs) units with off- road capabilities for use by SRT Operators in High Risk/High Threat operations with a ballistic protection of B7/NIJ Level IV capable of stopping .50 Ball Ammunition. In addition, this scope includes full replacement and/or repair warranty where applicable and the customer support necessary.
