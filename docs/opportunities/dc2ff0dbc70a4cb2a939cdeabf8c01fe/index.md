## Summary

US Army Contracting Command-Orlando will host a Hybrid PALT Industry Update on Tuesday, 12 May 2026 from 1100-1200 ET at the Central Florida Tech Grove, located on the ground floor of the Partnership IV building, 12809 Science Drive, Orlando, FL. The session will be hosted by ACC-Orlando, with contributors from US Army CPE ST3 and USMC PM TRASYS representatives. This hybrid session can accommodate a maximum of 160 industry partners for in-person attendance, and up to 300 virtually via MS Teams. To attend this Hybrid PALT Industry Update Session, individuals must register/RSVP for the in-person or virtual session. The deadline to register for either in-person or virtual attendees is Monday, 11 May 2026; 11:59 PM ET. The deadline to submit questions is Wednesday, 06 May 2026; 11:59 PM ET; Questions submitted after 06 May 2026 will not be answered during the PALT Industry update. Links to register/RSVP: https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/jAGL8KpkPE Note: Instructions for joining the session will be provided via email a few days prior to the actual session date. Note: In-person registration will close once we reach the 160 person limit. Once the limit is reached, attendees must RSVP using the link above for Virtual session to attend via MS TEAMS. IMPORTANT NOTES REGARDING IN-PERSON ATTENDANCE: Individuals who register for in-person attendance must present a photo ID to gain entrance to the Tech Grove facility.
