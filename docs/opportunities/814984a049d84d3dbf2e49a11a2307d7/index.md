# BATTLE Lab Varjo Headsets

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 11:53:04.889-04
- Solicitation Number: N6134026Q1013

## Summary

The Naval Air Warfare Center Training Systems Division (NAWCTSD) Orlando, in support of the NAWCTSD BATTLE Lab, intends to award, on a sole source basis, a purchase order to RAVE Computer Association, Inc. to procure supplies to be used in ongoing and future research and development efforts. This effort includes, but is not limited to, the procurement and custom modification of brand-name headsets, computers, and peripherals. These components are specifically designed for rapid deployment in secure or restricted environments. RAVE Computer supplies equipment that requires a unique combination of security and technical specifications that cannot be met by another vendor.
