## Summary

Industry Day: Host Nation Payroll & Timekeeping Software Services NAVSUP Fleet Logistics Center Sigonella, Naples Detachment (FLCSI-N) invites you to attend a Virtual Industry Day to discuss an upcoming requirement for Host Nation Payroll & Timekeeping Software Services & Support. This virtual event is an opportunity for the Navy to present technical requirements, gather industry feedback, and foster open communication. A DRAFT Performance Work Statement is attached for your review. Note: This virtual event was previously announced under Notice ID N68171-26-Payroll-ID. All questions received under that announcement will be shared prior to or at the time of the event. No further questions are being accepted at this time. Key Details: Event Date & Time: 21 May 2026 | 1500 – 1600 CET (Naples, Italy) Location: Virtual via MS Teams (link to be provided two days prior to meeting) How to Register: To register for the event, please send an e-mail by 18 May 2026 @ 12:00 UTC+2:00 Rome, Italy, as follows: To: patricia.r.folts.civ@us.navy.mil Subject Line: HN Payroll Industry Day Registration – [Insert Company Name] Body of Email to include the following: Company Name Cage Code Attendee Name and Title Attendee E-Mail Address All interested federal contractors, both large and small, are encouraged to attend. There is no cost for the event. This event does not constitute a specific offer or commitment by the Navy to fund, in whole or in part, any opportunities referenced herein. Neither the planning nor the establishment of an Industry Day constitutes a Request for Proposal (RFP) or Request for Quote (RFQ).
