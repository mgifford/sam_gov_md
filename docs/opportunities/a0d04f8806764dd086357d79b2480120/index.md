# Port of Jordan S&RTS

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 11:47:43.473-04
- Solicitation Number: HTC71126RE136

## Summary

27 May 2026-Post solicitation and attachments. Provide Stevedoring and Related Terminal Services (S&RTS) handling the import and export of designated USG cargo through the Aqaba Main Port, Aqaba Container Terminal, Royal Jordanian Naval Base and under certain circumstances the airports of Muwaffaq Salti Air Base (MSAB) and Marka Air Base. The contractor shall complete all tasks as outlined in the PWS. The contractor is required to fulfil all requirements for this contract for which the USG will compensate the contractor at the applicable commodity rate, per the designated Schedule of Rates (SOR).
