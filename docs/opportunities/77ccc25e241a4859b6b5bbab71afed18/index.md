# Packing and Crating Service FY26

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 09:27:59.873-04
- Solicitation Number: W519TC-26-R-A034

## Summary

The Army Contracting Command - Rock Island is issuing this pre-solicitation synopsis for W519TC-26-R-A034. This is a notice that a solicitation is anticipated to be forthcoming and will have all the necessary documents, including a scope of work. This requirement will be a 100% total small business set-aside. The Government has a requirement for Moving Services for the Personal Property Processing Office (PPPO) at the Rock Island Arsenal. Objective is for the Contractor to provide Moving Services for all Packing, Containerization, and local Drayage of Direct Procurement Method (DPM) Personal Property Shipments. The performance of the services is to provide Moving Services include Packing and Crating Services for all personnel, equipment, facilities, supplies, services, and materials for the preparation of the personal property (including servicing of appliances) of Department of Defense (DoD) personnel for shipment, drayage and/or storage and related services for the Personal Property Processing Office (PPPO) at the Rock Island Arsenal.
