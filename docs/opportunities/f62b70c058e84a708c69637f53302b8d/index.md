# INANLYSER DEXA SCANNER COMPREHENSIVE EXTENDED WARRANTY PLAN

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 10:22:50.302-04
- Solicitation Number: 75N98026Q00363

## Summary

THE PURPOSE OF THIS ACQUISITION IS TO PROVIDE THE NIH MOUSE IMAGING FACILITY(MIF) WITH HARDWARE AND SOFTWARE EXTENDED WARRANTY MAINTENANCE SUPPORT ITS INALYZER DEXA SCANNER. THIS EXUIPMENT USES DUAL ENERGY X-RAY ABSORPTIOMETRY (DEXA ORT DXA) TO MEASURE BONE MINERAL DENSITY(BMD) AND BODY COMPOSITION (BMC, FAT, LEAN) OF SUBJECTS FOR ONGOINGING BIOMEDICAL RESEARCH STUDIES OF HUMAN CONDITION.
