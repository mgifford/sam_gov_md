# Travis AFB CA - Faraday Tents

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-04 18:08:07.39-04
- Solicitation Number: FA442726QZ042

## Summary

The 60 OSS at Travis AFB, CA has a requirement for two (2) Portable RF/EMI Shielded Faraday Tents. The tents must be 7’x6’x6’ and provide >85dB attenuation from 400MHz-11GHz. They must be constructed with Berry Compliant Fabric and an outer layer of black sports nylon. The requirement also includes a Cyber 600 Series Metal Frame (8’x7’x6.5’), heavy-duty transport cases, specific ventilation panels, high-performance IO plates, and replacement parts. See the attached Statement of Work (SOW) for further requirements and full specifications.
