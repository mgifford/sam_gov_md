# Repair Launch Facility Concrete

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 12:20:36.914-04
- Solicitation Number: FA462626R0013

## Summary

This project is for a Launch Facility (LF) Concrete Repair Requirements Contract at Malmstrom AFB, MT. Work under this contract will consist of concrete joint, crack, and de-laminated/damaged repair. The contractor shall provide all plant, labor, equipment, supervision, services, and materials to execute and satisfy the requirements of this contract.
