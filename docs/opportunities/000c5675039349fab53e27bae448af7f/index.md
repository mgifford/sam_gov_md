# SFS Speed Trailer

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-19 16:08:16.132-04
- Solicitation Number: FA468626Q0005

## Summary

THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT ANNOUNCEMENT ONLY. This notice does not constitute a commitment by the Government. It is issued for market research purposes to identify interested and capable sources for a future requirement. Information submitted is voluntary, and the Government will not pay for any costs incurred in responding. The 9th Contracting Squadron at Beale AFB, CA is conducting market research to identify interested and capable sources for the following requirement: Requirement Overview The requirement includes providing all supervision, labor, tools, materials, parts, and equipment to fulfil the following requirement: - Purchase, delivery, and one time instruction of Speed Trailer with license plate recognition capabilities. See Attached Statement of Need for details. Response Information Requested Interested vendors should email the following information: 1. Company Information: Company Name, Address, CAGE Code, and UEI Number. Point of Contact (Name, Title, Phone, and Email). Business size and socio-economic status (e.g., Small Business, HUBZone, 8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB) 2. Capabilities Statement: Confirm the ability to complete the requirement to include delivery and one time training of operator and basic maintenance of Speed Trailer. Feedback: Provide any suggestions, questions, or concerns regarding the Statement of Need. Submission Instructions: Please submit your response via email to Wyatt Lundgreen at wyatt.lundgreen@us.af.mil and Estefania Restrepo at estefania.restrepo@us.af.mil
