# Portable Toilet Services, Birch Hill Dam and Tully Lake, Royalston, MA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 15:01:34.235-04
- Solicitation Number: W912WJ26QA098

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers – New England District is issuing a Pre-Solicitation Notice for Portable Toilet Services, Birch Hill Dam and Tully Lake, Royalston, MA. QUESTIONS REGARDING THE PROCUREMENT WILL NOT BE ANSWERED DURING THE PRESOLICITATION PHASE. SUBMIT QUESTIONS TO THE CONTRACT SPECIALIST AFTER THE SOLICITATION AND ASSOCIATED DOCUMENTS HAVE BEEN POSTED. THE WORK CONSISTS OF: Provide six (6) portable toilets and all the necessary labor, equipment, and materials to clean and maintain the toilets for the duration of the contract. Four (4) portable toilets will be provided to Tully Lake Dam, Royalston, MA and two (2) portable toilets will be provided to Birch Hill Dam, Royalston, MA. This is not a request for quotes at this time. The solicitation requirements and contract documents, to include instructions for submission, and the response date will be available on or about May 13, 2026. Neither telephonic, mailed nor faxed requests for hard copies of this solicitation will be accepted. The solicitation will only be available online; in order to download the solicitation offerors must access the Contract Opportunities website (www.SAM.gov). This procurement is set-aside for small business vendors. The applicable NAICS code is 562991 with a Small Business Size Standard of $9 Million. ALL VENDORS MUST HAVE AN ACTIVE REGISTRATION IN SAM.GOV AT THE TIME OF SUBMISSION IN ORDER TO BE CONSIDERED. ANY VENDOR THAT DOES NOT HAVE AN ACTIVE SAM.GOV REGISTRATION WILL BE REJECTED AS NON RESPONSIVE.
