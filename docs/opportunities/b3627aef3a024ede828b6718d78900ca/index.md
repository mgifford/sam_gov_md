## Summary

COMBINED SYNOPSIS/SOLICITATION The VA Medical Center located at 7305 North Military Trail, West Palm Beach, FL 33410 has a requirement for Graphic Designs. The Contractor shall provide all, Designing of flyers, brochures, posters, newsletters and other graphic design work order request. Most work order requests have 5 working days for completion with the exception of the initial proof of the Annual Report at the Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center, West Palm Beach, FL 33410. The attached Statement of Work shall be adhered to. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation, Request for Quotation (RFQ), for commercial items prepared in accordance with FAR Subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotations are being requested, and a written solicitation will not be issued.
