# FY26 UAWC Creator Pro and Terra Vista License Renewal

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 09:36:49.308-04
- Solicitation Number: FA561326Q0035

## Summary

SOLICITATION NOTICE: The 700th Contracting Squadron (700 CONS) intends to award a Firm Fixed Price (FFP) purchase order on a brand name basis to vendors capable of providing the brand name requirement for Presagis Creator Pro and Terra Vista Software License Renewal for a period of 12 months from 01 JUN 2026 - 31 MAY 2026. The anticipated award date is 29 MAY 2026. In accordance with (IAW) FAR6.103-1(d), Presagis is the only source capable of fulfilling this requirement for this software license renewal. Although the Department of the Airforce (DAF) is mandated by DAFMAN 17-1203 to procure software from existing Department of War (DoW) Enterprise Software Initiative (ESI) or GSA 2GIT blanket purchase agreements/contracts, market resrearch has determined that there are no capable vendors on these mandatory sources that can fulfill this requirement for the needed software. Therefore an open market solicitation will be posted on SAM.gov. See attached solicitation (COMBO-SOLICITATION FA561326Q0035)
