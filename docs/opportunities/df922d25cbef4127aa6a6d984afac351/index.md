# Radio Program - Infrastructure and Radio Repair BPA

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 12:39:13.126-04
- Solicitation Number: SHOP-PR-26-000695

## Summary

Request the establishment of a Blanket Purchase Agreement Contract for one Base Year and four one-year option periods for repairs and upgrades to the FBI’s land mobile radio (LMR) legacy equipment currently used in all of its 56 field offices. The LMR equipment is manufactured by Motorola Solutions, Inc., and spans several model generations. FBI radio communications consist of hybrid and specialty components proprietary to Motorola required to communicate with the LMR infrastructure.
