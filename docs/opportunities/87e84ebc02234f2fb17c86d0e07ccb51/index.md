## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products prepared in accordance with the Format in Subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested, and a written solicitation will not be issued. The solicitation is issued as a Request for Quote (RFQ). This solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular FAC 2026-01 dated March 01, 2026. This requirement is being solicited as a Total Small Business Set Aside using FAR Part 12 Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services. The NAICS Code is 326291 – Rubber Product Manufacturing for Mechanical Use with a small business size standard of 750 Employees. The solicitation number is 6923G526Q0041. **NOTE: Quoters must be registered in SAM.gov and have an active registration at the time of this solicitation closing and at the time of award.
