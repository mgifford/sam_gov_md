# Request for Proposals - Central Utility Building Purge Unit Replacement for Chiller No. 5 Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory, Batavia, Illinois

- Agency: ENERGY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 14:15:59.099-04
- Solicitation Number: RFP380663CJ

## Summary

The Offerors shall provide services to remove, replace, test and return Chiller No. 5 to operational condition at the Central Utility Building (CUB) on Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory (Fermilab) campus, in Batavia, Illinois. The Offerors shall furnish all labor, supervision, materials, tools, equipment, transportation and items required to perform these services.
