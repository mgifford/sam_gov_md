# RIANG Lodging BPAs

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 09:14:40.699-04
- Solicitation Number: W50S9426QSY07

## Summary

REFER TO ATTACHED B08 RIANG Lodging BPA W50S9426QSY07 and attachments 1-4: The Rhode Island Air National Guard (RIANG) has a requirement to obtain on-demand lodging for non-local Airmen in a drill status or on official government orders. In support of this requirement, the 143 Mission Support Contracting intends to award multiple Blanket Purchase Agreements (BPA) to local area hotels. Agreements will only be made directly with the hotels, not third-party vendors. a.Refer to specifications in Performance Work Statement (Attachment 1), Ordering Procedures, Terms and Conditions (Attachment 2), Service Contract Act WD 2015-4089 Rev 32 (Attachment 3), and Department of Defense Lodging Adequacy Standards - FY25 (Attachment 4). b.Lodging facility must be located within a 20-mile radius and/or a 30-minute drive of the Quonset Air National Guard Base (QANGB), located at 1 Cripe Street, North Kingstown, RI 02852. c.Resulting BPAs will be pre-priced and in accordance with the established U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) per diem rates for Rhode Island and the applicable fiscal year. https://www.gsa.gov/travel/plan-book/per-diem-rates/per-diem-rates-results?action=perdiems_report&fiscal_year=2025&state=RI&city=&zip= d.If awarded, payment will be made by Government Purchase Card (GPC) for BPA calls less than $15,000; payment will be made by Wide Area Work Flow (WAWF) for calls greater than $15,000. See Attachment 2 for details.
