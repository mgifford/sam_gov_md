## Summary

An autoclave is an essential, reliable, and cost-effective scientific instrument that uses high-pressure steam to sterilize equipment, media, and biohazardous waste. It ensures the elimination of microorganisms, prevents contamination of microbiological cultures and laboratory tools, and safely sterilizes infectious waste prior to disposal.
