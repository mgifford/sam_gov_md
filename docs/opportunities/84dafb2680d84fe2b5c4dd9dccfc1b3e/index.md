# LOGISTICS AND TECHNICAL SUPPORT SERVICES, ROTA (SPAIN)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-21 07:57:16.85-04
- Solicitation Number: N6817126Q6100

## Summary

This announcement constitutes a sources sought for written information only. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR QUOTE (RFQ) and a formal solicitation is not available at this time. The Department of the Navy, Naval Supply Systems Command (NAVSUP) FLC Rota, Spain contracting office is seeking responsible contractor for logistics and technical support services. The Place of Performance is primarily at EXPLOSIVE ORDNANCE DISPOSAL MOBILE UNIT EIGHT (EODMU8) buildings located aboard Naval Station Rota in Rota, Spain. The period of performance is a 12-month base and four (4) 12-month option periods. See attached draft Performance Work Statement for additional information. The NAICS Code for this requirement is 561990 – All Other Support Services, and the size standard is $16.5M. Interested, responsible business concerns capable of meeting these requirements are invited to submit a capabilities statement (not to exceed three pages) that demonstrates their capability to provide such services. The capability statement must also include the following information: Include company name, address, CAGE code, small business status (8A, small, small disadvantaged, women-owned, HUBZone, veteran-owned, or service-disabled veteran-owned), and point of contact (POC) information for the interested firm. Identify, by contract number, any past or current contracts and/or subcontracts that were awarded to your firm, period of performance, contract type, contract amount, and points of contact for any customer references. Please respond no later than 28 MAY 2026 at 1200 Local Rota, Spain Time to: julius.j.arrascue-pastor.civ@us.navy.mil Other general information: NAVSUP anticipates conducting an acquisition pursuant to FAR Part 12 and awarding a firm-fixed price (FFP) type contract. IF A SOLICITATION IS ISSUED AT A LATER DATE, all interested parties must respond to that solicitation announcement separately from the responses to this announcement. No reimbursement will be made for any costs associated with providing information in response to this announcement.
