# Notice of Sole Source: Fire Truck Pumps

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-13 14:02:13.088-04
- Solicitation Number: FA452826JT0005

## Summary

The Waterous CPK-5 is a two-stage, iron-body centrifugal pump designed for Ultra-High Pressure (UHP) applications on Aircraft Rescue and Firefighting (ARFF) vehicles, specifically integrated into the 2016 Oshkosh fire truck platform. The specific build for serial number 156882 requires a pump with a rated capacity of 320 GPM at 1350 PSI, a clockwise input rotation, and an inverted transmission mounting configuration.
