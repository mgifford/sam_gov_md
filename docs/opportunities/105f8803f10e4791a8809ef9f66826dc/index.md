## Summary

Project Title: BLDG 8214 WATER PLANT VALVE REPAIR Project Location: Dyess AFB Texas DESCRIPTION: The purpose of this requirement is to provide a standalone service to remove and replace 2 altitude valves at Dyess AFB TX, as well as re-paint the altitude valves. Both altitude valve removal and re-painting will be performed IAW provided drawing specifications. Site Visit: An optional site visit will be held on May 11th, at 2:00 P.M. (CST). Please see Section L. for site visit instructions. Please RSVP by May 8th, at 10:00 A.M. (CST) by emailing victor.oliveras-miranda@us.af.mil and jesse.wisowaty@us.af.mil to coordinate attendance. SET- ASIDE: This is a total small business set aside. Offerors who are not registered as a small business in SAM.gov will not be considered for this award. REQUIRMENTS: Interested vendors must submit a capability statement, technical approach document outlining how all requirements in the SOW will be accomplished, and this form with all information filled out. DUE DATE: May 20th, 2026, at 12:00 P.M. (CST). Please send your offers to SSgt Victor Oliveras at victor.oliveras-miranda@us.af.mil EVALUATION OF OFFERS: The evaluation for this acquisition will be solely based on lowest price found to be technically acceptable. By signing its offer, the offeror certifies that each price stated on each CLIN includes an appropriate apportionment of all costs, direct and indirect, all state and local taxes, overhead, and profit. Q&A: All questions are due by 13th May 2026 at 12:00 PM CST, to be sent to both SSgt Victor Oliveras (victor.oliveras-miranda@us.af.mil) and SrA Jesse Wisowaty (jesse.wisowaty@us.af.mil).
