# Detachable Wheel Unit - ZackLift Z453

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 09:41:13.417-04
- Solicitation Number: H9224026QE048

## Summary

1. Description This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products prepared in accordance with RFO 12.202(b). This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested, and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. The Government intends to issue a firm-fixed-price purchase order for the required items. This solicitation is conducted using simplified procedures under RFO 12.201-1. This solicitation is issued as a 100% small business set-aside. The associated NAICS Code is 333924 (Industrial Truck, Tractor, Trailer, and Stacker Machinery Manufacturing), with a small business size standard of 900 employees. Quotes are due 29 May 2026 by 10 AM EDT. Quotes shall be submitted via email and in accordance with 52.212-2, to the Contracting Officer Jeffrey Morris at jeffrey.s.morris.civ@socom.mil. Quotes must include a completed Attachment 1 – Price Worksheet. 2. Required Items 0001 – (2026 5TH-453) ZackLift Z453-FIFTHWHEELER DETACHABLE 2026 ZACKLIFT 5th wheel attachable/detachable. Super Heavy Duty Towing capacity, lift capacity of 45,000lb retracted and 20,000lbs extended. Tow capacity of 100,000lbs. Extended reach of 132in. 25 degree underlift tilt above ground. 5 degree underlift below ground. 12” extension reach 144in. Unit weight of 5,805 lbs. Min. 25’ corded remote control – Fork Rack. 36” LED lightbar. Universal fork bracket. Aerodyne Tall Tool Boxes (pair) Heavy duty wheel lift: 15” to 24.5” 25,000lb Stiff Legs 5”x5” Leg 18,000lb rating trailer hitch/5th wheel hitch/pintle & 2 5/16th ball Scoop n Go Fork pair Steertek Fork pair Chain attachment block *See Pricing Worksheet for additional information. Delivery Details Delivery Address: 1122 Amphibious Drive, Virginia Beach, VA 23459. Delivery Date: Offerors shall propose their best delivery schedule (e.g., XX Days After Receipt of Order). The Government desires delivery within 30 days. FOB Terms: FOB Destination. The Contractor is responsible for all shipping to destination. 3. Brand Name Justification This is a brand name only requirement. The ZackLift Z453 is required to maintain standardization within the vehicle inventory. 4. Submission Instructions Submit quotes via email to: jeffrey.s.morris.civ@socom.mil Quotes must include a completed Attachment 1 – Price Worksheet. Quotes must remain valid for 30 days. 5. Applicable Provisions and Clauses See attached SF 1449 (H9224026QE048)
