# Vermont Army National Guard Non-Clinical Case Management

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-04-26 20:10:02.138-04

## Summary

Non-Clinical Case Management - the Vermont Army National Guard (ARNG) requires Medical Case Management Services to achieve and maintain Medical Readiness goals established by the ARNG. This contract consists of three (3) Registered Nurses and (2) two Medical Records Technicians. The Medical Records Technicians and Registered Nurses will have responsibility for implementing administrative decisions directly affecting the care of medical records programs. The aforementioned positions shall assemble, analyze, code, abstract, report, maintain, and extract medical records information.
