# Officer Training School - Alaskan Style Tents

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-26 14:24:53.634-04
- Solicitation Number: FA3300OTS26I

## Summary

The 42d Contracting Squadron, Maxwell AFB, AL, is requesting information from industry to procure brand name CAMSS-HIT-20QM4W, or equal, temporary shelter systems, environmental control units, electrical distribution, and associated delivery/installation services to support housing and training operations at the Officer Training School (OTS) Blue Thunder location. This requirement involves unique characteristics necessary for compatibility with the end-user's existing equipment and operational footprint. Information gathered from this notice will be used to finalize the acquisition strategy and potential small business set?aside decisions for the requirement detailed in the attached draft Statement of Requirements (SOR).
