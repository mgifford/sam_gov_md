# Jackson Public Schools STEM

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 11:04:00.202-04
- Solicitation Number: W9124D26QA245

## Summary

Provide services for lodging, meals, in and around transportation, training facilities, and a residential Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) experience for 360 Cadets from 1 June - 17 July 2026. There will be six (6) one-week five (5) days for each cycle with 60 Cadets and four (4) Cadre per cycle. Cycle 1: 1-5 June 2026, Cycle 2: 8-12 June 2026, Cycle 3: 15-19 June 2026, Cycle 4: 22-26 June 2026, Cycle 5: 6-10 July 2026, and Cycle 6: 13-17 July 2026.
