## Summary

CBP OIT Virtual Industry Day Event Description Join U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Office of Information and Technology’s (OIT) upcoming Virtual Industry Day on June 9, 2026, at 1:00PM EST, designed to strengthen collaboration between federal government teams and industry partners. This session will provide a platform to share insights, align priorities, and explore innovative approaches to addressing mission-critical challenges. During the event, government leaders will highlight key initiatives and upcoming opportunities, offering valuable perspectives on strategic direction and future areas of focus. Participants will gain a clearer understanding of agency priorities and upcoming initiatives, receiving valuable insight into strategic direction and future areas of focus. We encourage you to join and hear how you can help shape future efforts. Registration: Each company may register up to 4 attendees. Registration must be completed for each attendee. All interested participants should submit their registration by Friday, June 5, 2026. If you have questions regarding the registration process, please reach out to oitindustrydayevents@cbp.dhs.gov. Pre-questions: We encourage you to submit questions prior to the event. Please email your questions to oitindustrydayevents@cbp.dhs.gov by Friday, June 5, 2026. To ensure we address your questions either during or after the event, please use the same email address that you registered with when sending your questions.
