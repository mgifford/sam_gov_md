# JLG Lift Repair

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 16:20:04.245-04
- Solicitation Number: FA480026Q0061

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all personnel, supervision, parts, tools, materials, and equipment necessary to repair the JLG 600S Telescopic Boom Lift in accordance with (IAW) the Statement of Work (SOW), restoring it to a safe, reliable, and fully operational condition that meets all manufacturer specifications. Questions are due no later than 29 May 2026 at 1500, and responses will be provided by 1600 on 02 June 2026. Only one set of questions will be recorded and responded to per contractor so please ensure that your latest submission is a compilation of the previous questions (if applicable).
