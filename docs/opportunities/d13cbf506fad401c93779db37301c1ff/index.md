# F--CGB Goat Grazing Vegetation Management

- Posted: 2026-05-29 11:59:03.326-04

## Summary

Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) Setup for Goat Grazing Vegetation Management This is a Sources Sought Notice for small businesses capable of providing vegetation management in the form of goat and/or sheep grazing for the Bureau of Reclamation’s (Reclamation) Central California Area Office (CCAO) and Northern California Area Office (NCAO) lands. See the attached Performance Work Statement. This notice is being issued to help determine the availability of qualified small businesses technically capable of meeting the Government requirements and determining the method of acquisition. This Sources Sought is issued solely for information and planning purposes and does not constitute a Request for Quote (RFQ). Respondents are solely responsible for all expenses associated with responding to the Sources Sought Notice. Information received from this Sources Sought Notice may be utilized in preparing potential solicitation documentation. It is the contractor’s responsibility to identify proprietary information in response to this notice. The purpose of this announcement is to gain knowledge of potentially qualified small businesses that capable of fulfilling this requirement. NAICS Code 112420 – Goat Farming - $2.5 million. Responses Due Date: June 18, 2026 @ 1:00 PM PD (Pacific Daylight Time) Send Responses to: Mouang Phan, mouang_phan@ios.doi.gov Potential vendors shall provide the following information at a minimum in order to be considered a viable source: 1. Business Size & Classification(s) 2. Contact Information (Name, Phone, email & website (If Applicable)) 3. Capabilities Statements 4. Appropriate document that demonstrates capabilities to provide the services needed. * If any of the required information, items 1 to 4, are not submitted in its entirety by the response date, the vendor will be considered incapable of providing the required services and shall not be considered a viable source for market research purposes. Notice Number 140R2026Q0085 has been assigned and should be referenced on all correspondence regarding this announcement.
