# Purchase of 2 (two) snowmobiles for the Chequamegon Nicolet National Forest

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 10:13:55.026-04
- Solicitation Number: 12444626Q0042

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12444626Q0042 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Two(2) new SKI DOO Skandic 900 ACE or other manufacturer’s Equivalent Horsepower 24 inch wide track snowmobiles. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 336991 The small business size standard is Small Business This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
