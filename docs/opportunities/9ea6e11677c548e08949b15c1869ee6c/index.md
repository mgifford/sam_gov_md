# 115 Fighter Wing - Construct Alert GOV Parking

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 07:54:21.519-04
- Solicitation Number: W50S9F26R0005

## Summary

115 Fighter Wing Construct Alert GOV Parking Category: Y1BZ Construction of Other Airfield Structures NAICS Code: 236220, Commercial and Institutional Building Construction Contract: 115th Fighter Wing, 3110 Mitchell Street, Madison, WI 53704, POC: Lt Col Dave Mattila Synopsis: 115 Fighter Wing Construct Alert GOV Parking The purpose of this sources sought is to gain knowledge of potential sources for this project including interested Small Business sources. This notice is for planning purposes only. No reimbursement will be made for any costs associated with providing information in response to this notice or any follow up information requests. All interested parties should respond. The 115th Fighter Wing intends to issue a solicitation and award a firm-fixed price construction contract to construct Alert Gov’t Owned Vehicle (GOV) Parking facility in support of the F-35 Alert Mission. The magnitude of this project is estimated to be between $1,000,000 and $2,000,000. All interested concerns will provide this office, in writing, a notice stating their positive intent to submit a proposal as a prime contractor no later than time/date specified in SAM.gov posting. This notification, which shall not exceed five typewritten pages, must include ALL of the following: (1) Positive statement of intent to bid as a prime contractor (2) A completed and signed Source Sought Information Request Form (Attached) (3) A listing of projects completed during the past six years, both for government and private industry. The type of project, dollar value, contract number, location, and point of contact are to be included. Indicate if you were prime or subcontractor, and if subcontractor, provide the name and point of contact for the prime contractor. (4) A brief outline of resources, subcontractors, and key personnel that would be used to accomplish the contract. In the event adequate Small Business contractors are not available for adequate competition for this project, it may be advertised as unrestricted. Information shall be provided via email to: dave.mattila.2@us.af.mil. Include the following in the subject line: Sources Sought W50S9F-26-R-0005. No facsimile responses will be honored. PROGRAM DETAILS: The 115th Fighter Wing at Dane County Regional Airport -Truax Field, Madison, WI is conducting market research seeking capable sources for services, non-personal, to provide all plant, labor, transportation, materials, tools, equipment, appliances, and supervision necessary to construct project XGFG182006 Construct Alert GOV Parking Shelters. This requirement will construct two (2) new parking shelters that house transport vehicles, ground service equipment, and snow removal equipment. Scope includes, but is not limited to, foundations, cold-formed steel framing, metal panel roof and Walls, EIFS insulation, power distribution, interior/exterior lighting, and automated controls. SUSTAINMENT: Relocate storm drain inlet and replace site asphalt pavement. Demolish 3 temporary structures (2 tents and “grain bin”). All work will be in accordance with design requirements, specifications, and drawings provided within the master specifications. This solicitation will result in a single construction contract.
