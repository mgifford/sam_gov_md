The subject line of the email should include the solicitation number: 52000PR260025266. Performance Work Statement (PWS) / Statement of Work (SOW) 1. Scope of Work: The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to perform the camshaft and rocker arm assembly replacement on the Starboard S60 Engine (S/N: 06R) of USCG vessel RB-M 45767. All work must be performed by MTU-certified technicians using genuine MTU OEM parts. Subcontracting is strictly prohibited. 2. Specific Tasks: A certified S60 MTU engine technician shall provide labor and consumable parts to repair the starboard engine. Remove all interferences to access and replace the S60 camshaft, 1 rocker arm assembly, and #1-cylinder injector. Remove and reinstall the valve cover and gearcase housing cover. Replace the camshaft, the #1 rocker arm assembly, and the #1-cylinder injector. Torque all fasteners and seal the gearcase housing cover according to MTU specifications. Time and tune the engine in accordance with MTU specifications for the USCG S60 engine. Conduct a sea trial to ensure repairs are acceptable to the Station EPO. 3. Deliverables: Service Completion: Successful completion of all repairs and a satisfactory sea trial. 4. Place of Performance: Services shall be performed at: USCG Station Ketchikan 1300 Stedman St Ketchikan, AK 99901 5. Period of Performance: The period of performance will be determined upon contract award.
