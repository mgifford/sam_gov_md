# Powertrain Component Overhaul, Repair and Parts

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 14:44:06.151-04
- Solicitation Number: FA248626PWRTR

## Summary

The Air Force Test Center (AFTC/PZZC) at Eglin AFB, Florida, has issued a Request for Information (RFI) to conduct market research and identify capable sources—specifically targeting various categories of small and socio-economically disadvantaged businesses—for an upcoming Multi-Award Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (MAC IDIQ) contract. The primary mission of this acquisition is to support the 96th Range Support Squadron, Targets Flight (96 RANSS/RNRT) by providing overhauled, rebuilt, fabricated, and acquired powertrain components, driveline components, and repair parts restored to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) specifications. The anticipated contract features a five-year base period and a five-year option period, totaling a potential ten-year duration, and covers a diverse fleet of domestic and foreign military armored and non-armored assets, radar systems, trucks, and watercraft. In accordance with attached RFI Document and SOW.
