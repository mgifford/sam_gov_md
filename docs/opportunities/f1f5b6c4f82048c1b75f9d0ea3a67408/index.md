# nCoup Inc. IDS Software Annual Subscription

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-28 18:24:43.856-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C26026Q0610

## Summary

Customer shall deploy nCoup IDS, a web application, to manage the inventory and accountability of investigational drugs. All drug inventory transactions (receipts, transfers, dispenses, wastes, and returns) shall be recorded in nCoup IDS and inventory levels shall update accordingly. Customer shall also have the ability in nCoup IDS to create patient labels when dispensing, verify transactions with secondary authentication, track drug expiration, record patient returns, set up drug specific notifications, manage study documents, track par levels, and more.
