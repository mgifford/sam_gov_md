# STARCOM Delt 1 Enlisted Initial Skills Training

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-08 08:47:27.768-04
- Solicitation Number: FA254926EISTRFI

## Summary

The contractor shall develop, instruct, and maintain an Enlisted Initial Skills Training course for Guardian Space-Domain Fundamentals; Space Systems Operations (5S); Intelligence (5I), and Cyberspace Operations (5C). The effort also includes Mission Integration, Exercises, and Capstone Events, as well as training support and modeling and simulation.
