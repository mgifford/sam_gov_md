# Extended Stay Hotel Services in Ottawa Ontario

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 10:44:59.575-04
- Solicitation Number: 19CA5226Q0014

## Summary

Embassy of the United States of America in Ottawa, Ontario, has a requirement for extended stay hotel accommodations and the related services for the U.S. Government personnel under the authority of the U.S. Diplomatic Mission to Canada. Hotel accommodations shall provide the following: One-bedroom, or two-bedroom, or three-bedroom suites Fully furnished kitchen in suite including refrigerator, stove/cooktop, microwave, dishwasher, cookware, cooking utensils, dishes and flatware. Separate living and sleeping areas Climate control (heating and air conditioning) In-suite washer and dryer or on-site laundry facilities Work desk with ergonomic seating and adequate lighting High-speed internet access Television with cable or streaming television service Adequate storage and closet space Housekeeping service Fresh linens and towels provided weekly 24-hour front desk service One parking spot in a secure area (one space per suite) Pet friendly preferred
