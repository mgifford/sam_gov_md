1.0 Description The 30th Contracting Squadron is conducting market research to identify potential sources for the procurement and installation of a new telescopic bleacher system for the Youth Center Gymnasium at Vandenberg Space Force Base (VSFB). The objective is a turn-key solution that includes the professional delivery and installation of the new bleacher system. This is a Sources Sought Notice/Request for Information (RFI) only. This is not a solicitation for proposals, and no contract will be awarded from this notice.

2.0 Background In accordance with DAFI 34-144, the previous bleachers were removed for safety reasons and to accommodate new flooring. To ensure the gymnasium can continue to offer youth sports, host events, and adhere to Air Force standards, new, safe, and modern bleachers are required.

3.0 Minimum Specifications The Government is seeking a complete bleacher system that meets the following minimum requirements: • Type: Telescopic, manually via provided operating handles AND/OR electronically operated using standard US 110V, 115V, or 120V. • Configuration: 4 rows high by approximately 57 feet 3 inches wide. • Row Dimensions: 9-5/8” rise per row and 24” row spacing. • Decking: Clear, UV resistant finished decks. • Seating: Courtside XC10” polymer seats or an equivalent model. • Aisles & Steps: Two (2) aisles featuring intermediate steps, aisle rails, and hinged front aisle steps. • Safety Rails: Self-Storing end guard rails. • Services: A complete turn-key solution including professional delivery and installation. Installation is to be performed during regular business hours (Monday-Friday, 0700-1700). • Timeframe: Installation should take no longer than 5 days to complete once all equipment has been delivered on-site. The delivery and installation timeframe is anticipated between June and September 2026.

4.0 Information Requested from Vendors Interested parties are requested to provide a capabilities statement that addresses the following: 1. Company Information: Company Name, Address, Point of Contact (Name, Email, Phone), CAGE Code, and Unique Entity ID (UEI). 2. Business Size: Please identify your business size (e.g., Small Business, 8(a), HUBZone, Woman-Owned Small Business, Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, etc.) under the NAICS code that best fits this requirement. 3. Capability: A brief description of your company's capability to meet the minimum specifications listed in Section 3.0. Please confirm if you can provide a "Courtside XC10" polymer seat or describe your proposed equivalent, including product specifications. 4. Past Performance: A brief description of similar projects (up to three) completed within the last five years for government or commercial customers. Please include the project name, a brief description of the scope, period of performance, and customer point of contact. 5. Turn-key Solution: Confirmation that your company can provide the required turn-key solution, including delivery and complete installation.
