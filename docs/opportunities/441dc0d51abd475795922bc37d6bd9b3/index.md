# Giant Voice

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 14:27:54.253-04
- Solicitation Number: FA252126QB049

## Summary

The current Mass Notification System at Patrick SFB and Cape Canaveral SFS rely on older hardware and software that is approaching its end-of-life. To prevent compatibility issues with modern operating systems and mitigate the risk of service disruptions, an upgrade is necessary. This project will replace key components to align with current technology standards and maintain the base's critical alert and notification capabilities.
