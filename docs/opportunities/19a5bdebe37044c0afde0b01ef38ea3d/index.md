## Summary

Swagelok brand fittings are high quality fittings that are the industry standard for laboratory applications. As such, these specific fittings have been completely integrated into laboratory operations. No other fittings are compatible with Swagelok, and no known fittings offer the performance and value. This order is for replacements and additions to the supply of fittings that the laboratory already has available and in use. Other fitting manufacturers are available, but are of inferior quality and incompatible with the Swagelok fittings the laboratory already has in use.
