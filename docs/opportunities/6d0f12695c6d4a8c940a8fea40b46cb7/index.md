# Interview and Surveillance System - Eielson AFB, AK

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 19:54:10.281-04
- Solicitation Number: FA500426QA001

## Summary

The 354th Security Forces Squadron requires an updated interview and surveillance recording system to replace their outdated equipment, which hampers investigations. The new system must support at least three interview rooms and one monitoring station, ensuring high-quality audio and video capture, secure evidence management, and seamless integration with existing operations. Case Cracker is the preferred system due to its proven capability to integrate with our existing data analytics and case management infrastructure.
