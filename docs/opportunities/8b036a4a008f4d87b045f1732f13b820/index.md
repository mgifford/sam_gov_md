## Summary

The Government requires one (1) 500kW XQ570 Caterpillar Generator Set. The generator must include a trailer and a double-walled fuel tank with 110% spill containment. Delivery is required to Andersen Air Force Base, Guam. The generator must comply with all emissions requirements for operation on Guam and USAF installations.
