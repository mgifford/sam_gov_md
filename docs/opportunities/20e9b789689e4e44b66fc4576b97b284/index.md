- Posted: 2026-05-01 12:16:42.003-04

## Summary

The International Law Enforcement Academy (ILEA) San Salvador requires comprehensive lodging services to support its mission of providing specialized training, professional development, and multilateral exchanges for foreign law enforcement officials. The ILEA program is complex and multifaceted, requiring professional management and maintenance of on-site lodging facilities to ensure uninterrupted training operations. Evaluation Method: Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA) Proposal Submission Volume I: Technical Proposal Volume II: Past Performance Volume III: Pricing (Excel format) For complete requirements, detailed specifications, quality assurance standards, contractor responsibilities, and evaluation criteria, please review the full Statement of Work (SOW) and solicitation documentation.
