# Furniture for CRO at Library of Congress

- Agency: LIBRARY OF CONGRESS
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-22 16:39:11.852-04
- Solicitation Number: 2026-ISS-0057

## Summary

The Library of Congress (LOC) requires new office furniture to replace worn out items. This procurement is a full-and-open competition. There is no set-aside for small business. Proposals are due no later than Wednesday May 27, 2026 at 5:00pm Eastern Standard Time and must be submitted via email to the Contracting Officer, Abby Schultz at aschultz@loc.gov and the Contract Specialist, Mieke Bruch, via email at mbruch@loc.gov Please note, any proposals received after the due date and time may not be considered. This RFQ is not to be construed as a contract or commitment of any kind. LOC shall not be liable for payment of any costs attributed to the preparation and submission of requested proposal documentation.
