## Summary

VISN 1 has a requirement to establish an enterprise-wide, 5 year Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) for Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) to support automated delivery operations across all VISN 1 VA medical centers. The BPA will provide AMR equipment, system infrastructure, installation, training, and full maintenance services to enable autonomous delivery of medications, laboratory specimens, supplies, and related materials throughout VA facilities. Please refer to the attached solicitation documents for complete details and requirements.
