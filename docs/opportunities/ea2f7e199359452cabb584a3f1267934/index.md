## Summary

Sources Sought Notice: Tele-Radiology Services 1. This is a Sources Sought Notice for market research purposes only and is not a request for proposals or quotes. The purpose of this notice is to identify potential contractors capable of providing Tele-Radiology Services for the Department of Veterans Affairs. Ordering Station: Temple VA Central Texas Healthcare System Radiology Service 1901 Veterans Memorial Drive Temple, TX 76504-7451 2. General Scope: The VA is seeking information from qualified vendors on their ability to provide a full range of Tele-Radiology services including, but not limited to: · Interpretation of diagnostic imaging exams: Mammograms General Radiology CT MRI Ultrasound PET/CT Nuclear Stress Tests General Nuclear Studies · STAT and routine readings · Subspecialty expertise (as applicable) · Secure electronic transmission and receipt of images and reports · Board-certified radiologists · Availability of 24/7 coverage (as applicable) 3. Requested Information: Interested vendors are requested to submit the following: · Company name, DUNS/UEI, and point of contact information. · Description of Tele-Radiology services available. · Documentation of capability to provide Tele-Radiology services, including security for transmission of PHI as required by HIPAA and VA policy. · Experience providing Tele-Radiology services to VA or other federal facilities (please list contracts, dates, and points of contact if available). · Ability to meet turnaround time requirements for STAT and routine studies. · Availability of sub-specialty reads (if applicable). · Any additional relevant certifications or accreditations (e.g., Joint Commission, ACR). · Any feedback, questions, or recommendations regarding the draft Performance Work Statement (PWS). 4. Submission Details: This is not a request for quote or proposal; no award will be made as a result of this notice. Responses are due by Friday, 8 May 2026, at 12:00 PM Mountain Time. Please submit responses to the Contracting Officer at noel.ramirez2@va.gov. Information received will be used for market research purposes only. 5. Disclaimer: This Sources Sought Notice is issued solely for information and planning purposes and does not commit the Government to contract for any supply or service. We thank you for your interest and assistance with this market research. End of Notice
