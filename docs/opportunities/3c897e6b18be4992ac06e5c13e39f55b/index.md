# Construction of Fort Hood Staging/Marshalling Area

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 14:24:04.718-04
- Solicitation Number: W9126G26R1B95

## Summary

Construct (DBB) a staging/marshalling area with container loading aprons for line haul operations. Primary facilities include staging/marshalling area, operations building, loading/unloading docks and ramps, non-organizational vehicle parking, and building information systems. Low Impact Development (LID), Best Management Practices (BMP) and sustainability and energy enhancement measures are included.
