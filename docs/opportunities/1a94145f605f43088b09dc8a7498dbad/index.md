## Summary

The contractor shall furnish all labor,equipment, transportation, supplies, and materials to provide Pest and Rodent Control Treatment Services at four administrative sites, on the Grindstone Ranger District, Mendocino National Forest. The pest-control services shall adhere to recommend practices to minimize attraction and harboring of rodents, roaches, and other pests and to perform extermination, trapping, and control procedures at regular intervals. This contract will be a base with potential 4 options years.
