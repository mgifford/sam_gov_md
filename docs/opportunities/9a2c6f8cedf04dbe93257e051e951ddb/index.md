# Procurement of Non-Tactical Vehicles

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 19:56:09.566-04
- Solicitation Number: M67400-26-RFI-0006

## Summary

Presolicitaion Notice Requirement: Procurement of Non-Tactical Vehicles Sources Sought Notice Number: M67400-26-RFI-0006 Office: MCIPAC Regional Contracting Office 1. REQUIREMENT OVERVIEW The Government requires Non-Tactical Vehicles for delivery to Camp Foster (Okinawa) and MCAS Iwakuni (Yamaguchi). All vehicles must meet Japanese road safety standards. 2. RESEARCH CONDUCTED To determine market capability, a Sources Sought Notification (SSN) was posted to SAM.gov seeking information from commercial vendors able to fulfill the requirement. 3. FINDINGS • Capable Sources: The Government identified multiple vendors fully capable of sourcing all requested vehicles and managing delivery to military installations both in Okinawa and Iwakuni. • Market Capability: The responses confirmed that the commercial market can easily support this requirement without any need for specialized, non-commercial development. Industry-standard lead times will be factored into the delivery schedule. 4. ACQUISITION STRATEGY The Government intends to solicit quotes directly from local vendors who demonstrate the capability to provide all items. The Government intends to award a single contract for all vehicles in response to the solicitation.
