# SPECTROMAX Optical Emissions Spectrometer

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 14:12:47.513-04
- Solicitation Number: SP4706-26-Q-XX17

## Summary

This is a pre-solicitation notice, a solicitation will be posted at a later date. This requirement is for a SPECTROMAX brandname optical emissions spectrometer (OES), to be delivered to the Mechanical Product Test Center (MPTC) located in Columbus, Ohio. Scope of work includes one (1) meter, instlalation, calibration, and three (3) days of on-site training for up to four (4) MPTC associates. Please see the attached technical specifications for more details. Interested parties may contact the Contracting Officer (KO) Elizabeth Dickstein via email. Contact info is provided in the Primary Point of Contact section this pre-solicitation notification.
