## Summary

Statement of Work USCGC MUNRO (WMSL 755) is requesting quotes for supplies/services as described in the following statement of work: -2000 gallons of Shell Rotella T3 15W-40 (must be specific brand and name) No substitutes. -The delivery will be coordinated with the selected vendor but will be required during the week of MAY 18 2026. -Preference will go to bulk delivery that will occur after 0800 (8:00 a.m.) and finish before sundown. . -The vendor will provide hoses for delivery from the truck to the cutter and shall have at least 150 feet of hose to transfer from the pier to the cutter. -Oil on load will take place at Coast Guard Island and will take place at berth 1 on a concrete pier. -Vendor shall have proper permits to transfer oil over water. -The cutter connection point is a 2 inch cam lock fitting and vendor shall provide connection from hose to cam lock. Selection will be based on lowest price technically acceptable. Questions shall be directed to MK2 Matthew Barnett, Matthew.N.Barnett@uscg.mil, 443-618-9775.
