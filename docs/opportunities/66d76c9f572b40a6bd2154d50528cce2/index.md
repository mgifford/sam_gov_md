# Temperature Baths

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 17:18:17.457-04
- Solicitation Number: PANRSA26P0000033423

## Summary

Questions and Answers document has been uploaded for your review. The U.S. Army TMDE Activity (USATA) has a requirement to implement a brand name purchase of three (3) Temperature Bath models or equivalent that will be a life cycle replacement of an existing model that is becoming inoperable. The unit shall be delivered to USATA’s Army Physical Standards Labs located at Building 5435, Fowler Road Redstone Arsenal. The Temperature Baths will also include the additional measurements to support USATA’s mission as follow: Requirements: Fluke Model 6055, High Temperature Bath Deep-Well Qty: 1 200°C to 550°C 60Hz/220VAC Fluke Model 7340-16, Compact Temperature Bath Qty: 2 -40°C to 150°C 60Hz/115VAC Delivery Requirements Shipping Address: U.S. Army TMDE Activity, Attn: Matthew Watts, Building 5435, Redstone Arsenal, AL 35898
