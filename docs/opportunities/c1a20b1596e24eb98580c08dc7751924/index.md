## Summary

The Bay Pines VA Healthcare System, at 10000 Bay Pines Blvd, Bay Pines, FL 33744, has a requirement for Laser Making Equipment (brand name or equal). All vendors must be properly registered in the System Award Management database (SAM) and SBA Small Business Search (SBS). Price quotes shall include the Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) Number, SBA Dynamic Small Business Search (DSBS) SDVOSB certification, any other supporting information for the price submitted, and any items identified in ADDENDUM to FAR 52.212-1 Instructions to Offerors. The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 339112 (Surgical and Medical Instrument Manufacturing) with a small business size standard of 1,000 Employees. The solicitation will be available on or about May 4, 2026 and will be distributed solely through the SAMs website (www.SAM.gov). The anticipated date for receipt of quotes is on or about May 14, 2026 with responses due to William Sean Penhallegon at william.penhallegon@va.gov.
