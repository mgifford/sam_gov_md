The Government will in no way be bound to this information if any solicitation is issued. The Department of Veterans Affairs Garner VA Outpatient Clinic, 2700 Benson Road, Garner, NC 27529, has a requirement for ice and water dispensers to support patient needs. Garner VA Outpatient Clinic is requesting contractors to provide brand name or equal equipment that meets or exceed the physical, functional, and performance of: Ice Maker, Countertop, Dispenser, Ice and Water: Follett, LLC. 125 lb Countertop Nugget Ice and Water Dispenser Model # 7CI100A-IW-CF-ST-00 Countertop nugget ice and water dispenser produces up to 125 pounds of ice every day. The storage area can hold up to 7 pounds at a time. High-density, foamed-in-place polyurethane slows the melting of ice stored there. Features: A sleep mode saves energy, and the harvest cycles are quiet. The blue lights on the front show you where to get ice and water dispensed. There is an internal filter for the ice and water. Internal components coated with a silver-based antimicrobial agent. 20-ounce drip tray collects water. Size approximately 22 L x
