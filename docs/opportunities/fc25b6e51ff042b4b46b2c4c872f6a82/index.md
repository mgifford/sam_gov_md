# General Electric (GE) Engineering Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 13:02:14.006-04
- Solicitation Number: N3220526R6003

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products and commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; Offers are being requested, and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number is N3220526R6003 and is being issued as a Request for Proposals (RFP) for a broad range of General Electric (GE) Power Conversion systems and items and exclusively authorized Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts and technician services to maintain the automation systems onboard multiple MSC ship classes. Specifically, the required parts and services are for the following automation systems: Machinery Plant Systems and Machinery Centralized Control Systems (MCCS), CoGen Power Take Off Systems, (PTO), Power Take In Systems (PTI), Assured Power Modification Systems, and other associated items onboard MSC vessels. This acquisition is not set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul. Defense Priorities and Allocations System (DPAS): Orders placed under this contract may be subject to the Defense Priorities and Allocations System (DPAS) regulation (15 CFR 700). Should an order require a priority rating, the Request for Quote (RFQ) will include the provision FAR 52.211-14 Notice of Priority Rating for National Defense, Emergency Preparedness, and Energy Program Use and clause FAR 52.211-15 Defense Priority and Allocation Requirements for Notice of Priority Rating for National Defense, Emergency Preparedness, and Energy Program Use, which will specify the applicable rating. See attached SF 1449 for a list of all details of the supplies and/or services required and a list of all solicitation provisions and contract clauses that apply to the acquisition. Responses to the solicitation are due 5 June 2026 no later than 5:00 P.M. Eastern Time Norfolk, Virginia. Proposals may be e-mailed to Shelby Beach at shelby.m.beach.civ@us.navy.mil, Kourtney Brooks at kourtney.k.brooks.civ@us.navy.mil, and Timothy Lewis at timothy.lewis.60.civ@us.navy.mil. To be considered timely, an e-mail proposal must be received in its entirety in the designated e-mail inbox by the due date and time for quote submission. Reference the solicitation number on your quote. E-mail quotes shall be in the format specified in the solicitation. No other method of submission is acceptable. The anticipated award date is 01 October 2026. One or more of the items under this acquisition is subject to the World Trade Organization Government Procurement Agreement and Free Trade Agreements. This is a sole source requirement. The intended source is GE as the Original Equipment Manufacturer for the proposed supplies and services. Questions or comments regarding this notice may be addressed to the point of contact of this announcement.
