# Solid Waste Management Refuse and Recycling

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 12:07:04.922-04
- Solicitation Number: W56ZTN26BA002

## Summary

Aberdeen Proving Ground/Adelphi Laboratory Center and associated satellite locations require (non-hazardous) refuse, bulk waste, and recycling services during the normal course of daily activities and operations. Services will collect and remove materials to appropriate facilities for the items collected. Period of performance (PoP) desired is for a five (5) year contract. This requirement encompasses all areas of the various facilities/areas on both APG and ALC, including areas with limited access. The recycling component of this requirement utilizes both offsite recycling terminals as well as an onsite metal recycling area on APG.
