# HNJT-OL Commercial Coworking Space

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 11:02:49.733-04
- Solicitation Number: FA205726RB002

## Summary

AFLCMC/HNJT-OL requires a Special Access Program Facility (SAPF) to house an operational IT team consisting of 44 personnel responsible for the management of the Secure Voice Service (SVS). The space will need to accommodate 8 teams, dedicated office spaces, IT equipment storage, a loading dock, robust communication infrastructure, and a secure communications room. - Each seat should include 6 fiber drops for various phone and data equipment. Each desk will require space for 2 PCs, 3 monitors, and 2 phones. - 1,500 square feet of storage space is required to maintain an inventory of over 2,500 pieces of IT equipment. - A loading dock is required for delivery of hardware. - A 2 GB commercial data circuit is required for remote management of the IT system. - A comm room large enough for 2 racks is required for installation of IT equipment.
