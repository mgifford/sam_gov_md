# Electric Low Speed Vehicle Supply, Cape Cod Canal, Buzzards Bay, MA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 09:47:23.274-04
- Solicitation Number: W912WJ26QA104

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers - New England District is issuing a Presolicitation Notice for Electric Low Speed Vehicle Supply, Cape Cod Canal, Buzzards Bay, MA. QUESTIONS REGARDING THE PROCUREMENT WILL NOT BE ANSWERED DURING THE PRESOLICITATION PHASE. SUBMIT QUESTIONS TO THE CONTRACT SPECIALIST AFTER THE SOLICITATION AND ASSOCIATED DOCUMENTS HAVE BEEN POSTED. THE WORK CONSISTS OF: Contractor shall supply, deliver, and unload one (1) new Electric Low Speed Vehicle (eLSV) to the US Army Corps of Engineers Cape Cod Canal Field Office located in Buzzards Bay, Massachusetts. The eLSV shall be new and delivered complete and operational and include all requirements listed in the scope. This is not a request for quotes at this time. The solicitation requirements and contract documents, to include instructions for submission, and the response date will be available on or about May 19, 2026. Neither telephonic, mailed nor faxed requests for hard copies of this solicitation will be accepted. The solicitation will only be available online; in order to download the solicitation offerors must access the Contract Opportunities website (www.SAM.gov). This procurement is set-aside for small business vendors. The applicable NAICS code is 336999 with a Small Business Size Standard of 1,000 Employees. ALL VENDORS MUST HAVE AN ACTIVE REGISTRATION IN SAM.GOV AT THE TIME OF SUBMISSION IN ORDER TO BE CONSIDERED. ANY VENDOR THAT DOES NOT HAVE AN ACTIVE SAM.GOV REGISTRATION WILL BE REJECTED AS NON RESPONSIVE.
