# Architecture, Development, Engineering, and Management Services ADEMS

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF

- Posted: 2026-05-04 11:00:04.404-04

## Summary

ADEMS provides highly specialized technical contractor support services to assist the Office of the Chief Information Officer (OCIO) and its customers in executing and accomplishing its mission objectives. These services include application analysis, architectural design, engineering, development, maintenance, and support, requiring expertise in platforms such as Dynamics 365, Power Platform, SharePoint, Office 365, Azure, Dataverse, Confluence, Jira, and ServiceNow, as well as agile software development, project management, technical architecture, continuous integration, testing, configuration management, ongoing operations and maintenance, and cloud migration.
