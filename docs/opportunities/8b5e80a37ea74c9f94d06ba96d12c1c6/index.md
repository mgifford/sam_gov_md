## Summary

SOURCE SELECTION INFORMATION – See FAR 2.101 and

3.104 PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT (DD-LA-(AR) 1279) By Light Professional IT Services LLC., Mclean, Virginia was awarded a non-competitive single award indefinite delivery/indefinite quantity, firm fixed price, new award contract to provide the Full Content Inspection (FCI) managed service in support of the Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) on May 8, 2026. The minimum guarantee will be funded by Fiscal Year 2026 Operations & Maintenance Funds. The total cumulative face value of the contract is $493,788,139.00. Performance will take place at various mission site locations throughout the Continental United States, Hawaii, Japan, and Germany and is expected to be completed by November 8, 2031. The proposal was solicited via email. The Defense Information Technology Contracting Organization, Scott AFB, IL is the contracting activity (HC1084-26-D-0004).
