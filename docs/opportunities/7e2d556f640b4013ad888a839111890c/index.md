# LP Gas and Tank Monitoring Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 16:06:35.415-04
- Solicitation Number: M6700126Q0072

## Summary

Contracting Department, MCIEAST, Camp Lejeune, NC has a requriement for the delivery of Liquefied Petroleum (LP) Gas and Tank Monitoring by use of installed tank monitors. The requirement is for a base period of twelve (12) months and one (1) twelve-month option period to be exercised at the discretion of the Government. The contractor will be responsible for providing all maintenance, monitors, and approximately 250 tanks ranging from 25 to 30,000 gallon size capacity. It is the Government’s intent to issue a single firm fixed price contract. The proposed acquisition is unrestricted. The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this requirement is 221210. The NAICS Industry Description is Liquefied Petroleum (LP) gas distribution through mains. The Government will solicit and award this contract in accordance with FAR Part 12, Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services. The Solicitation will be posted on or about fifteen (15) days after this posting and made available on the Contract Opportunities portal located at https://www.sam.gov/. This is not a request for quotes. If you are an interested party, please inform the procuring agency of your interest in the upcoming solicitation. It is the responsibility of the offeror to monitor the website for solicitation and any associated amendments. Contractors interested in doing business with the Government must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) database at https://www.sam.gov/. Contractors must also ensure annual representations and certifications are current within the SAM website and possess a Commercial and Government Entity Code (CAGE Code). Interested parties must agree to implement the Department of Defense web-based software application Wide Area Work Flow-Receipt and Acceptance (WAWF-RA). Additionally, contractors must also be registered with Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment (PIEE) at https://piee.eb.mil. The point of contact for this notice is Mrs. Tiffany Morris, Contract Specialist, at phone number (910) 451-3017 or email tiffany.morris@usmc.mil.
