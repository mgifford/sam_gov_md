- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

## Summary

Notice of intent for a sole source follow-on AE contract. The current A-E contractor was selected following the Brooks Act procedures and chosen as the most highly qualified contractor. The MATOC that the action was awrded thru has expired and changes to the program necesitate a new contract. As a logical follow-on and the very tight time-line alloted for the changes, USACE Kansas City intends to sole source the requirement to the current contractor as time does not afford us the opportunity to facilitate competition and would create unacceptable delays in fulfilling the agancy's requirements. This is not a solicitation for proposals or a list of interested parties.
