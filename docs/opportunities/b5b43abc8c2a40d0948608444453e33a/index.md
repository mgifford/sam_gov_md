## Summary

The purpose of the contract is to provide continued support to the Office of Readiness and Response (ORR), including but not limited to the Office of the Director (ORR/OD) and the Division of Emergency Operations (DEO), in expanding, enhancing, and sustaining CDC’s workforce preparedness program. Building upon the established foundation currently in place. This new contract will ensure seamless continuity of operations while further evolving the program and training infrastructure to meet the agency’s preparedness and response goals. *See attached documents for more information* Contractors are encouraged to respond with capability statements, past performance, and suggestions for contract structure and/or delivery methods.
