# DRAFT Anniston Army Depot FORGE 2 Other Transaction Authority (OTA) Request for Proposal (RPP)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-06 18:15:41.451-04
- Solicitation Number: W91QEY-26-R-A002

## Summary

Registration for Industy Day is extended to Thursday, 7 May 2026 at 11:00 CT. Effective May 4th, 2026: added industry day agenda, industry day registration attachment ANAD FORGE

2.0 Industry Day Registration, and instructions for requesting a potential prime vendor one-on-one with the Government. Special Notice: Industry and Government Technical Information Exchange / Industry Days Event –Anniston Army Depot (ANAD) FORGE

2.0 Hosted by the Mission & Installation Contracting Command (MICC) Theater Support Center (TSC) with several Army partners. Date: May 13 - 14, 2026, Time: See agenda below Note: ANAD is in the Central time zone, and all times are local to ANAD. Wednesday, May 13, 2026 0800 - Opening Remarks 0815 - Overview of the Requirement 0845 - Explanation of OTA process and acquisition timeline 0915 - Board transportation to visit sites 0930-1200 - View sites and requirements 1200 - Return to initial meeting room 1200 - Closing remarks 1215-1345 - Lunch 1345-1725 - One-on-one meetings with Potential Vendors Thursday, May 14, 2026 0800 – 1500 - One-on-one meetings with Potential Prime Vendors Location: Anniston Army Depot Registration Deadline: Noon, May 6, 2026 The Army Contracting Command - Mission & Installation Contracting Command (MICC) Theater Support Center (TSC) in conjunction with the Department of War (DoW), Army Materiel Command (AMC) G4, U.S. Army Installation Management Command (IMCOM), U.S. Army I Corps, and Anniston Army Depot (ANAD) Department of Public Works (DPW) is sponsoring an Industry and Government Technical Information Exchange day on May 13 - 14, 2026 to facilitate discussions on the potential establishment of a Section 10 USC 2808a: Other Transaction Agreement (OTA) for the development and maturation ANAD facility Modernization program to manage several key construction projects to support the warfighter into the future. This is an excellent opportunity for businesses of all sizes and socioeconomic categories—large and small—to learn about federal contracting opportunities and discover how best to engage with the federal government. Industry Days Event Highlights: Exclusive Leadership Briefings Get the inside track with detailed mission and procurement overviews from key decision-makers at the MICC TSC, AMC G4, and ANAD DPW. Spotlight on ANAD FORGE Discover the ANAD FORGE 2.0 initiative, a large-scale program focused on modernizing facilities, enhancing infrastructure, and boosting operational capabilities for the U.S. Army and Air Force at Anniston Army Depot. Mastering Federal Contracts Gain valuable knowledge on the federal procurement process. Learn the best practices and strategies to successfully navigate and win government contracts. High-Value Networking Connect directly with leadership from MICC, AMC, and ANAD. This is your opportunity to build relationships with industry peers and explore powerful new collaborations. Influence the Conversation Share your unique insights and expertise in an open forum. Provide direct feedback to our team and help shape the future of our partnerships. Who Should Attend? Entrepreneurs, business leaders, and representatives from companies of all sizes and socioeconomic backgrounds interested in federal contracting, particularly in sectors related to engineering, construction, and support services for military and civil works projects. The Government will coordinate with the vendors to schedule the meetings and do our best to accommodate the day and time requests. --------------Previous Announcement Below------------------------------------------ The Anniston Army Depot (ANAD) Military Construction (MILCON) project to design and construct buildings is to build four (4) buildings with different purposes at ANAD. The four (4) buildings resulting from this Other Transaction Authority are a Component Remanufacture Facility, a Missile Maintenance Facility, a Paint Facility, and a Storage Facility. This agreement operates under Title 10, United States Code (U.S.C.), section 2808a (Facility construction on repair: transactions other than contracts and grants) that are directly relevant to enhancing the ability of the Department of War to carry out repair and construction projects for facilities, including the planning, design, engineering, prototyping, piloting, and execution of such repair and construction projects.
