# Indo-Pacific Command OCONUS Medical Q Coded Services Enterprise Strategic Sourcing Program

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 12:01:15.671-04
- Solicitation Number: HT941026N0053

## Summary

This is a Special Notice of Intent to Consolidate contract requirements. This notice is issued for informational and planning purposes only in accordance with the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) FAR 7.107-3(b), "Notification to the public." THIS IS NOT A SOLICITATION FOR OFFERS/PROPOSALS/QUOTES AND NO CONTRACT WILL BE AWARDED FROM THIS NOTICE. No proposals or quotes are being requested or accepted with this notice. The Government is not obligated to and will not pay for any information received from potential sources as a result of this notice. Pursuant to RFO FAR 7.107-3(b)(1), the Defense Health Agency (DHA) Contracting Activity (CA), hereby provides public notification that the Senior Procurement Executive (SPE) has formally determined that the consolidation of contract requirements for a strategic sourcing acquisition program for Medical Q Coded services outside the continental United States (OCONUS), including but not limited to, Military Treatment Facilities (MTFs) in the Indo-Pacific Command/Pacific Rim Command (INDOPACOM/PAC-RIM) regions, such as MTFs in Japan and Korea. This is designed to implement a performance-based, strategic sourcing approach with respect to entering into Firm Fixed-Price (FFP), Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contracts and task orders for the services of health care professional staff.
