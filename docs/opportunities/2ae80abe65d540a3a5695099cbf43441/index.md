# DLA Troop Support Hospital Supply Tailored Logistics Support Program (TLSP)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-27 17:22:48.298-04
- Solicitation Number: SPE2DH25R0001Amendment0005

## Summary

Amendment 0005 was issued on April 27, 2026. Solicitation number SPE2DH-25-R-0001 is hereby amended as follows: The purpose of this amendment is to provide further clarifications and revise solicitation requirements. The following is a summary of the changes: 1. Attachment 5 - PEL Revisions (Amendment 0005) * Section I: Clarifications – Provides clarifications based on vendor questions regarding TAA compliance, alternate item submissions, and details the specific items removed from the Price Evaluation List (PEL). * Section II: Instructions for Price Submission –Contains updated pricing instructions and introduces the newly revised pricing spreadsheet: Attachment 1 - Price Evaluation Book (Amendment 0005).xlsx. 2. Proposal Submission Deadline (No Change): The proposal submission deadline is not extended by this amendment. The deadline remains May 5, 2026, at 3:00 P.M. EST, as previously established in Amendment 0004. 3. Revised Pricing Submission : The official pricing spreadsheet is hereby replaced with Attachment 1 - Price Evaluation Book (Amendment 0005).xlsx. The Price Evaluation List (PEL) is now reduced from 75 to 72 items, and the quantities for Line 1 have been corrected to reflect package counts. The following instructions are mandatory: * Submission Tab: Offerors shall use the worksheet tab titled "PEL AMENDMENT 0005" for all price submissions. * Reference Tabs: The original "Price Evaluation List (PEL)" and "PEL Amendment 0003" tabs are for reference only and shall not be used. * Completeness Requirement: To be considered technically acceptable, proposals must include a price for all 72 items on the "PEL AMENDMENT 0005" tab, unless an acceptable alternative item is provided. 4. Attachments: This amendment includes the following attachments, which are hereby incorporated into the solicitation: * Attachment 1 - Price Evaluation Book (Amendment 0005).xlsx * Attachment 5 - PEL Revisions (Amendment 0005).pdf 5. All other terms and conditions of the solicitation remain unchanged and in full force and effect.
