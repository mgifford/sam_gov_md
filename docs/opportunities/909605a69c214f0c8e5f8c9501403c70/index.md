# Construction water for Matsushima AB

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 03:28:17.998-04
- Solicitation Number: FA5025-26-Q-A007

## Summary

The Contractor shall deliver non-potable water for construction efforts. The contractor shall provide non-potable water delivery of 19,800 liters to the site weekly, transferring the delivered water into the Government-procured water truck tanks (2x9,900 liters), OR provide a location to refill the water truck(s).
