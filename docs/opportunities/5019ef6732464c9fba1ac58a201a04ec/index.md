# Sole Source Award to Georgia Tech Professional Education

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-27 13:41:07.098-04
- Solicitation Number: M6700426Q0045

## Summary

The Marine Corps Logistics Command Contracts Department (CONT) intends to solicit and contract with Georgia Tech Professional Education (CAGE 557R3) of Atlanta, GA on a sole-source basis for Marine Corps Logistics Command Industrial Engineering Skills. The North American Industry Classification Standard (NAICS) code assigned is 611430-Professional and Management Development Training. No solicitation will be posted on this website for this requirement. A Justification and Approval document is attached to this notice. Any potential source may contact the persons indicated above to discuss their ability to offer the required services for future acquisitions.
