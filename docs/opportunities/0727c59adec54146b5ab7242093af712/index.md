# Remanufacture of Electro-mechanical Linear Actuator

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-05-26 14:55:35.842-04
- Solicitation Number: FD20302600853

## Summary

The United States Air Force through Air Force Sustainment Center (AFSC), Tinker AFB 73145 is contemplating the Remanufacture of Electro-mechanical Linear Actuator The contractor shall provide all labor, facilities, equipment and all material to accomplish remanufacture. The work encompasses the disassembly cleaning, inspection, maintenance re-assembly, testing, and finishing actions required to return the item to a like-new condition in accordance with the solicitation requirements and all attachments. A firm fixed price IDIQ type contract is contemplated with a 5-Year Basic (no options). The requirements set forth in this notice are defined per Purchase Request FD20302600853 as follows: Written response is required.
