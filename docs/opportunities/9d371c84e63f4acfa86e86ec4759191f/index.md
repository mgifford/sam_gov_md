## Summary

WWYK190230 - REPAIR ARNOLD AVE BETWEEN H AVE AND M AVE The work to be performed under the terms of this Contract consists of furnishing all materials, labor, plant, tools, transportation, equipment, testing services, quality control and incidentals required to accomplish the repair of pavement by full depth replacement and mill & overlay of Arnold Ave between H & M Avenues at Tinker Air Force Base (AFB), Oklahoma WWYK150185 - REPAIR ROADWAYS, PARKING AT MUNITIONS AREA The work to be performed under the terms of this Contract consists of furnishing all materials, labor, plant, tools, transportation, equipment, testing services, quality control and incidentals required to accomplish the repair of the roadways and parking lots near B3812 and B3804 and expand and repair the entrance B3812 at Tinker Air Force Base (AFB), Oklahoma
