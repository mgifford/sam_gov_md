GENERAL INFORMATION Title: NESTOR CELL AUTOMATION DISPENSING UNIT - RUO Background Information: The Cellaven NESTOR system is uniquely capable of delivering a compact, fully automated, sterile cell culture solution with integrated refrigeration and programmable workflows specifically designed for sensitive iPSC cultures. These cultures require highly controlled and reproducible environments to maintain pluripotency and experimental integrity. Due to these unique characteristics and the requirement for precise, reproducible iPSC culture conditions, Cellaven is the only responsible source capable of meeting the Government’s needs.
