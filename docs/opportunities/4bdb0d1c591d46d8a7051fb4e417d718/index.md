# Parking Spaces at IRS Spokane, WA

- Agency: TREASURY, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 14:16:47.36-04
- Solicitation Number: 2032H8-26-Q-00026

## Summary

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) has a requirement for a parking facility to provide parking spaces for the Fresno IRS Territory. The contractor shall provide services of 3 parking lot/garage spaces for IRS to park and use vehicles. For information purposes, the services described shall be in accordance with the IRS Statement of Work (SOW) entitled, “Parking Spaces at IRS Spokane, WA” The RFQ (RFQ Number 2032H8-26-Q-00026) will be distributed to at least three sources. All future information about this Request for Quotation (RFQ), including solicitation amendments, will also be distributed via e-mail. Interested parties are responsible for monitoring SAM.gov or reaching out to the government point of contact to ensure that they have the most up-to-date information about this acquisition. The estimated closing date of RFQ Number 2032H8-26-Q-00026 will be at 5:00 PM on June 8th, 2026.
