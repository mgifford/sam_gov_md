# Leadership Assessment and Development

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 11:28:23.056-04
- Solicitation Number: W15P7T-28052026

## Summary

Description of Requirement Estimated Contract Value is below the Simplified Acquisition Threshold of $250,000.00. The Department of War (DoW) is conducting market research to identify potential sources capable of delivering a highly specialized, closed-loop Leadership Assessment and Development Engagement. This effort requires a vendor capable of providing a proprietary, fully integrated methodology (combining curriculum, assessment, and coaching) specifically engineered to enhance critical thinking, resilience, and adaptive leadership KSAs (Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities) within high-stress, dynamic defense environments. Core Technical Requirements: To be considered capable, interested vendors must demonstrate the ability to provide an integrated developmental system that meets all of the following stringent requirements: Requirement Area Technical Specifications & Required Capabilities 1. Proprietary Curricula for High-Stress Environments The vendor must possess and deliver original, proprietary curricula (equivalent to "Management Essentials" and "Leadership Essentials") developed entirely in-house. These curricula must be specifically designed for high-stress, dynamic environments (like those faced by military leaders) and cannot be derived from, licensed from, or based upon existing commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) training programs. 2. Proprietary Diagnostic Assessment The vendor must utilize an exclusively owned, original diagnostic instrument (equivalent to the "Management Effectiveness Assessment"). This assessment must be precision-engineered to measure the exact KSAs built by the proprietary curriculum (e.g., adaptive decision-making, sound judgment under pressure). Generic or third-party behavioral assessments (e.g., Myers-Briggs, standard 360-degree tools) are unacceptable. 3. Integrated Coaching Methodology The vendor must provide a proprietary coaching methodology that is inextricably linked to the training framework and diagnostic assessment. This cannot be a generic coaching approach; it must be architected specifically to translate the proprietary curriculum learning into sustained behavioral change through the creation of personalized Leadership Action Plans. 4. Strategic Readiness Alignment The proposed solution must be purpose-built (not adapted) to develop KSAs critical for military leadership under stress, specifically: critical thinking, resilience, adaptive decision-making, and the ability to build high-performing teams in austere environments. 5. Cohesive System Integration The vendor must demonstrate that their curriculum, assessment, and coaching methodology function as a single, fully integrated system that they own exclusively. Disaggregated solutions utilizing multiple third-party tools or vendors will not meet the requirement.
