# 4820 VALVE, LEVER

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 16:35:37.54-04
- Solicitation Number: N0040626Q0223

## Summary

Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound (FLCPS) intends to solicit for VALVE, LEVER supplies: Valve, Lever part number A49373-1MFV, Seat, Valve part number B50809MFD, and Spring, Adjusting part number A51078MFU. This requirement is sole sourced to Hunt Valve Company due to being direct replacements and owning the design, characteristics and production of the item by means of its engineering drawings D49445 and D50959-VTN. The Government will evaluate the offeror’s price for reasonableness and past performance as part of the responsibility determination in accordance with RFO 12.203. Quotes will be evaluated in accordance with RFO 12.203, the Government will select the most advantageous offer based on the following evaluation factors: technical including delivery date, price, and past performance in no order of relative importance. Anticipated Award Date: June 2026 Contracting Officer: Lorraine Small / Lorraine.m.small.civ@us.navy.mil
