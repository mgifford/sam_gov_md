## Summary

RLP 36C25026R0006 will be set-aside for Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB). Description: The Department of Veterans Affairs seeks to lease a minimum of 10,000 ABOA Square Feet (SF) of medical office space and 77 surface/outside parking spaces , inclusive of seven (7) ADA compliant parking spaces, reserved for the exclusive use by the VA as a Consolidated Medical Clinic in the delineated area explained below within Kalamazoo, Michigan. The space shall be located within the delineated area stated below. VA will consider space located in an existing building as well as a building being constructed for multi-tenant use and not specifically a build-to-suit for VA use only. ABOA is generally defined as the space remaining once common areas, lessor areas, and non-programmatic areas required by code are deducted from the rentable square feet of the facility. ABOA does not include areas such as stairs, elevators, mechanical and utility rooms, ducts, shafts, vestibules, public corridors, and public toilets required by local code. VA will provide all detailed definitions, should a solicitation for offers or request for proposals be issued for this project.
