## Summary

FL-ST. MARKS NWR, FDOT Road Base, Rip Rap, and #57 Limerock. FL DOT approved Road Base delivered and tailgate spread. Road Base will be tailgate spread. Other material will be stockpiled. See attached solicitation packet. SOW is at the end of the packet. Road Base must originate from within approximately 80 miles of the delivery location to prevent invasive species contaminants. Contractor will provide a minimum of 5 trucks per day. More than 5 dump trucks is acceptable and encouraged. No Dump trailers (semi trucks with dump trailers). Vendor is required to provide a statement that the vendor understands the standard for Florida Department of Transportation approved Road Base and the requirement to source all material from approximately 80 miles of the delivery site. A not to exceed value is provided for each line in the packet, vendor must provide the quantity that can be provided and delivered for the stated amount. See attached map and SOW at the end of the solicitation packet. Be sure to read the instructions that appear towards the end (before the SOW) of the packet. Include your CAGE code in the body of the response email. Email the above statement and pricing with your CAGE code to Robert_Sung@fws.gov with "140F0S26Q0037 - FL Gravel" in the subject line. Questions and requests for extensions may also be submitted to the same email.
