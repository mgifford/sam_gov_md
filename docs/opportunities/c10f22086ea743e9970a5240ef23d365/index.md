# RIMPAC 2026 Port-a-Potties Hand Wash Stations Rental

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 21:48:12.5-04
- Solicitation Number: N0060426Q4044

## Summary

Solicitation number N0060426Q4044 is issued as a Request-For-Quotation (RFQ) for the rental of portable toilets and hand wash stations to include consumables and maintenance in support of the USS Theodore Roosevelt (CVN71) RIMPAC 2026 exercises at Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam. The proposed acquisition action is 100% set aside for small business concerns. The Period of Performance is 23 Jun 2026 to 01 Aug 2026.
