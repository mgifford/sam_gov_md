# Amplifier for Integration into a Custom NIST-built Absolute Magnetometer

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-22 10:13:54.786-04
- Solicitation Number: 1333ND26QNB030191

## Summary

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) has a requirement at its Gaithersburg, MD campus for an amplifier that is brand-name or equal to the AE Techron 8712. This amplifier will be integrated into a custom, NIST-built Absolute Magnetometer. Please see attached Solicitation# 1333ND26QNB030191 and related attachments 1 (Statement of Work) and 2 (Applicable Provisons and Clauses) for full details.
