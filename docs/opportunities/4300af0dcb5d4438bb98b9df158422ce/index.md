# Domestic Sources for Zero-Porosity, High-Performance Coated Parachute Cloth

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-28 13:04:29.499-04
- Solicitation Number: SCIE_SOAR-COAT_Zero-Porosity_RFI

## Summary

1.0 INTRODUCTION The U.S. Army, on behalf of the Program Manager for Soldier Clothing and Individual Equipment (PdM-SCIE), is issuing this Request for Information (RFI) to conduct market research. The Government is seeking to identify domestic manufacturers capable of producing a Berry Amendment-compliant (10 U.S.C. 2533a) parachute cloth that is functionally equivalent to the material specified in U.S. Army Source Control Drawing 11-1-9306.

2.0 BACKGROUND The specified parachute cloth is a critical component for the production of ram-air parachute systems (e.g., RA-1 Canopies) supporting U.S. Army operations. The established domestic supply chain for the legacy proprietary coated material (historically known as SOAR-COAT® Formulation 9120 R3(228)) has been disrupted due to the loss of a key domestic coating capability. The Government is seeking to identify and qualify new domestic sources to ensure supply chain continuity and industrial base resilience.

3.0 TECHNICAL SUMMARY The Government requires a high-performance, zero-porosity, silicone-coated ripstop fabric. The base fabric must conform to PIA-C-44378, Type IV. The finished cloth must meet all physical, performance, and visual defect requirements outlined in Source Control Drawing 11-1-9306, including, but not limited to: Zero air permeability (0 CFM maximum per ASTM D737) Specific breaking, tear, and elongation strengths A proprietary-equivalent high-performance silicone formulation applied equally to both sides Strict tolerances for visual defects (e.g., pinholes, blisters, coating streaks, blocking) Note: The full technical requirements are detailed in the attached RFI document and Source Control Drawing 11-1-9306.

4.0 REQUESTED INFORMATION Interested parties are requested to submit a Capabilities Statement outlining their technical expertise, production capability, and quality control processes necessary to manufacture this material. Specifically, respondents should address: Company profile, CAGE code, and socioeconomic status. Confirmation of domestic manufacturing capabilities compliant with the Berry Amendment. Current availability of a Berry Amendment-compliant (10 U.S.C. 2533a) parachute cloth that is functionally equivalent to the material specified in 11-1-9306. Technical approach to developing/applying a functionally equivalent zero-porosity silicone coating. Estimated timelines for formulation development, sample production, testing, and full production readiness. Relevant past performance in manufacturing and coating technical textiles for military or aerospace applications.
