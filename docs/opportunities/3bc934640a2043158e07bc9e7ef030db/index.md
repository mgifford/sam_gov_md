## Summary

Notice ID: W912LR26QA021 Purchase: Occupational Health Medical Surveillance Examination Non-Personal Services Agency: National Guard, Puerto Rico Army National Guard Contracting Office: USPFO PR Purchasing & Contracting Division This is a direct request for quotations for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with FAR Part 12.201-1, Simplified Procedures.
