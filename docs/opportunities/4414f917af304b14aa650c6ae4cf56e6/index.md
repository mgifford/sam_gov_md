# 6515--FY26 Hemodynamic Monitoring Devices Intent to Award Sole Source Contract

## Summary

The Orlando VA Healthcare System (OVAHCS), Anesthesiology Service, requires the acquisition of five (5) HemoSphere Alta Advanced Monitoring Systems and associated accessories to support hemodynamic monitoring in the perioperative and critical care environment. These systems are purpose-built for predictive and smart hemodynamic parameter monitoring designed to detect hemodynamic instability before it occurs, enabling clinicians to proactively manage patient care and improve outcomes for Veterans. This acquisition is conducted in accordance with the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), the VA Acquisition Regulation (VAAR), and applicable VHA policies governing the procurement of medical equipment and devices. This Notice of Intent is not a request for Proposals or Quotations. No solicitation document exists, and no telephone or email inquiries will be accepted. No contract award will be made on the basis of proposals, or quotations received in response to this notice. Interested persons may identify their interest and capability to respond to the requirement and should furnish detailed data concerning their capabilities to provide services sought including pricing data and technical data sufficient enough to determine that a comparable source to the government is available. The government may consider all Expressions of Interest received prior to June 3nd, 2026, A determination by the government not to compete this proposed contract based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the government. Information received will be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement. Responses to this notice must be emailed to Stephen.Mancuso@va.gov and received by 14:00 hours EST on June 3nd, 2026. Submitters are responsible for ensuring their EOI reaches the designated contracting officer on time.
