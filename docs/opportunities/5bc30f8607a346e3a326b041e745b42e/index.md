# GROUNDS MAINTENANCE FORT STEWART/HAAF

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 12:05:40.271-04
- Solicitation Number: W9124M26RA007

## Summary

(i)This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with RFO FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number W9124M-26-R-A007 is issued as a RFP for Grounds Maintenance for Fort Stewart and Hunter Army Airfield, GA. This acquisition is set-aside for 100% small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. (ii) See Solicitation SF 1449 for a list of line-item number(s) and items, quantities, and units of measure. (iii) This is a non-personal services contract to provide grounds maintenance services for the U.S. Army Garrison Fort Stewart (FS) and Hunter Army Airfield (HAAF), Georgia. The Contractor shall inspect, service, maintain, and landscape grounds. The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items necessary to perform all grounds maintenance service tasks and frequencies as described and defined in this Performance Work Statement (PWS). (iv) Period of Performance: The base period of performance will be from 01 November 2026 to 31 August 2027. This includes a 2-month phase-in period from 01 September 2026 to 31 October 2026, followed by four (4) one-year option periods beginning each 01 September thereafter. Place of Performance: Fort Stewart (FS) and Hunter Army Airfield (HAAF), Georgia. F.O.B. Point: Destination (v) The following Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) provisions are required and apply to this acquisition IAW FAR 12.205(a) (DFARS CD 2026-O0028). (vi) The following Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) clauses are required and apply to this acquisition IAW FAR 12.205(b) (DFARS CD 2026-O0028). (vii) Offer Receipt: Proposal Due Date and Time: 05 June 2026, at 2:00 PM EST. Proposals must be submitted via email to the following Point of Contacts: Contracting Officer: Lacey Turner; lacey.m.turner2.civ@army.mil Contract Specialist: Cosmin Popa; cosmin.popa.civ@army.mil *****NOTE***** Due to a technical issue in PIEE, Offerors must disregard all PIEE submission guidance in the solicitation. Proposals shall be submitted via email to both the Contracting Officer and Contract Specialist at the email addresses provided in the solicitation. (viii) The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this acquisition is 561730, Landscaping, Lawn Maintenance, with a corresponding Small Business Size Standard of $9.5M. (viii) To receive an award resulting from this solicitation, offerors MUST be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) database. Registration may be done online at: https://sam.gov.
