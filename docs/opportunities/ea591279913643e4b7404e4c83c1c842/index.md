# Travel Management Services

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-28 03:30:24.465-04

## Summary

Please note this solicitation is not ready to be issued as of this date. The Embassy of the United States in Seoul Korea will launch the solicitation on SAM.gov when it is available, and no paper copies will be mailed. The Embassy of the United States in Seoul, Korea requires Travel Management Services. The contractor shall furnish managerial, administrative, and direct labor personnel to accomplish all travel management services as required in the contract. The anticipated performance is for one base period and 2 one-year periods at the option of the Government. The resultant contract will be an I.D.I.Q. type contract. All responsible sources may submit an offer, which will be considered. The Government may award the contract based on the initial offer without discussions. The Government is planning a pre-quotation conference and the date for this conference will be in the solicitation and plans to issue the solicitation in due course. Please refer to attached file for further details.
