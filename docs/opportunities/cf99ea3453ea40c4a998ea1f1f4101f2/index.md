# Blanket Other Transaction (OT) Solicitation with Showcase Topics

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 11:17:11.234-04
- Solicitation Number: FA6800-26-S-C001

## Summary

THIS OTA ORDERING PERIOD ENDS 16 July 2031. Every 4th quarter, Rapid Capabilities Division (formally CCIT) will share OTA topics for white paper submissions for the following calendary year showcase that will be held in May. MULTIPLE NAICS APPLICABLE! The AFGSC CCIT OTA Showcase solicitation process may include a multi-phase solicitation and evaluation approach that is dependent upon specific Showcase Topics. The topics and their problem statements are listed in Section III of this solicitation. that will be published and amended (if applicable) via the GPE at www.sam.gov. The following phases may be used: o Phase 1 – Submission of White Paper, Quad Chart and solution video o Phase 2 – Presentation/Demo (if applicable), and o Phase 3 – Request for Other Transaction Agreement 2026 Showcase Topics AFGSC seeks revolutionary or innovative solutions for the following topics. Offerors must review the detailed problem statements (available within the Valid Eval submission tool) to identify the topic most closely associated with their solution. 1. Quantum Sensing Beyond Positioning, Navigation, and Timing (PNT) 2. Deployable Charging Solution for Electrified Support Equipment and Vehicles 3. Advanced Manufacturing (AM) for Sustained Weapons, Decoys & Expendable Countermeasure Production During Wartime Operations (topic cosponsored by AF Rapid Sustainment Office) 4. Rapid Certification and Utilization Framework for Advanced Manufactured Parts (topic cosponsored by AF Rapid Sustainment Office) 5. Large Aircraft Defensive Systems (In Contested Environments) (topic cosponsored by Air Mobility Command) More details per topic can be found at https://www.smallbcc.com/2026-afgsc-showcase. Note: There is an "ASK ME ANYTHING" (AMA) session on 22 January Registration Link: https://www.eventbrite.com/e/ask-me-anything-webinars-2026-afgsc-commercialcapabilities- showcase-tickets-1758449402499 The question period runs from the release of this solicitation through 9 February 2026. ? Submit questions not addressed during the AMAs to: AFGSC.A5N.IndustryEngagement@us.af.mil. ? Answers to all questions will be posted on SAM.gov, as an amendment/attachment of this request. Future amendments to this solicitation will be for notices for those vendors selected to the next phase. Please refer to OTA Solicitation CCIT Shocase PDF for more information.
