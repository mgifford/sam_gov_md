# Weapons Systems Integration Support Services (WSISS) VI

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-15 15:40:11.939-04
- Solicitation Number: N6893626R5002

## Summary

DISCLAIMER: All notifications included herein are subject to change. The Naval Air Warfare Center Weapon Division (NAWCWD) is issuing this special notice to inform industry that it is the Government’s intention to award this requirement as a small business set-aside. Additionally, the Government intends to issue a draft Request for Proposal in the Fall whereby industry will have the opportunity to provide feedback. Furthermore, the Government is aware of the Executive Order 14402 mandates and is evaluating potential changes per this directive.
