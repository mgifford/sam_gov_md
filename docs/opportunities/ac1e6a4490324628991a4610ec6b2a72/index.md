## Summary

This is an amendment to a previously synopsized requirement for the National Park Service, The National Park Service, Pinnacles National Park to rehabilitate the west side power and cooling systems. The purpose of this Amendment no. 02 is to extend the response date, changing the date when proposals are due from 3pm PDT on May 29, 2026 to 3pm on June 19, 2026. Watch for an additional amendment which will provide additional Q&A currently being worked through. QUESTIONS MUST BE SUBMITTED IN WRITING VIA EMAIL. Submit questions to Phaedra Fuller, Contracting Officer. Email address is phaedra_fuller@ios.doi.gov. Please reference the solicitation number in the subject line of the email. The Government reserves the right to cancel this solicitation.
