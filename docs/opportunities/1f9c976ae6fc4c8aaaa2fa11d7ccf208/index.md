# AGTS Visual Augmenters

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 17:01:19.191-04
- Solicitation Number: FA481926Q0031

## Summary

The 325th Contracting Squadron at Tyndall AFB, FL, is issuing this sources sought notice seeking vendors interested, qualified, and capable of providing the following items manufactured by Meggitt Defense Systems, Inc. 1. Target Assembly (608280-1) QTY - 7 EA 2. Visual Augmenter (P/N: 219833-7) QTY - 12 EA 3. Rupture Disc (P/N: 40536-1) QTY - 4 EA 4. High Pressure Gauge (P/N: 40527-1) QTY - 4 EA 5. Connector (P/N: MS3106F8S) QTY - 4 EA The 82nd Aerial Target Squadron requires the aforementioned parts as replacement components of the AGTS Visual Augmenter, which is used in support of the 53d Weapons Evaluation Group (WEG) air-to-air Weapon System Evaluation Program at Tyndall AFB, FL. All interested parties may submit the following information in response to this presolicitation. 1. Company name 2. Company point of contact (POC) to include telephone number and email address 3. Company address 4. Business size under NAICS 336413 5. Company UEI/Cage code 6. Brief description of company capability to meet this requirement This announcement is for a presolicitation notice only and is not a Request for Quotes.
