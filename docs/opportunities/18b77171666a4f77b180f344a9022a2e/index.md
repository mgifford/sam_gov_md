# JOFOC for Solutions For Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP) V Extension

- Agency: NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-26 21:41:46.576-04
- Solicitation Number: NNG15SC00B

## Summary

This justification provides the rationale for contracting by other than full and open competition to award an extension to the Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP) V contracts. The SEWP V contract extension will be for a period of no more than 12 months (to include a 6-month extension and two 3-month options). The total IDIQ maximum value will not be increased.
