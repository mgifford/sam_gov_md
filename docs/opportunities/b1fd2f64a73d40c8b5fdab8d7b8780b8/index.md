Project Description: This is a Design-Build (DB) project for the complete design and renovation of an existing facility at Villamor Air Base, Pasay City, Philippines. The project includes, but is not limited to: ? Full renovation of a multi-level facility supporting administrative, billeting, and command-and-control (C2) functions. ? Basement renovation and expansion to accommodate additional workstations. ? Architectural upgrades, including modernization of finishes, fixtures, and interior layouts. ? Structural modifications, including reinforcement of the existing roof to support a future antenna farm. ? Mechanical and electrical system upgrades, including HVAC systems, Power distribution and lighting, Fire protection systems, and Communications and IT infrastructure. Work will be performed in an active military installation, requiring coordination with host nation stakeholders and adherence to applicable U.S. and Host Nation standards. Project Magnitude is between US$1,000,000 and US$5,000,000 (FAR

36.204 Disclosure of the magnitude of construction projects). A Pre-Proposal Site Visit (PPSV) for this project is tentatively scheduled for June ? July 2026. The site visit is to provide Offerors with an opportunity to familiarize themselves with the job sites and conditions to be encountered and is not for the purpose of answering questions. Offerors interested in the project must complete the attached Base Access Request (BAR) and provide the required information to attend the PPSV. Submit it to the site access coordinators listed below by May 14, 2026, no later than 11:00 a.m. Thailand time.
