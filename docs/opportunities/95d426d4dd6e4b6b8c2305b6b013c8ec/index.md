# VA Office Space - Jackson, MS. Metro Area

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-27 14:17:54.331-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C25626Q0700

## Summary

VA seeks to lease approximately 7,000 RSF of office space and 15 parking spaces for use by VA staff. VA will consider leased space located in an existing building. The space will be used by the Geriatrics and Extended Care, Home-Bsed Primary Care staff, which serves a crucial role in healthcare operations. The delineated area, attached, was crafted to serve as a place for providers, nurses, and administrative staff to work in office and commute to their catchment areas for patient care within the Jackson, MS. metro area. Northern Boundary: Lakeland Drive, Flowood, MS Southern Boundary: Flowood Drive (Highway 468), Flowood, MS Eastern Boundary: Flowood Drive at the intersection with Lakeland Drive, Flowood, MS Western Boundary: Lakeland Circle at the intersection with Lakeland Drive, Flowood, MS
