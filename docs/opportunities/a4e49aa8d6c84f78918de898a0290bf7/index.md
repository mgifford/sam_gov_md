# FDIC - DELL EMC - HARDWARE & SOFTWARE MAINTENANCE

- Agency: FEDERAL DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 08:54:33.198-04

## Summary

The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) invites vendors to submit a quote in response to RFQ No. CORHQ?26?Q?0200 for the FDIC Dell EMC (HM/SM) requirement. All offerors must use the provided Price Schedule template to submit pricing for all required line items and should include their most favorable discount to the Government. Any Terms and Conditions, End?User License Agreements (EULAs), or Master Service Agreements (MSAs) must be submitted with the quote for incorporation into the resulting contract. The FDIC prefers Government?approved terms. Each quote submission shall include the following components, preferably as separate documents: • Price Schedule Spreadsheet (REQUIRED) • Section K – Representations and Certifications (REQUIRED) • SCRM INFORMATION (See Section J of RFQ) • Proof of active registration in SAM.gov (REQUIRED) • Proof of partnership with Dell EMC (REQUIRED) • EULAs, MSAs, or Warranty Terms (REQUIRED) Quotes that do not include all required components listed above will not be considered for award. The selected awardee must have an active SAM.gov registration prior to contract award. Questions regarding this RFQ must be submitted no later than 2:00 PM EST on June 4, 2026. All quotes must be submitted by 2:00 PM EST on June 11, 2026. All quote packages (and/or questions to the RFQ) must be submitted directly to Harold Groves at hgroves@fdic.gov and Tammy Mattox at tmattox@fdic.gov. All communications with the FDIC shall include the solicitation number in the subject line (CORHQ-26-Q-0200) Thank you for your interest. We look forward to receiving your submission.
