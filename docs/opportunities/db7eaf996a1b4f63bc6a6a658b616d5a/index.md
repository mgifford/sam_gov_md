# Psychological Assessment Services

- Agency: COURT SERVICES AND OFFENDER SUPERVISION AGENCY
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 17:31:09.946-04

## Summary

Attached is Request for Quotation (RFQ) 9594CS26Q0015, which is the full request for the Psychological Assessment Services at the Court Services and Offender Supervision Agency (CSOSA) in Washington, DC. The requirement is for a contractor to provide evidence-based Clinical Assessments, Comprehensive Psychological Evaluations, and Comprehensive Neuropsychological Evaluations to CSOSA offenders based upon an assessed level of care or a court order for treatment. The forthcoming solicitation will be advertised as a total set-aside for small businesses under NAICS code 621330, Office of Mental Health Practitioners (Except Physicians). Additional information regarding this RFQ is located at the original Presolicitation notice 9594CS26Q0015. See attachment for RFQ and all RFQ attachments.
