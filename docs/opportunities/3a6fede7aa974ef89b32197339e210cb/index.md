# USGS EROS Utility Lawn Tractor

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 10:02:24.866-04
- Solicitation Number: 140G0126Q0159

## Summary

The Department of Interior, U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), Earth Resources Observation and Science (EROS) Center, in Sioux Falls, South Dakota is seeking firms to supply a replacement utility lawn tractor, within 120 days from the date the contractor receives the purchase order. There are no options. This competitive RFQ is being issued in accordance with Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) 12.201-1 Simplified Procedures. The anticipated award date is 06/15/2026. All responsible small business sources may submit a quotation which shall be considered by the agency. A vendor must be registered at https://www.sam.gov to be awarded the purchase order. Quotations shall be submitted to Miranda Blankenship at miranda_blankenship@ios.doi.gov no later than 1:00PM Eastern Daylight Time (ED), Wednesday, June 3, 2026. The NAICS code for this solicitation is 333112 Lawn and Garden Tractor and Home Lawn and Garden Equipment Manufacturing. The size standard is 1500 employees. All questions shall be submitted via email to miranda_blankenship@ios.doi.gov no later than 1:00PM EST, Monday, June 1, 2026.
