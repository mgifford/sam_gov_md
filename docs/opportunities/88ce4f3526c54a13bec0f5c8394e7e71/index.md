# Ozone Carts

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 13:39:18.623-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8125-26-Q-0058

## Summary

**THIS IS A PRE-SOLICITIATION NOTICE. THE OFFICIAL SOLICITATION WILL BE POSTED AT A LATER DATE** The Air Force intends to solicit the following supply: The AMXG group is requesting utilization of funds for the purchase of Ozone Carts. The requested equipment specifications are outlined below. Ozone Carts with the following requirements. a. Quantity: Three (3) Each • Shall be a Commercial off the shelf (COTS) item • Power shall be 110 VAC/15 amps • Shall provide minimum ozone concentration of 3.5 ppm • Plug shall be standard NEMA 5-15 configuration • Power cord shall be 100 ft with retractable cord wheel • Shall include on/off valve on inlet hose • Shall include on/off valve on outlet hose • Shall include input/output pressure gauge max 100 psi • Inlet hose shall satisfy the following conditions: o Shall be 5/8-inch diameter o Shall be a minimum length of 15 ft o Shall include ¾ inch female coupler for potable water service cart hookup • Outlet hose shall satisfy the following conditions: o Shall be 50 ft length and 5/8-inch diameter o Shall include downstream and upstream shutoff valve o Shall include ¾ inch female long grip coupler for aircraft hookup • Ozone Gas Output shall be between 40 gr/hr to 70 gr/hr. • Ozone Cart shall be mounted on a trailer with wheels • Supply Water shall be 3.5 – 32 gpm at 10 – 100 psi • Shall include warranty • Shall include at least 1ea. maintenance manual • Shall include training for a maximum of three (3) designated personnel on the proper use and maintenance of the three (3) ozone car A Firm Fixed Price type contract is contemplated. Estimated Period of Performance: 90 Days ARO This is a small business set-aside System for Award Management (SAM) is required to receive any award. Contractors can register electronically at https://www.sam.gov/portal/public/SAM/. The NAICS code for this requirement is 333310 with a size standard of 1,000 EMP. Interested persons may identify their interest and capability to respond to the requirement by submitting a proposal. Electronic procedures will be used for this solicitation through Beta.sam.gov at https://sam.gov/content/home. No telephone requests. See Note 22. Authority is 10 USC 2304 (c)(1). Foreign Participation: No For further information contact: Karsyn Smith Contract Specialist
