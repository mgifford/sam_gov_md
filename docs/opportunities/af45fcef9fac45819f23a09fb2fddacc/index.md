# Janitorial Services

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF

- Posted: 2026-05-12 11:29:50.985-04

## Summary

The U.S. Embassy Nassau, The Bahamas has a requurement for janitorial services in Nassau, The Bahamas. The Contractor shall perform janitorial work, including furnishing all labor, material, equipment, and services, for the U.S. Nassau, The Bahamas. The price listed below shall include all labor, materials, insurance (see FAR 52.228-4 and 52.228-5), overhead, and profit. The Government will pay the Contractor the firm fixed price per month for standard routine services and a firm fixed rate per square meter for any temporary additional services that have been satisfactorily performed. A pre-proposal conference will be held in Nassau, The Bahamas at 1:00pm on May 27, 2026. Please email NelsonDA@state.gov no later than 15:00 on May 25, 2026 if you wisn tio attend. Bids are due no later than 17:00 on June 10, 2026. No late bids will be accepted A copy of the solicitiation documents are attached. ** Non Bahamian companies are encouraged to visit www.bahamas.gov.bs to see ther requirements to operate in The Bahamas**
