## Summary

The contractor shall provide all management, transportation, tools, supplies, equipment, labor, testing and supervision to perform field investigations, vehicles and equipment necessary to perform demolition services on Joint Base Langley-Eustis (JBLE), Virginia (VA) in a manner that shall ensure continuous and reliable service. The contractor shall demolish buildings 656, 657 and 2701 on Fort Eustis, Virginia. A site visit will be held for this requirement 28 May 2026 starting at 10:00AM. Meeting location: Fort Eustis Pass/Visitor Control Office Base Access Address: 1407 Washington Blvd, Fort Eustis, VA 23604. Contractor Badging: If you need DBIDS access, please reach out to wendy.macdonald@us.af.mil and chester.terrill@us.af.mil for the base access form to be submitted. Please arrive at the pass office at least 1 hour prior to site visit start to process your visitor pass. Required ID: Ensure all attendees bring a REAL ID-compliant driver’s license or valid passport to enter the installation.
