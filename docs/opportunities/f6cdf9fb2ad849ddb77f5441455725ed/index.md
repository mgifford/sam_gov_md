# Burkart Flutes

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 12:24:17.818-04

## Summary

U.S. Navy Fleet Bands require Lillian Burkart flutes with customized headjoints. These instruments are essential to all naval ceremonial, public outreach, and diplomatic missions worldwide. Burkart flutes are the benchmark for performance quality, world-class customer support, and long operating life. They represent the best quality and value to the United States government.
