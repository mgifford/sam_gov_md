## Summary

COLONIAL NATIONAL HISTORICAL PARK - REPLACE HVAC AT JAMESTOWN HISTORICAL RESEARCH CENTER The National Park Service, Contracting Operations East, New England Major Acquisition Buying Office, issues this Request for Proposal (RFP) for a construction requirement at Colonial National Historical Park in Williamsburg, Virginia. Work will consist of furnishing all labor, supervision, materials, tools, supplies, transportation, license, certificates, permits, personal protection equipment, professional services, and equipment as required to replace the HVAC unit in the Jamestown Historic Research Center as detailed in the Project Manual/Summary of Work, included with the solicitation documents. Provisions and clauses - 05/14/2026 Attachment 1 - Project Manual/Summary of Work - 03/02/2026 Attachment 2 - DOL Construction Wage Rate Determination - 01/30/2026 Attachment 3 - Construction Contract Administration - 05/14/2026 An organized site visit has been scheduled for May 28, 2026, at 9:00 am ET. Participants will meet at 1368 Colonial National Historical Parkway, Williamsburg, VA 23081.
