# Solicitation - Presque Isle Beach Nourishment

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 16:00:47.327-04
- Solicitation Number: W912P426BA007

## Summary

THE U.S. ARMY CORPS OF ENGINEERS (USACE), BUFFALO DISTRICT, HAS A REQUIREMENT FOR CONSTRUCTION SERVICES FOR THE ONGOING BEACH NOURISHMENT OF PRESQUE ISLE STATE PARK IN ERIE, PENNSYLVANIA. THE GENERAL SCOPE OF WORK INCLUDES THE PURCHASE, DELIVERY, AND PLACEMENT OF SAND AT DESIGNATED LOCATIONS ALONG THE PENINSULA TO MITIGATE SHORELINE EROSION AND PRESERVE THE ENVIRONMENTAL AND RECREATIONAL INTEGRITY OF THE PARK.
