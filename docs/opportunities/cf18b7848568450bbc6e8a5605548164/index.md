## Summary

This notice is for a BPA Call Order to be issued against an existing Land Managementlntegrated Resources (LMIR) BlanketPurchase Agreement (BPA). To respond to this solicitation, contractors must have an existing LMIR BPA that includes Heritage Survey Services for the Medicine Bow-Routt National Forests and Thunder Basin National Grassland. Please see the attached BPA Call Order Form, Price Quote-Schedule of Items, submittal instructions, statement of work, map, and experience questionnaire.
