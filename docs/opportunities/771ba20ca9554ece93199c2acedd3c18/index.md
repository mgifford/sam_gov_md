## Summary

Solicitation number 70US0926Q70092688 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Portable Restroom and Port-a-John rental services in West Palm Beach, Florida.This acquisition is set aside for woman owned small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this solicitation is 562991 – Septic Tank and Related Services. Quote Submission Instructions: Offerors shall prepare and submit quotes in accordance with the instructions outlined in the attached RFQ. Failure to comply with these instructions may result in the quote being deemed non-responsive and eliminated from further consideration. Quotes must be clear, concise, and complete, and must address all requirements of the solicitation. The Government reserves the right toaward without discussion; therefore, offerors are encouraged to submit their best terms in their initial quote. For compliance, all quotes shall include the following: 1.Technical Quote: Shall include the specifications (including color and layout) for the proposed items. Shall include a picture of the actual portable restroom trailer at Area 2 (inside and outside); A “stock” or “catalog” image is not acceptable. Shall include a janitorial service plan. 2.Price Quote: Attachment 1- Price Sheet Question Submission: All questions regarding this solicitation shall be submitted in writing via email to the Contracting Specialist at melissa.chabot@usss.dhs.gov and the Contracting Officer Jade Gaston at jade.gaston@usss.dhs.gov no later than 11:00 AM Eastern Standard Time (EST), May 22, 2026. Responses to questions will be provided via an amendment to the solicitation. Quote Submission Deadline: Quotes shall be received in writing via email to the Contracting Specialist at melissa.chabot@usss.dhs.gov and the Contracting Officer Jade Gaston at jade.gaston@usss.dhs.gov no later than 11:00 AM Eastern Standard Time (EST), May 29, 2026. Late submissions will not be considered.
