# School Intercom / PA System

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-01 12:23:09.359-04
- Solicitation Number: FA4418SchoolIntercomPASystem

## Summary

School House Intercom / PA System Introduction: The Government is conducting market research and attaining information to identify contractors that are able to provide the following requirement. Additionally, the Government is also seeking industry feedback to identify any gaps, omissions, or recommended improvements to the Statement of Objectives (SOO) to ensure the requirement is complete, accurate, and reflective of current commercial practices. All information provided is STRICTLY VOLUNTARY. Requirement: The requirement includes the procurement, installation, configuration, and testing of a fully remote, standalone Public Address (PA) and intercom system for a three story schoolhouse facility located on the Weapon Station of Joint Base Charleston. Additionally the requirement will include the complete removal and proper disposal of any old PA/intercom equipment, including speakers, wiring, and control devices.
