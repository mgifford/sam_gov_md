# REQUEST FOR INFORMATION (RFI) - US Coast Guard Polar Security Cutter (PSC) Human Performance Support and Training (HPS&T) System Technical Support Services

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-13 15:29:12.634-04
- Solicitation Number: PSC_HPST_SVCS_RFI

## Summary

The United States Coast Guard (USCG) Human Systems Integration Division (DPR-23) seeks contractor support services to conduct analysis, design, develop, implement, evaluate, and maintain a complete training system for the Polar Security Cutter's (PSC) initial crew. This includes, but is not limited to, creating and delivering training content for various ship systems, developing performance support tools, and managing the overall training program in alignment with USCG standards, in accordance with the attached Performance Work Statement (PWS). Contractor personnel will support this requirement primarily from the Contractor's facilities, with some on-site support at Bollinger Mississippi Shipbuilding (BMS) in Pascagoula, MS, and potentially other Gulf Coast locations. Primary tasks will include the following, which are further detailed in Attachment 1: PSC HPS&T Performance Work Statement (PWS): Task 1: Develop the Training System Development Plan (TSDP) Task 2: Conduct iterative analysis and design of the PSC HPS&T System Task 3: Conduct iterative development of the PSC HPS&T System Task 4: Conduct implementation and evaluation of the PSC HPS&T System Task 5: Conduct maintenance support of the PSC HPS&T System Contractor personnel must be able to work in office and heavy industrial environments and meet all security requirements. The contemplated period of performance, including all options, is five (5) years from date of contract/order award. Attachments provide further details on requirements and deliverables.
