## Summary

MACA 258051 - Replace Seasonal Housing NPS will be awarding a firm fixed price contract for construction of seasonal employee housing at Mammoth Cave National Park in Kentucky. This project is being solicited as a TOTAL SMALL BUSINESS SET-ASIDE. Please see the attached solicitation package for further information. An on-site presolicitation meeting will be held at MACA in the near future. The date/time for that meeting will be sent out via an amendment at a later date. NPS appreciates your interest in this project. Submit all questions directly to the Contracting Officer (Tony Jasper) and Contract Specialist (John Kotchian). Email addresses are contained in Section L of the solicitation.
