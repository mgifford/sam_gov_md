# Rental Heat Exchanger

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 06:05:16.174-04
- Solicitation Number: N3904026Q3159

## Summary

Portsmouth Naval Shipyard (PNSY) is seeking to lease a steam-to-fluid heat exchanger and associated components. The system must include a steam to fluid heat exchanger; temperature, pressure and flow monitoring capability; and safety, control and isolation valves. The system must be frame/skid mounted with fork truck pockets as well as lifting pad eyes in a footprint no greater than 8 FT x 8 FT and a maximum weight of 4000 LB. Please review attached documents and return a signed completed copy of the Combined Synopsis Solicitation. Please provide any supporting technical information with the proposal. This is a solicitation for quotes and any submission does not constitute any form of legally binding contract. A purchase order will be provided to the awardee following the close of the solicitation and evaluation period. If you have any questions, please email them to william.v.leduciii.civ@us.navy.mil
