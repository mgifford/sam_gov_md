# REPLACE HVAC UNITS, BLDG 582, NAVAL AIR STATION OCEANA VIRGINIA BEACH, VA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 10:37:30.501-04
- Solicitation Number: N4008526R0172

## Summary

The Contractor shall demolish and provide select HVAC equipment in Building 582, Naval Air Station Oceana, including FCUs, ductwork, air devices, refrigerant piping and condenser units. Hard ceiling and lighting in three rooms will be demolished. Contractor shall also provide new wall mounted ductless split systems and wall mounted directional fan.
