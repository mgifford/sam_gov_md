# Law Enforcement Officer Safety Act (LEOSA)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 15:38:48.797-04
- Solicitation Number: A062074

## Summary

The scope of this agreement includes all work necessary to accomplish verification of eligibility and credentialing for all interested active duty, guard and reserve personnel. The work consists of database and website maintenance, investigative services, verification/validation services, consistent coordination with the Air Force Security Forces Center (AFSFC) and designing and printing of credentials.
