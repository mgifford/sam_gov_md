## Summary

The objective of this procurement is to obtain an annual calibration agreement for the listed equipment listed in Section 7. These calibration services will be provided by accredited test equipment calibration vendor(s) to support FCC OET Laboratory testing requirements. The Combined Synopsis/Solicitation is attached This is amendment 0001 - "The Government reserves the right to make a split award and issue multiple contracts for the items described in this solicitation. Awards may be made to the lowest responsive, responsible bidder for each individual line item, or combination of line items, whichever is more cost-effective to the Government."
