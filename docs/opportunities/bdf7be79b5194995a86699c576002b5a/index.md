# Fort Sam Houston National Cemetery, Phase 3 Expansion - Additional Road Repairs

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 12:45:03.152-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C10F26B0001

## Summary

Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Fort Sam Houston National Cemetery (FSHNC) Project Title: Phase 3 Expansion & Improvements - Additional Road Repairs Project Description: Provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision to complete the work described in the in the Statement Of Work, the Bid Items List, and construction drawings and specifications to complete the additional road repairs at the VA Fort Sam Houston National Cemetery in San Antonio, TX. This Invitations for Bids (IFB) is issued as a 100% set-aside for Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB). The project estimated magnitude for this project is between $1,000,000 and $2,000,000. NAICS Code for this procurement is 237310 – Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction (size standard of $45M) Product Service Code (PSC): Z2LB – Repair or Alteration of Highways, Roads, Streets, Bridges, and Railways In accordance with FAR 52.236-3, Interested Bidders are strongly encouraged to attend the scheduled Pre-Bid Site Visit. See Instructions to Bidders, paragraph 1.9 for detailed site visit information.
