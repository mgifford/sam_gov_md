# Survey Equipment Maintenance

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-27 12:34:45.808-04

## Summary

The Army Corps of Engineers is looking to procure a base year with 4 option years for the cleaning and Calibration of existing Survey Equipment. The survey parts being worked on are parts made by Trimble. Seiler Instrument is the only authorized dealer for Trimble’s Survey, Mapping and GIS Portfolio of products in Illinois, Indiana, Wisconsin, Kansas, Missouri, Michigan, Nebraska, Kentucky, and Iowa. If a company, not authorized by Trimble, performs the cleaning and calibration work, it will void the existing warranties on the equipment.
