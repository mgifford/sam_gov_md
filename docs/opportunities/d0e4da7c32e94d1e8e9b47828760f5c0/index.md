## Summary

NEW TIME: *Site Visit: Wednesday, 20 May 2026, 02:00 P.M. An amendment is forthcoming. The following dates supercede those listed in the SF 1442: - Site Visit: Wednesday, 20 May 2026, 10:00 A.M. - Questions Due: Friday. 22 May 2026. 4:00 P.M. - Proposal Due Date: Friday, 29 May 2026, 3:00 P.M. COMBINED SYNOPSIS/SOLICITATION NOTICE This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number FA309926Q0010 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ). This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. This is a 100% Small Business Set-Aside. The associated North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is 238330 (Flooring Contractors) with a corresponding small business size standard of $19.0 Million. REQUIREMENT DESCRIPTION The 47th Contracting Squadron at Laughlin Air Force Base, Texas, intends to award a single-award, Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) Indefinite-Delivery, Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) contract. The Contractor shall provide all labor, equipment, and materials to repair and replace flooring in up to 41 Student Pilot Housing units. Work includes the demolition of existing baseboards, subfloor preparation, and the complete installation of new Government-specified Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) flooring, vinyl wall base, transition strips, and thresholds. SITE VISIT AND BASE ACCESS Laughlin AFB is a controlled-access installation. All site visit attendees must be U.S. citizens and be able to obtain a base pass for entry. To be granted access, attendees must not have any derogatory information that would prevent access to a Federal installation. The Government will not sponsor any individual unable to meet these standard access requirements. See Schedule for further instructions.
