THIS IS A RE-SOLICITATION OF FA460026Q0024. The reason for this re-solicitation is to provide more time for site visit, quote submission, and clarification on specifications. OVERVIEW Offutt Fire and Emergency Services is seeking to purchase and have installed a non-resettable flow meter, with the ability to be calibrated, at the Fire Department Training Grounds. Additionally, the contractor shall replace four (4) valves within the propane system. Requirements: a. Non-resettable b. Totalizer c. Fits on 2 ½” Schedule 40 Pipe d. Ability to be Calibrated e. Calibration materials (if user can calibrate) f. Compatible with liquid propane g. Rated for use in hazardous areas h. Rated for continual outside use Scope of work: a. Work shall include the installation of the flow meter on the distribution line from the propane tank to the fire department aircraft training tower. b. Work shall include replacement of 4 valves withing the propane system on the distribution side. i. Includes transferring propane from storage tank ii. Includes holding propane until work is complete iii. Includes transferring propane back to storage tank once work is complete. c. All electrical hookups that are required for a fully functioning flow meter. d. Contractor shall provide all available tools, materials, equipment, permits, and necessary, but unlisted items to perform the installation of the flow meter. INSTRUCTIONS Submit Entry Authority Listing (attached) if site visit is needed by Monday, 4 May 2026 at 11:00 AM CST. Site Visit (if needed) will be Wednesday, 6 May 2026 at 09:00 AM CST. Questions for solicitation are due on or before Thursday, 7 May 2026 at 11:00 AM CST. Answers to questions will be provided by Friday, 8 May 2026 at 11:00 AM CST. Quotes will be submitted by email to TSgt Jennifer Prestridge (jennifer.prestridge.1@us.af.mil) and Daniel Kuchar (daniel.kuchar@us.af.mil).
