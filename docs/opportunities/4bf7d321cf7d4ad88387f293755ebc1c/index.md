## Summary

Applied Research Associates, Inc. (ARA) is being awarded a $111,500,000.00 single award, indefinite delivery, indefinite quantity (IDIQ) contract with the ability to issue cost plus fixed fee (CPFF) task orders for services supporting the Center for High Performance Computing, Research and Development Efforts. The IDIQ includes a five-year ordering period. ARA was also awarded a $44,566,270.29 CPFF task order concurrently for the Center for High Performance Computing, Research and Development Efforts. This task order has a base year and two option year periods.
