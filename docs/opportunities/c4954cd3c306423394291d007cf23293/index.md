# Kitchen Hoods and Ducts Cleaning

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 14:02:14.649-04
- Solicitation Number: FA449726Q0030

## Summary

The 436 Civil Engineer Squadron, Dover Air Force Base (DE) requires a contractor to provide all management, tools, supplies, equipment, and labor necessary to ensure that kitchen hoods, ducts and dryer ventilation duct cleaning services are performed in manner that will conform to all local, state and federal regulations at Dover AFB, DE, as further defined in the Performance Work Statement (PWS). 1. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in Subpart 12, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotes are being requested and a written solicitation will not be issued. 2. This solicitation is being issued as a Request for Quote (RFQ). 3. The North American Industry Size Classification System (NAICS) code associated with this requirement is 561790 and the Small Business Size Standard associated with this NAICS is $9,000,000.00. 4. The Provisions and clauses listed within this document and Attachment 3- Provisions and Clauses apply to this acquisition and can be viewed through internet access at https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul. *** This is a FY27 requirement. Funds are currently not available for this action but will be made available upon the start of the new fiscal year.*** Questions: Questions may be submitted no later than the specified date and time 12 June, 2026. Questions submitted after this date and time may not be answered. Quote Response Deadline: Quotes must be received no later than 1:00 P.M. EST on 29 June 2026.
