# SYNOPSIS: Wireless Mobile Column Lift System USMA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 15:22:10.257-04
- Solicitation Number: W911SD26QA079

## Summary

SYNOPSIS: The United States Military Academy intends to issue a Request for Quote (RFQ) for a wireless mobile column lift system for the Logistics Readiness Center. This will be a Firm Fixed Price type contract. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this work is 333921, Motor Vehicle Maintenance And Repair Shop Specialized Equipment. The Small Business Size Standard is 1,000 employees. This procurement will be competed on small business set-aside. The source selection will be evaluated on price and product specifications. The solicitation process will consist of the following: Solicitation will be issued on/or about 04 May 2026 and will be solicitated through GSA. Quotes will be due on/or about 11 May 2026. The selection process will be conducted in accordance with FAR Part 12 source selection procedures. Interested contractors must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) database. This SYNOPSIS is not a request for quotation or a solicitation of offers. Please do not respond to this presolicitation notice. If you have any questions, please contact Leslie Duron at: leslie.a.duron.civ@army.mil
