# 514 AMW Lodging Basic Purchase Agreements (BPAs)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 13:31:46.587-04
- Solicitation Number: FA448426Q0014

## Summary

This is an amendment A0001 to RFQ FA4484-26-Q-0014 to post answers to the questions. Please see attachments. This requirement is for the 514th Air Mobility Wing Lodging Blanket Purchase Agreements (BPAs) at JB MDL. The Government plans to establish multiple BPAs with hotels to provide lodging support for reserve training and drill weekends on an as-needed basis. Hotels must be located in New Jersey and within a 30-mile driving radius of Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst. Room rates must stay within the applicable DoD lodging per diem rates, unless specifically authorized. Vendors must submit a completed price list, technical information showing they can meet the lodging requirements, business information, and proof of active SAM registration. Awards will be made to responsible vendors whose quotes are technically acceptable and whose pricing is fair and reasonable.
