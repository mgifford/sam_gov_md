# Amendment 2: Questions and Answers - Whiteriver Service Unit Fire Sprinkler System Services

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 12:15:54.722-04
- Solicitation Number: IHS1524765

## Summary

The Whiteriver Service Unit (WRSU) is located on the White Mountain Apache Reservation and serves the members of the community. It consists for five buildings the hospital, Special Service Building, Cibecue Clinic, Annex Building, Garrett Building, and government quarters. The hospital, Special Service Building, Annex Building, Garrett Building, and government homes are located at and near 200 West Hospital Drive, Whiteriver, Arizona 85941. The Cibecue clinic is located in the town of Cibecue, AZ located at West Cromwell Road, 2 W Third St, Cibecue, AZ 85911. The objective of this contract is to test, inspect, repair or replace fire safety systems and fire safety building features in accordance with manufacturer’s requirements, and Joint Commission (TJC). These standards reference NFPA 101-2012, NFPA 99-2012, NFPA 72-2010, NFPA 25-2011, NFPA 96-2011 and shall be considered part of the requirements. This contract will also improve the tracking of accreditation scheduling requirements, standardize testing methods and reporting, and help ensure proper procedures are completed. The contractor shall furnish all plant, labor, materials, supervision, and transportation required to perform tests, inspections and repairs of fire safety systems and fire safety building features and to complete documentation of services as described in this contract. The contractor shall perform all work in accordance with the applicable National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) and Joint Commission documentation requirements. Work shall be performed at locations identified in this Statement of Work. SEE ATTACHMENTS FOR MORE INFORMATION AND SUBMITTAL INSTRUCTIONS.
