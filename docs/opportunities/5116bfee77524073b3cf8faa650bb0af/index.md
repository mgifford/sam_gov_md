## Summary

****Update Jan 12, 2025***** Amendments 0005 incorporates answers to the questions from ProjNet and updates the Construction Wage Rates.****Update Nov 25, 2025**** Amendment 0004 removes the requirement for PLA and extends the due date to Jan 23, 2026****Update Nov 3, 2025**** Amendment 0003 extends the due date to Dec 4, 2025 while potential changes to the Div 00 specifications are developed****Update Oct 28, 2025**** Amendment 0002 provides answers to PROJNET questions and related specs and drawings.****Update Oct23, 2025**** PROJNET questions answered and proposal due date extended to Nov6 2025 @11:00CsT******The US Army has a requirement to construct a standard design Advanced Individual Training (AIT) Battalion Complex to support 600 Soldiers. Primary facilities include standard design AIT Barracks/Company Operations Facility(ies)(B/COF) for two companies, two (2) lawn equipment buildings, and 2 physical training stations. Construction includes building tornado/storm protection measures; information systems; fire detection and sprinkler suppression systems; integrated energy monitoring and control system (EMCS) connected to base monitoring systems; intrusion detection system (IDS) and alarm systems; and antiterrorism/force protection (AT/FP) measures. Pay close attention to the Site Visit and Bidder Inquiry information located in the Solicitation. All drawings are located online in the PROJENT file by using the Plan Room Key
