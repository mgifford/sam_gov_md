# Veterans Health Administration (VHA) seeks Expression of Interest for 21,768 ABOA / 24,489 RSF of Leased Medical Space near Bothell, Snohomish and surrounding area in WA State for a Community Based Outpatient Clinic (CBOC).

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-29 14:14:43.237-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C24W26R0051

## Summary

Veterans Health Administration (VHA) seeks Expression of Interest for Leased Medical Space in the Bothell, Snohomish and surrounding area in WA State for a Community Based Outpatient Clinic (CBOC). Lease Term of 20 years, 15 Firm. Sq. Ft. 21,768 ABOA / 24,489 RSF. See attached Notice for more details. Potential Offerors must be capable of successfully performing a lease contract, including design and construction of the facility on a VHA pre-approved site, as well as all maintenance and operation requirements for the duration of the lease term. More information on VHA’s requirements can be found on its Technical Information Library: http://www.cfm.va.gov/til/.
