## Summary

This is a sources sought announcement in accordance with RFO FAR 15.101 and in anticipation of a future procurement. Southwest Regional Maintenance Center (SWRMC) is conducting market research to determine industry?s capability to provide Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) direct replacement parts and components for the USS SAVANNAH (LCS-28)?s Number One, 2, 3 and 4 Ship Services Diesel Generators (SSDG) 7,000-hours maintenance kit and Number One and 2 Main Propulsion Engines (MPDE) 8,500-hour maintenance kits. This is in direct support of the ongoing FY26 Chief of Naval Operations (CNO) Availability. See Product Description List (Enclosure (1)). The contractor shall furnish ALL materials required in the Product Description List. SWRMC is especially interested in determining small business capability and interest. SWRMC is contemplating award of a firm fixed price contract for the requirement specified in the attached Product Description List. The applicable NAICS code is 333618 ? Other Engine Equipment Manufacturing. What/Where to Submit: Interested companies should submit, on company letterhead, a letter of interest including the name, telephone number, mailing address, and email address of one (1) point of contact. Please scan your letter of interest and email it to Contract Specialists, Cindy Wong, at cindy.p.wong2.civ@us.navy.mil, and Mira Brown, at miramaje.c.brown.civ@us.navy.mil, with ?USS SAVANNAH (LCS-28) LLTM SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE? in the subject field and hand or digitally sign your letter of interest. The Letter of interest should include a brief description of your company and its ability to provide all the OEM materials no later than 31 August 2026 or best availability as set forth in Enclosure (1), and answers to the following specific questions. Please limit your response to no more than five (5) pages. 1. Is your company a small or large business as defined by the Small Business Administration (SBA) NAICS code 333618 (the small business size standard is 1,500 in number of employees)? If small business, please specify (small, 8(a), women-owned, HUBZone, veteran-owned, or service-disabled veteran-owned) [more than one category may be selected as applicable]. 2. Are you an authorized distributor of Rolls-Royce Solutions America?
