# Houston Ship Channel - Sims to Turning Basin Dredging

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 15:59:47.672-04
- Solicitation Number: W912HY26BA015

## Summary

The U. S. Army Corps of Engineers, Galveston District Office requires a contract for USACE Galveston District, FY26 Houston Ship Channel - Sims to Turning Basin Dredging Unrestricted Procurement THE WORK CONSISTS OF: Schedule 1 - The scope of this contract is to perform dredging between Sims Bayou all the way to, and including, the upper Turning Basin area of the Houston Ship Channel. Option No. 1 – Dredging of the Port of Houston Authority Docks and tire and debris removal. Additional details are available in the attached notice.
