# Pre-Solicitation Synopsis: Contract Modification OY3-OY4 Design Agent Engineering Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 14:45:14.854-04
- Solicitation Number: N00024-24-C-5510-P000XX

## Summary

20 May 2026 – Pre-Solicitation Synopsis The Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) intends to issue a solicitation to modify the previously awarded contract N00024-24-C-5510, held by Lockheed Martin (LM), 497 Electronics Pkwy, Liverpool, NY 13088, on a sole-source basis for the continued requirement of Design Agent Engineering for the SEWIP AN/SLQ-32(V)6 Electronic Warfare (EW) subsystem. The proposed contract modification increases the ceiling on Option Year (OY) 3, OY 4 Level of Effort (LOE) Cost-Plus-Fixed-Fee (CPFF), and Cost-only line items. The proposed contract action is for Design Agent Engineering, for which the Government intends to solicit and negotiate with only one source under authority of FAR 6.302-1(a)(2)(ii)(B). LM is the only vendor qualified and capable of meeting the Navy’s OY3 and OY4 schedule requirements to provide continued, uninterrupted Design Agent Engineering for SEWIP Block 2 EW subsystems. Parties interested in subcontracting opportunities should contact LM directly. This notice of intent is not a request for competitive proposals. However, all responses received within 15 days after date of publication of this synopsis will be considered by the Government. A determination by the Government not to compete this proposed contract action based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. DISCLAIMER: This pre-solicitation notice is for informational only and shall not be construed as a commitment by the Government to solicit contractual offers or award contracts. The Government is not soliciting, nor will it accept, proposals as a result of this synopsis. The Government will not reimburse respondents for any questions submitted or information provided as a result of this notice. This is not a solicitation or a promise to issue a solicitation, and this information is subject to modification and in no way binds the Government to award a contract.
