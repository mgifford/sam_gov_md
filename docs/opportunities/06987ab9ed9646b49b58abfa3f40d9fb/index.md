# Travis AFB CA - 60 OSS Faraday Tents

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 13:29:16.459-04
- Solicitation Number: FA442726Q1074

## Summary

The 60th Contracting Squadron at Travis AFB, CA, is issuing this Request for Quotation (RFQ) for the procurement of two (2) portable, RF/EMI shielded Faraday tents to support the 60th Operations Support Squadron (OSS). The required tents must measure 7'x6'x6' internally, feature an 8'x7'x6.5' external metal frame, provide >85dB shielding attenuation (400MHz to 11GHz). Requirement must be in accordance with the Statement of Work. This is a Total Small Business Set-Aside requirement. Any questions or clarification shall be routed to both Point of Contacts (POCs) listed no later than 26 May 2026 4:30 PM PST. Any inquiry submitted past the deadline will not be accepted. Offerors shall provide quotes via email to both Point of Contacts (POCs) listed no later than 01 June 2026 at 12:00 PM PST. kirk.matthew.7@us.af.mil and juliet.diggs.1@us.af.mil.
