# Investigate Hydraulic Fluid leak on Elevator in B663

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 14:48:26.192-04
- Solicitation Number: FA301626Q0102

## Summary

This requirement investigate Hydraulic Fluid leak on Elevator in Bld.663 at Randolph Air Force Base. The contractor shall furnish all supervision; labor, transportation materials, tools, incidentals, equipment, and quality assurance for investigation of the hydraulic jack leak and associated parts to get the full scope of the leak and remediate the affected area located at B663 JBSA-Randolph San Antonio TX.
