## Summary

SOLICITATION FOR THE ACQUISITION OF BRAND NAME CBP MSC-LITE TECH REFRESH IN SUPPORT OF CBP DETAILED IN THIS ANNOUNCEMENT FOR THE FAA. NO PHONE CALLS PLEASE. ALL QUESTIONS MUST BE IN WRITING AND EMAILED TO THE CONTRACTING OFFICER. CONTACT INFORMATION BELOW. This is a solicitation for the acquisition of the items listed in the attached solicitation titled “16 SIR_6973GH-26-R-00123_CBP MSC-LITE TECH REFRESH” with the pricing listed on page 3 of the solicitation which must be completed and included with the signed solicitation (SIR) for the response to considered complete, in addition to ALL highlighted areas within the solicitation.
