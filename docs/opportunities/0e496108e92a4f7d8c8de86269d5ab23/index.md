STATEMENT OF NEED (SON) Monopolar Dual-Frequency Ophthalmic System for Dry Eyes DESCRIPTION: The Optometry Department of Coatesville VA Medical Center (VAMC) is requesting a new ophthalmic Monopolar Dual Frequency RF device capable of treating dry eye syndrome, meibomian gland disease, blepharitis, Acne Rosacea, and associated skin conditions surrounding the eyes using dual frequency Radio Frequency (RF) technology to deliver heat across different tissue layers, increasing blood circulation, to promote improved tissue function. Monopolar is required because it mitigates the risk of electrical arcing. It also provides more rapid and even heating of deeper eyelid tissues. Dual frequency RF technology is required due to it not being associated skin depigmentation and is not contraindicated for use with any population. Dual frequency RF equipment will allow all of Coatesville s veterans who require more aggressive management of their dry eyes to be treated. Treatments provide temporary reduction in the appearance of cellulite, heating for the purpose of elevating tissue temperature and increasing local circulation. SCOPE: The contractor shall provide a new FDA approved Monopolar Dual Frequency RF device capable of treating all skin tones for dry eye syndrome, meibomian gland disease, blepharitis, Acne Rosacea, and associated skin conditions surrounding the eyes. The device shall meet the minimum salient characteristics: The Lumenis OptiPLUS RF System is a Name Brand Product provided for reference of salient characteristics only.
