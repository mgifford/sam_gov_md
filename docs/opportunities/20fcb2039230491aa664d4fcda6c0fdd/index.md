# Absorber Generator Tubes Replacement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-27 11:15:34.649-04

## Summary

W912EE The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers - Vicksburg District (USACE-MVK), in accordance with Revolutionary Far Overhaul (RFO) Part 12 - Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services, intends to award a sole source purchase order to Terry Service (CAGE Code: 0S6W0) to make repairs and perform maintenance on the aborsption chiller on Power Barge 5014, repairs and maintenance include the removal and replacement of all tubes in the generator section of the machine. Refer to the attached statement of work for complete requirement details. This is not a Request for Quote (RFQ), and no solicitation is available for this procurement. Nevertheless, any firm or individual that believes it is capable of meeting the Vicksburg District’s requirements described herein may submit a capabilities statement, which, if received by 12:00 PM CST on 29 April 2026, will be considered. Responses to this posting must be submitted via e-mail, no phone calls will be accepted. A determination not to compete the described purchase order based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Federal Government. Any questions or comments should be directed to Genna Wooten, via email at Genna.W.Wooten@usace.army.mil, and Dustin Cannada, via email at Dustin.G.Cannada@usace.army.mil.
