# National Defense University Catering Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 10:28:09.457-04
- Solicitation Number: N0018926QD087

## Summary

National Defense University (NDU) located in Washington, D.C. requires the procurement of catering services in support of International Student Management Office (ISMO) Spring and Fall Graduate Reception hosted by the National Defense University (NDU). The Contractor shall provide all personnel, materials, equipment, tools, supervision, and services necessary to plan, support, and execute formal standing receptions at Fort McNair, Marshall Hall Atrium (Building 62), or an alternative location provided by the university.
