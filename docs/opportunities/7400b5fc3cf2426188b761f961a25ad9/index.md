# Electrical Subpanel Replacement

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-22 14:30:40.768-04
- Solicitation Number: 19PE5026R0001

## Summary

This contract is to provide all labor, materials, equipment, engineering, quality control, safety oversight, and supervision necessary to replace the existing electrical subpanel at the INL warehouse (Air Wing #2) located at the FAP Base–Callao, Lima, Peru. The Contractor shall deliver a safe, code-compliant, fully commissioned system with complete documentation and minimal operational disruption.
