National Cemetery Administration Morovis National Cemetery Phase 1B Project Scope In accordance with the master plan for the entire 247.5-acre site, Puerto Rico National Cemetery, Morovis Phase 1 developed approximately 50 acres at the Morovis National Cemetery. The purpose of this Phase 1 project at Morovis National Cemetery was to enable an existing national cemetery to continue to provide burial services for eligible veterans in Puerto Rico. The new Morovis National Cemetery Phase 1A construction provides approximately 10 years of burial capacity (14,050 gravesites), including casket, columbarium, and in-ground cremation sites; two committal shelters; a Public Information Center/Administration building; a Maintenance/Honor Guard building; a Flag Assembly Area; and supporting infrastructure including a main entrance and signage, interior roadways and parking areas, site improvements, utilities, irrigation, and landscaping. Phase 1B project goal is to complete the Phase 1 project by completing new cemetery infrastructure to incorporate: As-Built Survey: An as-built survey of fencing alignment shown in Phase 1A. In addition, an as-built survey of all retention ponds. Landscaping: Landscaping around the ponds. Irrigation System: Complete the irrigation system design, including portions that were removed from the previous phase. Stormwater Pond Liner: Removal and replacement of 9 existing pond liners. New pond liners will have concrete bottoms with a gas venting system. Select roadways, curbs, gutters, and guardrails will be removed and reinstalled to accomplish this work. Pond Fencing: Chain-link fencing around all ponds as a safety barrier, with the exception of Pond 8, which will have ornamental fencing. Perimeter Fencing: Barbed wire fencing will be installed around the perimeter of this cemetery according to the previous fence alignment provided and a new survey performed. A vehicle access gate will be installed at the southwest corner of the site for access to State Road PR-155. Construction Phasing: The project will be divided into 3 phases. A phasing plan to complete the construction activities of Phase 1B will be provided. French Drain: Develop a French drain, 32 meters long, near the entrance of the site. Committal Shelter: Committal Shelter 1 pavers will be removed and reinstalled. Temporary Committal Shelter: The temporary Committal Shelter will be relocated to the maintenance yard at the completion of construction. Construction Trailers: Construction trailers and laydown yard location will be shown, including restoration after construction completion. OPTION: POW/MIA Flag Assembly: Flag assembly to house the POW/MIA flag with concrete slabs, landscaping, and ornamental fencing. There will be a two-year construction duration, with the midpoint set to June 2027. The project will be divided into three phases.
