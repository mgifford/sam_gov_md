# R--SWAN - GWA Marine Nearshore Monitoring

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-28 19:38:36.768-04
- Solicitation Number: 140P9726Q0015

## Summary

The National Park Service, Southwest Alaska Network (SWAN), intends to award a purchase order to James L. Bodkin for continued Marine Nearshore Monitoring in support of the Gulf Watch Alaska (GWA) and SWAN long-term monitoring programs at Katmai National Park & Preserve. This acquisition is noncompetitive because market research identified only one responsible source with the required long-term experience, site-specific expertise, and continuity with established monitoring protocols necessary to perform the work. This notice is issued in accordance with the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 5 publicizing requirements. This is not a solicitation.
