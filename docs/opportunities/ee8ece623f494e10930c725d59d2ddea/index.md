# Hydrostatic / Ultrasonic Testing of Nitrogen Cylinders

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 15:55:57.098-04
- Solicitation Number: W911N226QA044

## Summary

Solicitation number W911N226QA044 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ) for Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract with a five (5) year ordering period to perform Department of Transportation (DOT) and International Standard Organization (ISO) recertification services by testing empty high pressure Nitrogen storage cylinders at Letterkenny Army Depot. The procurement will be 100% small business set aside. The 2022 North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code is 541380 - Testing Laboratories and Services, the size standard is $19.0 mil, and the product service code is H242 - Equip/Materials Testing- Fire Fighting/Rescue/Safety Equipment; Environ Protect Equipment/Matls. This will be Freight On Board (FOB) destination to Letterkenny Army Depot. All interested sources may submit a quote, which shall be considered by this agency. It is noted all quoters must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) Database to receive a Government contract award. Quoters may register for SAM at: https://www.sam.gov. Quotes shall be provided no later than 5 June 2026 at 1200 Eastern Time via email to the Contract Specialist Megan Smith at megan.m.smith110.civ@army.mil and Contracting Officer Bobie Burkett at bobie.j.burkett.civ@army.mil. This combined synopsis/solicitation by no means binds the Government and is for information purposes only.
