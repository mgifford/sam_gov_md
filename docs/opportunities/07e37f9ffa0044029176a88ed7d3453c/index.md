## Summary

The Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division (NSWC IHD) Contracting Office is seeking companies with the technical expertise, experience, capability, and resources to provide comprehensive Industrial Facility Support Services (FSS) at Indian Head, Maryland. The scope of work includes the sustainment, restoration, and maintenance of energetic manufacturing facilities.
