# Hill AFB Actuator Phase 2A

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 17:06:57.168-04
- Solicitation Number: SPRHA1-26-R-7002

## Summary

Solicitation Document for Parker 2A. The Enterprise Hill Commodity Council (EHCC) and Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) Weapons Support, in coordination with the 409th, 411th, 416th, 417th, 421st, 422nd, and 423rd Supply Chain Management Squadrons (SCMS), are developing an outcome-based support strategy for actuator assemblies that are proprietary to Parker Hannifin Corporation (Parker). The goal of this effort is to utilize the existing operational framework established from the previous Actuators Performance Based Logistics (PBL) effort established in contract SPRHA1-22-D-0001 and expand the portfolio of PBL supported sole source National Item Identification Numbers (NIINs) associated with actuator assemblies sole source to Parker referred to as Phase 2A.
