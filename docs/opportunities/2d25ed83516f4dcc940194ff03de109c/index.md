# FAIRBANKS MORSE, LLC METAL HOSE

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 11:24:32.301-04
- Solicitation Number: N0040626Q0254

## Summary

Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound (FLCPS) intends to solicit for Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) Fairbanks Morse, LLC METAL HOSE FOR COMPRESSED AIR, JET ASSIST (PN M12700182). SOLE SOURCE STATEMENT: This acquisition is a sole source requirement to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) Fairbanks Morse, LLC IAW Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO)

12.102 Restricting competition. The sole source is the only responsible source based on the requested parts designation as ship critical safety items in accordance with (IAW) COMSCINST 4490.1C QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS FOR ITEMS CRITICAL TO SAFETY ON VESSELS ASSIGNED TO MILITARY SEALIFT COMMAND, which restricts the US Navy to procurement from only the OEM and authorized sources for parts and systems directly related to critical equipment. The requested parts directly support the Main Diesel Engines onboard the vessel. All responsible sources may submit a quote or offer prior to the solicitation closing date. In accordance with RFO 12.203(a), the Government will select the most advantageous offer based on the following evaluation factors in no relative order of importance: RFO 12.203(a)(1) Technical including delivery date RFO 12.203(a)(2) Past performance RFO 12.203(a)(3) Price Anticipated Award Date: June 2026
