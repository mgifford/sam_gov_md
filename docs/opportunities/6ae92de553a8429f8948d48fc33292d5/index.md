## Summary

This is a Request for Information (RFI) only, and is being issued for the sole purpose of conducting market research and to determine a viable path for solicitation. Please see the two (2) attachments for full details and instructions. The mission of the VA Electronic Health Record Modernization Integration Office (EHRM-IO) is to transform how the VA delivers health care by implementing an enterprise-wide electronic health record (EHR) to standardize care across facilities, strengthen interoperability and collaboration with federal and community partners, foster innovation, improve provider and Veteran experiences, and ensure the continued delivery of high-quality, safe, and efficient health care to our nation’s Veterans. EHRM-IO is in the process of migrating VA’s Medical Centers (VAMCs) from the Veterans Information Systems and Technology Architecture (VistA) to the Oracle Health (OH) Millennium EHR.Many of these VAMCs operate a blood bank and/or perform transfusion services and will be using the Oracle Health Pathnet Blood Bank system after go-live. Validation Services are required by regulatory and accreditation organizations (VA, Food and Drug Administration (FDA), Association of Advancement of Blood and Biotherapies (AABB), Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA) and The Joint Commission (TJC)) to ensure patient and blood products safety. Compliance with the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Regulations requires that these environments are validated prior to go-live. EHRM-IO seeks a contractor to provide blood bank validation services at each site with Full Blood Bank/Transfusion Medicine Services and those with transfusion only services to validate the build and configuration of the system, both for the local site build and enterprise review of all orderables, interpretation patterns, blood products, instrumentation connectivity and patient product compatibility.
