## Summary

This is a sources sought notice prepared in accordance with the solicitation format in FAR Part 12, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. The Division of Acquisition Policy (DAP) at the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), Indian Health Service (IHS) wants to identify businesses that can provide the IHS Office of Clinical and Preventive Services, Division of Clinical and Community Services, with services outlined in section (vi) below. (ii) The solicitation number 75H70426R00008 is being issued as a Sources Sought Notice. (iii) A statement that the solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular 2026-01. (iv) Pending sufficient industry responses, the Government intends to issue a combined synopsis/solicitation RFP following the closure of this sources sought notice. Pending insufficient industry response, a Sole Source award with a previously identified vendor may be pursued. The NAICS code for the requirement is 561990, All Other Support Services, with a small business size standard of $16.5M. (vi) IHS seeks professional assistance with the following supply type requirement: Enterprise-Wide Online Evidence-Based Clinical Decision Support Resource *Associated Statement of Work is attached. *Please ignore the specification of Rockville, MD as the Place of Performance. Any location within the USA is acceptable (work will primarily be performed at the contractor’s location). SAM now requires a selection for the City and State. (vii) The Period of Performance will start from the Date of Award. Anticipated award is for a BASE + 4 Option Periods (annual). The work is to be performed from the Contractor site.
