# Nikon instruments (optical and fluorescence microscopy)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-15 12:26:03.528-04
- Solicitation Number: N00173-26-Q-1301367818

## Summary

Requested items are for the upgrade of two existing upright microscopes that are failing. Upgrading these microscopes will extend their lifespan and enable their use to investigate adhesive contacts using optical and fluorescence microscopy, as is required by the funded program to explore the reactivity of enzymes under confinement.
