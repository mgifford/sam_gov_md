## Summary

AMENDMENT 1 The purpose of this amendment is to add a site visit to the solicitation, revise questions and quote response dates. A Site Visit will be conducted on Tuesday 12 May at 10:00AM PST Travis AFB, CA. Please meet at 60 Contracting Squadron (Building 549). If you need a Travis Air Force Base pass, provide the visitor’s first and last name via email to the Contract Specialist by Monday 11 2022 at 11:00 AM PST. Passes are available at the Visitor’s Control Center outside the main gate. Depending on volume, obtaining a base pass can take 30-45 minutes or more, so plan to arrive at the Visitor Control Center outside the main gate early. All VISITORS MUST HAVE A REAL ID OR PASSPORT TO BE GRANTED ACCESS TO THE BASE. 60 AES needs a painting and repair service for a C-130 Trainer. The objective of this requirement is to repaint the exterior of the C-130 FUT, replacing the markings of the Delaware National Guard with the 60th Aeromedical Evacuation Squadrons markings. This project ensures proper environmental requirements are met and accurately denotes the unit responsible for the Fuselage Trainer (FuT). In accordance with the Statement of Work (SOW). All questions or clarification shall be routed to both point of contacts (POC) listed no later than 14 May 2026 1:30PM PST. any inquiry submitted past the deadline will be not accepted. Quotes shall be provided to both POC no later than 20 May 2026 at 12:00PM PST, hunter.losen@us.af.mil and juliet.diggs.1@us.af.mil.
