# Inert Warhead for Reduced Range Practice Rocket (RRPR) FY26-28

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 15:25:11.83-04
- Solicitation Number: W31P4Q-26-R-0013

## Summary

The Army Contracting Command – Redstone Arsenal issues this solicitation number W31P4Q-26-R-0013 to fulfill the requirement for Inert Warheads, Part Number 13540611, for the Reduced Range Practice Rocket, M28A2. The anticipated resultant contract will be Firm-Fixed Price with a minimum quantity of 2,000 warheads and a maximum quantity of 7,000 warheads for each year, one base year and two option years. This solicitation is a 100% Small Business Set-Aside. Offers are not solicited from other than small business concerns. By U.S. law, participation in small business set-asides are restricted to U.S. small businesses, therefore foreign requests for this solicitation will not be honored. Refer to attachments for additional information.
