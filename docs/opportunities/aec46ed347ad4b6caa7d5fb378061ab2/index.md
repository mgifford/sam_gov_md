# Rolling Door Installation at Lake Washington Ship Canal

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 14:52:30.001-04
- Solicitation Number: W912DW26QA040

## Summary

This is a non-personal professional service to provide materials and install new roll up garage doors and equip current doors with mechanically operated functionality at Lake Washington Ship Canal at Seattle, WA. The Government shall not exercise any direct supervision over the contract service providers performing the services herein. Work will include the removal of some doors to be replaced by those of similar size, that will not require alterations to the existing exterior openings. Work will also consist of upgrading manually operated doors to mechanically operated with actuators. The installation of these doors, or upgrading to allow powered operation, will require minor alteration work on interior jams to accommodate new hardware. Upgrades will include replacement of torsion springs and tracks as required, and the addition of the operating hardware, which includes mounts for motors and safety features.Site VisitsDate: 11 May 2026 Time: 10:00Request for Site visit due by 6 May 2026 by 14:00.Please send RSVP request to Charles.d.idle@usace.army.mil if interested in attending.
