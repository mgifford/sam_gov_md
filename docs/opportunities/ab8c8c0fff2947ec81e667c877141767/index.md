# Davidson River Campground Brevard Sewer Project

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-06 10:57:56.154-04
- Solicitation Number: 12445226Q0005
- Awardee: WRIGHT BROS LLC Andalusia AL 36421 USA
- Award Amount: 5015701.09

## Summary

This project will replace the existing Schenck Job Corps pump station and existing force main to connect The Davidson River Campground and Pisgah Ranger District facilities north of US 276 to the City of Brevard Wastewater Collection System. This infrastructure is failing and incurring significant sewer surcharges to the Forest Service as well as contributing to Sanitary Sewer Overflows.
