# Agentic AI Platform

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-28 09:28:36.713-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8604AgenticAI

## Summary

Notice of Intent to Sole Source This is a notice of intent to sole source a procurement under RFO subpart 19.106-3 Sole Source Awards, Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB). HQ AFMC/A4N has a new requirement for an enterprise-grade, cloud-hosted platform that provides shared infrastructure, standardized tooling, governance frameworks, and managed services enabling rapid development and deployment of autonomous and semi-autonomous AI agents. Due to the highly technical nature of the AFMC/A4 logistics IT domain, it requires specialized Contractor support with technical and engineering skillsets aligned to Air Force environments to successfully enable future state transformational objectives. ACC3 International Incorporated will be awarded this effort. The anticipated NAICS Code is 541519 – Other Computer Related Services. The PZIT-A Contracting Office at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in Dayton, OH intends to award a sole source contract to ACC3 International Incorporated, 8996 Miramar Road STE 207, San Diego, CA, 92126 in accordance with RFO 19.106-3 Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB). This notice of intent is NOT a request for competitive quotes; however, all responsible sources may submit a capability statement or quotation which shall be considered by the agency. Capability statements, if received timely, will be considered; however, a determination by the Government not to compete the requirement based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. Information received will normally be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct competitive procurement. Responses shall be emailed to Isabel Pappaterra at isabel.pappaterra@us.af.mil and Shannon Scott at shannon.scott.4@us.af.mil no later than 12 June 2026, 0800 am Eastern Standard Time. Late responses will not be considered.
