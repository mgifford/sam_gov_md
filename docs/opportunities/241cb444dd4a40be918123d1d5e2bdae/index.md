## Summary

SOURCES SOUGHT SYNOPSIS - FURNITURE KIT BOM S-SERV, INSTALL KIT-SERV, STORAGE The Defense Logistics Agency Maritime Puget Sound is issuing this sources sought and request for information as a means of conducting market research to identify parties having an interest in and the resources to support a material requirement in Bremerton, WA. FURNITURE KIT – QTY 1 COMPLETE BILL OF MATERIALS (BOM) FROM (2) MFRS; MFR: SWIFTSPACE WORKSTATIONS MODEL: SHIFT; MFR: SCS WORLDWIDE MODEL: GRAFIA STORAGE COMPONENTS. FREIGHT, DELIVERY, STORAGE FOR UP TO (6) MONTHS, EXTRA HANDLING PER THE BOM AND SOW KIT MATERIAL SUMMARY (105) 48 SF WORKSTATIONS: MFR. SWIFTSPACE MODEL SHIFT: OPTIONS: L-SHAPE HEIGHT-ADJUSTABLE WORKSURFACE PLUS ADDITIONAL ACCESSORIES. (105) STORAGE TOWERS WARD/FILE/FILE (105) CUSHION BOX/FILE MOBILE PEDESTAL. ELECTRICAL: INCLUDES ALL INTERNAL FURNITURE POWER COMPONENTS, POWER POLES, BASE FEEDS, OUTLETS, POWER STRIPS ETC. (SHALL BE NRTL CERTIFIED). P/N: 64860-26-B81SWIFTEX MFG: SWIFTSPACE WORKSTATION MFG: SCS-ISS WORLDWIDE 2. INSTALL KIT SERVICE – QTY 1 INSTALL THE COMPLETE BOM (KIT # 64860-26-B81SWIFTEX) WHEN OFFICE IS READY. RECEIVE, DELIVER, ASSEMBLE, INSTALL, TEST ALL WORKSTATIONS PER SOW. CONTACT 900F POC FOR STATUS AND TO COORDINATE DELIVERY. P/N: SERVICE MFG: 3. STORAGE SERVICE – QTY 1 PROVIDE OFF SITE STORAGE FOR COMPLETE BOM (KIT # 64860-26-B81SWIFTEX) UNTIL GOVERNMENT OFFICE IS READY TO BE OUTFITTED WITH FURNITURE PER THE SOW. CONTACT 900F POC FOR STATUS AND TO COORDINATE INSTALLATION DATE. P/N; SERVICE MFG: The results of this market research will contribute to determining the method of procurement.
