## Summary

The requested equipment item descriptions/specifications are outlined below. a.Towable-Dual Height 757/767 Wheel Well Stands b.Quantity: Four (4) each c.Adjustable Top deck size: 24"-36" wide and 20"-24" deep. d.Top deck adjusts manually from 60" ± 5” to 80" ± 5" at a maximum without hydraulic equipment. e.Total Footprint less than or equal to 120" long x 60" wide. f.Skydrol resistant coating. g.Anti-Slip Stair Tread h.Collapsible handrails along the sides of the stand. Railing or chain on the forward end of top step. Swivel casters. Breaks. i.Removable tow bar on rear side. j.Contractor responsible for offloading equipment k.Include Operations and Maintenance (O&M) manual for the stand l.OSHA Compliant. m.Part will be delivered fully assembled.
