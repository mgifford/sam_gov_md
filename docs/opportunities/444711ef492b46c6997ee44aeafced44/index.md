## Summary

The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) Office of Administration (ADM) requires a sole source bridge contract with OCT Consulting LLC to provide contract administration and contract closeout support services. This justification supports the issuance of a sole source six-month bridge contract to OCT Consulting LLC.
