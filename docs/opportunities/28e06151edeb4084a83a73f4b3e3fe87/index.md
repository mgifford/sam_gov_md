# Cooler, Lubricating

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 14:04:53.379-04
- Solicitation Number: SPRDL1-25-R-0192

## Summary

This requirement is for a heat exchanger specifically designed to cool the lubricating oil in an engine or machinery. Its primary function is to dissipate heat generated during the operation of the engine or machinery, preventing the lubricating oil from reaching excessively high temperatures. This is used in Recovery Vehicles M51/M74/M88. A complete Technical Data Package (TDP) of the engine oil cooler has not been developed for competitive use. The performance specifications, manufacturing knowledge, and/or TDP needed to develop and manufacture the engine oil cooler are proprietary to Renk America, LLC (CAGE 02978) and Tecmotiv USA, Inc. (Cage 0L7R7).
