- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

## Summary

The Mission and Installation Contracting Command Installation Contracting Office, West Point, NY (MICC-West Point) is issuing this SYNOPSIS Black Parka Coats at West Point, NY. The estimated delivery will be for March 2027. This requirement will be a 100% set aside for total Small Business concerns. The NAICS code 315210 Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors, size standard is 750 Employees. Please verify that both the UEI #, CAGE Code, and System for Award Management (SAM) profile are current and valid in SAM. Offerors are reminded to carefully review all attached solicitation documents issued as criteria is stated in the solicitation and shall govern. The solicitation will be issued on or about the week of May 11, 2026, in electronic format only and will be available on the internet at: https://www.sam. In order to be considered samples are required, it is the offeror’s responsibility to check the https://www.sam/ website provided as necessary for details, instruction, and any posted changes. Quotes and additional supporting documentations must be submitted via email to Ignacio J. Cordova, ignacio.j.cordova2.civ@army.mil on or before the close date noted in the solicitation.
