# MOF FR86 Base Repair and Resurfacing

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 09:53:43.417-04
- Solicitation Number: 12445526Q0061

## Summary

Solicitation Amendment 0001, Issued 20 May 2026, implements the following changes: 1) Quotation due date and time is extended to 1700 EDT on 29 May 2026 2) Schedule of Items is updated with revised quantities 3) Project Plan Set is incorporated into the Solicitation as Attachment 10 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12445526Q0061 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for MOF FR86 Base Repair and Resurfacing. PROJECT SUMMARY: The USFS is pursuing a Purchase Order to provide road repair construction services on Forest Road 86, on the Monongahela National Forest. Work includes but is not limited to base failure repair, pavement failure repair, and resurfacing. Services shall be performed by the awardee in accordance with the specifications, terms, and conditions of this solicitation, including those found in the solicitation attachments. SET-ASIDE: This solicitation is set aside 100% for Small Business entities under NAICS 237310 - Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction (Size Standard: $45M). PROJECT LOCATION: Monongahela National Forest, Forest Road 86. See solicitation and its attachments for additional location details. PROJECT MAGNITUDE: between $1,000,000 and $5,000,000 SOLICITATION POINT OF CONTACT: Matthew (Matt) Killian, Contracting Officer matthew.killian@usda.gov PLEASE REVIEW ALL SOLICITATION MATERIALS IN-FULL, INCLUDING ALL INSTRUCTIONS TO OFFERORS, PRIOR TO SUBMITTING A QUOTATION. LATE QUOTATIONS AND/OR QUOTATIONS THAT DO NOT COMPLY WITH SAID INSTRUCTIONS MAY BE DETERMINED NON-REPONSIVE AND ELIMINATED FROM AWARD CONSIDERATION.
