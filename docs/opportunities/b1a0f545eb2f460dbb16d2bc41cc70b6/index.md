Project Initiation and Planning Site Survey and Assessment Conduct a comprehensive site survey for each AHU listed. Assess the current condition of each AHU, including mechanical, electrical, and control components. Identify deficiencies and areas requiring refurbishment or replacement. Project Kick-off Meeting Organize a kick-off meeting with all stakeholders to discuss project scope, timelines, and deliverables. Designate roles and responsibilities. Documentation and Approvals Prepare and submit detailed project plans, including risk assessments and health and safety plans. Obtain necessary approvals and permits from relevant authorities. All placement & relocation of coils and humidifier on AHU43 shall be coordinated with the Contracting Officers Representative (COR) prior to start of any work to ensure proper clearances. Preparation and Mobilization Logistics and Site Preparation Arrange for the delivery of necessary tools, equipment, and materials to the site. Set up a secure and organized staging area for storage and preparation of materials. Shutdown and Isolation Schedule and coordinate the shutdown and isolation of each AHU with minimal disruption to facility operations. Implement lockout/tagout procedures to ensure the safety of all personnel. Refurbishment and Rehabilitation Work AHU 43 Specific Tasks Remove the Energy Recovery coil. Refrigeration removal will be handled by others. Replace the direct expansion (DX) coil with a new replacement coilÂ to match the existing. Install the new DX coil at the location of the Energy Recovery coil. Patch the old location. Replace the existing Chilled Water coilÂ with a new one to match the existing. Remove and reinstall in the same location. Relocate the humidifierÂ to the current location of the DX coil. Patch and reseal all parts of AHU 43. Coating and Surface Protection for All AHUs Apply an internal multi-layer coating system that includes: An engineered epoxy layer for structural integrity. A fire barrier layer for enhanced safety. An antimicrobial waterproof topcoat for durability and sanitary conditions. Ensure the coating is specifically designed for restoring Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) units to prevent corrosion and leaks. Ensure the coating provides protection against scuffs and is eco-friendly. The coating product shall have no detectable odors or Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs). The coating shall be National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) 90A fire code compliant. Apply the multi-layer system to re-slope condensate pans and seal surfaces to improve indoor air quality, in accordance with American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE) 62.1 standards. Quality Control and Documentation Inspections and Audits Conduct regular inspections and quality audits to ensure compliance with project specifications and standards. Maintain detailed records of all refurbishment activities. Final Inspection and Handover Perform a final inspection of each AHU to verify compliance with project requirements. Conduct a handover session with facility management, providing detailed reports, manuals, and maintenance guidelines. Project Closeout Demobilization Remove all equipment, tools, and materials from the site. Restore the work area to its original condition. Project Documentation and Reporting Compile and submit a comprehensive project completion report, including before-and-after photos, test results, and documentation. Training and Support Provide training sessions for facility staff on the operation and maintenance of the refurbished AHUs. Offer post-project support and warranty services as agreed upon. Health, Safety, and Environmental Considerations Ensure compliance with all health, safety, and environmental regulations. Implement measures to minimize the environmental impact of refurbishment activities. Deliverables Detailed project plan and schedule Refurbished and rehabilitated AHUs Comprehensive project documentation and reports Training and support for facility staff Period of Performance The period of performance for this project is 90 days from the start date.
