# Financial Investigation Basics and Lead Development Training Services

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 21:26:49.022-04
- Solicitation Number: PR20157679

## Summary

This is a Combined Solitation/Synopsis Notice for commerial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with Revolutionary FAR Overhaul Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotes are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. This is Total Small Business Set-Aside announcement to request quotes for training and educational services to support the Yuma Border Patrol Station who is responsible for highway interdiction and border enforcement operations in the Yuma Sector where agents frequently encounter bulk currency and contraband that may be linked to smuggling organizations and broader criminal networks. The services offered must meet at least the minimum requirements found in Attachment 1 - Statement of Work (SOW). All interested vendors shall provide a firm-fixed-price quote for professional training services to design, tailor (if necessary), and deliver a “Financial Investigations Basics and Lead Development” course to Yuma Border Patrol Station personnel, and must fulfill all the requirements stated in the attached SOW. In order to be considered, quote must be submitted before this contract opportunity notice closes; any quotes received after will not be considered. Attachments: Attachment 1 - Statement of Work Attachment 2 - Instructions to Offeror Attachment 3 - Evaluation Instructions Combined Solicitation and Synopsis_PR20157679_Financial Inv Basics and Lead Dev
