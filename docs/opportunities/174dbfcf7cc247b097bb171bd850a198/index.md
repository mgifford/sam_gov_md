# Omnicell Medication Dispensing Cabinets - Loopback Update

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-25 22:56:56.923-04
- Solicitation Number: VA2600044494

## Summary

RFI Notice ID VA2600044494 was posted on 02/20/2026 to SAM.gov as market research for Omnicell Medication Dispensing Cabinets. This notice is to inform vendors of the acquisition decision for the below requirement in accordance with (IAW) VA Technology Acquisition Center (TAC) Market Research Loopback procedures. IAW Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) 19.106-3, the Contracting Officer does not have a reasonable expectation that offers would be received from two (2) or more Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business concerns. As a result, the subject requirement shall be pursued via direct-award to Trillamed, LLC IAW RFO 19.106-3 "Sole Source Awards", utilizing the GSA eBuy Federal Supply Schedule contract 36F79720D0041 under Special Item Number 65 II A-92 "Medication and Supply Packaging and Dispensing Equipment". A Justification for Other than Full and Open Competition has been approved, and a redacted version shall be posted with the official award synopsis. This Amendment is for informational purposes only to inform previously interested vendors of the chosen acquisition path; do NOT submit any proposals or responses to this posting. The original RFI confirmation is attached to this notice to assist vendors in identifying the previous opportunity.
