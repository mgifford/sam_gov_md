# Industry Day Notice: FY26 TSA Innovation Office – Electronic Gates (eGates)

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-13 11:57:53.661-04

## Summary

The Transportation Security Administration (TSA) Innovation Office will host an Industry Day to highlight TSA’s Electronic Gates (eGates) capability. The event will take place on June 3, 2026, at 1:00 PM EST, due to conclude at 2:00 PM EST, via Microsoft Teams. TSA is expanding its third-party partnership efforts to accelerate innovation and provide industry with opportunities to increase efficiency and deploy scalable, mature screening solutions. TSA envisions utilizing eGates at the Travel Document Checker (TDC) by collaborating with industry partners and leveraging TSA’s public-private partnerships (P3). Additional Information: An attachment with further details about TSA’s eGate capability is included with this notice. All interested parties are encouraged to review the attached document prior to the Industry Day to familiarize themselves with the requirements and objectives. Who Should Attend: Industry partners with capabilities in the aviation security ecosystem, including those specializing in electronic gates, screening technologies, and related systems, are encouraged to participate. Registration Instructions: If you are interested in attending, please RSVP by May 27, 2026, 10:00 AM EST by sending an email to TSA.Innovation@tsa.dhs.gov with “eGates Industry Day RSVP” in the subject line. Attendance is limited to those who RSVP by the deadline. A Teams calendar invite will be sent to all registered industry partners.
