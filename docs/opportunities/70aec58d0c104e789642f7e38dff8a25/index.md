# Office furnitures

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-27 10:22:58.019-04
- Solicitation Number: PAN40926P0000028416

## Summary

The United States Army in Bavaria is conducting market research and anticipates a future requirement for office furniture supplies to support operations across multiple Army facilities. This notice is issued solely for information and planning purposes to identify capable and interested vendors. This announcement does not constitute a solicitation. No proposals are being requested at this time. Additional details—including specific items, quantities, delivery requirements, and anticipated solicitation timelines—will be provided in a subsequent notice should the Government proceed with a formal procurement. Vendors with the capability to provide office furniture supplies are encouraged to respond and monitor future updates.
