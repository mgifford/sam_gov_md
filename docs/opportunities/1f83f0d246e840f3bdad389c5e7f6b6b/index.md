## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes a solicitation for quotes/bids. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, at Modoc National Wildlife Refuge, has a project to replace and overhaul the existing water main system extending from the holding tank junction to Refuge Quarters 14. The water main currently serves the Headquarters office, visitor area, and residential house. The project also includes installation of a new shutoff system to improve water control and service access for the house and backyard area. The construction magnitude is estimated to be between $50,000 to $65,000.00. The office is located: Modoc National Wildlife Refuge 5364 County Road 115, Alturas, CA 96101-1610 The Government anticipates 124 days, period of performance from July 1, 2026, through November 1, 2026, issuance of the Notice to Proceed (NTP) or contingent upon contract award.
