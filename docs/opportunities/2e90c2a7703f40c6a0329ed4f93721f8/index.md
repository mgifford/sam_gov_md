## Summary

Combined Synopsis-Solicitation for Commercial Products and Commercial Services Effective Date: 01/15/2026 Revision: 03 Description This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products and commercial services prepared in accordance with the format Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) in Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) subpart 12.202, Streamlined Procedures for Evaluation and Solicitation for Commercial Products and Commercial Services, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested, and a written solicitation document will not be issued. This solicitation is issued as an RFQ. The solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular 2026-01. This solicitation is set-aside for small business. The associated North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 334510, with a small business size standard of 1,250. The FSC/PSC is 6515. The Phoenix VA Health Care System at 650 E Indian School Road, Phoenix, AZ 85012 is seeking to purchase electro-surgical analyzers. All interested companies shall provide quotations for the following: Supplies/Services Line Item Description Qty UOM Unit Price Total Price 0001 398A912 Electro Surgical Analyzer 2 ea Salient Characteristics: Must be portable/battery powered Must have integrated oscilloscope Must have onscreen power profile analysis Must have onboard automation and data capture Must have full color user-interface Must be able to test cut AND coagulation feature from a remote foot switch control Must have high current test capability up to 6 Ampere RMS Must have a Non-inductive load bank from 0 5115 Must be capable of variable load AND fixed load Must have power range: 0-500W Must have current range: 0-6000mA Must have voltage range: 0-700V Must have crest factor range: 1.4-20 Must have bandwidth, with load bank, 30-2.5MHz Must have interference frequency filter: 100Hz high pass filter Must have all IEC 60601-2-2 leakage tests Delivery shall be provided no later than 30 days after receipt of order.
