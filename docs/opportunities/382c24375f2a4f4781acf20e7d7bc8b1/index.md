# Engineering, Technical, and Programmatic Services Supporting the NSWCPD Machinery Research and Silencing Division C/32 (SeaPort-NxG)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 14:27:29.352-04
- Solicitation Number: N6449826R3102

## Summary

***THIS REQUIREMENT IS BEING SOLICITED TO SEAPORT-NXG MAC HOLDERS ONLY***This requirement includes research, engineering, test, and program support services for the Machinery Research and Silencing Division of Code 32. The contractor shall provide engineering, programmatic, and technical services to support the RDTE of new and In-Service shipboard automation and controls, electric power, machinery technology, machinery concept design, machinery silencing technology, energy conversion, additive manufacturing, digital transformation, and advanced machinery systems integration. The deadline for receipt of questions is 10 days after issuance of the solicitation. Although every effort will be made, the Government makes no guarantee that questions received after the date above will be answered. All questions shall be submitted via email to the points of contact listed below: Tiffany Vu, Contract Specialist: tiffany.o.vu.civ@us.navy.mil Angela Cusati, Contracting Officer: angela.f.cusati.civ@us.navy.mil
