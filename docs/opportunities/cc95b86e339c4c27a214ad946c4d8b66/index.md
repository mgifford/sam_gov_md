# Peterson SFB Backflow/Cross Connection Survey

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 13:02:31.217-04
- Solicitation Number: FA251726Q0032

## Summary

The 21st Civil Engineering Squadron (21 CES) has a new requirement for a one time service for the contractor to survey and inventory all cross connections throughout Peterson Space Force Base (PSFB), Colorado in accordance with the provided Performance Work Statement. The Air Force requires that Water System Backflow Prevention/Cross-Connection survey of all facilities and water-using equipment and systems be completed every five years. This survey is to ensure our Peterson SFB water supply is pollution and contamination free.
