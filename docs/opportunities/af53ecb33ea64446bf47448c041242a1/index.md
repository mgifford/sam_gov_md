- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 14:42:33.277-04

## Summary

This notice is issued as a related announcement under Commercial Solutions Opening (CSO) W911KF-25-S-C002. This is an Area of Interest (AoI) for a Computer Numerical Control (CNC) Gantry Machining Center for Anniston Army Depot. Interested contractors must review and reference all requirements, instructions, and information contained in the original CSO posted on SAM.gov (and within this posting), as well as the details provided in the Area of Interest (AoI A004). Amendment 0001 - posted 4 May 2026 answers industry questions and extend Phase I. Phase I responses due by 12 May 2026 at 5:00 PM CDT.
