# CNSGSW Office Reconfiguration

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 18:23:14.207-04
- Solicitation Number: N00244-26-Q-0026

## Summary

This is a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) subpart 12.6, “Streamlined Procedures for Evaluation and Solicitation for Commercial Items,” as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; “quotations” are being requested, and a written solicitation document will not be issued. The Naval Supply Systems Command (NAVSUP) Fleet Logistics Center San Diego (FLCSD) has a requirement in support of Commander Naval Surface Group South West (CNSGSW) to reconfigure office space in N47 and N55. All the requirements and details of this purchase can be found in the SOW. Mandatory site visit scheduled for Wednesday, May 6, 2026 at Naval Base San Diego. To register and receive instructions for the site visit, please email : dionte.l.grays.civ@us.navy.mil by 11:00 AM PST Tuesday, May 5, 2026 and include "N00244-26-Q-0026 Site Visit for CNSGSW Office Reconfiguration" in the subject line.
