## Summary

Combined Synopsis/Solicitation (Unrestricted/Open Market) This is a combined synopsis/solicitation This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items/services prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Subpart 12.6 as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. Request for Quotations number is 70Z08526Q024250 and is issued as a request for quotations (RFQ). This announcement constitutes only solicitation; quotations are being requested, and a written solicitation will not be issued. This Request for Quotations incorporates provisions and clauses in effect through the most current Federal Acquisition Circular. The NAICS Code is 336611 (Ship Building and Repairing) with a small business size standard of 1,250 employees. This acquisition is Unrestricted (Full and Open Competition/Open Market). All responsible sources, including large and small businesses, may submit quotations which shall be considered by the agency. The contract will be awarded on a firm-fixed-price basis using simplified acquisition procedures in accordance with FAR Part 13.5. All responses from interested vendors must be received on or before 0800 Pacific Time, May 27, 2026, and will be reviewed by the Government. The United States Coast Guard Surface Force Logistics Center has a requirement for the following: Contractor shall provide parts requested for USCGC WAESCHE Fire Detection System upgrade. Statement of Work (SOW) CGC WAESCHE (WMSL 751) - Fire Detection System Components

1.0 SCOPE This Statement of Work (SOW) defines the requirement for the procurement of specific Siemens fire detection system components. These parts are required to support a scheduled maintenance availability for the CGC WAESCHE (WMSL 751). This is a parts-only procurement.

2.0 DELIVERY REQUIREMENT All parts listed below must be delivered no later than 01JUN26. The Government's Period of Performance for the follow-on installation work is 01 JUN 2026 – 28 JUL 2026, making on-time delivery of these components critical. Parts are required to be shipped to: CGC WAESCHE (WMSL 751) SFLC LRE PL 1 Eagle Road, Alameda, California 94501 c/o Port Engineer: LT Kevin Holmes
