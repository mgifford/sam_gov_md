# HVAC Vent Cleaning

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation

## Summary

The Contractor shall provide all necessary labor, tools, equipment, materials, and supervision required to perform a comprehensive, one-time cleaning of the Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) air duct and vent systems at the designated facility. The work involves deep cleaning and sanitization of both internal and external components of the HVAC system. Reference Attachment 1- Statement of Work.
