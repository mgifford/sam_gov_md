# Rental of air compressor and regenerative air drier

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 14:11:49.164-04
- Solicitation Number: N42158260007

## Summary

The Norfolk Naval Shipyard (NNSY) Service Contracting Department, Portsmouth, VA 23709-5000, intends to award a firm fixed price contract for the rental of one (1) Grade D Oil-Free Electric Air Compressor and one (1) Regenerative Air Dryer in support of a USS Vessel using the policies and procedures of Federal Acquisition (FAR) part 12 Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services. This requirement will be solicited as 100% Total Small Business Set-Aside. The NAICS code applicable to this procurement is 532490. The size standard is $35.0 million. This requirement will contain a period of performance from May 18, 2026 to October 17, 2026 with the potential of 6 (1) one-month options. The Government intends to fulfill this requirement via Direct Solicitation in accordance with FAR 12.201-1(c)(2). Because the Government will solicit direct quotes, a formal written solicitation document (RFQ/RFP) will NOT be issued or posted to SAM.gov.
