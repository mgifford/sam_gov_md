# USDA Forest Service Flathead National Forest Equine Winter Boarding

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 10:37:11.573-04
- Solicitation Number: 1284LM26Q0067

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1284LM26Q0067 is issued as a request for quote (RFQ) for equine winter boarding and supplemental feeding within 200 miles of the Forest Service Flathead National Forest Office in Kalispell, Montana. This acquisition is a small business set-aside. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 115210. The small business size standard is $11M. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the Agency. Please see attachments for a detailed description of the requirement and submittal instructions. Questions should be emailed to joanne.meiers@usda.gov no later than May 20, 2026, COB.
