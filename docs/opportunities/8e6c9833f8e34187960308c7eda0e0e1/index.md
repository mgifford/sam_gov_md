# OEM Belfort Windspeed Measuring System Certification Aboard T-AKE Class Vessel

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 18:28:45.863-04
- Solicitation Number: N0040626Q0095

## Summary

NAVSUP Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound intends to solicit and contract a firm fixed price, supply type contract to inspect, service, test, and certify the Belfort Instruments Company wind speed measuring and indicating system aboard a T-AKE Class vessel on a Brand Name basis. See attached solicitation for requirements. Solicitation closes on 10 June 2026 at 10:00 AM PST. The Government anticipates awarding on or about 24 June 2026. All questions regarding this solicitation should be submitted to J. Brian Fergus, james.b.fergus.civ@us.navy.mil.
