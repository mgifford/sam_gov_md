# Two gate replacement, Lewis and Clark NHP

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 20:42:37.879-04
- Solicitation Number: 140P8326Q0029

## Summary

Lewis and Clark National Historical Park (LEWI) is seeking firm fixed-price quotations for all labor, wiring, ditching, materials and equipment necessary for installation of two (2) Government owned security gates as described in the attached scope of work. This RFQ will result in a single firm fixed-price contract to a single contractor. The applicable NAICS code is 238910 with $19 million dollars within the past three years. No site visit is scheduled. Offers will be made available for award for a minimum of 60 days. Only email questions/inquiries/quotes will be accepted, by email to: hal_hoversten@ios.doi.gov The Government reserves the right to cancel this solicitation.
