Brand Name or Equal to MICROMILL 2 Micro-Sampling Instrument 1) This Synopsis/Solicitation Notice is to announce the U.S. Department of Interior (DOI), U.S. Geological Survey has a requirement for Brand Name or Equal to a Quantity of: One (1), ESL Micromill2 system per the attached solicitation. 2) The synopsis/solicitation is 140G0226Q0060 and is being issued as a Request for Quote. 3) The award for this requirement shall be made using the policies in the Federal Acquisition Regulations (FAR) Part 12, Acquisition of Commercial Items, and in conjunction with the policies and procedures for solicitation, evaluation, and award prescribed in FAR
