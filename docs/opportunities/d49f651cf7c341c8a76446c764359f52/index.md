- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

## Summary

This requirement is for the purchase of professional-grade tools with etching and foam shadowing in accordance with the associated equipment lists and Statement of Work for the new Low Observable (LO) facility at Ellsworth AFB, SD. All questions or Request for Information (RFI) must be in writing and shall be emailed to tyree.ross@us.af.mil, RFIs will not be answered via phone. Verification of receipt will be emailed back to each vendor. If verification is not received, vendors may contact Tyree Ross at 605-385-1736. All questions must be submitted no later than 1:00 PM MDT, 15 May 2026.
