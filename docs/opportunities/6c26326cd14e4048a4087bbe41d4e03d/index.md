# Roads Multifunctional 4x4 Equipment Truck with Attachments

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 08:50:21.766-04
- Solicitation Number: W912CM26QA020

## Summary

The Regional Contracting Office (RCO) Wiesbaden intends to solicit for a Multifunctional 4x4 Equipment Truck with attachments for the Directorate of Public Works (DPW) Roads Maintenance Branch at USAG Wiesbaden. The requirement is for a specialized, compact municipal-grade 4x4 carrier chassis. The Government anticipates awarding a Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) contract as a result of the upcoming solicitation. A detailed technical specifications, capacity requirements, and host-nation compliance standards will be provided within the solicitation package. Competition is restricted to local vendors and the solciitation package will be sent directly to vendors. This restriction ensures the equipment meets European road safety standards (DIN, StVZO) and guarantees rapid response for future technical support. IMPORTANT: Quoters must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) database at time of submission of quotes. For registration instructions visit the following website: www.sam.gov
