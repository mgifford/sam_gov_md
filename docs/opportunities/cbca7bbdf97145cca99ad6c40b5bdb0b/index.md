# PreSolicitaton-REMAN F-15 Fuel Tank Safety Relief Valve

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 14:27:49.252-04
- Solicitation Number: FD20302600725-01

## Summary

THIS IS A 100% SMALL BUSINESS SET-ASIDE OPPORTUNITY The United States Air Force, through the Air Force Sustainment Center (AFSC), Tinker AFB 73145, is contemplating a requirement for the remanufacture of the F-15 Internal Fuel Tank Safety Relief Valve. The contractor shall provide all labor, facilities, equipment, and material necessary to accomplish the remanufacture. The work encompasses disassembly, cleaning, inspection, maintenance, re-assembly, testing, and finishing actions required to return the item to a like-new condition in accordance with the solicitation requirements and all attachments. A firm fixed-priced Indefinite Delivery Requirements type (IDIQ) contract is contemplated with a 5-Years Total (3-Year Basic Period + One 2-Year Option Period).
