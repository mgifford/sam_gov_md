# Cyber Security Engineering and Risk Management Framework Support Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 09:45:22.873-04
- Solicitation Number: PANAPG-26-P-0000041835

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to perform Cybersecurity Engineering and DoD Risk Management Framework (RMF) Support Services except for those items specified as government furnished property and services. IT Solutions Architect Sr – must have CISSP certification and minimum 5 years experience managing RMF lifecycle reporting and progressive requirements for network expansion and cloud/enclaves. The other IT Certified Professionals should have Security+ or CASP/SecurityX certification and minimum 3 years experience supporting RMF lifecycle reporting and software/hardware reconciliation. Specifically, the one surge position for Top Secret effort must have minimum 3 years supporting the lifecycle documentation for Top Secret environments. All positions require experience managing across different platforms (Windows/Linux/Apple) and various security classification environments (Unclassified/Classified).
