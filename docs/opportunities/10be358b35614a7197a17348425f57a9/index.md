# U.S. National Day Venue and Catering - 2026

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 08:53:23.984-04
- Solicitation Number: 19BA3026Q0002

## Summary

Please note this solicitation is not ready to be issued as of this date. The Embassy of the United States in the Kingdom of Bahrain will launch the solicitation on the SAM.gov website when it is available. The U.S. Embassy Manama requires a venue and catering service for the celebration of its National Day 2026. All qualified sources may submit a quotation which will be considered. The Government intends to award a contract resulting from this solicitation to the lowest priced technically acceptable offeror who is a responsible contractor. The Government may award the contract based on the initial offer without discussion. SAM registration is not required; however, qualified offerors must have a valid CAGE/N-CAGE code for verification. Electronic Submission/Responses: This opportunity allows for electronic responses. Please submit your offer prior to the response date and time. Please ensure your email with your offer does not exceed 30 MB, if it does then you might have to send more than one email.
