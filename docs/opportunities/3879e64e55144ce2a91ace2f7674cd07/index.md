Announcement, not a solicitation: The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA), U.S. Forest Service (FS), Intermountain Zone, Caribou-Targhee National Forest, intends to award from a BPA: 38ea bear resistant, 4 cu yard, dumpsters that have been certified and approved by the International Grizzly Bear Committee.
