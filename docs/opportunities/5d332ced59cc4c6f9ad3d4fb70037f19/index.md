## Summary

The Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), Ginnie Mae (GNMA) requires contractor support to strengthen its project management office operations function. Services shall include strategic partnering and ensuring business and technology alignment in strategic planning with strategy execution, analyzing and enhancing project management automation solutions, portfolio management and providing recommendations to assist with the development of streamlined processes. This sources sought synopsis is a means of conducting market research to identify parties having an interest in and the resources to support this requirement.
