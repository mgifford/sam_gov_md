## Summary

Salient Characteristics (MP2015A OR EQUAL) Advanced 3D Directionality & Precision: Real-time, 3D directional guidance to pinpoint lesions with high accuracy, ensuring smaller incisions and better tissue conservation. Sleek, Ergonomic Design: Specifically designed with a slimmer, pen-like form factor, the wand is easier to handle and allows for better maneuverability during surgery, fitting seamlessly into surgical workflows. Real-Time Feedback: It works with a tablet to provide four types of feedback distance, visual, auditory, and 3D directional to accurately guide surgeons to the marker. Optimal Performance in Complex Tissue: Capable of locating markers (3.2 mm). Improved Patient Experience & Efficiency: The wire-free, nonradioactive system increases patient satisfaction by allowing for flexible scheduling and reducing anxiety. Key Salient Characteristics (MT2001 OR EQUAL) Real-Time 3D Directional Guidance: 3D directional guidance alongside distance measurements, allowing surgeons to know exactly where the marker is and how to navigate to it. Intuitive Visualization & Feedback: Provides instant, accurate visual, auditory, and distance data. The interface reduces cognitive load and provides feedback without distracting from the operative field. Reliability: 100% successful identification of the \(3.2\text{ mm}\) (sesame seed size) marker. Ergonomic Design: Mountable on IV poles for optimal viewing, reducing physical strain on the surgeon. Workflow Integration: The system supports "on-demand" localization, allowing the marker to be placed days or even weeks before surgery, which improves scheduling for both patients and clinicians. Performance: Provides marker retrieval and localization times (mean 17.9 seconds).
