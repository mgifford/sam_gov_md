# Bldg. 882 Roof Repair

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 17:24:34.482-04
- Solicitation Number: FA462626R0014

## Summary

The Government’s obligation under this contract is contingent upon the availability of appropriated funds from which payment for contract purposes can be made. The Government reserves the right to cancel this solicitation at any time. This firm fixed price contract will be for the Bldg. 882 Roof Repair project at Malmstrom AFB, MT. The objective of this project is to remove the existing roof and replace it with a new EPDM roof. The building is approximately 21,000 square feet. The Request for Proposal will be available no sooner than 25 May 2026. The Request for Proposal should close on or about 25 June 2026. The Period of Performance (PoP) will be 270 Calendar Days After Receipt of Notic to Proceed (NTP). This acquisition is set aside for Total Small Businesses under the Small Business Jobs Act of 2010. The Request for Proposal will only be available via Electronic Transmission on System for Award Management (SAM) site (http://www. sam.gov).
