## Summary

COMBINED SYNOPSIS/SOLICITATION Replace 17 Eyewash Stations Pascagoula Lab (i) This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1333MF26Q0033 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ), for Services, non-personal, to provide all labor, equipment and materials (unless otherwise provided herein) necessary for repair, rebuild, and installation of components necessary to restore proper operation of seventeen (17) laboratory eyewash stations located throughout the SEFSC Pascagoula Laboratory facility. The work will ensure the stations provide tempered water and comply with ANSI Z358.1 emergency eyewash standards. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The associated NAICS code is 238220. The small business size standard is $19M. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov.
