# WPC Ventilation System Component Purchase

## Summary

This is a MARKET SURVEY / SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE. This is not a pre-solicitation notice or solicitation for proposals/quotations. REQUIREMENT: The USCG SFLC PBPL will procure approximately 502 parts in various quantities under twenty national stock numbers over the course of this contract. These parts are required as government furnished equipment (GFE) in support of the 87’ WPB and 154’ WPC class vessels. Vendors responding to this requirement shall provide the parts in various quantities IAW issued delivery orders. The various parts under this requirement shall be manufactured to or meet the requirements of the OEM ventilation system, Novenco. Although Novenco is not the original manufacturer of all the parts associated with their ventilation systems, many of these parts have been modified to fit or function with their system designs. It is imperative that any vendor providing offers to this contract guarantee the parts they are offering will meet the OEM requirements by providing certification by the OEM. The ventilation systems are critical to vessel operations and the 87’ WPB and 154’ WPC class vessels to complete their mission. Novenco is the OEM of the ventilation systems installed on these vessels by the shipbuilder to provide fresh air circulation and exhaust throughout the vessel during operations. The parts listed in section VII; B. of this report are essential for these systems to function as designed. Each part has a significant purpose in the operations of these systems. Period of Performance: It is anticipated that one firm, fixed-price, requirements contract for five (5) one (1) year ordering periods will be awarded for this requirement.
