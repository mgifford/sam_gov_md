# NOTICE: Intent to Award Sole Source – MIDWEST MICROWAVE SOLUTIONS – for Wideband Microwave Digital Receiver

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 15:23:53.059-04
- Solicitation Number: N66001-26-Q-6109

## Summary

Naval Information Warfare Center, Pacific (NIWC Pacific) intends to award a firm fixed price purchase order on a sole source basis to MIDWEST MICROWAVE SOLUTIONS, INC. (CAGE 4S5 KG, 2000 Progress DR Hiawatha, Iowa 52233-2406, United States). The requirement is for the purchase of the following: (1) 0.1 to 1 BGHz Wideband Microwave Digital Receiver (500MHz Bandwidth), Part No. WRX-18G-500, qty. 1. This notice of intent is not a request for quote. A determination by the Government not to complete based upon responses to this notice is solely at the discretion of the Government. Questions by phone or fax will not be considered. Acquisition of Commercial Items under FAR Part 12 and Simplified Acquisitions Procedures under FAR Part 13 apply. The applicable NAICS Code is 334511 and the size standard is 1350 Employees. The point of contact for this announcement is Sarah Coe at sarah.a.coe2.civ@us.navy.mil. To respond to this announcement, you must be registered to the NIWC E-Commerce website at https://e-commerce.sscno.nmci.navy.mil and upload your inquiry under NIWC Pacific/Simplified Acquisitions/ N66001-26-Q-6109. All responding vendors must be registered to the System for Award Management (SAM) website (i.e., registered UEID and CAGE Code numbers) prior to award of contract. Information can be found at https://www.sam.gov.
