## Summary

PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE BRAND NAME - NMIS BioDose Software and Support Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Network Contracting Office (NCO 21) intends to award a Brand Name only, firm fixed price contract for NMIS BioDose Software and Support. The procurement is being competed as a Small Business Set- Aside under FAR Part 12-102: Brand Name Only and will result in a purchase order for NMIS BioDose Software supporting VA Central California Health Care System. The solicitation for this action will be posted to SAM.gov, and all responsible sources may submit a capability statement, proposal, or quotation, which shall be considered by the agency. The North American Industrial Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 541511: Custom Computer Programming Services, with a size standard in millions of dollars $34.0 million. The FSC/PSC is 6525 Imaging Equipment and Supplies: Medical, Dental, Veterinary The solicitation will be posted on or about 05/19/2026 to SAM.Gov.
