# Stator Cores for Small Brushless DC Motors

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 14:17:17.674-04
- Solicitation Number: SC050726

## Summary

Tobyhanna Army Depot (TYAD) is issuing this Request for Information (RFI) to identify potential commercial sources capable of manufacturing and supplying stator cores for small outrunner brushless Direct Current (DC) motors utilized in small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS). The Government requires scalable, high-volume production capabilities of up to 40,000 units per month. See attached RFI and Draft SOW for additional details.
