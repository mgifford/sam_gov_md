# X2 WATER CHEMICAL BPA - FSC 6810, 6830

## Summary

NAVSUP Weapon Systems Support (NAVSUP WSS) Nuclear Supply Chain Management Directorate is dedicated solely to providing logistics and sustainment to the Navy Nuclear Propulsion Program. We are responsible for the provisioning, procurement, and quality assurance of all components within the Navy’s nuclear propulsion plants, also known as nuclear reactors. This is an annual synopsis for the Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) for competitively procured nuclear reactor products with a Federal Supply Code (FSC) OF 6810 AND 6630. The NAICS Code for this material is 325199. The attached Request for Information contains a detailed overview of the NAVSUP WSS Nuclear Program and is being used as a market research tool to identify small business concerns interested in becoming valued NAVSUP WSS supplier.
