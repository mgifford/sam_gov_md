# Sector Humboldt Bay Janitorial Services

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 12:41:44.22-04
- Solicitation Number: Janitorial

## Summary

The Contractor shall ensure that these services are performed in a manner that ensures the health and safety of federal employees and members of the public; and both the sanitary conditions and aesthetic appearance of federal property as required by federal, environmental, safety and health regulations. Services shall provide superior customer service and well-maintained facilities that present a healthy, clean, neat and professional appearance in accordance with (IAW) all the terms and conditions of the contract.
