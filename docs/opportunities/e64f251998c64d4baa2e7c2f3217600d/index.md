# Spokane VA Asbestos Inspection/Abatement Services

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-28 18:03:15.991-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C260-26-AP-3112

## Summary

Spokane VAMC requires Asbestos Management Services, to include semi annual surveillance and triennial reinspections of asbestos containing materials, independent oversight of asbestos abatement projects, and pre-construction asbestos surveys for renovation and small scale projects.
