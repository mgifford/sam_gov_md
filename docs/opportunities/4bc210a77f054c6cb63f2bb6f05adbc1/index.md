# NATO Request for Information: Imagery Exploitation and Visualization Tools (IMINT)

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-27 09:25:44.235-04
- Solicitation Number: RFI-424252-IMINT

## Summary

The NATO Communications and Information Agency (NCIA) has issued a Request for Information (RFI) to identify qualified vendors and gather input on potential solutions to support the upcoming acquisition of Imagery Exploitation and Visualization Tools (IMINT). NATO’s Joint Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance Centre (JISRC), in coordination with NCIA, is seeking to enhance and expand its IMINT capabilities. The IMINT project aims to acquire modern, commercially available software solutions that support end-to-end Tasking, Collection, Processing, Exploitation, and Dissemination (TCPED) of imagery and associated intelligence across a federated enterprise. This RFI is issued for planning purposes only and is not a request for solicitation. It is part of NCIA’s effort to confirm available capabilities from industry. Do NOT submit your RFI response to the Department of Commerce. Submission instructions and additional details can be found in the RFI (attached). The NCIA reference for this RFI- 424252-IMINT, and all correspondence concerning this matter should reference this number. The response deadline is NLT 17:00 hours Central European Time (CET) on 08 June 2026 Your participation in this RFI is greatly appreciated.
