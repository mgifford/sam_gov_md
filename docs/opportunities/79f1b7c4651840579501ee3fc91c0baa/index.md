# PRESOLICITATION NOTICE_Joint Region Marianas_CISSP IT Training_GUAM

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 20:18:18.565-04
- Solicitation Number: N62649_JRM_CISSP_PRESOLICITATION

## Summary

DATE OF THIS POSTING: 22 MAY 2026 A forthcoming solicitation containing further information for this requirement is anticipated. Once a solicitation is issued, all responsible sources may submit a quotation, bid, or proposal, as appropriate, which will be considered by NAVSUP FLCY. If applicable, technical data will be provided at the time of solicitation. POINTS OF CONTACT: Alice Mendoza, Contract Specialist Amenda Davila, Contracting Officer DESCRIPTION: NAVSUP FLCY Site Marianas (Guam) has a requirement for professional cybersecurity training in support of Joint Region Marianas (JRM) in Guam. JRM personnel must be fully qualified under the Department of Defense (DoD) Cyber Workforce Framework (DCWF), in accordance with DoD Manual 8140.03. This training is essential for developing and maintaining a highly qualified cadre of certified information security professionals to protect critical information systems. This Statement of Work outlines the requirement for a comprehensive, on-site ISC2 Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) training course. The primary objective is to deliver a five-day, instructor-led CISSP certification training and refresher course for twenty (20) students. The course must effectively prepare students to pass the ISC2 CISSP certification exam and equip them with the knowledge to implement and manage enterprise security programs. The contractor shall provide a complete, turn-key training solution. This includes providing a qualified instructor, all course materials, necessary equipment, exam vouchers, and associated services required to conduct the training at a government-provided facility. The course must cover the most current version of the ISC2 CISSP Common Body of Knowledge (CBK). The anticipated period of performance is five (5) consecutive days, Monday through Friday, from 0800 to 1600. The specific week will be scheduled for August 2026 upon contract award and coordination with the Government POCs. The Government anticipates issuing a solicitation for a Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) contract on or about 3 JUNE 2026. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: This notice is NOT a request for proposals. The solicitation, when issued, will be available electronically via the System for Award Management (SAM.gov) website. All responsible and eligible sources may submit a quotation, bid, or proposal, as appropriate, in response to the subsequent solicitation, which will be considered by the agency. It is the responsibility of interested parties to monitor this site for the release of the solicitation and any subsequent amendments.
