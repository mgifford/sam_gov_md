DESCRIPTION OF REQUIREMENT: The Rhode Island Army National Guard (RIARNG) requires a commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) handheld Explosive Trace Detector (ETD). The system must be lightweight, capable of sustained standalone operation, and designed to detect a wide variety of explosive compounds at trace levels to ensure the safety of mailroom personnel and facilities.
