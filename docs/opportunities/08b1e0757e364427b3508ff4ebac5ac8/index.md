## Summary

This announcement is a Sources Sought (Market Research) Notice. This is not a Request for Quote (RFQ) or Request for Proposal (RFP). This annoucement is for information and planning purposes only and is not to be taken as a commitment by the Government, implied or otherwise, to issue a solicitation or award a contract. Responses due no later than 29 MAY 2026, 12:00PM HST. Send responses to Emma Newbegin at emma.a.newbegin.civ@army.mil The Hawaii National Guard is seeking sources to complete an acquisition for musical instruments, specifically professional grade oboes. Specifics: Howard XM Oboe in Grenadilla Wood Body in Grenadilla wood, professional model oboe, XM Model, full conservatoire system, silver plated keywork, semi-automatic octaves, 3rd octave key, C-D Trill Key (X2), C-C# Trill Key, Forked F-Vent, extra trill mechanisms (Ab-Bb, Ab-A), "Banana Key" - Low C-C# Trill, Eb-E trill mechanism, Articulated C# mechanism, metal-lined tenon sockets and sleeved tenons, undercut tone holes, low Bb vent, all necessary adjusting screws, adjustable thumb rest. All new instruments must come with professional solid wood case, oboe swab, polishing cloth, adjustment screwdriver, cork grease. Quantitiy: 2 EA In the case that the Howarth XM Oboe in Grenadilla Wood is not available the alternate brands are acceptable: 1. Howarth XL 2. F. Loree Royal 3. Marigaux 2001 FOR RESPONSES TO THIS SEEKING SOURCES NOTICE: 1) Your intent to submit a quote for this requirement when it is formally advertised; 2) Name of firm with address, phone number, e-mail address and point of contact 3) CAGE code and Unique Entity ID#; 4) Size of firm including category of small bsuiness, such as 8(a), Women-Owned Small business (WOSB), HUBZone, Small Disadvantaged Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB); and 5) Statement of Capability stating your skills, experience, and knowledge required to perform the specific type of work. Responses due no later than 29 MAY 2026, 12:00PM HST.
