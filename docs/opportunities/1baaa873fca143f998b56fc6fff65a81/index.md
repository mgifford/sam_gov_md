# Armory Shelves

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 18:45:01.001-04
- Solicitation Number: FA521526Q0015

## Summary

The delivery and installation of one additional adjustable row of six shelves for the storage of twenty-one (21) M240's, thirty (30) M249's, Ten (10) M110's, and one stand alone rack that can hold four (4) M2's. - Three (3) of weapons racks must hold seven (7) M240's each, two (2) of the weapons racks must hold twelve (12) M249's, and one (1) of the weapons racks must hold four (4) M110's and six (6) M249's. - Five (5) of the weapons racks must be 89 1/2" high x 42 3/8" long x 16 5/8" wide and have doors that can be locked from the outside. - The mobile shelve must be 10'7 1/8' long and 33 1/4" wide and fit on the existing rolling tracks. - The stand alone rack must be 61 3/8" high x 42 3/8" long x 16 5/8" wide, have doors that can be locked from the outside, and must hold four (4) M2's. - The existing tracks will need to be extended 33 1/4" The available space is too small for standalone storage racks. The current layout and remaining space only supports modular configuration with the existing storage system of spacesaver weapon racks and lockers. This is a brand name solicitation for SpaceSaver Storage Systems Inc systems.
