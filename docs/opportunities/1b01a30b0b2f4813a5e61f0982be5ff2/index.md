# Notice of Intent to Sole Source - Instrumentacion Analitica

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 08:33:31.67-04
- Solicitation Number: N6817126RT004

## Summary

NAVSUP Fleet Logistics Center Sigonella – Rota Detachment intends to award a commercial Sole Source contract to Instrumentacion Analitica (CAGE: Pending) in accordance with FAR 12.102. Naval Station Rota, Fuels Laboratory Department has (8) jet and diesel fuel testing assets from varying manufacturers which require annual calibration and minor maintenance by each respective Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM). The equipment manufacturer brands include Prevision Analyzer Company, Seta Analytics, PCS, ILC and Cannon Instrument Company. OEM servicers must be registered to perform services in Spain. Instrumentacion Analitica is the sole calibration firm capable of providing these services. The applicable North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is 811210 with a small business standard of $34M. This Notice of Intent is not a request for competitive quotes or proposals and no solicitation nor RFQ will be forthcoming. Responsible sources may identify their interest and submit a capability statement in response to this notice via e-mail to Melissa.e.ferri.civ@us.navy.mil which shall be considered by the agency. Information received will be considered for the sole purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement. The determination to not compete this proposed contract is entirely at the discretion of the Government.
