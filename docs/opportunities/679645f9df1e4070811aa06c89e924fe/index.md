## Summary

NCTS Sicily L.NSC Department requires 54 rack-mounted uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) that support base routers, switches, and network gear. The requested UPS's will be used for the annual phase replacement of outdated and discontinued rack UPS in preparation for the 2026 ONE-NET Network Device Refresh (ONE-NET NDR). New Uninterruptable Power Supplies are needed for the protection of critical network assets necessary for mission functions for the NASSIG and tenant commands.
