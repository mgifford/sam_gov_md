# Tactical Weapons Training Facility Services for the United States Embassy in Jerusalem Israel

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 10:01:31.948-04
- Solicitation Number: 191S7026R0001

## Summary

Please note that this solicitation is not ready to be issued as of this date. The requirement is for providing the United States Embassy Jerusalem, Israel with Tactical Weapons Training Facility Services. The anticipated performance is for a base period of twelve months and four one-year periods at the discretion of the Government. All responsible sources may submit an offer, which will be considered. The solicitation will be published on SAM.GOV on May 4th, 2026. Note: Please carefully read the RFQ for the response date and time for proposal submission. A Pre solicitation conference will be held on 05/28/2026 at 10:30 AM (local Israel time) at The Embassy Branch Office Tel Aviv, Hertzeliya Annex building, 11 Galgalai Haplada St., Hertzeliya Pituach, Entrance B, 3rd Floor. Prospective offerors/quoters should contact Ronen Ben Eli at BeneliRR@State.gov for additional information or to arrange entry to the building.
