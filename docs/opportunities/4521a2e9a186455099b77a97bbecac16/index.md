# TRANSCOM CSO - Specific AoI 001: In-Transit Visibility Modernization for Global Defense Logistics

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 10:22:43.152-04
- Solicitation Number: TRANSCOM_CSO_Specific_AoI_001

## Summary

This posting is for the Specific Area of Interest (AoI) 001: In-Transit Visibility Modernization for Global Defense Logistics, under the USTRANSCOM Commercial Solutions Opening (CSO) HTC71126SCSO1. USTRANSCOM seeks innovative commercial solutions to modernize In-Transit Visibility (ITV) for global defense logistics. Responsive commercial solutions shall demonstrate a scalable, interoperable, and secure prototype solution that provides near real-time, end-to-end ITV of DoW cargo and assets in a controlled environment. The solution shall create a trusted Common Operating Picture (COP) by ingesting, fusing, and visualizing data from a variety of tracking sources. For detailed submission requirements, refer to the Specific AoI 001 – In-Transit Visibility Modernization for Global Defense Logistics attachment to this posting and the USTRANSCOM CSO (HTC71126SCSO1). As detailed in the Specific AoI 001 – In-Transit Visibility Modernization for Global Defense Logistics attachment, Phase 1 Solution Briefs must be submitted via the Vulcan platform. Companies may register for a Vulcan account at https://vulcan-sof.com/.
