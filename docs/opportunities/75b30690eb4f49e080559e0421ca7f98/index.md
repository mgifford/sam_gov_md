# CAIRS MIGRATION TO AIR FORCE STACK, BILLING, AND TRAINING AT YOKOTA AIR BASE, JAPAN

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 01:48:10.376-04
- Solicitation Number: FA520926Q0047

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and services necessary to install new CAIRS servers and successfully migrate associated data for the 374th Communications Squadron (CS) at Yokota Air Base, Japan. The work includes hardware installation, software configuration, database conversion, system migration, and comprehensive user training. The result of this effort will be a modernized, secure, and fully functional CAIRS and billing system, integrated as part of the Air Force’s enterprise CAIRS solution. All parts and materials must be compliant with the Trade Agreement Act (TAA)
