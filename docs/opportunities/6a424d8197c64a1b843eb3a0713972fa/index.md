## Summary

Sole source award made to InVeris Training Solutions Inc. for 30mm SRDT Delta Kits. This action was awarded on a non-competitive basis because InVeris Training Solutions Inc. is the only source capable of providing the specific Delta Kits that are compatible with existing proprietary systems, and the government does not possess the technical data rights to compete the requirement.
