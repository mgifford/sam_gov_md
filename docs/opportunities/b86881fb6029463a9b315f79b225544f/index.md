# Pre-Solicitation Notice-Clinical East IDIQ

- Posted: 2026-04-28 14:31:46.375-04
- Solicitation Number: PSCFOH-ClinicalEast-PSC348805

## Summary

The purpose of this synopsis is to provide notification of the proposed contract action under solicitation number: 7571MN26R00022 for the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), Program Support Center (PSC), Federal Occupational Health (FOH) Clinical East Staffing Services. The Government intends to release the solicitation in 3rd Quarter Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 through the System of Award Management (SAM.gov) as a Small Business set-aside. The purpose of this requirement is to secure continuing services for occupational health professionals to support Federal Occupational Health (FOH), a component within the Program Support Center (PSC) under the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). FOH defines Occupational Health Services (OHS) as the seamless coordination of comprehensive services using a data-driven approach to assist agencies in achieving a culture of health and high performance. FOH services are designed to improve health outcomes and employee productivity, while reducing occupational health risks, absenteeism, and overall employee health care costs. This requirement is a commercial service and will utilize FAR Part 12/15, as supplemented. The Government intends to issue a single award Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) for a period not to exceed five (5) years. Place of Performance: United States of America (USA) East region. This contract will cover work at various FOH Service Provision Sites (SPS) in the FOH Clinical Eastern Region (EAST) including, but not limited to: Alabama, Arkansas, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Vermont, West Virginia, Wisconsin, and Puerto Rico. Please do not call or submit questions/inquiries via email, concerning this presolicitation notice. Your interest in this requirement is appreciated.
