# Flooring IDR Sources Sought

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-20 16:14:42.971-04
- Solicitation Number: JFSD260019

## Summary

The 319th Contracting Squadron, Grand Forks AFB, North Dakota, is issuing this Sources Sought as a means of conducting market research to identify parties having the resources to provide the following: The work under this project consists of providing all material, equipment, and labor necessary for maintaining flooring/carpeting and other services necessary to perform Grand Forks Air Force Flooring IDR work for GFAFB in accordance with the attached specifications. The work may include, but is not limited to, the following: Verification of Dimensions, Flooring Removal, Patch flooring, Surface Preparation, Installation, Disposal of Debris, Furniture. Submission Instructions: Interested parties who consider themselves qualified to provide the above-listed service are invited to submit a response to this Sources Sought Notice by Friday, 5 June 2026, at 1600 CST. All questions and responses under this Sources Sought Notice must be emailed to SSgt Asante Nieves, Contract Specialist at asante.nieves@us.af.mil. For further information, please see attached: 1. Sources Sought 2. Statement of Work
