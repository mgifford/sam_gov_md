# BAE CAS BFN ATP Workstation Replacement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 15:12:27.146-04
- Solicitation Number: N00019-25-RFPREQ-TPM265-0854

## Summary

The Naval Air Systems Command PMA-265 intends to award a sole source, firm-fixed price (FFP) delivery order to BAE Systems, Information and Electronic Systems Integration (BAE Systems) under the Basic Ordering Agreement (BOA) (N0001922G0009) for supplies and services associated with the Combined Interrogator Transponder (CIT), and its related products – Conformal Antenna Subsystem (CAS) Beam Forming Network (BFN). The scope of work will support upgrading a Conformal Antenna Subsystem (CAS), Beam Forming Network (BFN) Acceptance Test Procedure (ATP) Workstation, upgrading/replacing antiquated computer hardware, test equipment and fixtures to the latest Windows operating system, as well as re-write, verify and validate test software as required to function with the new hardware operating system; in support of F/A-18E/F, EA-18G, and E-2D aircraft programs. The period of performance for this effort will be 24 months from award (FY26-28). This synopsis is for informational purposes only. THIS NOTICE IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE PROPOSALS. This acquisition is being pursued on a sole source basis under the statutory authority of 10 USC 2304 (C)(1) as implemented by Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 6.302-1, "Only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements." BAE Systems is the only company with unique knowledge, experience, special skills, facilities, manufacturing techniques, and technical data required to accomplish this effort. This announcement is for informational purposes only. Companies interested in subcontracting opportunities should contact BAE Systems directly.
