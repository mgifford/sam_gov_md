## Summary

The requirement was for the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), Office of Information Technology (OIT) has a requirement for the VA Informatics and Computing Infrastructure (VINCI) for one brand name NetApp C30 Storage Area Network (SAN) two-node storage array including the associated cabling, components, software, installation services, with maintenance and support.
