Salient Characteristics for 120 + Boom Lift A boom lift is a hydraulic aerial work platform that features a platform or bucket at the end of a jointed or telescopic arm (the boom ). This arm is mounted on a base, which can be stationary, self-propelled, or towable. They are designed to provide both vertical and horizontal reach, making them indispensable for a wide range of tasks where ladders or scaffolding simply aren t practical or safe. The aerial lift shall meet the following minimum performance requirements: Vertical Reach Must provide a minimum platform height of 120 feet. Horizontal Outreach Must provide no less than 70 feet of horizontal outreach under normal operating conditions. Platform Capacity Platform shall support a minimum unrestricted capacity of 500 lb, and A higher restricted capacity (e.g., for limited reach positions) of at least 750 1,000 lb. Articulating Jib Boom must include an articulating or telescoping jib capable of providing multi axis positioning adequate for precise placement at extreme height. Must provide wide range of vertical articulation sufficient for accessing overhead or recessed work areas. Powertrain Unit must be diesel powered and capable of reliable operation in outdoor industrial environments. Engine must provide sufficient horsepower and hydraulic performance to achieve the required lift speed, drive performance, and gradeability. Tires Unit must have foam filled, rough terrain tires suitable for jobsite surfaces and stable operation at maximum elevation. Ground pressure performance shall support safe operation on mixed terrain. Platform Rotation Must include platform rotation capability, minimum 150 180 degrees, to allow operator positioning.
