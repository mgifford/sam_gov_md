# WATER TOWER REFURBISHMENT

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 17:07:51.824-04
- Solicitation Number: 140G0126Q0123

## Summary

The USGS Wetland and Aquatic Research Cent located in Gainesville, FL has a requirement to refurbish a 10,000-gallon elevated water tank. It includes the following: - Repainting Exterior: Pressure washing, surface prep (Surface Preparation Standards #2 & #3 Hand or power tool cleaning removes loose rust, paint, and mill scale), primer and polyurethane finish. - Repainting Interior: Tank draining (by others), abrasive blasting to (Surface Preparation Standards #10 Near-White Metal), epoxy prime/finish coats, weld/ladders stripe coat, debris disposal. - Ladder Repair: Remove old ladder, install new 10-ft steel ladder with welded standoff brackets, paint per interior spec. - Work will take place in the following county in Florida: Alachua - Period of Performance is 30 days after notice to proceed is issued. - Request for Quotes (RFQ) 140G0126Q0123 will close May 22, 2026, at 10:00 am pacific time. - A site visit will be held on May 15, 2026, at 1:00 PM.
