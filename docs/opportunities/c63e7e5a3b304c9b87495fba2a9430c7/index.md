# AJW Portfolio-AJW Integrated Support Services (AISS), and Business, Financial and Administrative Support (BFAS) Requests for Information

- Agency: TRANSPORTATION, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-22 10:32:21.248-04

## Summary

This a Notice for all interest parties that the opportunity AJW Integrated Support Services or AISS is no longer being pursued. After careful consideration, the program office has decided not to proceed further with the AISS requirement. We extend our sincere gratitude to all parties who have shown interest and engaged with us in the past. Your contributions and responses have been invaluable, and we appreciate your dedication and efforts. We encourage you to continue monitoring SAM.gov for any future opportunities that may align with your interests and expertise. We are committed to keeping you informed through SAM.gov about any future opportunites Please note that the Business, Financial and Administrative Support (BFAS) requirement is being consolideted with the EFS requirement and will be solicited in the future. An RFI is pending and should be posted within the next couple weeks.
