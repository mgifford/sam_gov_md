# BPA Orders for Base Life Support Services in Indonesia

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 19:27:07.835-04
- Solicitation Number: W91QVPBLSSGS26

## Summary

Point of Contact: CPT Emily DeYoung @ emily.z.deyoung.mil@army.mil and MAJ Molly Libowski at molly.a.libowski.mil@army.mil. 0413 AQ HQ CONTRACT AUG 536 PALM CIRCLE DR BLDG T 122, FORT SHAFTER HI 96858-5100 Set-aside: No set aside - Performance is outside of the US and its outlying areas. PSC Code: W099 NAICS Code: 561210 Description: This is a Pre-Solicitation Notice for multiple contract actions (BPA Orders) for Base Life Support Service (BLS) in Indonesia across multiple locations during FY26 under the established Indonesia Base Life Support BPAs. Proposed BPA Orders include, but not limited to, Baturaja BLS, Gadot Subroto BLS, and Lampung BLS. Performance for the first contract action is templated to start as early as August 2026. Specific services for this Base Life Support contract include, but are not limited to, portable latrines, showers, hand wash stations, water tanks, black water removal, GRAY water removal, laundry, ice, reefers, indoor/outdoor lighting, power generation, air conditioning, fans, tentage, laundry, material handling equipment, sandbags, tables and chairs and other furniture, trash/waste/hazmat storage and removal, Van Transportation and Interpreter Services. BPA Orders will be competed IAW RFO 12.201-1(e)(3) and the Terms and Conditions of the BPA. All responsible sources solicited IAW RFO 12.201-1(e)(3) and the Terms and Conditions of the BPA may submit a quotation, bid, or proposal, as appropriate, which will be considered by the agency. Please contact the POC listed in bullet (1) if you are interested in being a BPA holder. BPAs are reviewed annually and the government may consider the possibility of adding additional BPA holders if it is in the best interest of the government.
