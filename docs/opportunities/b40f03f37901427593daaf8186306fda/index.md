# Subsea and Seabed Warfare and Robotic Autonomous Systems Industry Day

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-13 13:23:53.005-04

## Summary

SAVE THE DATE! Event: Industry Day -- NUWC Division, Keyport Focus: Subsea and Seabed Warfare and Robotic Autonomous Systems Date: June 15, 2026, 9:00am-3:00pm (PST) Location: Naval Undersea Museum, Keyport, WA To Our Valued Industry Partners: Please save the date for the upcoming Naval Undersea Warfare Center (NUWC) Division, Keyport Industry Day, focusing on Subsea and Seabed Warfare and Robotic Autonomous Systems. This event is designed to bring together government and industry stakeholders to discuss future capabilities, emerging technologies, and acquisition strategies within the undersea domain. Who Should Attend This event is highly recommended for industry partners, academic researchers, and technology developers whose work aligns with: Subsea and Seabed Warfare Robotic Autonomous Systems (RAS) Anticipated Highlights Include: Strategic briefs and capability updates from NUWC Keyport leadership. Insights into current and future undersea challenges and technological requirements. Opportunities to network with government representatives and fellow industry professionals. Next Steps: A detailed agenda and additional logistical information will be distributed in the coming weeks. We look forward to your participation and a productive exchange of ideas.
