## Summary

The Department of Veterans Affairs, Network Contracting Office 8 (NCO8) Orlando VA Healthcare System (OVAHS) is soliciting quotations from interested contractors to provide an on-site full-service Mobile CT Scanner/Simulator for the Orlando VA Medical Center (OVAMC) located at Lake Nona. Place of Performance: Orlando VA Healthcare System (OVAHCS), 13800 Veterans Way, Orlando, FL 32827 The Government will award a contract for a planned period of performance of six months and six option months: not to exceed one year. The solicitation is publicized as 100% Small Business Set-Aside. For the purpose of this acquisition, the NAICS code is 334510 and the small business size standard of 1,250 employees is applicable. All quotes and information must be received no later than: June 15, 2026, 4:00 pm EST. Email all quotes and information to the Contracting Officer, Victoria Robles at victoria.roblesburgos@va.gov. using the following title in the email s subject line: 36C24826Q0654 Mobile CT Scanner Orlando VA. Telephone inquiries will not be accepted or returned.
