# 2 ROPS Forklift and Flatbed Trailer Rental

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 16:59:49.552-04
- Solicitation Number: FA461026Q0025

## Summary

Regulatory Authority and Acquisition Strategy This notice is published in accordance with the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) 5.101 and 12.201-1 for acquisitions at or below the Simplified Acquisition Threshold (SAT), as supplemented by Department of War (DoW) guidance, including DFARS Deviation 2026-O0003 and 2026-O0028. Please note the following: This is a Presolicitation Notice, NOT a Request for Quotations (RFQ). A solicitation document does not currently exist and will not be posted to the Governmentwide Point of Entry (GPE / SAM.gov) or the PIEE Solicitation Module at this time. Following the conclusion of this mandatory 15-day posting period on 20 May 2026, the Government intends to directly solicit quotations from at least three (3) selected sources capable of meeting the requirement, in accordance with RFO 12.201-1(c)(2). The Government does not intend to award a contract based on this presolicitation notice or to otherwise pay for the information solicited herein. This notice is issued solely for informational and planning purposes. Instructions for Interested Parties Although the Government intends to directly solicit quotes from selected vendors after this notice expires, all responsible sources may submit a capability statement, proposal, or quotation, which shall be considered by the agency in determining the final list of vendors to solicit. Interested parties wishing to demonstrate their capability to fulfill this requirement must submit the following information via email no later than 09:00 AM Pacific Daylight Time (PDT) on 20 May 2026: Company Name, Address, and Point of Contact (Phone & Email) CAGE Code and UEI Number Business Size Standard and Socioeconomic Status under NAICS 532490 A brief capability statement (maximum of five (5) pages) demonstrating the ability to provide the requested equipment and meet the 4-hour maintenance response requirement. This statement should include relevant past performance examples if available. Submission and Contact Information: All responses and inquiries must be submitted electronically to the primary point of contact listed below. Telephone inquiries will not be accepted. Primary Point of Contact: TSgt Flores, Moises, Contract Specialist, moises.flores.1@spaceforce.mil
