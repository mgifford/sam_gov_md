# FTW480 FIRE PROTECTION REPAIRS

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 14:06:10.51-04
- Solicitation Number: W911KB26RA038

## Summary

THIS PROJECT IS TO BRING BUILDING 3030 LIFE SAFETY SYSTEMS INTO CODE COMPLIANCE TO PROVIDE AS COMPLETE AND USABLE FACILITY. SCOPE OF WORK WILL CONSIST OF REMOVAL AND REPLACEMENT OF THE FIRE SUPPRESSION SYSTEM, UPGRADE OF THE EMERGENCY LIGHTING SYSTEMS, AND NEW EXISTING PATHWAYS FROM OFFICE AREAS LOCATED WITHIN THE WAREHOUSE BAYS.
