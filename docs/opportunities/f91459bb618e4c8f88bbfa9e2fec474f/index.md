# Fort Randall Custom Shallow - Water Boat and Trailer

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 10:39:04.511-04
- Solicitation Number: W9128F26QA063

## Summary

This contract is for the fabrication, purchase, and delivery of a custom boat, trailer, and motor package built to the provided Government specifications. This equipment is required to support the Threatened and Endangered (T&E) Species bird monitoring program at the Fort Randall Project. All fabrication and construction shall be performed at the contractor's facility. The contractor's sole on-site requirement is the final delivery of the completed package to 113 Randall Creek Road, Pickstown, SD 57367, which shall include providing comprehensive instruction on the operation and characteristics of the boat, trailer, and motor.
