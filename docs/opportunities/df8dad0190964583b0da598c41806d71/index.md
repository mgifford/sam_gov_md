# Fuel System Maintenance Service Contract

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-31 05:24:16.889-04
- Solicitation Number: PR16012652

## Summary

PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE: The U.S. Embassy Algiers intends to issue a solicitation for “Fuel System Maintenance Service” on or around June 15, 2026. The solicitation will close on or around June 30, 2026. The U.S. Embassy in Algiers seeks a qualified vendor to provide preventive maintenance services for its Fuel Management System. The contractor shall maintain all systems covered under this agreement in good operational condition. The contract will be for a period of one-year, with a maximum of four one-year optional periods of performance. No documents or other information will be available until the solicitation is issued. Point of contact: Please direct your questions to Algiers_Procurement@state.gov, telephone questions will not be accepted.
