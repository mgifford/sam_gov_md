# Notice of Intent to Award BPA Call Orders Against the Land Management Integrated Resources BPA (LMIR)

- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 09:06:10.379-04

## Summary

NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD A BPA CALL ORDER Requisition Number: 1169820 Title: BPA Call Order Exceeding $20,000 – Synopsis Solicitation The U.S. Department of Agriculture, Forest Service (USFS), intends to issue a competitive Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) Call Order under the Land Management Integrated Resources (LMIR) BPA for requirement 12444726Q0028 – Perform targeted chemical control of non-native invasive plant species (NNIS) located at the Cherokee National Forest. The Land Management Integrated Resources (LMIR) BPA, 12363N23Q4023, is a national Blanket Purchase Agreement that provides a broad range of professional and technical services. These services include, but are not limited to, natural resource restoration, project management, engineering, NEPA, communications, and related support. The BPA is available for use by all Forest Service units and all federal agencies. This is not a solicitation. This notice is issued solely as a synopsis of the Government’s intent to award a BPA Call Order. This notice does not constitute a request for proposals, quotations, or any submission of information. This procurement is restricted to LMIR BPA awardees eligible to receive call orders under the LMIR BPA for the U.S. Forest Service. The LMIR BPA utilizes a continuous Presolicitation process. Contractors may request to join the BPA at any time while it remains active. Interested firms may contact the LMIR BPA Team at: SM.FS.lmirbpa@usda.gov Original BPA documents and attachments can be found on SAM.gov at: https://sam.gov/opp/8f26188b0e7445b3b4f9bb71484448f4/view (under “Attachments/Links”) Questions regarding this notice must be submitted to: laniece.nixon@usda.gov Deadline for questions: 11:59 PM ET, June 4, 2026.
