# 6515--FY26 | VISN 8 Vascular Coils IDIQ

## Summary

This is a Source Sought Notice only. This notice is for market research purposes only to identify businesses capable of providing a requirement. This is not a solicitation. This notice does not obligate the Government to award a contract. A solicitation/quote will not receive a response. The Government will not pay nor reimburse any costs for preparing this response. This notice is to gain knowledge of potential qualified businesses and their Small Business Administration (SBA) size classification in relation to North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code 339113, Surgical Appliance and Supplies Manufacturing with an associated size standard of 800 employees. The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), Network Contracting Office (NCO) 8, is seeking interested sources to provide Vascular Coils manufactured by Penumbra as consignment agreement under an Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity contract for a base of twelve months and four-twelve-month options. If your business can meet or exceed the requirement, please provide a capability statement. The capability statement shall be limited to no more than two pages and must clearly state the following: Capability in industry to manufacture or provide the required Vascular Coil (Penumbra). Offerors who cannot provide historical information from their company regarding the outlined attachment or similar scope and scale should provide corresponding information to show how the company would meet the VA medical facility requirements. This information should provide level of confidence in the Government that an offeror would meet or exceed the delivery requirements to provide the required product. In addition, provide and answer the following: 1. Company name, address, point(s) of contact, title, telephone number, fax number, email address, cage and SAM UEI number. 2. Company s business size, to include number of employees. 3. Company's socioeconomic status. 4. If SDVOSB/VOSB is planning to provide a response they need to demonstrate the ability to provide a product manufactured by a small business to be considered capable. Submit this information to Contracting Specialist, Jose Hernandez, via email to jose.hernandez@va.gov. Your response to this notice must be received in writing on or before 4:00 PM (Eastern Standard Time) on May 18, 2026.
