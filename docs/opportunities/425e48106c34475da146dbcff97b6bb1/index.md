# Sources Sought notice - JMMU Range at Fort Hood,TX

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 16:02:51.669-04
- Solicitation Number: DOIDFBO260051

## Summary

This Sources Sought notice is issued by the government to acquire information regarding able and available contractors to perform design and execute repairs and modernization of the Jack Mountain Multi-use (JMMU) Range on Fort Hood, TX. Contractors who choose to respond to this notice do so voluntarily with no expectation of reimbursement of any kind at any point. The government is looking for active System of Award Management (SAM) registered small businesses with the NAICS 236220 in their SAM profile. In the past, this work has been performed by Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Businesses (SDVOSB). Each respondent is asked to submit a capabilities statement reflective of their ability to do the work as outlined in the draft Statement of Work (SOW) attached to this notice, including all base and optional tasks, along with proof of their active SAM registration. The resulting contract will be awarded using Best Value. The disclosure of magnitude for this project is between $5M and $15M, which includes all base and optional tasks. Responses to this notice are due no later than May 15, 2026, at 1:00PM (EDT) and shall be emailed to David Boyd at david_boyd@ibc.doi.gov. No questions will be answered regarding this notice.
