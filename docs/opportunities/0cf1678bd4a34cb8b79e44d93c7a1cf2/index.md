# Grand Jury Court Reporting Services; U.S. Attorney's Office, District of Idaho

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF

## Summary

The Executive Office for United States Attorneys (EOUSA) on behalf of United States Attorney’s Office, District of Idaho(USAO-ID), contemplates the Multiple Award of an Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract for Grand Jury court reporting services. The award of this IDIQ will assist USAO-IDin fulfilling the mission for United States Attorneys to serve as the nation’s principal litigators under the direction of the Attorney General. Anticipated award date is in June 2026.
