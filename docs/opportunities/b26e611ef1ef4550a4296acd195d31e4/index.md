# B2316 Steam Boiler Replacement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 10:37:54.887-04

## Summary

Contractor is to provide all labor, materials, services, oversight, and direction to complete the construction/installation of the requirements identified in the Statement of Work (SOW) to replace the current 50 horsepower steam boiler with one 25 horsepower steam boiler at B2316, the Mountain Home AFB Dining Facility.
