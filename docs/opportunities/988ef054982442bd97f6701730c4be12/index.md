# Residential Reentry Center Located Within the City limits of Raleigh, North Carolina. Home Confinement Radius is Within 200 miles of the Facility

## Summary

This announcement is posted as a Request For Information (RFI) only for an intended upcoming solicitation that will be posted on the SAM.gov website. This RFI is being issued to determine whether or not there are qualified sources that will be able to meet the requirements of this intended upcoming solicitation. This RFI is for Residential Reentry Center (RRC) to include Home Confinement (HC) placements for male and female offenders located within the city limits of Raliegh, North Carolina. The Home Confinement radius is 200 miles of the facility. Please see the attached RFI document and SOW.
