# Market Research - Air Traffic Control Tower, Warner Robins, AFB

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-29 13:48:19.991-04
- Solicitation Number: W912HN26BA007

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE), Savannah District, is conducting market research in regard to a potential Small Business Set-Aside for the construction of an Air Traffic Control Tower under NAICS 236220 - Commercial and Institutional Building Construction with a size standard of $45M. The project will be located at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia. Scope Summary - Construct a new Air Traffic Control Tower consisting of reinforced concrete foundations and footings, brick veneer and insulated metal panel exterior walls, load-bearing masonry for the ground floor and structural steel frame for the tower and sloped standing seam metal roof. Functional areas include offices for personnel, training and briefing area, ready/breakroom, simulator room, elevator, mechanical/electrical/communications rooms, storage area, restrooms, and control cab. The new facility will include appropriate power, 750KW backup generator, heating ventilation and air conditioning, fire and lighting protection, and communications systems. The project includes all utilities, site preparation, site improvements, pavements, and all necessary work for a complete and usable facility.
