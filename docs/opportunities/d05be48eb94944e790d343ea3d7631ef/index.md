# RFI: Integration of Advanced Medium Mobile Power Sources (AMMPS) Kits

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-27 12:05:22.492-04
- Solicitation Number: W909MY-26-R-TRLR

## Summary

The Department of the Army is conducting market research in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Part 10 to identify sources capable of integrating Advanced Medium Mobile Power Sources (AMMPS) Kits onto either the 1-ton Light Tactical Trailer Heavy Chassis (LTT-HC) or the 2.5-ton M200A1 trailer. The Government owns the competitive Technical Data Package (TDP) for this integration, which will be provided as Government Furnished Information (GFI) under subsequent competitive action. This RFI is for informational and planning purposes only and does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP) or a promise to issue an RFP in the future. The Government will not award a contract on the basis of this RFI, nor will it pay for any information submitted by respondents.
