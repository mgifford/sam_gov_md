# Upgrade Target Electrical Boxes, Range 117/118, Grafenwoehr Training Area (Project # TI-00623-5P)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-29 10:06:50.066-04
- Solicitation Number: W912PB26QA060

## Summary

1. The project includes the following work: - Remove and dispose of 365 pieces receptacle connection boxes from all existing target facilities on Range 118 and the south half of Range 117 and shooting range boundaries and cameras of the range complex. - Install and connect 365 new socket connection boxes suitable for the existing cable length. - Renew flexible grounding cables in ca. 365 target pits. - Renew of flexible Schuko plug cables to the junction distributor. 2. The solicitation will result in a Firm Fixed Price contract. 3. The performance period is 250 calendar days, whereby a commencement time of 20 calendar days is included. 4. The solicitation will be posted on or about 18 May 2026 with an anticipated bid opening date of approximately 23 June 2026. Paper copies will not be available 5. All responsible sources may submit a bid, which will be considered by the RCO Bavaria. 6. Work will be performed in Germany. Contractor must comply with German law and must have authorization to do business in Germany.
