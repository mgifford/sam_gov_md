## Summary

The Mission and Installation Contracting Command Installation Contracting Office, West Point, NY (MICC-West Point) is issuing this SYNOPSIS for Wireless Access Points at Camp Buckner for the Army CHESS IT Platform In order to be eligible for award, business must be registered as an Army CHESS IT Small Business Concern Contract Holder, on the Army CHESS IT Platform, per the AFARS Part 5139- Acquisition of Information Technology(a)(1).
