# Combat Air Force Contracted Air Support (CAF CAS)

## Summary

The objective of the Combat Air Forces (CAF) Contracted Air Support (CAS) program is to obtain non-personal services to provide realistic advanced adversary air threats from industry Adversary Air (ADAIR) sources to support pilot combat readiness training and Contracted Close Air Support (CCAS) sorties in order to facilitate Joint Terminal Attack Controller (JTAC) training. Contractor ADAIR and CCAS services are needed to support both CONUS and OCONUS operations within the Air Force and DoD. CAF CAS contractors shall support multiple customers/locations across the CAF and shall provide complete CAS services including, but not limited to: aircraft; aircraft systems support; pilots; aircraft maintenance; support equipment; program, quality and contract management to meet contract requirements.
