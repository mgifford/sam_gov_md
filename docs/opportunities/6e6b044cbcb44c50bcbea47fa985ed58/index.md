## Summary

The University of California, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (“University” or “LBNL”) requests a proposal for the supply and delivery of flexible hoses and associated hose end connection components required for the new Storage Ring (SR) low-conductivity water (LCW) cooling system, in accordance with this RFP, Sample Subcontract, and other enclosures.
