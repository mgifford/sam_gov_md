## Summary

Phase 1 Breakwater. This contract involves constructing the initial phase for the restoration of Deer Island, specifically restoring the extension of the rock breakwater structure known as Katrina Key, approximately 12,000 feet, to soften the effects of normal wave erosion. This restoration includes placement of armor layer stone or bioengineered reef as the breakwater and associated geocomposite placement to return Katrina Key to near historical 1850’s dimensions.
