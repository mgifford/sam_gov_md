# Tyndall Base Custodial Services IDIQ

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 13:08:51.516-04
- Solicitation Number: FA481926R0018

## Summary

The 325th Contracting Squadron at Tyndall AFB, FL, is issuing this RFI for the purpose of locating qualified 8(a) business firms that would have the capabilities to provide all management, tools, equipment, and labor necessary to ensure that custodial services are performed at Tyndall AFB, in a manner that will maintain a satisfactory facility condition and present a clean, neat and professional appearance. The contractor shall accomplish all cleaning tasks and be required to provide and replenish soap and paper products in all restrooms identified in Appendices A in order to meet the requirements of this PWS and the Service Summary (SS). Maximum cleaning frequencies are established in Appendix A, Air Force Custodial Common Levels of Service Standards. All work performed by the contractor shall be performed in accordance with AFCOLS 3 standards, all applicable laws, regulations, HAF standards, instructions, and commercial practices. The 325th Contracting Squadron intends to award a single-award IDIQ contract. The Ordering Period will consist of a one-year base period with four (4) one-year option periods at the Task Order Level. Individual requirements will be fulfilled via Task Orders issued on a Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) basis.
