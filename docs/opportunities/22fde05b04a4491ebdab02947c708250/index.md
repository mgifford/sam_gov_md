# Architect-Engineer (A-E) Services IDIQ for Offutt AFB

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 09:46:24.957-04
- Solicitation Number: FA4600R0004

## Summary

The 55th Contracting Squadron at Offutt Air Force Base, Nebraska, is issuing a Combined Synopsis/Solicitation for Architect-Engineer (A-E) services in accordance with the Brooks Act (FAR Subpart 36.6). The Government intends to award up to four (4) single-award, Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) Indefinite-Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) contracts to provide a full range of multi-disciplinary Title I, Title II, and other specialized engineering services. These services will support sustainment, restoration, modernization, and new construction projects primarily at Offutt AFB and its associated off-site facilities in eastern Nebraska. Acquisition Strategy & Contract Structure: Set-Aside: This acquisition is a 100% Total Small Business Set-Aside. NAICS Code: 541330 (Engineering Services), with a Small Business Size Standard of $25.5 Million. Period of Performance: Each contract will consist of a five (5) year base period and one (1) five (5) year option period. Contract Ceiling: The maximum capacity for each IDIQ contract is $49,000,000. Pool Structure: To ensure the Government has access to highly specialized expertise, the contracts will be awarded across four (4) distinct pools of work. A firm may submit qualifications for one or more pools; however, a firm will only be awarded a maximum of one (1) contract. The pools are: Pool 1: Specialized Engineering & Consulting (e.g., Blast, RF Shielding, SCIF/SAPF, Acoustics) Pool 2: Mechanical & Electrical Engineering Pool 3: Civil & Structural Engineering Pool 4: Architecture & Renovation Key Requirements: Offerors are advised that this solicitation includes stringent mandatory pass/fail requirements that must be met at the time of submission, including: A geographic requirement for a design office located within a 200-mile radius of Offutt AFB. A requirement to possess a current Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Level 2 self (or higher) in the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS). Submission Instructions: This is NOT a Request for Proposal (RFP). Interested firms must submit a Statement of Qualifications using Standard Form (SF) 330. For complete details on the scope of work, mandatory requirements, evaluation criteria, and specific instructions for submitting an SF330, please download and carefully review the attached document titled "Combo Synopsis-Solicitation_Offutt AE IDIQ." Submission Deadlines: Questions regarding this solicitation shall be received no later than 08 May 2026 at 3:00 PM Central Time. Questions received after this deadline may not be answered. SF330's shall be receieved no later than 27 May 2026 at 3:00 PM Central Time.
