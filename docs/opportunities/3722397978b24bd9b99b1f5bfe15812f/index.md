# Demolition Services Dodd Hall, Facility 448

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-08 15:57:45.803-04
- Solicitation Number: FA480026Q0047

## Summary

THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE FOR INFORMATION AND MARKET RESEARCH PURPOSES ONLY. This is not a Request for Quote (RFQ) or a Request for Proposal (RFP), and it does not constitute a commitment by the Government. No contract will be awarded from this notice. The Government is seeking to identify qualified 8(a) Certified Small Business concerns under NAICS 238910 (Site Preparation Contractors; size standard: $19M) capable of performing the complete demolition of Dodd Hall (Facility 448) located at Joint Base Langley?Eustis (JBLE) – Langley, VA. The anticipated scope of work includes, but is not limited to: Complete structural demolition and removal of Facility 448. Abatement and proper disposal of hazardous materials (e.g., asbestos, lead-based paint) prior to and during demolition activities. Site clearing, debris removal, and final site restoration in accordance with base and environmental regulations. The purpose of this notice is to gather information to determine the availability and capability of 8(a) firms for this future requirement to support an 8(a) set-aside or direct award strategy. PLEASE REFER TO THE ATTACHED DRAFT STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) AND SOURCES SOUGHT INSTRUCTIONS FOR DETAILED INFORMATION ON THE ANTICIPATED REQUIREMENT AND HOW TO SUBMIT A CAPABILITY STATEMENT.
