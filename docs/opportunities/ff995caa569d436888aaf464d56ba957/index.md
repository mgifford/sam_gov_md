# United States (US) Marine Corp Enterprise Commercial Satellite Services (MECS2)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 09:48:25.378-04
- Solicitation Number: FA2541-25-R-B005
- Awardee: INMARSAT GOVERNMENT, INC. Reston VA 20191 USA
- Award Amount: 307102288.00

## Summary

Inmarsat Government, Incorporated (Reston, Virginia) was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for global communication connectivity on the United States Marine Corps (USMC) Enterprise Commercial Satellite Services (MECS2) Contract. The work is expected to be completed by 16 March 2031. This contract was a full and open acquisition, without any restriction on competition. The Space Systems Command Commercial Space Office, Chantilly, Virginia, is the contracting activity.
