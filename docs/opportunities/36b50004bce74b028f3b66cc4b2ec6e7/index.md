# The contractor shall provide all management, tools, supplies, equipment, uniforms and labor necessary to provide Vehicle Maintenance (VM) uniform lease and laundering services for the 6th Logistics Readiness Squadron (LRS) at MacDill AFB, Florida.

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 07:06:54.629-04
- Solicitation Number: FA481426Q0015

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all management, tools, supplies, equipment, uniforms and labor necessary to provide Vehicle Maintenance (VM) uniform lease and laundering services for the 6th Logistics Readiness Squadron (LRS) at MacDill Air Force Base, Florida, in accordance with all applicable federal, state, local laws and regulations and this performance work statement (SON). Vehicle Management Technician Coverall Uniforms (Avg. 25 Technicians) -Quantity: 7 sets per technician. Each set shall consist of one (1) top, one (1) bottom. Only one (1) jacket per individual. Also, at least (50) red shop rags per month will be needed.-Description: Three-piece, dark blue work uniform, comprising of one (1) short-sleeve shirt, (1) matching pant, and (1) matching jacket. The material shall be a durable breathable fabric blend comparable in quality to Carhartt and Dickies brands.
