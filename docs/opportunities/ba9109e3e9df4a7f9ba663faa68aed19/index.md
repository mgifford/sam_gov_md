# FDO Exterior Door Canopies

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 10:01:27.853-04
- Solicitation Number: 140L6326Q0006

## Summary

The Bureau of Land Management, Alaska State Office has a requirement for fabrication and installation of three entry canopy structures in Fairbanks, Alaska. Detailed information will be included in the solicitation package. The solicitation will be available at https://sam.gov/ on or about May 21, 2026. Offers will be due June 4, 2026. The solicitation is subject to the provisions of the Federal Acquisition Regulations (FAR) Part 12. This procurement is a set-aside for small businesses. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) Code for this procurement is 238190 size standard $19 million. Offerors must have a Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) number and register at www.sam.gov (System for Award Management) to be eligible for award of any federal contact. Subscribers to this service are responsible for reviewing this site for all information relevant to this solicitation. Prospective offerors will be required to visit this website periodically to check for amendments and other changes to the solicitation documents. No other notification will be sent. Offers will be accepted at the email address listed in the solicitation.
