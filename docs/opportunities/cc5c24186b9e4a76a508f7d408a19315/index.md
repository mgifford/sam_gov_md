# Sources Sought for Fourteen (14) digital storage oscilloscopes (DSOs)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-21 13:45:45.01-04
- Solicitation Number: W911QX26QA085

## Summary

The Contractor must provide fourteen (14) digital storage oscilloscopes (DSOs) in accordance with the below minimum requirements of the Government stated below: Must provide eight (8) entry-level DSOs having four analog input channels with At least 200 megahertz (MHz) bandwidth At least two (2) gigasamples per second (GS/s) sample rate At least two (2) megapoints (Mpts) memory depth At least eight (8)-bits of Analog to digital conversion (ADC) resolution A display of at least seven (7) inches (in) Must provide four (4) mid-level DSOs having four (4) analog input channels with At least 200-megahertz (MHz) bandwidth At least five (5) GS/s sample rate At least four (4) megapoints memory depth At least eight (8)-bits of Analog to digital conversion (ADC) resolution A display of at least 12 inches Must provide two (2) high-level DSOs having eight (8) analog input channels with At least 500 MHz bandwidth At least 16 GS/s sample rate At least 100 megapoints per channel memory depth At least 10-bits of Analog to digital conversion (ADC) resolution A display of at least 15 inches Must be Keysight Technologies, Inc authorized reseller
