## Summary

Replace roofs and gutters on two Peaks of Otter buildings. The roofs and gutter systems on these two buildings have exceeded their useful life and need replacement. The buildings in this project are the Peaks of Otter Ranger Office and the Peaks of Otter Maintenance Shelter. Both buildings are located in the Peaks of Otter sub-district.
