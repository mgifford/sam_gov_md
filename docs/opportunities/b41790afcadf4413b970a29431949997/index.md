The provisions and clauses incorporated in this solicitation are those in effect through the Federal Acquisition Regulation and Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement as of the date of issuance and are included in the attached SF 1449. The Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren Division (NSWCDD) intends to procure Systems Modeling Language (SysML®) training classes by GEORGIA INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY of Atlanta, GA 30308-1031. Refer to Statement of work and SF1449 for details. Training is required from GEORGIA INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY due to their familiarity with NSWCDD’s existing MBSE environment, tools, and instructional history. This continuity is essential to maintain consistent modeling conventions, avoid retraining or re-baselining the workforce, and ensure seamless progression. These foundational courses are essential to meeting current and emerging tasking requirements from multiple sponsor organizations and are the prerequisite pathway for more advanced training. All costs associated with this requirement must be included in the quoted price. This includes shipping and freight to Dahlgren, VA 22448-5114 as well as any tariff or duty charges. No additional costs may be added after award. Where applicable, DFARS 252.225-7013, Duty-Free Entry, will be incorporated.
