Requirement: The purpose of this RFI is to identify qualified vendors, who are able to meet Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), Veterans Health Administration (VHA), Veterans Integrated Service Network 8 (VISN 8) requirement for brand name Cleo Streem software licenses, application maintenance and technical support. Background: The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), VHA, VISN 8 has a requirement for brand name Cleo Streem software licenses, installation, application maintenance and technical support. The required Cleo Streem solution provides secure network fax capabilities and electronic faxing to automate workflow utilizing secure faxing, secure file transfer, communications and messaging capabilities. As a standard solution at many VA facilities, the Cleo Streem solution can be seamlessly integrated with multifunctional devices as an alternative to analog faxing. This feature eliminates the need for analog phone lines and modems in the multifunctional devices. The Cleo Streem solution provides the ability to securely fax documents directly to and from the desktop, eliminating the need for excess usage of paper and consumables. The Contractor shall provide technical support, project management services and training services to enable the solution contained. The Local VA Office of Information and Technology (OIT) will request two virtual servers configured with Windows Server 2019 or 2022 operating systems and will provide remote access to virtual servers and technical assistance as needed to Contractor personnel, who will perform the installation and testing of the solution remotely. OIT will also ensure that the required data feed from VA is sent securely to the Contractor s servers. The virtual servers will be managed by the VA, and standard server maintenance (such as operating system patching, backups, and related tasks) will be performed by VA personnel. The vendor will be responsible for supporting the application, in coordination with the Community Care application administrators.
