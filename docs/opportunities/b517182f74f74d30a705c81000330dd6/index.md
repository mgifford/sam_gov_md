# Sole Source Award to Petterson Pope Inc.

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-02 09:35:10.36-04
- Solicitation Number: M6700425Q0056

## Summary

The Marine Corps Logistics Command, Contracts Department (CONT) intends to solicit and contract with Patterson Pope Inc. (CAGE 1H7V5) of Charlotte, North Carolina on a sole-source basis for the purchase of maintenance and repir parts for the ActivRac P30 system with option years. The North American Industry Classification Standard (NAICS) code assigned is 811310 - Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment (except Automotive and Electronic) Repair and Maintenance. No solicitation will be posted on this website for this requirement. A Justification and Approval document is attached to this notice. Any potential source may contact the persons indicated above to discuss their ability to offer the required services for future acquisitions
