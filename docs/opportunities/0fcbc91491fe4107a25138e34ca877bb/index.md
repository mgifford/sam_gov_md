## Summary

Country: Mozambique Project Name: Rural Connectivity and Transport (CTR) Procurement Title: Design and Build (D&B) of the New Licungo River Bridge (1.8 Kms) and Mocuba Bypass Road (17.2 Kms), in Zambezia Province - Mozambique Procurement Ref. Number: MOZ-26-2002 Type of Procurement: Design & Build Selection Method: Quality and Price Based Selection (QPBS) Accountable Entity: Millennium Challenge Account – Moçambique Publication Date: April 28, 2026 Submission Deadline: August 3rd, 2026 1. Millennium Challenge Account-Moçambique (MCA-M) has received financing from the Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) for the Connectivity and Rural Transport Project (CTR) and intends to apply part of the proceeds towards payments under the contract for Design and Build (D&B) of the New Licungo River Bridge (1.8 Kms) and Mocuba Bypass Road (17.2 Kms), in Zambezia Province – Mozambique in order to undertake the detailed design and execution of works as per the Employer`s Requirements. 2. The project is in the Zambézia Province of Mozambique on the N1 national road that forms the key backbone of the primary road network in Mozambique and is the main link between the north and south of the country. 3. MCC’s funding is appropriated by the U.S. Congress and obligated to the Compact II up-front, with no incremental or partial funding. So, when a contract is signed with an Accountable Entity, money is already available to the Accountable Entity and, for most contracts, invoices are paid directly to Offerors by the US Treasury. 4. Bidding will be conducted through the Quality and Price-Based Selection (“QPBS”) procedure as specified in the MCC Procurement and Policy Guidelines (PPG) and is open to all eligible offerors as defined in the PPG. 5. Offerors interested in receiving a complete set of bidding documents should be registered by using the following link: https://forms.office.com/r/tzz6a2yWXE 6. A Site visit will take place on May 14th, 2026, and May 15th, 2026, as specified in the ITO 8.2. 7. A Pre-Offer Conference will be held at 3:00 pm Mozambique time on May 22nd, 2026, via an online event specified in the ITO 8.4. 8. Offers must be delivered electronically to https://u.pcloud.com/#/puplink?code=j8eXZzWAP74lER0jASydQu7gm9QNDK0Yy on or before 3:00 pm Mozambique time, on August 3rd, 2026. Late offers will be rejected. Offers will be publicly opened in the presence of the offerors designated representatives and anyone who chooses to attend August 3rd, 2026, at 4:00 pm Mozambique time via an online event specified in the ITO 25.1. 9. Clarifications may be requested by e-mail to the Procurement Agent (mcamocambiquepa@dt-global.com) and responses shall be provided ten (10) days before the date of submission of Offers.
