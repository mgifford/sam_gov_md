# Refrigerator Repair Service

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-29 16:02:41.585-04
- Solicitation Number: 1SOCONS-PKB-Dorm_Refrigerator

## Summary

Hurlburt Field is seeking information for Market Research purposes only. This is a Request for Information and does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP) or a promise to issue an RFP in the future. This RFI does not commit the Government to contract for any supply or services. Further, the Air Force is NOT seeking proposals at this time. Hurlburt Field is attempting to identify qualified contractors authorized to repair Absocold refrigerator model #ARD1033FW21R/L. Units were purchased in 2024 and are no longer cooling. Contractor must be able to provide all labor, parts and materials. To be considered qualififed, a contractor must have an active SAM.gov registration, and must have certifications/qualifications to perform repairs on specified units. *****UPDATE***** The Air Force is seeking information from LOCAL vendors who have the ability to make service calls. Local area should be within a 50 mile radius of Hurlburt Field, FL 32544.
