# Tower installation engineering and services

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-30 09:14:55.454-04
- Solicitation Number: D-26-ST-0159

## Summary

The Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) intends to award a purchase order on a sole source basis to Installation Services, LLC.,427 Borden Avenue, Sycamore, IL 601, to provide tower infrastructure subject matter expert (SME) solutions. This notice of intent is not a request for competitive quotations. A Request for Quotation (RFQ) is not available. The Class Justification for Other than Full and Open Competition form is attached with this notice. The acquisition will be conducted through FAR Part 13, Simplified Acquisition Procedures. The NAICS code is 237130 and the requirement is not a total small business set-aside. A determination by the Government not to open the requirement to competition based on the responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. See attached for details.
