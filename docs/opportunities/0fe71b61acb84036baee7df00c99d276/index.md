# Notice of Intent to Sole Source - ACS Clinical Congress 2026 Booth Space

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-26 10:58:54.608-04

## Summary

Notice of Intent to Sole Source The U.S. Army Mission and Installation Contracting Command (MICC) - Fort Knox, 199 6th Avenue, Suite 250, Fort Knox, KY, 40121, intends to award a sole-source, firm-fixed-price contract to the American College of Surgeons (ACS) (UEI: MNSSA5BU44K9 | CAGE Code: 8L533), located at 633 N Saint Clair St, Chicago, Illinois 60611-3234. The procurement is for one (1) 20' x 30' (600 sq ft.) convention booth space at the American College of Surgeons (ACS) Clinical Congress 2026, to be held at the Walter E. Washington Convention Center, 801 Allen Y. Lew Place NW, Washington, DC 20001. This action is being conducted under FAR Part 12 (DFARS CD 2026-O00028), Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services. The statutory authority permitting other than full and open competition is simplified acquisition procedures for commercial products and commercial services, Title 41 U.S.C. 1901, as implemented by FAR 12.102(a) (DFARS CD 2026-O0028), Only one source is available valued at or below the simplified acquisition threshold. This is not a request for competitive offers, quotes, or capability statements. No solicitation will be posted as a result of this notice. This notice complies with FAR 5.101 (DFARS CD 2026-O0003), Methods of disseminating information. However, interested parties may submit capability statements demonstrating their ability to meet this requirement within the required timeframe. All capability statements must be received no later than June 04, 2026, at 10:00 AM Eastern Time. Submitted capability statements shall include sufficient detail to demonstrate the ability to meet the requirement, including relevant experience, pricing information (if available), and supporting documentation. Submissions shall be sent via email to Contract Specialist, Emily H. Ryan at emily.h.ryan.civ@army.mil and Contracting Officer, Andrea M. Skilinski at andrea.m.skilinski.civ@army.mil. The anticipated award date is June 2026.
