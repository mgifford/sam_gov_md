## Summary

The Department of Veterans Affairs, Network Contracting Office 8 (NCO8) Orlando VA Healthcare System (OVAHS) is soliciting quotations from contractors to provide Data and Voice cabling services for the Orlando VA main campus and its Outpatient Clinics. The Government will award a contract with a planned period of performance of a 5 year ordering period. The solicitation will be publicized as 100% Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business Set-Aside. For the purpose of this acquisition, the NAICS code is 238210 and the small business size standard of $19M is applicable. All quotes and information must be received no later than: May 28, 2026, 4:00 pm EST. Email all quotes and information to Victoria.roblesburgos@va.gov, Contracting Officer using the following title in the email s subject line: 36C24826Q0448 Data and Voice Cabling. Telephone inquiries will not be accepted or returned. Place(s) of Performance: Orlando VA Healthcare System (OVAHCS), 13800 Veterans Way, Orlando, FL 32827 Includes outpatient clinics as stated in the performance work statement (PWS). See attached Solicitation.
