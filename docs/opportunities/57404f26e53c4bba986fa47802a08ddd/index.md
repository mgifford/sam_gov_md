# Repair/Replace Traffic Lights

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 12:05:36.223-04
- Solicitation Number: FA446026Q0383

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all qualified personnel, labor, transportation, tools, equipment, materials and services required repair/replace the traffic light controls and cameras at the intersections of Arnold Drive at Cannon West and Arnold Drive and Cannon East on Little Rock AFB, AR 72099. The contractor shall accomplish all services below to meet the requirements of this Performance Work Statement (PWS) to include training of CE personnel on the system programming, use and maintenance. All work shall follow all Federal, State and local regulations, codes and the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD).
