## Summary

The purpose of the RFI Amendment is to extend the RFI responses due date through May 4, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. Eastern time, and to post all questions and answers resulting from the question-and-answer period of the RFI. The Q&A porting of the RFI is concluded. VA is seeking to identify qualified contractors and identify brands of nursecall systems with a code blue communication system capable of integrating with existing Hill-Rom hospital beds without the use of middleware to capture all the required bed data as well as other patient/room data and provide that information in a visual dashboard accessed via the workstations located at the nurse stations.
