# Intent to Sole Source- RPS-62 Radar Systems

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 16:22:25.762-04
- Solicitation Number: M67854-26-I-0025

## Summary

Program Manager Ground Based Air Defense (GBAD) within Portfolio Acquisition Executive (PAE) Mission Systems (MS) Marine Corps (MC) intends to negotiate and award on a sole-source basis in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 6.103-1 to RADA Technologies, LLC’s (hereafter referred to as RADA), 20511 Seneca Meadows PKWY STE 100 Germantown, Maryland 20876 United States, for the procurement of non-commercial RPS-62 radar systems, software/firmware releases and associated licenses, spares, ancillary components, maintenance and repairs, and engineering services for the Marine Air Defense Integrated System (MADIS) program. The RPS-62 radar system is a readily available solution that has been integrated within the size, weight, and power parameters of the JLTV, to include all electrical and mechanical interfaces. The RPS-62 radar system is also compatible with the MADIS software baseline. The RPS-62 radar system is the only known and currently available solution that satisfies Product Manager Future Weapon Systems’ (PdM FWS) requirements. The Government anticipates award of a hybrid Cost-Plus-Fixed Fee/Firm-Fixed-Price contract, with a period of performance of five years. This acquisition is being pursued on a sole source basis under the statutory authority of 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(1), as implemented by Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 6.103-1(c)(2)(iii) "Only One Responsible Source and No Other Supplies or Services Will Satisfy Agency Requirements.” RADA is the designer, developer, and producer of the RPS-62 radar system. This is not a request for competitive quotes. No formal solicitation package is available. The Government will not be responsible for any cost incurred in responding to this notice; however, interested parties may identify their interest and capability to meet the requirements and past performance data no later than 1000 local time on 11 June 2026. Responses received will be evaluated; however, a determination by the Government not to compete the proposed procurement based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. All responses to this notice shall be submitted to Ms. Natalia Bruno at natalia.bruno@usmc.mil and Mrs. Megan Brady at megan.brady.civ@usmc.mil. Please reference the Notice ID on all responses. Telephone calls will not be accepted.
