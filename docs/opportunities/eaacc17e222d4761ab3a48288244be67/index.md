# Hot Weather Combat Boots

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 11:19:22.529-04
- Solicitation Number: 26-P-043744

## Summary

The U.S. Army Contracting Command-Aberdeen Proving Ground (ACC-APG), an agency of the Government, intends to procure a Qty. 5,000 pair Hot weather combat boots in support of the U.S. Army Security Assistance Command (USASAC). This requirement is for Foreign Military Sales (FMS) for the country of Tunisia under Case TU-B-UVE. The Government anticipates the issuance of a single Firm Fixed Price (FFP) contract as a result of subject synopsis/solicitation. The anticipated date of award to be determined but no later than 12 June 2026. Comparison P/N: 8177- (corresponds to Belleville Boot brand) Required Sizes: 5.5,6.5,7.5,8,8.5,9,10,11,13 Required Color: COYOTE Material: LEATHER Special Features: Berry Amendment Compliant 8" Standard Military Height Backward-Facing Forefoot Seams & Tendon Relief Dip Construction-Direct Attach with Reinforced Stitched Toe & Heel Tabs Insole-Removable/Washable Cushioned Orthotic Footbed Hardware-Nylon-Coated Speedhooks & Eyelets Upper material-Genuine Cowhide Suede & 1000D Cordura™ Nylon Meets all AR 670-1 and AFI 36-2903 specifications
