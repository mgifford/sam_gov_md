# Comprehensive Business Financial Capability Assessment Licenses

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 19:59:30.177-04
- Solicitation Number: S5121A26PE002

## Summary

The successful vendor shall provide web-based comprehensive company financial capability assessments to lower the Government’s risk of poor contract performance through access to company-provided financial statements, publicly available financial information, industry comparison studies, credit history information and market analysis information to include a comprehensive review of a company’s financial capability to support is current and future workload and assessment of the probability of a company encountering liquidity or solvency problems. 24 Licenses required.
