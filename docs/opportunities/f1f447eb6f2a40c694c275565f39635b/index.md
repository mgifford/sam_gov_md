# FY26 507 ARW Upgrade existing CCTV system, Bldg 1066

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-13 10:11:17.376-04
- Solicitation Number: FA813626P0007

## Summary

Upgrade to existing video surveillance CCTV system on the 507th Air Refueling Wing's campus to support the Command Post (CP) in bldg 1066. Current surveillance equipment and software has reached end of life. The lack of adequate video surveillance for the 507th ARW hinders situational awareness for the Commander, does not provide safety of both Air Forces assets and personnel, and prevents security of assets. The Command Post requires the ability to monitor assets and provide security for personnel. The product and services acquired from this effort will support the 507th ARW CP's mandated requirement for closed circuit viewing of the facility, the flight line, and the alert ramps supporting multiple MAJCOM missions of aircraft assigned to the 507 ARW on Tinker AFB. The specific software for the security surveillance system is proprietary and solicitation from another security service provider will incur significant cost increases due to having to replace the entire camera system and infrastructure used by Digi Security Services on Tinker. The system configuration, architecture, and deployment design are proprietary to DIGI. The software is maintained by Motorola and they assign dealer licenses to integrators. DIGI is the only authorized integrator to provide support to the existing system. A new provider would not have access to the current licensed environment and would require full system reconfiguration and redeployment. The camera system operates on Tinker AFB network and was cleared by all applicable Air Force Information Technology entities for use. The initial approval timeline for the original installation took approximately 60 months to clear all security approvals.
