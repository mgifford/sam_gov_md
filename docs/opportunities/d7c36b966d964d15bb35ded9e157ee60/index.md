# Communication Services Support

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-29 10:53:43.615-04
- Solicitation Number: ACQ-26-1014

## Summary

The USPTO is performing market research for professional media communication services which may include, but are not limited: Drafting media strategies and communications plans for USPTO; Drafting press releases and media advisories; Supporting media relations and coordinating interviews, when applicable; Monitoring media coverage and providing reports; Producing content for social media, USPTO staff communications, and USPTO internet postings; Training for USPTO staff on social media, communication strategies, and other related topics; and Assessing USPTO’s communication skillsets and performing a gap analysis to determine needed skillsets.
