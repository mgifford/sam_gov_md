# Catholic Religious Education Coordinator

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 14:14:32.575-04

## Summary

The 45th SLD Chapel has a requirement for Catholic Religious Education Coordinator support to the Catholic worship community on Patrick SFB, FL in accordance with PWS, dated 24 Mar 26. The period of performance for this requirement will be a base + four(4) options with one 6-MO option to extend services. Services include daily masses, weekend masses and confessions.
