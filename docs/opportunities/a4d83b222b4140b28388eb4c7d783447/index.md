## Summary

This is a COMBINED SYNOPSIS/SOLICITATION for commercial items prepared in accordance with the information in FAR Subpart 12.2, using Simplified Acquisition Procedures (SAP) found at FAR Part 12, as supplemented with the additional information included in this notice. This combined synopsis/solicitation SHALL be made available on the following websites: System for Award Management (SAM) - https://www.sam.gov/ The reference number is N0018926QL131. The instant requirement is to provide EBSCO subscription database service and Engineering Core eBook Subscription to support NAVOCEANO.
