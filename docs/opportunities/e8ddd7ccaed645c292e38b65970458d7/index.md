# Telephone Service

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 11:03:42.408-04
- Solicitation Number: RFQ2600021

## Summary

The National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA), a bureau within the U.S. Department of Commerce and the President’s principal advisor on telecommunications and information policy, holds statutory responsibility for managing frequency assignments for federal agencies. In support of this critical mission, NTIA operates a remote facility in Gettysburg, Pennsylvania, which serves as a vital component of its Continuity of Operations (COOP) strategy. The NTIA intends to establish a contract for telephone services to support remote facilities located at 1270 Fairfield Road, Gettysburg, PA 17325, and TMFS 8600 N 39th St. Longmont, CO 80503. The designated service provider will be responsible for ensuring the continuous, reliable, and uninterrupted delivery of telephone service to these mission-critical sites.
