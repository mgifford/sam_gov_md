# Fire Alarm Suppression and Testing - Timber Lake Job Corps.

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-08 12:00:53.868-04
- Solicitation Number: 1282A726Q0022

## Summary

Job Corps Centers serve students and thus require life safety systems to be operational at all times to ensure safety of building occupants in the event of an emergency. This effort will provide all personnel, labor, equipment, tools, materials, instruments, meters, gauges, test equipment, transpo1tation, supervision, and other items and services necessary to perform inspection and testing (11) for the Fire Alarm Systems (FA) & Fire Suppression Systems (FS) for the Timber Lake Job Corps Center located in Estacada, Oregon.
