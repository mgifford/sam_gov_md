## Summary

NAVSUP Weapon Systems Support (NAVSUP WSS) Philadelphia intends to solicit on a sole source basis with Bright Lights USA, INC’s (0RMF7) for the procurement of a supply. \\ Six (6) Spares of NSN: 1680 014834393, P/N: 901-266-008-101, TOOL COMPRESSION FU\\ There are no drawings/data available.
