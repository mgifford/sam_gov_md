# VFA Site Survey/Activation

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 15:42:19.447-04
- Solicitation Number: N00019-26-RFPREQ-TPM265-0624

## Summary

The Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR) at Patuxent River, MD intends to award a Firm Fixed Price (FFP) contract to Northrop Grumman Systems Corporation, Melbourne, FL, on a sole source basis. This contract will provide for the performance of a Site Survey, Mx Academics training to one squadron, Logistics Consumables as well as Peculiar Support Equipment. Northrop Grumman is the only source with the requisite knowledge, experience, and technical data required to successfully complete the Site Survey and Training without causing unacceptable delays in meeting the Navy’s operational requirements. The authority permitting other than full and open competition is Title 10, U.S.C. § 3204(a)(1), as implemented by FAR 6.103-1, "Only One Responsible Source," and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. This synopsis is for informational purposes only and is not a request for competitive proposals. Interested parties may identify their interest and capability by responding to this synopsis. The Government will consider all capability information received prior to the closing date. Information received will be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement. A determination by the Government not to compete based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. There is no commitment by the Government to issue any solicitation, make an award, or be responsible for any monies expended by any interested party before award of a contract for the efforts detailed above. Information provided herein in no way binds the Government to solicit for or award a contract. If you have any questions regarding this synopsis, please contact Shannon Buckalew at shannon.r.buckalew.civ@us.navy.mil or Jessica Myers at jessica.v.myers.civ@us.navy.mil.
