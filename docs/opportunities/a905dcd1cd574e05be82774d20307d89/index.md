# PreSolicitation Notice_19NP4026Q0022_PMSC of New WTP (Chlorine and ClO2 Component)

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 06:39:39.075-04
- Solicitation Number: 19NP4026Q0022

## Summary

The United States Department of State requires preventive maintenance services for the electrochemical chlorine and chlorine dioxide generator systems installed at the Singh House Compound, U.S. Embassy Kathmandu, Nepal. These systems are manufactured by Dioxide Pacific. The water treatment systems to be maintained are as follows • Electrochemical Chlorine Generator (Dioxide Pacific, Model: HGB-2.5) • Chlorine Dioxide Generator System (Dioxide Pacific, Model: CDE-10) • Associated Dosing Pumps (SEKO, Model: Spring MS1B) The equipment is manufactured by Dioxide Pacific. The contractor shall provide all logistics, customs, shipping, transportation, labor, water treatment chemicals, tools, water treatment testing kits/equipment, administrative and all associated management support functions necessary for the maintenance of these systems.
