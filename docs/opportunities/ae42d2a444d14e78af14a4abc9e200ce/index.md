# RFI - UMTE X Band Transmitter

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-19 13:05:57.359-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8210-26-RFI-UMTE

## Summary

Unmanned Threat Emitter (UMTE) X Band Transmitter The Government is conducting market research to identify potential industry sources capable of providing solutions to consolidate and replace obsolete, single-function transmitter systems. Currently, the Government relies on multiple ground-based threat emitter platforms (designated TK-1, TK-2 and TK-4) which utilize legacy transmitters. See attached RFI document for more information. The drawings are CUI and require approval for access to unclassified military technical data.
