# Notice of Intent to Sole Source - Comcast Government Services (Hardwired Cable Services)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-22 14:36:26.757-04

## Summary

NOTICE OF INTENT TO SOLE SOURCE. This notice is being published in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 5. This is a notice of intent to award a sole source contract and is not a request for competitive quotes/proposals. The statutory authority for this notice is 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(1) set forth in FAR 6.103-1, Only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. Supervisor of Shipbuilding, Conversion Repair, Groton (SSGR) intends to award a firm fixed price contract with base year and 4 option years to Comcast Government Services, LLC, 11951 Freedom Drive, Suite 900, Reston, VA 20190 for hardwired cable television services. All other responsible sources may submit a capability statement clearly demonstrating the ability to provide the required service in accordance with the specification before 7:00 A.M. EST on 08 JUNE 2026, which may be considered by the Agency for a future award. Responses to this notice shall be submitted to stephanie.l.neale.civ@us.navy.mil and andrea.a.cook.civ@us.navy.mil. The Government will not pay for any information received. A determination by the Government not to compete this proposed contract based on responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. No solicitation will be posted as a result of this notice. This special notice is issued for informational purposes only. The justification and approval for sole source is attached.
