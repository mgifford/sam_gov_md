# US Consulate Sao Paulo - Brazil - Purchase of two Official Vehicles

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 08:42:06.898-04
- Solicitation Number: 19BR9326Q0017

## Summary

The United States State Department has a requirement for contractors to provide two (02) Jeep Commander or similar vehicle. You are invited to submit a quotation. Scope or Supplies: Jeep Commander or similar Must be manufactured for the Brazilian market Transmission: Automatic Transmission Electronic stability system Anti-lock braking system (ABS) Antislip regulation (ASR) or traction control system (TCS) Brake assist (BAS) Number of doors: 4 Spare tires: one (1) Intermittent wipers: front & rear Dual Air Bags Anti-theft system: alarm & immobilizer Minimum number of seats: 7 Seat material: Leather or Suede Only vehicles from the current model year (2026/2027) will be considered Payment method: EFT - Electronic Funds Transfer - Net 30 days after the delivery of vehicle. Important Note: The government will not handle any import procedures for the vehicle. Interested parties must have an active SAM registration and provide their Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) along with their expression of interest. The State Department plans to award a Purchase Order. You are encouraged to make your quotation competitive. You are also cautioned against any collusion with other potential offerors with regard to price quotations. This announcement does not commit the State Department to make any award. The State Department reserves the right to change or cancel this announcement, or any part of it, without notice. If you or your firm are interested in participating in this process, and to receive a copy of the requirements, you must send an email message to SaoPauloProcurement@State.gov with the title "RFQ - 19BR9326Q0017” no later than 15 calendar days after this announcement is posted online. Please see attached the RFQ. Upon receipt of the requirements, please read it carefully, and if interested,submit your quotation. Oral quotations will not be accepted. Sincerely, Sao Paulo GSO Procurement
