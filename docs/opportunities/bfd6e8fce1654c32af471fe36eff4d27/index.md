# Fabricate and Install Cooling Tower Platforms

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 14:37:47.159-04
- Solicitation Number: 1232SA26Q0544

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1232SA26Q0544 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for the fabrication and installation of cooling tower platforms. Contractor will be required to design, fabricate, and install cooling tower service platforms. All platforms will be hot dip galvanized after fabrication. Platforms will have handrails, safety gates at stairs, tie points, and any other required safety equipment. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 332999, All Other Miscellaneous Fabricated metal Product Manufacturing. The small business size standard is 750 Employees. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
