Must perform the following tests: pH, pCO2, pO2, measured total hemoglobin (tHb), measured oxygen saturation (sO2), oxyhemoglobin (O2Hb), carboxyhemoglobin (COHb), methemoglobin (MetHb), actual bicarbonate (cHCO3-), actual base excess (ABE), potassium (K+), sodium (Na+), ionized calcium (Ca2+), chloride (Cl-), glucose (Glu), lactate (Lac) Reports all tests performed in less than a minute. Provides detection of hemolysis. Provides a measured Total Hemoglobin (tHb) and Oxygen Saturation (sO2) instead of calculated values. This provides a more accurate result minimizing the need for unnecessary, expensive, blood transfusions and possible injury to the patient. The data manager must conform to the VistA Lab Point of Care HL7 specifications. Does not require an Individual Quality Control Plan (IQCP) as prescribed by CMS guideline 493.1256. Has three individual onboard internal aqueous quality control solutions (separate from calibration material) as well as external aqueous quality control solutions that follow the same sample pathway as actual patient samples including the inlet probe. This ensures the most accurate patient results repeatedly. All-in-one, multi-use room temperature storage cartridge containing all components for analytical testing. This reduces inventory management and improves staff efficiency. Consumables must have no less than 31-day onboard in-use life Contains an integrated barcode scanner, automatic inlet, automatic blood clot detection, air detection and parameter lockout with failure of QC or calibrations. Capable of utilizing safePICO syringes containing metal mixing balls which assist in mixing the sample and anticoagulant in the syringe. This help ensure samples are well mixed and helps to standardize the way staff mixes a sample resulting in improved patient results. Capable of utilizing the safePICO syringes whose needle shield design maximizes operator safety. The self-sealing cap tip does not need to be removed from the syringe to run the sample therefore minimizing operator contact with patient blood. The self-sealing cap tip allows the operator to quickly remove air bubbles which can affect patient results.
