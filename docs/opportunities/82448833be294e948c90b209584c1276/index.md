# Red Ribbon Week Paraphernalia - Intent to Sole Source

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-12 14:52:22.397-04
- Solicitation Number: FA446026Q0379

## Summary

Notice of Intent to Sole-Source: The 19th Contracting Squadron (CONS) – Little Rock Air Force Base, Arkansas intends to award on a sole-source basis a contract to NIMCO Inc. Innovations & Education to purchase Red Ribbon Week paraphernalia for distribution to 25 schools surrounding Little Rock Air Force Base. NIMCO Inc. is the sole manufacturer and distributor of this product, therefore, making them the only source capable of fulfilling this requirement. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE OFFERS and a solicitation will not be issued. All responsible sources may submit a quote which shall be considered. Any response to this notice must show clear and convincing evidence that competition of this requirement would be advantageous to the Government. All responsible sources may submit a capability statement, proposal, or quotation, which shall be considered by the agency. If no affirmative written response is received, the award will be made without further notice. Oral communications are not acceptable in response to this notice. RESPONSES ARE DUE NO LATER THAN 27 May 2026 by 2:00 PM CST at 19 CONS, Little Rock AFB to 2d Lt Luke O. Kohout, email at Luke.Kohout.2@us.af.mil.
