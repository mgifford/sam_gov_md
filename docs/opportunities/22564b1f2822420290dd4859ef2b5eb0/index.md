# Walla Walla VA Replacement of Four Evoqua (Brand Name or Equal) Chlorinator cell cartridges

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 19:29:24.276-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C26026Q0625

## Summary

This is for the acquisition of four Evoqua (Brand Name or Equal) Chlorinator cell replacement cartridges for a public water purification system at Walla Walla VA Medical Center. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 36C26026Q0625 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for the acquisition of four Evoqua Chlorinator cell replacement cartridges for the Evoqua/W&T OSEC L, 20 PPD chlorine generator. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov.
