# Cancellation Notice - Route 1 Road Repair

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 17:03:46.951-04

## Summary

The purpose of this notice is to announce the cancellation of Request for Quotation (RFQ) 127EAW26Q0013 for Route 1 Road Repair, effective immediately. The Government is currently reassessing and refining its requirements, and a new solicitation may be issued at a later date. No quotes will be accepted or evaluated under this RFQ. The Government appreciates the interest shown by industry and thanks all parties for the time and effort invested in this opportunity.
