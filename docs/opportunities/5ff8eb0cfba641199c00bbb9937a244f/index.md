# Mobile Telephone Service for the U.S. Embassy Riga, Latvia

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 04:26:07.185-04
- Solicitation Number: 19LG7526Q0002
- Awardee: LATVIJAS MOBILAIS TELEFONS Riga LVA

## Summary

This contract provides U.S. Embassy Riga with mobile voice, SMS, and data services, including international roaming, for approximately 150-185 Embassy-issued lines. Services support both physical SIM and eSIM technologies in Latvia, the United States, the European Economic Area (EEA), and globally.
