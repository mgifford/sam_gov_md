Solicitation Number: N6523626QE234Title: ARC TECHNOLOGIESThe purpose of this synopsis is to provide notice of Naval Information Warfare Center (NIWC) Atlantic?s intent to award a Firm-Fixed-Price purchase order on a noncompetitive basis to ARC TECHNOLOGIES for Radar Absorbing Material.
