# USTRANSCOM IT Programs and Support Services Division – Industry Forum

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-01 14:55:07.837-04
- Solicitation Number: TRANSCOMIF26

## Summary

DATE: Friday, June 26, 2026 TIME: 0900-1200 Central Time (Tentative) Please mark your calendar for our Summer FY26 Industry Forum, which will be held at Scott Air Force Base (AFB) on June 26, 2026. An agenda is in development and currently includes the traditional acquisition updates. The forum will be held at the Scott Community Center, located at 47 Ward Drive, Bldg. 1560, Scott Air Force Base, IL 62225 and attendance is open to all contractor representatives. Anyone planning to attend who does not have access to Scott AFB is required to submit a visit request to the email below by June 18, 2026. The request must include the following information: Full name, valid driver’s license number, driver's license state of issue, and date of birth. Due to the coordination process, visitor requests received after this date are not likely to be approved. VISITOR REQUEST EMAIL: transcom.scott.tcaq.mbx.d-industry-day@mail.mil Please continue to monitor this notice for updates, to include the final agenda and additional details on the event. Slides will be made available as attachments on this announcement following the event. Additionally, please submit any suggested topics or discussion items via email at transcom.scott.tcaq.mbx.dindustry-day@mail.mil no later than June 1, 2026.
