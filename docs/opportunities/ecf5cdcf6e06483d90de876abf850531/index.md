# 2040 HATCH ASSEMBLY

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 12:44:44.571-04
- Solicitation Number: N0040626Q0211

## Summary

Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound (FLCPS) intends to solicit for Hatch Assembly supplies: HATCH ASSEMBLY 01-6-1, RAISED WATERTIGHT INDIVIDUALLY DOGGED HATCH WITH WATERTIGHT QUICK ACTING SCUTTLE. All responsible sources may submit a quotation, bid, or proposal, as appropriate, which will be considered by the agency. Quotes will be evaluated in accordance with RFO 12.203, the Government will select the most advantageous offer based on the following evaluation factors: technical including delivery date, price, and past performance in no order of relative importance. Drawing, references and TGIs will not be provided or furnished as part of this solicitation due to distribution restrictions. Drawings, references and TGIs are available to quoters listed on the Joint Certification Program (JCP) website. Quoters may request these by providing their CAGE code and DD-2345 for their data custodian. For instructions on how to register for JCP please go to https://www.dla.mil/Logistics-Operations/Services/JCP/ . Anticipated Award Date: June 2026 Contracting Officer: Lorraine Small / Lorraine.m.small.civ@us.navy.mil
