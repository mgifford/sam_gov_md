# Mission Support - WMD Threat Reduction Consultation Services

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 09:22:08.492-04
- Solicitation Number: 19AQMM26R168

## Summary

This is a Solicitation Notice for Weapons of Mass Destruction (WMD) threat reduction advisory and assistance consulting services. This WMD threat reduction advisory and assistance consulting service is an instrument for synchronizing efforts with different government agencies and host countries centered in preventing and reducing global WMD threats through border protection, nonproliferation, counter-smuggling activities, removing, destroying, or disabling WMD, conventional munitions, or other dangerous materials. Questions regarding the solicitation is due on/before Tuesday 19 May 2026 at/before 11:00AM EST. Proposals is due on/before 22 June 2026 at/before 11:00AM EST. The Government do not intent to extend the proposals due date.
