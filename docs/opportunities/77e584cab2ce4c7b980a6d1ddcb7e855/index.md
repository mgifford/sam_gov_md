# Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-30 13:18:48.787-04

## Summary

Revision #10.0 to W519-23-S-CTSM. Effective 05/01/2026. Please Note Revision #9.3 was removed on 04/30/2026. All submissions on 05/01/2026 and after shall be in accordance with Revision #10.0. Please read the full Tradewind Solutions Marketplace Open Call Revision #10.0 attached to this announcement as several areas have been updated. Any and all questions regarding the Tradewind Solutions Marketplace shall be submitted via success@tradewindai.com. The Department of War (DoW) Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO) in conjunction with its marketplace manager, the Applied Research Institute (ARI), is initiating this long-term, open call solicitation for solutions to support the DoW in advancing research, development, experimentation, prototyping, test, procurement, and sustainment of artificial intelligence and machine-learning (AI/ML), digital, and data hardware, software, and service solutions. Specifically, the CDAO seeks to obtain solutions or potential capabilities that fulfill requirements, close capability gaps, or provide potential technological advancements. This initiative, entitled the “Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace” will solicit, collect, assess, and curate AI/ML, digital, and data solutions, and make those solutions available through rapid acquisition pathways.
