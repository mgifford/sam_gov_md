## Summary

Edward Hines Jr. VA Hospital (Hines VA) requires a full-service plan with DirectTV, which provides high-definition digital satellite television service. The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to provide DirecTV channels as defined in this Statement of Work (SOW) except for those items specified as government furnished property and services. The contractor shall perform in accordance with the standards in this contract.
