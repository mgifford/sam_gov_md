# FTQW235100 Repair Hangar Door B1340

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 16:09:42.456-04
- Solicitation Number: FTQW235100

## Summary

Repair of two tip-up aircraft hangar doors at Building 1340, Eielson Air Force Base (AFB), Alaska. The work includes repairs to mechanical operating systems; replacement of counterweight systems; door insulation and cladding; installation of heated hangar door thresholds; and all incidental and related work necessary to provide fully functional, operable, and weather-tight hangar doors. A site visit will be conducted on 29 April 2026 at 10:00am AKDT Participants will meet at B1340 parking lot, 628 Cargain Road, Eielson AFB, Alaska. Names of attendees to 354CONS.PKA.MonitorConstruction@us.af.mil with B1340 Hangar Doors site visit in the subject line due by 5:00pm AKDT on 28 April 2026. Questions are due no later than 01 May 2026 at 2:00pm AKDT All questions will be answered in the form of an amendment to the solicitation posted to the SAM.gov website.
