INTRODUCTION / BACKGROUND. Commander, Navy Region Hawaii (CNRH), Code N6, currently operates a proprietary Lenel OnGuard Access Control System, Version 8.2, supporting the physical security infrastructure at Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam (JBPHH). The Government is conducting market research to identify capable commercial sources that can provide the required Lenel OnGuard Enterprise software upgrade to Version 8.3, associated software licensing, database upgrades, and professional support services necessary to maintain and support the existing system environment. The required upgrade is necessary to: Maintain system stability and operational capability; Support native M-Series hardware panels; Maintain compatibility with Microsoft SQL Server 2022; and Ensure continued manufacturer support and software compatibility within the existing proprietary Lenel OnGuard architecture. The Government is contemplating a brand-name only acquisition for Lenel OnGuard Enterprise software and associated support services due to compatibility, interoperability, licensing, cybersecurity, warranty, and operational requirements associated with the existing installed system environment. However, this Sources Sought notice is being issued for market research purposes in accordance with FAR Part 10 to determine whether capable commercial sources exist that can satisfy the Government’s minimum requirements. Interested vendors are encouraged to review the attached draft Statement of Work (SOW) for additional details regarding the contemplated requirement. 2. NAICS CODE. The applicable North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is anticipated to be: 561621 – Security Systems Services (Except Locksmiths) The associated small business size standard is $25.0 million. 3. SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS. Interested sources capable of meeting the requirements identified in this notice shall submit a capability statement not exceeding three (3) pages, excluding cover page and table of contents. Responses shall be concise and specifically address the Government’s requirements. Capability statements should address the following: a. If proposing an alternative to the Lenel OnGuard software upgrade, provide: Manufacturer name; Product name and version; Part numbers and specifications; and A detailed explanation demonstrating how the proposed product would satisfy the Government’s minimum requirements and integrate with the existing Lenel OnGuard system environment without degrading system functionality, compatibility, manufacturer support, warranty coverage, cybersecurity posture, or operational capability. b. Identify whether your company is: The manufacturer; An authorized distributor; or An authorized Value Added Reseller (VAR) for LenelS2 products and services. c. Address how the proposed solution would maintain compatibility with the currently deployed Lenel OnGuard system architecture, databases, hardware panels, firmware, licensing structure, and existing integrations utilized at JBPHH. d. Provide the following company information: Company name and address; UEI and CAGE Code; Point of contact information; Business size under the identified NAICS code; Small business socioeconomic status, if applicable; and Confirmation of active SAM.gov registration. e. If your company holds a GSA Federal Supply Schedule (FSS) contract, provide: GSA contract number; Applicable Special Item Number(s) (SINs); Whether the proposed products/services are available under the schedule contract; and Rough order of magnitude pricing information for market research purposes only. f. Vendors may provide questions, comments, or recommendations regarding the draft requirement, acquisition strategy, or draft SOW. 4. MARKET RESEARCH PURPOSE. This notice is issued solely to support acquisition planning and market research efforts in accordance with FAR Part 10. Information received may be used to: Assess industry capability and interest; Determine whether adequate competition exists; Evaluate potential small business participation; Assist in determining the appropriate acquisition strategy; and Support any future justification regarding the use of brand-name specifications or restricted competition, if applicable. 5.
