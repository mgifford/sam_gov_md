## Summary

General: The North Florida / South Georgia VA Healthcare System Pathology and Laboratory Department has a Brand Name or Equal requirement for the procurement of one (1) Automated Osmometer. This new equipment will ensure continuity of care, for the Pathology and Laboratory Department. Scope: An automated osmometer simplifies and speeds osmolality testing, reducing manual steps and variability making it a vital tool in scientific and clinical settings.
