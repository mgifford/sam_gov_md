## Summary

Saguaro National Park (SAGU) manages septic tanks, lift stations, and vault toilets in both the Rincon Mountain District and the Tucson Mountain District. These systems support nearly one million annual visitors, staff, and operational facilities including visitor centers, administrative complexes, heli-base operations, and roadside vault restrooms. Regular pumping and maintenance are required to ensure public health, safety, and compliance with environmental standards. See attached specifications for additional details. This acquisition is 100% for Total Small business Set-Aside. Anticipated Base Year Period of Performance is 6/15/2026 through 6/14/2027. Actual award will determine Period of Performance. Price List includes Base Year plus four (4) Option Years.
