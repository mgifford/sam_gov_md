# C-130H Formation Position System Draft RFP

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-12 12:50:22.915-04

## Summary

This special notice is for C-130H Formation Position System (FPS) Engineering and Manufacturing Development (EMD) and Production and Deployment (P&D) support. The purpose of this notice is to provide potential Offerors information regarding the program requirements and to facilitate industry review and maximize the quality of market research. THIS INFORMATION IS FOR PLANNING PURPOSES ONLY AND IS SUBJECT TO CHANGE. The Government encourages questions, comments and feedback to the documentation attached to this notice. As applicable, this notice will be updated based on continued industry engagement and feedback. This notice does not constitute a formal solicitation, Request for Proposals (RFP), Request for Quotations (RFQ), or a promise to issue a formal solicitation in the future. No contract will be awarded from this notice, and the Government will not reimburse any costs associated with submitting information in response to this posting.
