# LTV MX Repair BPA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 15:01:30.784-04
- Solicitation Number: FA448426Q0019

## Summary

The 621st Contingency Response Group (CRG) utilizes Lightweight Tactical Vehicles (LTVs) to conduct mission essential work throughout Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst (JBMDL), NJ and locations worldwide. The 621st CRG has 24 LTVs to perform these mission critical duties. This solicitation is for a Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) to provide parts, maintenance, inspections and repairs to 24 Polaris Lightweight Tactical Vehicles (LTVs). Contractor shall be within 100 miles radius of JBMDL, NJ.
