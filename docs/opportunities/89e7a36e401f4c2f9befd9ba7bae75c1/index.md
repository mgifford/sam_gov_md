## Summary

This solicitation is for the contractor to furnish all materials, supplies, tools, parts, equipment, transportation, labor, supervision, communication, and quality control to provide Mowing and Maintenance at CJ Brown Dam and Reservoir in Springfield , Ohio. All work shall be performed in strict compliance with the attached specifications and all other terms, clauses, and conditions incorporated herein. Quotes are due on 15 May 2026 at 10:00 AM Eastern Time.NOTE: THIS RFQ IS 100% SET ASIDE FOR SMALL BUSINESS. The NAICS code for this procurement is 561730. The size standard is $9.5M.The solicitation will be available on the web only. Telephone and Fax requests for this solicitation will not be honored.
