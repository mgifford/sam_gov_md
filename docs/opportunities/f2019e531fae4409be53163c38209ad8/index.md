- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 14:21:53.164-04

## Summary

The purpose of this synopsis is to provide notification for the intended acquisition of Gleason GMS300 gear tester software and training. The software and training is for one (1) 300GMS Gear Metrology System Software Upgrade used at the Defense Supply Center Columbus (DSCC), DLA Land and Maritime, Product Test Center Division, Mechanical Lab, Columbus, Ohio. For technical specifications and a proposed Statement of Work (SOW), please see Attachments 1 & 2. NOTE: This is only a notification. A formal solicitation is expected June 2, 2026.
