# Delano Hall -USMMA under ground storage removal

- Agency: TRANSPORTATION, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 09:00:37.807-04
- Solicitation Number: 6923G2Delano

## Summary

DOT/MARAD has a requirement for The United States Merchant Marine Academy (USMMA) for a New York licensed contractor to remove three (3) steel underground storage tanks (UST) that contain No. 2 Fuel Oil. Two (2) 20,000 and one (1) 10,000 gallons tanks in accordance with all Federal, State and local laws and regulations. Permanently remove from service and seal associated piping from the tanks to the equipment they serve and remove all wiring and components associated with the operation and monitoring of the tanks. These tanks are located on the southern side of Delano Hall and eastern side of O’Hara at United States Merchant Marine Academy (USMMA), 300 Steamboat Rod, Kings Point, NY 11024. See attached draft Statement of Work. Responses are due by Thursday, May 21, 2026 at 1pm EST. All responses including the comments on the draft SOW, capabiliyt statement and responses to the questionnaires must be emailed to durba.ray@dot.gov; cc brian.wiley.ctr@dot.gov before 1pm EST on May 21, 2026.
