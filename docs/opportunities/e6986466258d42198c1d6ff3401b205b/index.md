# Sole Source Award - Infusion Pump Server and Software

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 07:42:45.479-04
- Solicitation Number: 26-HRCO-54
- Awardee: CAREFUSION SOLUTIONS, LLC San Diego CA 92130 USA
- Award Amount: 121384.54

## Summary

The Health Readiness Contracting Office awarded a Firm-Fixed Price Purchase Order to Carefusion Solutions, LLC 3750 Torrey View CT San Diego, CA 92130 to provide the Blanchfield Army Community Hospital (BACH), Fort Campbell Kentucky with a Virtual Server, Software, and its associate implementation services to establish a centralized management, monitoring, security, and continuous quality improvement (CQI) data analysis of the facility's government-owned infusion pumps and Progressive Care Unit (PCU) devices. This system is a critical component of modern patient care and safety protocols. The requirement includes annual server software support and maintenance. This contract includes a base year period of performance plus four one-year option periods, total contract period of performance from 1 May 2026 through 30 April 2031. See attached Sole Source Justification for details.
