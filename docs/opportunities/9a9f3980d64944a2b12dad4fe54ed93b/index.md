# INL/Haiti: Modular Prisons

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-13 15:52:47.358-04
- Solicitation Number: 191NLE26N0088

## Summary

The U.S. Department of State, Bureau of International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs (INL), is conducting market research to identify qualified sources capable of designing, fabricating, and delivering 80 double-bunk stackable modular "SteelCell" detention units and four structural steel mezzanines for a high-security prison facility adjacent to Base Vertieres in Port-au-Prince, Haiti. CRITICAL TIMELINE REQUIREMENTS: Period of Performance: No greater than THREE (3) MONTHS from contract award Target Completion Date: July 31, 2026 Immediate Availability Required: Vendors must have "SteelCell" units already fabricated and warehoused, ready for immediate deployment upon award This is a Sources Sought notice only and does NOT constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP) or a commitment by the Government to issue a solicitation or award a contract. RESPONSE REQUIREMENTS Interested vendors MUST review the complete Statement of Work (attached) in its entirety before responding. If you are capable of meeting all requirements, please email Contracting Officer Courtney Wesely directly at WeselyCJ@state.gov with the following information: Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) Small business size status for NAICS 332311 Confirmation that delivery requirements can be met (three-month period of performance and/or an expeditated delivery of July 31, 2026) Confirmation that "SteelCell" equipment is immediately available for deployment SCOPE OVERVIEW The contractor will provide: 80 double-bunk "SteelCell" stackable modular units (8' 4.25" W x 12' 6.5" L x 8' 4.5" H) Engineered design and fabrication of four structural steel mezzanines with railings, stairs, and bar grating All installation components (shims, welding plates, nuts/bolts, etc.) Shipping and delivery to Port-au-Prince, Haiti (FOB destination) Shop drawings, O&M manuals (French and English), warranty plan Installation training for INL implementers Place of Performance: Design and fabrication in the United States; delivery to Port-au-Prince, Haiti SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS Contracting Officer: Courtney Wesely Email: WeselyCJ@state.gov Submission Deadline: Tuesday, May 18th, no later than 9:00PM ET IMPORTANT NOTICE The Government will use responses to determine the appropriate acquisition strategy, including set-aside considerations and evaluation methodology. The Government will not pay for information submitted in response to this Sources Sought notice and will not reimburse any costs associated with preparing responses.
