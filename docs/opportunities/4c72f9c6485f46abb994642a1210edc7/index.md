# Presolicitation Notice for Field Folding Stool NSN 7105-01-326-1816

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-11 09:00:45.174-04
- Solicitation Number: SPE8ED-26-R-0006

## Summary

General Information The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) Troop Support intends to issue a solicitation for a Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) with Economic Price Adjustment (EPA), Indefinite-Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ) contract. The anticipated period of performance is a five-year base period with no option periods. This acquisition is anticipated to be a 100% Total Small Business Set-Aside. Award Information & Fair Opportunity The Government intends to make multiple awards (up to a maximum of three) to responsible offerors submitting responsive proposals that offer fair and reasonable pricing. However, the Government reserves the right to make a single award. If multiple awards are made, the Government will provide awardees a fair opportunity to be considered for subsequent delivery orders in accordance with FAR 16.505. Requirement Details Item Description Field Folding Stool National Stock Number (NSN) 7105-01-326-1816 Unit of Issue Box (6 units per box) Annual Estimated Quantity (AEQ) 4,356 units Delivery Destinations Tracy, California (CA) and New Cumberland, Pennsylvania (PA) Solicitation Availability The estimated issuance date for the formal solicitation is June 1, 2026. This date is an estimate and is subject to change. Upon issuance, the solicitation and all associated documents will be made available electronically via the DLA Internet Bid Board System (DIBBS) at https://www.dibbs.bsm.dla.mil/. All responsible sources may submit a proposal, which shall be considered by DLA Troop Support. Disclaimer This pre-solicitation synopsis is issued for informational and planning purposes only in accordance with FAR Subpart 5.2. This notice does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP), Request for Quotation (RFQ), or Invitation for Bids (IFB). This notice does not obligate the Government to award a contract or to procure any materials.
