# SERVMART walk-in store

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 08:15:21.512-04
- Solicitation Number: N0018926DL008
- Awardee: MANCON LLC Virginia Beach VA 23455 USA
- Award Amount: 96000000.00

## Summary

MANCON LLC, Virginia Beach, Virginia, was awarded $87,272,727.00 for an indefinite-Delivery/Indefinite-Quantity contract in support of the Norfolk Super Servmart store where the contractor is responsible for the provision of supplies, parts, equipment, and store operation services required by Naval Supply Systems Command Fleet Logistics Center Norfolk for the Norfolk Super Servmart. The five-year contract is fixed-price with provisions for economic price adjustments that will include terms and conditions for the placement of firm-fixed-price delivery orders. The contract will include a five-year ordering period and a six-month option pursuant to Federal Acquisition Regulation 52.217-8 which if exercised will bring the total estimated value of the contract to $96,000,000.00.
