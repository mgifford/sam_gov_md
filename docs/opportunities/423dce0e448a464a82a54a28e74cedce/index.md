## Summary

Government work locations requiring service and support for proprietary equipment manufactured and exclusively serviced by Mettler Toledo. The service will provide maintenance, calibration, technical support, software updates, etc., for proprietary equipment installed at locations listed below. These services are identical to those performed under the existing contract but apply to equipment newly deployed after contract award HQC00425CE002 Cherry Point, Edwards, Fort Belvoir, Fort Eustis, Fort Gordon, Fort Knox, Fort Meade, Fort Riley, Great Lakes, Harrison Village, Laughlin, Meridian, Mitchel Field, Moffett Field, Picatinny Field, Selfridge, West Point, and Yuma MCAS.
