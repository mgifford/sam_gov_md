## Summary

Tinker Air Force AFSC/PZAAC has a requirement to for Honeywell Engineering Services which will be advertised under solicitation FA8109-26-R-B005. This contract facilitates rapid response to USAF technical inquiries (TIs) through Engineering Assignments (EAs). All responsible sources may submit a proposal which will be considered by the agency.
