# TCCC Tier III Training

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 20:00:16.22-04
- Solicitation Number: FA664326Q0002

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with RFO Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number FA664326Q0002 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ), for a Firm Fixed Price (FFP), single award IDIQ for TCCC Tier III Training for 8 days and 64 hours of didactics to include all training, care, feeding, lodging and transportation for 110 students. This acquisition is not set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. CLIN X001: TCCC Tier III training to include all care, feeding, housing, and transportation over 8 days and 64 hours of didactics with FTX for 110 students. CLIN X002: TCCC Tier III training to include all care, feeding, housing, and transportation over 8 days and 64 hours of didactics with FTX for 55 students. Initial order for training 17 August 2026 – 24 August 2026 (travel on 16 August and 24 August). A list of solicitation provisions that apply to the acquisition see attached SF 1449. A list of contract clauses that apply to the acquisition see attached SF 1449. The date, time, and place for receipt of offer and point of contact see attached SF 1449.
