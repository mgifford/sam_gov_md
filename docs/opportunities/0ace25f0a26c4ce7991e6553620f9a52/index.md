## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 31140PR260000096 is issued as a request for quotation (RFQ) for USCG Base Kodiak Rockmore King Clinic Laundry Services. The NAICS Code is 812320. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The small business size standard is $8M per annum. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. USCG Base Kodiak, Kodiak, AK, has a requirement for the Rockmore King Clinic Laundry Services in accordance with the attached Statement of Work (SOW). The base period of performance will be from 16 June 2026 – 31 May 2027, with four one-year option periods from 1 Jun – 31 May. If all options are exercised, the period of performance will expire on 31 May 2031. FAR 52.212-1 (DEVIATION 25-21) and addenda to FAR 52.212-1 apply to this acquisition.
