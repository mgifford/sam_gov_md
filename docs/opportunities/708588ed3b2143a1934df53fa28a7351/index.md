# M82 and M82A1 Percussion Primers

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 09:53:16.226-04
- Solicitation Number: W519TC-25-R-0016

## Summary

The Army Contracting Command - Rock Island intends to issue Request for Proposal, W519TC-25-R-0016, to the SAM.GOV website on or about 15 June 2026 with a closing date of 30 days after issuance. This requirement is for the M82 and M82A1 Percussion Primers: (a) Nomenclature: M82 Percussion Primer NSN: 1390-01-648-3692 DODIC: N523 (b) Nomenclature: M82A1 Percussion Primer NSN: 1390-01-675-6755 DODIC: NA35 The applicable NAICS code is 332993. This acquisition is restricted to the United States, its outlying areas, and Canada in accordance with Public Interest Determination Under Section 3204(a)(7) of Title 10, United States Code and FRO 6.103-7(b). The United States Government (USG) intends to award up to three (3) five-year, Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contracts. The number of awards will be based on the USG requirements and the need to mitigate risk. However, the USG reserves the right to make a single award if determined to be in its best interest. The USG will be using Best Value source selection procedures incorporating Fixed Priced with Economic Price Adjustment pricing. The technical data package (TDP) is classified as Distribution D limited distribution. This TDP contains technical data whose import is restricted by the Arms Export Control Act, (Title 22, U.S.C., Sec 2751 et seq.) or the Export Administration Act of 1979, as amended titled, 50 U.S.C. App 2401 et seq. Violations of these export laws are subject to severe criminal penalties. Disseminate IAW provisions of DoD Directive 5230.25. Offerors must be registered with the U.S./Canada Joint Certification Office to obtain the TDP. The TDP will NOT be available until after the solicitation is formally issued on the SAM.GOV website, with valid contractor credentials. The response date listed above is for archive purposes only and is NOT the date in which offers are due. The date offers are due will be identified in the solicitation. Estimated solicitation issue date is 15 June 2026. This is an estimated date only and is subject to change. This solicitation will be issued electronically in accordance with FAR 4.5, 5.102 and 14.205-1 (a), therefore hardcopies will not be provided. Vendors wishing to obtain an electronic copy may download it on or after the issue date by accessing the following address via the internet: https://sam.gov/content/home NOTE: This solicitation will be issued electronically as will any amendments thereto. Because of this, the Government is under no obligation and is in fact unable to maintain a bidder's mailing list. It is therefore incumbent upon any interested parties to periodically access the above internet address in order to obtain any amendments which may be issued. Failure to obtain any said amendments and to respond to them prior to the date and time set for receipt of proposals may render your proposal non-responsive and result in the rejection of it. Direct any questions prior to the applicable closing date in writing to Army Contracting Command - Rock Island, ATTN: CCRI-MIP-A / Mr. Benjamin Riessen, 3055 Rodman Ave, Rock Island, IL 61299-8000 or benjamin.c.riessen.civ@army.mil.
