# Tactical Weapons Training Facility Services for the United States Embassy in Jerusalem Israel

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 03:04:28.983-04
- Solicitation Number: 191S7026R0001

## Summary

The requirement is for providing the United States Embassy Jerusalem, Israel with Tactical Weapons Training Facility Services for firearms, defensive tactics and classroom training, to the US Embassy's security guard force. The anticipated performance is for a base period of twelve months and four one-year option periods at the discretion of the Government. Note: Please carefully read the RFQ for the technical requirements, response date and time for proposal submission.
