# Monongahela NF - Greenbrier RD Middle Mountain Cabins Chimney Repairs

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 14:52:05.559-04
- Solicitation Number: 12444326Q0018

## Summary

Repair of chimneys at Middle Mountain Cabins to ensure safety, efficiency, and compliance. Services include masonry repair, crown repair, liner inspection/installation, and verification of clearance to combustibles to mitigate fire hazards, ensure proper drafting, and prevent carbon monoxide leakage. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 12444326Q0018 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Monongahela NF -Middle Mountain Cabins Chimney Repairs Project .
