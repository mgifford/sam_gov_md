# NAWCWD Attends Advancing Rapid Defense Innovation Symposium (ARDIS)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 18:46:17.443-04
- Solicitation Number: N68936_NAWCWD_ARDIS_SEP2026

## Summary

The Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division (NAWCWD) will be participating in the Advancing Rapid Defense Innovation Symposium (ARDIS), on September 15-16, 2026 at the Kerr McGee Center in Ridgecrest, CA. ARDIS 2026?will expand to a?two-day event?designed to focus on innovation as an operational necessity, examine?how the character of war is changing in real time, and what those changes mean for defense technology, acquisition, integration, testing, and production. Organized as a forum for?government, industry, startups, academia, and operators, ARDIS 2026 will focus on the pathways required to turn battlefield problems into fielded capability.
