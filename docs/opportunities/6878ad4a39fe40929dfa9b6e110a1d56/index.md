# Bldg Repair & Renovation - Garrison Consolidation

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-12 17:23:07.822-04
- Solicitation Number: W519TC26RB104

## Summary

The Army Contracting Command - Rock Island Arsenal is issuing this Sources Sought Notice for the Building 104 Garrison Consolidation construction project which involves the repair and alteration of an office building located at the U.S. Army Garrison (USAG) Rock Island Arsenal (RIA) in Rock Island, Illinois. Project Summary – This project involves the repair and renovation of approximately 13,290 sq. ft. of an existing office building. Work includes demolition, minor asbestos abatement, construction of new offices and conference rooms, installation of new walls, doors, and flooring, and significant repairs/revisions to HVAC, plumbing, and electrical systems. The Contractor shall be required to commence work under this contract within 30 calendar days after the Contractor receives the Notice to Proceed (NTP), prosecute the work diligently, and complete the Scope of Work in 120 working days. See attached Statement of Work dated 30 April 2026. Interested sources should provide responses no later than 3:00PM Central Time on May 22, 2026. Responses should include the following information: Details of previous, in-scope projects, including dates and the organizations for which the work was performed. CAGE code. Small business designation. Business size status. Please provide a capability statement to Hila Anderson, hila.r.anderson.civ@army.mil. This is a SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE only and shall not be construed as a solicitation or as an obligation on the part of the Government. This notice does not obligate the Government to award a contract or otherwise pay for the information provided in the response. The Government will not reimburse expenses incurred to interested parties for responses to this announcement. The Government will not accept requests for meetings or briefings. No phone solicitations or email with regards to the status of the Request for Proposal (RFP) will be accepted prior to the solicitation release. However, information obtained as a result of this announcement may be reflected in subsequent solicitation, this announcement is Government market research and may result in revisions to both its requirements and its acquisition strategy based on industry responses.
