# N0016426R3114: Technical and management support services to the Environmental Office at NSWC Crane.

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 17:46:15.116-04
- Solicitation Number: N0016426R3114

## Summary

Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) Crane has a requirement for technical and management support services to the Environmental Program Office. This support encompasses the workload for the Environmental Protection (EP) Branch within the Infrastructure Division at Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) Crane. For industry awareness, market research in advance of this solicitation was conducted on SeaPort NxG via Request for Information (RFI)/Sources Sought (SS) with the Opportunity Name of CR-102-0153 (Posted on 03 April 2025).
