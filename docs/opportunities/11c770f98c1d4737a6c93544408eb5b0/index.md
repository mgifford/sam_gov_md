## Summary

Repair of motor grader equipment for BIA, NRO, Shiprock Agency, Branch of Transportation NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD A SOLE SOURCE CONTRACT. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITVE PROPOSALS. The Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) intends to enter into a sole source Firm-Fixed Price contract with Wagner Equipment for the repair of a motor grader equipment for BIA, Navajo Regional Office, Shiprock Agency, Branch of Transportation, under the authority of FAR 12.102(a) Single Source, Contracting officers may solicit from one source if the contracting officer determines that the circumstances of the contract action deem only one source reasonably available (e.g., urgency, exclusive licensing agreements, brand-name or industrial mobilization). No solicitation is available and no technical questions will be answered for this requirement. The NAICS code for this acquisition is 811310.
