## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with the format in part 12 - Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested, and a written solicitation will not be issued. The solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular (FAC) 2026-01; effective March 13, 2026, and the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO), supplemented by Department of Commerce Acquisition Policy. This solicitation is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ). The solicitation number is 1333ND26QNB770225. This procurement is being issued as a 100% small business set-aside. The associated North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this procurement is 334515 – Instrument Manufacturing for Measuring and Testing Electricity and Electrical Signals with a small business size standard of 750 employees. The PSC Code is 6625: Electrical and Electronic Properties Measuring and Testing Instruments. Background The purpose of this requirement is to procure one Keysight N7747C-STD 2-Channel fiber optical power meter (OFPM) on a brand name only basis. The OFPM will be used for the DARPA Quanet phase 2 integration event to characterize the optical fiber links used in this event. The OFPM will be integrated into an existing quantum network and component measurement suite as a moveable rack-mounted measurement capability. Only the Keysight N7747C-STD-2 is acceptable to ensure compatibility with the existing system. It will assist with NIST’s research on developing a movable measurement test suite for quantum network components and protocol testing, verification, and characterization, as well as DARPA's Quanet phase 2 event. The test suite will be implemented on the DC-QNet infrastructure, with a fiber network dedicated to quantum-optical signal transfer between various quantum network nodes. It is anticipated that a Firm-Fixed Price type Purchase Order will be issued as a result of this combined synopsis/solicitation.
