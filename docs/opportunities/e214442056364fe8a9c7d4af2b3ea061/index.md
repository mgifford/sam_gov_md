# Laboratory Washer and Accessories

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF

- Posted: 2026-04-30 15:29:12.162-04

## Summary

The purpose of this RFQ 36C26226Q0760 is to seek quotes from qualified sources under NAICS code 339113 for Laboratory Washer and Accessories. Responses to this RFQ will be used by the Government to make appropriate acquisition decisions. The Department of Veterans Affairs Veterans Integrated Service Network (VISN) 22 is seeking potential sources capable of supplying Laboratory Washer and Accessories. This acquisition will support Clinical Research at the New Mexico VA Health Care System. Please review attached document RFQ 36C26226Q0760, the Statement of Work and Performance Work Standards required.
