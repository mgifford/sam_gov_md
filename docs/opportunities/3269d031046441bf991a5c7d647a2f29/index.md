## Summary

1. Notice Title Sources Sought - Storage Rental Units (IEE Set Aside) - Office of Justice Services 2. Notice Type Sources Sought / Market Research Only Set Aside: Indian Economic Enterprise (IEE) - Buy Indian Act 3. Description The Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA), Western Region, Office of Justice Services is conducting market research to identify qualified Indian Economic Enterprises (IEE) capable of providing storage rental units, including but not limited to: - Provision of secure storage rental units suitable for office and operational needs - Delivery, placement, and removal of units at specified locations - Regular maintenance and servicing of storage units - Ability to provide units of varying sizes as required - Optional climate-controlled units, if applicable - Compliance with all federal, state, and tribal regulations for storage services - Electrical outlets available in every storage space - Electronic gates with keypad access for secure entry - Climate-controlled self storage options to protect sensitive items - Video cameras and 24-hour surveillance for enhanced security - On-site security guard patrol to monitor the facility - Enclosed garages with automatic doors for vehicle and equipment storage - Fully enclosed and well-lit storage spaces for safety and visibility - Extra wide drive aisles to accommodate large vehicles and facilitate easy access - Access to storage units seven days a week - Extended access hours from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily - Availability of month-to-month rental terms for flexibility This notice is NOT a solicitation, request for proposal, or commitment by the Government. No award will be made from this notice. The Government will not reimburse costs associated with responding. 4. IEE Verification Requirement Interested vendors must submit a completed IEE Representation Form (attached). This form is required to verify eligibility under the Buy Indian Act (25 U.S.C. 47). 5. Place of Performance Western Regional Office - Office of Justice Services 2600 N Central Ave, Phoenix, AZ 85004 Require a nearby storage location. 6. Capability Statement Requirements Vendors must provide a brief capability statement that includes: - Company name, UEI, and point of contact - Description of storage rental unit services provided - Relevant experience - Service capacity for rural/remote locations - Completed IEE Representation Form 7. Submission Instructions Responses are due: May 11, 2026 @ 5:00 pm MST Submit via email to: Danielle Bitsilly, Contract Specialist, danielle.bitsilly@bia.gov, 602-669-4442 Email subject line: "Sources Sought Response - 140A1126Q0044 - Storage Rental Units - IEE - [Your Company Name]" 8. Disclaimer This Sources Sought is for market research only . 9. Attachments - Statement of Work - IEE Representation Form
