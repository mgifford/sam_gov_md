## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial service prepared in accordance with the format in FAR 12.6 as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. The solicitation/contract will include all applicable provisions and clauses in effect through Federal Acquisition Circular 2026-01.
