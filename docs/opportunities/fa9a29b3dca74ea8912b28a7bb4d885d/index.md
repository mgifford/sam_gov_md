# Roofing Maintenance and Repairs

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 14:36:44.166-04
- Solicitation Number: N6449826R0510

## Summary

The Naval Surface Warfare Center, Philadelphia Division is soliciting a Request for Proposal (RFP) to procure a roofing maintenance and repairs IDIQ. Work will include roof inspections, roof surveys, gutter cleanings, repairs, replacement, among others. The contract ordering period is expected to be 5 years on a Firm Fixed Price (FFP), Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) single award contract. The solicitation will be 100% Small Business set-aside.
