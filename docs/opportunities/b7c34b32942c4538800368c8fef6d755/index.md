# NPS - Property and Inventory Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 00:08:56.293-04
- Solicitation Number: N0024426R0004

## Summary

The contractor shall perform various commercial non-inherently governmental service functions and facilitate the uploading of key supporting documentation for property assets, creating fair market valuation documentation, processing excess equipment for re-use or disposition, and perform inventory functions. Provide assistance in receiving government equipment at the Naval Supply Warehouse located on the Monterey, California installation and assist in delivering government equipment to the PMD office or the end user also located in one of the fourteen buildings occupied by Naval Postgraduate School. **SEE ATTACHED RFP**
