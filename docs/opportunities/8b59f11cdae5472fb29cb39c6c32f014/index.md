# RIA-JMTC Industry Day

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-06 13:54:17.941-04
- Solicitation Number: W519TC26Q8877

## Summary

May 6, 2026: Vendors can register for this Industry Day at https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/ZB1e7Pj0wj. Optional questions are included to help shape the topics of the industry day. Registrations are due Tuesday, May 26, 2026. All visitors without a Common Access Card (CAC) will be required to register at the Rock Island Arsenal Visitor Center and have an appropriate form of identification. Please see https://home.army.mil/ria/about/visitor-information for details on acceptable identification. The access form has been attached to this posting. It is HIGHLY recommended that individuals attending complete the form and bring it with them to expedite access for themselves and all attendees. Further details and an agenda will be forthcoming. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Army Contracting Command - Rock Island (ACC-RI), on behalf of Rock Island Arsenal Joint Manufacturing and Technology Technology Center plans to hold an Industry Day on June, 4th 2026. It is tentatively scheduled to be held at Rock Island Arsenal. This posting is to notify Industry of the Government's intent to hold the Industry Day to assist with planning and travel arrangements. Registration information and agenda will be forthcoming in a subsequent posting.
