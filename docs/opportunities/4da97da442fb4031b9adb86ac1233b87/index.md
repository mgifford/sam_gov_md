# REMANUFACTURE OF F-15 CABIN RAM AIR BUTTERFLY VALVE

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 12:42:57.658-04
- Solicitation Number: FD20302700021

## Summary

The United States Air Force through Air Force Sustainment Center (AFSC), Tinker AFB 73145 is contemplating the Remanufacture of F-15 Cabin Ram Air Butterfly Valve. The contractor shall provide all labor, facilities, equipment and all material to accomplish remanufacture. The work encompasses the disassembly cleaning, inspection, maintenance re-assembly, testing, and finishing actions required to return the item to a like-new condition in accordance with the solicitation requirements and all attachments. A firm fixed price, firm fixed quantity type contract is contemplated with a one- 2 year Basic. See attached for more information.
