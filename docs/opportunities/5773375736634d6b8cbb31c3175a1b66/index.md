# Mechanical Fabrication

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-21 07:17:32.916-04
- Solicitation Number: N6660426I0343

## Summary

Naval Undersea Warfare Center Division, Newport (NUWCDIVNPT) is seeking information for potential sources that possess the technical capability and qualifications to fabricate, produce, assemble, test, package, and deliver hardware in accordance with drawings provided by the Government. This is a direct follow on to BPA N6660421AJ101-106. THIS RFI IS NOT A REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL. It is a market research tool being used to determine potential businesses capable of providing the mechanical fabrication described herein prior to determining the method of acquisition and issuance of a Request for Quotes (RFQ). The contractor must be able to meet the minimum requirements. The North American Industry Classification Systems (NAICS) code for this requirement is 332710 - Machine Shops. The small business size standard is 500 employees. The results of this RFI will be used to determine capabilities, qualifications, and interest. All interested businesses are encouraged to respond. SEE ATTACHED BPA REQUEST FOR INFORMATION AND DRAFT STATEMENT OF WORK FOR MORE DETAILS.
