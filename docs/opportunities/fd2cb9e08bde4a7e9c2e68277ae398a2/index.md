# 200th Annual Training Meals

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 16:06:17.163-04
- Solicitation Number: W912MM26QA012

## Summary

The South Dakota National Guard has a requirement to provide meals to the 200th during their Annual Training (AT), 30 May, 2026 to 12 June, 2026. The meals will be breakfast and Dinner. It is expected to be 80 meals each sitting for a total of 1,040 Breakfast and 1,040 Dinners meals served for the duration. There will be no lunch meals under this contract. This solicitation is a Total Small Business Set Aside and a Firm Fixed Price Contract is expected. All interested parties must have an active SAM Registration at time the solicitation closes (8 May, 2026 @ 12pm MDT). All questions may be direct to douglas.w.harding.civ@army.mil.
