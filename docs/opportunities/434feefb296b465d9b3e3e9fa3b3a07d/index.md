# Z--Replace Existing Physical Security Alarm System

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-04 19:45:13.632-04
- Solicitation Number: 140P6026B0006

## Summary

Replace Existing Physical Security Alarm System at Saint Croix National Scenic Riverway (SACN), Saint Croix Falls, WI The National Park Service (NPS) is conducting a market survey to determine the interest and capability of contractors for a possible upcoming project. All costs associated with providing information in response to this market survey shall be the responsibility of the firm. Any information submitted is voluntary. The Contracting Officer will make an acquisition strategy determination based on the results of this market survey and other available information. The work shall take place at Saint Croix National Scenic Riverway, Saint Croix Falls, WI. A summary of the work follows: The required work includes replacing the aging fire alarm and intrusion alarm systems with new fire alarm and intrusion alarm systems at the Saint Croix National Scenic Riverway. The existing systems are experiencing significant false alarms and local servicing organizations are having difficulties servicing the existing systems. The National Park Service (NPS) desires to replace the existing systems with non-proprietary systems ensuring local service organizations can maintain the new systems. Work covered includes: a. Demolition and replacement of the existing fire alarm and intrusion alarm systems within multiple buildings b. The installation of a new electronic access control system with card reader access for selected doors c. The replacement of selective doors, frames and door hardware d. Contractor is responsible for minor patching, painting and firestopping of areas affected by the work to maintain existing conditions e. Optional - Installation of closed-circuit television (CCTV) system for selected buildings f. Optional - Installation of exterior lighting to compliment the installation of the video surveillance g. cameras The work of this contract includes furnishing all labor, equipment, and materials necessary to complete all contract requirements. The NAICS code for this project is 561621 - Security Systems Services (except Locksmiths), with a small business size standard of $25M. Contractors must have active registration as a small business and any socio-economic groups represented in SAM (System for Award Management) under NAICS code 561621 to be considered/confirmed. All scope of work documents/specifications will be provided if/when the government proceeds with a formal solicitation. Interested firms must submit the requested information in the attached Market Survey Form. All responses must be submitted via email not later than 1830 CDT, May 8, 2026, to email address james_bissaillon@ios.doi.gov and include the reference number (140P6026B0006) in the email subject line.
