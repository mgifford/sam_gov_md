## Summary

This is a Sources Sought Notice for market research purposes only. This is not a solicitation for proposals/bids, and no contract will be awarded from this notice. No reimbursement will be made for any costs associated with providing information or industry response to this notice or any follow-up information requests. All are highly encouraged to respond. Prior Government contract work is not required for submitting a response to this notice. However, respondents are reminded that all construction performance must follow the Engineer Manual (EM) 385-1-1, USACE Safety and Health Requirements Manual and any other requirements contained in the solicitation, and must also comply with all Federal, State and local laws, ordinances, codes and/or regulations. This announcement provides the detailed information necessary for your firm to prepare its response. Project Title: Obey River Campground Paving Project Location: Obey River Campground 100 Obey Park Rd, Monroe, TN 38573 NAICS Code: 237310 Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction PSC: Z1LB Maintenance of Highways, Roads, Streets, Bridges, and Railways Estimated Magnitude of Construction: $250,000-500,000 Anticipated Solicitation Issuance Date: Q4 FY2026 Estimated duration of the project: Approxiatley 210 calendar days Anticipated Contract Type: Anticipated project will be a competitive, firm-fixed price contract. Anticipated Acquisition Method: Anticipated project will be acquired using Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 12 Commercial Products and Commercial Services or RFO Part 14 Sealed Bidding High-Level Project Summary: Obey River Campground’s area of focus for this project consists of approximately 600 feet of roadway (average approximately 8-9’ wide) and parking area (approximately 150’ x 170’) to include 600 feet of curbing (4,250 sq. yards total) within Obey River Campground. The purpose of this ERFO project is to remove and replace pavement damaged in storms & highwater events. Several of the roadway sections and parking areas have significant damage including: alligatoring, longitudinal and transverse (block) cracking, raveling, and shoulder and curbing damage. Project will include striping parking lot for trailer & single car spaces, replacement of 1 stormwater drain, removing dilapidated wooded stanchions & cable, replace curbing, & widening of road up to 11’ total width. The contractor will mill and replace the top 2 inches of pavement with a leveling course after preparing the site and applying a tack coat, following Tennessee Department of Transportation (TDOT) standards. It will include replacing curbing around the parking lot. It is possible that small portions of the roadway and parking areas may need base course repair or leveling prior to repaving. Other Known Challenges: Work will be not able to begin until after the campground and recreation areas have closed for the season and must be complete prior to these areas opening in the following spring. Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Requirement: It is unknown at this time, anticipate this project could require Level 1 (Self) Certification. Prospective subcontractors are encouraged to submit so the information can be used when developing the potential Small Business Participation Plan percentages if the eventual acquisition is conducted as an unrestricted, negotiated acquisition.
