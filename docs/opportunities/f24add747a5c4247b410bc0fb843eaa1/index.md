## Summary

The purpose of Amendment 0004 is to submit responses to RFI's. Response date remains, May 6 ,2026 3pm EST The deadline for submitting RFI's is April 29, 2026. RFI's received after this date will not be considered. Please see the attached. This project is a design-build to upgrade the AC Plants to include three new chillers and associated cooling towers which generate additional heat than previous equipment. New equipment is controlled by variable frequency drive cabinets that also generate substantial heat and need to be cooled. Existing ventilation is not sufficient, and new equipment began to shut down during peak summer loads. Electrical switchgear is needed to provide minimum cooling to Main Hospital for patient care.
