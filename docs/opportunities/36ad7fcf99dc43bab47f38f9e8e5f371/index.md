## Summary

These specifications cover the production of the Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages Forms Packages consisting of either a letter or form with cover letter, return envelope, and mail-out envelope, requiring such operations as electronic prepress, printing and variable imaging, binding, construction, assembly, packing, and distribution. Additionally, the contractor will be responsible for processing the form responses
