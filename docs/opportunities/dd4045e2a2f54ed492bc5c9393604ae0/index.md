# NSPIRE IT Operations & Maintenance Support

- Agency: HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-29 12:14:21.533-04
- Solicitation Number: NOF-3

## Summary

A new stand-alone bridge contract for one (1) 6-month plus two (2) 3-month option periods is being requested, to continue performance while a competitive requirement is being procured for services under contract no. 86615322C00005, O&M in support of National Standards for the Physical Inspection of Real Estate (NSPIRE) Information Technology (IT) solution. These services will be used to continue the functionality of support with the current contractor for NSPIRE IT solution which utilizes Millsapps, Ballinger & Associates’ (MB&A’s) ExAM4 Inspections application to achieve the target state implementation of NSPIRE and agency mission objectives.
