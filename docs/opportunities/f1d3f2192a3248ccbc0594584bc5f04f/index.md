# USAG Italy Security Guards

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 10:02:43.296-04

## Summary

This is a non-personal services contract to provide armed security personnel employed under this contract to provide installation access control, roving security patrols, electronic security systems (CCTV) monitoring of the access control points (ACP), entry control points (ECP), pass and badgecontrol, vehicle inspections, static security, or any other security related function(s) associated with the protection of U.S. Government installations, materials, property, and personnel. The Government will not exercise any supervision or control over the contract service providers performing the services herein. Such contract service providers shall be accountable solely to the Contractor who, in turn is responsible to the Government.
