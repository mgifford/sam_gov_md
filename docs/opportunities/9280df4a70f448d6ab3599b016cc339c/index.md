# Expression of Interest – Residential Curtain Cleaning PR15988793

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-15 01:07:47.747-04
- Solicitation Number: PR15988793

## Summary

The project involves the taking down, cleaning and reinstallation of drapes, voiles, blinds and related hardware, in residential properties occupied by personnel of the US Government. Key components include: Inspect and report on the condition of all window treatments including drapes, voiles, blinds and related hardware Mark all curtains/voiles/blinds/hardware for point of reference Place curtains/voiles/blinds/hardware in boxes clearly marked with the physical address Clean all removed curtains/voiles/blinds/hardware as per recommended manufacturer’s specifications Store all the removed curtains/voiles/blinds/hardware, etc. for the duration of the make ready in a secure, clean area off site Deliver to GSO Warehouse all curtains/voiles/blinds/hardware, that are being replaced with new ones, instead of being cleaned for reinstallation. Clean all windowpanes and security grills inside before curtains/voiles/blinds/hardware are re-installed Ensure all hardware for proper functioning of curtains/voiles/blinds are present and working and replace where necessary Ensure full functionality of all parts of all curtains/voiles/blinds
