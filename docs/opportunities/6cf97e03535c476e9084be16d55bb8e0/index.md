# Transcription Services

- Agency: ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE OF THE US COURTS
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-04 15:40:28.372-04
- Solicitation Number: RFI1809807

## Summary

The Administrative Office of the United States Courts (AO) is issuing a Request for Information (RFI) FOR INFORMATION FOR PLANNING PURPOSES ONLY. As part of its market research, the Administrative Offices of the U.S. Courts is issuing this RFI to determine if there exists an adequate number of qualified and interested contractors capable of the scope of the requirement. The contractor shall provide legal transcription services from an audio recording to an encrypted document for official record.
