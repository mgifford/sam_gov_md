# Notice of Intent to Sole Source RS Means Online Complete

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-19 08:15:11.701-04

## Summary

MCICOM-GF is requesting subscriptions to RSMeans Data Online, allowing for creation of detailed cost breakdowns for materials, labor, and equipment, enabling users to create accurate cost analyses for new construction projects or renovations. RSMeans construction cost databases represent the industry standard for reliable cost estimation and provide critical capabilities that directly support USMC infrastructure requirements and fiscal responsibility mandates. RSMeans offers comprehensive construction cost databases that include labor, material, and equipment costs for thousands of construction activities, updated quarterly to reflect current market conditions.
