## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued.This acquisition is set aside for Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) concerns, or Economically Disadvantaged WOSB (EDWOSB) concerns. In accordance with FAR

19.107 Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) Program. Title: Personal Assistance Services (PAS), Region 2. EPA, R2, requires Personal Assistance Services (PAS) commercial services. The services are for one federal EPA employee with targeted disabilities. The PAS provider shall provide non-medical, non-work task assistance, enabling the employee to perform activities they would typically handle themselves. Fulfilling the agency's obligations under Section 501 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973. Non-personal services.
