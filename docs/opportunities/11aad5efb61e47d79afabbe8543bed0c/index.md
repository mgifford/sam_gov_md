# Gravel Road Maintenance

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-26 12:53:17.934-04
- Solicitation Number: W912DR26QA048

## Summary

The contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, and equipment, including equipment operators, necessary to perform gravel road maintenance services on the Raystown Lake project in Huntingdon County, Pennsylvania. The road network is in Hopewell, Lincoln, Juniata, and Penn Townships of Huntingdon County. Work includes a variety of gravel road maintenance tasks to include culvert removal, culvert, headwall, and riprap splash pad installation, road surface preparation, drainage ditch cleaning, stone placement and compaction, and seeding.
