# Intent to Sole Source Janitorial Restroom Services for the Zablocki VAMC in Milwaukee, WI.

- Agency: VETERANS AFFAIRS, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-27 11:26:07.122-04
- Solicitation Number: 36C25226Q0422

## Summary

Under Statutory Authority Public Law 109 Sec. 502 and VAAR 819.7008, the Government anticipates entering into a sole-source procurement with JTM ENTERPRISE, LLC. a certified/verified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) for Janitorial services at the Clement J. Zablocki VAMC located in Milwaukee, WI (restroom cleaning only). A determination that only one SDVOSB can meet the requirement is not required. This notice is not a request for competitive quotes. This award with be for a base year contract ($143,195.22 for base year) with four (4) additional Option Years, which may be awarded at the discretion of the Government. SEE ATTACHMENT
