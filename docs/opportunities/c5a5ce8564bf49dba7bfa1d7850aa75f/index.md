# Notice of Intent to Single Source - PIO Scissor Lift

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-27 22:54:12.988-04
- Solicitation Number: FA520526QB086

## Summary

Under the authority of RFO 12.102(a), 35th Contracting Squadron intends to issue a single source contract to AKTIO CORPORATION, CAGE JS962 for teh rental of Scissor Lifts and Telehandlers for 35 FW/PIO. Rental duration is less that 2 weeks at Misawa Airbase, Japan. Market research found that AKTIO is the only vendor capable of providing all the equipment necessary to complete the requirement in the time needed. Market research indicated that addditional vendors can provide MHE, but not all the specific MHE needed for this project that are in limited stock in this region. Scissor Lift x2 Telehandler MT 1440 x1 Telehandler MRT 2150 x1 This notice of intent is not a request for competitive quotations. Any questions regarding this notice must be submitted in writing via e-mail to SrA Alexander T. Holliday at alexander.holliday@us.af.mil and 1st Lt Kade N. Forrester at kade.forrester.1@us.af.mil
