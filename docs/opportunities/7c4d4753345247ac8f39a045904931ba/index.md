# Earth Covered Magazines

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-19 00:01:40.307-04
- Solicitation Number: FA527026PKC001

## Summary

THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT ANNOUNCEMENT ONLY. This notice does not constitute a commitment by the Government. All information submitted in response to this announcement is voluntary, and the Government will not pay for information requested nor will it compensate any respondent for any cost incurred in developing information provided to the Government. 18 CONS/PKC is searching for sources who can provide construction for the requirement, Earth Covered Magazines. The scope is to construct several Earth Covered Magazines with reinforced concrete floor, wall, and roof slabs. An Earth Covered Magazine (ECM) is a reinforced, bunker-like structure designed to safely store explosives and munitions, using earth and structural design to mitigate blast risks and protect contents. If your company can provide the construction needs, to fulfill this requirement, please reach out to the Lt Merina R. Cazares via email at merina.cazares@us.af.mil
