## Summary

The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), Network Contracting Office (NCO) 21, plans to issue a Request for Quote (RFQ) for ground ambulance services for San Francisco VA Health Care System. The solicitation number for this RFQ is 36C26126Q0480. The solicitation will be issued June 1, 2026, with a due date of July 1, 2026. The North American Industry Classification Systems (NAICS) is 621910, size standard $22.5 Million. Contractors must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) at www.SAM.gov for award eligibility. Please send any questions or inquires to joshua.barrios@va.gov.
