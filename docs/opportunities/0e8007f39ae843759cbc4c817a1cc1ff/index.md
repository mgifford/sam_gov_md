# Purchase of 5 New All-Terrain Utility Vehicles

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 10:46:42.112-04
- Solicitation Number: PR15994760

## Summary

The U.S. Consulate General Frankfurt invites you to submit an offer for a firm fixed price for: 5 New All-Terrain Utility Vehicles (UTV) 3 x Electric Utility Vehicle (UTV) 2 x Gas-Driven Utility Vehicle (UTV) Request for Quotation Number: PR15994760. The United States Consulate General in Frankfurt requires new vehicles: 5 x all-terrain utility vehicles (UTV), with German specifications to be delivered to Frankfurt am Main, Germany within a fixed, reasonable lead time. The vehicle must meet all relevant German and EU specifications. Quoted models must have availability of manufacturer-authorized repair shops within the greater Frankfurt area. The price quoted in the bid must include delivery and VAT as follows: DDP – Delivered Duty Paid: The seller is responsible for the entire shipment, including customs clearance and fees [if delivered outside of Germany] and delivering the goods to the buyer’s premises in Frankfurt.
