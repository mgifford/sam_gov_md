# N0018926QW034 GSI Audio Calibration/Installation (Customized Booth)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-13 11:47:45.43-04

## Summary

NSMRL’s audio booth warrants recertification and equipment calibration to maintain industry standards, to ensure compliant, precise results for upcoming audiometry-related efforts. As a result, the laboratory needs to upgrade its audiology booth used to facilitate upcoming studies. NSMRL leverages its onsite audiology equipment to develop, test, and evaluate underwater noise systems that measure high-noise levels in operational environments, monitor the effects of noise exposure on personnel hearing, and investigate the range and limits of underwater hearing, among other efforts. The procurement, for updated equipment/the audio booth itself will be coordinated with the respective contractor. Following the installation and calibration of equipment, the contractor will provide an electronic copy/documentation of these completed services/efforts, detailing the results of the calibrations and certification to NSMRL. Devices and Speaker: 1) GSI AudioStar Pro 2-Channel, Product #GSI-8515319 a. Frequency range: 125 - 8K (AC); 250-8K (BC). b. Features: wave files recorded speech tests; pediatric noise; fine frequency testing; customer startup settings; QuickSIN; BKB-SIN; VRA control. c. Special tests included: SISI; ABLB; tone decay; TEN. d. SW: GSI Suite for data transfer, reporting and counseling; ASP Config App for instrument programming. e. Accessories: mic/monitor headset; gooseneck mic; pt resp switch; TB mic; USB cable; keyboard & mouse. f. ASP standard audiometry. 2) GSI Tympstar Pro, Product #GSI-8525169 a.
