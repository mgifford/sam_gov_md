## Summary

The General Services Administration currently leases 35,692 Rentable Square Feet (RSF), yielding 31,088 ANSI/BOMA Office Area (ABOA) square feet for the Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) along with 24 surface and 14 structured parking spaces located at 6999 Sierra Center Parkway, Reno, NV. The Agency has a continuing need for 35,692 RSF under lease LNV03490. The Total Contract Value for the 15 year/10 year firm term lease is $24,986,317.25 The Succeeding lease term is August 5, 2026 through August 4, 2041. Justificatoin for Other Than Full and Open Compettiion was approved August 22, 2024.
