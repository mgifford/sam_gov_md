# Two Precision 1-Circle Segment Stages (Goniometers)

- Solicitation Number: NIST-AMD-NCNR-SSN26-03

## Summary

***THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE*** BACKGROUND The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) is seeking information from sources that may be capable of providing two precision 1-circle segment stages (goniometers) that are capable of supporting a load of at least 50 kg with required cabling under the authority of FAR 12. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this acquisition is 334516 Analytical Laboratory Instrument Manufacturing. The NIST neutron imaging team, in the Radiation Physics Division of the Physical Measurement Laboratory, is developing a far-field grating neutron interferometer to measure the projection of the pair correlation function, G(Z), of a sample of interest. This interferometer geometry requires aligning two phase gratings in pitch, with respect to the neutron beam axis. The Phase Grating Pitch Alignment system will support other components and must be cable to control a large load. Specifications for a two precision 1-circle segment stages (goniometers) that are capable of supporting a load of at least 50 kg with required cabling. The tilt stage shall be a 1-circle segment (goniometer) and have a travel range of at least +/- 15 degrees (larger is better) The tilt stage shall have an angular resolution of at least 0.002 degrees (smaller is better) The tilt stage shall have a maximum load of 50 kg (larger is better) The tilt stage shall have a mass of less than 5 kg (without motor) The tilt stage shall include a motor (DC or 2-phase stepper) with a cable at least 1.5 m in length. The tilt stage shall have a mounting surface that is greater than or equal to 150 mm by 50 mm and have at least four tapped thru holes for mounting other objects to the tilt stage.
