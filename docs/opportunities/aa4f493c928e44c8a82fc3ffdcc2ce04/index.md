## Summary

The 42d Contracting Squadron, Maxwell AFB, AL is seeking sources that perform the tasks necessary to modernize, sustain, and support the AU Student Information System. The scope includes establishing a robust governance framework, providing transformation and operational support, delivering technical maintenance, developing and executing a training plan, and furnishing detailed reporting IAW the Statement of Work (SOW).
