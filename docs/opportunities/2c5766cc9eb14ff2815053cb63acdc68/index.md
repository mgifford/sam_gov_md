# FAA APEX Industry Day

- Agency: TRANSPORTATION, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-01 13:14:01.768-04

## Summary

The Federal Aviation Administration’s (FAA) Vice President of Mission Support Services cordially invites you to our upcoming Air Traffic Performance EXcellence System (APEX) Industry Day. This event is a follow on to the FAA’s recent Request For Information (RFI) (Notice ID: APEXRFI032026) and will allow industry partners an opportunity to understand the FAA’s broader vision for evolving student success at the FAA Academy, the APEX project, and an overview of the FAA Academy infrastructure. Registration is limited to U.S. Citizens. For additional information on the FAA vision, please see the attached documents: “Improving FAA Training Outcomes through System Alignment” and “CAMI APEX”. Date: Friday, May 15th, 2026 Time: 11:00-12:00 P.M. Location: Bessie Coleman Conference Room 800 Independence Avenue SW Washington, D.C. 20591 Attendees: Limit two (2) per company
