Background The Indian Health Service (IHS) is the principal federal health care provider and health advocate for Indian people, and its goal is to raise their health status to the highest possible level. IHS provides a comprehensive health service delivery system for approximately 2.3 million American Indians and Alaska Natives (AI/AN) who belong to 573 federally recognized Tribes in 37 states. The services sought notice is for elevator preventive maintenance support services for a total of six (6) elevators at the Gallup Indian Medical Center in Gallup, NM. Contractor services shall include monthly inspections of all six elevators to ensure continuous operations, and complete preventive maintenance work per current ASME A17.1 Safety Code for Elevators and Escalators, Section 8.6 requirements, and provide emergency services 24 hours a day, 365 days a year with service callbacks. A complete Statement of Work is attached with the full requirements outlined. Purpose and Objectives Gallup Indian Medical Center (GIMC) requires a Preventative Maintenance agreement for passenger elevators and service elevators. The services include routine inspections, lubrication, adjustments, testing of safety devices, minor repairs, documentation of deficiencies, and emergency callback support. As requested, the services requested as written in the statement of work: All work must be conducted in accordance with manufacturer recommendations and applicable safety and building codes. The objective of this SSN is to determine the availability of qualified and responsible sources for elevator preventive maintenance services, evaluate small business participation and potential set-aside strategies, identify customary commercial practices, and support acquisition planning in accordance with the Indian Economic Enterprises set forth above.
