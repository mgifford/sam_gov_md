# Buckley SFB - Catholic Priest

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 14:11:37.834-04
- Solicitation Number: F1K0AA6132A001

## Summary

This sources sought is issued for the purpose of market research only. In accordance with RFO Part 10. The result of this market research will contribute to determining the method of procurement. This announcement is not to be construed as a commitment by the Government, implied or otherwise, to issue a solicitation or to award a contract. At this time, BSFB is not seeking quotes and will not accept unsolicited quotes. This sources sought is for a Buckley Space Force Base requirement for the services of a Catholic Priest to provide administrative support, religious services, and pastoral care in support of daily Mass. Please review the attached draft Performance Work Statement (PWS) for further information. Please note that possession of a valid DD 2088 Statement of Ecclesiastical Endorsement, from a recognized Religious-Endorsing Organization is a mandatory requirement to be able to fulfill this requirement. Please provide the following information as applicable: Socioeconomic status Capabilities statement If you are on a government vehicle already (ie. GSA) Any applicable pricing (Optional) Anything the government is potentially overlooking within this requirement. If there are any questions, please feel free to email the POC's listed on this posting.
