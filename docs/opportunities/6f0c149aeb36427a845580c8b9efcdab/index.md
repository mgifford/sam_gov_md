## Summary

AWARD NOTICE: Naval Surface Warfare Center, Indian Head Division - Picatinny Detachment (NSWC IHD) awarded an order under Basic Ordering Agreement (BOA) N00174-23-G-0001 to BAE Systems Land & Armaments L.P., Minneapolis, MN 55421 as a Cost Plus Fixed Fee (CPFF) Order on a sole source basis. The contractor shall provide Open and Inspect and repair the MK 38 Mod 2/3 major 2J Cog Components. BAE Systems Land & Armaments L.P., is the only known company with the requisite technical qualifications and facilities to perform this work within the required time frame.
