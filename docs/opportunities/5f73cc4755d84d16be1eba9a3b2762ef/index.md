# Group 2 unmanned aircraft system (UAS)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-06 06:41:25.685-04
- Solicitation Number: F5N0FM6036A001SS

## Summary

This document is a combined synopsis and solicitation/ Sole source for commercial items, prepared in accordance with the format in RFO Part 12, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; proposals are being requested and a written solicitation will not be issued. The 439th Contracting Flight, Westover Air Reserve Base, MA, intends to award a firm-fixed-price contract on a sole-source basis to Drone Express (DEXA) for the procurement of one (1) DEXA DE-2020 autonomous aircraft system. The DEXA DE-2020 is required to support the 439th Mission Support Group's need for a drone capable of autonomous delivery of supplies, as well as to provide airborne surveillance, identification, tracking, and response capabilities for small, unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) operating in or near restricted airspace. This system is essential for testing and validation of installation sUAS and Counter-sUAS (C-sUAS) capabilities. The procurement includes the following: DEXA DE-2020 Drone: A multi-rotor hexacopter with the following minimum specifications: Carbon fiber frame Six (6) brushless motors Pixhawk Blue Cube or greater autopilot Weight: 20-25 lbs Maximum cruise speed: 40 mph Payload capacity: 5 lbs Minimum battery life: 45 minutes Minimum range: 4 miles Emergency parachute recovery system FAA S-1 Approved List Certification (under Section 44807) and Part 135 certification Training: A two-day, onsite training program for government personnel on the UAS. This will include a "Train the Trainer" certification. Maintenance: A one-year maintenance plan. 2. Place of Performance and Delivery: The drone and all associated services shall be delivered to Westover Air Reserve Base, MA. The required delivery date is by August 2026. 3. Justification for Sole Source: The authority for this sole-source procurement is RFO 6.103-1(b) – "One responsible source." Market research has indicated that no other vendors are capable of providing a Group 2 weighted UAS comparable to the DE-2020 that meets the government's requirements. DEXA's (Drone Express) regulatory posture, including FAA S-1 designation and Part 135 certification, and its aircraft's specific capabilities for Beyond-Visual-Line-of-Sight (BVLOS) operations, make it the only vendor that can provide a comparable combination of autonomous airborne response, secure communications, and regulatory readiness. 4. Submission of Information: This notice is not a request for competitive proposals. However, all interested parties who believe they can meet the requirements are invited to submit, in writing, a capability statement, proposal, or quotation, which shall be considered by the agency. Information can be sent to Robert E. Stacy, Contracting Officer, at robert.stacy.2@us.af.mil. The response must be received no later than Friday, May 8, 2026 A determination by the Government not to compete this proposed contract based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. Information received will be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement.
