# Semi-Annual Maintenance for X-ray Generators and Cooling Units

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 19:32:35.944-04
- Solicitation Number: N4523A26Q0013

## Summary

The contactor who performs the preventative maintenance and troubleshooting shall be an authorized service provider/technician on COMET Technologies USA, Inc. industrial x-ray equipment that uses a PC-based controller. The contractor shall provide a training certificate from COMET as proof of training/certification. The contractor must have a thorough knowledge of COMET Technologies USA, Inc. industrial x-ray equipment and maintenance requirements. The current machines used at TRIREFFAC are COMET x-ray model: MXR-320-26AX, serial number: 626630 (with standard controller) and COMET x-ray model: MXR-320/26, serial number: 1060031 (controlled with PC using COMET iVario control software).
