## Summary

PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE Solicitation No.: 19GE5026R0074 The Regional Procurement Support Office, Frankfurt provides the following Pre-Solicitation Notice regarding the issuance of a Request for Proposal (RFP) for: Design and Construction Services for renovation of four Police Stations in Lebanon The Regional Procurement Support Office in Frankfurt, Germany, intends to issue a Firm Fixed-Price Construction type contract during the third quarter of calendar year 2026. The successful contractor shall provide complete design and construction services, contract coordination, and supervision, including but not limited to the management and construction necessary to meet the requirements of the contract within the established timeframe. The United States Embassy on behalf of the Bureau of International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs (INL) intends to renovate four key police stations in the south of Lebanon. These stations need specific civil and electromechanical repairs and upgrades to be restored to full operational capability. The magnitude of this project is anticipated to be between $1,000,000 and $3,000,000 for the entire project. Companies interested in the solicitation should express their interest by written correspondence, no later than 28 May 2026, at the following email addresses gallonea@state.gov and MUST include the following information: Name of Firm, Name, Telephone, Email Address of Point of Contact and Complete Physical and Mailing Address of Firm. DO NOT TELEPHONE. Firms shall be knowledgeable in written and spoken English.
