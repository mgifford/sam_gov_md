# RFI - Two Fiber Winders

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 10:16:21.57-04
- Solicitation Number: W911QY26RFIBR

## Summary

Description SYNOPSIS: This Sources Sought Announcement is to assist the US Army Combat Capabilities Development Command Soldier Center (DEVCOM Soldier Center) to identify potential sources to provide a supply contract for two (2) Fiber Winders. The Government requests that responses be submitted electronically to kevin.m.morris24.civ@army.mil and trisha.r.scott.civ@army.mil by June 11, 2026 by 5pm EST. Background: The DEVCOM Soldier Center Distinctive Material Solutions Branch needs two replacement winders capable of spooling Nylon 6/6 fibers up to 4000 total denier at 1100 meter/min to a package of ~ 30 pounds. Winders provided should include dual switching capabilities, spare parts packages, as well as commissioning and installation to certify the equipment. Specific Specifications: Winding speed = Up to 3000 m/min Yarn Count range = 2800 – 3500 den Traverse Length = 12 to 17 inches Tube inside diameter = Max is 5 inches Package diameter = Max is 16 inches Package weight = Max is 30 lbs. Yarn tension = 35 to 40 psi Frame = 1 Tier, Double Winder not to exceed 12 cubic feet. Additional Specifications Requested: Brand name and model number Product/item features/description to include the following performance characteristics: Automatic Switching of rolls Load volume and load weight limitations Cycle Time at least 30 minutes. Product/item specifications to include: Overall Dimensions Overall Weight Power requirements Other utility (e.g., gas, water, air) requirements Exhaust requirements Deliverables: Two Fiber winders with all the associated hardware, software, controllers, and safety equipment installed as well as recommended spare parts required for immediate use in government facilities. Installation/start-up (if required)/shipping recommended spare parts shall be itemized and included in the Fiber winder contract. Delivery Date: The Government anticipates 10 months’ lead time for delivery of the Fiber Winders, after receipt of order. Note: Interested parties are invited to submit a response to this Sources Sought Announcement. THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT ANNOUNCEMENT ONLY This Sources Sought Announcement is issued solely for information and planning purposes and to identify interested sources. THIS IS NOT A SOLICITATION. RESPONSES: Interested parties may identify their interest and capability by sending responses regarding this requirement to DEVCOM Soldier Center via e-mail ONLY to kevin.m.morris24.civ@army.mil and trisha.r.scott.civ@army.mil no later than June 11, 2026 by 5:00 p.m. EST.
