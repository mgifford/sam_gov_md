DLA Troop Support-Medical in Philadelphia anticipates possible award to purchase maintenance for PowerScribe 360 systems at multiple Army Bases both Contiguous United States (CONUS) and Outside Contiguous United States (OCONUS) to include a one year contract Vendor shall provide maintenance for the Nuance/Powerscribe voice dictation systems throughout the throughout various Army Military Treatment Facilities (MTFs). Vendor will support the system's connections, configurations, hardware and proprietary databases at each site accordingly. The Government plans to procure the maintenance as a commercial item through fixed price award(s). The only source known is Carahsoft Technology Corporation as they are the only known PowerScribe 360 authorized distributor. Minimum requirements: -Vendor shall have access to the DISA business to business gateways that allow for remote access. -Vendor must have already established understanding of Nuance PowerScribe 360 database architecture, proprietary storage mechanisms and system configurations. -Vendor must provide hardware/software support than can extend beyond the normal service life of the hardware currently installed throughout the Army MTFs.
