# Notice of Intent to Sole Source - Pipette Repair Service

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 08:33:47.496-04
- Solicitation Number: 98017

## Summary

The selected vendor will provide cleaning and calibration of 1000 pipettes during a one-week onsite visit. This service should include cleaning, calibration, and minor repair on most major brands and types of pipettes. Brands include, but are not limited to, Rainin, Eppendorf, Oxford, Labsystems, Finn, Titertek, Biohit, Wheaton, MLA, Matrix, Gilson, Denville, Nichiryo, and Tranferpette. Multi-channel and single-channel pipettes will need to be serviced.
