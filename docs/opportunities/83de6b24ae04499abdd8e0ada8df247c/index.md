# Methane Gas Analyzer

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 14:58:58.517-04
- Solicitation Number: 1333MJ26Q0009

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1333MJ26Q0009 is issued as a Request for quotation (RFQ) for one (1) Methane Gas Analyzer. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. See SF1449 for line item Description of requirements for the items to be acquired: One (1) commercial methane gas analyzer, brand name or equal to the Aeris Technologies MIRA Ultra (Part Number 95.000053). Salient Characteristics: Utilizes Mid-infrared Absorption (MIRA) technology for trace gas monitoring. Highly portable, lightweight form factor suitable for benchtop, mobile laboratory, tower, or drone/aircraft deployments. High-precision, parts-per-billion (ppb) measurement capability for Methane (CH4) and Ethane (C2H6) dry mole fractions to distinguish between biogenic and thermogenic sources. Must include or seamlessly integrate with a Nafion dryer (for moisture correction), external pump, and flow control. Near real-time automated data transmission capabilities. Measurement precision of 1 ppb for CH4 in one second at approximately 2000 ppb CH4 Data output should be available via ethernet port or USB connection. The size of the optical component (temperature-controlled cell and detector) must be no larger than 7” x 8” x 5” (not including associated electronics and gas handling components). Power consumption < 25W at steady state Must be compatible in size, power, and electrical and data connections with the Aeris MIRA Ultra portable gas analyzer Delivery FOB Boulder, CO, 60 days after receipt of order or less. See Section 5 for included solicitation provisions that apply to the acquisition. See Section 3 for included contract clauses that apply to the acquisition. Offers must be sent to Darrin Moore, at darrin.moore@noaa.gov. See SF1449, Page 1 for due date and time.
