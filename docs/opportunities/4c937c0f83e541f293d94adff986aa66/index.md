# Industry Day Notice: FY26 TSA Innovation Office – Transporting Security Equipment (TSE) Connectivity

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-13 11:25:37.42-04

## Summary

The Transportation Security Administration (TSA) Innovation Office will host an Industry Day to highlight Transporting Security Equipment (TSE) Connectivity: The next generation of transportation security. The event will take place on June 18, 2026, at 1:00 PM EST due to conclude at 2:00 PM EST, via Microsoft Teams. TSA seeks industry partnership in a joint ecosystem focused on significantly improving security and transforming the passenger experience through innovation. TSA is advancing a connectivity foundation guided by two principles: an open architecture approach and data standardization, to support rapid deployment of new solutions and offer greater opportunities for innovation with industry partners. Additional Information: An attachment with further details about TSA’s TSE Connectivity initiative is included with this notice. All interested parties are encouraged to review the attached document prior to the Industry Day to familiarize themselves with the requirements and objectives. Who Should Attend: Industry partners with capabilities in the aviation security ecosystem, including those specializing in connectivity solutions, open architecture, and data standardization, are encouraged to participate. Registration Instructions: If you are interested in attending, please RSVP by May 27, 2026, 10:00 AM EST by sending an email to TSA.Innovation@tsa.dhs.gov with “TSE Connectivity Industry Day RSVP” in the subject line. Attendance is limited to those who RSVP by the deadline. A Teams calendar invite will be sent to all registered industry partners.
