# Provision of Gardening Services at the U.S. Embassy Windhoek, Namibia

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 03:24:17.702-04
- Solicitation Number: 19WA8026Q0013

## Summary

Please note this solicitation is not ready to be issued as of this date. The Embassy of the United States in Windhoek will launch the solicitation on the SAM.gov website when it is available. The U.S. Embassy Windhoek hereby provides the following Pre-solicitation Notice in respect of the issuance of a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for: Provision of Gardening Services. This is a contract for landscaping / gardening services for the U.S. Embassy Windhoek, Namibia. The contract contains certain ordering provisions by which the Contracting Officer or his/her representative may require additional amounts of service for special events. The Contractor shall furnish managerial, administrative and direct labor personnel to accomplish all work required by this contract. All responsible sources may submit a quotation, which will be considered. The Government may award the contract based on the initial offer without discussion. The resultant contract will be a firm fixed price type contract. The Government is planning a pre-quotation conference and the date for this conference will be in the solicitation. Electronic Submission/Responses: This opportunity allows for electronic responses. Please submit your offer prior to the response date and time. Please ensure your email with your offer does not exceed 15 MB, if it does then you might have to send more than one email. Alternatively, offers may be submitted at the US Embassy offices at address below 38 Metje Street, Klein Windhoek, Windhoek, Namibia.
