# USTRANSCOM Acquisition Program Support

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-29 15:06:59.763-04
- Solicitation Number: TRANSCOM26D002

## Summary

Background United States Transportation Command (USTRANSCOM) Program Executive Office – Transcom (PEO-T) is an acquisition program management organization chartered to manage cost, schedule, performance, and risk to develop, field, and sustain effective enabling activities to support USTRANSCOM. The PEO-T manages acquisition programs, program-related activities, and select services acquisition for USTRANSCOM and for its other associated supporting organizations when USTRANSCOM requirements are included as part of the effort. USTRANSCOM PEO-T also manages the Department’s Joint Transportation Management System (JTMS) Program. The JTMS will implement an Enterprise Resource Planning solution to integrate the transportation and financial domains of the Joint Deployment and Distribution Enterprise to achieve auditability, transparency, and optimized resource allocation. The requirement This requirement will provide non-personal advisory and assistance services (A&AS) including program management, engineering, financial management, functional support, and acquisition related support to the PEO-T and the JTMS Programs. Request for Information Please see attached document “Request for Information” for specific information requested, instructions, and response date.
