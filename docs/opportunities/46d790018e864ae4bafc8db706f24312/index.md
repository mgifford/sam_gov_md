- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

## Summary

Solicitation No. N0040626Q0133 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for Multiple Equipment Repair Services in support of Fleet Readiness Center Northwest (FRCNW) at Naval Air Station (NAS) Whidbey Island. The resulting contract will include a base period and two option years. This acquisition is not set aside for small business concerns.
