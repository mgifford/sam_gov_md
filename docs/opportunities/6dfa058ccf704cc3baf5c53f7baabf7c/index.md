The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) has a requirement for Rockwell Emulate 3D Software to support WMS in the implementation of the SAP Material Flow Services (MFS) solution at both Red River and Tinker.. Solicitation is being issued via Open Market Procedures to the Contract Opportunities Website (sam.gov).
