## Summary

SOURCES SOUGHT ANNOUNCEMENT Fleet Logistics Center Puget Sound (FLC PS) is posting a Sources Sought notice, as a means of conducting market research to identify any interested vendors for the procurement of Insulation Blanket/Thermal/Mineral Wool Part(s), in support of SRF-JRMC. The applicable North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code assigned to this requirement is 327993, with a small business size standard of 1,500 employees. The results of this market research may contribute to future procurement process decisions. NO feedback or evaluations will be provided regarding any submission against this Sources Sought notice. Please provide responses to this announcement no later than the posted closing date. DESCRIPTION OF REQUESTED SUPPLIES: OEM "Roxul, Inc. dba Rockwool Fonden" (QTY 200) Insulation Blanket, Thermal, Mineral Wool, SPEC: MIL-PRF-2818, Size: 24” X 48” X 2” THK, DENSITY: 8 LBS/CU FT (P/N: SEAROX SL 620 (128) (NO FACING)). REMARKS: **NO FACING TYPE IS REQUIRED. OMIT METALLIC SUPPORT MEMBERS FROM BOTH SIDES** BIW CATALOG NO. 47111-030. All responses to this Sources Sought notice should be emailed to Walid Aslan (walid.m.aslan.civ@us.navy.mil) and include the following information - Your organization name, physical address, CAGE code, email address, website, telephone number, classification (Small Business/Other Than Small) status under NAICS code 327993. **THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR QUOTE** - submission of any information in response to this Sources Sought notice is purely voluntary and the Government assumes no financial responsibility for any costs incurred. The results of this market research may contribute to future procurement process decisions. No feedback or evaluations will be provided to responses against this Sources Sought notice.
