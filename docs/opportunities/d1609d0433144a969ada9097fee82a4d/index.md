# Flight Control Surface Free Play Inspection Set

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 09:52:22.656-04
- Solicitation Number: N68335-26-Q-1145

## Summary

This effort is for the manufacture and delivery of quantity one (1) of the F/A-18 Flight Control Surface Freeplay Inspection Set, P/N 3867AS1645-1. This procurement is in support of the VFC-204 Sand-up effort at NOLA. It is the Government's intention to issue a Small Business Set-Aside (SBSA) because there is a reasonable expectation that offers will be obtained from at least two (2) responsible small business concerns and the award will be made at a fair market price.
