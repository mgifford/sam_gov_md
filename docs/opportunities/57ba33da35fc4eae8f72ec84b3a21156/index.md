# ADN Schneider Maintenance Service Contract

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-19 16:37:49.407-04
- Solicitation Number: N6426726Q4079

## Summary

The Naval Surface Warfare Center Corona Division (Corona Division) is issuing this Sources Sought Notice as a means of conducting market research and to inform industry that the Government intends to purchase preventative maintenance and repair services from Schneider Electric (CAGE code 0MG77). This notice is intended to fulfill the requirements of DFARS PGI 206.302-1. Notwithstanding, Corona Division is seeking information from potential, responsible sources that have the interest and capability to provide preventative maintenance and repair services in accordance with the attached Draft Statement of Work (SOW). This notice is published for informational purposes only. It is not a request for competitive quotes. However, all responsible sources may submit their Capability Statement, which if received by 02 June 2026, shall be considered by Corona Division. The Capability Statement shall also include the following information: 1. Company name, address, point of contact, CAGE code and UEI number. 2. Business size for NAICS code 811210 3. Estimated unit price and lead time for market research purposes. 4. Industry commentary on the Draft SOW. Responses must be submitted in writing to the following procurement specialist via email: Julia Hunt, julia.m.hunt3.civ@us.navy.mil, Responses shall be received no later than 12:00pm PDT on 02 June 2026. Email should reference Sources Sought Notice N6426726Q4079 in the subject line. Disclaimer: This Sources Sought Notice is published for informational purposes only and not a request for competitive proposals. This notice does not constitute a solicitation and shall not be construed as a commitment by the Government. Responses in any form are not offers and the Government is under no obligation to award a contract as a result of this announcement. No funds are available to pay for preparation of responses to this announcement. Any information submitted by respondents to this notice is strictly voluntary.
