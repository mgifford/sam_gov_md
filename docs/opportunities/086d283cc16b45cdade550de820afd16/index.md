# Definity Ultrasound Contract Agent

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-20 15:18:11.287-04
- Solicitation Number: W81K00-26-Q-A998

## Summary

The Brooke Army Medical Center Health Contracting Branch (BAMC HCB), Medical Readiness Contracting Office – West (MRCO-W), U.S. Army Health Contracting Activity (USAHCA), Army Medical Command (MEDCOM), JBSA Fort Sam Houston, Texas intends to award a Firm Fixed-Price, sole source contract to Lantheus Medical Imaging, Inc., a Small Business (SB), under the authority of FAR § 13.101(b), only one source reasonably available (e.g., urgency, exclusive licensing agreements, brand-name or industrial mobilization). This requirement is for Definity Ultrasound Contract Agents needed for the omptimization of the visual imaging of the ultrasound frequency signals that refract between the structure and the piezoelectric crystals in the probe to improve the visualization of cardiac structures and doppler signals at BAMC, JBSA Fort Sam Houston, Texas. Award will be on or about 19 June 2026. The applicable NAICS code for this requirement is 325412, “Pharmaceutical Preparation Manufacturing”. The Small Business Size Standard is 1,300 employees. This notice is not a request for competitive quotes. No requests for capability briefings will be honored as a result of this notice and any information provided will not be used as part of any subsequent solicitation requirement. No telephonic inquires will be honored. A determination by the Government not to compete with this proposed contract based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE PROPOSALS. This is a notice of intent to award a sole source contract for Definity Ultrasound Contract Agents. The Contracting Officer determined that the circumstances of the contract action deem only one source reasonable available. BAMC HCB intends to make a sole source award to Lantheus Medical Imaging, Inc., 331 Treble Cove Rd, North Billerica, Massachusetts, 01862-2849.
