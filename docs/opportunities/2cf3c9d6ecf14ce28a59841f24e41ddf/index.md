# TELOSPIC DRAWBAR, NRP

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 11:39:27.352-04
- Solicitation Number: SPE8EF26R0005

## Summary

This is a Pre solicitation Notice only. This is NOT a Request for Proposal, Request for Quote or Invitation for Bid. DLA Troop Support anticipates the award of a Firm Fixed Price Contract for the manufacture and delivery of TELOSPIC DRAWBAR, NRP NSN 1740-01-622-1432, which are made I/A/W military specification NR 3000303992AS200. The anticipated Request For proposal will be posted on or around May 25, 2026. Support of this item will be based on DLA Direct Deliveries; the material would be shipped to either the New Cumberland, PA or Tracy, CA. DLA stock distribution locations, where the material is delivered to DLA customers throughout the United States. The acquisition is 100% Set-Aside for Small Business. The estimated dollar value is $1,500,000.00. The item description is fully competitive and will be available during the open period of the solicitation. Copies of the RFP will be available on DLA Internet Bid Broad System DIBBS at https://www.dibbs.bsm.dla.mil. All responsible sources may submit an offer which shall be considered by DLA Troop Support.
