# Air Compressor with Remote Fill Station

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 04:09:51.025-04
- Solicitation Number: W912CM-26-Q-A019

## Summary

The Regional Contracting Office intends to issue a solicitation for the procurement, delivery, and potential installation of an air compressor system equipped with a remote fill station. This equipment will directly support the Directorate of Emergency Services (DES) Fire Department operations. The Government anticipates awarding a Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP) contract as a result of the upcoming solicitation. A detailed technical specifications, capacity requirements, and host-nation electrical compliance standards will be provided within the solicitation package. Competition for this requirement is restricted to vendors maintaining a primary place of business within Germany and the surrounding European area. This restriction ensures rapid delivery, localized warranty support, and compliance with regional maintenance standards for life-safety equipment. IMPORTANT: Quoters must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) database at time of submission of quotes. For registration instructions visit the following website: www.sam.gov
