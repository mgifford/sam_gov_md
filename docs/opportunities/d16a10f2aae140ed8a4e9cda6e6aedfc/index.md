# Special Notice: Forthcoming Solicitation for Building Control System (BCS) and Utility Control System (UCS) Maintenance and Support Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-13 13:07:51.685-04
- Solicitation Number: SP470525R002026

## Summary

SPECIAL NOTICE Subject: Forthcoming Solicitation for Building Control System (BCS) and Utility Control System (UCS) Maintenance and Support Services Notice ID: SP470525R002026 (new solicitation number SP470526R0003) Agency: Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) Office: Contract Services Office (DCSO) DCSO-R3 NAICS Code: 561210 - Facilities Support Services Product Service Code (PSC): J059 - MAINT/REPAIR/REBUILD OF EQUIPMENT-ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT COMPONENTS Set-Aside: Small Business Set-Aside Place of Performance: Multiple Locations (see below) Description: The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) is issuing this special notice to inform interested parties of a forthcoming solicitation, which is expected to be posted within the next 30 days. DLA intends to award multiple Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contracts to provide comprehensive support for the operation, maintenance, repairs, service, testing, inspection, and certification of Building Control Systems (BCS) and Utility Control Systems (UCS). This includes both routine Preventive Maintenance (PM) and non-routine/ad-hoc requirements. The requirement will cover the following five DLA locations: Defense Supply Center Columbus, OH (DSCC) Defense Supply Center Richmond, VA (DSCR) DLA Distribution Depot, San Joaquin, CA (DDJC) DLA Distribution Center Susquehanna, PA (DDSP) DLA Headquarters Complex, Fort Belvoir, VA (HQC) This notice is for informational and planning purposes only and does not constitute a formal solicitation. The official solicitation, when posted, will provide all details and instructions for submitting proposals. Interested parties are encouraged to monitor the official government point of entry for the release of the solicitation.
