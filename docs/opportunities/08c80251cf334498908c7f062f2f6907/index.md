# Acoustic Device Countermeasure MK 2

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 10:08:34.046-04
- Solicitation Number: N00024-26-R-6262

## Summary

Team Submarine intends to solicit and award a sole source contract to ULTRA Maritime, 115 Bay State Drive, Braintree, MA 02184. The contract will be for production, testing, associated program management, data management, systems engineering, non-recurring engineering (NRE), configuration management (CM), hardware reliability and maintainability, and integrated logistic support required to support fabrication of the ADC MK2 and ADC MK2 training devices. The contemplated contract will be awarded pursuant to 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(1) as implemented by FAR 6.302-1. Parties interested in subcontracting opportunities should contact ULTRA Maritime directly.
