## Summary

!!!Due to system limitation with adding the NAICS 541810, this requirement will be set aside for EDWOSB as documented on the SF 1449!!! Provide a comprehensive marketing package to the California Army National Guard (CAARNG) for recruitment and retention goals. The contractor shall provide services to generate qualified recruitment leads, offer a customized lead capture and nurturing solution, and support the Recruiting and Retention Battalion (RRB) leadership, recruiters, and marketing staff. CAARNG’s RRB Marketing mission is to promote the CAARNG to gain interest and attention of prospective recruits. The CAARNG RRB has a dual mission that is not understood well, and the general public has a hard time distinguishing National Guard from Active Duty. The primary target market is non-prior service (NPS) individuals 17-34 years of age. The CAARNG requires the services of a contractor with experience providing marketing solutions for the Army National Guard to develop and execute a comprehensive marketing program that includes, branding, engagements, leads, and recruiter support to help CAARNG achieve recruitment and retention goals.
