## Summary

This is a Combined Synopsis/Solicitation for the acquisition of Tactical Data Links Studio (TDLS) Software License. - this software provides an integrated software environment for ccomprehensive testing, simulation, validation, and real-time analysis of military tactical data link (TDL) communications Please see attached solicitation documents for further details. This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products prepared in accordance with RFO Part 12. This announcement constitutes only the solicitation. A quotation is being requested, and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation Number: FA930226Q0059 **Please provide the full solicitation number on all packages** NOTICE OF INTENT TO SOLE SOURCE: The Air Force Test Center (AFTC/PZZF) at Edwards AFB intends to award a sole-source, firm-fixed-price purchase order to Aeronix, Inc. (CAGE: IEW38) under the authority of 6.302-1, "Only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements." Market research has determined that only the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), Aeronix, possesses the proprietary software to span the critical telemetry and testing platforms with the legacy equipment at Edwards AFB.
