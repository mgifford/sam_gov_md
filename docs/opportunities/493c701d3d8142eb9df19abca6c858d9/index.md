# Carrier 20 Ton Chiller

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 16:27:49.966-04
- Solicitation Number: FA469026Q0021

## Summary

Amendment 1 published on 5 May 26 to add Questions and Answers. The 28th Contracting Squadron, Ellsworth AFB, South Dakota, has issued this solicitation FA469026Q0021, to compete and award a single Firm Fixed Price contract for 1 Carrier 30RC 20T Air Cooled Chiller. Please note that this is a brand name specific requirement. This will be a 100% Small Business set-aside solicited under the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code 333415 with a size standard of 1,250 Employees. To be eligible to complete for Small Business set-aside awards, vendors must be certified in accordance with current Small Business Administration (SBA) regulations. All interested and responsible entities are invited to submit a quote that will be considered by the 28th Contracting Office at Ellsworth AFB, South Dakota. All vendors should reference RFO 52.212-1 instructions to offerors before submiting a quote. Quotes are to be submitted via email to Joseph Mace at joseph.mace.3@us.af.mil no later than 8 May 2026 at 3:00 PM MDT.
