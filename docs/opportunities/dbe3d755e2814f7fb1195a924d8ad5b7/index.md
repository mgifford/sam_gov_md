## Summary

O'NEIL PPA COTTONWOOD FUEL BREAK MOWING 2026, BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT, ELKO FIELD OFFICE, ELKO COUNTY, NEVADA This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in Subpart 12.6, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement and attachments constitute the only solicitation; quotes are being requested, and a written solicitation will not be issued. Consider this solicitation (140L3726Q0072) as a Request for Quotes (RFQ). This solicitation documents incorporate provisions and clauses in effect through Federal acquisition Circular 2019-03.
