# VA STLHCS-JC Northeast Parking Structure Solicitation

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-01 19:33:10.528-04
- Solicitation Number: W912DQ26RA023

## Summary

The Kansas City District, United States Army Corps of Engineers (USACE), has a need for design/build construction services to construct a parking structure that will provide a minimum of 627 parking stalls over seven levels (6 stories) of parking. Each level of the structure will be approximately 35,665 square feet and support 1 additional level of parking which may be added at a later date. The structure may be cast in place, or precast. The scope includes electrical and telecommunications rooms, elevators, site work, walk-ways, access points, wayfinding, and a parking space counting system.
