# Maintenance Plan for Mass Spectrometry Instrument

- Agency: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 15:52:19.681-04
- Solicitation Number: 75N98026Q00413

## Summary

The objective of this requirement is to provide preventive maintenance for the LC/MS/MS system to ensure the continued and reliable measurement of dehydroascorbic acid and vitamin C in human and cellular samples. Service Specifications: · The service plan shall provide full-service maintenance coverage for the Waters XEVO TQS micro-LC/MS/MS system. · The coverage shall include Waters FlexCHOICE mySystem service plan for a period of twelve (12) months. · The service plan shall include a minimum of one (1) scheduled Performance Maintenance (PM) visit during the coverage period. · Preventive maintenance shall be performed using Waters Quality Parts and standardized service protocols by a certified Waters Field Service Specialist. · The service plan shall include repair services, including parts, labor, and technical support necessary to maintain system performance. · The service plan shall provide access to trained and authorized service personnel for troubleshooting, repair, and maintenance of the system. · The service level shall be standard, with standard response time for service requests. · The coverage shall include support for all listed system components and modules under the service agreement. · The service plan shall ensure the system operates at peak performance through proactive maintenance and support services. · The equipment covered under this agreement shall not be classified as obsolete or limited support status. Maintenance Requirements - The plan provides preventive maintenance (PM) to keep the system operating at peak performance. - At least one scheduled PM visit is included during the 12-month coverage period. Maintenance is performed using: - Waters Quality Parts - Standardized service protocols - Certified Waters Field Service Specialists - Coverage includes repairs due to normal use, including parts, labor, and service.
