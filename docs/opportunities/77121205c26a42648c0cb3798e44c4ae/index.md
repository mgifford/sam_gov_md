# US EMBASSY LUANDA VEHICLES PURCHASE

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 03:10:37.622-04
- Solicitation Number: 19A05026Q0007

## Summary

4WD SUVs and HIGH ROOF PASSENGER’S VANs. Post requires 2025-2027 model year SUV and passenger VANs (3 each) in previously unsold, new condition as replacement vehicles for current ICASS assets. Post intends to pursue a brand name or equal approach, citing Toyota Land Cruisers and Ford Transit (passenger vans) or equivalent models as the basis, respectively. Toyota and Ford models integrate with the current fleet to simplify support, and parts are the most readily available in Angola’s limited vehicle support market. However, alternatives that meet specifications / stated salient characteristics may be acceptable. Thus, the brand name or equal approach.
