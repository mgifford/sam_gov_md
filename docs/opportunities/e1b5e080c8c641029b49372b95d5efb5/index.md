# NC3 Specialized Engineering and Technical Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-11 14:25:22.157-04
- Solicitation Number: FA229926SNCW2

## Summary

The AFNWC Nuclear Command, Control, and Communications (NC3) Directorate (AFNWC/NC) purpose is to integrate the AN/USQ-225 NC3 Weapon System across the Department of the Air Force, to ensure our Nation’s leaders and warfighters have assured communications anytime, under any conditions. This request is for technical services, modeling & simulation, and Analysis of Alternatives in support of the Air Force NC3 Weapon System
