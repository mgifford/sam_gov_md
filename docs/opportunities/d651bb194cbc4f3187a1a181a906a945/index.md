# FY26 LeMay Center SECURE DOOR FOR SAPCA

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 11:15:08.451-04
- Solicitation Number: FA330026RFI0009

## Summary

The 42nd Contracting Squadron, Maxwell AFB, Alabama is conducting market research to determine the existence of interested sources capable of providing a Vault Door and Installation. This is not a Request for Quote, but instead a market research method to determine if this requirement can be fulfilled via a Governmentwide Acquisition Contract (GWAC) and the availability of small business firms for a potential small business category type set-aside. NO AWARD will be made from this Sources Sought. See the attached draft Statement of Requirements (SOR) for the general description, requirements, and specifications. To assist the Government in developing a potential acquisition strategy, please provide the following information: 1. GENERAL INFORMATON: a. Company name, address, point of contact, voice and fax telephone numbers and e-mail address. b. Type of business, (i.e., small, Sa, woman-owned, Hub Zone, serviced-disabled veteran-owned small business, large business, etc.). c. Under which NAICS code does your company usually provide the requirements described in the attached SOR. d. Please describe any customary commercial contract terms or conditions that you feel would make any resulting contract more effective, if applicable. 2. CAPABILITIES: The Government is seeking qualified, experienced sources interested in meeting the requirements of the attached draft PWS. Please address the following regarding your capabilities in meeting the requirements contained in the attached "draft" PWS: a. Is your company capable of meeting this requirement in accordance with the attached "draft" SOR? b. Is your company capable of meeting all requirements via your existing GWAC? If so, please provide GWAC Schedule Number.
