# Purchase of Vehicle - HEV/ AWD Executive SUV

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 07:36:07.491-04
- Solicitation Number: PR15924060

## Summary

The U.S. Embassy in Tirana invites you to submit a quotation for “Purchase of Vehicle - HEV/ AWD Executive SUV” If you would like to participate, please send an email to TiranaBids@state.gov to obtain the solicitation package with technical details. Proposals must be in English and incomplete proposals will not be accepted. The U.S. Government intends to award a purchase order to the responsible company submitting an acceptable offer at the lowest price. We intend to award a purchase order based on initial quotations, without holding discussions, although we may hold discussions with companies in the competitive range if there is a need to do so. Further details are announced in the solicitation package.
