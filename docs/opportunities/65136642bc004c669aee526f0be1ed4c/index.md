## Summary

NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD TO A SOLE SOURCE This Notice of Intent is not a request for competitive proposals. This is not a solicitation or request for offers. No solicitation package is available, and telephone requests will not be honored. Only one responsible source, and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. The National Park Service, Golden Gate National Recreation Area, located in Marin County, California intends to award a firm fixed price contract to Recology Sonoma Marin located at 3400 Standish Ave. Santa Rosa, California, 95407, United States. This purchase will be made with Sole Source Procedures in accordance with: Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Part FAR 6.302-1 Only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. Interested parties may identify their interests and capability to respond to this requirement within fifteen (15) calendar days of the publication of this notice. Any response to this notice must show clear and convicting evidence that competition would be advantageous to the Government. Information received will be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement. A determination by the Government not to compete this proposed purchase order based upon response to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. No reimbursement for any cost connected with providing capability information will be provided. The point of contact is: Patty Payne, email: patty_payne@ios.doi.gov. Please reference this Notice of Intent number on your correspondence and in the Subject line of your email. This notice will close on: 5/19/2026 at 12:00 p.m., Pacific Daylight Time.
