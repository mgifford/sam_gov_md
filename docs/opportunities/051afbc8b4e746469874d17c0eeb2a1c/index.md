- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

- Posted: 2026-05-04 10:11:07.311-04

## Summary

This soliciation was amended on 4 May 2026. This is an RFQ for the subject requirement to be awarded in accordance with Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) 12 procedures, the terms and conditions, specific information, instructions and evaluation criteria outlined in this RFQ and attachments. The Government intends to award a, single Firm Fixed Price (FFP) Purchase Order for the subject requirement. The 20th Air Support Operations Squadron (ASOS) requires the BNO Tactical Operator Kit containing INVISIO X7 Headsets, V50 4 PTT (2-Com, Gen II), Com Cables (800 MM), and Foam Tips (Snap on).
