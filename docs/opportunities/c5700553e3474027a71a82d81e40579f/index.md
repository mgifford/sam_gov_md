# Memphis, TN Regional Processing Distribution Center (RPDC) Matrix East West Sorter (MEWS)

- Agency: POSTAL SERVICE
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 14:05:25.95-04
- Solicitation Number: 3APCKG-26-B-0003-MOD03
- Awardee: DESIGNED CONVEYOR SYSTEMS, LLC Franklin TN 37067 USA
- Award Amount: 16203651.00

## Summary

Designed Conveyor Systems LLC (DCS) has been awarded a non-competitive contract modification for the Matrix East West Sorter (MEWS) that will be deployed to the Memphis, TN Regional Processing Distribution Center (RPDC). The value of the award is $16,203,651 for the purchase of hardware, components, and services for the design, integration, installation, and commissioning of the Memphis MEWS.
