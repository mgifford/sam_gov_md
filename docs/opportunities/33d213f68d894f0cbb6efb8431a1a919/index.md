## Summary

THIS IS A SOURCES SOUGHT ANNOUNCEMENT ONLY. This notice does not constitute a commitment by the Government. It is issued for market research purposes to identify interested and capable sources and to determine the appropriate level of competition for the requirement. Information submitted is voluntary, and the Government will not pay for any costs incurred in responding. The 9th Contracting Squadron at Beale AFB, CA is conducting market research to identify interested and capable sources for the following requirements. Company Info: Name, address, POC, phone, email, CAGE, UEI, business size/socio-economic status (e.g., Small Business, HUBZone, 8(a)). 2.
