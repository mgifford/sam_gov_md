# 99CES - PKA - WT16747339 - Install Lift Station Disconnect B1730

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 17:33:15.289-04
- Solicitation Number: FA486126Q0010

## Summary

Provide single line plans. Provide electrical plan to show conduit route. Provide and install (1) Disconnect sized to serve existing lift station load. Provide and install an electrical pathway from the new disconnect to T1775 transformer. Pathway will be surface mounted to building 1730 and then trenched from the edge of the building to T1775.All work will be done in accordance with UFC, IFS, NEC, ICD, local, and state codes. Proposals will be evaluated using lowest price, technically acceptable. A site visit will take on 5/21/2026 at 10AM PDT, meeting in front of building 1730s main entrance. Please ensure your base pass request has been submitted to me by 2PM PDT on 5/18/2026 if you do not have base access to Nellis AFB to ensure you have access by the date of the site visit. Questions are due by close of business 5/26/2026. Answers will be provided via a solicitation amendment.
