# Industry Day for FY27 South Atlantic Division (SAD) Regional Harbor Dredging Contract (RHDC)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-05 15:06:26.713-04
- Solicitation Number: W912PM26BA010

## Summary

The Industry Day Meeting for the South Atlantic Division (SAD) Regional Harbor Dredging Contract (RHDC) will occur on 18 May 2026 at 13:00 AM EST via Teams. The areas of consideration for these dredging services are: Savannah Harbor, Georgia; Wilmington Harbor, North Carolina; and Charleston Harbor, South Carolina. All interested dredging companies are encouraged to attend. In addition, breakout sessions will be provided with individual contractors following the group industry day meeting. Please indicate with your RSVP if you would like to request a breakout meeting. Please RSVP no later than 11AM ET 15 May 2026 to both Ros Shoemaker and Dina Curl at Rosalind.m.shoemaker@usace.army.mil and diana.d.curl@usace.army.mil if you plan to attend the Industry Day and/or request a breakout session. A Teams calendar invite will be sent out to all RSVP’s.
