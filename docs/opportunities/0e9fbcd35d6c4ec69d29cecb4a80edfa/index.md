# NEW Electric Scissor Lift, NIST, Gaithersburg, MD

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 12:12:50.729-04
- Solicitation Number: 1333ND26QNB190212

## Summary

SEE ATTACHMENT FOR FULL DETAILED SOLICITAITON. May 22, 2026 - Published Amendment 0001 incorporating 1 RFI May 28, 2026 - Published Amendment 0002 incorporating 1 RFI May 29, 2026 - Published Amendment 0003 incorporating 1 RFI New Electric Scissor Lift: Provide one new electric scissor lift that meets the minimum specification requirements below, and in accordance with attached statement of work. Tasks: Task 1: Provide a new scissor lift that meets the minimum Specification Requirement. Task 2: Delivery and demonstration of the scissor lift and operating features by an authorized manufacturer?s representative. Task 3: Initial training and demonstration for four operators by an authorized manufacturer?s representative. Minimum Specification Requirement: Req 1: Provide one electric scissor lift (110VAC charger included) for indoor use on flat, level, and hard surfaces only Req 2: Minimum 25-foot platform height. Req 3: Capacity at maximum height 500 lbs. Req 4: Folding rails. Req 5: Zero turning radius Req 6: Minimum 36-inch deck extension Req 7: Dual flashing warning lights. Req 8: Tilt alarm Req 9: 3-year warranty
