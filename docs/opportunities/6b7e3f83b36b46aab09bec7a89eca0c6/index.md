Brief Description of Supplies/ Services required and the intended use/Estimated Amount: The products proposed for purchase are upgraded end of life Phacoemulsification consoles and upgraded Ozil Handpieces. Unity CS CTP is a multifunctional surgical cataract system indicated for use in anterior segment ophthalmic surgeries. Used to facilitate management of fluid as well as removal, cutting, and coagulation of ocular materials. Alcon 4D phacoemulsification Handpieces deliver up to 2x faster nucleus removal than Ozil and 41% less energy into the eye. Unique characteristics that limit availability to only one source, with the reason no other supplies or services can be used: Alcon Unity CS CTP feature many first-to-market technologies designed to deliver transformative surgical innovation for cataract surgery. Unity CS is designed to allow cataract surgeons to operate with increased stability and efficiency at more physiologic (normal) conditions through its Intelligent Fluidics functionality. This new technology maintains a consistent IOP (Intraocular Pressure) level to help support patient comfort during surgery, while allowing for higher average vacuum during lens removal. Additionally, the system offers two surgical performance enhancements: the Unity Intelligent Sentry real time occlusion break sensing and dual venting that is better at maintaining anterior chamber stability. And Thermal Sentry is the first phacoemulsification handpiece that features a thermal sensor that works with a proprietary algorithm to estimate incision temperature in real-time and regulate ultrasound power. 4D phacoemulsification handpieces integrate irrigation, aspiration, and emulsification to simultaneously maintain or inflate the anterior chamber, emulsify the lens, and aspirate the lens material from the eye. This is 2x faster nucleus removal than an Ozil with 40% less energy into the eye. This advanced technology gives the Veteran less discomfort during the procedure, shorter operating time with better outcomes Description of market research conducted and results or statement why it was not conducted: Market research not conducted by the End User due to our facility currently utilizing Alcon cataract disposables, that are used with current compatible devices, with Unity CS CTP for useÂ during phacoemulsification and removal of cataracts.
