The new Sleep Lab will have four (4) sleep study rooms with adjoining toilet shower and associated support spaces as follows: Reception area Waiting room Sleep Study monitoring room Nourishment room Soiled Utility Clean Linen Staff Locker room Physician s office Clinic Workstations Exam rooms Additional support spaces may be included per staff requirements, Space Planning Criteria PG-18-9 212 Pulmonary Medicine Service and Design Guide PG-18-12 Pulmonary Medicine Service.
