## Summary

PRESOLICITATION NOTICE Contractor shall provide for and furnish all labor, materials, equipment, qualified supervision, and other items designated in this contract in accordance with the specifications and drawings to complete Project # 695-22-123 Upgrade North Campus Medium Voltage Feeders. The primary purpose of this project is to convert the current Milwaukee VA Campus north underground medium voltage electrical distribution system from a radial system to a composite primary loop radial system. The primary loop radial system will enable improved redundancy, maintainability, safety, isolation capability, and fault detection. Work in the project primarily includes addition and replacement of underground medium voltage feeders, trenching, addition and replacement of electrical pad-mounted equipment. The solicitation, specifications, and drawings will be posted on Contact Opportunities. There will be a pre-bid conference held on or about May 27, 2026, for this project and all prospective bidders are strongly encouraged to attend. Details of the exact date and time of the pre-bid conference will be included in the solicitation package.
