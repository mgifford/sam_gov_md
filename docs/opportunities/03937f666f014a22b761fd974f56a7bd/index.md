# RFI: Integration of Advanced Medium Mobile Power Sources (AMMPS) Kits Amendment 1

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-06 12:19:08.352-04
- Solicitation Number: W909MY-26-R-TRLR

## Summary

Amendment 1 The purpose of this amendment is to address questions submitted by industry regarding the original RFI posting. Accordingly, the following updates have been made and are provided as attachments to this notice: Revised RFI Document: Please review the updated RFI for requirements clarifications. Associated Part Drawings: Supplemental technical drawings have been provided to assist in the development of responses. Extension of Response Deadline: To ensure industry has sufficient time to review the revised documentation and incorporate these updates into their submissions, the response deadline is hereby extended. Original Response Deadline: May 11, 2026, at 3:30 PM EST New Response Deadline: May 13, 2026, at 3:30 PM EST __________ The Department of the Army is conducting market research in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Part 10 to identify sources capable of integrating Advanced Medium Mobile Power Sources (AMMPS) Kits onto either the 1-ton Light Tactical Trailer Heavy Chassis (LTT-HC) or the 2.5-ton M200A1 trailer. The Government owns the competitive Technical Data Package (TDP) for this integration, which will be provided as Government Furnished Information (GFI) under subsequent competitive action. This RFI is for informational and planning purposes only and does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP) or a promise to issue an RFP in the future. The Government will not award a contract on the basis of this RFI, nor will it pay for any information submitted by respondents.
