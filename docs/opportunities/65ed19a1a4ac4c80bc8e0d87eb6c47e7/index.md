## Summary

**Amendment 1** The purpose of this Amentment is to: 1-Include maps, latitude/longitude coordinates for possible staging and nearest fuel areas. 2-This solicitation is for 2 Water Trucks only. Application of Magnesium Chloride is a separate solicitation. 3.Closing date/time remains the same of Tuesday, May 19, 2026 at 1:00 PM local time (Mountain Time). This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1240LS26Q0033 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for The Salmon-Challis National Forest, Idaho, is in need of two water trucks (with operators) to support road preparation and application of Chloride dust palliatives on the Salmon River Road (60030), Stanley-Landmark (40579), and Dagger-Boundary (40568) roads. Watering shall be performed to prepare the road by pre-wetting the road to support grading and application of the dust palliative product. The application of chloride dust palliatives requires that the roads be pre-wetted to ensure that the chloride brine penetrates the road. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 532120 Truck, Utility Trailer and RV (Recreational Vehicle Rental and Leasing The small business size standard is $47 Million This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
