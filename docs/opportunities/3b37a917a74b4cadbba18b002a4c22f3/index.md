# 3BDE JCLC Marseilles Meals

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-28 15:23:26.465-04
- Solicitation Number: W9124D26QA140

## Summary

The Mission and Installation Contracting Command (MICC) - Fort Knox is requesting quotes to support the Cadet Command Junior Cadet Leadership Challenge (JCLC). The requirement is located at the Marseilles Training Center, 1700 Army Road, Marseilles, IL 61341. The selected contractor shall provide meal services for cadets participating in the JCLC camp for the period of performance from 8 June 2026 to 26 June 2026. Detailed requirements are provided in the attached supporting documentation.
