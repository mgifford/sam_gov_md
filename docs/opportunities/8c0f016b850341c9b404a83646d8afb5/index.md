## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial construction prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested, and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 127EAX26Q0022 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for the Gibb Wash Bridge Replacement on the Coronado National Forest. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification System Code is 237310. The small business size standard is $45 Million. This acquisition is a total Small Business set-aside.
