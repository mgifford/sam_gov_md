## Summary

Supports the Technology Advancement Group (TAG), Joint Modernized Handheld Increment 1 (JMHH Inc 1) Global Positioning System (GPS) unit requirement. The JMHH Inc 1 GPS is critical for the F-15, F-16, F/A-18, and JDAM programs, encompassing both Foreign Military Sales (FMS) and other partnership initiatives. Successful integration of the JMHH GPS enables the use of modernized, GPS-enabled weapons and the interpretation of their data by aircraft and weapon systems. Currently, software development and integration are underway to facilitate data retrieval and translation. The JMHH Inc 1 GPS unit is essential for providing M-code capability to our customers for testing and integration. Without it, delivery of this crucial capability is impossible. Training Quantity: 1 Ea. 3. DLA Fees (if applicable) Quantity: 1 Ea. 4. Shipping Quantity: 1 Ea
