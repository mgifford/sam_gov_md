# AMARG B-Series Maintenance Stands

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 17:24:06.434-04
- Solicitation Number: FA487726QA099

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products or commercial services prepared in accordance with RFO Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Offers are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation FA4877-26-Q-A099 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ). This acquisition is a total set-aside for small business concerns. This solicitation incorporates provisions and clauses by reference. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. The Government intends to award a Firm Fixed-Price Contract for the following commercial products: CLIN 0001: B-1 Hydraulic Maintenance Platform/Stands PN: 2001098 - Qty 13 EA CLIN 0002: B-4 Hydraulic Maintenance Platform/Stands PN: 2003642 - Qty 17 EA The contractor shall provide B-Series Maintenance Stands as identified above which meet all specification and performance requirements as identified in Attachment 3 – Salient Characteristics. The delivery shall occur NLT 6 months ARO. All items shall be delivered to the 309th Aerospace Maintenance and Regeneration Group (AMARG) located at Davis-Monthan AFB in Tucson, Arizona – FOB DESTINATION to zip code 85707. The Point of Contact for this solicitation, Contracting Officer, Patricia Murray, can be reached at (520)228-3095 or patricia.murray.6@us.af.mil.
