# 4th Annual NAVFAC Hawaii Government Procurement Industry Forum

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-22 18:34:05.195-04
- Solicitation Number: N62478

## Summary

The 4th Annual NAVFAC, HI, Government Procurement Industry Forum will occur August 5, 2026 (revised from July 28, 2026) at the Oahu Veterans Center (1298 Kukila St. Honolulu, HI, 96818) from 8:00 am to 12:30 pm. This is an annual event targeted to vendors seeking to do business with NAVFAC, HI and enhance their knowledge of the agency's mission and goals. Presenters will cover topics regarding all items NAVFAC, HI related and also provide key tips and best practices when seeking to do business with the Government. This is a great opportunity to enhance your knowledge on a variety of government procurement topics and mission objectives pertaining to the agency. Items will be presented specifically by the organization’s subject matter experts with topics including the following (subject to change): Procurement forecasts, Agency Operations/Execution, Cyber Security, Environmental, and Contracting requirements. Additional outside government peer agencies will also present their organizational program updates. This is also an enormous opportunity to network and collaborate with industry partners and government procurement representatives in one location. Great for small vendors seeking subcontracting opportunities. The forum is a stand-alone NAVFAC, HI, outreach event; all topics will be directly related to the agency’s mission. The event is FREE and targeted to ALL industry partners (current, previous, and potential). *** There are NO registration requirements; seating is first come, first serve until max capacity is reached. Use below Eventbrite link to confirm your interest in the event. Be sure to complete the ticket request form on Eventbrite.
