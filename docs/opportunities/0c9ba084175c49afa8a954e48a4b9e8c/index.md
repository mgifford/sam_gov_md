# Sanitation and Refuse Services, Cordova Alaska

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-22 08:24:28.391-04
- Solicitation Number: W91ZRU26QA016

## Summary

The Alaska Army National Guard (AKARNG) requires a contractor to provide all necessary personnel, equipment, and materials for comprehensive trash and human waste removal services. These services will support the AKARNG's Annual Training in Cordova, Alaska. Scope of Work: The contractor shall be responsible for the delivery, maintenance, and final removal of sanitation equipment. The specific requirements include: Equipment Delivery: Deliver and set up four (4) portable toilets, one (1) handwashing station, and one (1) 6-yard dumpster. All equipment must be fully serviceable and ready for use upon delivery. Cleaning and Maintenance: Provide weekly sanitation services, including pumping, cleaning, and restocking of portable toilets and the handwashing station. Perform weekly refuse extraction for the 6-yard dumpster. Ensure sanitation units do not exceed 75% capacity and handwash stations remain at least 25% stocked. All operations must comply with Federal, State of Alaska, and local health and safety guidelines. Final Removal and Site Cleanup: At the conclusion of the contract period, remove all provided equipment from the site. Ensure the area is left clean, free of residual waste, and restored to its original condition. Period of Performance: 01 August 2026 – 29 August 2026
