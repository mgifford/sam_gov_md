## Summary

Installation of 360-degree emergency lights and sirens along with vaults in our vehicles is vital for several reasons. These tools enable the team to signal the need for immediate action and heightened alertness, ensuring that all parties involved, whether internal personnel or external agencies, recognize the seriousness of the situation and respond accordingly. This capability allows for prompt initiation of security protocols and swift access to secure areas during emergencies.
