## Summary

TECHNICAL SERVICES FOR CALIBRATION OF MAIN PROPULSION DIESIL ENGINE GOVERNORS ONBOARD USCGC ALDER 1. Background: This requirement is for the contractor to calibrate both Main Propulsion Diesel Engine’s (MPDE) 723+ electronic and EGB-13P mechanical governors that manage engine speed and load sharing. 2. Scope: The Contractor shall provide all personnel including technical support, labor, travel, parts, tools, and test equipment to accomplish tasks. The Contractor shall provide the services of a qualified Tech Rep, who is familiar with the Woodward 723+ electronic and EGB-13P mechanical governor controls. The Tech Rep will be responsible for the following: • Advise on manufacturer's proprietary information pertinent to the system. • Assist with proper repair methods and ensure compliance with manufacturer's procedures and standards during calibration of the equipment/systems. • Ensure compliance with manufacturer's procedures and standards during calibration. 3. Objective: The objective of this requirement is to properly groom both Woodward 723+ electronic governors to ensure proper operation, settings, load sharing, auto declutch functions, and alarm settings/operation; and to properly groom both Woodward EGB-13P mechanical governors to ensure proper operation and settings to include proper setting of ball-head backup function and settings. 4. Tasks: The contractor shall provide labor to calibrate the governor IAW TP 3593 723+ Digital Control, and TP 3518 Main Diesel Engine – Model 3608/1, to include but not limited to: 4.1. Calibrate electronic and mechanical governors 4.2. Ensure safe operation of all modes and handle positions though sea trials. 4.3. Train Coast Guard Personnel on calibration procedures and setpoints. 5. Delivery: Contractor shall provide a final report within 3 days of contract period of performance completion. 6. Government furnished property: N/A 7. Security Requirement: Base access is required. Vendor shall have a valid driver’s license and show identification. A CG representative from the cutter will sponsor and escort vendor to the cutter. 8. Place of performance: Coast Guard Sector San Francisco 1 Yerba Buena Rd. San Francisco, CA 94130 9. Period of performance: 31 May – 15 June 2026, or 06-25 July 2026 10. Points of Contact: 10.1. USCG-SFLC-IBCTPL Asset Management: EMCS Daniell Hurtado, Daniell.E.Hurtado@uscg.mil, (510) 290-8808 10.2.
