## Summary

Knovel 35/AI Enhanced ebook annual subscription (all web-based, no software required); for the base period of 06/01/2026 - 05/31/2027 + 4 option years. The Knovel 35/AI Enhanced e-book database collection is a unique collection of specialized engineering and math and science e-books. Also includes modules on Fire Protection engineering and emergency response, Industrial engineering and operations management, Manufacturing engineering, Welding engineering and materials joining, Optics and photonics, and Safety and industrial hygiene, which directly support the USMMA Marine Engineering curriculum. Contractor to submit 1-2 page document for quote. Quote should list base year pricing and each option year. Quotation deadlines are due by May 07, 2026 at 2:30PM via electronic mail (e-mail).
