# NASA Wallops Flight Facility Mixed Metals Term Contract

- Agency: GENERAL SERVICES ADMINISTRATION
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 17:45:51.198-04
- Solicitation Number: 2-1-QSC-T-26-054

## Summary

INVITATION FOR BIDS (IFB) NOTICE 2-1-QSC-T-26-054 DEMIL A Mixed Metals Scrap Term Contract NASA Wallops Flight Facility – Wallops Island, VA 23337 NOTE: Amendment 1 has been issued for this solicitation. Please see the AMENDMENTS section at the end of this summary. The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) is issuing this Invitation for Bids (IFB) for the sale and recurring removal of Government-generated DEMIL A Mixed Metals Scrap located at NASA Wallops Flight Facility, Wallops Island, Virginia. This solicitation establishes a term contract for the sale of scrap property, with removal required as a condition of sale. The successful bidder shall remove scrap material on a recurring, as-needed basis in coordination with the Agency Government Representative (AGR), and in accordance with all site-specific operational, security, and safety requirements. OVERVIEW Commodity: DEMIL A Mixed Metals Scrap Location: NASA Wallops Flight Facility, 34200 Fulton Street, Wallops Island, VA 23337 Contract Type: Term Contract (3-year base with up to two 1-year options) Estimated Volume: Approximately 185,000 pounds annually Disposal Requirement: Material must be recycled for material content only SOLICITATION ACCESS This solicitation is available for review and bidding on SAM.gov and GSA Auctions. GSA Auctions Link: https://gsaauctions.gov/auctions/preview/360492 All solicitation documents, including the IFB, Amendment 1, and Appendices, are located in the Documents section of the GSA Auctions listing. SUBMISSION INFORMATION Submission Method: Email (sealed bid submission) Bid Submission Deadline: June 2, 2026 – 5:00 PM Central Time Bid Opening: June 4, 2026 – Time provided via email notification KEY REQUIREMENTS (MANDATORY) MANDATORY Pre-Bid Site Inspection Required (IFB Section 3.3) Submission must include: Appendix A – Site Verification & Bidder Certification Appendix B – Bid Sheet & SF 114 Appendix C – Removal Execution Plan Pricing is based on a percentage of a published market index. Certified weight tickets are required; contractor-owned scales are not permitted. Performance Guarantee of $15,000 required post-award. All amendments must be acknowledged. Failure to meet these requirements may result in the bid being deemed non-responsive. SITE ACCESS / SECURITY NASA Wallops Flight Facility is a secured Federal installation. Advance coordination is required for all site access. Visitor registration and approval are required prior to arrival. Access is restricted to U.S. citizens with valid identification. REAL ID requirements may apply. All inspection scheduling must be coordinated with the Government.
