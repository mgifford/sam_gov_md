# H Dept Weapons Control and Integration Prototyping and Fabrication

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 14:47:30.482-04
- Solicitation Number: N0017826R0010

## Summary

Please find attached Solicitation No. N0017826R0010 (Draft Solicitation No. N0017827R4402) in support of the Weapons Control and Integration Department (H Department), Naval Surface Warfare Center, Dahlgren Division (NSWCDD). This solicitation actively supports Naval, Joint, and national needs by providing design and fabrication, rapid prototyping, special acquisition, and technology integration related to circuit board design and Radio Frequency (RF) distribution assemblies, mechanical fabrication, fabric assemblies, battery assemblies, and mechanical and electromechanical assemblies. Please follow procedures identified in Section L for both questions about this requirement as well as access to the bid sample technical data package (TDP). Solicitation Amend 0001 was released on 28 May 2026 at 2:30PM.
