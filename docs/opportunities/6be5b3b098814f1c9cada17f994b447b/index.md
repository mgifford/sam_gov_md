# United States Space Force Service Wheel Caps

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-13 12:33:34.206-04
- Solicitation Number: FA706026Q0026

## Summary

This is a Sources Sought Notice to determine the availability and technical capability of small businesses to provide the required products. The U.S. Space Force (USSF) Honor Guard, located on Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling, Washington, D.C., has a requirement for service wheel caps as part of the performance uniform for non-ceremonial missions. These service wheel caps are used on an as-needed basis for joint service training events, including rehearsals at the White House and other high-visibility locations throughout the National Capital Region. The contractor shall provide 264 service wheel caps to The United States Space Force Honor Guard.
