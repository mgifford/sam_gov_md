# RFI – ITOD Ruggedized USB Thumb Drives

- Agency: JUSTICE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-30 16:51:18.618-04
- Solicitation Number: RFI-ITOD

## Summary

The FBIs Information Technology Divisions IT On Demand is conducting market research to identify vendors capable of providing ruggedized USB thumb drives with customization, serialization, and durable housings. This includes two required color variants—red and green—to support internal operational use. This RFI is for planning purposes only and does not constitute a solicitation.Responses must be emailed to Jennifer W. Frederick @ Jwfrederick@fbi.gov by 10am ET May 11, 2026.
