# Blanket Purchase Agreement for Medical Gases and Dry Ice

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 13:22:43.324-04
- Solicitation Number: HT942626QE021

## Summary

This requirement is for a Blanket Purchase Agreement (BPA) which orders may be placed for a variety of compressed and bulk medical grade gas, laboratory and specialty gases, gas-related equipment, supply and services, dry ice pellets, for customer leased and/or government owned cylinders in support of the Defense Health Agency/US Army Medical Research and Development Command and other research labs as needed. Please read attachments carefully. Full list of BPA users in in the body of the RFQ. Questions are due no later than 7 May 2026 at 12:00 pm EDT. Instructions to Offerors start on page 19 of the RFQ. Evaluation Factors start on page 22.
