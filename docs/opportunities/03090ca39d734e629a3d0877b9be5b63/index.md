# NATO Business Opportunity: Provision Of Specialized Air Domain SMEs for Integrated Air Command and Control (IAC2) Support

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-08 14:01:07.443-04
- Solicitation Number: RFP-ACT-JFTC-04-26

## Summary

The NATO Joint Force Training Center (JFTC) in Bydgoszcz, Poland has issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) for the provision of specialized air domain Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) for Integrated Air Command and Control (IAC2) support. The result of this bidding will be an Indefinite Delivery – Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) Commercial Personnel Services Contract in accordance with the JFTC Special Terms and Conditions for Short Term Operational Personnel Services Contracts in Support of Training Events. The reference for the RFP is RFP-ACT-JFTC-04-26 and all correspondence regarding the RFP should include that reference. Bids shall be received at the JFTC Contracting Office, no later than 15 June 2026, 13:00 hours, Central European Time. No bids shall be accepted after this time and date. REQUIREMENT Please see attached. BECOMING ELIGIBLE TO BID A Declaration of Eligibility (DOE) is NOT required for participation. All of the information needed for bidding is included in the RFP.
