# Dresden Lock & Dam Catwalk

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 12:56:12.916-04
- Solicitation Number: W912EK26QA035

## Summary

The US Army Corps of Engineers has a requirement at the Dresden Lock and Dam, in support of the Illinois Waterway Project Office. The requirements are for a catwalk to be used with the Generator at this location. The specifications for this catwalk can be found in the Scope of Work.
