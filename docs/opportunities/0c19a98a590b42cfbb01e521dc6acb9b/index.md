# Yuba Gravel Placement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-27 17:52:54.934-04
- Solicitation Number: W9123826QA014

## Summary

This project is being performed as a requirement of a Biological Opinion issued by the National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS) in accordance with Section 7 of the Endangered Species Act. This project involves placement of a heterogeneous mix of gravel and cobble into the lower Yuba River channel below Englebright Dam.
