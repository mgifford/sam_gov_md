# 15108 DCSA Sources Sought Synopsis

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-26 15:40:31.992-04
- Solicitation Number: 15108

## Summary

The Government requires manufacturing support to produce Digital Computer System Assembly (DCSA) enclosures. These fabricated parts will serve as the physical outer shell for an updated prototype of the Common Munition Bit Reprogramming Equipment (CMBRE) system. See attachments for specifications. In accordance with FAR 15.101(c)(3), responses to this notice are not offers and cannot be accepted by the Government to form a binding contract. The Government will not reimburse respondents for any costs associated with submitting information in response to this notice.
