# PAE RAS Town Hall #2

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-04 18:24:10.226-04

## Summary

“Scaling the Fleet: PAE RAS Town Hall on C-UxS” The Department of Navy continues to build the Arsenal of Freedom with autonomous capabilities. Tuesday, May 19, the Portfolio Acquisition Executive for Robotics and Autonomous Systems (PAE RAS) will hold their second Town Hall. This session will be focused on Counter-Unmanned Systems (C-UxS). These continuing PAE RAS Town Halls are intended to: Counter-UxS Innovation: Collaborate directly with industry pioneers to develop robust defensive strategies against emerging unmanned threats. Fast-Track Defensive Solutions: Accelerate the transition of cutting-edge counter-drone technologies from initial concepts to active field deployment. Town Hall Details Date: Tuesday, May 19, 2026 Time: 1200-1300ET/0900-1000PT Location: MS Teams. This event is for Industry. Registration: Parties interested in participating in this live/streaming event are required to register by 1700 PDT May 14, 2026, using the following form: https://forms.osi.apps.mil/r/DguKPX6C6n . Representatives from the PAE RAS team will follow up with registered parties and provide additional instructions for accessing the event.
