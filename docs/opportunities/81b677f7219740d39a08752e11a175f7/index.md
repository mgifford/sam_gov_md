# USPS Solicitation Request for Information - Government Mail Irradiation

- Agency: POSTAL SERVICE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-12 11:32:19.151-04
- Solicitation Number: 3A-26-A-0052

## Summary

This Request for Information (RFI) is for planning purposes only. It provides an opportunity for the industry to verify the reasonableness and feasibility of the program objectives, as well as to promote competition. This RFI is not to be construed as a commitment by the U.S. Postal Service (“Postal Service” or “USPS”), nor will the Postal Service pay for the development of information submitted in response. The Postal Service does not intend to award a contract based on this RFI or to pay for the information requested. This RFI is issued for the purpose of obtaining market information regarding Government Mail Irradiation Services. Attached documents provide additional details regarding this RFI.
