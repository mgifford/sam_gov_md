# Harry S. Truman (HST) DOS Credit Union Refurbishment

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-12 08:42:00.249-04
- Solicitation Number: 19AQMM26QHSTCREDITUNION

## Summary

The purpose of this sources sought is to conduct market research to identify potential sources capable of completing a refurbishment of the Department of State Credit Union in the Harry S. Truman building. The Government is seeking potential total small business contractors who have specific experience completing construction services in occupied Financial Institutions. This post is only for market research and does not constitute a solicitation. The Government will not accept any proposal/quote submissions and an award isn't anticipated as a result of this notice. The Government will also not accept or respond to any questions.
