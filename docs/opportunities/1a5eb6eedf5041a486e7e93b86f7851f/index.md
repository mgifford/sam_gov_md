# Mobile Command Post Floor Replacement

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-28 18:12:57.94-04
- Solicitation Number: FA462026QA275

## Summary

PURPOSE: The purpose of the sources sought is to identify available authorized businesses to provide the requirement for Mobile Command Post Floor Replacement at Fairchild AFB. This sources sought notice is for informational and planning purposes only. Pricing information is not being requested at this time. It does not constitute a solicitation and is not to be considered as a commitment by the Government. REQUIREMENT: This notice is issued by the U.S. Air Force, 92d Contracting Squadron, Fairchild AFB, WA. This sources sought is seeking to identify responsible potential sources to provide essential Mobile Command Post Floor Replacement supporting Fairchild AFB (FAFB), WA. The Mobile Command Post Floor Repacement services must meet the specifications in accordance with the attached Statement of Work (SOW)(Draft). The current SOW is in draft status. ***The SOW is in draft stautus. ANTICIPATED NAICS/SIZE STANDARD: This requirement falls under the NAICS Code of 238330, Flooring Contractors, with a size standard of $19,000,000. SUBMISSION OF INFORMATION: All firms responding to this sources sought notice are advised that their response to this notice is not a request that will be considered for contract award. All interested parties will be required to respond to the resultant solicitation separately from their response to this sources sought notice. A responsible potential sources shall have registered with the System for Award Management (SAM). The response should be no more than five pages providing the following information: Specifications of the Mobile Command Post Floor Replacement Services Business name, address, CAGE code Primary NAICS Size and socioeconomic classification based on primary NAICS size standard If claiming a specific small business category, provide supporting documentation Point of contact including email and phone number Responses may be submitted through this site or electronically to the following points of contact: SrA Michael Salone at michael.salone@us.af.mil and Mr. Zachery Ray at zachery.ray.2@us.af.mil. Please respond to this request by 11 June 2026 at 10:00 AM Pacific Time.
