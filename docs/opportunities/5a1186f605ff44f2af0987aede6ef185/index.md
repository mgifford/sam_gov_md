# Construction of the ERCIP Wellfield Expansion at Fort Drum, NY

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-05 12:21:18.148-04
- Solicitation Number: W912DS26RA025

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, New York District intends to issue a solicitation package seeking to award a Firm Fixed Price (FFP) contract for the construction of a project at Fort Drum, NY. The project magnitude is between $10- $25 million. This will be a full and open solicitation. Summary of Work: This will be a Design-Build (DB) construction project for the construction of five (5) new water wells. This will include well houses, SCATA systems/controls, site work and grading, utilities, and storm water management features. The project also includes approximately 5 miles of waterline, comms, duct bank, overhead powerlines, and other related work at Fort Drum. A strong site superintendent will be required. This project is planned for advertisement starting in May 2026, with an award planned for by August 2026. The contract duration will be 910 calendar days for the entire project. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is for general construction; 237990 for Other Heavy and Civil Engineering Construction.
