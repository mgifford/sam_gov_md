# Industrial Fluid Cooler

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 08:31:07.185-04
- Solicitation Number: 4420012812470

## Summary

DLA Troop Support Philadelphia is issuing a Request for Proposal (RFP) for an Industrial Fluid Cooler under NSN 4420-01-281-2470. The anticipated award will be for an Indefinite Quantity contract with a 2-year base and 3 one-year option terms. This NSN is sole sourced to Aqua Chem (0EXU3) under part number 534-09630-000. This requirement will utilize FAR 15 Contracting by Negotiation. The solicitation will be issued on May 07, 2026 and close May 08, 2026. For any questions regarding this requirement, please contact Jabari Martin at jabari.martin@dla.mil.
