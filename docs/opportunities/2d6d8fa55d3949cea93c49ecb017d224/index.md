# JCLC Fort Devens Meals

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 16:44:50.659-04
- Solicitation Number: W9124D26QA240

## Summary

This requirement is for JCLC Fort Devens, MA. Refer to the three attachments for all instructions and information pertaining to this requirement. Excerpt from the Performance Work Statement. 1.4.1. The contractor shall furnish meal services as specified herein. Resource working group will occur on 1 June 2026 with two training sessions as indicated Session 1 (15-19 June 2026) and Session 2 (22-26 June 2026). The contractor will have a military dining facility to serve meals from; however, all meals MUST be cooked / prepared at the contractor’s normal place of business to comply with Fort Devens regulatory changes. 1.9.2 The contractor must be on the approved vendor list to serve on Fort Devens. The approved food vendors are located at: https://home.army.mil/devens/index.php/Misson%20and%20Vision/Garrison/logistics-readiness-center/food-service
