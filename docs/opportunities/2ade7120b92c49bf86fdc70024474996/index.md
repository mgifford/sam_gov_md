# Morse Watchmans KeyWatcher Touch Key Cabinet

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-14 13:34:36.483-04
- Solicitation Number: 06191982

## Summary

Defense Finance and Accounting Service (DFAS), Contract Services Directorate, is conducting market research to identify qualified and experienced sources for a KeyWatcher Touch Key Cabinet manufactured by Morse Watchman Inc. This requirement is critical for the security of government facilities. The system must provide seamless integration with the existing Lenel OnGuard access control system and support authentication via the Government Common Access Card (CAC). The ideal provider must be an authorized reseller for Morse Watchman products. The KeyWatcher Touch Key Cabinet must be meet the following minimum requirements: Hardware - Morse Watchman 120-Key KeyWatcher Touch Cabinet & Accessories System Integration - Certified for native integration with Lenel OnGuard® Access Control Regulatory Compliance - Aligns with NIST SP 800-53, 800-63-4, and CNSSI 4009-2015 Identity Management - Fully compatible with PC/SC certified smart card readers (CAC.mil) Qualified sources are invited to respond via email to Gerald Whitsett at gerald.l.whitsett.civ@mail.mil by May 19, 2026, at 11am ET. Submissions must include the following: Company Name and Business Address Primary Point of Contact (Name, Title, Email, Phone) CAGE Code and DUNS (UEI) Number Small Business Status DISCLAIMER: This request for information (RFI) is for market research purposes only. This is not a solicitation or a commitment by the Government to purchase services or hardware. Information received from this notice will not be disclosed outside the government.
