# Program, Project and Mission Support Services (PPMSS) 4th Bridge contract

- Agency: HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Justification
- Posted: 2026-05-05 09:37:24.321-04
- Solicitation Number: PR20157883

## Summary

To provide interim services during transition to a new award contract, only one source is available in the interim to avoid a lapse in services while CBP re-solicits the requirement. Program, Project and Mission Support Services (PPMSS) is to provide professional support to CBP’s OSF offices nationwide to conduct a full spectrum of program and project management support necessary to plan, evaluate, construct, sustain, maintain, and dispose of Program Management Office (PMO) projects and real property for Border Patrol and Air & Marine (BPAM).
