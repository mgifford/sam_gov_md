## Summary

THIS IS A PRE-SOLICITATION NOTICE/SYNOPSIS: The Army Contracting Command – Detroit Arsenal (ACC-DTA proposes to solicit a Firm-Fixed Price (FFP) one time requirement for the Environmental Control Unit (ECU) F100-60 Green and F100-60 Tan. This solicitation will be restricted to HDT Expeditionary Systems, Inc. (CAGE: 92878) under Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 6.302-1(a)(2), “Only One Responsible Source and No other Supplies or Services Will Satisfy Agency Requirements”. This requirement will be solicited under W912CH-25-R-0209: Noun: Environmental Control Unit (ECU) F100-60, Green and Environmental Control Unit (ECU) F100-60, Tan NSN: 4120-01-617-1273 (Green), 4120-01-617-1282 (Tan) MFG Part Number / CAGE Code: Green: 603-H015262-001 / 92878 Tan: 603-H015262-002 / 92878 NAICS: 333415 Inspection/Acceptance/ FOB: Origin/ Origin/ Destination Level of Preservation: Military Level of Packing: A Estimated Quantity: 367 EA Interested vendors shall not contact ACC-DTA seeking the solicitation as it will not be available for at least 15 business days following issuance of this synopsis. CAUTION: The solicitation will be issued electronically as will any amendments thereto. Because of this, the Government is under no obligation and is in fact unable to maintain a bidder mailing list. It is therefore incumbent upon any interested parties to periodically access the above internet address to obtain any amendments which may be issued.
