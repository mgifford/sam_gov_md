# Sole Source EOS M400 Metal 3D Printers Premium Service Contract

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-04-29 07:58:23.782-04
- Solicitation Number: M6700426P0017

## Summary

The Marine Corps Logistics Command, Contracts Department (CONT) intends to solicit and contract with Phillips Federal Division (CAGE 3MPQ0) of Hanover, Maryland on a sole-source basis for two M400-1 Full Service Flex (Premium)-Annual Plans. The North American Industry Classification Standard (NAICS) code assigned is 811310- Maint/Repair/Rebuild Of Equipment- Special Industry Machinery. No solicitation will be posted on this website for this requirement. A Justification and Approval document is attached to this notice. Any potential source may contact the persons indicated above to discuss their ability to offer the required services for future acquisitions.
