# F-16 Power Supply NSN 6130-01-373-0561WF 5 yr IDIQ

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 12:16:10.681-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8212-26-R-0002

## Summary

4/30/2026- At the time of awarding the IDIQ, the Government shall order the minimum qualtity of 5 each which includes First Article (FA) if FA is not waived. In addition, the Government has the option to order more quantities identified as Best Estimate Quantity (BEQ) per year as follows: Year 1- BEQ is 4 each; Year 2- BEQ is 8 each; Year 3- BEQ is 6 each; Year 4- BEQ is 6 each; Year 5- BEQ is 6 each. IDIQ maximun ceiling in $800,000.00. Requirement: Production contract to produce F-16 Power Supply NSN 6130-01-373-0561WF, 5 Year IDIQ Single Award (see attachments for more details on the requirement) Full & Open Competition to Sources Qualified per the Manufacturer Qualification Requirements (MQR) attached. All responsible sources may submit a quotation, bid, or proposal, as appropriate, which will be considered by the agency. If you are a foreign manufacturer, you must notify the Contracting Officer as early as possible and be approved by the Foreign Disclosure Office prior to receiving the technical data package.
