## Summary

NOTICE OF INTENT TO AWARD NAME BRAND PART Anchor NBX Box (BEP Part MAP 5651) The Treasury Common Services Center intends to award a Firm Fixed Price (FFP) contract on behalf of the Office of Supply Chain Management (OSCM). The purchase order will precure two-hundred (200), Anchor, Horizontal, For NBX Box, ZEISER P/N AFD7, KBA P/N 1002406890 for various press machines at the Western Currency Facility (WCF). The anchor NBX Box is proprietary and can only be acquired from either Zeiser Inc. or Koenig & Bauer (K&B) Banknote Solutions. Therefore, the Government intends to award a contract to Zeiser or K&B in accordance with FAR Subpart 6.302-1(a)(2)(c). This notice of intent is not a request for competitive offers/quotes/proposals. A determination not to compete this requirement is solely within the discretion of the Government. Information received will be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement.
