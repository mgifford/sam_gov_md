Project Title: Lake Barkley Paving at Cadiz Recreation Area and Canal Campground Project Location: Lake Barkley, near Cadiz, KY NAICS Code: 237310 Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction PSC: Z1LB Maintenance of Highways, Roads, Streets, Bridges, and Railways Estimated Magnitude of Construction: Between $1M and $3.5M Anticipated Solicitation Issuance Date: Q4 FY2026 Estimated duration of the project: Approxiatley 210 calendar days Anticipated Contract Type: Anticipated project will be a competitive, firm-fixed price contract. Anticipated Acquisition Method: Anticipated project will be acquired using Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) Part 12 Commercial Products and Commercial Services or RFO Part 14 Sealed Bidding High-Level Project Summary: Cadiz Recreation Area - Cadiz Recreation Area consists of approximately 1,350 feet of roadway (3,600 sq. yards at average width of 24 feet) and five parking areas (7,300 sq. yards). Several of the roadway sections and parking areas have significant damage including: alligatoring, longitudinal and transverse (block) cracking, raveling, and shoulder damage. Replace existing CMP culverts with HDPE smooth wall pipe, resurface approximately 10,900 square yards of pavement, install base material and new pavement, and compacted dense-grade aggregate (DGA) as shoulder protection along roadway and parking shoulder areas. Canal Campground - Canal Campground consists of approximately 3 miles of roadway (30,000 sq. yards at average width of 17 feet) and 9 parking areas (7,000 sq. yards). Several of the roadway sections and parking areas have significant damage including: alligatoring, longitudinal and transverse (block) cracking, raveling, and shoulder damage. The purpose of this project is to mill and replace approximately 22,000 sq. yards of roadway and 3,000 sq. yards of parking area within Canal Campground. The contractor will mill and replace the top 2 inches of pavement with a leveling course after preparing the site and applying a tack coat, following Kentucky Transportation Cabinet (KYTC) standards. It is possible that small portions of the roadway and parking areas may need base course repair or leveling prior to repaving. Contractor will remove 12 corrugated metal pipes (CMP) and replace them with smooth wall HPDE corrugated pipes. Minimum Technical Requirements and Critical Features of Work: Kentucky Transportation Cabinet (KYTC) Standards, KYTC SSRBC, and DOT regulations Other Known Challenges: Work will be not able to begin until after the campground and recreation areas have closed for the season and must be complete prior to these areas opening in the following spring. Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Requirement: It is unknown at this time, anticipate this project could require Level 1 (Self) Certification.
