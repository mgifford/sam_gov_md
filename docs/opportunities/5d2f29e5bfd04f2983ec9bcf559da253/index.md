## Summary

The Army Contracting Command New Jersey in support of the 99th Readiness Division has a requirement for Military Funeral Honors. The solicitation and subsequent award is set aside as an 8(a) direct award IAW FAR Subpart 19.108. This will be a single firm fixed price award. The contractor shall furnish all personnel, equipment, supplies, services and materials as noted in the solicitation and Performance Work Statement. This acquisition will be under the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) of 561110 with a size standard of $12,500,000.00. The proposed solicitation number is W15QKN-26-R-A048 and will be projected to distributed on or about 27 May 2026. The due date will be stated on the solicitation. Please note the response date listed with the pre-solicitation notice is not the actual due date.
