# PKA-Giant Voice Equipment -6 CS

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-21 14:08:34.651-04
- Solicitation Number: FA481426Q0072

## Summary

Questions are Due on 28 May at 1000 EST The 6th Communications Squadron located on MacDill AFB, Tampa FL is seeking to replace MacDill's Giant Voice (GV) System. The requirements are for the contractor to Engineer, Design, Furnish, Install, and Test (EDFI&T) a new, non-proprietary turn-key GV system solution to replace the existing GV system and provide complete services with real-time intelligible voice messages, tone alerts, and music for the coverage of all areas at MacDill Air Force Base, FL.
