## Summary

The Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) has a requirement for Antenna Services (non-communication) maintenance and support at the Mt. Weather Emergency Operations Center. The potential contractor will be responsible for labor and material if needed. The RFQ is attached along with the statement of work. The Purchase order is for a base of one year and four (4) one-year option periods. Award will be made to the vendor that is most advantageous to the government based on the criteria listed in the RFQ. Questions are due by Monday 6/1/2026 at 1100 AM local DC time.
