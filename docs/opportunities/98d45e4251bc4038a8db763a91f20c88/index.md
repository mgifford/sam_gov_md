## Summary

A0003 issued for the purpose of granting a one (1) week extension where proposals are now due June 4, 2026, no later than 3PM, to respond to Requests for Information thru May 13,2026, to advise that VA will accept RFIs through May25,2026 at 5PM EDT. No further questions will be accepted after May 25,2026 at 5PM EDT. Responses to RFIs shall be provided no later than May 27,2026, and to correct scrivener s errors by the VA. A0002 issued the purpose of responding to Requests for Information thru May 13,2026 A0001 issued for the purpose of: (a)responding to Requests for Information (RFIs) submitted by interested Parties up through April28,2026; (b)Exhibit G GSA 3517B- is hereby deleted in its entirety, and the revised updated Exhibit G- GSA3517B is amended into RLP 36C25026R0006, as required under recent governing authorities; and (c)issues an amended RLP 36C25026R0006 correcting the response date recital; and amending scrivener s errors by the VA. RLP 36C25026R0006 will be set-aside for Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB). Description: The Department of Veterans Affairs seeks to lease a minimum of 10,000 ABOA Square Feet (SF) of medical office space and 77 surface/outside parking spaces , inclusive of seven (7) ADA compliant parking spaces, reserved for the exclusive use by the VA as a Consolidated Medical Clinic in the delineated area explained below within Kalamazoo, Michigan. The space shall be located within the delineated area stated below. VA will consider space located in an existing building as well as a building being constructed for multi-tenant use and not specifically a build-to-suit for VA use only. ABOA is generally defined as the space remaining once common areas, lessor areas, and non-programmatic areas required by code are deducted from the rentable square feet of the facility. ABOA does not include areas such as stairs, elevators, mechanical and utility rooms, ducts, shafts, vestibules, public corridors, and public toilets required by local code. VA will provide all detailed definitions, should a solicitation for offers or request for proposals be issued for this project. Lease Term: Up to 20 Years The Government requests Space in an area bounded as follows: North Boundary: E G Ave; N. Riverview Drive. Commerce Lane; W. Mosel Ave; Barney Rd.; Nichols Road; Ravine Road, N. Drake Road; W H Ave; West Boundary: North 6th Street. M 43/West Main St, South 9th Street, Rt. 94; S. 4th St, S. 3rd St.; South Boundary: W Q Ave, West & East Centre Ave East Boundary: S Sprinkle Road; N. Sprinkle Road; Responses are due by 3PM EDT on May 28,2026
