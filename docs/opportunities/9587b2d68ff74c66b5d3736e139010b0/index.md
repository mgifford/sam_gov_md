## Summary

Subject: The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), Veterans Health Administration (VHA), Regional Procurement Office West (RPOW), Network Contracting Office (NCO) 21, on behalf of the Veterans Integrated Service Networks (VISN) 21, VA Central California Health Care System (VACCHCS). The purpose of this Sources Sought Notice is to conduct market research to support the procurement of a motorized weight trolley. This notice serves to survey the market in an attempt to ascertain whether or not sources are capable of providing the requested supplies/services. This notice allows potential contractors/vendors to submit a non-binding statement of interest and documentation demonstrating their capability. The qualifying North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code for this effort is 339113 Surgical appliance and supplies manufacturing. Additionally, this Sources Sought is to gain knowledge of potential qualified sources and their size classification(s) (Service Disabled/Veteran Owned Small Business (SD/VOSB), Hub zone, 8(a), small, small disadvantaged, woman-owned small business, or large business) relative to NAICS 339113 and the Size Standard of 800 employees. Responses to this notice will be used by the Government to make appropriate acquisition decisions. After reviewing the responses to this notice, a solicitation announcement may be published. Responses to this notice are not considered adequate responses to any corresponding solicitation announcement. All interested offerors are requested to respond to this notice but are required to respond to the official solicitation announcement in order to be awarded the contract. Background: The Department of Veterans Affairs is conducting market research to identify vendors capable of providing a motorized weight trolley. The weight cart is the only innovative solution with a motorized power drive setup and selectable weight choices in a small footprint, for any setting. These weight carts are utilized by hospital systems, clinical engineering, and leading ceiling lift companies Responses: Response is due Tuesday, June 9, 2026, at 12:00 Pacific Standard Time (PST). Products meeting all the salient characteristics will be considered ProCare IMMO 1210 lbs. weight Brand Name or Equal IMMO Portable Charger Station, Wall Mounted with Extra Battery Brand Name or Equal IMMO Strap and Connector Kit Brand Name or Equal Salient Characteristics: IMMO trolley The IMMO trolley is a specialized motorized electric transport cart designed for use in lift inspections and load testing procedures. Its primary function is to safely transport and apply test weights to patient lifts, ceiling lifts, and other lifting equipment without requiring any manual lifting by the technician. By eliminating the need to physically carry or stack heavyweight plates, the IMMO trolley significantly reduces the risk of musculoskeletal injury and enhances the efficiency, accuracy, and safety of lift certification work.
