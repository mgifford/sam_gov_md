- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-04 14:37:26.649-04

## Summary

The Department of Veterans Affairs, Portland VAHCS which includes Portland OR, Vancouver WA Campuses and associated CBOCS will be seeking a contractor to provide Hazardous Waste disposal service. This will be awarded as a firm fixed price base plus four (4) option year contract. Solicitation 36C26026Q0618 will be posted on 05/06/2026 and this procurement will be Open Market SDVOSB setaside.
