# RFQ - 12.5-Ton Gas/Electric Rooftop Unit Package

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 14:42:11.329-04
- Solicitation Number: W911S226U3282

## Summary

This requirement is for a complete, like-for-like replacement of an existing commercial rooftop unit, or RTU. The required equipment package must meet or exceed the following specifications. The base unit must be one 12.5-Ton Gas Heat and Electric Cooling Rooftop Unit with a 208-volt, 3-phase power supply. The unit must have single circuit, two-stage cooling with a minimum

10.8 EER or

15.0 IEER, and two-stage medium gas heat with a first stage of 180 MBH input and a second stage of 224 MBH input. It must also feature a high-static direct-drive vane axial fan, such as a Carrier EcoBlue or equivalent, and use a modern, low GWP refrigerant like R-454b. The package must also include the following compatible accessories: one Vertical Economizer with a controller, one Enthalpy sensor and harness for the economizer, and one standalone, two-stage programmable thermostat.
