## Summary

This action is to award a one-time buy contract for various NSNs in support for F-15 Armament Test Sets and the MUSTANG Test Sets. A list of NSNs, P/Ns and quantities for each item is listed below. Estimated delivery is 18 months. Ship To: SW3119 / F.O.B. Origin. Award is anticipated on or before September 30, 2026. The MUSTANG and F-15 Armament Test Sets are designed to check, test, diagnose, and repair B-52 and F-15 Armament components. The Government does not own the technical data package for this item. This requirement will be solicited sole source to manufacturer The Boeing Company (CAGE 0PXV4) pursuant to 10 USC 2304(c)(1); FAR 6.302-1. All responsible sources may submit a capability statement, proposal or quotation, which shall be considered by the agency.
