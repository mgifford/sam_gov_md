## Summary

Type 4 Subscale Missionization/ Launch Execution Support and Additional Direct Labor Hours to Support Engineering Services; Combined Sources Sought/Notice of Intent to Sole Source HQ014719C0005 Purpose: The Missile Defense Agency (MDA) is conducting market research to support Type 4 (T4) Missionization and Launch Execution of one (1) T4 target and the associated Engineering Services. The information contained herein only initiates communication with industry to assess market potential. No solicitation document exists currently. This Request for Information (RFI) constitutes no Government obligation to procure these items or issue a solicitation. The Government does not have an obligation to pay for information responding to this RFI. In accordance with the Revolutionary Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Overhaul (RFO) 6.103-1, the Government will allow for capacity statements for follow-on contracts. MDA is seeking alternate sources that can provide missionization and launch execution services to support the execution of one (1) T4 Subscale Air-Launched Short Range Ballistic Missile (SRBM). If no alternate sources are found to be available, the Government intends to issue a sole source modification on the T4 Subscale Contract, HQ0147-19-C-0005, to Northrop Grumman Systems Corporation (NGSC), Commercial and Government Entity (CAGE) code 8LM16, 1575 South Price Road Chandler, AZ 85286 under Statutory Authority 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(1) as implemented under FAR RFO 6.103-1(b) - Only One Responsible Source and No Other Supplies or Services Will Satisfy Agency Requirements. Based on currently available information, MDA believes that only NGSC can provide the required services without substantial duplication of cost that is not expected to be recovered through competition and without unacceptable delays based on the fact NGSC has already completed all design/development and most pre-missionization work. As MDA does not have data rights for portions of this design, MDA is unable to provide the Technical Data Package (TDP). The period of performance (PoP) for this effort will align with the existing contract, which ends in September 2027. A contract PoP extension through September 2030 is expected to be awarded by July 2027. This notice is intended to meet the requirements of both FAR Part 5 and Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) Procedures, Guidance, and Information (PGI) 206.103-170. MDA is also seeking alternative sources that can provide T4 Subscale Engineering Services, trade service, and re-missionization efforts to support the Missile Defense System test program. If no viable alternate sources are identified, the Government intends to award this effort as part of the aforementioned sole-source contract modification to NGSC under the T4 Subscale contract. Based on currently available information, MDA believes that only NGSC can provide the required services without substantial duplication of cost that is not expected to be recovered through competition and without unacceptable delays based on the fact NGSC has completed all design/development and pre-missionization work. As stated above, MDA does not have data rights for portions of this design and is unable to provide the TDP. The PoP for this effort is anticipated to be executed from September 2026 to September 2030. This notice is intended to meet the requirements of both FAR Part 5 and DFARS PGI 206.103-170. Description of the Requirement: Engineering Services are defined as requirements analysis, testing, verification, special studies, and support activities to determine the impact of changes in threats, missions, and tests that require quick reaction analysis and action. Engineering Services are performed on Task Instructions (TIs) with the understanding that implementation is within the present scope/Statement of Work and that it does not change the statement of work or other terms and conditions. TIs are used for quick-turn, pre-priced solutions to execute systems engineering activities including quick-look analysis of potential payload modifications, analysis of alternative test scenarios, studies and analysis, engineering test support and re-missionization. Pre-mission analysis: Develop mission execution specific test requirements including signature/trajectory and software requirements and conduct Mission System Requirements Reviews and Mission Design Reviews. Post-mission analysis: Perform post-flight data reduction and post-flight kinematic updates and signature performance assessments. Air-launch target preparation: Perform final target integration, missionization, and kit integration [if selected as option(s)]; integrate the target systems at the target integration site for air-launches and perform final assembly, integration and test of the Carriage Extraction System in support of mission operations. Air-launch target execution: Support missile launches dress rehearsals at forward staging area and conduct target launch operations to include support for Flight Termination System End-to-End checks and flight readiness review at the forward staging area. Engineering services are specific to T4 and Modified Ballistic Re-entry Vehicle (MBRV)-11. Direct knowledge of the design and capabilities of these products is required to perform these services. Responses: Responses should address the ability to perform engineering services and/or perform mission planning for air-launched T4 subscale targets while complying with MDA’s systems engineering processes. Responses should also address the associated detailed schedule that supports reviews, design, development, testing, production, launch vehicle integration, and flight test execution at the required Best Estimated Test Date. Responses should also address the cost estimate associated for the effort.
