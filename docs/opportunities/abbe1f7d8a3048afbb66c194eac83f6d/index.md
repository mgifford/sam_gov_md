# American Bureau of Shipping Inspection Service

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-06 16:20:02.155-04
- Solicitation Number: N0018926QL118

## Summary

The NAVSUP Fleet Logistics Center (FLC) Norfolk, Contracting Department, Norfolk, VA 23511-3392 intends to award a firm fixed-price contract for Engineering Inspection Regime in support of Carrier Strike Group Four. This is for commercial services, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotes are being requested and a written solicitation will not be issued. The requirement covers the procurement for Engineering Inspection Regime in support of Carrier Strike Group Four. This requirement is a sole source, and the justification will be attached to the solicitation. Perform all required inspections and surveys as mandated by current ABS rules and regulations. Report deficient conditions, systems or any other discrepancies that fall under the ABS rules and regulations when noted in inspections and surveys in a timely manner to eachships Master and Chief Engineer, as well as the CSG-4 Port Engineer assigned to that vessel and the CSG-4 TSV Squadron Program Manager.
