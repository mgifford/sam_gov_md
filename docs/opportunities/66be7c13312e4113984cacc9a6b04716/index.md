# T-6/T-34 Aircraft Maintenance and Contractor Logistics Support (CLS): Pre-Solicitation Conference/Industry Day and Site Visit

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-15 09:59:29.894-04
- Solicitation Number: N00421-25-RFPREQ-TPM273-0462

## Summary

The Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD) plans to host a combination Pre-Solicitation Conference/Industry Day and Site Visit for T-6/T-34 Aircraft Maintenance and Contractor Logistics Support (CLS) services on 9 June 2026 in Milton, FL. As part of this event, the Government will be seeking feedback on the N0042126R1011 T-6/T-34 Aircraft Maintenance and CLS draft Request for Proposal (RFP), which the Government expects to release through the PIEE Solicitation Module on or around 20 May 2026. This event will also include a site visit of the Naval Air Station Whiting Field (NASWF) facilities to allow prospective offerors the opportunity to view the site where the services will be performed. Please see attachments for more details.
