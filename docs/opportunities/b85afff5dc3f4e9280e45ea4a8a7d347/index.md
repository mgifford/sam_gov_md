Notes: Update — Drafts posted for industry planning (final solicitation pending AD?1205 approval) The U.S. Forest Service (USDA, Region 5) continues to post draft documents for the Pacific Southwest Regional Vault & Septic Pumping Services multiple?award IDIQ to support industry planning. The final RFP and seed task orders will be posted upon AD?1205 approval. Until that time, offerors should rely on the latest posted drafts for scope and coverage, with the understanding that terms may change at final release. Seed task orders are intended to be issued promptly after award to avoid delays at the beginning of the summer recreation season.
