## Summary

minimum capabilities. The equipment type is an Electric Slab Scissor Lift with an electric drive type powered by a 24V DC power source. Its key capability is a narrow width of 32 inches, which is designed for tight indoor and outdoor spaces, and it is drivable at full extension height. It produces zero emissions, making it highly suitable for indoor use.The physical dimensions and measurements for this class of narrow-width lift are detailed as follows. It features a maximum working height of 37 ft 7 in (11.46 m) and a platform height of 31 ft 7 in (9.63 m). The lowered platform height measures 51 in (1.30 m). It has an overall width of 32 in (0.81 m), an overall length of 91.6 in (2.33 m), a stowed height with rails up of 94 in (2.39 m), and a stowed height with rails down of 79.5 in (2.02 m). The inside platform dimensions are 83 in x 27.5 in (2.10 m x 0.70 m), and it includes a roll-out extension deck of 3 ft (0.91 m). Additionally, the stowed ground clearance is 4.0 in (10.2 cm) and the wheelbase is 69 in (1.75 m).Operating capacities, speeds, and weight specifications define this models performance. The overall capacity is 550 lbs. (249 kg), split between a main platform capacity of 300 lbs. (136 kg) and an extension deck capacity of 250 lbs. (113 kg), with a maximum occupancy of 2 persons. The total machine weight is 5,850 lbs. (2,654 kg) and it has a gradeability of 25%. Its turning radius is 43 in (1.09 m) inside and 104 in (2.64 m) outside. Operating times under rated load are 55 to 60 seconds to lift and 47 to 55 seconds to lower. The drive speed is 2.0 mph (3.2 km/h) when stowed and 0.5 mph (0.8 km/h) when raised. It is equipped with 16 x 5 x 12 solid rubber, non-marking tires, and is capable of a maximum drive height at full height.Standard operational features and safety systems for this model type include several integrated mechanisms. For control and drive, it utilizes a variable speed electric drive, dual holding brakes, and proportional controls for drive and lift. Safety systems consist of a pothole protection system, a tilt sensor and alarm with drive and lift cut-out, an all-motion audible alarm, and an overload sensing system. Platform convenience features include a 110V AC outlet on the platform with GFI, a half-height spring hinged entry gate, a fold-down hinged railing system, and lanyard attachment points. For maintenance, it features an on-board visual interface with diagnostics, low voltage battery protection, tie-down and lift lugs, and forklift pockets.
