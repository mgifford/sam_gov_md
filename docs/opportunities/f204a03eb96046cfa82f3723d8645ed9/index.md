# Installation Fiber Optics Networking Support Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Award Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-14 18:41:27.352-04
- Solicitation Number: N6893625R0026
- Awardee: BLACKWATCH INTERNATIONAL CORPORATION Mclean VA 22102 USA
- Award Amount: 17376437.62

## Summary

The Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division has awarded a Five Year, FFP, Single-Award Contract for Installation Fiber Optic Networking Support Services using full and open competition under solicitation N6893622R0026. This contract will provide Engineering, and the contractor will provide Installation. All support services shall execute network designs, fiber builds, infrastructure mapping, and networking solutions, which currently are not provided by, and are outside the framework of the Naval Enterprise Network (NEN) which encompasses the Navy Marine Corps Intranet (NMCI), Next Generation Enterprise Network (NGEN), and BLII/ONE-NET, as well as provide both trained and certified Fiber contractors to the NAWCWD community.
