- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE

## Summary

This requirement is to provide for a single award Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) Base Operation and Support (BOS) Contract at U.S. Marine Corps Air Station (MCAS) Iwakuni, Japan. The Contractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, management, tools, materials, equipment, facilities, transportation, incidental engineering, and other items necessary to provide the services as described in the Performance Work Statement (PWS).
