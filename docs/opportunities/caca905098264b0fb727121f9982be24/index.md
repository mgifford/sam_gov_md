# Circular Geometry Machine (CGM) Roundness Tester

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 14:27:11.689-04
- Solicitation Number: SP4706-26-Q-XX13

## Summary

This is a pre-solicitation notice, a solicitation will be posted at a later time. This requirement is for one (1) new, not refurbished Circular Geomtry Machine (OGM) Roundness Tester to be provided to the DLA Product Test Center Mechanical Lab in Columbus, Ohio. Scope of work includes the delivery, installation, and calibration of the machine, as well as providing a warranty of the machine and one day of training for up to five (5) associates. Please see the attached performance work statment and technical specifications for full details. Interested parties are not required to submit a quote at this time, but are encouraged to express an interest via email, to the Contracting Officer (KO) Elizabeth Dickstein, listed as the Primary Point of Contact under this pre-solicitation notice.
