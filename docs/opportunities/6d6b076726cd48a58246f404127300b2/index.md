## Summary

The Charles Geroge Veteran Affairs Medical Center (VAMC) located at 1100 Tunnel Road Asheville, NC 28805 has a requirement for a contractor to provide a 5-Cable Substation Recovery Trailer. The Contractor shall provide all materials necessary to fulfill the services for the Charles George VA Medical Center. This is a Pre-solicitation only. Interested contractors should watch for the issuance of the solicitation which will have specific instructions and requirements associated with this procurement. The tentative date for issuing the solicitation is on or about May 1st, 2026. In accordance with Public Law, P.L. 109-461, this is a 100% SDVOSB set-aside. The NAICS Code for this procurement 336212 Truck Trailer Manufacturing, size standard of 1,000 employees. All interested parties must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM) (www.sam.gov) and certified as a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) in SBA (sba.gov) prior to submitting a Quote. This is a PRESOLICITATION NOTICE ONLY. This is not a request for pricing. All correspondence and responses shall be e-mailed to Harold McQuillia at harold.mcquillia@va.gov.
