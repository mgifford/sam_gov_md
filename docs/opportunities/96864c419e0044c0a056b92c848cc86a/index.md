## Summary

The Department of Veterans Affairs has awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for a Mental Health Over Door Alarm System and installation under a competitive Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) set-aside. The contract provides for delivery and installation of over-door alarm systems in designated mental health care areas to enhance Veterans safety.
