# Generator and ATS Preventative Maintenance

- Agency: COMMERCE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-08 16:02:17.785-04
- Solicitation Number: NB194000-26-01569

## Summary

SEE ATTACHMENTS FOR DETAILED PROJECT DESCRIPTIONS AND COMPLETE SOURCES SOUGHT NOTICE The proposed project consists of the following: The contractor will provide maintenance services required to maintain NIST owned generators, and automatic transfer switches to equipment located on the NIST Boulder campus, CO; Fort Collins Radio Station, CO; and Kauai Radio Station, HI for a one (1) year base year, and for four (4) optional one-year periods. This requirement is assigned to NAICS code of 811310 Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment (except Automotive and Electronic) Repair and Maintenance with the Small Business Administration (SBA) size standard of $12.5 million for the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), Boulder, Colorado. Please make recommendations if this selection is not appropriate in your opinion.
