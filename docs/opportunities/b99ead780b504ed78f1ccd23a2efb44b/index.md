## Summary

The United States Department of Agriculture- Forest Service (USDA-FS) intends to award a sole source order to Stacy G. Hikes. This is a test pilot program for Satellite Adoption Center (SAC) in Pennsylvania to assist with Training & Placing Wild Horses and Burros. SAC’s will allow for trained and untrained horses to enter target market areas, reach a broader range of populations focusing on Wild Horse and Burro Adopter & Public outreach and education and allow more holding space at DDC for newly gathered animals. This action is conducted under the authority of FAR 13.106-1(b)(1) for single source.
