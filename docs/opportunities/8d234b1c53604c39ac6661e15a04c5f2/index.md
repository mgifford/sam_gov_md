## Summary

This is an official notice that an offer has been selected for award for the Misawa Construction Project Escorts contract. After careful consideration, T47 International Inc., was selected for contract award. This notice is provided in accordance with the RFO 15.207-2. Offerors solicited: Full and Open – Electronically solicited on SAM.gov via FA520526R0005 Number of proposals received: Seventeen (17) Contractor to receive award: T47 International, Inc. 16901 Melford Blvd Ste 129 Bowie, MD 20715-4480 4. Total Contract Price of Successful Offeror: $8,118,827.28 Evaluation of proposals was completed in accordance with Solicitation and RFO 12.203/RFO 15.103-2. The following factors were used to evaluate offers: Factor I - Technical Capability, Factor II – Past Performance and Factor III - Price. Technical Capability and Past Performance were determined to be of equal importance to price. Award was made to the contractor whose proposal conformed to the requirements of the solicitation, represented the best value to the Government in terms of the Lowest Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA).
