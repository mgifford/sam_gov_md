## Summary

SYNOPSIS for W912BU26BA006: This is a Pre-solicitation Notice. This Notice is being issued for planning and information purposes only. This notice is not a Solicitation, nor is it to be construed as a commitment by the Government. The Government will not pay for any effort expended in responding to this notice, nor will the Government accept proposals as a result of this notice. ***Solicitation W912BU26BA006 is expected to be released on or about 09 JUN 2026*** PROJECT DESCRIPTION: The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE), Philadelphia District has a requirement for the Maintenance Dredging, Delaware River, Philadelphia to Sea and Wilmington Harbor FY26 Project. Contract work consists of maintenance dredging of approximately 2.5 million cubic yards of sediment within the Delaware River, Philadelphia to Sea main navigation channel between Stations 0+000 and 515+000 and approximately 350,000 cubic yards within Wilmington Harbor, Christina River. Work includes hydraulic cutterhead dredging only south of Station 190+000 and mechanical or hydraulic cutterhead dredging north of Station 190+000 with placement of the dredged material into the Pedricktown, Delaware City, and/or Killcohook disposal areas. All dredging will be performed to the required depths indicated with 1 or 2-foot allowable overdepth. All dredging shall be performed as contour dredging. Before and after dredging surveys for measurement will be performed by the government. The period of performance will be 240 calendar days. DISCLOSURE OF MAGNITUDE OF THE CONSTRUCTION PROJECT: The estimated magnitude of this project is between $25,000,000 and $100,000,000. NAICS CODE, PSC CODE, AND SBA SIZE STANDARD: 237990 - Dredging and Surface Cleanup Activities, Z1KF – Maintenance of Dredging Facilities, Size Standard $37.0M. TYPE OF SET-ASIDE: Full and open (unrestricted). ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Specific information pertaining to this procurement will be available in the solicitation.
