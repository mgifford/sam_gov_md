## Summary

Combined Synopsis/Solicitation for Commercial Products Onity Trillium Lock System Upgrade This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial products prepared in accordance with the format in FAR RFO Subpart 12.2, as supplemented with additional information included in this notice. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation; quotations are being requested and no other solicitation will be issued. Solicitation Number: W50S8626QA003 Issued as a: Request for Quotation (RFQ) UNFUNDED DISCLOSURE: Funds are NOT presently available for this requirement. The government intends to make award should funding become available. QUOTE VALIDITY: The Government requests quotes to be valid through 30 September 2026. Requirement Description: This is a requirement for the purchase and installation of an Onity Trillium Lock system upgrade at the Alpena Combat Readiness Training Center (CRTC), Michigan. This is a brand-name-only requirement. Set-Aside: This acquisition is Full and Open Competition. The small business set-aside has been dissolved. NAICS Code: 332510 This solicitation document and incorporated provisions and clauses are those in effect through the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) guidance and applicable DFARS class deviations. The full text of provisions and clauses may be accessed electronically at www.acquisition.gov. Line Items (CLINs): CLIN 0001: Onity Trillium Lock System components (Supply), Brand Name. CLIN 0002: Installation of the Onity Trillium Lock System. A detailed list of required components is provided in the solicitation attachment. Delivery and Acceptance: Delivery, installation, and acceptance shall be at Alpena CRTC, 5884 A Street, Alpena, MI 49707. SITE VISIT: An organized site visit will be conducted on 16 June 2026 at 10:00 AM Local time. All interested vendors are encouraged to attend the site visit. No other site visit will be conducted. Attached to this notice is an Entry & Access List (EAL) Form. This must be filled out and submitted to the Contracting Officer NOT LATER THAN 10 days before the site visit. Failure to submit this form may result in denial of access to the installation. MEETING LOCATION: Alpena CRTC CE, BUILDING 2 CONFERENCE ROOM, 5884 A STREET, ALPENA MI, 49707. Questions: Questions are due by 18 June 2026 at 3:00 PM Local Time Response Date: All quotations are due by 30 June 2026 at 11:00 AM Local Time
