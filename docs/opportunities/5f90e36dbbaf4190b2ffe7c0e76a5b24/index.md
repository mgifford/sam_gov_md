## Summary

Solicitation Number: 36C26026R0075 NAICS Code: 238220 – Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors Size Standard: $19 million Set-Aside: Full and Open (Unrestricted) Contract Type: Firm Fixed Price, Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) Task Order Contract (SATOC) Issuing Office: Network Contracting Office 20 (NCO20), Boise VA Medical Center (BVAMC) Location: 500 West Fort Street, Boise, ID 83702-4598 Amendment 0002 is to issue Class Deviation Supplement - Executive Order (EO) 14398 Addressing DEI Discrimination by Federal Contractors. Amendment 0001 is to post the answers to the RFI questions. All offerors must acknowledge this amendment with their proposal. Description: Network Contracting Office 20 (NCO20) is soliciting proposals for a Firm Fixed Price Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) Single Award Task Order Contract (SATOC) at the Boise VA Medical Center, Boise, Idaho. The resulting contract will support the maintenance, repair, replacement, and/or demolition of Plumbing, HVAC-R, Mechanical, Electrical, and Control Systems, which may include ancillary construction and facility support services at the Boise VAMC campus. Work may include, but is not limited to, routine and emergency response tasks for these systems. All work will comply with the Buy American Act. The prime contractor must possess and provide evidence of a current, valid Plumbing contractor license and a current, valid HVAC-R contractor license in the State of Idaho, submitting updated documentation annually or upon any change in licensure status. A prime contractor representative must respond on-site at the Boise VA Medical Center within two (2) hours of any emergent need. Award is anticipated as a Single Award Task Order Contract (SATOC), under NAICS 238220, with an unrestricted, full and open competition. Please see the solicitation and attachments for further details. Reuestions for Information regarding this solicitation are due no later than May 11th, 2026. All proposals and questions must be submitted via email to Alan.Perez@va.gov and Helen.Woods@va.gov. No Site Visit will be held for this requirment. Proposals are due no later than 10:00AM PDT on June 1st, 2026.
