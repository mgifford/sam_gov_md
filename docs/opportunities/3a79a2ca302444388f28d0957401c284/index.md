## Summary

This is to procure a Brunauer–Emmett–Teller Surface Area Analyzer. Please review the attached solicitation and specificaitons documents. Questions and Answers. Q:1 Support for micropore volume determination using accepted adsorption models (e.g. t-plot, DR/DA) derived from adsorption isotherms; or A dedicated high-resolution micropore analyzer with specific low-pressure performance requirements (for example defined p/p? range, transducer resolution, or minimum measurable pore width).
