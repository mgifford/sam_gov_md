# Mississippi River Project Office - Facility Support Trailer

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-28 15:25:13.032-04
- Solicitation Number: W912EK26QA038

## Summary

This notice serves to notify interested parties that the Government intends to issue a solicitation for a Facility Support Trailer. The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Rock Island District, Mississippi River Project Office, has a requirement for a thirty-five (35) foot deck over gooseneck trailer for facility support. This pre-solicitation notice is for informational purposes only and is not to be construed as a commitment by the Government to procure any supplies or services, or for the Government to pay for the information received. No reimbursement will be made for any costs associated with providing information to respond to this pre-solicitatIon notice or any follow up information requested. Consequently, no solicitation is being issued at this time. Therefor, no award will be made as a result of providing information.
