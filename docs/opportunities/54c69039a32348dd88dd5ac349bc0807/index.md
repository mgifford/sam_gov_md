# Sole Source to New York Yacht Club

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-26 08:38:49.718-04
- Solicitation Number: N0018926QL157

## Summary

The Naval Supply Systems Command (NAVSUP) Fleet Logistics Center (FLC) Norfolk Contracting Department, Fleet Directorate intends to solicit – on behalf of the Commander Naval Operations (OPNAV) – New York Yacht Club, New York New York on a sole source basis in accordance with FAR 12.102(a). The instant requirement is for the New York Yacht club to hold various events as outline in the attached PWS. The resultant contract will be issued on a firm-fixed-price (FFP) basis. The period of performance is 02 July 2026 - 07 July 2026. The authority for use of other than full and open competition is FAR 6.302-1, only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements. The proposed action is for supplies. The NAICS code applicable to this procurement is 531120. The size standard is $25.5M. Interested persons may identify their interest and capability to respond to the requirement or submit proposals. This notice of intent is not a request for competitive proposals. However, all bids, proposals, or quotations received by the closing response date will be considered by the Government. A determination by the Government not to compete this proposed contract based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Government. Information received will normally be considered solely for the purpose of determining whether to conduct a competitive procurement. Please direct all questions regarding this requirement to Jill Joscelyn and jill.h.joscelyn.civ@us.navy.mil.
