# 7G--5g Solar PTT-100 Satellite Transmitters - 12

- Agency: INTERIOR, DEPARTMENT OF THE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-30 13:08:30.864-04
- Solicitation Number: 140G0125Q0200

## Summary

The U.S. Geological Survey intends to award a non-competitive purchase order to Microwave Telemetry, Inc, located in Columbia MD, for twelve (12) 5g Solar PTT-100 Satellite Transmitters to be delivered to USGS Eastern Ecological Science Center, Patuxent Research Refuge, Laurel MD within 10 weeks ARO. This study is a continuation of previous research therefore identical transmitter technology must be utilized to ensure continuity of data from previous years. Microwave Telemetry is the only source reasonably available that can meet this requirement. The NAICS code is 334419. The size standard is 750 employees. In accordance with FAR 5.207, all responsible sources may submit a bid, proposal or quotation which shall be considered by the agency; however, THIS NOTICE IS FOR INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY. THIS IS NOT A REQUEST FOR COMPETITIVE PROPOSALS. The Government intends to award this requirement noncompetitively in accordance with FAR 13.106-1(b)Soliciting from a single source. All responses must be emailed to sruggles@usgs.gov.
