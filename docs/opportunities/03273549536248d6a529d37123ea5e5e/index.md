# Demolish Buildings 656, 657, & 2701 at Fort Eustis, Virginia

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-20 13:47:16.245-04
- Solicitation Number: 5205056FE01

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all management, transportation, tools, supplies, equipment, labor, testing and supervision to perform field investigations, vehicles and equipment necessary to perform demolition services on Joint Base Langley-Eustis (JBLE), Virginia (VA) in a manner that shall ensure continuous and reliable service. The contractor shall demolish buildings 656, 657 and 2701 on Fort Eustis, Virginia.
