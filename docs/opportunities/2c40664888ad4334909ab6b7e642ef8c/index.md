# Preventive Maintenance and Repair Services on Fitness Equipment at Kunsan AB

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 22:27:04.342-04
- Solicitation Number: W90VN726QA005

## Summary

The contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, supplies, facilities, transportation, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and non-personal services necessary to perform monthly preventive maintenance and repairs on fitness equipment in Kunsan Air Base. This effort covers all equipment currently located in Building 1055 (Fitness Center), as well as any additional equipment added during the period of performance, as defined in this Performance Work Statement.
