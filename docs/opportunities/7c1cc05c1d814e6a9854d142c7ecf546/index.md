# Metal 3D Printer System

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-14 15:01:17.598-04
- Solicitation Number: SP4706-26-Q-XX14

## Summary

This is a pre-solicitation notice, a solicitaion will be posted at a later time. This requirement is for one (1) new, not refurbished metal 3D printer system to be provided to the DLA Product Test Center Mechanical Lab in Columbus, Ohio. Scope of work includes the delivery and installation of one system, as well as a one-year warrant, follow-up preventative maintenance and calibration of the system, and a minimum of three (3) days of on-site training for up to four (4) associates. Interested parties are not required to submit a quote at this time, but are encouraged to express an interest via email, to the Contracting Officer (KO), Elizabeth Dickstein, listed as the Primary Point of Contact under this pre-solicitation notice.
