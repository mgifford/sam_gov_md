## Summary

SAM.gov Pre-Solicitation Notice News and Market Research Product Notice Type: Pre-Solicitation NAICS Code: 519290 PSC: 7H20 Delivery: 1 day ARO Set-Aside Status: Small-business Place of Performance: United States Overview: The International Trade Administration (ITA) is seeking subscription news products and data services. The contractor shall provide to OHIT the range of data and analyses as specified below and shall make the data services available electronically to unlimited number of OHIT analysts for subscription. More specifically, the contractor shall provide database access covering spending and investment trends, global project activities in the wireless communications infrastructure market, as well as news and analysis of policy developments and industry trends. The requirement must include the following features, but not limited to these sectors and topics: mobile wireless communications infrastructure covering core, RAN, backhaul, 2G, 3G, 4G, LTE, 5G, DAS, small cells, and cloud and virtual RAN.
