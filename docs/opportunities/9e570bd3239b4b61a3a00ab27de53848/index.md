## Summary

In accordance with RFO 12.102(a), this Notice of Intent is to inform all parties the Department of Veterans Affairs, Network Contracting Office (NCO) 19 located at 6162 South Willow Drive Suite 300, Greenwood Village, CO 80111 intends to award a sole source procurement for the following requirement to the large business manufacturer, Howmedica Osteonics, Inc. DBA Stryker Orthopaedics, located at 325 Corporate Dr, Mahwah, NJ 07430: ITEM # DESCRIPTION OF SUPPLIES QTY UNIT OF ISSUE 0001 Alpha GT 1 EA 0002 PLFT20 ORIF Inst & Screw 1 0003 PSF T8 T15 ORIF Inst & Screws 1 0004 ASNIS 65 80 INST & SHORT SCREWS 1 0005 Omega Hip Inst and Screws 1 0006 Infinity Adaptis Total Ankle 1 The Stryker hardware uses a unique screw-head design that is compatible only with the Stryker surgical drills already in use at the RMR VAMC. Competing manufacturers utilize their own proprietary systems, rendering their components incompatible with the equipment currently deployed. Transitioning to another manufacturer would require the facility to replace the existing systems in their entirety, including procurement of new surgical drills, instrumentation, and associated implant system The associated North America Classification Systems (NAICS) Code is 339113 and the Business Size Standard is 750 employees.
