# Sources Sought: NSN 5180-00-596-1509, Toolkits Pipefitter Supplement (PSTK)

## Summary

Army Contracting Command-Detroit Arsenal (DTA) The Government is conducting market research to identify potential sources capable of manufacturing and assembling the Tool Kit, Pipefitter Supplemental (PSTK) (NSN 5180-00-596-1509, P/N 0684A0000:59678). This requirement is a comprehensive, portable Set, Kit, and Outfit (SKO) consisting of specialized hand tools—such as cutters, reamers, threaders, and heavy-duty wrenches—necessary for the installation, modification, and repair of piping systems. END ITEM: The end item is a portable, ruggedized Pipefitter's Supplemental Tool Kit (NSN 5180-00-596-1509), specifically designed to support field-level maintenance and construction of critical infrastructure piping. This kit provides Department of War (DoW) engineer units with the tactical capability to cut, thread, and join pipes up to 4 inches in diameter, enabling the establishment of essential water, fuel, and steam distribution networks in austere environments. There is a technical data package (TDP) associated with this procurement. It is distribution statement “D”, which limits access to Department of War (DoW) and U.S. DoW contractors, and is located under the Attachments/Links section of the associated SAM posting.
