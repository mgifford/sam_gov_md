# ARMY AMMUNITION MANUFACTURING INNOVATION INDUSTRIAL BASE INTEGRATION TEAM COMMERCIAL SOLUTIONS OPENING REQUEST FOR SOLUTIONS

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-04-28 14:13:14.479-04
- Solicitation Number: AMIP-CSO

## Summary

Through this CSO, the Army aims to identify and adopt innovative commercial solutions that accelerate the integration of advanced manufacturing technologies, enable secure and connected digital environments, and promote sustainable practices. The goal is to create a more agile, resilient, and technologically advanced industrial base that meets current and future operational demands while ensuring compliance with DoD standards for cybersecurity, sustainability, and readiness.
