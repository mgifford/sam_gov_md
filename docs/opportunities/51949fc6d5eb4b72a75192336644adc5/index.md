# CTNF Teton Basin Grants Darby Single Vault Toilet

- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-22 15:02:05.413-04

## Summary

This is a combined synopsis/solicitation for commercial items prepared in accordance with the format in FAR Part 12. This announcement constitutes the only solicitation. Quotations are being requested and a separate written solicitation will not be issued. Solicitation number 1240LT26Q0078 is issued as a Request for Quotation (RFQ) for hauling and installing a single vault precast toilet at the Darby Canyon Wind Caves Trailhead. This acquisition is set-aside for small business concerns. The applicable North American Industry Classification Standard Code is 237990 The small business size standard is $45.0M. This acquisition is a Total Small Business Set-Aside. All responsible sources may submit a quotation which will be considered by the agency.
