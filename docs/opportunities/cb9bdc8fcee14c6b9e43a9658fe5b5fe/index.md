# Special Notice - Portfolio Acquisition Executive (PAE) Air Maneuver AM, Capability Program Executive (CPE) Mission Autonomy, Program Manager (PM) Autonomous Robotic Capabilities (ARC), Robotic Logistic Support Center (RLSC)

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-27 10:33:08.772-04

## Summary

1. Purpose: This Special Notice is to inform industry that the Government intends to issue a solicitation for Robotics Logistics Support Services (RLSS) in June 2026. This requirement will support the Robotics Logistics Support Center (RLSC) and its mission to equip the Joint Warfighter with advanced robotic warfighting capabilities. 2. Requirement Overview: These non-personal, non-commercial services will provide comprehensive Robot Maintenance and Operations Support. The successful offeror will provide manning to the RLSC’s training, deployment, systems engineering, integration, testing, fielding, sustainment, and improvement of unmanned systems that form the backbone of the future force. Core support areas include: Program Management Maintenance Logistics System Engineering and Technical Assistance (SETA) 3. Acquisition Details: The upcoming solicitation will be issued against the General Services Administration (GSA) One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services Plus (OASIS+) Multiple Award Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (MA IDIQ) contract. Contract Vehicle - GSA OASIS+ MA IDIQ Set-Aside Status - Total Small Business Set-Aside OASIS+ Domain - Technical and Engineering Domain NAICS Code - 541330 (Exception 1) Anticipated Solicitation Release June 2026 4. Draft Performance Work Statement (PWS): A Draft PWS is attached to this notice for informational and planning purposes. Please note that this draft is subject to change prior to the official solicitation release. 5. Reference Information For information regarding the base contract vehicle, please review the OASIS+ Total Small Business Set-Aside Open Solicitation here: https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/17a7d2cf8a974d01961148d88497e67f/view
