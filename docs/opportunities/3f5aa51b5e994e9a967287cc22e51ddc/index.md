- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Presolicitation

## Summary

The 5th Security Forces Squadron at Minot AFB has a requirement for the design, delivery, and installation of two (2) Law Enforcement Desk (LED) Dispatch Consoles and two (2) Fire and Emergency Services (F&ES) dispatch consoles. This presolicitation notice is not a request for quote or solicitation. Quotes will be solicited directly from vendors via email, in accordance with RFO 12.201-1(c)(2).
