## Summary

This notice is to inform all interested parties that the Department of Veterans Affairs is cancelling the previously posted Intent to Sole Source under Notice Number 36C24926Q0183 for Water Analysis Service Contract. The original notice was posted on 4/28/2026 on SAM.gov. After further review, the Government has determined that it is in the best interest of the Department to cancel this procurement action. No contract will be awarded as a result of this notice. We appreciate the interest and responses received from vendors. Should there be any future procurement opportunities for this requirement, such information will be posted on SAM.gov.
