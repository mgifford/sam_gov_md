# T-7A Conference Hoteling

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-05-29 08:32:43.873-04
- Solicitation Number: FA8617-26-C-XXXX

## Summary

The USAF T-7A Program Office (AFLCMC/WNB) is conducting a Sources Sought to identify qualified vendors capable of providing Conference Hoteling Services for the T-7A Red Hawk System. The services must comply with AFI 11-418 and its AETC supplement. Interested contractors must possess (or demonstrate the ability to quickly acquire) in-depth knowledge and provide subject matter experts across the T-7A systems outlined in the attached Sources Sought Notice. Please review the attached Sources Sought Notice and submit response to Ms. Sonia Taylor at sonia.taylor.1@us.af.mil and Mr. Joshua Potter at joshua.potter.5@us.af.mil.
