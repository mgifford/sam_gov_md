# Lawn Mowing & Landscaping Services Needed for the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest Supervisor's Office

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-13 12:58:12.817-04
- Solicitation Number: 12444526Q0037

## Summary

The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, materials, tools, supervision, and transportation necessary to maintain lawn and landscaped areas. Services are from June through September. Property is located at the Chequamegon-Nicolet National Forest Rhinelander Supervisor Office, 500 Hanson Lake Rd, Rhinelander, WI 54501. All services shall be performed in accordance with industry standards and manufacturer specifications and applicable Federal, State, and Local laws. The property is 2.89 total acres and about 1/3 of that is grassy areas that will need mowing.
