## Summary

Dugway Proving Ground (DPG), Chemical Biological Testing Division (CBTD), requires annual certification, maintenance, and calibration of its STERIS VHP-1000-ARD Vaporized Hydrogen Peroxide (VHP) equipment. STERIS Corporation, as the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), is the only source capable of providing these services due to their proprietary software, specialized tools, factory-certified technicians, and exclusive authority to perform NIST?traceable calibration and issue required documentation. STERIS is also the sole supplier of factory?authorized parts and prohibits third?party servicing. No other vendor can meet the certification or calibration requirements for this equipment.
