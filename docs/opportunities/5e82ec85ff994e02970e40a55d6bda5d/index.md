## Summary

Request for Proposal (RFP) – Flooring Installation, Renovation, and Replacement Services This is a competitive solicitation and Request for Proposal (RFP) issued in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 15 and RFO 15. This solicitation satisfies the requirements of FAR Subpart 5.2, including FAR and RFO Parts 5.204, 5.201, 5.207, 5.203, and 5.101. The Mann-Grandstaff VA Medical Center (MGVAMC) in Spokane, Washington seeks a qualified contractor for flooring installation, renovation, and replacement services in accordance with the RFP 36C26026R0068 terms and conditions. The government anticipates awarding a Single Award Task Order Contract (SATOC) Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract, with a total ceiling of $2 million USD over a five-year period. The procurement is set aside for small businesses. The North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code is 238330 [Flooring Contractors], and the small business size standard is $19 million. To be considered eligible, firms must be registered as a small business in www.sam.gov under NAICS 238330 at the time of proposal submission, award, and throughout contract performance. Questions regarding this soliciation will be accepted by email only at derek.crockett@va.gov on or before 05/15/2026 at 15:00 PST. Interested parties may submit proposal and submission requirements on or before the close date of this solicitation on 06/04/2026 at 15:00 PST. Proposals will be accepted by email only at derek.crockett@va.gov. All businesses must be registered and confirm eligibility in the System for Award Management (SAM); each entity must represent its business status in SAM.gov. Registration information can be found at https://www.sam.gov.
