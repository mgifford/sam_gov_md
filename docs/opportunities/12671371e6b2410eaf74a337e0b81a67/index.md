# Furniture Management Section (FMS) Haulage, Maintenance and Repair Services

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 09:22:20.621-04
- Solicitation Number: FA558726Q0012

## Summary

The Furniture Management Section (FMS) requires haulage, maintenance and repair services for domestic household furniture and appliances, to and from government-designated residences at RAF Lakenheath, Mildenhall, Feltwell and the surrounding areas. NOTICE TO OFFERORS: Funds are not presently available for this effort. No award will be made under this solicitation until funds are available. The Government reserves the right to cancel this solicitation either before, or after the closing date. In the event the Government cancels this solicitation, the Government has no obligation to reimburse an offeror for any costs. Questions are encouraged to be submitted at your earliest convenience but will not be accepted past Tuesday 02 June 2026 at 14:00 BST (GMT+1).
