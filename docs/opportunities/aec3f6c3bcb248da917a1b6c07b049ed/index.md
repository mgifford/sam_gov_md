# DRAFT RFP - NASA Flown Space Vehicle Multimodal Transportation Multiple Award Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity Contract

- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 16:00:12.294-04
- Solicitation Number: 80JSC026R0014

## Summary

Modification 1 - May 12, 2026 NASA invites industry partners to participate in a One-on-One Reverse Industry Day for the NASA Flown Space Vehicle (FSV) Multimodal Transportation (MMT) Multiple Award (MAC) Indefinite Delivery Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ).?The purpose of these One-on-One sessions is to gather feedback from industry partners to assist NASA in developing acquisition strategies for a possible future long-term enterprise capability of multimodal transportation projects across the agency. ? This Reverse Industry Day Event is for market research purposes only in accordance with Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Part 10 - Market Research. Participation is voluntary. All costs associated with attendance or travel and preparation of capability statements shall be at the Contractor’s expense and no reimbursement will be provided by the Government. To facilitate interactive communication with industry, NASA’s Subject Matter Experts will be available for one-on-one meetings virtually with individual companies. ? The meetings are intended to be a Question and Answers (Q&A) session, in which neither side will make a formal presentation. ?NASA’s questions are provided via attachment to this posting. This event aims to enhance collaboration, share insights, and gather feedback from industry experts to support NASA's mission.
