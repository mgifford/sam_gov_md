# HiDef Camera UAV

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Combined Synopsis/Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-12 15:40:13.493-04
- Solicitation Number: W911S226U3211

## Summary

looking for two UAVs. One small and one nano. Must be able to withstand high winds, have gimbals for stabilization, must include storage cases and high definition 4k video and ISR. Drone A: 55min flight time, less than 37dBA at 25meters, operational temp -20 -45 degrees, 28 mile flight range, avoidance, GPS denied flight, 1080p streaming, 3 -axis gimbal stabilizer, 3840x2160 dual Sony 4K camera, operational at 30 kt wind, supports ATAK, watertight, dust proof case, 3x batteries, field swappable gimbal and repair bag w. Tools, 320p ISR Drone B: Gimbal stabilized 48MP 320p/640p IR, 36 min flight, 360 degree obstacle avoidance, GPS denied control, unseen and inaudible at 30ft, 153g takeoff weight, nano sized, watertight dust proof transit case, 8000x6000px still imagery, 4k mpeg-4 video
