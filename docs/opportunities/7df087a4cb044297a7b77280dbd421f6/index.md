# Kuwait Air Force Facility Maintenance and Support SATOC

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Sources Sought
- Posted: 2026-04-27 11:20:17.162-04
- Solicitation Number: W912ER26RA023

## Summary

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) Sources Sought announcement is issued to conduct market research and identify parties interested in, and with the resources to support, the Kuwait Ministry of Defense (KMOD), Kuwait Air Force (KAF) fixed-wing aircraft platform, and associated facilities and installations throughout the Country of Kuwait. The contemplated requirement is for facility maintenance and support services, to include recurring preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, inspections, repairs, spare parts procurement, inventory management, incidental minor construction, and other related support services under a potential Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ), Single Award Task Order Contract (SATOC), with an anticipated five-year ordering period. As a result of this market research, the U.S. Government will gain a better understanding of industry capabilities for this potential future requirement. The Government is currently contemplating soliciting this requirement on a Full and Open basis. Interested parties who consider themselves qualified are invited to submit a Statement of Capabilities in response to this notice. Submissions must be emailed no later than 10:00 AM EDT on May 12, 2026, to Elena Shapovalova at Elena.N.Shapovalova@usace.army.mil and Eric Caruso at Eric.L.Caruso@usace.army.mil. The email subject line should read: [Your Company Name] Response to Sources Sought W912ER26RA023.This is not a solicitation, and no contract will be awarded from this announcement. Submission of any information is purely voluntary, and the Government assumes no financial responsibility for any costs incurred. Do not submit classified material.
