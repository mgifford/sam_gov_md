# Inyo National Forest (INF) Capture and Removal of 450 Wild Horses.

- Agency: AGRICULTURE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-19 12:19:15.002-04
- Solicitation Number: 127EAS26Q0026

## Summary

To conduct a wild horse drive-trap gather on the Inyo National Forest that will capture and remove 450 wild horses outside and in the surrounding areas of the Montgomery Pass WHT. A Pre-Award Conference call will be held via Teams on May 26, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific Standard Time.
