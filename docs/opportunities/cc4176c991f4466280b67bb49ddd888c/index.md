# Mat Sinking Unit Dozer Repairs

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Special Notice
- Posted: 2026-05-21 12:44:12.698-04

## Summary

W912EE The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers - Vicksburg District (USACE-MVK), in accordance with RFO 12.102(a), intends to award a sole source purchase order to Puckett Machinery Company (CAGE Code: 1JX26) for the repair of five (5) Caterpillar bulldozers. All repairs shall be completed by Caterpillar certified technicians. Refer to attached statement of work for complete requirement details. All work is scheduled to be complete no later than July 31, 2026. This is not a Request for Quote (RFQ), and no solicitation is available for this procurement. Nevertheless, any firm or individual that believes it can meet the Vicksburg District’s requirements described herein may submit a capabilities statement, which, if received by 12:00 PM CST on 29 May 2026, will be considered. Responses to this posting must be submitted via e-mail, no phone calls will be accepted. A determination not to compete this purchase based upon responses to this notice is solely within the discretion of the Federal Government. Any questions or comments should be directed to Alexandria Long, Alexandria.E.Long@usace.army.mil.
