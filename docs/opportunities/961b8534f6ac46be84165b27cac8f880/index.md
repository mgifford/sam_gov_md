# Sepulveda Basin Oxnard Lot

- Agency: DEPT OF DEFENSE
- Type: Solicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-27 11:25:52.408-04
- Solicitation Number: DACW09-1-26-1213

## Summary

The Government is seeking to lease approximately 14,683 SQ/FT parking lot space in the Sepulveda Basin Flood Control Basin, Los Angeles County. The legal description and map attached. The lease may commence as early as is feasible. The lease will have a one-year term, with the option to extend for an additional year, and 30-day termination rights. Description of Use: Non-permanent developments to alter the premises for general uses such as parking or shipping/receiving. Potential Lessee will be responsible for the payment of administration fees, amount to be determined, to complete the action.
