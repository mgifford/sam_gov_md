# Refurbishment of the Winfield House Cottages, London, United Kingdom

- Agency: STATE, DEPARTMENT OF
- Type: Presolicitation
- Posted: 2026-05-29 11:09:10.014-04
- Solicitation Number: 19GE5026R0090

## Summary

The Regional Procurement Support Office (RPSO) hereby provides the following pre-solicitation notice in respect of the issuance of a Request for Proposal (RFP) for: Refurbishment of the Winfield House Cottages U.S. Embassy London, United Kingdom The solicitation may result in award of a firm-fixed price bid/build construction contract in the third quarter of 2026. Qualified firms interested in this solicitation shall submit an expression of interest in writing to Ms. Patticha Gruenbeck at GruenbeckP@state.gov, with a copy to FrankfurtRPSO@state.gov, no later than 15:00 Central European Summer Time (CEST) on Friday, June 12, 2026, using solicitation number 19GE5026R0090 in the subject line.
