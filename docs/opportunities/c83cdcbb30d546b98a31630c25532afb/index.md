General Description: The Department of Veteran Affairs (VA), Veterans Integrated Service Network (VISN) 8, Orlando Veterans Affairs Healthcare System (OVAHCS) has a requirement for construction services to address deficiencies at the Lake Baldwin campus focused on exterior construction and repairs. All work shall be executed in compliance with the terms and conditions outlined in Solicitation 36C24826Q0585 and all applicable OSHA, federal, state, and local regulations. The repairs focus on the repaving of the facilities, parking lot, sidewalk covering, and new designation signs. The contractor shall complete the work required within 360 calendar days from receipt of Notice to Proceed (NTP) in accordance with the construction drawings and specifications prepared by an independent Architect-Engineer firm. The solicitation is anticipated to be issued on or about 05/15/2026. All information for this solicitation will be posted on the SAM.gov website at www.sam.gov. All interested firms must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM.GOV). Important Notice: Prior to submitting a proposal under SDVOSB set-aside solicitation, offerors must be registered in the System for Award Management (SAM.GOV) and certified in the SBA Veteran Certification site https://search.certifications.sba.gov/ as an active SBA SDVOSB certified. A joint venture may be considered an SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program, if (1). The joint venture qualifies as small under 19.201-1(b)(1), (2). The managing SDVOSB joint venture partner is designated in SAM as an SDVOSB concern certified by SBA; and (3) The joint venture complies with the requirements of 13 CFR 128.402.
